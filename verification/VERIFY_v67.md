# v67, Lemma L62's sampling squeeze: is the pipeline's number the rank the conclusion is about?

*The brief was cold: the step at [results.md:631](../results.md) was put neutrally, with no prior verdict supplied, and I deliberately did not open the sibling reports in [verification/](.) so as to reach the identification independently. **I ran nothing**, no script, no Sage, no numerics, and I wrote none; every source was read as text. ## Verdict

**`VALID AS WRITTEN`.**

The number the pipeline computes is **not** $\operatorname{rank}(\mu_K)$, and the step does not claim it is. It claims a chain of inequalities running from the computed number up to $\operatorname{rank}(\mu_K)$ and a cap running down to it, and every link of that chain points the way the step says it does. The computed number attains the cap, so the chain collapses to equality and $\dim\mathrm{ANN}=1$ follows. The identification the step needs is therefore an *inequality* identification, and it holds.

Three further identifications are load-bearing and the sentence at [results.md:631](../results.md) states none of them. All three are made elsewhere in the record, two of them verbatim, one only in the unrestricted case, and I supply the restricted case below. Two defects of description are recorded at the end; neither touches the logic.

## What the pipeline actually ranks

The number is produced twice in one run of [run_n5_assemble.py](../scripts/runs/run_n5_assemble.py), as `rk_A` at [line 1232](../scripts/runs/run_n5_assemble.py) and again as `rr` at [line 1268](../scripts/runs/run_n5_assemble.py), from the matrix `RROWS`, built at [line 1181](../scripts/runs/run_n5_assemble.py) as `[herm_row(g, TR) for g in GSAMP]`. Read as text, that matrix is:

| | what it is |
| --- | --- |
| **rows** | $40$ of them, one per sample point. `GSAMP` at [line 913](../scripts/runs/run_n5_assemble.py) holds $40$ random *real* Weyl tensors $W_t\in\mathcal W_5$, coefficients drawn uniformly from $\{-4,\dots,4\}$ against the $35$-dimensional module basis under the fixed seed `20260829`. The points are **not** a sample of $K$. |
| **columns** | $47$ of them, one per real coordinate of an $O(5)$-invariant **Hermitian** form on $K$. `herm_row` at [line 848](../scripts/runs/run_n5_assemble.py) walks the $16$ surviving blocks in the order $(-m_\lambda,\lambda)$ and emits, per block, the diagonal entries $\operatorname{Re}H_{aa}$ and then $(\operatorname{Re}H_{ab},\operatorname{Im}H_{ab})$ for $a<b$. The stored shape $[3,3,2,1,2,1,2,1,2,2,1,1,1,1,1,1]$ gives $\sum m'^2=47$, $\sum\binom{m'+1}2=36$, $\sum\binom{m'}2=11$. |
| **entries** | $H_{ab}(W_t)$ where $H=(TX)\,Q\,(TX)^*$: $X$ is the expansion of $W_t^3$ in the adapted basis, $T$ the per-block restriction to $K$'s multiplicity space, $Q$ the invariant form on the abstract $V_\lambda$ read off copy $1$. |
| **ring** | $\mathbb F_p$, in pair arithmetic over $\mathbb F_p[x]/(x^2+1)$, with $p$ the run's `N5_PRIME` ([line 97](../scripts/runs/run_n5_assemble.py)). Every $p$ used is $1\bmod 4$, so that quotient *splits*; the code is written around that (the $T a=0$ diagnostic is asserted on $q$ itself, not on $q\bar q$, at [line 1094](../scripts/runs/run_n5_assemble.py)). |

So the entry in row $t$, column $c$ is the value at $W_t$ of the sextic $\mu(G_c)$ for the $c$-th coordinate direction $G_c$ of the invariant Hermitian Grams on $K$. The matrix is the matrix of $\mathrm{ev}_S\circ\mu_K$, where $S$ is the $40$-point sample; its rank is $\operatorname{rank}(\mathrm{ev}_S\circ\mu_K)$ reduced mod $p$.

## The squeeze, link by link

Write $\mu_K$ for multiplication restricted to the invariants supported on $K$, $A_{\mathbb Q}$ for the exact rational matrix the run reduces, and $A_p$ for what it computes.

$$\operatorname{rank}_{\mathbb F_p}(A_p)\ \le\ \operatorname{rank}_{\mathbb Q}(A_{\mathbb Q})\ =\ \operatorname{rank}(\mathrm{ev}_S\circ\mu_K)\ \le\ \operatorname{rank}(\mu_K)\ \le\ 13 .$$

- **Link 1, reduction.** Valid provided $A_p$ is the entrywise reduction of a $p$-integral $A_{\mathbb Q}$. It is: every per-weight block is inverted mod $p$ with an explicit pivot search that asserts non-singularity (`the adapted basis is SINGULAR at weight w`, [line 775](../scripts/runs/run_n5_assemble.py)), and a matrix invertible mod $p$ has $\det\not\equiv0$, hence a $p$-integral rational inverse reducing to the modular one. `gpinv` asserts separately that no pivot has zero norm, necessary precisely because $p\equiv1\bmod4$ makes the norm form isotropic.
- **Link 2, sampling.** Trivial: $\mathrm{ev}_S\circ\mu_K$ factors through $\mu_K$.
- **Link 3, the cap.** $\lVert W\rVert^6$ is an invariant sextic with $\lVert W_0\rVert^6=1$, so $\mathrm{ev}_{W_0}$ is a nonzero functional on the $14$-dimensional $(\mathrm{Sym}^6\mathcal W_5)^{O(5)}$. Every $f\in K$ vanishes at $W_0$, so every $\mu_K(G)=\sum G_{ab}f_a\bar f_b$ vanishes there, so $\operatorname{im}\mu_K$ lies in a $13$-dimensional subspace. This half is a theorem in six lines and needs no run.

The record reports $\operatorname{rank}_{\mathbb F_p}(A_p)=13$, so all four quantities equal $13$. The step's two directional claims (sampling under-counts, reduction under-counts), are stated correctly and are not reversed.

## From $\operatorname{rank}(\mu_K)=13$ to $\dim\mathrm{ANN}=1$

By [Lemma L59](../results.md), $\mathrm{ANN}=\{F\in(\mathrm{Sym}^6\mathcal W_5)^{O(5)}:\mathrm{Cat}_F(K,K)=0\}$ is, through $\langle F,\mu(B)\rangle=\langle\mathrm{Cat}_F,B\rangle$, exactly $(\operatorname{im}\mu_K)^{\perp}$ inside the $14$, the annihilator of the *image*, not the kernel of $\mu_K$. The apolar pairing is non-degenerate on the $14$ (L59 derives this from surjectivity of the unrestricted $\mu$; it is also immediate from positive-definiteness of the Fischer form), so $\dim\mathrm{ANN}=14-13=1$. The functional $\mathcal A(W_0^6)$ satisfies $\langle\mathcal A(W_0^6),F\rangle\propto F(W_0)$, hence lies in $\mathrm{ANN}$ and is nonzero on $\lVert W\rVert^6$; a nonzero element of a one-dimensional space spans it. And $F_5(W_0)=0$ exactly over $\mathbb Z$, so $F_5\perp\mathrm{ANN}$. Every clause of the conclusion follows.

## The three identifications the sentence does not make

**(i) The rows are $\mu^*(\mathrm{ev}_W)$ only if the multiplicity copies are identified equivariantly.** This is the one hypothesis whose failure breaks the squeeze in the dangerous direction: if the copies carry inconsistent identifications of the abstract $V_\lambda$, the row is not a functional pulled back along $\mu_K$, Link 2 fails, and a rank of $13$ proves nothing. The record has this, at [findings/sym3-blocks.md:233](../findings/sym3-blocks.md) ("the sampled row is $\mu^*(\mathrm{ev}_W)$ only when the copies are identified equivariantly"), with reviewer 209's $S_3$ counterexample on $\mathbb R^3$ giving sampled rank $10$ against a cap of $7$. The guard is the multi-probe Schur test at [line 501](../scripts/runs/run_n5_assemble.py), which asserts that $h_{ij}$ read at position $t$ is independent of $t$ across *every* position of every multi-copy block, with the skipped count separately asserted zero so that "no disagreement" cannot mean "never compared". [REVIEW_r214.md](../reviews/REVIEW_r214.md) names the same dependency and calls it the sharper of the two residual risks, because attainment on the cap is invisible.

**(ii) $T$ cuts out $K$, and vanishing at the single point $W_0$ is enough.** The code imposes one condition per $\lambda_2$-even block, $\sum_i a_iu_i=0$ with $u$ unconjugated and read off the *value* $\langle f,W_0^3\rangle$ rather than the expansion coefficients ([lines 1018–1048](../scripts/runs/run_n5_assemble.py)); the record notes that both earlier variants came out at rank $14$, above the cap. Two things make this the orbit condition. First, $C=\operatorname{span}\{(gW_0)^3\}$ is the submodule generated by one vector, so its multiplicity component in each block is the line $\mathbb R a_\lambda$, and $K=C^\perp$ has multiplicity space $a_\lambda^{\perp}$, one condition per block, codimension $\sum_{\lambda\ \mathrm{even}}d_\lambda=3570$, which is [Lemma L87](../findings/sym3-blocks.md)'s number. Second, the restriction cuts the copy index and leaves $V_\lambda$ whole, so the restricted cubics form an $O(5)$-submodule and a submodule vanishing at $W_0$ vanishes at every $gW_0$. The record states this second point verbatim at [findings/sym3-blocks.md:234](../findings/sym3-blocks.md). That $W_0^3$'s $\lambda$-component is a *pure* tensor $a_\lambda\otimes v_0$ (which is what licenses reading $u$ off a single position), is [Lemma L86](../findings/sym3-blocks.md)'s $\dim V_\lambda^H=1$ applied to the $H$-invariant $W_0^3$; the code assumes it at [line 1018](../scripts/runs/run_n5_assemble.py) without citing it.

**(iii) The $47$ columns and the $36$ unknowns give the same rank.** The quantity the record names is $\operatorname{rank}(\mu\vert_{\mathrm{Sym}^2(K)^{O(5)}})$, from the $36$ to the $14$ ([findings/sym3-blocks.md:247](../findings/sym3-blocks.md)); the matrix has $47$ columns. These agree because the $11$-dimensional $\Lambda^2(K)^{O(5)}$ lies in the kernel: $W^3$ is a real vector, so in a basis real for the real structure $\sigma$ the sextic is $\sum G_{ab}f_af_b$ with $f$ real, and an antisymmetric $G$ contributes nothing. Hence the map factors through the $36$-dimensional quotient and the two ranks coincide. The record states this for the unrestricted $89=65+24$ split, at [run_n5_assemble.py:582](../scripts/runs/run_n5_assemble.py); the restricted $47=36+11$ form of it I did not find written anywhere, and it is the one the pipeline uses. Note that the argument survives reviewer 204's refutation at [run_n5_assemble.py:599](../scripts/runs/run_n5_assemble.py) (that the gauge directions are *not* the "im" coordinates of this $\mathbb Q(i)$ basis) because rank is insensitive to which coordinates carry the kernel.

## Two defects of description, neither fatal

**"A rank computed from a sample of $K$" names the wrong set.** Nothing samples $K$. $K$ enters in full, as the exact restriction $T$ on all $25$ surviving multiplicities; what is sampled is the *evaluation points*, $40$ Weyl tensors. The sentence is nonetheless true under both readings, and the conclusion follows under both: a sample of points lowers rank by Link 2, and a subspace $K''\subseteq K$ would lower it too, since $13\le\operatorname{rank}(\mu_{K''})\le\operatorname{rank}(\mu_K)\le13$ closes either way. So this costs the step nothing beyond misleading a reader about which object the pipeline touched. The summary line at [results.md:206](../results.md) says only "a sampled rank is a lower bound" and is clean.

**"At four primes" is four reductions of one rational matrix.** The sample points are regenerated from the fixed seed at every prime so that the rows correspond entry for entry ([line 1245](../scripts/runs/run_n5_assemble.py)), and the four stored dumps, [n5_rows_1099511627873.json](../scripts/runs/n5_rows_1099511627873.json) and its three siblings, all $40\times47$ with identical `blocks` and `shape`, are four reductions of the same $A_{\mathbb Q}$. Logically one prime suffices, since reduction can only lower rank and $13$ already meets the cap. The four are not idle, though, and this is the strongest defence of the phrase: they guard **Link 1** rather than the inequality. `gp` at [line 158](../scripts/runs/run_n5_assemble.py) carries no denominator assert, unlike `to_p` at [line 223](../scripts/runs/run_n5_assemble.py), so a prime dividing a denominator would silently return the reduction of a pole and $A_p$ would not be a reduction of anything; agreement across four independent $40$-bit primes makes that artefact implausible. Stated as four *measurements* the phrase over-claims; stated as four *reductions*, it is exactly right and does real work.

## The strongest defence of the step

Read as a report of a computation, the sentence misnames its sample. Read as mathematics (which is how a lemma should be read), it asserts three things, and all three are true: that order-one vanishing at $W_0$ caps $\operatorname{rank}(\mu_K)$ at $13$; that any rank obtained by composing $\mu_K$ with an evaluation and then reducing mod $p$ is a lower bound for it; and that a lower bound meeting an upper bound is an equality. The first is a theorem the step proves in its own opening clause. The second is two factorisations. The third is arithmetic. What the step leaves out is not a step of the argument but the hypothesis under which the pipeline's number *is* such a lower bound (the equivariant identification of copies) and the record carries that hypothesis, names the counterexample that would violate it, and runs the test that guards it. The cap is doing the work, the measurement supplies only the matching lower bound, and the two together close.
