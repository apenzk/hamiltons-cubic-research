## Passage B, the section the lemma's argument is written in ([findings/brauer-pairing.md](../findings/brauer-pairing.md) lines 86-98)

## The seed is itself a diagram

The realisation theorem says one nonzero seed per block generates that block, and says nothing about **where a seed comes from**. That gap is what kept obligation (ii) open: if the seed must be found by search, finding a nonzero element of $\operatorname{Hom}(V_\lambda,V^{\otimes12})$ is the same construction problem the item started with, once per block.

**It need not be searched for.** Write $k=(12-|\lambda|)/2$, let $\iota_\lambda:V_\lambda\hookrightarrow V^{\otimes|\lambda|}$ be the inclusion, and let $\mathrm{cup}=\sum_a e_a\otimes e_a\in V\otimes V$ be the invariant metric element.

> **Lemma.** *Provided $\lambda$ is $O(n)$-admissible, i.e. $\lambda'_1+\lambda'_2\le n$*, the map $c_0:=\iota_\lambda\otimes\mathrm{cup}^{\otimes k}$ is $O(n)$-equivariant $V_\lambda\to V^{\otimes12}$ and **nonzero**.

**The admissibility hypothesis is load-bearing.** Without it $V_\lambda=0$ and $\iota_\lambda$ does not exist, witness $\lambda=(2,2)$ at $n=2,3$ and $\lambda=(2,1,1)$ at $n=3$. At $n=12$ it holds for **all $71$ blocks**, and it is **tight**: $\lambda=(2,2,2,2,2,2)$ sits at $\lambda'_1+\lambda'_2=12=n$, zero slack.

Equivariance is closure of the tensor product under equivariant maps, and it is equivariance for the **full** $O(n)$, reflection included, which is exactly what the $SO$-versus-$O$ gap turns on. Nonvanishing needs only that a tensor product of nonzero vectors is nonzero; the cap computation returning $n^k\iota_\lambda$ proves it too, and proves *the map* nonzero rather than merely some contraction. **Nonzero in $M_\lambda$ and nonzero as a map are the same statement**, $M_\lambda$ *being* $\operatorname{Hom}_{O(n)}(V_\lambda,V^{\otimes12})$.

So $c_0$ is a nonzero element of $M_\lambda$ **in closed form** and the realisation theorem applies to it: **every block has an explicit seed, with no search.** *It is a **padding** diagram only where $k>0$, at the $16$ blocks with $\lvert\lambda\rvert=12$ ($17$ of the $184$ multiplicities) $k=0$, there is no cup at all, and the content there is that an inclusion is nonzero.*

## Passage C, the theorem the seed feeds ([findings/brauer-pairing.md](../findings/brauer-pairing.md) lines 40-50)

## The diagram-realisation theorem, one seed per block

**The covariants reach diagram form through a seed, not through 184 separate expansions.** The construction emits vectors in a weight space, while the pairing theorem above, the padding transfer and the whole $n$-dependence are stated over Brauer diagrams; the gap between the two is what this theorem closes.

Write $f=12$ for the leg count, three Weyl tensors, four legs each.

> **Theorem.** At **every** $n$:
> 1. $M_\lambda=\operatorname{Hom}_{O(n)}(V_\lambda, V^{\otimes 12})$ is **irreducible** as a module over the **image** $\nu(B_{12}(n))$, hence generated by **any** nonzero vector;
> 2. so for one nonzero seed $c_0$ per block, $\{\, e\,\nu(D)\,c_0 : D \text{ a Brauer diagram} \,\}$ spans that block's covariant space, $e$ being the projector onto $\mathrm{Sym}^3(\mathrm{Weyl}_n)$.

Step 2 needs $e$ to lie in $\nu(B_{12}(n))$, and it does, as a **linear combination** of diagrams rather than as a single diagram. $e$ is an $O(n)$-equivariant endomorphism of $V^{\otimes 12}$, and the classical **First Fundamental Theorem for $O(n)$**, holding at every $n$, with no threshold, puts every such endomorphism in $\nu(B_{12}(n))$.

## Passage D, the file's opening statement, where the blocks are counted ([findings/brauer-pairing.md](../findings/brauer-pairing.md) lines 1-13)

# The Brauer pairing is a monomial in $n$, of degree at most six

## The statement

For two degree-$6$ diagram covariants $P,Q$ of $\mathcal W_n$, the Gram entry is

$$\langle P,Q\rangle \;=\; n^{L}\cdot q_D(W),$$

a **monomial** in $n$ times an $n$-independent contraction, not a general polynomial. $L$ is the number of cycle components of the pairing graph, and

$$L\;\le\;\min(a_P,a_Q)\;\le\;\Big\lfloor \tfrac{f}{2}\Big\rfloor,\qquad f=\lvert\lambda\rvert,$$

with $a_P,a_Q$ the numbers of bare metrics carried by $P$ and $Q$. Since $\lvert\lambda\rvert\in\{0,2,\dots,12\}$ for this certificate, **the ceiling is $n^6$**, attained only in the $16$ $O(12)$ blocks with $\lvert\lambda\rvert=12$. *($19$ is the $SO(12)$ count; three chiral pairs are the difference, and this record's blocks are the $71$ $O(12)$ labels.)*

For a block $\lambda$ of the sextic invariants, write $k=(12-\lvert\lambda\rvert)/2$, let $\iota_\lambda\colon V_\lambda\hookrightarrow V^{\otimes\lvert\lambda\rvert}$ be the inclusion of the irreducible into the tensor power, and let $\mathrm{cup}$ be the pairing. Then the block's seed is the composite of $\iota_\lambda$ with $k$ cups (one seed per block, given by a diagram), provided $\lambda$ is admissible for $O(n)$. Without admissibility $V_\lambda=0$ and the inclusion does not exist, so the hypothesis is load-bearing. The lemma removes a search from the certificate pipeline and adds no certificate.
