# Conventions, fixed once

**Every definition, normalisation and index convention the record uses, stated here and quoted elsewhere.** A findings file that needs one of these cites this file; it does not restate it.

*The rule this file exists to make possible: **definitions and conventions are quoted, never restated**. Half this programme's disputes between independent builds have been convention mismatches, and a convention restated in two files is a convention that will disagree with itself. Where a block needs a convention that is not here, it is added here and quoted there.*

## The normalisations, each a place a build has gone wrong

- **The Killing form of $\mathfrak{so}(n)$** is $B(X,Y)=(n-2)\operatorname{tr}(XY)$, with $\operatorname{tr}(v^2)=-2$ for a unit bivector, so $\kappa=2(n-2)$ in an orthonormal basis. **Killing constants come from the definition, never from an index convention.**
- **Structure constants** $c_{abc}=\langle[v_a,v_b],v_c\rangle$ are **totally antisymmetric**, the inner product being ad-invariant; so $T_{abc}=c_{abc}^2$ is totally symmetric and vanishes when two indices coincide.
- **$Q(W_{p,q})=\frac{n-1}{pq}W_{p,q}$ is a statement about the RAW representative, not about the unit sphere.** The eigenvalue of a critical point at unit norm is $\theta$, and the two differ: at $(3,3)$ the raw factor is $\tfrac59$ against $\theta_0(6)=\sqrt{10}/3$. *A criticality relation quoted without its normalisation reads as a $\theta$ value*, and has been tabulated as one.
- **$m$ carries two objects inside [route-a.md](route-a.md), forty-seven lines apart**: the complementary dimension $n-4$ in the $SO(m)$-branching, and $\sqrt{2/3}\max P$ (the attained maximum), in the tube proof's own bootstrap. *So "$\gamma_V\le m$" reads as a statement about $n-4$ unless the block is read first.* **Write $n-4$ for the dimension when both are in scope.**

## The second-kind operator, and the index order, SETTLED on Li's $iklj$

$\mathring W$ acts on $S^2_0$, and **the index order is load-bearing**: $W_{ikjl}$ in place of $W_{iklj}$ negates $\mathring W$ and flips the sign of the bi-spectral identity's $-\tfrac12$ term.

**The record's convention is Li's**, quoted from [Li 2021](../references/li-2021-nonnegative-second-kind/notes.md) §2, equation (2.2), on p. 6 of [arXiv:2112.08465](https://arxiv.org/abs/2112.08465) and again in the introduction on p. 2:

> The second one, denoted by $\mathring R:S^2(V)\to S^2(V)$, is defined by $\mathring R(e_i\odot e_j)=\sum_{k,l}R_{iklj}\,e_k\odot e_l$.

With $u\odot v=u\otimes v+v\otimes u$, so that $h=\tfrac12\sum_{i,j}h_{ij}\,e_i\odot e_j$, this is in matrix form

$$(\mathring Wh)_{ij}=\sum_{k,l}W_{iklj}h_{kl},$$

the pair symmetries making the symmetrisation of the output automatic.

**Two closed-form anchors, each reproducing its own paper's published signs.**

| check | Li's $iklj$ | the order $ikjl$ |
| --- | --- | --- |
| round $S^n$, $R_{ijkl}=g_{ik}g_{jl}-g_{il}g_{jk}$ | $\mathring R=+\mathrm{Id}$ on $S^2_0$, Li Example 2.4 | $-\mathrm{Id}$ |
| $\mathbb{CP}^m$ Fubini–Study, $\mathring Rh=h+3JhJ$ on $S^2_0$ | $-2$ at multiplicity $m^2-1$, $+4$ at $m(m+1)$, Li Example 2.5 | $-4$ at $m(m+1)$, $+2$ at $m^2-1$ |

**Both orders are in the literature, and each table is right in its own order.** [Bourguignon–Karcher 1978](../references/bourguignon-karcher-1978-pinching/notes.md) §2.2(ii), p. 74, define $\mathring R(h)(a,b)=\sum_{i,j}R(e_i,a,e_j,b)h(e_i,e_j)$ (contracted on slots 1 and 3, free on 2 and 4, which is the $ikjl$ order) and their §5.3 table on p. 84 accordingly reads $-4$ at multiplicity $n(n+1)$ and $2$ at $n^2-1$, the negation of Li's. The same difference appears on the trace part, as BK's $\mathring R(g)=\rho$ against Li's footnote-5 $\mathring R(g,g)=-S$. **Comparing a BK-order spectrum with a Li-order one without flipping the sign is the trap.**

**Where the record stands.** [bispectral.md](bispectral.md) and [target.md](target.md) are in Li's $iklj$ and are correct as written; target.md's anchor (the order making the round sphere satisfy $\mathring R=+\mathrm{Id}$), picks out the same order the quoted line does. [stratum-two-negative.md](stratum-two-negative.md) is written throughout in BK's $ikjl$, consistently, and now carries a note saying so: its operator is $-\mathring W$, so its $\lambda_{\max}(\mathring R)\le1$ statements read $\lambda_{\min}(\mathring W)\ge-1$ in the record's order. That conversion changes result statements and is queued for a verification round. The norm identity $\lVert\mathring W\rVert_F^2=3\lVert W\rVert^2_{\Lambda^2}$ holds in both orders.

## The vertex matrix

**$K$ is the symmetric hollow $n\times n$ **sectional matrix**, $K_{ab}=W_{abab}$ for all $a\ne b$, and $t=\lVert K\rVert_F^2=\sum_{a\ne b}K_{ab}^2$, the sum over **ordered** pairs. Then $t\le2$ with **$t=2$ iff $W$ is coordinate-diagonal in some frame**, and $t$ is the coordinate the bi-spectral relaxation is swept in.

> **THE ORDERED SUM IS THE WHOLE OF THE NORMALISATION AND WAS AMBIGUOUS HERE.** Writing $K$ as $(W_{abab})_{a<b}$ reads as an index set over *unordered* pairs, whose square sum is $\tfrac t2\le1$, so the bound $t\le2$ and that reading are inconsistent. *Checked: at a unit-norm coordinate-diagonal $W$ the unordered sum is $1$ and the ordered sum is $2$, and the record's $t=2$ is the ordered one.* In operator terms $t=2\lVert\operatorname{diag}W\rVert^2$ with the diagonal taken in the pair basis, so $t\le2$ is Cauchy–Schwarz against $\lVert W\rVert=1$, with equality exactly when $W$ is diagonal.

> **AND $t$ IS PER-FRAME UNLESS SAID OTHERWISE.** "$t=2$ iff coordinate-diagonal **in some frame**" is a statement about the *extremal* value $\max_Ft_F$ over $O(n)$, while every formula that uses $t$ as a quantity of a given configuration means the frame's own $t_F$. **The two differ, and substituting the extremal value into a bound that decreases in $t$ turns it into an unjustified claim**, see [DERIVE-mx1.md](../reviews/DERIVE-mx1.md), where exactly that would assert the uncorrected four-index inequality that is false off the eigenframe. *Write $t_F$ when the frame is fixed and $t=\max_Ft_F$ when it is not.*

**Diagonality is MEASURED as off-diagonal Frobenius mass minimised over $O(n)$**, not as the largest entry in the constructor's frame. *The two statistics disagree even on ordering.*
