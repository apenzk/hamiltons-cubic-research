#### Lemma L143, $\sup\lvert P\rvert\le1+\sqrt{n-2}$, from the two recorded marginals

*Derived 2026-09-03 as [I31](../plan.md). **Gate 2: [r444](../reviews/REVIEW_r444.md) and [r445](../reviews/REVIEW_r445.md), independently on identical briefs, CONFIRMED the inequality step by step**, including the two marginals, the index bookkeeping and both normalisations, and found no domain violation, the borrowed bound is pointwise at unit norm. It improves the record's standing $\Theta(n)$ bounds to $O(\sqrt n)$.*

> **[Post F10](fence-posts.md) already holds this bound and beats it, and the domination is logical rather than numerical.** F10's marginal LP runs over *exactly* these two constraints plus $\mathop{\mathrm{tr}}(W^\#)=-1$, so this lemma's feasible set is a **superset** of the LP's while $\lambda_{\max}p+B_5\ge P$ on it, forcing $\mathrm{LP}(n)\le1+\sqrt{n-2}$ at every $n$. *Measured: $1.606$ against $3.000$ at $n=6$; $2.53$ against $4.162$ at $n=12$.* **This lemma cannot beat F10 anywhere**, and F10's title is *marginal relaxations of the bracket tensor cannot bound $P$*, this is that route, executed by hand.
>
> **What survives is that it is a proved closed form at every $n$, where F10 has measurements at a few.** That is its whole residual value.

**Statement.** $\displaystyle\sup_{\lVert W\rVert=1}\lvert P(W)\rvert\;\le\;1+\sqrt{n-2}$.

**Proof.** $P$ is odd and the sphere compact with $\nabla P=3Q$, so $\sup\lvert P\rvert=\sup P$ is attained at a critical point, where $P=\theta$ and [second-order.md](second-order.md)'s bound $\theta\le\lambda_{\max}p+B_5$ applies, with $p=\sum_{\lambda>0}\lambda^2$, $\mu=\lvert\lambda\rvert$ and $B_5=\tfrac12\big(\sum_\alpha\langle T_\alpha\mu,\mu\rangle^2\big)^{1/2}$.

*The first term is bounded by $1$ outright*: $p\le\sum_\alpha\lambda_\alpha^2=1$ and $\lambda_{\max}\le1$.

*The second uses both recorded marginals of $T$*, the **pair cap** $\sum_\gamma T_{\alpha\beta\gamma}=\lVert[v_\alpha,v_\beta]\rVert^2\le2$ (**[Lemma L31](stratum-two-negative.md)**) and the **Killing row mass** $\sum_{\beta\gamma}T_{\alpha\beta\gamma}=2(n-2)$ (**[Lemma L153](#lemma-l153--the-killing-row-marginal-sum_betalvertv_alphav_betarvert22n-2)**). Each summand is nonnegative and, by $\mu_\beta\mu_\gamma\le\tfrac12(\mu_\beta^2+\mu_\gamma^2)$ with $T$ totally symmetric,
$$0\;\le\;\langle T_\alpha\mu,\mu\rangle\;\le\;\sum_{\beta}\mu_\beta^2\sum_\gamma T_{\alpha\beta\gamma}\;\le\;2 ,$$
so $\sum_\alpha\langle T_\alpha\mu,\mu\rangle^2\le2\sum_\alpha\langle T_\alpha\mu,\mu\rangle$. The same AM–GM against the **row mass** gives $\sum_\alpha\langle T_\alpha\mu,\mu\rangle=\sum_{\alpha\beta\gamma}T_{\alpha\beta\gamma}\mu_\beta\mu_\gamma\le\sum_\beta\mu_\beta^2\sum_{\alpha\gamma}T_{\alpha\beta\gamma}=2(n-2)$. Hence $B_5\le\tfrac12\sqrt{4(n-2)}=\sqrt{n-2}$. $\square$ *The estimate $B_5\le\sqrt{n-2}$ is machine-checked at every Weyl eigenframe as `B5_frame` in [L143.lean](../lean/HamiltonsCubic/Results/L143.lean), and the first term's $\lambda_{\max}p\le1$ and the sum $\lambda_{\max}p+B_5\le1+\sqrt{n-2}$ as `first_term_le` and `L143_at_frame` in [L143Assembly.lean](../lean/HamiltonsCubic/Results/L143Assembly.lean), and the attainment of $\sup\lvert P\rvert$ at a critical point as `sup_abs_attained` in [L143Attain.lean](../lean/HamiltonsCubic/Results/L143Attain.lean), with `sup_abs_le_of_critical` (a bound at every critical point bounds $\lvert P\rvert$), and the whole assembly as `L143_headline` there: if every critical point satisfies $P\le\lambda_{\max}p+B_5$ in its spectral frame, then $\lvert P\rvert\le1+\sqrt{n-2}$ on the unit Weyl sphere. The second-order bound is `second_order_pointwise` ($P\le\lambda_{\max}p+B_5$ at every unit Weyl eigenframe), so the lemma is proved in the kernel as `L143_unconditional`: $\lvert P\rvert\le1+\sqrt{n-2}$ on the unit Weyl sphere at every $n\ge4$. That bound holds pointwise at every unit Weyl tensor ([second-order.md](second-order.md), the flatness bound's sharper replacement), so the attainment is not needed for the inequality itself.*

**Where it stands against what is known.** At $n=12$ it gives $4.162$; the record's standing uniform bound is $n-2=10$ ([Lemma L70](#lemma-l70-the-deficiency-identity-and-what-saturates-it)), so this is a factor $2.4$ better. *The comparand for the truth is $\theta_0(12)=1.236$, giving a factor $3.4$ short, **not** the $\approx1.5449$ an earlier version wrote here, which is the supremum of the **relaxation bound** rather than of $P$, and which is [L142](#lemma-l142-withdrawn-dominated-by-bounds-already-on-the-record)'s withdrawn framing error repeated eighteen lines below its own withdrawal notice ([r444](../reviews/REVIEW_r444.md)).* **What $1.5449$ does say is that it is the floor of this lemma's own route**: no argument through $\lambda_{\max}p+B_5$ can get below it.

**Where the loss sits, which is not where it looks.** At flat $\mu$ the row-mass step is *saturated* and $B_5\to\sqrt2$; the whole loss is in $\sum_\alpha f_\alpha^2\le2\sum_\alpha f_\alpha$, which needs the mass concentrated on $n-2$ of the $\binom n2$ rows ([r445](../reviews/REVIEW_r445.md)). **A successor attacks that step, or carries the minimum of this bound and the flatness bound, which are incomparable pointwise.**

**A free strengthening to $1+\sqrt{n-1}$ is owed, from the same two inputs**, [r445](../reviews/REVIEW_r445.md) splits the Rayleigh matrix as $M=\mathop{\mathrm{diag}}(\lambda)+\tfrac12u$ with $u$ hollow, making the parts Frobenius-orthogonal, and reads $\lVert M\rVert_F^2\le n-1$ off the same pair cap and row mass. *This lemma is the triangle-inequality reading of a split whose Pythagorean reading is free*, $3.317$ against $4.162$ at $n=12$. **Registered as [I32](../plan.md), unaudited, and it does not change the growth rate or F10's domination.** **The two marginals are the whole of its input**, which is why it stops at $O(\sqrt n)$: the pair cap bounds each row of $T$ and the row mass bounds their total, and neither sees that $\mu$ concentrates where $T$ does not.

**What it does for [I2](../plan.md).** Substituting $\lVert P\rVert_\infty=C\sqrt n$ into [r443](../reviews/REVIEW_r443.md)'s own displayed chain returns $r_n=\Omega(n^{-3/2})$, **the same exponent an $O(1)$ bound gives**, because the defect-tail branch caps it there. *So $\sqrt n$ is exactly the threshold, this lemma already unblocks [I2](../plan.md)'s exponent, and [I31](../plan.md)'s absolute constant $K$ buys the constant alone.* **An earlier version of this paragraph had that backwards.** *Both reviewers reached this independently; it is the lemma's one load-bearing consequence.* *[Post F12](INDEX-traps.md) says why a single inequality cannot close it: the flat and low-rank regimes need separate arguments, and this lemma uses one inequality for both.*

#### Lemma L7, a bound at every critical point, and the graded-subalgebra equality class, canonical statement and proof

Compressing the criticality equation onto the negative spectral part gives, at every critical point,
$$\theta\mathop{\mathrm{tr}}(W_-)+\mathop{\mathrm{tr}}(W_-^2)\;\le\;\|\mathrm{Ric}(W_-)\|^2+6\,\delta^2,$$
with $\delta^2$ the defect of [Observation L15](problem.md), and $W_\pm$ the positive and negative spectral parts **as operators on all of $\Lambda^2$**, so that $\mathrm{Ric}(W_-)$ has its usual meaning.

**Proof.** Tracing $Q(W)=\theta W$ against $\Pi_-$ gives the exact identity $\theta\mathop{\mathrm{tr}}(W_-)+\mathop{\mathrm{tr}}(W_-^2)=-\mathop{\mathrm{tr}}(\Pi_-W^\#)$. Bilinearity of $\#$ splits $W^\#=W_+^\#-2\,W_+\#W_-+W_-^\#$; **rank-one positivity, $vv^{\mathsf T}\#ww^{\mathsf T}=\tfrac12[v,w][v,w]^{\mathsf T}$, a theorem rather than an assumption**, kills two terms against $\Pi_-\preceq I$ and leaves $\le2\mathop{\mathrm{tr}}(W_+\#W_-)$. Polarising [L15](problem.md)'s identity at $(W_+,W_-)$, with $\mathrm{Ric}(W)=0$ and $\mathrm{Alt}(W)=0$, turns that into $\lVert\mathrm{Ric}(W_-)\rVert^2+6\delta^2$ exactly. $\square$

**The hypotheses are criticality, Ricci-flatness and first Bianchi**, the last two used twice each in the final step, and all three load-bearing. *The version's ingredient list. There is no sign condition and no unit-norm requirement. **The constant $6$ is [L15](problem.md)'s and is dimension-free**, entering only at that last equality; it is invisible at every sphere product, where $\delta^2=0$, and pinned instead at $W_{\mathbb{CP}^2}$, where $\delta^2=\tfrac19$ and both sides are $\tfrac43$.

**L7 is the trace of the matrix inequality $X_{\mathrm{inv}}\succeq0$ that [L15](problem.md)'s refuted half needed**, which is why only L15's surviving identity is used here.

**Equality holds precisely when the eigenspace ranges are graded by the Lie bracket**, $[\mathfrak p,\mathfrak p]\perp\mathfrak m$, $[\mathfrak m,\mathfrak m]\perp\mathfrak m$, $[\mathfrak p,\mathfrak m]\subseteq\mathfrak m$. **The kernel closes too, so the class is a genuine graded subalgebra rather than one stated modulo $\ker W$**, [Lemma L155](#lemma-l155-x_mathrminv0-exactly-on-the-mathbb-z_2-graded-subalgebras-with-no-criticality) carries the closure clause and the hedge is retired.

> **READING THAT AS "A COMPACT SYMMETRIC PAIR WITH $E_+$ THE ISOTROPY ALGEBRA" IS FALSE IN BOTH DIRECTIONS, AND THIS FILE SAYS SO 79 LINES BELOW.** $W_{\mathbb{CP}^2}$ at $n=4$ attains equality exactly while $\dim E_+=1$, $\dim E_-=2$ and $\dim\ker W=3$, so $E_+\oplus E_-$ is $3$-dimensional inside a $6$-dimensional $\mathfrak{so}(4)$ and **$E_+$ is the even part of no grading of $\mathfrak{so}(4)$**, *the stronger reading once written here, that there is no $\mathbb Z_2$-grading at all, is overstated and corrected by [r701](../reviews/REVIEW_r701.md) and [r702](../reviews/REVIEW_r702.md): $\mathfrak{so}(4)=\mathfrak u(2)\oplus\mathfrak m$ is one, and $E_+\oplus E_-=\Lambda^2_+\cong\mathfrak{so}(3)$ is graded as $(\mathfrak{so}(3),\mathfrak{so}(2))$, which is what [L155](#lemma-l155-x_mathrminv0-exactly-on-the-mathbb-z_2-graded-subalgebras-with-no-criticality) needs*, and it is one of the block's own named equality points. The converse fails too: a symmetric pair gives equality only where $W$'s spectral splitting coincides with it, and [Lemma L23](ungraded-slack.md)'s cap makes that gap concrete. **"It fails strictly at the low-value ones" is false as well**, $S^2\times S^4$ at $n=6$ attains equality exactly at $\theta=0.968246<1$. *Gate 2, [r515](../reviews/REVIEW_r515.md) and [r516](../reviews/REVIEW_r516.md) on identical briefs, agreeing; the inequality itself each rebuilt cold, the block having carried an ingredient list and no proof.* So the graded and ungraded cases separate, and the graded one becomes a classification problem rather than an analytic one.

#### Lemma L154, the equality locus of $\mathop{\mathrm{scal}}(X_{\mathrm{inv}})\ge0$ is $X_{\mathrm{inv}}=0$, and at scalar $h$ that is the grant's equality

*Derived 2026-09-05 in [DERIVE-j5](../reviews/DERIVE-j5.md), batch 2. Gate 2: [r699](../reviews/REVIEW_r699.md) and [r700](../reviews/REVIEW_r700.md), identical briefs, CONFIRMED with corrections applied here.*

> **LEMMA L154.** *Let $W\ne0$ be a critical point, $Q(W)=\theta W$, and $X_{\mathrm{inv}}=\lvert W\rvert^\#-P_+W^\#P_++P_-W^\#P_-$. **(i)** $\mathop{\mathrm{tr}}X_{\mathrm{inv}}=0$ if and only if $X_{\mathrm{inv}}=0$. **(ii)** If $h=\mathrm{Ric}(\lvert W\rvert)=\lambda\,\mathrm{id}$ (as it is whenever the isotropy group of $W$ acts irreducibly on $\mathbb R^n$ over $\mathbb R$) then $\lambda=2\mathop{\mathrm{tr}}\lvert W\rvert/n>0$, $H=\mathrm{Ric}^*(h)=2\lambda\,\mathrm{Id}$, $J_5=\lambda\mathop{\mathrm{scal}}(X_{\mathrm{inv}})\ge0$, and $J_5=0$ if and only if $X_{\mathrm{inv}}=0$.*

**Proof.** (i) The three spectral-diagonal blocks are $P_+X_{\mathrm{inv}}P_+=4P_+CP_+$ with $C=W_+\#W_-$, $P_-X_{\mathrm{inv}}P_-=2P_-(W_+^\#+W_-^\#)P_-$ and $P_0X_{\mathrm{inv}}P_0=P_0\lvert W\rvert^\#P_0$ ([Result 5(a)](../ATTEMPTS.md), hypothesis-free algebra from $(W_+\pm W_-)^\#$), each PSD because $\#$ preserves the PSD cone; so $\mathop{\mathrm{tr}}X_{\mathrm{inv}}$ is a sum of three nonnegative traces and vanishes only if every block does. A PSD operator with a vanishing diagonal block vanishes on that block's whole row: $P_+C=0$, $P_0\lvert W\rvert^\#=0$, and then $P_0C=0$ since $4C=\lvert W\rvert^\#-W^\#$ and $W^\#=\theta W-W^2$ kills $\ker W$. Hence $C=P_-CP_-$, and [Theorem A](../ATTEMPTS.md)'s $X_{\mathrm{inv}}=4C-2\theta W_--2W_-^2$ at a critical point is supported in the $--$ block, which is zero. (ii) $h$ is symmetric and built equivariantly from $W$, so under an irreducible isotropy action over $\mathbb R$ each eigenspace of $h$ is an invariant subspace and $h$ is scalar; $\lambda=\mathop{\mathrm{tr}}h/n=2\mathop{\mathrm{tr}}\lvert W\rvert/n>0$ for $W\ne0$. $H=\mathrm{Ric}^*(h)$ is diagonal in $h$'s eigenframe with eigenvalues $\eta_i+\eta_j$ ([post F54](second-order.md)), here $2\lambda$, so $J_5=\langle X_{\mathrm{inv}},H\rangle=2\lambda\mathop{\mathrm{tr}}X_{\mathrm{inv}}=\lambda\mathop{\mathrm{scal}}(X_{\mathrm{inv}})$, and (i) finishes. $\square$

**Relation to [Lemma L7](#lemma-l7-a-bound-at-every-critical-point-and-the-graded-subalgebra-equality-class-canonical-statement-and-proof).** $\tfrac12\mathop{\mathrm{tr}}X_{\mathrm{inv}}$ is L7's slack ([r699](../reviews/REVIEW_r699.md)), so (i) upgrades L7's equality class from a trace statement to the operator statement $X_{\mathrm{inv}}=0$. *The record stated $X_{\mathrm{inv}}=0$ only at $\mathcal S^4(\alpha)$; it holds at every sphere product by [r697](../reviews/REVIEW_r697.md)/[r698](../reviews/REVIEW_r698.md)'s hand proof and at $\mathbb{HP}^2$ numerically, to $1.4\times10^{-15}$.*

#### Lemma L155, $X_{\mathrm{inv}}=0$ exactly on the $\mathbb Z_2$-graded subalgebras, with no criticality

*Derived 2026-09-05 in [DERIVE-j5](../reviews/DERIVE-j5.md), batch 3, and reached independently by [r699](../reviews/REVIEW_r699.md). Gate 2: [r701](../reviews/REVIEW_r701.md) and [r702](../reviews/REVIEW_r702.md), identical briefs, both CONFIRMED. **Stated here at every symmetric $W$**, which is [r701](../reviews/REVIEW_r701.md)'s strengthening, r702 proved the critical-point case by a second route using $P_0W^\#P_0=0$, so the two verdicts differ in reach rather than in content.*

> **LEMMA L155.** *Let $W\ne0$ be a symmetric operator on $\Lambda^2\mathbb R^n$, $\mathfrak p=\mathop{\mathrm{ran}}W_+$, $\mathfrak m=\mathop{\mathrm{ran}}W_-$, $\mathfrak k=\ker W$. Then $X_{\mathrm{inv}}=0$ if and only if $\mathfrak p\oplus\mathfrak m$ is a $\mathbb Z_2$-graded subalgebra of $\mathfrak{so}(n)$ with even part $\mathfrak p$, that is, $[\mathfrak p,\mathfrak p]\subseteq\mathfrak p$, $[\mathfrak p,\mathfrak m]\subseteq\mathfrak m$, $[\mathfrak m,\mathfrak m]\subseteq\mathfrak p$, making $(\mathfrak p\oplus\mathfrak m,\mathfrak p)$ a symmetric pair, proper in $\mathfrak{so}(n)$ whenever $\mathfrak k\ne0$. Consequently $J_5=0$ on that class, with no hypothesis on $h$.*

**Proof.** Each spectral-diagonal block of $X_{\mathrm{inv}}$ is a bracket Gram sum with **strictly positive** weights, the sums running over eigenbases of $\mathfrak p$ and $\mathfrak m$ alone: $\langle C\xi,\xi\rangle=c\sum a_\alpha b_\beta\langle[\varphi_\alpha,\psi_\beta],\xi\rangle^2$ and likewise for $W_\pm^\#$. A positive-weight sum of squares vanishes termwise, and bilinearity of the bracket carries the conclusion from one eigenbasis to the whole span, so degenerate eigenvalues do not bite. Reading the three blocks of [Result 5(a)](../ATTEMPTS.md) (themselves hypothesis-free algebra from $(W_+\pm W_-)^\#=W_+^\#+W_-^\#\pm2C$), gives $[\mathfrak p,\mathfrak m]\perp\mathfrak p$ on the $++$ block, $[\mathfrak p,\mathfrak p]\perp\mathfrak m$ and $[\mathfrak m,\mathfrak m]\perp\mathfrak m$ on the $--$ block, and $[\mathfrak p\oplus\mathfrak m,\mathfrak p\oplus\mathfrak m]\perp\mathfrak k$ on the $00$ block. Since $\mathfrak{so}(n)=\mathfrak p\oplus\mathfrak m\oplus\mathfrak k$, each bracket orthogonal to two summands lies in the third, and the four conditions are exactly the three inclusions plus closure. Conversely, under the inclusions $W_\pm^\#$ are supported on $\mathfrak p$ and $C$ on $\mathfrak m$, so $X_{\mathrm{inv}}=(W_+^\#+W_-^\#+2C)-(W_+^\#+W_-^\#)-2C=0$ by expansion. $\square$

**Two conditions, not three.** Antisymmetry of $\omega(x,y,z)=\langle[x,y],z\rangle$ makes $[\mathfrak p,\mathfrak m]\perp\mathfrak p$ and $[\mathfrak p,\mathfrak p]\perp\mathfrak m$ the same condition, so the $++$ block adds nothing the $--$ block does not already carry (*[r702](../reviews/REVIEW_r702.md)*).

**What is new against the record.** [ATTEMPTS.md](../ATTEMPTS.md)'s $S=T_1+2T_2+T_3$ already has the **trace-level** class, Gate 2 passed. New here: the **closure into $\ker W$**, which retires L7's "modulo $\ker W$" hedge, and the passage to the **operator level** via [L154](#lemma-l154-the-equality-locus-of-mathopmathrmscalx_mathrminvge0-is-x_mathrminv0-and-at-scalar-h-that-is-the-grants-equality)(i).

**Named points, by hand rather than by screen.** $W_{\mathbb{CP}^2}$ at $n=4$ **confirms** the lemma: $\mathfrak p\oplus\mathfrak m=\Lambda^2_+\cong\mathfrak{so}(3)$ is an ideal graded as $(\mathfrak{so}(3),\mathfrak{so}(2))$, nothing leaks into $\mathfrak k=\Lambda^2_-$, and $X_{\mathrm{inv}}=(8-6-2)\varepsilon^2P_-=0$ with $\theta=3\varepsilon=\sqrt{3/2}$. *Zero-padding preserves the grading, $X_{\mathrm{inv}}(W\oplus0)=X_{\mathrm{inv}}(W)\oplus0$, so $W_{\mathbb{CP}^2}\oplus0$ is graded at every $n$.* $SU(3)$ **fails exactly $[\mathfrak m,\mathfrak m]\subseteq\mathfrak p$**: $\Lambda^2\mathbf8=\mathbf8\oplus\mathbf{10}\oplus\overline{\mathbf{10}}$ makes $\mathfrak m$ a submodule, so the first two inclusions hold, while no involution of $\mathfrak{so}(8)$ has an $8$-dimensional fixed algebra, the dimensions are $21,16,13,13,12$.

**Screened** with [run_j5_grading_screen.py](../scripts/runs/run_j5_grading_screen.py), which builds its own objects: graded but **non-critical** $W$ on $\mathfrak{so}(p)\oplus\mathfrak{so}(q)$ gives $X_{\mathrm{inv}}=0$ to machine zero at four configurations with criticality residuals from $3.0$ to $76.1$ (the hypothesis-free half), against $\lVert X_{\mathrm{inv}}\rVert\approx0.6$–$0.7$ at random symmetric $W$.

#### What $\Delta$ IS, orbit-motion mass, and why that kills the second-order route through it

$g_\gamma=\lVert[\mathrm{ad}(v_\gamma),W]\rVert^2$ is the squared norm of the **orbit-tangent vector** in direction $v_\gamma$, so

$$\Delta(W)=\sum_{\gamma\in\mathfrak p}\lVert v_\gamma\!\cdot\!W\rVert^2$$

is the share of the orbit motion generated by the *positive* eigenvectors, out of a total $\sum_\gamma g_\gamma=4(n-1)$ that is constant on $\mathcal W_n$. That is the same content as the recorded "$\Delta=0\iff\mathfrak p\subseteq\mathfrak k(W)$", read as a mass rather than as a vanishing.

**And it closes the obvious route to the second-order condition, a MECHANISM, not the implication.** *Gate 2, Reviewer 298, which confirmed the mechanism, proved it more strongly, and refuted two of the sentences first written here.*

The real reason is one line further back and is unconditional: **$P$ is exactly constant along the orbit** ($\le4.4\times10^{-16}$ over random $g\in SO(n)$), so the orbit lies inside the level set and every derivative of $P$ along orbit directions, at every order, is the derivative of a constant. Consequently $\mathcal O\subseteq E_{L_e}(\tfrac12)$, and since $L_e$ is self-adjoint the complement is invariant and the orbit part of any test direction splits off exactly:

$$v=o+x,\ o\in\mathcal O,\ x\perp\mathcal O\ \Longrightarrow\ \langle v,(L_e-\tfrac12)v\rangle=\langle x,(L_e-\tfrac12)x\rangle$$

to $\le3.3\times10^{-16}$, cross term $\le1.2\times10^{-16}$. **So a violating direction can never draw any part of its violation from the orbit, not even as a mixed term.**

**The route is NOT closed; only one mechanism inside it is.** *Gate 2, Reviewers 297 and 298 on identical briefs, they agree on every number and differ on what "the route" meant, and the reconciled statement is sharper than either.*

**The premise was misread here, and that is a logical error rather than an overreach.** [Lemma L24](second-order.md) says *if $W$ is a maximizer then $\lambda_{\max}\le\tfrac12$*. This record used the conclusion as a fact about **critical points**, under which no second-order test could ever fail, contradicting [Observation L25](second-order.md), which lives in that file rather than this one. And *"the orbit cannot supply a violation"* is not *"nothing can"*: between them lies the orthogonal complement, which is where the violation actually lives. At the $C_5$ blow-up the top eigendirection has $\lambda_{\max}=1.206011330$ and an orbit component of $1.1\times10^{-15}$ (**zero**), with $P$ genuinely rising along it, $(P-\theta)/t^2\to1.420820=3\theta(\lambda-\tfrac12)$.

**So the implication $\Delta>0\Rightarrow$ not a local maximum is ALIVE and strongly supported**: $35$ independent $\Delta>0$ critical classes at $n=6,8,10$ plus the $C_5$ blow-up, every one violating, $\lambda_{\max}$ from $1.067$ to $96.292$ (the last reproducing Observation L25's recorded ceiling from code that never read it) and one of them **on the live target set** ($n=8$, $S_{\mathfrak{mmm}}=0$ exactly, $\Delta=11.0449$, $\lambda_{\max}=1.380653$). Rescues checked and absent: Krasnov–Tkachev's "$1$ simple" is unviolated everywhere, and the $\mathop{\mathrm{tr}}L_e^2$ budget has a factor of two of slack at $n=8,10,12$.

#### The same identity in Hilbert–Schmidt form, derived from the adjoint orbit

**A second derivation gives the correction terms as norms rather than as a weighted sum, and it needs no eigenbasis.** Expanding $W_t=\mathrm{Ad}(\exp tv)W=W+t[A,W]+\tfrac{t^2}2[A,[A,W]]+\cdots$ with $A=\mathrm{ad}(v)$, the second-order coefficient of $\langle W,W_t\rangle$ is $\langle W,[A,[A,W]]\rangle=2\mathop{\mathrm{tr}}(A^2W^2)-2\mathop{\mathrm{tr}}(WAWA)=-\lVert[A,W]\rVert^2$, since $[A,W]$ is *symmetric* ($A$ antisymmetric, $W$ symmetric). Solving for $\mathop{\mathrm{tr}}(AWAW)$ and substituting into $\langle v,W^\#v\rangle=-\tfrac12\mathop{\mathrm{tr}}(AWAW)$,

$$\langle v,W^\#v\rangle=-\tfrac14\big\lVert[\mathrm{ad}(v),W]\big\rVert^2-\tfrac12\mathop{\mathrm{tr}}\!\big((\mathrm{ad}\,v)^2W^2\big),$$

**an identity on all of $\mathcal W_n$**, verified to $3\times10^{-16}$ at every catalogued point *and at non-critical $W$*, criticality never being used. Both terms are signed: $(\mathrm{ad}\,v)^2\preceq0$ and $W^2\succeq0$, so the second is $\ge0$ and equals $\tfrac12\lVert\mathrm{ad}(v)W\rVert^2$.

**At a critical point, for every unit $v\in E_{\min}$, this reads**

$$\boxed{\;\theta\;=\;\lambda_{\min}+\frac{g_v}{4\lvert\lambda_{\min}\rvert}-\frac{\lVert\mathrm{ad}(v)W\rVert^2}{2\lvert\lambda_{\min}\rvert}\;},\qquad g_v=\big\lVert[\mathrm{ad}(v),W]\big\rVert^2=\sum_{\alpha\beta}(\lambda_\alpha-\lambda_\beta)^2T_{v\alpha\beta},$$

**exact to $2\times10^{-15}$ at every critical point tested.** *An earlier sentence here read the $0.46$ failure at non-critical $W$ as a control showing this and the line above it are different statements; that is a category error and is withdrawn, this form contains $\theta$ and the one above does not, so only this one can fail off criticality, and both share the same criticality-free core.* [Reviewer 324](../reviews/REVIEW_r324.md). $g_v$ is [Petersen–Wink](../references/petersen-wink-2019-bochner-technique/notes.md) Prop 1.7's quantity, the one the record already sums to $4(n-1)$ and $\lambda$-weights to $-4\theta$; at the balanced products $g_v=16(n-1)/n^2$ exactly ($2.2\overline2,\,1.75,\,1.44,\,1.2\overline2$ at $n=6,8,10,12$).

> **THIS FORM CARRIES NO NEW INFORMATION, AND CLAIMING OTHERWISE HERE WAS WRONG.** *Both Gate 2 reviewers, independently, in their first phase.* It is the eigenbasis identity under the change of variables $2ab=a^2+b^2-(a-b)^2$: in the eigenbasis $g_v=\sum c_{vab}^2(\lambda_a-\lambda_b)^2$ and $\lVert\mathrm{ad}(v)W\rVert^2=\tfrac12\sum c_{vab}^2(\lambda_a^2+\lambda_b^2)$, so $-\tfrac14g_v+\tfrac12\lVert\mathrm{ad}(v)W\rVert^2=\tfrac12\sum c_{vab}^2\lambda_a\lambda_b$ identically. **Same data, re-partitioned.** What it buys is presentational (the two correction terms are signed Hilbert–Schmidt norms of explicit operators) and a route may not treat it as an extra constraint.

#### Lemma L111, criticality in bracket coordinates is $M_{kl}=2\lambda_k(\lambda_k-\theta)\delta_{kl}$, and the bracket form is strictly weaker

*Audited in [ROUND_L111](../verification/ROUND_L111.md): `PROVEN, hypotheses corrected`.*

**Statement.** Let $W\in\mathcal W_n$, $n\ge4$, with an orthonormal eigenbasis $\{\varphi_k\}$ of $W$ on $\Lambda^2$, $W\varphi_k=\lambda_k\varphi_k$, structure constants $c_{k\alpha\beta}$ in that basis, and $M_{kl}=\mathop{\mathrm{tr}}\big(\mathrm{ad}(\varphi_k)W\,\mathrm{ad}(\varphi_l)W\big)$.

1. $M_{kl}=-2(W^\#)_{kl}$ for every symmetric $W$ on $\Lambda^2$, with $\#$ in the halved convention of [conventions.md](conventions.md). Hence $Q(W)=\theta W$ if and only if $M_{kl}=2\lambda_k(\lambda_k-\theta)\,\delta_{kl}$ for all $k,l$, and the diagonal entries are relation (C).
2. The bracket form, $\sum_{\alpha\beta}\lambda_\alpha\lambda_\beta\,c_{k\alpha\beta}c_{l\alpha\beta}=0$ whenever $\lambda_k\ne\lambda_l$, is $[W,Q(W)]=0$. Criticality implies it, and it is strictly weaker.

**Proof.** Polarising $\langle v,W^\#v\rangle=-\tfrac12\mathop{\mathrm{tr}}(\mathrm{ad}(v)W\mathrm{ad}(v)W)$ gives $M_{kl}=-2(W^\#)_{kl}$, using only total antisymmetry of $c$. Substituting $W^\#=\theta W-W^2$ gives the matrix form, and conversely the matrix form reads $W^\#=\theta W-W^2$ in the eigenbasis, which is $Q(W)=\theta W$, the $\delta_{kl}$ being part of the conclusion. In the eigenbasis $(W^\#)_{kl}=\tfrac12\sum_{\alpha\beta}\lambda_\alpha\lambda_\beta c_{k\alpha\beta}c_{l\alpha\beta}$, so the bracket form says $W^\#$ has no entries across distinct eigenvalues, which is $[W,W^\#]=0$ and so $[W,Q(W)]=0$. Criticality gives it, since the matrix form vanishes at every $k\ne l$. For strictness, take the coordinate-diagonal $W\in\mathcal W_4$ with $w_{12}=w_{34}=1$, $w_{13}=w_{24}=-1$ and $w_{14}=w_{23}=0$, padded by zeros to $\mathcal W_n$. Its row sums vanish, so it is Ricci-flat, and [problem.md](problem.md)'s diagonal formula $Q(W)_{ij,ij}=w_{ij}^2+\sum_{k\ne i,j}w_{ik}w_{jk}$ makes $Q(W)$ diagonal, with entries $1,1,1,1,-2,-2$ on $e_{12},e_{34},e_{13},e_{24},e_{14},e_{23}$ against $W$'s $1,1,-1,-1,0,0$. So $Q(W)$ commutes with $W$, and no $\theta$ gives $Q(W)=\theta W$. $\square$

**Scope.** *Every coordinate-diagonal Weyl tensor satisfies the bracket form, because $Q$ preserves coordinate-diagonality ([problem.md](problem.md)), and that family has dimension $n(n-3)/2$ ([second-order.md](second-order.md)). A generic member is not critical, while the balanced products $S^2\times S^2$ and $S^3\times S^3$ are members that are.* The matrix form is $W^\#=\theta W-W^2$ written out in bracket coordinates, a change of language rather than new information. Its $N$ diagonal defects $d_k=M_{kk}-2\lambda_k(\lambda_k-\theta)=-2\big(Q(W)-\theta W\big)_{kk}$ satisfy $\sum_kd_k=0$ and $\sum_k\lambda_kd_k=2\big(\theta-P(W)\big)$ on every unit Weyl tensor, so at $\theta=P(W)$ they are at most $N-2$ independent constraints. The off-diagonal entries are not functions of $T=c^2$, since they change sign under $\varphi_l\mapsto-\varphi_l$.

#### Lemma L71, saturation forces the balanced sphere product, with no two-eigenvalue hypothesis

$$[\mathfrak m,\mathfrak m]\subseteq\mathfrak h,\qquad[\mathfrak m,\mathfrak h]\subseteq\mathfrak m,\qquad[\mathfrak m,\mathfrak r]=0 .$$

*Machine-checked at the frame vectors of $E_{\min}$ in the eigenframe of a saturating critical point: `L71_mm`, `L71_mh`, `L71_mr` in [L71Incl.lean](../lean/HamiltonsCubic/Results/L71Incl.lean); the subspace inclusions follow by frame completeness and bilinearity.*

Jacobi also gives $[\mathfrak r,[\mathfrak m,\mathfrak m]]\subseteq[[\mathfrak r,\mathfrak m],\mathfrak m]+[\mathfrak m,[\mathfrak r,\mathfrak m]]=0$, so **$\mathfrak r$ centralises $\mathfrak g'$ entirely.**

#### Lemma L169, the grading and tracelessness determine the saturating operator

*Gate 2, [r749](../reviews/REVIEW_r749.md) and [r750](../reviews/REVIEW_r750.md), on identical briefs, **both `PROVEN, hypotheses corrected`** and agreeing on every finding; each derived $\theta_0(n)$ at the balanced member cold rather than importing it, by a triangle census and by the sharp identities respectively.*

> **THE CASIMIR FAMILY'S CLOSED FORM IS NOT AN IDENTITY IN $(r,N)$, WHICH IS WHY THIS BLOCK NO LONGER CITES IT.** *$\theta=\tfrac2n\sqrt{rN/(N-r)}$ fails at $\mathfrak{so}(n-1)$, giving $\tfrac8{15}$ against the formula's $\tfrac{10}3$ at $n=6$, and again at $\mathfrak u(m)$ and at unbalanced $\mathfrak{so}(p)\oplus\mathfrak{so}(q)$.* **None of those three is in $\mathcal W_n$**, so the formula stands in its intended scope, but reading a value off it is a route through criticality, and the sharp identities reach $\theta_0(n)$ without one.

> **WHAT WOULD COLLAPSE THE ARGUMENT, RECORDED AS A QUESTION.** *Weakening "$E_{\max}$ conjugate to $\mathfrak h$" to "$\dim E_{\max}=r$" leaves the plane uncut, and **first Bianchi (the hypothesis this proof never spends), is the only thing that could rule the extra operators out**.* It does so by hand at $n=4$; **at $n\ge6$ it is open.**

**Statement.** Let $n$ be even, $W\in\mathcal W_n$ with $\lVert W\rVert=1$, exactly two eigenvalues $\lambda_{\max}>0>\lambda_{\min}$ as an operator on $\Lambda^2\mathbb R^n$, and $E_{\max}$ $SO(n)$-conjugate to $\mathfrak h=\mathfrak{so}(n/2)\oplus\mathfrak{so}(n/2)$. **Then $W$ is $SO(n)$-conjugate to the Weyl tensor of $S^{n/2}\times S^{n/2}$ at unit norm**, and $\theta=\theta_0(n)$.

**Neither criticality nor saturation is used, and neither is first Bianchi.** *[L71](#lemma-l71-saturation-forces-the-balanced-sphere-product-with-no-two-eigenvalue-hypothesis) supplies both hypotheses of this lemma and nothing else it assumes is consumed here*, so the identification is a statement about $\mathcal W_n$ rather than about the critical set, **and in fact about symmetric traceless operators on $\Lambda^2$, a strictly larger class**. *Ricci-flatness is spent only at the strength of scalar-flatness, $\mathop{\mathrm{tr}}_{\Lambda^2}W=\tfrac12\mathop{\mathrm{scal}}$, an implication rather than an equivalence; Bianchi and $\mathrm{Ric}=0$ come back out as consequences.* **And $\lambda_{\max}>0>\lambda_{\min}$ is redundant**, forced by tracelessness, unit norm and a two-element spectrum.

**Proof.** Conjugate so that $E_{\max}=\mathfrak h$ exactly. Two eigenvalues and $E_{\min}=E_{\max}^\perp$ give

$$W=\lambda_{\max}\Pi_{\mathfrak h}+\lambda_{\min}(\mathrm{Id}-\Pi_{\mathfrak h}),$$

so $W$ lies on the two-parameter plane spanned by $\Pi_{\mathfrak h}$ and $\mathrm{Id}$. **Ricci-flatness makes $W$ traceless on $\Lambda^2$**, and with $r=\dim\mathfrak h=\tfrac{n(n-2)}4$ and $N=\binom n2$, so that $N-r=\tfrac{n^2}4$, that reads

$$\lambda_{\max}\,r+\lambda_{\min}(N-r)=0\iff\frac{\lvert\lambda_{\min}\rvert}{\lambda_{\max}}=\frac r{N-r}=\frac{n-2}{n}\iff(n-2)\lambda_{\max}=n\lvert\lambda_{\min}\rvert .$$

*So the ratio [L71](#lemma-l71-saturation-forces-the-balanced-sphere-product-with-no-two-eigenvalue-hypothesis) records is not an extra constraint once the grading is fixed, it and tracelessness are the same equation, read in the two directions.* **The equivalence is confined to the two-eigenvalue stratum with $\dim E_{\max}=r$ and is false lifted out of it.** This puts $W$ on the **line** $\mathbb R\big(\Pi_{\mathfrak h}-\tfrac rN\mathrm{Id}\big)$; $\lVert W\rVert=1$ fixes the scale up to sign and $\lambda_{\max}>0$ fixes the sign, so $W$ is the unique unit positive multiple.

**And the sphere product is that multiple.** The balanced member of [L4](../findings/ungraded-slack.md)'s Casimir family is the unit positive multiple of $\Pi_{\mathfrak h}-\tfrac rN\mathrm{Id}$ at $r=\tfrac{n(n-2)}4$, with $E_{\max}=\mathfrak h$ since $1-\tfrac rN>0>-\tfrac rN$, and it is the balanced sphere product's Weyl tensor. Two unit positive multiples of one operator coincide, so $W$ is it; conjugating back gives the statement. Its $\theta$ is then read off the sharp identities rather than off the Casimir family's closed form. With $m=n/2$, $\mathrm{Id}^\#=(n-2)\mathrm{Id}$, $\Pi_{\mathfrak h}^\#=(m-2)\Pi_{\mathfrak h}$ and $\Pi_{\mathfrak h}\#\mathrm{Id}=(m-1)\mathrm{Id}-\Pi_{\mathfrak h}$ give

$$\theta^2=\frac{(m-1)(2m-1)}{m^2}=\frac{2(n-1)(n-2)}{n^2}=\theta_0(n)^2,$$

**with no criticality, no saturation and no Bianchi anywhere in it**. *The $\#$-normalisation is pinned rather than chosen here: $\#\mapsto c\,\#$ matches $\theta_0$ at every $m$ only at $c=1$.* $\square$
