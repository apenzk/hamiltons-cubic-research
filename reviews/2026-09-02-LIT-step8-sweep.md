## Outcomes by batch

### Batch 1, new-route searches: ODE fixed points, equivariant bifurcation, sharp cubic constants

- **Fixed points of $\dot R=R^2+R^\#$.** [Bhattacharya, arXiv:1310.4618](https://arxiv.org/abs/1310.4618), *On the curvature ODE associated to the Ricci flow*: the zeros up to normalisation studied are the curvature operators of $M\times\mathbb R^k$ with $M$ Einstein locally symmetric of compact type, and the ODE is **unstable** near every one of them except the spherical space forms at $k=0$. Read at abstract level only. *Contact with the record*: the linearisation of $Q$ at a symmetric-space fixed point is the record's $L_e$; instability of the ODE is a statement about the *unprojected* linearisation on $\mathrm{Sym}^2_B$, where the scalar direction dominates, so it is weaker than Lemma L24's $L_e\le\tfrac12$ on the Weyl sphere. **No classification of fixed points beyond symmetric spaces exists in print** (the search returned none) which is consistent with the record's finding that R15's SRG family supplies non-symmetric two-eigenvalue critical points. Outcome: **searched, nothing that adds to the record**; the paper is not filed.
- **Equivariant bifurcation for I14's branching clause.** The equivariant branching lemma and finite determinacy (cubic truncation suffices for absolutely irreducible representations of compact groups) are the standard tools. **They answer I14's clause in the direction the record does not need**: they *produce* branches at every axial isotropy subgroup once a Hessian block crosses the critical value, whereas I14 needs that a non-diagonal critical point *exists only if* a block equals $\tfrac12$ exactly, and that direction is the implicit function theorem, which the record already has. Outcome: **searched, standard theory only; I14's clause is an IFT statement and needs no import.**
- **Sharp constants on the cubic term.** [Ge 2026](../references/ge-2026-sharp-weitzenbock-pic2/notes.md) is **already on file**; its estimate $q_2(E)\ge-\tfrac{2(\ell-1)}{3\ell}\mathop{\mathrm{Scal}}(E)$ holds under nonnegative sectional curvature, a cone no nonzero Weyl tensor enters. Böhm–Wilking's $l_{a,b}$ pinching family is on file. Nothing new.

### Batch 2, the Kronecker-square reading, the Peirce second moment, gradedness

- **Kronecker-square algebra of $\mathfrak{so}(n)$** ($\beta((a\otimes b)\otimes(c\otimes d))=[a,c]\otimes[b,d]$, so $R^\#=\tfrac12\beta(R\otimes R)$). No literature treats this product as an algebra; the nearest objects in print are Ellis's non-abelian tensor product of Lie algebras and the quadratic-Lie-algebra literature, neither of which is it. **What the reading is, in the record's own terms**: $R^\#=\tfrac12(\mathrm{ad}\otimes\mathrm{ad})(R)\cdot R$, the element $R\in\mathfrak g\otimes\mathfrak g$ acting on $R$ through $\mathrm{ad}\otimes\mathrm{ad}$, which is [Wilking's](../references/wilking-lie-algebraic-cones/notes.md) $\langle R^\#x,y\rangle=-\tfrac12\mathop{\mathrm{tr}}(\mathrm{ad}_xR\,\mathrm{ad}_yR)$ written as an operator. At $R=\mathrm{Id}$ it is the split Casimir, which is relation (A). **Outcome: the lead is Wilking's form, already on file; closed by search.**
- **The Peirce second moment at an idempotent** (the Fox exact / Killing-metrised identity $\theta^2=\kappa_n/(1+\sum\lambda_i^2)$). The Peirce-spectrum literature located, [Krasnov–Tkachev 2018](../references/krasnov-tkachev-2018-idempotent-variety/), [Krasnov–Tkachev 2022](../references/krasnov-tkachev-2022-medial-isospectral/), [Tkachev 2019](../references/tkachev-2019-spectral-nonassociative/), is **all on file**, and it is where Lemma L24's $\lambda\le\tfrac12$ at an extremal idempotent comes from. Nothing in it constrains the *second* moment beyond the trace identity, so the lead still rests on L24 plus $\dim E(\tfrac12)$ (Lemma L127) plus multiplicities. **Outcome: searched, no new constraint in print; the lead is a derivation question and is carried to phase 3 as such.**
- **(G) gradedness.** Lie triple systems and $\mathbb Z_2$-graded triples appear only as the symmetric-space structure itself; no source states a necessary condition of the form *a maximiser of an invariant cubic on an irreducible module is a grading*. [Michel's theorem](../references/) territory (critical orbits of invariant functions at maximal isotropy) is on record and does not give gradedness either. **Outcome: searched, nothing found.**

### Batch 5, the certificate node, the frame-diagonal characterisation, the residual bound

- **Symmetry-adapted SOS (Gatermann–Parrilo, [arXiv:math/0211450](https://arxiv.org/abs/math/0211450)).** The isotypic block-diagonalisation of an invariant SOS programme is the tool R1 would have used; it reduces size, and it leaves the endgame a machine certificate. The record does not cite it, and nothing in it makes a certificate readable or dimension-independent. **Outcome: searched; it belongs to R1's closed world and is not filed.**
- **Frame-diagonal curvature operators.** The literature's name for the record's diagonal locus is **pure curvature**, an orthonormal frame whose bivectors diagonalise $R$, equivalently $R_{ijkl}=0$ whenever $\{i,j\}\ne\{k,l\}$, with $\mathbb{CP}^2$ the standard non-pure example (its Kähler form is an indecomposable eigenvector). No characterisation of purity beyond the definition exists in print for $n\ge5$; the term is worth adopting in the record so a reader can find the locus. ## Phase 3 addendum, an independent derivation, and what it confirmed

## Phase 3, second derivation batch, the lifted chain's open grant, rederived and reduced

**Instruction being served:** "u must get us closer to a solution." This batch attacks the one recorded object whose proof settles the conjecture outright, [Conjecture L16](../findings/problem.md), through its single analytic grant, $J_5\ge0$ ([ATTEMPTS.md, Results 1–5](../ATTEMPTS.md)). Analysis only; every step below is hand-derived and checked by hand at $S^m\times S^m$; nothing was computed.

**Setup.** $W$ unit, Weyl, critical: $W^2+W^\#=\theta W$. $W=W_+-W_-$, $W_\pm\succeq0$, $W_+W_-=0$, $|W|=W_++W_-$, $C=W_+\#W_-\succeq0$. Ricci-flatness gives $\mathrm{Ric}(W_+)=\mathrm{Ric}(W_-)=:\rho\succeq0$ and $h:=\mathrm{Ric}(|W|)=2\rho$. Result 2's Ricci-level identity, for every symmetric $R$ on $\Lambda^2$: $\mathrm{Ric}(R^2+R^\#)=R(\mathrm{Ric}R)+D(R)$, $R(\sigma)_{ij}=\sum_{kl}R_{kilj}\sigma_{kl}$, $D(R)\succeq0$ a Gram form in $\mathrm{Alt}(R)$.

**Step 1, $X_{\mathrm{inv}}$ (rederived).** $|W|^\#=W^\#+4C$ and $W^\#=\theta W-W^2$ give $X_{\mathrm{inv}}=|W|^\#+(W-\theta)|W|=4C-2\theta W_--2W_-^2$. Agrees with Theorem A.

**Step 2, $\mathrm{Ric}(C)$ in closed form.** Apply Result 2 to $R=|W|$: $\mathrm{Ric}(|W|^2+|W|^\#)=|W|(h)+D(|W|)$. Since $|W|^2=W^2$ and $|W|^\#=W^\#+4C$, and $\mathrm{Ric}(W^2+W^\#)=\theta\,\mathrm{Ric}(W)=0$: $$4\,\mathrm{Ric}(C)=2|W|(\rho)+D(|W|).$$

**Step 3, $J_5$ without the $\#$.** $J_5=\langle\mathrm{Ric}(X_{\mathrm{inv}}),h\rangle$ becomes $$J_5=4\langle|W|(\rho),\rho\rangle+J_4-4\theta\|\rho\|^2-4\langle\mathrm{Ric}(W_-^2),\rho\rangle,\qquad J_4=\langle D(|W|),h\rangle .$$ So $J_4-J_5=-4K$ with $K:=\langle|W|(\rho),\rho\rangle-\theta\|\rho\|^2-\langle\mathrm{Ric}(W_-^2),\rho\rangle$, and the negative-block criticality $\theta W_-=-W_-^2-P_-W^\#P_-$ gives the equivalent form $K=\langle|W|(\rho),\rho\rangle+\langle\mathrm{Ric}(P_-W^\#P_-),\rho\rangle$.

**Hand check at $S^m\times S^m$.** $\lambda_c=-\tfrac{m-1}m\lambda_w$, $(m-1)(2m-1)\lambda_w^2=1$, $\rho=(m-1)\lambda_w\,\mathrm{id}$. Then $K\propto(m-1)\lambda_w\big[\tfrac{(2m-1)(m-1)}m\lambda_w-\theta\big]$ and $\tfrac{(2m-1)(m-1)}m\lambda_w=\sqrt{(m-1)(2m-1)}/m=\theta_0(2m)$, so $K=0$: $J_4=J_5=0$ there, as the record states.

**What this is against the record.** Writing $\langle|W|(h),h\rangle=\langle|W|,h\wedge h\rangle$ and using Jäger's $e$-decomposition $|W|=\sqrt{1-e^2}\,h\wedge h/\|h\wedge h\|+E$ turns $4\langle|W|(\rho),\rho\rangle$ into $\sqrt2\,r^2\sqrt{1-\mathop{\mathrm{tr}}S^4}\sqrt{1-e^2}$, so Step 3 **is Result 1**, the lifted master identity, now derived by hand from Result 2 and criticality alone. Independent confirmation of Results 1, 2 (as used) and Theorem A; no new identity. The record's split of $J_4-J_5$ into "bound $J_4$ by $24\delta^2\lambda_{\max}(h)$, grant $J_5\ge0$" is one lossy step on a single quantity $K$ that the chain then needs sharp to $0.011$; the operator strengthening that would give $J_5\ge0$ outright is exactly the refuted $\mathrm{Ric}(X_{\mathrm{inv}})\succeq0$ ($=2|W|(\rho)+D(|W|)-2\theta\rho-2\mathrm{Ric}(W_-^2)$), so the $\rho$-weighted scalar is the right level, as Result 5 says.

**The one structural fact this batch surfaces.** In an eigenframe $F$ of $\rho$, with $M$ the sectional matrix of $|W|$ in $F$: $\rho=\tfrac12M\mathbf1$, $\|\rho\|^2=\tfrac14\mathbf1^\top M^2\mathbf1$, $\langle|W|(\rho),\rho\rangle=\tfrac14\mathbf1^\top M^3\mathbf1$. So the open grant is a statement about the first three moments $\mathbf1^\top M^k\mathbf1$ of one hollow nonnegative matrix, plus the $W_-^2$ and $D$ terms, the same objects I11's frame identity is written in ($\mu_F,\Sigma_F,\varepsilon_F$). **I11 and the $J_5$ grant are the same frame calculus read at two rows; I11 is a live plan item, the $J_5$ grant is in no plan item and no route** (`grep J_5 plan.md routes.md` is empty; [results.md:67](../results.md) carries it as "the closing inequality's open ingredient").

**Verdict of the batch.** No new inequality. The identity axis of the lifted chain is exhausted at the same place the eigenframe axis was in the previous batch. What is new is a planning fact: the only recorded chain that closes the conjecture outright has fallen out of the plan and the route register, and its open grant shares its objects with I11.
