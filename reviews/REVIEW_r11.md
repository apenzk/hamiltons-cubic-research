### Reviewer 11: the graded enumeration, the ungraded measurement, and the slack identity

**Method.** Everything under review rebuilt from [findings/INDEX.md](../findings/INDEX.md)'s definitions in one fresh file, [scripts/review_r11a_independent.py](scripts/misc/review_r11a_independent.py), importing nothing from the pipeline under review (the `review_r11_*`, `gap_push_*` and `gap_attack*` scripts were read as text; the two saved `.npy` witnesses were loaded as data): own pair-basis/4-tensor dictionary, own $R^\#$, own Ricci/Alt/Weyl projections, own constructions of every competitor. Validated before use against the standard anchors.

**A methodological point governing several verdicts.** First-order searches (L-BFGS on $\|Q\|^2-\theta^2$) stall at criticality residual $10^{-8}$–$10^{-9}$, which is ambiguous between a genuine critical point and a positive local minimum of the residual functional. A **Levenberg–Marquardt polish of the residual vector** settles it: candidates either drop to $\sim10^{-15}$ or stall. This distinction drives the corrections below, and it is the screen the repo should have been using.

**1. The slack-identity chain, CONFIRMED as genuine, non-circular theorems.** Steps 1–4 and 7, and the supporting lemmas (A) $\mathop{\mathrm{tr}}(W_+^2)+\mathop{\mathrm{tr}}(W_+^\#)=\mathop{\mathrm{tr}}(W_-^2)+\mathop{\mathrm{tr}}(W_-^\#)$ and (B) $\mathop{\mathrm{tr}}(W_-^2)+\mathop{\mathrm{tr}}(W_-^\#)=\mathop{\mathrm{tr}}(W_+\#W_-)$, were re-derived by hand and hold at $10^{-15}$ at all 18 structured points, **including where F1/F2 fail**, which is what shows they are not circular.

**2. F1 and F2 as consequences of criticality, REFUTED.** A 60-seed hunt at $n=6$ produced **22 genuine critical points** (LM-polished to residual $\sim3\times10^{-16}$, clean spectra) violating both conditions at order one ($F_1$ up to $0.62$). So does the pipeline's own catalogued $\delta^2=3/25$ violator (residual $8\times10^{-16}$, $F_1=0.360$, $F_2$ residual $0.240$, $S/G_1$ ratio $11.5$), **the tenth row of FINDINGS' own table breaks the pattern its other nine rows established**. The "nine of nine" sample was biased by the spectral screen that excluded it. $S=\tfrac12G_1$ is therefore **conditional on F1/F2**, not universal.

**3. The equivalences F1 $\iff[E_+,E_+]\perp E_-$ and F2 $\iff[E_+,E_-]\subseteq E_-$, CONFIRMED**, in both directions.

**4. The framing "two of the three grading conditions are automatic", REFUTED as stated.** At generic critical points the slack carries F1/F2 mass too: there exist points with $[E_-,E_-]$-into-$E_-$ mass $0.024$ yet $S=1.73$ against $G_1/2=0.017$. So $S$ measures strictly more than the third condition off the structured class. **The (GC) inequality $S\ge0$ itself is untouched.**

**5. The monotone refutation and the wide gap, CONFIRMED**, every row of the measured-population table reproduced digit for digit from independent constructions.

**6. The classification trap, mechanism CONFIRMED, but the screen built on it is INVALID.** Perturbing a clean graded point by $\varepsilon=10^{-3}$ does make the sign-split bracket test report all three conditions violated at once ($0.29/0.41/0.25$, the diagnosed signature) while $S$ correctly reads $\approx0$. But **the condemnation of the $\theta=0.900828$ witness was wrong**: it LM-polishes from residual $4.2\times10^{-8}$ to $1.0\times10^{-15}$ with $\theta$ *and* the $-1.010\times10^{-3}$ eigenvalue quadruple unchanged. Those eigenvalues are real structure, not an uncollapsed kernel. The witness is genuine, ungraded and F1-violating.

**7. The ceiling, the retracted value stands, and holds at $n=12$ too.** A fresh $\theta$-pinned sweep at $n=8$ (10 targets $\times$ 25 seeds, every acceptance LM-polished to $\le10^{-13}$) converged 171 times to 22 unique critical points: 13 graded reaching $1.224745$, and five genuine ungraded points from $0.854425$ up to **$0.900828016$**, re-found independently from random seeds. Padding makes the witness critical at $n=12$ (residual $10^{-15}$), so "nothing ungraded above $0.9$ at $n=12$" is false. **Gap to $\sqrt{3/2}$ is $\approx0.324$ and survives; the ceiling remains observational.**

**8. The "empty stretch" $0.92$–$1.05$, REFUTED.** A genuine **graded** point sits at $\theta=0.979796=2\sqrt6/5$ (residual $1.2\times10^{-15}$), with further graded points at $1.0647$–$1.0988$ beyond the named catalogue. The stretch is populated; the earlier search simply failed to converge there.

**9. The graded enumeration, CONFIRMED at its stated scope.** The $\theta$ formula, the structure-sum identity (exactly $336$ at $\mathbb{HP}^2$), the Casimir dictionary, both (R1) rejections (exhaustive family scans: minimum residual $0.34$–$0.52$ against $2.4\times10^{-5}$ for the $\mathbb{HP}^2$ control), the $S^3\times S^5$/$\mathbb{HP}^2$ isospectral pair (12 digits, $\delta^2$ $0$ versus $2/63$), and the $\delta^2=0$ decomposability proof all reproduce from own brackets. Four corrections: strike the stale $(\mathfrak{su}(2m),\mathfrak{sp}(m))$ paragraph (contradicts Step 10, ansatz residual $0.34$); the spin-dimension count needs the restriction $j,N-j\ge3$, the full solution set is $(4,2),(8,1),(8,3),(8,5),(8,7)$; state (R1)'s Casimir convention; and phrase Step 10's rejections as "no **isotropy-scalar** graded critical point".
