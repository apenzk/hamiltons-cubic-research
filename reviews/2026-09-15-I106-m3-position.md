## The target

**I106, the $\lvert J\rvert=3$ step of [R27](../routes.md), at $m=n-3\ge3$ outer indices.** *Theorem sought: every unit $W\in\mathcal S_J\cap\mathcal W_n$, $\lvert J\rvert=3$, has $P(W)\le\Theta_n$, attained at commuting blocks.* R27 climbs $\lvert J\rvert$, and the conjecture's range is $n\ge12$, so the case that matters is $m\ge9$. Work $m=3$ first only if it exposes the mechanism.

**What each outcome means.**
- A proof closes the $\lvert J\rvert=3$ node of R27, and the question becomes whether the same mechanism climbs to $\lvert J\rvert=4$.
- A unit $W$ with non-commuting blocks and $P(W)>\Theta_n$ is a counterexample to the node, and R27 as stated is dead at $\lvert J\rvert=3$. Record it as a fence post with the witness.
- **A reduction to separately bounded terms is [F67](../findings/fence-posts.md)'s shape and abandons R27**: bounding the block cubics by [L225](../findings/second-order.md) and the couplings by their own suprema, independently, never beats $\Theta_n$.
- A genuine ordering conflict between alignments, with no counterexample, is a new fence post naming the two alignments.

## Where to look first, ranked

1. **Klein-four split with L219 at three reflections.** At a maximiser, each $\varepsilon_i\varepsilon_j$-odd part $O_{ij}$ is a $\tfrac\theta2$-eigenvector of the even part's $L$, three eigen-equations coupling $D$ and the $O$'s. The question is whether those equations with the value identity force $t\le$ what a commuting configuration of the same norm achieves. *Risk: summing three L224 bounds and bounding $t$ separately is F67's shape.*
2. **Fluctuation form with a common rotation.** The only frame invariant beyond pairings is $\sum_a\operatorname{tr}Y_a^3$ under $\sum Y_a=0$. Ask whether, at fixed norms, the maximum of $(3+\tfrac3m)\operatorname{tr}(XT)+\sum_a\operatorname{tr}Y_a^3+\sum\beta_{ab}G_{ab}$ over relative frames is attained at simultaneously diagonal $X,Y_a$. *The $m=2$ case is L227, where $\sum\operatorname{tr}Y_a^3=0$.*
3. **The criticality system.** Whether $[M_a,H']$ with one shared $H'$ forces commuting blocks at a maximiser, using second-order information (L24's cap) if first-order is not enough.
