## 1. Batch 3

- **$u\in D^\perp$.** For $x\in D$, $\langle\hat A\circ\nu,x\rangle=\langle\hat A\circ x,\nu\rangle=0$ since $D\circ D\subseteq D$. $u\perp\nu$ by the choice of $r$, so $u\perp\hat A,\nu$ and hence $u\perp W,w_c$. Correct.
- **The $D^\perp$ component.** $W\circ W=a^2\hat A\circ\hat A+2ab\,\hat A\circ\nu+b^2\nu\circ\nu$, with $\hat A\circ\hat A\in D$ and $\hat A\circ\nu\in D^\perp$. So $2ab\,\hat A\circ\nu+b^2\pi_\perp(\nu\circ\nu)=\theta b\nu$. Pairing with $u$ and using $\langle\hat A\circ\nu,u\rangle=\lvert u\rvert^2$ gives $b\langle\nu\circ\nu,u\rangle=-2a\lvert u\rvert^2$. Correct.
- **The links.** $\langle W\circ\nu,u\rangle=a\lvert u\rvert^2-2a\lvert u\rvert^2=-a\lvert u\rvert^2$. $\langle W\circ\hat A,u\rangle=a\langle Q(\hat A),u\rangle+b\lvert u\rvert^2=b\lvert u\rvert^2$, the first term vanishing as $Q(\hat A)\in D$. Then $T(W,w_c,u)=a(-a\lvert u\rvert^2)-b(b\lvert u\rvert^2)=-\lvert u\rvert^2$. Correct.
- **Normalisation of $h_c$.** Along $\cos t\,W+\sin t\,v$ the second derivative is $-3P(W)+6T(W,v,v)=6\big(T(W,v,v)-\tfrac\theta2\big)$. The great circle through $W$ in direction $w_c$ is batch 1's circle through $\hat A$ and $\nu$, parametrised by arc length, whose second derivative is $3E$ with $b^2E=2ap-\theta(1+a^2)$. So $H(w_c,w_c)=E/2=\frac{2ap-\theta(1+a^2)}{2b^2}$. The normalisation is right.
- **The $2\times2$ form and the criterion.** Off-diagonal entry $T(W,w_c,\hat u)-\tfrac\theta2\langle w_c,\hat u\rangle=-\lvert u\rvert$. With $h_c<0$ a symmetric $2\times2$ matrix has a positive eigenvalue iff its determinant is negative, $h_ch_u<\lvert u\rvert^2$, which is $h_u>-\lvert u\rvert^2/\lvert h_c\rvert$. Correct and an iff on this plane.
- **Scope remark.** $\hat u$ and the $2\times2$ form need $u\ne0$. The note treats $u=0$ separately, so this is wording only.
- The character remark (both $w_c$ and $u$ meet $\nu$'s characters, $w_c$ also the trivial one) is correct by $\Gamma$-equivariance, $\hat A$ being $\Gamma$-fixed.

## 2. Batch 4

- **The $D$ row.** Expanding directly, $T(W,w_c,x)=a^2T(\hat A,\nu,x)+ab\big(T(\nu,\nu,x)-T(\hat A,\hat A,x)\big)-b^2T(\nu,\hat A,x)=ab\langle\nu\circ\nu-Q(\hat A),x\rangle$, the mixed terms vanishing by closure. The $D$ component of criticality, $a^2Q(\hat A)+b^2\pi_D(\nu\circ\nu)=\theta a\hat A$, gives $\langle\nu\circ\nu,x\rangle=-\tfrac{a^2}{b^2}\langle R,x\rangle$ on $D\ominus\hat A$. So the row is $ab\big(-\tfrac{a^2}{b^2}-1\big)\langle R,x\rangle=-\tfrac ab\langle R,x\rangle$. It also equals r1916's $\tfrac ba\langle Q(\nu),x\rangle$. Correct. $R\in D\ominus\hat A$ since $Q(\hat A)\in D$ and $\langle R,\hat A\rangle=p-p=0$.
- **The $D^\perp$ row.** $T(W,w_c,y)=(a^2-b^2)\langle\hat A\circ\nu,y\rangle+ab\langle\nu\circ\nu,y\rangle$ and $b\langle\nu\circ\nu,y\rangle=-2a\langle\hat A\circ\nu,y\rangle$ for $y\perp\nu$, giving $-(a^2+b^2)\langle\hat A\circ\nu,y\rangle=-\langle u,y\rangle$. Correct.
- **Decomposition and $V$.** $W^\perp=\mathbb Rw_c\oplus(D\ominus\hat A)\oplus(D^\perp\ominus\nu)$ since $\operatorname{span}\{\hat A,\nu\}=\operatorname{span}\{W,w_c\}$. Because $\hat R\in D$ and $\hat u\in D^\perp$, the complement $V$ of $\operatorname{span}\{\hat R,\hat u\}$ splits as $(D\ominus\hat A\ominus\hat R)\oplus(D^\perp\ominus\nu\ominus\hat u)$, and the row vanishes on it. $w_c$ is second-variation-orthogonal to $V$. Correct.
- **Sufficiency.** A positive eigenvalue of the restriction gives a vector with $H>0$. Correct and trivially so. It is sufficient only, which is what the note claims.
- **The bound.** $h_c\le-\theta(1-a)^2/(2b^2)=-\theta(1-a)/(2(1+a))$, using $p\le\theta$ (L57 above $\Theta_n$) and $b^2=(1-a)(1+a)$. Correct.
- **Correction to the heading.** "Couples to exactly two directions" should read "at most two": the span drops a direction when $R=0$ or $u=0$, and $\hat R$, $\hat u$ are undefined there.
- **This is L284.** With $t=a$, [L284](../findings/second-order.md)'s $z=(A/t-tW)/b$ is $-w_c$, and $\mathcal Uz=-\theta^{-1}\big(W\circ z-\tfrac\theta2z\big)$ is $-\theta^{-1}$ times the second-variation row at $z$. So $C=(\mathcal U-\eta)z$ is $\theta^{-1}$ times batch 4's row with its $w_c$ entry removed, and $\eta=-h_c/\theta$. Batch 4 gives $\theta^2\lVert C\rVert^2=\tfrac{a^2}{b^2}\lvert R\rvert^2+\lvert u\rvert^2$, hence $\theta^2a^2b^2\lVert C\rVert^2=a^4\lvert R\rvert^2+a^2b^2\lvert u\rvert^2$. L284's right side matches term by term: $q_\perp=Q(A)-\tfrac{P(A)}{t^2}A=a^2R$, and $A\circ B-\bar\lambda B=ab\,u$ with $\bar\lambda=ar$, whose character split is $\sum_U\lVert Y_U\rVert^2+\sum_Up_U(\lambda_U-\bar\lambda)^2=a^2b^2\lvert u\rvert^2$. **So batch 4 is L284 read as the second-variation row**, and batch 3's $\lvert u\rvert$ is L284's second and third defects together. L284 already notes that $\eta\ge0$ is L214's inequality, which is batch 1's $h_c\le0$. *What batches 3 and 4 add is the reading: $C$ is the only coupling of $w_c$ to the rest of $W^\perp$, and the $2\times2$ and $3\times3$ ascent tests on it.* The note should cite L284 and [r1793](REVIEW_r1793.md), [r1794](REVIEW_r1794.md).

## 3. Batch 5, equivalence and closure

- $R=0$ iff $Q(\hat A)=p\hat A$, which (as $Q$ is the full gradient up to the factor $3$) is criticality of $\hat A$ on the sphere, a diagonal one since $\hat A\in D$. $u=0$ iff $\hat A\circ\nu=r\nu$. Conversely an eigenvector's eigenvalue is its Rayleigh quotient $r$. Correct.
- With $R=0$ the $D$ component gives $\pi_D(\nu\circ\nu)=\tfrac{\theta a-a^2p}{b^2}\hat A=r\hat A$ by $\theta a=a^2p+b^2r$. With $u=0$ the $D^\perp$ component gives $\pi_\perp(\nu\circ\nu)=\tfrac{\theta-2ar}b\nu=q\nu$ by $\theta b=2abr+b^2q$. Closure and the restriction of $P$ to the circle cubic follow. Correct.
- **On the record**: the [I121 audit](2026-09-28-I121-MG-audit.md), section "27.1 screened against the record", derives the same statement from L284: $C=0$ makes $e=A/t$ a diagonal critical point, $f=B/b$ one eigenvector of $L_e$, and $f\circ f=\tau e+qf$, the outside author's closed algebra $e^2=de$, $ef=\tau f$, $f^2=\tau e+qf$ of Conjecture 27.1, with $(d,\tau,q)=(p,r,q)$. R=0 and u=0 is exactly $C=0$ by the identity above.

## 4. The screen claim, padded $W_{\mathbb{CP}^2}$

**By hand.** Take the Kähler form $\varphi_{01}+\varphi_{23}$ and $W=c\,\mathrm{diag}(2,-1,-1)$ on the self-dual basis $e^+_1=(\varphi_{01}+\varphi_{23})/\sqrt2$, $e^+_2$, $e^+_3$, zero on $\Lambda^-$, with $c=1/\sqrt6$.
- Its diagonal entries in the pair basis are $c(1,-\tfrac12,-\tfrac12,-\tfrac12,-\tfrac12,1)$ (pairs $01,02,03,12,13,23$), so $\pi_DW\propto$ the $S^2\times S^2$ weights and $a^2=3c^2=\tfrac12$. The off-diagonal entries $c,\tfrac c2,-\tfrac c2$ on the three complementary pairs give $b^2=\tfrac12$.
- $aA=\tfrac12(W^+\oplus W^+)$ and $b\nu=\tfrac12(W^+\oplus-W^+)$ under the identification $e^+_i\leftrightarrow e^-_i$. Products split over $\Lambda^\pm$ since $[\Lambda^+,\Lambda^-]=0$, and on each $\mathfrak{so}(3)$ factor (structure constant $\sqrt2$, from $I\#I=2I$) $X^2+X^\#X=c^2(6,-3,-3)=3c\,X$ for $X=c(2,-1,-1)$, which is traceless and Ricci-free. So $\hat A\circ\hat A=p\hat A$, $\hat A\circ\nu=p\nu$, $\nu\circ\nu=p\hat A$: $R=u=0$, $r=p$, $q=0$.
- $P(W)=\sum\lambda^3+6\lambda_1\lambda_2\lambda_3=18c^3=\sqrt{3/2}$, and $p=P(S^2\times S^2)=36k^3=\sqrt3/2$ with $k=1/\sqrt{12}$. Also $q=0$ follows from batch 2: $\nu$'s three entries all carry the one character $\varepsilon_0\varepsilon_1\varepsilon_2\varepsilon_3$.
- **Padding.** Every product above is built from operators supported on the block $\Lambda^2\mathbb R^4$ and lands in Weyl tensors of $\mathbb R^4$ with no Ricci part, and $\#$ of block-supported operators stays in the block. So the same identities hold in the coordinate frame of $\mathbb R^n$ for every $n\ge4$, and padded $W_{\mathbb{CP}^2}$ is decoupled at $n\ge12$, which is where the note's use of it lives. The note's screen was at $n=4$ only, and this argument is what carries it to the padded case.
- **Screen** (seconds, [r1926_cp2.py](/private/tmp/claude-501/-Users-ap-code-private-hamiltons-cubic/d3790d3b-3383-4c5d-9548-46ea15965c86/scratchpad/r1926/r1926_cp2.py), $n=4$, Weyl space built from the Bianchi and Ricci constraints): $\theta=1.2247449=\sqrt{3/2}$, $a=b=0.7071068$, $p=r=0.8660254$, $q\sim10^{-17}$, $\lvert R\rvert,\lvert u\rvert\sim10^{-15}$. - **Consistency with L24.** Here $r=p>\tfrac p2$, so $S^2\times S^2$ is not a local maximum at $n=4$ (it is a saddle between $W^+$ and $W^-$). The local-maximum argument of the next bullet therefore does not reach this point, as the note intends.

## 5. The circle analysis at $q=0$

- From $\theta b=2abr$ and $b>0$, $\theta=2ar$. Then $\theta a=a^2p+b^2r$ gives $a^2(3r-p)=r$. At $r=p$, $a^2=\tfrac12$ and $\theta=\sqrt2p=\sqrt{3/2}$. Correct.
- **Correction: the range.** An interior point needs $0<a^2<1$. For $0<r\le\tfrac p3$ the equation $a^2(3r-p)=r>0$ has no solution with $a^2>0$. For $\tfrac p3<r\le\tfrac p2$ it gives $a^2\ge1$. For $r\le0$, $\theta=2ar\le0$, and batch 2's $r\ge\tfrac{a\theta}{1+a}>0$ excludes it at $\theta>0$. So the sentence should read: **with $q=0$ and $0<r\le\tfrac p2$ there is no interior critical point with $\theta>0$**. The note's "$\tfrac p3<r<\tfrac p2$" omits $r\le\tfrac p3$ and the endpoint $r=\tfrac p2$ that L24 actually delivers. Both are one line and the conclusion stands once they are added.
- **$L_{\hat A}\le\tfrac p2$ off $\hat A$ at a diagonal local maximum** is [L24](../findings/second-order.md) at $\hat A$ with $p>0$, applied to $\nu\perp\hat A$. Correct as a conditional. Note that the decoupled case needs $\hat A$ critical only, so this covers the local-maximum subcase.
- **Correction: $q=0$ is a restriction.** $q=0$ holds whenever $\nu$ lies in one sign character (batch 2), which covers $W_{\mathbb{CP}^2}$. For general $q$ the closed branch's circle is already analysed on the record: the outside author's binary endpoint criterion, the circle's maximum is $d$ iff $dq^2\le(d+\tau)^2(d-2\tau)$, rebuilt and CONFIRMED in [r1801](REVIEW_r1801.md) and carried in [R54](../routes.md)'s row. At $q=0$ it reduces to $d\ge2\tau$, which is the note's $r\le\tfrac p2$.
