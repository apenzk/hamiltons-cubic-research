### Reviewers 30 and 31: the nondegeneracy route, audited twice on identical briefs

**They agree: the nondegeneracy claim is not merely confirmed but provable, and the reduction it was serving is refuted.** Independent implementations throughout ([review_r30_core.py](scripts/review_r30_core.py) and eight companions; [review_r31_core.py](scripts/review_r31_core.py) and four), reports in [review_r30_report.md](agent_logs/review_r30_report.md) and [review_r31_report.md](agent_logs/review_r31_report.md).

**I1 CONFIRMED, and upgraded from a sweep to an identity.** Both derived the closed form independently. With $x=p-1$, $y=q-1$, the dangerous family is

$$v(p,q)=\frac{n-2}{2(p-1)(q-1)},\qquad v\ge\tfrac12\iff x+y\ge xy\iff (p-2)(q-2)\le1,$$

so $p=2$ (strictly) and $(3,3)$ (equality) are the only solutions. **$S^3\times S^3$ is the unique two-block product with $p,q\ge3$ at which any eigenvalue reaches $\tfrac12$, at any $n$**, an identity rather than a scan, verified as an exact eigenvector at 36 splits to $n=30$ including $3{+}27$ and $4{+}20$, and extended by the other reviewer to $n=20$. The $10^{-8}$ threshold does no work anywhere: the gap to $\tfrac12$ is $1/(2\max(p,q)-2)$. Unbalanced splits move **away** from $\tfrac12$, so the danger sat at the balanced-small end and is now exhausted.

**I2 CONFIRMED, but the evidence offered for it was insufficient.** At $S^3\times S^3$ all nine principal cosines are $1.000000000000$ while $\dim E(\tfrac12)=18$, the cosine test would have passed the one known failure. The invariant that works is $\|\Pi_E-\Pi_T\|$, which is $\le3.4\times10^{-14}$ everywhere and exactly $1.0$ at $(3,3)$.

**I3 CONFIRMED with a scope error.** "Every other eigenvalue below $\tfrac12$" fails for the $p=2$ splits the claim itself lists, and for every $\mathbb{CP}^m$; it holds for $p,q\ge3$. Separately, $\mathfrak{so}(1)\oplus\mathfrak{so}(n-1)$ has a **zero**-dimensional invariant Weyl space, not a one-dimensional one.

**I4 REFUTED as stated, and the error was a restatement of something already withdrawn.** Step 3 is circular: $A/\mathop{\mathrm{tr}}(W_-)=\theta+S/\mathop{\mathrm{tr}}(W_-)\ge\theta$, so "$A/t\le\theta_0$" is strictly stronger than the target it was meant to establish. [findings/INDEX.md](../findings/INDEX.md) had already withdrawn "no estimate at any step" once, and the claim reinstated it. Steps 1 and 2 are sound, and the failure mode the brief proposed (ungraded points converging to a graded one), is precisely what step 1 forbids. Also: the gap is $O(1/n)$, uniformly $1/(2(q-1))$, so nondegeneracy never fails but yields no constant uniform in $n$.

**I5 REFUTED, at $n=8$, by both reviewers independently.** Cartan classifies symmetric subalgebras up to $\mathrm{Aut}(\mathfrak{so}(n))$, while the argument needs $SO(n)$-conjugacy; the two agree for every $n\ge5$ **except $n=8$**, where $\mathrm{Aut}(\mathfrak{so}(8))\supsetneq PO(8)$. One reviewer built the triality automorphism from octonions, Lie-homomorphism error exactly $0.0$, preserving $\mathrm{Weyl}_8$, criticality and $\theta$, and produced $\tau(S^3\times S^5)$: a graded critical point at $\theta=1.115546702$ whose isotropy is 13-dimensional and acts **irreducibly** on $\mathbb R^8$, so it is neither a sphere product nor a $\mathbb{CP}^m$ and no element of $O(8)$ conjugates it to one. The other reached the same point as $\mathfrak{sp}(1)\oplus\mathfrak{sp}(2)\subset\mathfrak{so}(8)$ on $\mathbb H^2$, a verified symmetric pair with a one-dimensional invariant Weyl line and the same $\theta$ and $L_e$ spectrum. Two further facts: $\mathbb{CP}^4$ **is** $\tau(S^2\times S^6)$ rather than an independent family, and $\mathfrak{spin}(7)\subset\mathfrak{so}(8)$ has a zero-dimensional invariant Weyl space, so that class is empty.

**A second, independent refutation of I5's premise.** $W_{\mathbb{CP}^2}$ has slack **exactly zero** and $[\mathfrak m,\mathfrak m]\to\mathfrak m$ vanishing at $10^{-32}$, yet $[\mathfrak h,\mathfrak m]\to\mathfrak h$ and $[\mathfrak h,\mathfrak h]\to\mathfrak m$ both leak at $4.0$ ($n=8$) and $4.899$ ($n=10$), re-verified here independently. So $\mathfrak h$ is not a subalgebra, the decomposition is not an involution, and **$S=0$ does not imply $\mathbb Z_2$-graded**. The earlier identification held only on the subalgebra family, where $\mathfrak h$ is a subalgebra by construction. $W_{\mathbb{CP}^2}$ is moreover Morse–Bott nondegenerate at $n=8,10,12$ and sits at $\sqrt{3/2}$, above $\theta_0$ at $n=8$ and $10$.

**Reconciliation.** The two audits agree on every verdict. The $W_{\mathbb{CP}^2}$ finding was reported by one and not tested by the other, so it was checked here directly rather than taken on one report; it stands. Each reviewer also caught a bug in its own implementation, one a missing inner transpose in the polarization of $\#$, the other a QR orthonormalisation on a rank-deficient matrix, and both note that the eigenvalue-$1$ eigenvector $W$ is a free self-check for any implementation of $L_e$.
