## Item 1: the margin

**The identity chain is right.** Row (B) traced over $E_0$ reads $t_{022}-t_{000}+t_{012}-t_{001}=p$, so $t_{022}-t_{000}-p=t_{001}-t_{012}=(4D+t_{122})-(2D+t_{122})=2D$ on the solved rows. The record's $4T=p+\kappa^{-1}(t_{000}-t_{022}+p)$ then gives $2T/p-\tfrac12=(t_{000}-t_{022}+p)/(2p\kappa)=-D/(p\kappa)$. With $t_{000},t_{011},t_{122}\ge0$, r1149's identity gives $-16D\ge(\kappa-n+5)p$, hence $-D/(p\kappa)\ge(\kappa-(n-5))/(16\kappa)$. This is exactly `margin_lower` in [ClassTraceTest.lean](../lean/HamiltonsCubic/Results/ClassTraceTest.lean), whose right side $-((t_{222}-t_{002}+p)/2)/(p\kappa)$ is $-D/(p\kappa)$ with the record's $D$. Here $T$ is the exact trace of $B_{\mathrm{lead}}$, so the only leading-order step is $\lambda_{\max}(L_e)\approx2\lambda_{\max}(B_{\mathrm{lead}})$.

**The size near $\theta_0$ is right at the top of the band.** $2p\le\binom n2$ gives $\kappa\le\theta\sqrt{n(n-1)/2}$. At $\theta=\theta_0(1+\delta)$ this is $(1+\delta)(n-1)\sqrt{(n-2)/n}$, and $\kappa_{\max}^2/(1+\delta)^2=(n-2)(n-2+1/n)$, so $\kappa_{\max}=(1+\delta)\big(n-2+O(1/n)\big)$. Then $\kappa-(n-5)\le3+(n-2)\delta+O(1/n)$ and the margin at $\kappa_{\max}$ is $\big(3+(n-2)\delta\big)/\big(16(n-2)(1+\delta)\big)$ up to $O(n^{-2})$, which is the stated $(3+n\delta)/(16n)$ to leading order. Screen: at $\delta=0$ the top margin is $0.0105$ at $n=20$ and $0.00192$ at $n=100$, against $3/(16n)=0.0094$ and $0.00188$.

**Correction: that is the maximum of the margin over the band, not its size.** The margin $(\kappa-(n-5))/(16\kappa)$ is increasing in $\kappa$ and vanishes at the band's lower edge $\kappa=n-5$. Since $\kappa=\theta\sqrt{2p}$ steps by about $\theta^2/\kappa\approx2/n$ between consecutive $p$, the smallest margin in the band is typically of order $n^{-2}$ and can be smaller when $(n-5)^2/(2\theta^2)$ falls just below an integer. Screen at $\theta=\theta_0$: the bottom margin is $1.1\times10^{-3}$ at $n=10$, $1.0\times10^{-4}$ at $n=20$ and $6\times10^{-6}$ at $n=100$. The same holds at $\theta=\theta_0(1+\delta)$: the band then extends further down and its lower edge still has margin near $0$, so "of order $\delta$ for $\theta$ well above $\theta_0$" is again the top value.

**Correction: "independent of $p$".** The margin depends on $p$ through $\kappa=\theta\sqrt{2p}$. What is true is that it carries no factor $p^{-1/2}$, unlike the open-region margin of [r1146](REVIEW_r1146.md). Suggested wording: "a function of $\kappa$ alone, with no $p^{-1/2}$ factor, rising from $0$ at $\kappa=n-5$ to about $(3+n\delta)/(16n)$ at the top of the band."

## Item 2: the band

- $2p+m=\binom n2$ gives $p\le n(n-1)/4$. CONFIRMED.
- Lower end at $\theta_0$: $n^2(n-5)^2/(4(n-1)(n-2))$. Expanding $n^2/((n-1)(n-2))=1+3/n+7/n^2+O(n^{-3})$ against $n^2-10n+25$ gives $\tfrac14(n^2-7n+2+O(1/n))$. CONFIRMED, and for $\theta>\theta_0$ the lower end only drops.
- Width: $\tfrac14(n^2-n)-\tfrac14(n^2-7n+2)=\tfrac14(6n-2)\approx\tfrac32n$. CONFIRMED. The screen counts $14,30,74,150,1500$ integer $p$ at $n=10,20,50,100,1000$ against $1.5n$. Nonempty at every $n\ge6$ (the width exceeds $1$), and at $n=5$ the whole range is forced.
- $m=N-2p<N-2\cdot\tfrac14(n^2-7n+2)=3n-1$ up to $O(1/n)$. CONFIRMED as an upper bound ("at most about $3n$"). $m$ also takes small values, including $0$ when $N$ is even, so "of order $n$" should read "at most of order $n$".
- $m=0$ excluded: with $E_1=0$ every $t$ with an index $1$ vanishes. Rows (B$_0$), (B$_2$), (C$_0$) of [ClassTraceTest.lean](../lean/HamiltonsCubic/Results/ClassTraceTest.lean) then give $t_{222}-t_{002}=-p$, i.e. $D=0$ directly, and r1149's identity reduces to $(n-5-\kappa)p=4t_{000}\ge0$, so $\kappa\le n-5$. CONFIRMED. The citation "$D=0$ (r1150)" can be replaced by this one-line derivation from $t_{001}=4D+t_{122}$.

## Item 3: the trace reduction

**The inequality follows, including $\dim V_+<p$.** Let $k=\dim V_+\le\mathop{\mathrm{rank}}M_0=p$. The positive eigenvalues of $B_{\mathrm{lead}}$ sum to at least $T$ since the others are nonpositive, so $\mathop{\mathrm{tr}}_{V_+}B_{\mathrm{lead}}\ge T$. With $C=C^+-C^-$, $\mathop{\mathrm{tr}}_{V_+}C\ge-\mathop{\mathrm{tr}}_{V_+}C^-\ge-\mathop{\mathrm{tr}}C^-$. So $k\,\lambda_{\max}(B_{\mathrm{full}})\ge\mathop{\mathrm{tr}}_{V_+}B_{\mathrm{full}}\ge X:=T-\mathop{\mathrm{tr}}B_{\mathrm{Ric}}^--\mathop{\mathrm{tr}}B_{\Lambda^4}^-$. When $X>0$ this forces $k\ge1$ and $\lambda_{\max}(B_{\mathrm{full}})\ge X/k\ge X/p$. The sign condition is exactly what licenses replacing $k$ by $p$. Then $\lambda_{\max}(L_e)\ge2\lambda_{\max}(B_{\mathrm{full}})$ from $G\preceq\tfrac12I$: a top eigenvector $x$ of $B_{\mathrm{full}}$ has $x^{\mathsf T}B_{\mathrm{full}}x>0$, hence $\sum_\alpha x_\alpha X_\alpha\neq0$ and $0<x^{\mathsf T}Gx\le\tfrac12\lvert x\rvert^2$, so the quotient is well defined even though the family is linearly dependent. Exact, with no leading-order qualifier: the reduction is the one step in the chain that is not approximate.

**Threshold.** $\tfrac2pX>\tfrac12$ iff $X>p/4$, and $T-\tfrac p4=\tfrac p2(2T/p-\tfrac12)\ge p(\kappa-n+5)/(32\kappa)$ by `margin_lower`. CONFIRMED.

**Ricci negative part.** Write $K_{\alpha\beta}=\langle h_\alpha,\mathring eh_\beta\rangle=H^{\mathsf T}\mathring eH$ and split $\mathring e=\mathring e^+-\mathring e^-$, so $K=K_1-K_2$ with $K_i\succeq0$. Then $B_{\mathrm{Ric}}=-cK$, $c=\tfrac1{2(n-2)}$, and $B_{\mathrm{Ric}}^-=cK^+$. From $K\preceq K_1$, Weyl monotonicity gives $\lambda_i(K)\le\lambda_i(K_1)$, so $\mathop{\mathrm{tr}}K^+\le\mathop{\mathrm{tr}}K_1=\sum_\alpha\langle h_\alpha,\mathring e^+h_\alpha\rangle\le\lVert\mathring e^+\rVert S_{02}$. CONFIRMED, with $\lVert\mathring e^+\rVert$ the operator norm on traceless symmetric matrices restricted to the span of the $h_\alpha$.

**$S_{02}$ and the normalisations.** Consistent across the record: $\Lambda^2$ carries $\langle x,y\rangle=\tfrac12\mathop{\mathrm{tr}}(x^{\mathsf T}y)$, so a unit $x$ has $\lVert x\rVert_F^2=2$ and $\mathop{\mathrm{tr}}(xx^{\mathsf T})=2$, and $\lvert h_{uv}\rvert^2=\tfrac14\lVert\{u,v\}\rVert_F^2=\mathcal S_u(v)$, matching the half-summed note's $\sum_v\lvert h_v\rvert^2=S_2(u)$. Checked by hand on $x=e_1\wedge e_2$: $\mathcal S_x(x)=2$, $\mathcal S_x(y)=\tfrac12$ on the $2(n-2)$ vectors $y=e_i\wedge e_k$, $i\le2<k$, zero otherwise, total $n$, and $\mathop{\mathrm{tr}}\mathcal R_x=n-2$. The derivation extension has $\mathop{\mathrm{tr}}D(A)=(n-1)\mathop{\mathrm{tr}}A$, and $D(xx^{\mathsf T})\succeq\mathcal S_x$ because $\mathcal R_x=-\tfrac12\mathrm{ad}_x^2\succeq0$, so $\sum_{v\in E_2}\mathcal S_x(v)\le2(n-1)$ and $S_{02}\le2p(n-1)$. CONFIRMED.

**Sharper, from the record's own line.** $\mathcal S_x\succeq0$ and $\mathop{\mathrm{tr}}\mathcal S_x=n$ for every unit $x$ (the record's $\sum_aS_a=n$, equivalently $2(n-1)-\tfrac12\cdot2(n-2)$ by the Killing form), so $S_{02}\le np$ and $\mathop{\mathrm{tr}}B_{\mathrm{Ric}}^-\le\tfrac{n}{2(n-2)}\lVert\mathring e^+\rVert p\approx\tfrac12\lVert\mathring e^+\rVert p$. The constant halves and the scale is unchanged.

## Item 4: overreach

- **The final sentence is right for the norm route**: against $p(3+n\delta)/(32n)$ the estimate $\tfrac12\lVert\mathring e^+\rVert p$ (or $\lVert\mathring e^+\rVert p$ as written) needs $\lVert\mathring e^+\rVert\lesssim(3+n\delta)/(16n)$, i.e. $O(1/n)$ at $\delta=0$, and Huisken's $c_n\lvert e\rvert=2c_n/\theta\approx1$ is $O(1)$. The same arithmetic at fixed $\delta$ gives $\lesssim\delta/(16(1+\delta))$ with the sharper constant.
- **Correction: it is stated at the band's top only.** Across the band the requirement is $\lVert\mathring e^+\rVert<(\kappa-n+5)/(16\kappa)$ (to leading order) with the $np$ bound, which tends to $0$ at the lower edge. So the norm route, even with an $O(1/n)$ bound, can close only the upper part of the band, and the sentence should say "on the top of the band" or state the requirement as a function of $\kappa$.
- **Correction: "$o(1/n)$" in the margin paragraph.** What is required is corrections below $(\kappa-n+5)/(16\kappa)$, which at the top is $O(1/n)$ with constant about $3/16$. "$o(1/n)$" is stronger than needed at the top and weaker than needed near the edge.
- **A note, not a correction.** The $\Lambda^2$ operator norm of $e$ on this family is $a/\theta=1/\kappa\approx1/n$ in the band, so the needed $O(1/n)$ is exactly the $\Lambda^2$ scale of $e$. The gap is that $\lvert\langle h,\mathring eh\rangle\rvert=\lvert\langle e,h\owedge h\rangle\rvert$ is bounded by $\lVert e\rVert_{\mathrm{op}}$ times the trace norm of $h\owedge h$ on $\Lambda^2$, which is of order $\mathop{\mathrm{rank}}(h)\lvert h\rvert^2$, and $h_{uv}=\tfrac12\{u,v\}$ has rank of order $n$ for generic $u,v$. So "a cancellation" can be sharpened to: a bound on $h\owedge h$ against $E_0\ominus E_2$ better than its trace norm, for anticommutators of the cross pairs.
- The $\Lambda^4$ term is not bounded ("likewise through the cross Bianchi overlap" names the quantity only), which the paragraph's "Owed" already carries.
