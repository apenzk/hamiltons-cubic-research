## A. Fidelity

- **The inequality.** Lemma L178 bounds $\theta(W)=\mathop{\mathrm{tr}}W^3+\langle W,W^\#\rangle$ at $\lVert W\rVert=1$. `Pcubic R` $=\mathop{\mathrm{tr}}(R^{\mathsf T}(R^2+R^\#))$ is that pairing (r1499 A: `basis4` is `inner2`-orthonormal, so coordinate Frobenius is the operator pairing), and `(Rᵀ * R).trace` is $\lVert R\rVert^2$. $P$ is cubic and $\lVert R\rVert^2$ quadratic, so $P^2\le\tfrac32\lVert R\rVert^6$ is scale-invariant and reads $\lvert\theta\rvert\le\sqrt{3/2}$ at unit norm, the record's sign-closed statement, which implies the one-sided $\theta\le\sqrt{3/2}$. **Faithful.**
- **$R=0$ and every norm.** At $R=0$ both sides are $0$ and the statement holds trivially, and the record's hypothesis "nonzero" is only needed to define $\theta$, which the homogeneous form never divides by. At any other norm the scale invariance above transfers the unit-norm bound. **Right at every norm.**
- **`IsWeyl` is $\mathcal W_4$.** Sharp.lean line 115: symmetric, `IsBianchi` ($R_{01,23}-R_{02,13}+R_{03,12}=0$ in `pair4`'s order $(01,02,03,12,13,23)$, the single first-Bianchi equation at $n=4$), and `IsRicciFlat` with `Ric` built as $\sum_c\langle R(\phi_{ac}),\phi_{bc}\rangle$ through `toMat4`/`coord4`, so orientation signs come from the basis. Symmetric operators on $\Lambda^2\mathbb R^4$ satisfying first Bianchi are algebraic curvature tensors, and Ricci-flat ones are Weyl tensors, so this is $\mathcal W_4$ restricted to rational coordinates. **Faithful.**
- **Rank.** `Matrix.rank` over ℚ is the dimension of the ℚ-span of the columns. For a rational matrix this equals the rank over ℝ (rank is invariant under field extension, since it is the largest nonvanishing minor size), so for every $R$ the theorem quantifies over, its hypothesis is exactly the record's $\mathop{\mathrm{rank}}(W:\Lambda^2\to\Lambda^2)\le3$. The statement itself needs no such lemma, since hypothesis and conclusion both live on ℚ. It would be needed only in a transfer to real $W$, which is the owed step of C-1. **Faithful.**
- **Scope.** The record's $W$ ranges over real $\mathcal W_4$. The theorem ranges over $\mathcal W_4\cap\mathbb Q^{6\times6}$. Nothing in L178N4.lean reaches an irrational $W$. **CONFIRMED over rational $R$, with the real case owed (C-1).**

## B. Soundness

- **`mixed_zero4`.** The ten Ricci equations $\mathop{\mathrm{Ric}}_{ab}=0$ ($a\le b$) are unfolded to linear equations in the 36 entries, symmetry supplies the 15 relations $R_{qp}=R_{pq}$, and each of the nine mixed entries $\omega^{+\mathsf T}_iR\,\omega^-_j$ is closed by `linarith` in those. The mixed block is the $\Lambda^+\times\Lambda^-$ part of $W$, which is the traceless Ricci tensor, so Ricci-flatness alone kills it: first Bianchi is correctly not a hypothesis. The goal is elaborated before the tactic block, so the script can only close it or fail.
- **`trace4`.** Sum of the two block traces from $\mathop{\mathrm{Ric}}_{aa}=0$ (scalar flatness), difference $\omega^+$-trace minus $\omega^-$-trace $=4(R_{05}-R_{14}+R_{23})$, killed by `IsBianchi`. This is the record's step-4 remark that Bianchi is consumed exactly once in step 1. Both conjuncts close by `linarith`. **Sound.**
- **`decomp4`.** `Rp` is $\tfrac12\sum B_{ij}w_iw_j^{\mathsf T}$, the operator with block $B$ in the orthonormal basis $w_i/\sqrt2$, so the block of $R$ is $B_{ij}=\tfrac12w_i^{\mathsf T}Rw_j$, which is `blkP`/`blkM`. Since $\{w^+_i/\sqrt2,w^-_j/\sqrt2\}$ is an orthonormal basis of $\mathbb Q^6$ (over ℝ), $R$ equals the sum of its four blocks, and the mixed ones vanish by `hmix` and symmetry. The 36 entries are linear identities closed by `linarith` with the nine `hmix` facts and symmetry. **Sound.**
- **`rank4`.** Suppose both blocks are nonzero. Each is symmetric (`dot_symm`) and trace-free (`trace4` halved), so `exists_minorQ` (via `eq_zero_of_minorsQ`, the sum-of-squares argument reviewed at r1497 over ℝ, here restated over ℚ) gives a nonzero $2\times2$ minor $(i_1,i_2;j_1,j_2)$ of $B^+$ and $(k_1,k_2;l_1,l_2)$ of $B^-$. The four range vectors $Rw^+_{j_1},Rw^+_{j_2},Rw^-_{l_1},Rw^-_{l_2}$ are independent: pairing a vanishing combination with $w^+_{i_1},w^+_{i_2}$ kills the $\Lambda^-$ terms by `hmix'` and leaves a $2\times2$ system whose determinant is the unscaled minor, so `two_by_twoQ` forces $c_0=c_1=0$, and likewise $c_2=c_3=0$. **The ½-scaling conversion** is correct: the minor of `blkP` is $\tfrac14$ times the unscaled minor, so `field_simp` clears the $\tfrac14$ and `linarith` transports $h_0$. Four independent vectors in `range R.mulVecLin` give `finrank ≥ 4`, contradicting `R.rank ≤ 3` after `unfold Matrix.rank`. **Sound.**
- **`L178_n4`.** In the branch $B^+=0$, `decomp4` reads $R=R^+(0)+R^-(B^-)$, `Rp_zero` and `zero_add` drop the first summand, and the substitution $f=-a-d$ (`hf`, from `trace4`'s second conjunct halved) puts $R$ in exactly the shape `L178_theta_asd` takes. The other branch mirrors it with `L178_theta_sd`. `rw [hd]` rewrites every occurrence of `R` in the goal, which is what is wanted, and the goal after it is syntactically the `.2` of the Theta lemma. **Sound.**
- **Hypotheses.** Every hypothesis is consumed: `hS` in the symmetry relations, `hR` in `mixed_zero4` and `trace4`, `hB` in `trace4`, `hrank` in `rank4`. None is missing: the trace-freeness `L178_theta_*` builds in by $f=-a-d$ is supplied by `trace4`. **CONFIRMED.**
