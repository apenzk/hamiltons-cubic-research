### Reviewers 32 and 33: the two-block spectrum, and whether the symbolic proof reaches nondegeneracy

**They agree on all six verdicts.** Independent implementations throughout, 13 scripts in [review_r33_*.py](scripts/) and a matching set in [review_r32_*.py](scripts/), with neither importing the code under review. Reports in [review_r32_report.md](agent_logs/review_r32_report.md) and [review_r33_report.md](agent_logs/review_r33_report.md).

**I6, I7, I9 CONFIRMED.** $Q(W_{p,q})=\frac{n-1}{pq}W_{p,q}$ for the unnormalised operator, proved in closed form for all $p,q\ge2$ rather than checked; $X$ is Ricci-flat and Bianchi with $\|X\|^2=4/3$ on exactly three pairings; $\mu\ge\tfrac12\iff(p-2)(q-2)\le1$.

**I8 CONFIRMED, and it is a stronger and cheaper theorem than the one offered.** Holding the three block values as **free symbols**, $e\circ X=\lambda X$ with every off-support entry identically zero and
$$\lambda=\tfrac12(\varepsilon_{PP}+\varepsilon_{QQ})-\varepsilon_{PQ},$$
for **any** three-block diagonal $e$. Criticality is never used; it only fixes the number, and substituting the sphere-product values collapses $\lambda$ to $(n-2)/(2(p-1)(q-1))$. The route through $\theta_{\mathrm{raw}}$ was therefore an unnecessary detour. Both reviewers verified steps (i)–(iv) and both justified the finite universe rather than assuming it: one by rebuilding at $0,1,2,3,4$ spare indices per block (up to $4356$ entries) and getting identical symbolic answers, the other by observing that $a$ and $b$ must share the *same* outside label. One reviewer sharpened step (ii): the vanishing holds **entrywise**, not merely in trace, because $X_{mq}\ne0$ forces $m$ and $q$ to be disjoint core planes covering all four indices, so a plane carrying an outside index misses one of them and kills a structure constant. Exact `Fraction` checks at 27 splits including $(2,13)$, $(4,11)$ and $(7,7)$ return zero residual.

**I10 REFUTED at $(p,q)=(2,2)$, and confirmed at every other split reached.** Both found the same failure independently. The named multiplicities sum to $pq(pq+3)/2=14$ against $\dim\mathrm{Weyl}_4=10$, so the claim that the zero eigenvalue carries a positive residual is a residual of $-4$; and "$1$ simple" fails, the exact spectrum being $(-1)^4,(\tfrac12)^4,1^2$, because $\mu=1$ there. The mechanism is structural rather than an artefact: $\Lambda^2(\mathbb R^4)$ splits under the Hodge star and $e=e^++e^-$ with $Q(e^\pm)=e^\pm$ and $\langle e^+,e^-\rangle=0$, so $e$ is **non-primitive**. Stating I10 for $n\ge5$ makes it clean, and $n=4$ is far below the conjecture's range. Elsewhere it survived three independent attacks: full diagonalisation at all 45 splits to $n=16$; exact rational nullities summing to $\dim\mathrm{Weyl}_n$ at every split with $n\le8$ including $(4,4)$, with mod-$p$ upper bounds matching at $n=9\ldots12$; and a diagonalisation-free test, $\prod_v(L_e-vI)=0$ to $9\times10^{-14}$ with seven trace moments pinning the multiplicities by Vandermonde. Every $p=4$ case matched, so the reducible $\Lambda^2(\mathbb R^4)$ splits off no distinct eigenvalue at any split through $n=14$. Two identities hold *identically in $(p,q)$*: $\mathop{\mathrm{tr}}L_e=0$ and $\mathop{\mathrm{tr}}(L_e^2)=\kappa\|e\|^2$.

**I11: the measurement is confirmed and the stated route to it is REFUTED, a wrong-direction bound, in text written the same cycle.** [findings/INDEX.md](../findings/INDEX.md) claimed that once the symbolic $L_ev=\mu v$ check was done "the statement holds at every $n$ at once". It does not. An exhibited eigenvector bounds $\lambda_{\max}$ and one multiplicity from **below**, while "$(3,3)$ is the unique split reaching $\tfrac12$" and "$\dim E(\tfrac12)=pq$" are **upper** bounds on the rest of the spectrum. I8 closes only the half running the right way (the $p=2$ violation and the $(3,3)$ degeneracy, both now genuinely proved at every $n$) and nondegeneracy for $p,q\ge3$ rests entirely on I10. Corrected in place. What is now rigorous: nondegeneracy at all five splits of $n=12$, from the mod-$p$ upper bound plus a one-line proof that the orbit tangent lies in $E(\tfrac12)$. $E(\tfrac12)$ is moreover *equal* to the orbit tangent, principal cosines $1.000000000$ and $\|L_eU-\tfrac12U\|\le9\times10^{-16}$, with $(3,3)$ reproducing the known $18$ against $9$.

**A coverage defect in the code, found by both.** Part 2 of [b_twoblock_symbolic.py](scripts/b_twoblock_symbolic.py) checks eight hand-picked pair types, five of them trivially zero, and omits pairs sharing a core index, repeated generic labels, and the diagonal, the only types where a spill was possible. The conclusion was right and the coverage was not. The exhaustive enumeration cited in support of it had been run from a scratch directory and never preserved; it is now [b_twoblock_exhaustive.py](scripts/route-b/b_twoblock_exhaustive.py), re-run at 784 pairs with zero mismatches, and both reviewers supply their own.

**Reconciliation.** No verdict differs between the two, and both refutations were reached independently rather than by one reviewer following the other. Neither exact-rational nor modular rank of $E(\tfrac12)$ was computed above $n=12$, and at $p=2$ the $\lambda_2=0$ family is indistinguishable from the residual zeros, so that piece of I10 is unfalsifiable there.
