## 1. Fidelity

- **A-1. The |D| characterisation** ([L140Abs.lean:38–63](../lean/HamiltonsCubic/Results/L140Abs.lean)). Confirmed. The record's K × K step needs ‖½|D|‖ = ½‖D‖ with D = P₀HP₀, which is exactly the old binder `habs` ([L140Statement.lean:143](../lean/HamiltonsCubic/Results/L140Statement.lean)). `frob_eq_of_sq_eq` proves Σ_{i,j∈K} A_ij² = Σ_{i∈K} Σ_{c∈K} A_ic A_ci = tr_K(A²) for A symmetric on K × K, then equates the two traces through `hsq` at i = j, which is the standard proof. `kk_sum_eq` converts the masked double sum to the block sum by `Fintype.sum_ite_mem` twice, correctly. The hypotheses: `hAs` (|D| symmetric on K × K) and `hsq` (Σ_{c∈K} |D|_ic |D|_cj = Σ_{c∈K} H_ic H_cj) hold for the record's |D|, the unique positive semidefinite square root of D² on ker u, and D² computed inside the block is (P₀HP₀)² because D is supported on K. Positivity of |D| is not required, so any symmetric square root qualifies, a genuine generalisation that costs nothing since the norm is all L140 uses. `hHs` is used to supply D's symmetry on the block. It is asked on all of N × N where only K × K is used, which is stronger than needed but true of the record's H (a symmetric operator on Λ²), so it restricts nothing the record instantiates. Every hypothesis is consumed: the remaining ones pass straight to `g1_sq_le_quarter`, and the build reports no unused-variable warning in the file.
