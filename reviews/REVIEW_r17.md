### Reviewer 17: the second-order obstruction and the predictor–corrector

**The formula is correct; the certificate built from it was not.** The branching condition $\Phi(v)=\Pi_K\pi Q(v)=0$ was rederived from scratch and validated against a positive control the original script never ran. But $\Phi$ is **quadratic**, so evaluating it on nine SVD basis vectors of a nine-dimensional space says nothing about the rest. At $S^3\times S^3$ a global minimisation reaches $|\Phi|<10^{-10}$ from **400 of 400** random starts (best $9.4\times10^{-16}$ against a typical basis value of $0.32$), and $\operatorname{rank}\mathrm d\Phi=4$ at every zero, so the zero set is a **five-dimensional cone**, the Segre rank-one cone of $T\cong\mathbb R^3\otimes\mathbb R^3$, the same isomorphism that produces the excess kernel. A counterexample lies inside the script's own code path: an explicit unit combination of its basis vectors returns $1.4\times10^{-15}$.

**The conclusion survives, by a different argument at each point.** At $\mathfrak{su}(3)$ Kähler, $|\Phi(v)|^2=c|v|^4$ exactly ($c=0.447813411$, tensor residual $2.8\times10^{-15}$), so the obstruction is genuinely nonzero on all of $T$. At $S^3\times S^3$ it takes a **third**-order invariant: since $A_vv=\Phi(v)=0$ on the cone, the scalar $\Psi_1(v)=\langle\pi Q(v),L^{+}\pi Q(v)\rangle$ is free of the undetermined second-order constant, and equals $-0.5929271$ constantly on the cone (300 samples, 120 constrained restarts) against $10^{-16}$ on orbit directions, as a genuine branch would require. Not excluded: Puiseux arcs of leading exponent $\ge2$.

**The predictor–corrector is refuted, on two counts.** Measuring $\|W'-W_0\|$ is meaningless when the orbit is $9$- or $11$-dimensional and the corrector is ungauged; re-measured as $\min_g\|gW'g^{\mathsf T}-W_0\|$, every point flagged "moved off" is within $3.8\times10^{-3}$ of the orbit and the one flagged a genuine branch is $1.1\times10^{-6}$ from it. The spectrum comparison used to repair that error is contaminated identically, spectral differences track orbit distance at $\approx0.58$, Weyl's inequality saturated. A damped Gauss–Newton with analytic Jacobian reaches residual $3$–$7\times10^{-16}$ while orbit distance falls to $10^{-6}$ and $10^{-8}$: the original points were a stalled solver.

**An independent cross-validation.** The residual obeys $\|F\|\approx d^3$ at $S^3\times S^3$ and $d^2$ at $\mathfrak{su}(3)$, precisely the cubic and quadratic valleys the third- and second-order obstructions predict, so the two halves of the audit confirm each other.

**Actions.** The certificate "$\Pi_{\ker}Q(v)\ne0$ on a basis" is withdrawn from [plan.md](plan.md) and [findings/INDEX.md](../findings/INDEX.md); it needs a global argument on $\mathbb P(T)$. Reviewer 16's "slice corrector, residual $1.9\times10^{-13}$ at transverse distance $0.2$" has no script in the tree and must be re-measured against the orbit before reuse.
