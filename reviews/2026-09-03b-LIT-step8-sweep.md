## Batch 1, I5 stripped of names: which half is free, and what L16(b) actually owes

**Screened before searching, and the screen decided the batch.** I5 as written in [plan.md](../plan.md) owes two clauses: *(i)* the non-pure critical set above $\sqrt{3/2}$ has compact closure within $\{\theta\ge\sqrt{3/2}\}$, and *(ii)* that closure lies at positive distance from the pure locus $O(n)\cdot\mathcal D_n$.

**Clause (i) is free, and the record says so twice.** $\mathrm{Crit}(P)\cap S$ is the zero set of the polynomial map $W\mapsto Q(W)-P(W)W$ on the sphere, hence a compact real algebraic set; $\theta=P(W)$ is continuous on it, so $\{\theta\ge\sqrt{3/2}\}\cap\mathrm{Crit}$ is compact with no hypothesis. On record at [ATTEMPTS.md:920](../ATTEMPTS.md) (*"critical values are automatically finite and isolated (compact real algebraic set)"*) and at [ungraded-slack.md](../findings/ungraded-slack.md) (§ *Why this is a better-posed question*): *"$\mathrm{Crit}(P)$ inside the unit sphere is a compact real algebraic set, so it has finitely many connected components."* **Confirmation, not discovery.** The plan's I5 wording (*"its closure within $\{\theta\ge\sqrt{3/2}\}$ is compact"* as a thing to prove), carries a clause the record already has for free; **I5 is clause (ii) alone: separation.** The [ungraded-slack.md](../findings/ungraded-slack.md) passage states the same reduction in its own words: if the graded set is a union of connected components of $\mathrm{Crit}(P)$, the rest is handled *"by compactness alone, with no estimate required."*

**So L16(b)'s block is misdescribed, and the correct description is worse.** [plan.md:55](../plan.md) blocks the Łojasiewicz closure on *"uniformity across the band needs the critical set compact"*. Compactness is free; what a Łojasiewicz argument on a compact analytic set gives with no further input is *an* exponent and *a* constant, and [post F4](../findings/INDEX-traps.md) already prices those, *Łojasiewicz constants are astronomically large* (Milnor bound $3\cdot5^{1637}$ at $n=12$, [ATTEMPTS.md:920](../ATTEMPTS.md)), *location-blind*. **Exponent $\tfrac12$ with a usable constant is a Morse–Bott statement, and it is available exactly where [Lemma L128](../findings/second-order.md) proves nondegeneracy (the isolated pure orbits) and nowhere on the non-pure set, where nondegeneracy is the unknown.** So L16(b) is blocked on *nondegeneracy of the non-pure critical set in the band* (or its emptiness, which is the conjecture), and separation (clause ii) does not supply that. **The plan's statement that I5 unblocks both R16 and R17 overstates R17's side**: I5 hands L16(b) a compact set at positive distance from the pure locus, and Łojasiewicz on that set still runs into post F4 unless the set is Morse–Bott.

**What survives for R17.** Near the balanced orbit, exponent $\tfrac12$ with an explicit constant *is* on record, [Corollary L128.1](../findings/second-order.md) plus the within-factor margin $1/(2(M-1))$, and [plan.md:119](../plan.md) prices the resulting perturbation neighbourhood as *provably not enough*: the margin decays like $1/n$ while the band widens to $0.189$. **So the value-gap-to-distance mechanism is fully priced on both sides**: explicit and too small near the orbit, existent and useless (F4) away from it. (6)](../references/boehm-wilking-2008-space-forms/notes.md), $\mathrm{Ric}(R^2+R^\#)_{ij}=\sum_{kl}\mathrm{Ric}_{kl}R_{kijl}$, is on record only as *"$\Phi$ preserves Weyl"* ([problem.md](../findings/problem.md), table row). Polarised on Bianchi tensors it reads $\mathrm{Ric}(AB+BA+2A\#B)=\mathring A(\mathrm{Ric}B)+\mathring B(\mathrm{Ric}A)$, and with $W_+W_-=0$ (spectral parts of one operator) it gives $2\,\mathrm{Ric}(W_+\#W_-)=\mathring{|W|}(g)$ and $\mathrm{Ric}(W_-^2)=\mathring W_-(g)-\mathrm{Ric}(W_-^\#)$, hence, up to the $\mathrm{Alt}$ corrections that $W_\pm\notin S^2_B$ force, which are the defect terms the record already carries,

## Batch 3, Route 3 reread: the partition mechanism has no analogue, but the stability-constant dichotomy splits L16(b)

**What Route 3 asked for.** The first pass ([2026-09-03 sweep, Route 3](2026-09-03-LIT-step8-sweep.md)) proposed borrowing the *partition-with-cutoffs* mechanism of multi-bubble stability proofs, with the falsifier "no partition exists for compact orbit families at fixed distance". **The falsifier fires, by inspection of the objects.** A partition argument needs (i) a scale parameter along which pieces separate, and (ii) additivity of the functional across pieces. Here $P(W)=\langle W,W^2+W^\#\rangle$ is additive, $P(W_1\oplus W_2)=P(W_1)+P(W_2)$, only when $W_1,W_2$ have orthogonal supports in $\mathbb R^n$ (the block-sum case) and a block sum of Weyl tensors is Ricci-flat only when each block is, so a Weyl tensor never decomposes this way with both blocks nonzero (a block-sum of Weyl tensors of $\mathbb R^{p}\oplus\mathbb R^{q}$ carries no mixed-plane entries and is then Weyl only if each block is separately Weyl on its own factor, which is the diagonal-locus class the conjecture already grants). And there is no scale: the ambient sphere is compact, the balanced orbit is compact, and the competitor orbit is compact. **Route 3 as written is closed by its own falsifier**, and the $\ell_{a,b}$ / Route 4 material is independent of it (batch 4).

**What survives is the *shape* of a stability-constant problem, and it is standard.** Stored this batch: [König 2022](../references/koenig-2022-bianchi-egnell-sharp-constant/README.md), [arXiv:2210.08482](https://arxiv.org/abs/2210.08482), Theorem 1: the Bianchi–Egnell constant $c_{BE}=\inf_{f\notin\mathcal M}(E(f)-E_{\min})/\mathrm{dist}(f,\mathcal M)^2$ is *strictly* below the local (Hessian, spectral-gap) constant, so "the study of the sharp constant cannot be reduced to a local analysis near the manifold". Its reference [7] (Chen–Lu–Tang, [arXiv:2210.06727](https://arxiv.org/abs/2210.06727)) states an abstract condition under which global = local. **Conjecture L16 is this problem with the sign reversed.** Write
$$\kappa_n^*:=\sup_{W\in S,\ \delta(W)\neq0}\frac{P(W)-\theta_0(n)}{\delta(W)^2},\qquad\text{L16}\iff\kappa_n^*\le\kappa_n=9\big(\sqrt{3/2}-\theta_0(n)\big)<0 .$$
Since $\delta=0$ exactly on the diagonal locus (granted $P\le\theta_0$ there), the ratio's supremum is one of three numbers, and the dichotomy is the whole content of the reading:

**Scenario (α) is a by-hand lemma, and the objects it needs are on the record.** Three facts, each checked here rather than assumed:

1. *$W^*$ is invertible on $\Lambda^2$, so $W_\pm$ and $\delta^2$ are real-analytic near it.* For $S^m\times S^m$ with unit factors, $n=2m$, $R=\mathrm{id}$ on $\Lambda^2V_1\oplus\Lambda^2V_2$ and $0$ on the mixed planes $V_1\wedge V_2$; the metric is Einstein with $\mathrm{scal}=2m(m-1)$, so $W=R-\tfrac{\mathrm{scal}}{n(n-1)}\mathrm{id}=R-\tfrac{m-1}{2m-1}\mathrm{id}$, with eigenvalues $\tfrac{m}{2m-1}$ (multiplicity $m(m-1)$) and $-\tfrac{m-1}{2m-1}$ (multiplicity $m^2$); the trace $m(m-1)\cdot m-m^2(m-1)=0$ confirms tracelessness. The spectral gap at $0$ is $\tfrac{m-1}{2m-1}$, so $W\mapsto W_-$ is analytic in a neighbourhood, and $\mathrm{Alt}(W_-^*)=0$ because $W_-^*$ is $-\tfrac{m-1}{2m-1}$ times the projector onto mixed planes $=\mathrm{id}-P_{\Lambda^2V_1}-P_{\Lambda^2V_2}$, each term of which satisfies Bianchi. Hence $\delta^2=\|D(\mathrm{Alt}W_-)[H]\|^2t^2+O(t^3)$ along any curve $W^*+tH+\dots$ on $S$, and the ratio's limit in direction $H$ is $\mathrm{Hess}P[H,H]\big/2\|D(\mathrm{Alt}W_-)[H]\|^2$.
2. *$D(\mathrm{Alt}W_-)$ at $W^*$ is explicit and vanishes off the $(2,2)$-split four-set blocks.* With two eigenvalues only, Daleckii–Krein gives $DW_-[H]=P_-HP_-+c\,(P_-HP_++P_+HP_-)$, $c=\tfrac{\lambda_-}{\lambda_--\lambda_+}=\tfrac{m-1}{2m-1}$. In the frame decomposition of [second-order.md](../findings/second-order.md) (diagonal entries, pair blocks $H_{ij,ik}$, four-set blocks), $\mathrm{Alt}$ kills every tensor whose only entries are diagonal or pair type (the $\Lambda^4$ component needs four distinct indices), and $DW_-$ preserves each type since it rescales entries by plane-type. So $D(\mathrm{Alt}W_-)[H]\neq0$ forces $H$ into a four-set block $\{i,j,k,l\}$, where in signed coordinates $(x,y,z)=(H_{ij,kl},H_{ik,jl},H_{il,jk})$ with Bianchi $x+y+z=0$ ([Lemma L121](../findings/second-order.md)): for a $(4,0)$-split all six planes are $+$ and $DW_-[H]=0$; for a $(3,1)$-split every entry couples a $+$ plane with a mixed one and $DW_-[H]=cH$, whose $\mathrm{Alt}$ is $c\,\mathrm{Alt}(H)=0$; **for a $(2,2)$-split** ($i,j\in V_1$, $k,l\in V_2$) the entry $x$ couples two $+$ planes and $y,z$ couple two mixed planes, so $DW_-[H]=(0,y,z)$ and $\mathrm{Alt}\,DW_-[H]=\tfrac{y+z}{3}\mathbf 1=-\tfrac x3\mathbf 1$, of squared norm $x^2/3$ in these coordinates.
3. *On the $(2,2)$-split four-set block the Hessian of $P$ is [Lemma L121](../findings/second-order.md)'s $G$ with $B=C=0$*: eigenvector $(0,1,-1)$ at $-A/2$ has $x=0$ (denominator zero, Hessian negative, nothing to prove) and eigenvector $(2,-1,-1)$ at $+A/2=+\mu$, $\mu=\tfrac{n-2}{2(m-1)^2}$ ([Lemma L26](../findings/nondegeneracy.md)), has $x=2$. **So the pencil is one number per $n$:** $\kappa_n^{\mathrm{Hess}}=\mathrm{Hess}P[H_0,H_0]\big/2\|D(\mathrm{Alt}W_-)[H_0]\|^2$ at $H_0=(2,-1,-1)$, and (α) reads $\kappa_n^{\mathrm{Hess}}\le\kappa_n$. The Hessian's normalisation against $L_M$ (whether $\mathrm{Hess}_S P=2(L_W-\theta)$ on the tangent space, as [Lemma L24](../findings/second-order.md) is used) is the one thing this batch leaves to the cycle; under that normalisation the sketch gives $\kappa_n^{\mathrm{Hess}}\approx 2(\mu/2-\theta_0)\cdot\tfrac{6}{4/3}\cdot\tfrac12=\tfrac92(\mu/2-\theta_0)$, about $-5.1$ at $n=12$ against $\kappa_{12}=-0.102$ and $\to-\tfrac92\sqrt2\approx-6.4$ against $\kappa_\infty\approx-1.705$. *A sketch, under a normalisation not yet checked, and reported as one.*

**What (α) would deliver and what it would not.** If the sketch's factor survives, (α) is a lemma with a factor of several of room, the balanced-orbit scenario is far from binding, in agreement with [plan.md:119](../plan.md)'s reading that the band lies elsewhere, and with a third-derivative bound ($P$ is a cubic polynomial, so $D^3P$ is constant on directions; $\delta^2$'s third derivative is bounded by the resolvent through the gap $\tfrac{m-1}{2m-1}$) it yields an **explicit radius $r_n$ around the balanced orbit on which L16 holds**, an analytic piece of L16(b) that [plan.md:119](../plan.md)'s Łojasiewicz margin $1/(2(M-1))$ could not give. If the factor fails at any $n\ge12$, **R17 is refuted by a direction at the balanced orbit**, the cheapest possible falsifier. What it does not touch is (γ): König's theorem is precisely that the local constant need not be the global one, and here the conjecture asserts the global one sits at the competitor. (γ) is the original problem in penalised form, $\Phi_\kappa=P-\kappa\delta^2$ critical on $S$, and this batch found nothing outside that reformulates it.

**Screen against the record.** *[Lemma L17](../findings/route-a.md)* is first order at the competitor, scenario (β), a different point. *L20*, no maximiser of $P$ near the competitor, again (β). *[ATTEMPTS.md:295](../ATTEMPTS.md)* is second order for $P$ alone at $W^*$ (the local-maximum statement, [problem.md:147](../findings/problem.md)) and supplies the numerator's sign, not the pencil. *[ATTEMPTS.md:1545](../ATTEMPTS.md)* retired the penalised second-order question under a different item's framing and records no computation of it. *[ATTEMPTS.md:819–835](../ATTEMPTS.md)* is the numerical battery, consistent with (α) at $n=12,14$ and establishing nothing for general $n$. **Genuinely new as a plan item**; the ingredients (L121, L26, L24, Daleckii–Krein with two eigenvalues) are all on record.

## Batch 4, Route 4: the $\ell_{a,b}$ transformation law is the identity on Weyl tensors and Ricci-only on the spectral parts

**The source, read at the statement.** [BW08 Theorem 2](../references/boehm-wilking-2008-space-forms/notes.md): for $a,b\in\mathbb R$ the equivariant map $\ell_{a,b}:S^2_B(\mathfrak{so}(n))\to S^2_B(\mathfrak{so}(n))$, $R\mapsto R+2(n-1)a\,R_I+(n-2)b\,R_{\mathrm{Ric}_0}$, equivalently $\ell_{a,b}(R)=R+2b\,\mathrm{Ric}\wedge\mathrm{id}+2(n-1)(a-b)R_I$, satisfies
$$D_{a,b}:=\ell_{a,b}^{-1}\big((\ell_{a,b}R)^2+(\ell_{a,b}R)^\#\big)-R^2-R^\#=\big((n-2)b^2-2(a-b)\big)\mathrm{Ric}_0\wedge\mathrm{Ric}_0+2a\,\mathrm{Ric}\wedge\mathrm{Ric}+2b^2\mathrm{Ric}_0^2\wedge\mathrm{id}+\frac{\mathop{\mathrm{tr}}(\mathrm{Ric}_0^2)}{n+2n(n-1)a}\big(nb^2(1-2b)-2(a-b)(1-2b+nb^2)\big)I,$$
"the key fact [being] that it does not depend on the Weyl curvature". Its proof is Lemma 2.1 plus eq. (6), both already transcribed.

**Applied to the objects the route would feed it, the law is empty in two ways, and both are on record.**

- *On $W$ itself.* $\mathrm{Ric}(W)=0$ gives $\ell_{a,b}(W)=W$ and $D_{a,b}(W)=0$ for every $(a,b)$: the transformation law is the identity on the Weyl space, and the "evaluation at $b=1$" the first pass held in reserve evaluates $Q(W)=Q(W)$.
- *On the spectral parts $W_\pm$.* Theorem 2 is stated on $S^2_B$ and $W_\pm\notin S^2_B$; the extension it needs is exactly [Lemma L131](../findings/isotropy-cap.md), the non-Bianchi Lemma 2.1 with its $-3\,\mathrm{Alt}(R)$ correction, which Route 1 already produced. Granting the extension, every term of $D_{a,b}(W_\pm)$ is a polynomial in $\mathrm{Ric}(W_\pm)=g$ alone, and $\mathrm{Ric}$ is rank-one on $\mathrm{span}\{W_+,W_-\}$, so any pairing of the law with the grant is a rewrite of $J_5$ through $g$ and $\mathring W_\pm(g)$, the class [post F56](../findings/second-order.md) closes, and its $\Lambda^4$ content is the defect terms [post F55](../findings/second-order.md) already accounts for. The demonstration consumes its objects: with $W_+W_-=0$ and $Q(W)=\theta W$ one has $Q(W_+)+Q(W_-)+4C=\theta(W_++W_-)$, so $Q(W_\pm)$ is determined by $C$ and $\theta$, and the law adds a Ricci-only $D_{a,b}(W_\pm)$ to each side, a term in $g$ that the grant's own $\mathrm{Ric}$-pairing already carries.

**Outcome.** Route 4 closed: it is Route 1 with two free parameters, and Route 1 is spent (the position file, §previous sweeps). Searched: `ell_{a,b}` and the transformation law across [findings/](../findings/), [ATTEMPTS.md](../ATTEMPTS.md), [routes.md](../routes.md), nothing on record beyond the first pass's reservation, and nothing outward beyond BW08 §2 itself, whose Kähler analogue (their §4) is the only extension the authors name.

## Phase 3b, routes, ranked analytical-first

### Route A, L16(b) by scenario: prove the balanced-orbit pencil lemma, with an explicit radius *(my pick)*

- **Mechanism.** $\text{L16}\iff\kappa_n^*\le\kappa_n$ with $\kappa_n^*=\sup_{\delta\neq0}(P-\theta_0)/\delta^2$, and $\kappa_n^*$ is the largest of three scenario constants (batch 3 table). Scenario (α) is a generalised eigenvalue problem for the pencil $(\tfrac12\mathrm{Hess}_{W^*}P,\ \|D(\mathrm{Alt}W_-)[\cdot]\|^2)$ on the normal space of the balanced orbit; the denominator lives only on the $(2,2)$-split four-set blocks, where [Lemma L121](../findings/second-order.md) gives the numerator in closed form and Daleckii–Krein with two eigenvalues gives the denominator as $x^2/3$, so (α) is **one closed-form number $\kappa_n^{\mathrm{Hess}}$ per $n$**, compared with $\kappa_n$. A third-derivative bound ($P$ cubic; $\delta^2$ analytic through the gap $\tfrac{m-1}{2m-1}$) then converts the pencil into an explicit radius $r_n$.
- **Cost.** One cycle by hand: fix the normalisation of $\mathrm{Hess}_SP$ against $L_M$ ([Lemma L24](../findings/second-order.md)), evaluate the pencil on $H_0=(2,-1,-1)$, prove the sign for all $n\ge12$ (the sketch gives $\kappa_n^{\mathrm{Hess}}\approx\tfrac92(\mu/2-\theta_0)$, a factor of several below $\kappa_n$), then the remainder bound. Gate 2 on the lemma. **No computation beyond a screen of the closed form at one $n$**, which the analysis-only rule permits.
- **Delivers.** Lemma: *$\Phi=P-\kappa_n\delta^2$ has nonpositive Hessian on the normal space of the balanced orbit for every $n\ge12$, with $\kappa_n^{\mathrm{Hess}}$ in closed form*; corollary: *L16 holds on $N_{r_n}(SO(n)\cdot W^*)$ with $r_n$ explicit*. It **splits** L16(b) into (α) settled, (β) on record to first order, (γ) the interior, and corrects the plan's phrasing of L16(b) (blocked on nondegeneracy of the non-pure set, with I5's compactness clause free, batch 1).
- **Falsifies it.** $\kappa_n^{\mathrm{Hess}}>\kappa_n$ at any $n\ge12$: a direction at the balanced orbit along which $P-\theta_0>\kappa_n\delta^2$, which refutes Conjecture L16 and closes R17 at the cost of one lemma. Either outcome is a deliverable.
- **Sources.** [König 2022, Theorem 1](../references/koenig-2022-bianchi-egnell-sharp-constant/notes.md) for the dichotomy; [BW08 Lemma 2.1](../references/boehm-wilking-2008-space-forms/notes.md); [second-order.md](../findings/second-order.md) L121, L122, L24, L26.
- **Grade (proposed).** closes 1 (a named sub-step; 3 if it refutes) × attackable 3 (route exists, untried) × margin 2 (the sketch's factor) × analytic 1 = **6**, moves: *splits* L16(b).

### Route B, the competitor-side radius: turn ATTEMPTS 1372 into a lemma

- **Mechanism.** Scenario (β). [Lemma L17](../findings/route-a.md) proves the one-sided first derivative of $\Phi$ at the competitor is nonnegative in every direction with equality on an explicit flat; along the flat the second-order coefficient is known only numerically (ATTEMPTS 1372: worst $-1.83$ at $n=12$). The lemma is the second-order coefficient along the flat in closed form, negative for all $n\ge12$, and an explicit radius $r'_n$ from it.
- **Cost.** One cycle by hand; the flat is $\mathcal W_{n-4}$ padded plus the hyper-Kähler rotations, both on record, so the second variation of $P$ there is a block computation with L121-type ingredients; the kink of $\delta^2$ makes the remainder term the harder half.
- **Delivers.** The second local piece of L16(b); with Route A, L16 reduces to (γ) on a compact set away from both orbits.
- **Falsifies it.** A flat direction with vanishing second-order coefficient, then the competitor is degenerate for $\Phi$ and the radius argument needs third order, or a positive coefficient, which refutes L16.
- **Sources.** [route-a.md](../findings/route-a.md) L17, L20; ATTEMPTS 1372, 819–835.
- **Grade (proposed).** closes 1 × attackable 2 (partly spent: first order done, second order sampled) × margin 2 × analytic 1 = **4**, moves: *splits* L16(b). Ranked below A because A's outcome can refute R17 and A's ingredients are all closed forms; B's remainder term is the harder piece.

### Route C, I18 through the perturbed criticality equation

- **Mechanism.** Scenario (γ) is attained at a critical point of $\Phi_\kappa$ on $S$, which satisfies $Q(W)-\kappa\nabla\delta^2=\theta' W$ with $\nabla\delta^2=2(D\,\mathrm{Alt}W_-)^*\mathrm{Alt}(W_-)$. I18 is blocked because every rearrangement of the unperturbed eigen-equation is inert ([post F47](../findings/INDEX-traps.md)); the perturbed equation carries a term F47 has never seen, and the question is whether the twin-pair clause (I18e) reads differently against it.
- **Cost.** A route must be invented: attackable 1. The first cycle would only derive the perturbed equation's pair and four-set clauses in the record's frame decomposition and compare them with F47's.
- **Delivers, if anything.** A restatement of I18 with a source term; **it would not unblock I18 for R16**, whose critical points are those of $P$, only for R17's (γ).
- **Falsifies it.** The source term projects to zero on the pair clauses, which the batch 3 computation makes likely, since $D(\mathrm{Alt}W_-)$ is supported on four-set blocks.
- **Grade (proposed).** closes 1 × attackable 1 × margin 1 × analytic 1 = **1**, moves: *adds* a node on R17 only. Listed so that the (γ) scenario has a named next step rather than none.

**Which I would pick, and why.** **Route A.** It is the only candidate whose every ingredient is a closed form already on record, it costs one cycle by hand under the analysis-only rule, and it is two-sided: a pass delivers the first explicit piece of L16(b) and a fail refutes R17 outright. Route B follows it naturally and shares its plan structure. Route C is a placeholder for (γ) and should be worked only if A and B both land and I18 is still without a method.
