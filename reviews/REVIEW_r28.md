### Reviewer 28: the $L_e$ trace machinery, and whether any moment bound can bite

**The identities hold and are stronger than claimed; the reformulation carries less than claimed; and the sharpening route is closed, not merely short.** Rebuilt from definitions in [review_r28_core.py](scripts/review_r28_core.py), with the anchors $Q(\mathrm{Id})=(n-1)\mathrm{Id}$, $\dim\mathcal W_n$ as the exact nullity of (Bianchi, Ricci) at $n=4\ldots13$, and the $S^4\times S^4$ spectrum recovered with the multiplicity list already on record.

**T1 CONFIRMED and understated.** $\tau=0$ in two orthonormal bases while the individual summands are $O(1)$, so it is cancellation rather than smallness, and $\mathop{\mathrm{tr}}L_x=0$ at random **non**-critical $x$ across $n=4\ldots13$ ($\le1.3\times10^{-15}$). The argument needs only $\mathcal W_n^{O(n)}=0$, which is weaker than irreducibility.

**T2 CONFIRMED.** $\kappa_W=(2n-1)(n-1)/4$ exact at $n=4\ldots13$, including the dimensions the claim never tested, $19.5$ at $n=7$, $52.5$ at $n=11$, $75$ at $n=13$. At $n=4$ it returns $21/4$, matching Fox's $21/16$ under his normalisation, so it **is** $\kappa_F$. And $\mathcal W_n$ is absolutely irreducible **already for $SO(n)$** from $n=5$ on (commutant $=1$ at $n=5,6,7,8$), so the $O(n)$-versus-$SO(n)$ caveat can be dropped.

**T3 CONFIRMED as arithmetic, REFUTED as a consequence.** It is T2 evaluated at $x=y=e$ and holds at **every** unit element of $\mathcal W_n$, critical or not, checked at eight non-critical points to $3\times10^{-14}$. Criticality never enters; the hypothesis is membership in $\mathcal W_n$. The reformulation of the conjecture survives, since $\theta=P$ on the critical set, but the identity itself carries no critical-point information.

**T4's inequality CONFIRMED, two premises REFUTED.** Cauchy–Schwarz runs in the right direction and $D-1-\mathrm{orb}$ is the right count, and the ratios reproduce at $1.507$ and $2.012$ at $n=12$ with limits $\sqrt2$ and $2$. But the spectral description is false at degenerate points: $\lambda=1$ has multiplicity $3$ at $(S^3)^3$ and $6$ at $(S^3)^4$, and $\lambda=\tfrac12$ has multiplicity $18$ against an orbit of $9$ at $S^3\times S^3$, the pattern being $m(1)=\binom k2$ and $m(\tfrac12)=k\cdot\mathrm{orb}$ at $(S^3)^k$. The bound survives because it uses both only as lower bounds and the expression is increasing in the multiplicity, but the derivation must read "contains at least". Separately, $\lambda=1$ is the top eigenvalue only at local maxima: fifty random $n=6$ critical points reach $\lambda_{\max}=96$.

**The sharpening route is REFUTED, and this is the report's main result.** A new second-variation fact: at a local maximum, $P''\le0$ gives $\langle L_ev,v\rangle\le\tfrac12$ for every $v\perp e$, which is exactly the constraint the Cauchy–Schwarz minimiser already satisfies. **T4 is therefore the exact optimum of its moment problem, not a lossy relaxation**, and no moment-based sharpening exists. Three escapes are closed with it: the third moment is proportional to $\langle x\circ y,z\rangle$ only at $n=4,5$, there being two independent cubic Weyl invariants from $n=6$ ($\theta^2\sum\lambda^3$ is $0.3125$ at $S^3\times S^3$ against $-1.5104$ at $\mathbb{CP}^3$, same $n$ and same $\theta$); a forced kernel would need $\dim\ker\ge0.930\,D$ at $n=12$ and $0.999\,D$ at $n=100$ against a largest observed $0.714\,D$; and the operator-norm ceiling $c_n=\max\|Q(y)\|$ equals $\sqrt{3/2}=\max P$ at $n=6,7,8$, tight and therefore useless, which incidentally re-confirms $\max P=\sqrt{3/2}$ at those dimensions independently.

**The deficit is structural**: $\kappa_W/\theta_0^2\sim n^2/4$ against a forced $\sum\lambda^2\sim\mathrm{orb}/4\sim n^2/8$. No bound in this family ever drops below $\theta_0$.
