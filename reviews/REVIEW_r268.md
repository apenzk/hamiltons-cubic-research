## Claim 1, is the new argument the retracted one, relabelled?

Evidence: [c1_cubes_span.py](../scripts/reviewers/r268/c1_cubes_span.py) part (B), [out_c1.txt](../scripts/reviewers/r268/out_c1.txt).

**The two arguments differ in their domain, and the difference is measurable.** The retracted claim (`d46cda0`, retracted by `bc593d4`) took the $\gamma_D$, functionals on $V^{\otimes12}$, and asserted that restriction to the diagonal loses only the non-symmetric part. r265 and r266 refuted it by splitting the map into *restrict to $\mathcal W_n^{\otimes3}$*, then *restitute*, and showing the whole collapse sits in the first factor. `a5f8c0d` addresses the second factor only, on a domain that is already the subspace.

That the first factor really is where the loss lives is exhibited directly, with no representation theory in it. For a random $\dim S$-dimensional subspace $S<U$:

| $\dim U$ | $\dim S$ | rank of $\{s^{\otimes3}:s\in S\}$ in $\mathrm{Sym}^3(U)$ | $\dim\mathrm{Sym}^3(S)$ | $\dim\mathrm{Sym}^3(U)$ |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 6 | 2 | 4 | 4 | 56 |
| 6 | 4 | 20 | 20 | 56 |
| 8 | 5 | 35 | 35 | 120 |
| 10 | 4 | 20 | 20 | 220 |

The cubes of a subspace reach exactly $\dim\mathrm{Sym}^3(S)$ and never one dimension more, so a functional on the *ambient* $\mathrm{Sym}^3(U)$ can vanish on every cube of $S$ and be nonzero, the retracted argument's hole, at four cells. Move the domain to $\mathrm{Sym}^3(S)$ and the same table reads *full rank*, which is the new argument. **The distinction is the one both refuting reviewers drew, applied in the direction where it holds.**

**Which domain is the programme's.** The certificate is an SOS decomposition of a degree-$6$ invariant of $W$, so its carriers are cubic forms on $\mathcal W_n$; the block table the $184$ comes from is the table of $\mathrm{Sym}^3(\mathcal W_{12})$; and the realisation theorem's own conclusion, [brauer-pairing.md:52](../findings/brauer-pairing.md), is $e\,M_\lambda=\operatorname{Hom}_{O(n)}(V_\lambda,\mathrm{Sym}^3\mathcal W_n)$ **exactly**. So the constructed objects are elements of the $184$-dimensional space, and the subspace step has indeed already happened when the cubes argument begins. r259's *"a covariant into the block $\lambda$ is a map $V^{\otimes12}\to V_\lambda$"*, still at [plan.md:72](../plan.md) three paragraphs above obligation (i), is a leg count for Lehrer–Zhang and does not compete with this.

**The unstated step.** The realisation theorem hands over $\operatorname{Hom}(V_\lambda,\mathrm{Sym}^3\mathcal W_n)$, while *"a covariant is an equivariant map $\mathrm{Sym}^3(\mathcal W_n)\to V_\lambda$"* and *"evaluation at $W\otimes W\otimes W$"* are statements about $\operatorname{Hom}(\mathrm{Sym}^3\mathcal W_n,V_\lambda)$. The two are identified by the invariant inner products, which is free for $O(n)$ (every representation is orthogonal, hence self-dual) but it is the hinge between the theorem's objects and the commit's, and it is written down nowhere. One sentence fixes it.

## Claim 2, cubes span $\mathrm{Sym}^3(U)$

Evidence: [c1_cubes_span.py](../scripts/reviewers/r268/c1_cubes_span.py) parts (A), (C), (D), [c5_tolerance.py](../scripts/reviewers/r268/c5_tolerance.py), [out_c5.txt](../scripts/reviewers/r268/out_c5.txt).

**The statement is an identity, and the script measures it instead of using it.** In the commutative ring, $6xyz=(x+y+z)^3-(x+y)^3-(y+z)^3-(x+z)^3+x^3+y^3+z^3$. Substituting basis vectors rebuilds each monomial $t_it_jt_k$ from seven cubes, with exact rational residual $0$ at $\dim U=2,3,4,5,6,8,10$, $220$ of $220$ monomials at the largest. The general form $d!\,t_1\cdots t_d=\sum_{\varnothing\ne S}(-1)^{d-\lvert S\rvert}\big(\sum_{i\in S}t_i\big)^d$ does the same at degrees $2,3,4$ for $\dim U=3,4,5$. **So all of `run_cubes_span.py`'s cells are settled without a sample point, a floating-point rank or a tolerance**, and the statement holds over any field of characteristic $0$, for any $U$, generic or not.

**Nothing in the setting breaks it, and the relevant $U$ is $\mathcal W_n$.** The only property used is that $\mathcal W_n$ is closed under addition and scaling, and it is: the Riemann symmetries, the first Bianchi identity and tracelessness are linear conditions, so $\mathcal W_n$ is the null space of an explicit matrix. Built that way and sampled, cubes of genuine Weyl tensors span $\mathrm{Sym}^3(\mathcal W_n)$ in full, rank $220$ of $220$ at $n=4$ and $\mathbf{7770}$ of $7770$ at $n=5$, with $\dim\mathcal W_n=10,35$ matching $n(n+1)(n+2)(n-3)/12$. Restriction to the unit sphere costs nothing either, degree-$3$ homogeneity carrying vanishing on the sphere to vanishing everywhere. **No smaller $U$ is in play**: the covariants are cubic forms on the whole of $\mathcal W_n$, and the orbit and criticality conditions of A2′ constrain the certificate, not the domain of its carriers.

**Two defects in the script.** It prints $6+6=\mathbf{12}$ $(\dim U,\deg)$ cells; the commit message and [plan.md](../plan.md) both say **fourteen**. And its rank tolerance is a bare absolute `1e-9` applied to rows that are *cubic* in the sampled vector, so a pure rescaling of the samples (mathematics unchanged, rank scale-invariant), changes the answer:

| $\dim U$ | sample scale | reported rank | $\dim\mathrm{Sym}^3$ |
|---|---|---|---|
| 4 | $10^{0}$ | 20 | 20 |
| 4 | $10^{-4}$ | **0** | 20 |
| 6 | $10^{-4}$ | **0** | 56 |
| 8 | $10^{-4}$ | **0** | 120 |

At $10^{-4}$ the assert fires with a message stating that complete polarization has been refuted at a dimension where it is checkable. The degree-$4$ loop breaks one decade earlier, at $10^{-3}$. **r266 filed exactly this against [run_restitution_kernel.py](../scripts/runs/run_restitution_kernel.py) in the immediately preceding cycle** (`6c5cd5d`, *"the bare `tol=1e-8` is ABSOLUTE"*); the repair is `np.linalg.matrix_rank`'s own relative default, or normalising the rows, and neither reached the new file.
