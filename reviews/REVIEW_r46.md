### Reviewers 46 and 47: what a vanishing slack actually constrains

**They agree on T1–T4 and split on T5, where the reviewer who found a counterexample is right.** Own implementations in [review_r46_core.py](scripts/review_r46_core.py) with twelve scripts and [review_r47_core.py](scripts/review_r47_core.py) with eleven; nothing under review imported. Reports in [review_r46_report.md](agent_logs/review_r46_report.md) and [review_r47_report.md](agent_logs/review_r47_report.md).

**T1 CONFIRMED exactly.** Padded $W_{\mathbb{CP}^2}$ has 2 negative, 1 positive and 25, 42, 63 zero eigenvalues at $n=8,10,12$, $\theta=\sqrt{3/2}$, residual $3\times10^{-16}$.

**T2 REFUTED on both halves, and the correction is structural.** $\langle[X,Y],Z\rangle$ is **totally antisymmetric** (residual exactly $0.0$), so $S=0$ is $\binom{\dim\mathfrak m}{3}$ conditions, at $\dim\mathfrak m=2$ that is **zero** conditions, not the one the brief claimed. Hence $S\equiv0$ whenever $\dim\mathfrak m\le2$, confirmed over 200 random 2-planes at $6\times10^{-17}$. The same antisymmetry collapses the grading conditions from three to two: $\|\Pi_{\mathfrak m}[\mathfrak h,\mathfrak h]\|^2\equiv\|\Pi_{\mathfrak h}[\mathfrak h,\mathfrak m]\|^2$ identically ($16/16$, $24/24$, $32/32$), and $\sqrt{16}=4.0$, $\sqrt{24}=4.899$ are exactly the two numbers Reviewers 30 and 31 reported as separate leaks. **FINDINGS' "both leak at $4.0$" is one quantity reported twice.** The slack magnitudes the brief compared are eigensolver noise: one audit's run reverses the ordering, $2.6\times10^{-64}$ at padded $W_{\mathbb{CP}^2}$ against exactly $0.0$ at $S^4\times S^4$.

**T3 REFUTED, by two independent mechanisms.** One audit proved a theorem: **$\{S=0\}$ is closed under orthogonal direct sums and padding, while gradedness is not.** $Q$ is additive over orthogonal blocks and $\mathfrak m=\mathfrak m_A\oplus\mathfrak m_B$ keeps $[\mathfrak m,\mathfrak m]$ in $\mathfrak h$, but $\mathfrak h$ contains $e_a\wedge e_z$ and $e_b\wedge e_z$ with $z$ in the other block, whose bracket $\pm e_a\wedge e_b$ returns to $\mathfrak m$. So every block sum and every padded point is ungraded with $S$ inherited, eleven such critical points, residuals $\le6\times10^{-16}$, $\dim\mathfrak m$ from 2 to 36. At $S^6\times S^6$ padded into $n=14$, $S=0$ is **7140 equations on a 1980-dimensional Grassmannian, $3.6\times$ overdetermined, all satisfied, at an ungraded point.** The other audit killed it from a different direction with strongly regular graphs: the Clebsch graph's two-value operator at $n=16$ is an exact critical point (Ricci $0.0$, Bianchi $0.0$, residual $3\times10^{-18}$) with $\dim\mathfrak m=40$, $S=0$ exactly and $\sigma_1=480$, joined by Petersen and the triangle-free strongly-regular list to $\dim\mathfrak m=1100$. **A $\dim\mathfrak m$ hypothesis cannot rescue the implication.**

**A closed-form law that makes the graded case exact.** At every two-eigenvalue critical point, $\sigma_1=3\sigma_3+\frac{6d}{n}(n^2-4d)$, derived symbolically, verified on six points and four held-out graphs with $\theta$ reproduced to $10^{-17}$. So **graded $\iff\dim\mathfrak m=n^2/4$**, and $S=0$ at any other $\dim\mathfrak m$ is always ungraded. It contains this project's subalgebra-family formula as its $\sigma_1=0$ slice.

**T4 CONFIRMED, with the continuity half corrected.** $W_{\mathbb{CP}^2}$ is Morse–Bott nondegenerate at $n=8,10,12$, so openness holds there. But $S$ is **not** continuous as a function: over 200 random perturbations at $\varepsilon=10^{-6}$ the *minimum* value of $S$ is $16.5$, against $O(\varepsilon^2)$ at the kernel-free $S^4\times S^4$. $S$ is **lower semicontinuous**, which is all the compactness step ever required, so FINDINGS' "$S$ is a polynomial on $\mathrm{Crit}(P)$" is false and should be replaced by the semicontinuity argument.

**T5 REFUTED, and checked here because only one audit found it.** Two copies of $-W_{\mathrm{Petersen}}$ on disjoint $10$-blocks of $\mathbb R^{20}$ give $Q\equiv0$ on the entire 2-plane, so the unit circle lies in $\mathrm{Crit}(P)$ while $S_w$ runs $0.1054\to0.0745$ by a factor $\sqrt2$ and the spectrum changes, so it is not a single orbit. Reproduced here at $\|Q\|\le3\times10^{-17}$ across the circle. **$S$ is therefore not locally constant on $\mathrm{Crit}(P)$**, and the hope that $\{S=0\}$ is a union of components for free is dead. The circle sits at $\theta=0$ and never meets $\{S=0\}$, so the crux itself is untouched.

**What survives, and it is the important part.** Every ungraded $S=0$ point found sits low, $\theta/\theta_0\le0.53$ measured, with the closed-form law capping admissible $d\ne n^2/4$ at $0.89$ for $n=12$, because block sums strictly lower $\theta$. **So a $\theta$-localised repair is untouched; only a $\dim\mathfrak m$-localised one is dead.** And $W_{\mathbb{CP}^2}$ is **graded in its native dimension 4** ($\sigma_1=1.3\times10^{-30}$, $\mathfrak h=\mathfrak u(2)$, a symmetric pair), so the counterexample that scoped the graded classification to the subalgebra family is a **padding artefact** rather than evidence about small $\dim\mathfrak m$.

**Off-brief, and it reaches the tooling.** The repository's `slack(...)` helpers evaluate the $\theta$-formula, which is a **different function** from the structural slack away from the subalgebra family: it returns $+3.333$ at Petersen where $\sigma_3=0$ exactly, and is *negative* at every $\mathbb{CP}^m$ ($-2/3$, $-8/15$, $-10/21$). Convention-checked, since it reproduces the published family value $0.571428571$ at the $n=8$ Cartan point exactly. Its $\delta^2$ term is evaluated at $W$, where it vanishes identically because $W$ is Weyl; the term that repairs the $\mathbb{CP}^m$ rows is $4\delta^2(W_-)$.
