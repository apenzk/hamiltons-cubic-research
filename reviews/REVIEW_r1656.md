## What would break each claim, and what I did

- **Symmetry (1a–1c):** an asymmetric definition of `θ` or `μ`, or an `IsCrossW` whose row-sum clauses are not a swapped pair. I read the definitions and traced `key`'s clause mapping by hand. None found.
- **Primed theorems (2a–2d):** a rewrite that changes meaning, e.g. `sphereWeight_compl` holding only up to scale, or `dnK Tᶜᶜ` differing from the `λ_p` ratio. I read `sphereWeight` and `dnK`. The first is an equality of matrices, and the second agrees by `compl_compl`. For dimension mismatches I checked `n = 4, 5` by hand.
- **Exclusion (3):** a part of `dn_decomp2` whose eigenvalue lemma needs `≥ 3`, or a coincidence the independence argument mishandles. I read the proof. The set is a `Set`, and every sphere lemma sits at `≥ 2`.
- **Repairs (4a–4d):** I grepped the three Lean headers and the INDEX row for `p, q ≥ 3`, `dn_decomp`, `dn_direct` and the multiplicity sentence. That found G1 and G2.
- **Owed (5):** I grepped for `dn_finrank_sum` and `finrank_eig_line_two'`. That found G3.
