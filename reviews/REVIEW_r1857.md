## 1. Attack line A, the three sector lemmas

- **`dn_le`.** `dn_excl_fox` places the fox eigenvalue `ν = λθ` in `{θ·1, θλ_p, θλ_q, −θμ, 0}` with `θ = t(p+q−1) > 0`. The value `1` is excluded by `D ⊥ M`: `dn_eig_line` makes the `θ`-eigenspace of `KL` on weights the line of `sphereWeight T`, so `D = (s/t)·M`, and `⟨M, D⟩ = s/t = 0` forces `u = 0` against `hD0`. The remaining four give `λ ∈ {λ_p, λ_q, −μ, 0}`, and `dn_units` (`0 < λ_p`, `−μ < 0`) puts `−μ` and `0` below `λ_p`. The record's values on the diagonal sector ($\lambda_m$ on $S^2_0(\mathbb R^m)$ for each factor, $-\mu$, $0$) are exactly this set with `M`'s own value `1` removed. Correct.
- **`pair_le`.** A pair component is `pairVec j k c` (`pair_eq_pairVec`). The four membership cases of `(j, k)` against `T` are exhaustive (`by_cases` twice). Cross pairs give `{0, ½}` (`cross_LeLin_spectrum`, the second cross case by `pairVec_comm` and a filter rewrite). Pairs inside `T` give `{0, λ_p, −μ}`, pairs inside `Tᶜ` give `{0, λ_q, −μ}`. Each non-½ value is at most `max(λ_p, λ_q)` because `λ_p, λ_q ≥ 0` at `p, q ≥ 2` and `−μ ≤ 0`. Correct, and the lemma needs no `⊥ M` hypothesis, rightly, since `M` lives in the diagonal sector.
- **`four_le`.** `four_sphere` gives `4c² ≤ K²θ²` with `K = (p+q−2)/((p−1)(q−1))` and `c = λθ`. Dividing by `θ² > 0` gives `(2λ)² ≤ K²`, hence `2λ ≤ K` since `K ≥ 0`, i.e. `λ ≤ K/2 = μ`. The lemma in fact yields `|λ| ≤ μ`, of which only the upper half is stated. Correct. The record's values on the four-set summands (`μ` on $\Lambda^2\otimes\Lambda^2$, `−μ` on $S^2_0\otimes S^2_0$, `0` elsewhere) are all within `[−μ, μ]`.

## 2. Attack line B, `L3_gap`

- **One nonzero component suffices.** `comp_eigen` shows that every isotypic component `comp s H` of an eigenvector of `foxProd2 M` (with `M` of trivial character) is again an eigenvector with the same `c = λθ`. Since `H ≠ 0` and `sum_comp` writes `H` as the sum of its components, some `comp s H ≠ 0`, and a sector lemma on it bounds the common `λ`. The logic is sound: an eigenvalue is a single number, and any nonzero witness in any sector pins it.
- **Exhaustiveness.** `comp_zero` kills every component with `|s| ∉ {0, 2, 4}`, so the three cases `s = ∅`, `|s| = 2` (`card_eq_two`), `|s| = 4` (`card_four`) cover every nonzero component. The diagonal case gets `comp ∅ H ⊥ M` from `comp_perp` (the whole of `⟨M, H⟩` sits in the `∅` component), which is exactly what `dn_le` needs. The pair case uses `hhalf` to discard `λ = ½`. The four-set case bridges `coordOp` to `restrict` by `coordOp_eq_restrict`. Exhaustive and correct.
- **Scope observation (O1).** At `(p, q) = (3, 3)` the bound is vacuous in the useful sense: `μ = 4/8 = ½`, so `L3_gap` gives `λ ≤ ½` and, with `λ ≠ ½`, only `λ < ½`, no gap. This is the known L27 exclusion (`L27_half_span` carries `¬(p = 3 ∧ q = 3)`), and the balanced corollary at `m ≥ 4` avoids it. The docstring's "spectral gap" reads uniformly over `p, q ≥ 3` and should note that at `(3, 3)` the four-set value reaches `½`.

## 3. Attack line C, `L3_gap_balanced`

- **The comparison.** At `p = q = m`, `μ = (2m−2)/(2(m−1)²) = 1/(m−1)` and `λ_m − μ = (m−2)/(2(m−1)) − 1/(m−1) = (m−4)/(2(m−1))`, nonnegative iff `m ≥ 4`. The Lean cross-multiplies by the positive denominators and closes with `(m−1)²(m−4) ≥ 0`, which is the same inequality up to a positive factor. Correct, with the tie at `m = 4` as the record says.
- **"`λ ≠ ½`" for "off the orbit tangent".** At the level of eigenvectors this is right at `m ≥ 4`: `L27_half_span` identifies the `½`-eigenspace in `W^⊥` with the span of `crossOrbit T t`, and an eigenvector with `λ ≠ ½` is orthogonal to that span by `LeLin_selfAdj`. So "an eigenvector of `L_e` in `W^⊥` off the orbit tangent has eigenvalue at most `λ_m`" is fully supported.
- **Eigenvalues are not the quadratic form.** The record's claim is that the Hessian **transverse to the orbit**, a quadratic form on the orthogonal complement of the orbit tangent in `W^⊥`, is at most `−6θ/(n−2)`. A vector `v ⊥ orbit tangent` is not in general an eigenvector. The passage needs: (a) `LeRestr` on `weylPerpR M` is symmetric (`LeRestr_isSymmetric`, typed), hence diagonalisable in an orthonormal eigenbasis (Mathlib's `LinearMap.IsSymmetric.eigenvectorBasis`, available, not used); (b) the complement of the orbit tangent is the sum of the eigenspaces with eigenvalue `≠ ½` (from `L27_half_span`, not composed); (c) on that sum `⟨L_e v, v⟩ ≤ λ_m ‖v‖²` (from `L3_gap_balanced` per eigenvector, not composed); (d) `hessQ M v = ⟨6θ(L_e − ½)v, v⟩` (`hessQ_op`, typed, but its hypotheses are `normSq2 a = 1`, `Qop2 a = lam • a`, `Pcubic2 a ≠ 0`, and a bridge from L3Gap's `restrict (diagOp_isWeyl …)` with `hn` to those hypotheses is not written). None of (a)–(d)'s composition is in the commit. The docstring's "so the Hessian … is at most … off the orbit tangent" and the INDEX's matching sentence assert the composed statement.
- **Verdict.** The theorem as typed (eigenvalue bound) is CONFIRMED. The "so the Hessian" clause is REFUTED AS STATED as a claim of what is machine-checked: it is the correct mathematical consequence, reached by a standard step, but that step is untyped. Everything it needs is in the repository or in Mathlib, so this is a bounded formalisation task, not an `out`.

## 4. Attack line D, the INDEX L3 row

- **"The Hessian half, re-filed from `out` to proved".** The record's Hessian half is the sentence "The Hessian transverse to $\mathcal O$ is at most $-6\theta/(n-2)$, and the bound is attained." What is proved is the eigenvalue gap. Untyped: the spectral composition of §3 (a)–(d), and the attainment on $S^2_0(\mathbb R^m)$. The row names attainment as owed ("statement: not typed") but not the composition, so the re-filing overclaims. REFUTED AS STATED. **Repair**: either file the half as "eigenvalue gap proved, quadratic-form bound owed (spectral step, Bucket: typeable)" and add the step to the owed list, or type it: a theorem `L3_hess_transverse` stating `hessQ M v ≤ −6θ/(n−2) · ‖v‖²` for `v ∈ weylPerpR M` orthogonal to `span (range (crossOrbit T t))`, from `eigenvectorBasis`, `L27_half_span`, `L3_gap_balanced` and `hessQ_op`.
- **Attainment.** Attainment needs an explicit `D` in the diagonal sector with `λ = λ_m`, i.e. a weight `u` in the `θλ_p`-eigenspace of `KL`. `dn_excl_fox` gives only the inclusion of the spectrum in a five-point set, not that `λ_p` occurs. The INDEX is honest that this is not typed. Nothing in the commit proves it, correctly unclaimed.
- **The local-maximum clause `out`.** The record's strict local maximum transverse to the orbit needs a normal form of `P` near a compact critical orbit, a Morse–Bott lemma or a tubular neighbourhood with a uniform second-order Taylor bound across the orbit. Mathlib has neither a tubular neighbourhood theorem for submanifolds nor a Morse–Bott lemma, so `out` is justified as a bucket. **Observation (O3)**: the repository already contains `no_critical_in_tube_Le` and `taylor_bound` ([L217Le.lean](../lean/HamiltonsCubic/Results/L217Le.lean)), an analytic step at a critical point from a spectral gap along rays. A route through compactness of `O(n)` and a Taylor bound uniform over the orbit, with the normal slice given by the orthogonal complement of the orbit tangent at each orbit point, may avoid the manifold machinery. That makes `out` a judgement about effort rather than a hard absence, and the row could say so.

- **O3.** The `out` bucket for the local-maximum clause is defensible, but the repository's `taylor_bound` route with compactness of the orbit may reach it without a tubular neighbourhood theorem. Say "no Mathlib Morse–Bott, route via uniform Taylor bound untried" rather than presenting `out` as forced.
- **O4.** The docstring of `L3_gap_balanced` says "so the Hessian … is at most … there". Reword to "every eigenvalue of the Hessian on an eigenvector off the orbit tangent is at most …" until the spectral step is typed.
- **O5.** The M-to-`LeRestr` bridge (hypotheses `normSq2 M = 1`, `Qop2 M = lam • M`, `Pcubic2 M ≠ 0` from `hn`, `sphere_isCritical`, `hPM`) is the one piece of glue the composed theorem needs beyond Mathlib. It should be named in the owed list.
