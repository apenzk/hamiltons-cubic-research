**One-line summary: CONFIRMED WITH GAP.** `tF_le` and `tF_eq_iff` are Lemma L54's item 3 in the record's norm and its sense of coordinate-diagonal, on weaker hypotheses than the lemma states, and the gap is two neighbouring texts that still call the bound untyped plus the row's pending tag.

## 1. $t_F$ and the norm

The record defines $t_F=\sum_{a\ne b}K_{ab}^2$ with $K_{ab}=W_{abab}$. `Kmat R a a = inner2 (R (phi a a)) (phi a a)`, and `phi a a = 0` makes the second slot zero, so the diagonal vanishes for every `R`, linear or not. The screen `Kmat R a a = 0` elaborates with no hypothesis on `R`. Summing over all `a, b` is therefore the record's $t_F$.

The record's norm is the Frobenius norm of the operator on $\Lambda^2$, one term per pair of unordered index pairs, and L94's statement warns that the four-tensor norm would make the bound slack by a factor $4$. `inner2 X Y = ½ tr(XᵀY)` gives `phi i j` unit norm for $i\ne j$, so `innerOp R R`, which halves a sum over ordered $(i,j)$, is $\sum_{i<j}\lVert M\varphi_{ij}\rVert^2=\lVert M\rVert_F^2$. `innerOp_R4` rewrites it as $\tfrac14\sum_{ijkl}W_{ijkl}^2$, and the factor $4$ is exactly the two pair antisymmetries, so `2 * normSq R` is the record's $2\lVert M\rVert_F^2$ and not the slack four-tensor version.

## 2. Coordinate-diagonal

The record's step (3) reads the equality case as "every off-diagonal entry of $M$ in the pair basis of $F$ vanishes, which is $W$ coordinate-diagonal in $F$". `IsCoordDiag` quantifies over all ordered quadruples. On a row $i\ne j$ its excluded entries are $W_{ijkl}$ with $(k,l)\notin\{(i,j),(j,i)\}$, which split into $k=l$ (zero for every `R`), $k<l$ with $\{k,l\}\ne\{i,j\}$ (the off-diagonal entries of $M$), and $k>l$ (their negatives). On a row $i=j$ every entry is zero once `R` is linear. So under the hypotheses of `tF_eq_iff`, `IsCoordDiag R` holds exactly when $M$ is diagonal in the pair basis.

L94Statement's `CoordinateDiagonal` is stated on a four-index array with the same excluded set. The screen proves `(∀ i j k l, ¬((k = i ∧ l = j) ∨ (k = j ∧ l = i)) → W i j k l = 0) ↔ CoordinateDiagonal W` on `Fin n` arrays by `tauto`, so the two definitions are one predicate on two carriers.

## 3. Hypotheses

`row_ge` (each pair-basis row carries at least $2K_{ij}^2$) uses `R4_anti2`, which holds for any `R`, and `R4_self_left` at $i=j$, which needs linearity. `tF_le` then needs `innerOp_R4`, whose hypothesis is that `R` maps skew matrices to skew matrices. Without it `normSq` would also count the symmetric part of $R\varphi_{ij}$, which lies outside $\Lambda^2$. That is the record's setting, $M$ acting on $\Lambda^2$. The record's argument is the elementary inequality "squared diagonal at most squared Frobenius norm", which holds for any square matrix, so self-adjointness, first Bianchi, Ricci-flatness, $n\ge4$ and $\lVert M\rVert_F=1$ all drop out, and the Lean statement is stronger than item 3 in each.
