### Claim 3, the psd screen is sound, and the leading-minor shortcut is genuinely wrong, CONFIRMED

Script: [c3_psd_minors.py](../scripts/reviewers/r246/c3_psd_minors.py), exit 0, exact arithmetic throughout.

**The criterion is correct.** For symmetric $A$, $\det(tI+A)=t^3+E_1t^2+E_2t+E_3$ with $E_k$ the sum of the $k\times k$ principal minors; all $E_k\ge0$ forbids any eigenvalue $<0$, and the converse is inheritance of psd-ness by principal submatrices. The screen's `is_psd` in [run_a2pp_constant_t.py](../scripts/runs/run_a2pp_constant_t.py) computes all seven principal minors of the $3\times3$ (three $1\times1$, three $2\times2$, the determinant, formulas checked term by term against the symmetric cofactor expansion) and agrees with sympy's eigenvalue-based `is_positive_semidefinite` on 2407 exact cases including rank-0/1/2 boundary Grams, where the distinction lives.

**The plant the brief asked for.** Two non-psd matrices pass the LEADING-minor test and are rejected by the screen's full test: $\mathrm{diag}(0,-1,0)$ (leading minors $0,0,0$; eigenvalue $-1$) and $[[1,1,1],[1,1,1],[1,1,0]]$ (leading minors $1,0,0$; eigenvalue $1-\sqrt3<0$). A random hunt found 726 further separating matrices, eigenvalues siding with the full test in every one. Sylvester's leading-minor criterion certifies positive DEFINITEness only; for SEMIdefiniteness the screen's all-principal-minors form is required, and the screen has the required form.

**Rider, the scan, not the criterion.** `psd_in_kernel_span` scans only $\pm$(basis vectors) of the kernel span, and that scan is incomplete on spans of dimension $\ge2$: $\mathrm{span}\{\mathrm{diag}(1,-1,0),\mathrm{diag}(-1,2,0)\}$ has no psd $\pm$basis vector yet contains psd $\mathrm{diag}(0,1,0)$. The code's comment "check the rank-1 candidates and simple combos" claims combos it never forms. It does not bite here: on the screen's own points (seed 4, same draw order) the honest branch's nullspace is exactly $0$-dimensional (`None` is then a proof, not a scan result) and the plant's is exactly $1$-dimensional (rank-1, annihilating $(1,2,0)$), where the $\pm$ scan IS complete. If the screen is ever re-seeded or widened, the scan must become a real psd-feasibility check or the honest branch's assert can pass vacuously on a missed psd element.
