**The constant $\kappa$ is what fails.** The estimate needs $\lVert\pi^\perp(N\circ N)\rVert\le\kappa\lVert N\rVert^2$, so it needs the supremum. **The script produces a lower bound**, by $2000$ random samples and four ascents, which the table labels honestly as $\kappa\ge$ and $\gamma/\kappa\le$.
