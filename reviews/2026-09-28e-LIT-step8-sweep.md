Since $\#$ preserves positive operators, with $W=W_+-W_-$, $E=E_+-E_-+3\langle W_+\#W_-,W_-\rangle-3\langle W_+\#W_+,W_-\rangle$ with the last term $\le0$ and $E_\pm=\langle W_\pm\#W_\pm,W_\pm\rangle-\tfrac12\mathop{\mathrm{tr}}W_\pm^3$, so a large $E$ asks the positive part's $\#$ cubic to be large. *This routes the node through a classification of semisimple subalgebras of $\mathfrak{so}(n)$ carrying such idempotents, which is where I117 stands off the torus families: no method there either.*
- **The block equations** (candidate 3): in the [direct-attack note](2026-09-28d-I121-smallmass-direct.md), Gate 2 confirmed on their form ([r1851](REVIEW_r1851.md), [r1852](REVIEW_r1852.md)). ## The pick closes: the block equations add nothing past Euler and the matching cap (derived)

- **Summed over a block's three pairings, the visible projections are isotropic.** $\sum_\pi4X_\pi(u)X_\pi(v)=\tfrac32\langle u,v\rangle$ on $V_Q$, the three $X$-axes lying at equal angles ([r1851](REVIEW_r1851.md): $\sum_\pi4X_\pi^2=\tfrac32\lVert\nu_Q\rVert^2$, polarised). So pairing every block equation with its visible components and summing is $\tfrac32$ times the Euler identity $\theta\lVert B_f\rVert^2=2\langle A,D(B_f)\rangle+P(B_f)$, and the "twisted terms" are that identity rearranged.
- **Restricted to one matching $M$**, the block equations give $24T_M$ plus the rectangles leaving $M$ and the other pairings' terms equal to $\theta E(M)$ less the $A$ terms, a bound of order $\theta t^2$ on a cubic whose trivial cap is already $\lVert X_M\rVert_F^3\le(t^2/2)^{3/2}$.
- **So the block equations, isotropically or per matching, do not bound the visible cubic past what Euler and the matching inequality give.** *What they would need is a weighting of pairings that is neither isotropic nor a single matching and that the maximal frame singles out, and none is in hand.*

**All three candidates are closed or waiting**: the Kneser bound cannot suffice alone, the block equations add nothing past Euler, and the $\#$ route waits on I117's classification. The node is reduced (at pair-free $W$, $n\ge14$) to the visible cubic in a globally maximal frame, with no method.

## Batch 2, after the manager's decision (aimed at the general inequality $E(W)\le Ct_{\max}(W)$): a duality route (derived, unreviewed)

- **$t_{\max}$ is a support function.** $t_{\max}(W)=\sup_{g\in O(n),\,D}\langle W,gD\rangle$ over unit diagonal $D$, since $\lVert\pi_{\mathcal D}(gW)\rVert=\sup_D\langle gW,D\rangle$. So $t_{\max}$ is the support function of $K=\mathop{\mathrm{conv}}(O(n)\cdot S_{\mathcal D})$, the convex hull of the rotated unit diagonal tensors, an $O(n)$-invariant body in $\mathcal W_n$, and it is the dual norm of the gauge $\lVert\cdot\rVert_K$.
- **$E$ is a pairing with a quadratic.** $P(W)=\langle W,Q(W)\rangle$ and $\mathop{\mathrm{tr}}W^3=\langle W,\pi(W^2)\rangle$ with $\pi$ the projection to $\mathcal W_n$, so $E(W)=\langle W,\Phi(W)\rangle$ with $\Phi(W)=W^\#-\tfrac12\pi(W^2)$.
- **Hence $E(W)\le\lVert\Phi(W)\rVert_K\,t_{\max}(W)$.** The inequality holds with constant $C$ wherever $\Phi(W)\in C\,K$, so **the route is to show that the quadratic map $\Phi$ sends the unit sphere into a bounded multiple of the diagonal body**. *The trivial bound $\lVert X\rVert_K\le\lVert X\rVert/r_n$ with $K$'s inradius $r_n$ (the least $t_{\max}$ of a unit tensor, of order $\tfrac1n$ by Schur's average) loses a factor $n$, so the content is that $\Phi$'s image is structured: $\Phi(e)$ is diagonal at the product, and $W^\#$ is a sum of rank-one terms on brackets of eigenvectors.*
- **A first test says little** ([gauge lower bound at $n=8$](../scripts/misc/screen_I121_E_vs_tmax.py), via $\lVert X\rVert_K\ge\lVert X\rVert^2/t_{\max}(X)$): the product gives $1.064$, equal to its $E$, and three random unit tensors give $0.43$ to $0.51$. At $n=8$ random tensors already have $t_{\max}\approx0.57$, so the dimension where the $n$-loss would show is out of reach of this ascent.
- **Why the uniform version probably fails, by hand.** $W^\#=\tfrac12\sum_{k,l}\lambda_k\lambda_l[\varphi_k,\varphi_l]\otimes[\varphi_k,\varphi_l]$ over eigenvectors, a sum of rank-one terms on brackets. A rank-one term $\pi(u\otimes u)$ has gauge about $\lVert u\rVert^2$ when $u$ is decomposable, being diagonal in an adapted frame. When $u$ is spread, as the Kähler form $\omega=\sum_ie_{2i-1,2i}$ is, $\omega\otimes\omega/m$ has diagonal mass about $\tfrac1m$ in the unitary frame, so its gauge is of order $\sqrt m$ if that frame is near-maximal. $\mathbb{CP}^m$-like tensors carry such terms, so **a bound on $\lVert\Phi(W)\rVert_K$ uniform over the unit sphere is unlikely**, and the route would have to use the part of $\Phi(W)$ that correlates with $W$, where $\mathbb{CP}^m$ contributes little ($E\to0$).

## Batch 3: polarity, and the pair-only sub-case

- **Polar representations** (the frame problem read as "how close does the orbit come to a Cartan-like subspace", $\mathcal D_n$ being the sign group's fixed space): searched, and the record already holds the negative, [Richard 2014's card](../references/richard-2014-curvature-cones-ricci-flow/README.md): no paper treats $(O(n),\mathcal W_n)$ as polar or with a section technique. [Deninger–Grundhöfer–Kramer](../references/deninger-grundhoefer-kramer-2023-srg-quadratic/README.md), which surfaced again, is on file.
- **The pair-only sub-case hits the same barrier** (derived here). With no four-set part, $E(B_p)$ is the pair-triangle sum of the [exact pair part](2026-09-28d-I121-smallmass-direct.md), bounded by a constant times $\big(\sum_{jk}\lVert x^{jk}\rVert^2\big)^{3/2}$ (a weighted triangle count is at most the Frobenius norm cubed), and single-plane maximality caps $\sum\lVert x^{jk}\rVert^2$ by $\tfrac12(n-1)t^2$. So $E(B_p)\lesssim n^{3/2}t^3$ and $E\ge\varepsilon$ gives only $t\gtrsim n^{-1/2}$. *Every piece of $E$ in a maximal frame, visible cubic and pair part alike, meets the $n^{-1/2}$ scale under the constraints single rotations and single matchings supply.*
