## A. Fidelity and hypotheses

- **A-1. The `SRG` structure** ([L144Statement.lean:244–268](../lean/HamiltonsCubic/Results/L144Statement.lean)). Confirmed. The fields are the standard ones: $v,k,\lambda,\mu\in\mathbb Z$, the counting identity $k(k-\lambda-1)=(v-k-1)\mu$, the restricted eigenvalues as the roots of $x^2-(\lambda-\mu)x-(k-\mu)$ through `hsum` and `hprod`, and $f,g\in\mathbb Z$ pinned by $f+g=v-1$ and $\operatorname{tr}A=k+fr+gs=0$. Every strongly regular graph with $0<k<v-1$ satisfies every field, the imprimitive ones included ($r=0$ at $\mu=k$ still meets `hr : 0 ≤ r`). Nothing forces vacuity: $r\ge0>s$ makes the roots distinct, so `hmult` and `htrace` determine $f,g$, and `clebsch` at [:717](../lean/HamiltonsCubic/Results/L144Statement.lean) inhabits the primitive class. $f,g\ge0$ is not required, which only strengthens the theorem. The structure quantifies over feasible parameter sets rather than graphs, which is again the stronger statement.
- **A-2. `Primitive`** ([:271](../lean/HamiltonsCubic/Results/L144Statement.lean)). Confirmed. For $0<k<v-1$ the graph is disconnected exactly when $\mu=0$ and the complement exactly when $\mu=k$, so $0<\mu<k$ is "graph and complement connected". Both halves are used: $\mu<k$ gives $rs<0$ hence $r>0$, and $\mu>0$ excludes $s=-1$.
- **A-3. `thetaSq`** ([:274–276](../lean/HamiltonsCubic/Results/L144Statement.lean)). Confirmed. It is the square of the record's $\lvert1+\lambda-\mu\rvert\sqrt{2(n-1)/(nk(n-1-k))}$ at $n=v$, and `l74Bridge_holds` ties it to the critical multiplier through `thetaSRG_sq_eq_L144_form`.
- **A-4. The identity** ([:369–370](../lean/HamiltonsCubic/Results/L144Statement.lean)). Confirmed by hand. $(f+g)(r+s)=(v-1)(\lambda-\mu)$ and $fr+gs=-k$ give $fs+gr=(v-1)(\lambda-\mu)+k$, and $(f-g)(r-s)=(fr+gs)-(fs+gr)=-2k-(v-1)(\lambda-\mu)$. The `linear_combination` coefficients match.
- **A-5. The conference case** ([:372–388](../lean/HamiltonsCubic/Results/L144Statement.lean)). Confirmed. $f=g$ gives $(v-1)(\mu-\lambda)=2k\in(0,2(v-1))$, so the integer $\mu-\lambda$ is $1$, the numerator $1+\lambda-\mu$ vanishes and $\theta=0<\tfrac12$. These are exactly the conference parameters $2k=v-1$, $\lambda=\mu-1$.
- **A-6. The integral case** ([:389–421](../lean/HamiltonsCubic/Results/L144Statement.lean)). Confirmed. With $f\ne g$, $r-s$ is rational and $r+s=\lambda-\mu$ is an integer, so $r$ is rational, and as a root of the monic $x^2-(\lambda-\mu)x-(k-\mu)$ it is an integer by `rat_root_int` (a correct rational-root argument: $\operatorname{den}q\mid\operatorname{num}q^2$ with the two coprime). $s=\lambda-\mu-r$ is then an integer. $r\ge1$ from $rs=\mu-k<0$ and $s<0$. **$s=-1$ is excluded by primitivity**: it gives $r=k-\mu$ and $r-1=\lambda-\mu$, so $\lambda=k-1$, and the counting identity reads $0=(v-k-1)\mu$ with $v-k-1>0$, forcing $\mu=0$. Multiplicity integrality is used once, at `hrq`, as the commit says.
- **A-7. The inequality** ([:301–341](../lean/HamiltonsCubic/Results/L144Statement.lean), [:422–435](../lean/HamiltonsCubic/Results/L144Statement.lean)). Confirmed. With $B=-s-1\ge1$: $k=\mu-rs=\mu+r(B+1)$, $k-\lambda-1=r(B+1)-r-s-1=(r+1)B$, and $1+\lambda-\mu=1+r+s=r-B$. The counting identity gives $\mu(v-1-k)=k(r+1)B$, so after multiplying by $\mu>0$ the goal $\theta^2<\tfrac14$ is $8(v-1)(r-B)^2\mu<vk^2(r+1)B$. AM–GM on $k=\mu+r(B+1)$ gives $k^2\ge4\mu r(B+1)$, and `poly_le`, $2(r-B)^2\le rB(r+1)(B+1)$, holds on each side of $r=B$ since $(r-B)^2\le\max(r,B)^2$ and the other factor pair is at least $2$. Chaining gives $8(r-B)^2\mu\le k^2(r+1)B$, and strictness comes from $v-1<v$ against the positive right side. The route differs from the record's outline (no complement invariance, no affine-in-$\mu$ step) and is self-contained.
- **A-8. "Sharp, never attained".** Confirmed consistent. "Never attained" is the strict $<$, which the theorem gives, and the conference case sits at $0$, below the bound rather than at it, so it neither attains nor contradicts the supremum. Sharpness (that $\tfrac14$ is the supremum) is not typed and the file says so at [:139–140](../lean/HamiltonsCubic/Results/L144Statement.lean). *A naming hazard, no gap*: the record's box says "conference graphs giving $f=0$ exactly" with $f$ the numerator, while the file's $f$ is a multiplicity and its conference case is $f=g$.
