# R32, the bounded session (2026-09-25): invariant curvature cones for the value bound

**Hold lifted by the user 2026-09-25** for one bounded session, derivation and reading only.

**What the record already held.** [Beitz's notes](../references/beitz-2018-bianchi-convexity/notes.md): Böhm–Wilking conjecture (Remark 6.3.15) that for $n\ge12$ the cones $\Omega_a=\{(\tfrac{n-2}4+a)\lVert R\rVert^2\le\lVert\mathrm{ric}\,R\rVert^2,\ \mathrm{scal}>0\}$ are invariant under $\dot R=R^2+R^\#$ for $a\in[0,\tfrac n4]$, and **P is exactly the left-hand side of their invariance criterion** (Lemma 2.3.4), Weyl tensors being the cone family's extremal directions. Beitz proves invariance only near $a=\tfrac n4$, by a local Hessian at $I$. *So R32's cone question is P restated unless the cone machinery supplies invariance without the bound.*

**The one mechanism that would: Wilking cones.** Every Wilking cone $C(S)$ is Ricci-flow invariant (Theorem 2.10 of [the Lie-algebraic cones source](../references/wilking-lie-algebraic-cones/notes.md)), and for $n\ge5$ every Wilking cone lies in $C_{IC}$ (Theorem 3.1). **$\Omega_0\not\subseteq C_{IC}$ at every $n\ge6$**, so $\Omega_0$ is no Wilking cone: $R=I+tW$ with $W$ a unit Weyl tensor lies in $\Omega_0$ for $t^2\le\tfrac{nN}{n-2}$, $N=\tfrac{n(n-1)}2$ (the decomposition form of $\Omega_a$ in [problem.md](../findings/problem.md)); $I$ adds $4$ to every isotropic curvature and $W$ adds $t\,\mathrm{IC}(W)$; a unit Weyl tensor on $\mathbb R^6$ with $\min\mathrm{IC}=-0.902$ (a screen, Nelder–Mead over frames on random Weyl tensors), padded into $\mathbb R^n$ with norm and isotropic curvature unchanged, gives $4-0.902\,t_{\max}(n)<0$ from $n=6$ ($t_{\max}(6)=4.74$, $t_{\max}(12)=8.90$). *A screen-found witness; an exact one is owed before this becomes a fence post.*

**Outcome B, as agreed: no mechanism.** The cone formulation is P restated at $a=0$, Wilking's automatic invariance cannot reach $a=0$, and Böhm–Wilking's own method reaches only the endpoint $a=\tfrac n4$ (Beitz). The conjecture has stood unpublished with its authors. **Per the session's terms, both loops pause and what is proved is written up.**
