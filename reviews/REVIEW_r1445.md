## A. L71's inclusions

**The record** ([bounds.md](../findings/bounds.md) lines 676 to 680): *"Write $\mathfrak m=E_{\min}$, $\mathfrak h=E_{\max}$ and $\mathfrak r$ for everything else (the other eigenspaces, kernel included). Lemma L70's equality case says $\mathrm{ad}(v)$ is supported on $E_{\min}\otimes E_{\max}$ for every $v\in\mathfrak m$, which reads $[\mathfrak m,\mathfrak m]\subseteq\mathfrak h,\ [\mathfrak m,\mathfrak h]\subseteq\mathfrak m,\ [\mathfrak m,\mathfrak r]=0$. Machine-checked in the eigenframe of a saturating critical point: `L71_mm`, `L71_mh`, `L71_mr`."* The lemma's hypotheses (line 692): a critical point at unit norm, $n\ge5$, $\lambda_{\min}<0<\lambda_{\max}$, saturating L70.

**The Lean.** In the spectral-theorem eigenframe `frameVec` of a linear Weyl operator with eigenvalues $\mu$, under `IsCritical W θ`, `lmin < 0 < lmax`, `lmin ≤ μ ≤ lmax` and `hsat : θ = lmin + (n−2)·lmax`:
- `L71_mm`: $\mu_\gamma=\mu_a=$ `lmin` and $c_{\gamma ab}\ne0$ give $\mu_b=$ `lmax`,
- `L71_mh`: $\mu_\gamma=$ `lmin`, $\mu_a=$ `lmax` and $c_{\gamma ab}\ne0$ give $\mu_b=$ `lmin`,
- `L71_mr`: $\mu_\gamma=$ `lmin` and $\mu_a\notin\{$`lmin`, `lmax`$\}$ give $[v_\gamma,v_a]=0$ on the nose.

**Frame components give the subspace inclusions.** `EigenFrame.complete` writes every skew $X$ as $\sum_b\langle X,v_b\rangle v_b$, and $[v_\gamma,v_a]$ is skew (`br_skew`), so $[v_\gamma,v_a]=\sum_bc_{\gamma ab}v_b$. `L71_mm` says every $b$ with $c_{\gamma ab}\ne0$ has $\mu_b=\lambda_{\max}$, so $[v_\gamma,v_a]$ lies in the span of the frame vectors at $\lambda_{\max}$, which is $E_{\max}$ because the frame is an orthonormal eigenbasis. $\mathfrak m$ is the span of the frame vectors at $\lambda_{\min}$, and the bracket is bilinear, so $[\mathfrak m,\mathfrak m]\subseteq\mathfrak h$. The same argument gives the other two. **That bilinear step is one line and is not typed**, and bounds.md line 680 sits under the subspace display without saying the Lean stops at frame vectors. The L71 row says "every frame component", which is exact. *A reader-level gap, not a defect of the statement.*

**$\mathfrak r$ is "the other eigenspaces, kernel included".** `L71_mr` quantifies over every frame index $a$ with $\mu_a\ne$ `lmin` and $\mu_a\ne$ `lmax`. Since `lmin` $<0<$ `lmax`, every kernel index qualifies, and so does every intermediate eigenvalue of either sign. That is the record's $\mathfrak r$ exactly.

**The binders pin $\lambda_{\min}$ and $\lambda_{\max}$.** `hγ` with `hlo` makes `lmin` the attained minimum. `hhi` makes `lmax` an upper bound only, but the hypotheses force attainment: if no $\mu_b$ equalled `lmax`, `support_of_saturation` would leave no nonzero $c_{\gamma ab}$ at all, so $\mathrm{ad}(v_\gamma)=0$, against the Killing row mass $\sum c^2=2(n-2)>0$ (`killing_frame`) at $n\ge3$. So on every satisfiable instance `lmax` is $\lambda_{\max}$, $\mathfrak h$ is $E_{\max}$, and $\mathfrak r$ cannot silently swallow $E_{\max}$.

**Hypotheses.** Unit norm and $n\ge5$ are absent, and neither is needed for the inclusions: they are homogeneous, and the record uses $n\ge5$ only later, for simplicity of $\mathfrak{so}(n)$. `hc`, `hneg`, `hpos`, `hlo`, `hhi`, `hsat` all feed `support_of_saturation`, and `hneg`, `hpos` also separate `lmin` from `lmax` in the `absurd` branches. None is vacuous: the witnesses below meet them jointly.

- $S^2\times S^2$, $n=4$, normalised: $\lambda_{\max}=2/\sqrt{12}$ on $\{e_{12},e_{34}\}$, $\lambda_{\min}=-1/\sqrt{12}$ on the four mixed planes, and $\lambda_{\min}+2\lambda_{\max}=3/\sqrt{12}=\theta_0(4)=\theta$, so it saturates. $[e_{13},e_{14}]\propto e_{34}\in\mathfrak h$, $[e_{13},e_{12}]\propto e_{23}\in\mathfrak m$, and $\mathfrak r=0$.

## B. L119

**The Lean.** `L119_every`: $4P(W)\le(-m)\sum_{\lambda<0}g-c\sum_{\lambda>0}g$ for any EigenFrame, any $m\le\lambda$ on the spectrum and any $c\le\lambda$ on the strictly positive part.

**$M$ and $\Delta$.** $\sum_{\lambda>0}g$ is $\Delta$ by definition. $\sum_{\lambda<0}g=\sum g-\Delta-G_0$, and `first_identity` gives $\sum g=4(n-1)\lVert W\rVert^2$, which is $4(n-1)$ at unit norm, so $\sum_{\lambda<0}g=M$ there. With $m=\lambda_{\min}$ and $c=\lambda_{\mathfrak p}^{\min}$ the Lean is the record's box at unit norm, with $P=\theta$ by `theta_of_unit`.

**The sense of $\mathfrak m$.** r613's callout (line 335) says the identity $4\theta=A-B$ is false under conventions.md's $\mathfrak m=E_{\min}$ and needs $\mathfrak m=\{\lambda<0\}$. The Lean filters on `lam γ < 0`, the correct sense.

**The unit-norm reading.** The Lean form is homogeneous of degree $3$ ($P$ cubic, $\lambda$ linear, $g$ quadratic), so it holds at every scale with no norm hypothesis. The record's form substitutes the constant $4(n-1)$ for $\sum g$ and the scale-free $\theta$ for $P$, and that substitution is what needs $\lVert W\rVert=1$. The INDEX row says "at unit norm", which is right. **Its audit sentence is not**: *"with the left side cubic and the right quadratic"*. Under the record's form the left side $\theta$ has degree $0$ and the right side mixes degree $1$ ($\lvert\lambda_{\min}\rvert\cdot4(n-1)$) with degree $3$ ($\lvert\lambda_{\min}\rvert(\Delta+G_0)$, $\lambda_{\mathfrak p}^{\min}\Delta$). Under the $P$ form both sides are cubic. No reading gives cubic against quadratic. The conclusion the sentence draws (unit norm is load-bearing) is right: at scale $s$ the right side is $s\lvert\lambda_{\min}\rvert4(n-1)-s^3(\ldots)$ and goes negative once $\Delta+G_0>0$, while $\theta$ is fixed.

**Hypotheses.** `hlo` bounds the negative part below, `hc` the positive part, and the kernel drops out by $\lambda=0$. Both are used. `hc` holds vacuously for any $c$ when the positive part is empty, which happens only at $W=0$ by tracelessness, where both sides are $0$.

## E. L117's count

**The record** ([bounds.md](../findings/bounds.md) line 734): *"$\theta=\theta_0(n)$ exactly when $W$ has two eigenvalues $\lambda_{\min}<0<\lambda_{\max}$ and no kernel, and then $\dim E_{\min}=n^2/4$ and $\dim E_{\max}=n(n-2)/4$, so $n$ is even ... $\mathop{\mathrm{tr}}W=0$ with $(n-2)\lambda_{\max}=-n\lambda_{\min}$ fixes the two multiplicities. **The Lean.** `L117_counts`, under L117's saturation hypotheses and `htwo` (every eigenvalue is `lmin` or `lmax`), gives $4\#\{\mu=\text{lmax}\}=n(n-2)$ and $4\#\{\mu=\text{lmin}\}=n^2$ in `ℕ`. The proof is the record's: `L117_mass` gives $(n-2)\lambda_{\max}=-n\lambda_{\min}$, `sum_mu_zero` gives $\#A\lambda_{\max}+\#B\lambda_{\min}=0$, and `card_tri` gives $\#A+\#B=\binom n2$. By hand: $\#A\,n=\#B(n-2)$ and $\#A+\#B=n(n-1)/2$ give $\#B=n^2/4$, $\#A=n(n-2)/4$. At $n=4$ that is $4$ and $2$, the $S^2\times S^2$ screen of §A. `L117_no_eq_odd` concludes that `htwo` fails at odd $n$, from $4\#B=n^2$ forcing $n^2$ even.
