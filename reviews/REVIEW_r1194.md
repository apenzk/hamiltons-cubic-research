**The distinguishing object.** The Lean statement would differ from the record's claim if there were (a) a local maximiser of $P$ on the Weyl unit sphere with $\theta>0$ that fails `hcrit`, so the Lean theorem misses a case the record covers, or (b) a unit $Y\in W^\perp\cap\mathcal W_n$ with $\langle L_eY,Y\rangle\le\frac12$ for every such $Y$ yet $\lambda_{\max}(L_e\vert_{W^\perp})>\frac12$. I looked for (a) by asking whether Weyl-sphere criticality is only projected: L209 ([INDEX-results.md](../findings/INDEX-results.md) row 209, Gate 2 passed) gives $Q(\mathcal W_n)\subseteq\mathcal W_n$, so projected and unprojected criticality coincide and (a) is empty. Even without it, `hcrit` is used only for $\langle v,Q(a)\rangle=0$, which projected criticality also gives for Weyl $v$. For (b), Courant–Fischer for the self-adjoint operator $\pi_{\mathcal W}L_e$ on $W^\perp\cap\mathcal W_n$ rules it out. Neither exists. The Lean theorem is weaker than the record only by the untyped Hessian identity, simplicity of $1$, sharpness, and the $\lambda_{\max}$ passage.

**Verdict: CONFIRMED, the statement faithful as the quadratic-form reading, while the row's Not-typed list omits the $\lambda_{\max}$ passage and the module docstring is stale.**
