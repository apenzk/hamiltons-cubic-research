## A. L54's ψ-block splitting

**The record** ([bispectral.md](../findings/bispectral.md) line 486): *"$\psi_k=\mathop{\mathrm{diag}}(v_k)$ for an orthonormal **eigenbasis** $\{v_k\}$ of $K$ on $\mathbf1^\perp$ ... the $\psi$-block of $\mathring W$ is diagonal with $d_\psi=-(\text{eigenvalues of }K\text{ on }\mathbf1^\perp)$"*, with step (0) *"$K\mathbf1=0$ ... So $K$ preserves $\mathbf1^\perp$ and the eigenbasis exists."* Correction 1 (line 245): *"The $\psi$-block of $\mathring W$ is exactly $-K$ restricted to $\mathbf1^\perp$ (machine-checked in block form: `diag_block` ... with `K_compress` and `psi_one_null`)."*

**The ψ-block is −K on diagonal matrices.** `ringW R h` is $(i,j)\mapsto\sum_{k,l}R_{iklj}h_{kl}$, Li's `iklj` order, with $R_{ijkl}=\langle R\varphi_{ij},\varphi_{kl}\rangle$ (`R4`), and `Kmat R a b` is $R_{abab}=W_{abab}$. At $h=\mathop{\mathrm{diag}}(u)$ the $(i,i)$ entry is $\sum_kR_{ikki}u_k=-\sum_kR_{ikik}u_k=-(Ku)_i$ by pair antisymmetry, so $\langle\mathring W\mathop{\mathrm{diag}}(u),\mathop{\mathrm{diag}}(v)\rangle=-u^{\mathsf T}Kv$, which is `diag_block` for all $u,v\in\mathbb R^n$. `ringW` acts on all matrices, and $\mathring W$ on $S^2_0$ is its compression. For traceless $u$ and $v$ the trace part of `ringW` pairs to zero against $\mathop{\mathrm{diag}}(v)$, so the restriction of `diag_block` to $u,v\perp\mathbf1$ is the record's $\psi$-block.

**What `K_compress` and `psi_one_null` add.** `null_compress` is sound: $Bg=0$ and $B^{\mathsf T}=B$ give $BP=B$ and $PB=B$ directly. At $g=\mathbf1$, `K_compress` is $K=PKP$, so $K$ maps into $\mathbf1^\perp$ and kills $\mathbf1$, and in the decomposition $\mathbb R^n=\mathbf1^\perp\oplus\mathbb R\mathbf1$ the $\psi$-block $-K$ is $\begin{pmatrix}-K|_{\mathbf1^\perp}&0\\0&0\end{pmatrix}$. That is the record's block form and it is what licenses choosing the $\psi_k$ among eigenvectors of $K$ inside $\mathbf1^\perp$. `psi_one_null` is `diag_block` at $u=\mathbf1$ with `K_one`, the bottom row of that block, read on $\mathring W$ itself. **Correction 1's parenthesis is accurate.**

**The gap: the extra 0 is not typed as one extra eigenvalue.** The file header says *"so the $\psi$-block $-K$ over $\mathbb R^n$ is its restriction to $\mathbf1^\perp$ beside $0$ on $\mathbf1$"* and INDEX says the $\psi$-block over $\mathbb R^n$ is the record's *"with one extra $0$"*. As a block form this is `K_compress` with `K_one`. As a spectral statement (the multiset of eigenvalues of $K$ on $\mathbb R^n$ is that of $K|_{\mathbf1^\perp}$ plus one $0$) it is untyped. `K_zero_eigen` says only $\exists k,\ \kappa_k=0$ for Mathlib's eigenvalue list, which follows from $\det K=0$ and names no eigenvector. When $K|_{\mathbf1^\perp}$ itself has a zero eigenvalue (possible: $K=0$ at any $W$ with vanishing sectional curvatures in the frame, or a $K$ with a second null direction), Mathlib's eigenbasis may split the $0$-eigenspace in a basis not containing $\mathbf1/\sqrt n$, and then its $\mathop{\mathrm{diag}}(w_k)$ are not all traceless. This matters for [L54Rigid.lean](../lean/HamiltonsCubic/Results/L54Rigid.lean), whose `wK` is that basis. The sums of squares there are unaffected (the extra $0$ contributes nothing), but the Schur step lives on $S^2$ and not $S^2_0$. The rows declare the reading-off as prose ("by reading" on the L54 row, "the reading-off of $K'$ remaining prose" on the L52 row), so this is a recorded gap and not an overclaim.

**Hypotheses.** `hR` (linear), `hskew` (preserves bivectors), `hsym` (self-adjoint on bivectors) are used for the symmetry of `Kmat` (`Kmat_symm`) and inside `diag_block`. `hRic : ∀ a b, Ric R a b = 0` is used only at $a=b$ (`K_row_sum` reads `hRic a a`), so the statement asks for more than it uses. That is harmless since a Weyl $R$ meets it. `hn : 0 < n` in `K_zero_eigen` is needed ($\mathbf1\ne0$). `projAway_g` and `projAway_idem` need $g\cdot g\ne0$ and carry it. None is vacuous.

**Witness (screen).** In a scratch file, `diagOp (sphereWeight (firstP 4 2))`, the $S^2\times S^2$ weight on $n=4$, with `diagOp_isLinOp'` and the three components of `diagOp_isWeyl (sphereWeight_isWeight _ _ _)`, was passed to `K_compress`, `K_zero_eigen` and `psi_one_null`. It compiles, so the hypotheses are jointly satisfiable at a nonzero Weyl operator.

## C. L129's corollary (i)

## D. L146's in-block dominance
