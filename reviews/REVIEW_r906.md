## 5. The escaping and the tables

- **Integrity holds at every state.** Every line beginning with a pipe carries exactly three unescaped pipes, at 39cbaccf (425 lines), at cbddd1f6 and at 5bc25367. Section count equals separator count equals 52, and the row total matches the header's count. **No table is broken.**
- **The body escaping works.** Eleven bodies contain a pipe and `body.replace('|', '\\|')` renders each as a literal, with no doubling, since no note carries a pre-escaped `\|`.
- **The NAME is not escaped, which is latent rather than harmless.** `f"| **{name}** | ..."` passes `name` through untouched. No statement name currently holds a pipe, so no row is broken today. A heading or a bolded head containing one, `**Theorem 2 (the $a|b$ case)**` for instance, would split its row silently, and the heading patch has widened what lands in that field to arbitrary heading prose.
- **The escaping corrupts mathematics in 11 rows, and this is the one active defect here.** In LaTeX `\|` is the double-bar norm, so `$|SB|^2 \le 8|S|^2$` is stored and rendered as `$\|SB\|^2 \le 8\|S\|^2$`. [elhasan-li-npw-2026](../references/elhasan-li-npw-2026-calabi-operator/notes.md)'s Lemma 4.4, Lemma 4.5 and Remark 5.1 rows, and [huang-wang-2025](../references/huang-wang-2025-hopf-decomposition/notes.md)'s Weyl bound, all now display a norm of a different kind from the one the source states. A reader screening a bound against one of these reads the wrong object. The repair is `&#124;` or a math-safe delimiter rather than a backslash.
