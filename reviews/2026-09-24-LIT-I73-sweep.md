## Batch 1, approximate Clifford relations and commutator sums (web search)

- Queries: approximate representations of Clifford algebras / almost anticommuting matrices / quantitative Hurwitz–Radon; maximum sum of squared commutators of orthonormal skew matrices; maximal sectional-curvature sums of k-planes in compact Lie groups.
- [Pellegrino–Raposo, arXiv:2608.08246](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.08246): trilinear sign forms and Hurwitz–Radon extremizers. Abstract has no approximate-anticommutation stability statement. Not relevant to T.
- [Shen–Wang–Zhi, arXiv:2609.09938](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.09938) and [Tran, arXiv:2609.20161](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.20161): every traceless matrix is a commutator with a dimension-free operator-norm bound. A different question (representation, not a sum over a frame). Not relevant.
- [Bloch–Iserles, commutators of skew-symmetric matrices](https://www.damtp.cam.ac.uk/user/na/NA_papers/NA2004_04.pdf): $\lVert[X,Y]\rVert_F\le\sqrt2\lVert X\rVert_F\lVert Y\rVert_F$ on $\mathfrak{so}(n)$, one pair. Already implied by the record's pair caps.
- "On families of anticommuting matrices" (LAA 2015): classification of exactly anticommuting families. Exact, not quantitative.
- **Batch verdict: nothing found** on stability of Clifford relations in a form bounding $T$.

## Batch 2, DDVV constants depending on the family, and k-plane curvature sums (web search)

- Queries: DDVV skew with $m$-dependent constants; sectional-curvature sums of $k$-planes in $SO(n)$ ("partial scalar curvature"); subspaces maximising the bracket norm (octonions, $G_2$).
- [Ge–Tao–Zhou, arXiv:2410.17761](https://arxiv.org/abs/2410.17761), filed at [references/ge-tao-zhou-2024-austere-ddvv](../references/ge-tao-zhou-2024-austere-ddvv/notes.md): DDVV on the symmetric block spaces $Q(p,q)$, constant $\tfrac12$, and on $Q(n-1,1)$ the span-dependent constant $\tfrac{d-1}{2d}$. Symmetric, so no bound on $T$; its $Q(n-1,1)$ result is the symmetric analogue of the pencil frame and has the shape of L238's rank factor.
- [Onti–Polymerakis–Vlachos, arXiv:2406.11692](https://arxiv.org/abs/2406.11692): partial scalar curvatures of submanifolds. No algebraic lemma on $k$-frames.
- Searches on $k$-plane sectional-curvature maxima in compact Lie groups and on bracket-maximising subspaces returned only textbook curvature formulas. Nothing found.
- **Batch verdict: nothing found** that bounds $T$ on anisotropic seven-frames.
