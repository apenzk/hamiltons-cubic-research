## A. trace_negPart_compress

- **Statement.** Hypotheses: `hY : Y.IsSymmetric`, `hP : P.IsSymmetric`, `hPP : P ∘ₗ P = P`, and `hZ : (P ∘ₗ Y ∘ₗ P).IsSymmetric`. The last is a proof term the spectral function needs and follows from the first two, so it restricts nothing. Conclusion: `trace (specFn negPartScalar hZ) ≤ trace (P ∘ₗ specFn negPartScalar hY ∘ₗ P)`, which is $\operatorname{tr}((PYP)_-)\le\operatorname{tr}(PY_-P)$ with `negPartScalar x = (|x|−x)/2`, the record's convention. Symmetric plus idempotent is exactly an orthogonal projection. The statement is fully general.
- **Proof.** Both traces are read in the orthonormal eigenbasis $f_i$ of $Z=PYP$. The left term is $(\zeta_i)_-$. The right term is $\langle Pf_i,Y_-Pf_i\rangle$ after moving $P$ across by symmetry.
- **The $\zeta_i\ge0$ branch**, which contains $\zeta_i=0$: `negPartScalar_of_nonneg` makes the left term $0$ and `inner_specFn_nonneg` makes the right term $\ge0$, since $Y_-$ has nonnegative spectrum. The zero eigenvalue is handled here, and it needs no statement about where $f_i$ lies.
- **The $\zeta_i<0$ branch.** $P(Zf_i)=Zf_i$ from idempotence, so $\zeta_iPf_i=\zeta_if_i$ and $\zeta_i\ne0$ gives $Pf_i=f_i$. Then the left term is $-\zeta_i=-\langle f_i,Yf_i\rangle$ and `inner_add_negPart_nonneg` ($\langle w,Yw\rangle+\langle w,Y_-w\rangle=\langle w,Y_+w\rangle\ge0$, eigenvalue by eigenvalue $\lambda+\lambda_-=\lambda_+\ge0$) closes it.
- **Hand check 1**, the $\zeta=0$ case. $Y=\begin{pmatrix}0&1\\1&0\end{pmatrix}$, $P=e_1e_1^{\mathsf T}$. $PYP=0$, so the left side is $0$. $Y_-=\tfrac12\begin{pmatrix}1&-1\\-1&1\end{pmatrix}$ (eigenvalue $-1$ on $(1,-1)/\sqrt2$), so $PY_-P=\tfrac12e_1e_1^{\mathsf T}$ with trace $\tfrac12$. $0\le\tfrac12$. In the proof's basis both eigenvalues of $Z$ are $0$: $f=e_2$ gives $0\le0$ ($Pe_2=0$) and $f=e_1$ gives $0\le\tfrac12$.
- **Hand check 2**, the $\zeta<0$ case. $Y=\begin{pmatrix}-1&2\\2&0\end{pmatrix}$, same $P$. $PYP=\operatorname{diag}(-1,0)$, left side $1$. $Y$ has eigenvalues $\tfrac{-1\pm\sqrt{17}}2$, and the negative one $-\mu$, $\mu=\tfrac{1+\sqrt{17}}2$, has unit eigenvector with $v_1^2=\tfrac{4}{4+(\mu-1)^2}$. Using $\mu^2=\mu+4$, $4+(\mu-1)^2=9-\mu$, so $(PY_-P)_{11}=\mu v_1^2=\tfrac{4\mu}{9-\mu}\approx1.592$, and $\tfrac{4\mu}{9-\mu}\ge1$ exactly when $\mu\ge\tfrac95$, which holds. Right side $\approx1.592\ge1$. The inequality holds strictly, as it should when $Y$ couples $\operatorname{ran}P$ to its complement.

## B. The hypotheses of L17_at_u against the record

- **Bianchi.** `compOf H i j k l = ⟪φ_kl, H φ_ij⟫` is antisymmetric in $(i,j)$ and in $(k,l)$ because $\varphi$ is, and pair-symmetric because $H$ is symmetric. `alt4 T ijkl = ⅓(T_ijkl + T_iklj + T_iljk)`, and on an array with those three symmetries the vanishing of the cyclic sum over the last three indices is the first Bianchi identity. So `alt4 (compOf H) = 0` is a faithful reading of "$H$ satisfies the first Bianchi identity" for a symmetric operator on $\Lambda^2$. It is the full identity (the whole $\Lambda^4$ component), stronger than the one scalar consequence $\langle H,\star\rangle=0$ the proof consumes, and it is what the record's $H\in\mathcal W_n$ carries.
- **Tangency.** [conventions.md](../findings/conventions.md) line 85 puts the operator inner product as plain Frobenius on the pair matrix. For symmetric $H$ that is $\operatorname{tr}(H^{\mathsf T}u)=\operatorname{tr}(Hu)$ in any orthonormal basis of $\Lambda^2$, and `LinearMap.trace` is basis-free. Any positive rescaling of the inner product leaves the zero set unchanged, so the normalisation is irrelevant. `LinearMap.trace (H ∘ₗ uOp) = 0` is faithful.
- **What the lemma assumes that Lean drops.** $H\in\mathcal W_n$ also asks the Ricci contraction to vanish. `L17_at_u` does not assume it, so it holds on a superset of the record's directions. The record's argument likewise never uses it.
- **Criticality.** The record ([route-a.md](../findings/route-a.md) line 81, the "hypotheses, all of them" paragraph) says criticality enters only through the second display, $d^+\Phi=3\langle Q(u),H\rangle+2a\kappa_nF$. The δ² display uses the derivative identity, tangency and Bianchi, and $u$ is a concrete operator in Lean, so criticality is a property of $u$ that the δ² half never reads. It is correctly absent.
- **What Lean assumes that the lemma does not.** `hN` fixes `finrank (Biv n)` as a name, and `hW : ∀ t, (uOp + t • H).IsSymmetric` is a proof term for the spectral function, derivable from `hH` and `uOp_isSymmetric`. Neither restricts $H$. Nothing else is assumed.
- The $\lVert H\rVert=1$ the old block carried is inert per the record and is absent here.

## C. The discharges

- **`piASD_P0_eq`.** `uOp_asd` evaluates `rankSum lam6 vec` at `vec (γ+3)` by `rankSum_vec` and orthonormality, giving `lam6 (γ+3) • vec = 0 • vec` since `lam6 = (2,−1,−1,0,0,0)/√6`. So the three ASD bivectors lie in $\ker u$. `inner_schur_eigen₂` then gives $\langle\omega_\gamma,(P_0HP_0)\omega_\delta\rangle=\mathrm{zero}\varphi(0,0)\langle\omega_\gamma,H\omega_\delta\rangle=\langle\omega_\gamma,H\omega_\delta\rangle$, and the sandwich by the rank-sum $\Pi_{ASD}$ only reads those nine entries. Correct.
- **Bianchi route.** `pairing H` states $\langle\mathrm{Alt}(u_-),\mathrm{Alt}(H)\rangle=a\sum_\delta s_\delta\langle v_\delta,Hv_\delta\rangle$. Rewriting `hB` replaces `alt4 (compOf H)` by the zero array, `h0` evaluates `fourInner` against it to $0$, and $a\ne0$ cancels. So $\sum_\delta s_\delta H_{\delta\delta}=0$ with $s=(1,1,1,-1,-1,-1)$, that is $H_{11}+H_{22}+H_{33}=\sum_{\gamma}\langle\omega_\gamma,H\omega_\gamma\rangle$ over ASD, and `trace_piASD_sandwich` makes the right side $\operatorname{tr}(\Pi_{ASD}H\Pi_{ASD})=\operatorname{tr}B$. `htrB` then writes it as $\operatorname{tr}B_+-\operatorname{tr}B_-$. Here the record's $H_{11},H_{22},H_{33}$ are Lean's `vec 0, vec 1, vec 2`, the eigenvectors of $u$ for $2/\sqrt6,-1/\sqrt6,-1/\sqrt6$. Correct.
- **Tangency route.** `trace_comp_rankSum` gives $\operatorname{tr}(Hu)=\sum_\gamma\lambda_\gamma\langle v_\gamma,Hv_\gamma\rangle=(2H_{11}-H_{22}-H_{33})/\sqrt6$, and $\sqrt6>0$ gives $2H_{11}=H_{22}+H_{33}$. Correct.
- **$\operatorname{tr}B=\operatorname{tr}B_+-\operatorname{tr}B_-$.** `trace_specFn` reads each trace as a sum over $B$'s eigenvalues, `specFn_id` identifies $B$ with its identity spectral function, and `sub_partScalar` is $x_+-x_-=x$ pointwise. Nonnegativity: $x_+=(\lvert x\rvert+x)/2\ge0$ from `neg_abs_le`, $x_-\ge0$ from `negPartScalar_nonneg`. Correct.
- **The compression's instantiation.** $Y=P_0HP_0$ (`schur zeroφ H`), $P=\Pi_{ASD}$ with `piASD_idem` and `piASD_isSymmetric`. Its left side is `specFn` of $\Pi Y\Pi$, transported to $B$'s `specFn` by `hZeq` (a `rfl` after substituting `piASD_P0_eq`, so no proof-irrelevance gap). Its right side $\operatorname{tr}(\Pi Y_-\Pi)$ is the sliver by `hsliver`, where `asd γ = vec (γ+3)` matches the Finset $\{3,4,5\}$. Correct.
- **The chain.** `F_ge hcompress hbianchi htangent` is instantiated at `h11 = H₁₁`, `h22, h33` the `vec 1, vec 2` entries, `sliver` the ASD sum, `trBpos`, `trBneg` the two traces. `F_nonneg` adds `hpos`, `hneg`. `dDelta_nonpos rfl (by positivity) hnn` takes `dDelta := −2aF` literally with $a=1/(3\sqrt6)>0$. The first conjunct is `hasDerivWithinAt_defect_u`, whose value is the same expression, so the three conjuncts speak of one $F$. Correct.
