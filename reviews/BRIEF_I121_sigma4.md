## The $\Lambda^2$ loss is $\operatorname{tr}(W^2\#W^2)$, and its index-concentration bound fails on Weyl tensors (the failure and the $n=4$ formula: Gate 2 owed)

*The $E$ route's first loss is $\theta p_3(\lambda)\le1$. Weighting [L198](../findings/bounds.md)'s rows instead of capping them lands on the record's $S_{22}$ identity, and the step from there to a bound by the index concentration $\lVert N\rVert_F$ is false on Weyl tensors from $n=5$.*

- **The weighted rows are the record's $S_{22}$ identity** (Gate 2 [r1868](REVIEW_r1868.md), [r1869](REVIEW_r1869.md), both CONFIRMED). L198's row $k$ times $\tfrac12\lambda_k^2$, summed, gives $\theta p_3-p_4=\tfrac12S$ with $S=\sum c^2_{k\alpha\gamma}\lambda_k^2\lambda_\alpha\lambda_\gamma$, and [bounds.md](../findings/bounds.md)'s $S_{22}=2\operatorname{tr}(W^2\#W^2)=2\theta\operatorname{tr}W^3-2\operatorname{tr}W^4+\tfrac12G_2$ reads $\operatorname{tr}(W^2\#W^2)=\tfrac12S+\tfrac14G_2$ at a critical point. So the AM–GM drop $\tfrac12S\le\operatorname{tr}(W^2\#W^2)$ loses exactly $\tfrac14G_2\ge0$, and it is [L199](../findings/bounds.md)'s $\theta\lambda_k\le\lambda_k^2+\tfrac12R_k$ weighted by $\lambda_k^2$. With $p_4\le1/\theta^2$ (both extremes at most $1/\theta$ by L198, and $p_2=1$), every unit critical point at $\theta>0$ has $$\theta^2\ \le\ \frac3{2\theta^2}+\frac32\operatorname{tr}(W^2\#W^2)+\theta\,C_n\,t_{\max},\qquad C_n=\tfrac32\sqrt{\tfrac{2(n-2)}{n-1}} .$$
- **The exact form of the $\Lambda^2$ term.** With $N_{ik}=\sum_{m,p,q}W_{impq}W_{kmpq}$, [target.md](../findings/target.md)'s positive-semidefinite contraction of trace $4$ at unit norm, $$\operatorname{tr}(W^2\#W^2)=\tfrac18\lVert N\rVert_F^2-\tfrac12\Sigma_4,\qquad\Sigma_4=\sum_{ijkl}(W^2)_{ijkl}(W^2)_{jkli}=\tfrac14\sum W_{ijpq}W_{jkrs}W_{klpq}W_{lirs},$$ found by r1868 and r1869 independently and valid for every symmetric $G$ in place of $W^2$. **And $\Sigma_4=2\operatorname{tr}W^4-6\lVert\mathrm{Alt}(W^2)\rVert^2$** in the record's norm (`sigma4_comp`, in Lean for every array antisymmetric in its first pair and pair-symmetric): the index map $G_{ijkl}\mapsto G_{jkli}$ acts by $\tfrac12$ on the Bianchi part of a pair-symmetric tensor (by first Bianchi and $\sum R_{ijkl}R_{ikjl}=\tfrac12\sum R_{ijkl}^2$) and by $-1$ on $\Lambda^4$ (a four-cycle is odd). So $\operatorname{tr}(W^2\#W^2)\le\tfrac18\lVert N\rVert_F^2$ holds exactly when $\lVert\mathrm{Alt}(W^2)\rVert^2\le\tfrac13\operatorname{tr}W^4$, the $\Lambda^4$ part of $W^2$ carrying at most a third of its mass. [L204](../findings/bounds.md)'s $\lVert\mathrm{Alt}(A)\rVert\le\sqrt{(m-1)/(3m)}\operatorname{tr}A$ gives that only when $\operatorname{tr}W^4\ge1-\tfrac1m$.
- **At $n=4$ it holds, as $\Sigma_4=2\lVert W^+\rVert^2\lVert W^-\rVert^2$.** L199's $\mathrm{Alt}(W^2)=\tfrac16(\lVert W^+\rVert^2-\lVert W^-\rVert^2)\star$ with $\lVert\star\rVert^2=6$, and $\operatorname{tr}X^4=\tfrac12(\operatorname{tr}X^2)^2$ on each traceless $3\times3$ block, give $\Sigma_4=(s_+^2+s_-^2)-(s_+-s_-)^2=2s_+s_-$, zero exactly at one-sided $W$ such as $W_{\mathbb{CP}^2}$. *For positive semidefinite $G$ in general it fails: $G=P_+$ on $\Lambda^2\mathbb R^4$ has $\operatorname{tr}(G^\#G)=6$ against $\tfrac18\lVert N\rVert_F^2=\tfrac92$.*
- **From $n=5$ it is false on Weyl tensors** ([screen_I121_sigma4.py](../scripts/misc/screen_I121_sigma4.py), seconds, its own projector, $\#$ and $N$). Descent on $\Sigma_4/\lVert W^2\rVert^2$ over the Weyl space reaches $-0.046$, $-\tfrac16$, $-0.286$, $-0.408$ at $n=5,6,7,8$, $\Lambda^4$ fractions $0.364$, $\tfrac49$, $0.524$, $0.605$. **The $n=6$ witness has spectrum $\pm\tfrac1{2\sqrt2}$ on two four-dimensional eigenspaces, isotropic $N=\tfrac23I$, and $\operatorname{tr}(W^2\#W^2)=\tfrac38$ against $\tfrac18\lVert N\rVert_F^2=\tfrac13$**, with Ricci and Bianchi residuals of $10^{-15}$. It is a Weyl tensor and not a critical point. *The derivation's coordinate formula $(G^\#G)_{ij,ij}=\sum_k(G_{ik,ik}G_{jk,jk}-G_{ik,jk}^2)$ is the $k=l$ slice of a double sum over $(k,l)$, exact only at diagonal $G$ (r1868, r1869).*
- **At critical points $\Sigma_4\ge0$ is open**, positive at $S^3\times S^3$, $S^4\times S^4$, $S^2\times S^4$, $\mathbb{CP}^3$, $\mathbb{CP}^4$ and zero at padded $W_{\mathbb{CP}^2}$ (r1869). Granting it there, $\theta^2\le\tfrac3{2\theta^2}+\tfrac3{16}\lVert N\rVert_F^2+\theta C_nt_{\max}$, and the band below $\tfrac3{n+2}$ is empty at $n\ge30$ whenever $\lVert N\rVert_F^2\le\nu^\ast(n)=\tfrac{16}3\big(\theta_0^2-\tfrac3{2\theta_0^2}-\theta_0C_n\sqrt{3/(n+2)}\big)$, which is $0.618$, $2.53$, $3.54$ at $n=30,60,100$ against the floor $\tfrac{16}n$. At isotropic $N$ the bound reads $t_{\max}^2\ge0.029$, $0.064$, $0.097$, $0.134$, $0.150$ at $n=14,20,30,60,100$, limit $0.1736$, crossing $\mathbb{CP}^m$'s $\tfrac3{n+2}$ at $n=30$ exactly (both reviewers, to every digit). **So the split needs criticality to supply $\Sigma_4\ge0$, and nothing on the record does.**

## Givens, quoted from the record

- **The identity** ([r1868](REVIEW_r1868.md), [r1869](REVIEW_r1869.md), independently, Gate 2 passed): for every symmetric $G$ on $\Lambda^2\mathbb R^n$, $\operatorname{tr}(G^\#G)=\tfrac18\lVert N_G\rVert_F^2-\tfrac12Q$ with $Q=\sum_{ijkl}G_{ijkl}G_{jkli}$, which at $G=W^2$ is the block's $\Sigma_4$, and $N_{W^2}$ is the block's $N$.
- **The record's norm and $\mathrm{Alt}$** ([conventions.md](../findings/conventions.md), [AltPart.lean](../lean/HamiltonsCubic/Base/AltPart.lean)): $\lVert R\rVert^2=\tfrac14\sum R_{ijkl}^2$, and $\mathrm{Alt}(R)_{ijkl}=\tfrac13(R_{ijkl}+R_{iklj}+R_{iljk})$ is the orthogonal projection of $S^2(\Lambda^2)$ onto $\Lambda^4$. Inside $\Lambda^2\mathbb R^4$, $\Lambda^4$ is one-dimensional and $\mathrm{Alt}$ is the projection onto $\star=\Pi_{SD}-\Pi_{ASD}$, with $\lVert\star\rVert^2=6$.
- **L199's $n=4$ remark** ([bounds.md](../findings/bounds.md)): at $n=4$, $\mathrm{Alt}(W^2)=\tfrac{\lVert W^+\rVert^2-\lVert W^-\rVert^2}{6}\star$ for every Weyl $W$.
- **L204** ([bounds.md](../findings/bounds.md), Gate 2 r916, r917): for every positive semidefinite $A$ on $\Lambda^2\mathbb R^n$, $\lVert\mathrm{Alt}(A)\rVert\le\sqrt{\tfrac{m-1}{3m}}\operatorname{tr}A$, $m=\lfloor n/2\rfloor$.
- **Weyl tensor**: an algebraic curvature tensor (pair symmetries and first Bianchi) with zero Ricci contraction. Unit norm is $\lVert W\rVert=1$ in the record's norm, so $\operatorname{tr}W^2=1$ for $W$ as an operator on $\Lambda^2$.
- **Critical point** of $P(W)=\operatorname{tr}W^3+\langle W^\#,W\rangle$ on the unit sphere of Weyl tensors: the Weyl projection of $W^2+W^\#$ is $\theta W$, $\theta=P(W)$.
