## A. Statement fidelity

- **The function and the derivative notion.** The map is `fun t => LinearMap.toContinuousLinearMap (specFn hn g (hW t))` with `hW t : (u + t • H).IsSymmetric`, so it is $t\mapsto g(u+tH)$ read in `E →L[ℝ] E`, whose norm is the operator norm. `HasDerivWithinAt _ D (Set.Ici 0) 0` is the derivative within $[0,\infty)$ at $0$, i.e. $g(u+tH)-g(u)-tD=o(t)$ as $t\to0^+$. That is the one-sided (right) derivative.
- **The function $g$.** `hpos`, `hneg` and `hhom` at $c=0$ give $g=0$ on $(0,\infty)$, $g=-x$ on $(-\infty,0)$ and $g(0)=0$, so $g=\max(-x,0)$ and nothing else (`negPart_lipschitz` proves exactly this reduction first). `hhom` holds for that function, so the hypothesis set is satisfiable, and its content beyond $g(0)=0$ is automatic.
- **The derivative term by term.** `rotGen hn hu H ∘ₗ gu - gu ∘ₗ rotGen hn hu H` is $Kg(u)-g(u)K=[K,g(u)]$, and by `rotGen_commutator_specFn` (Schur.lean) its eigenbasis entries are $\frac{g(\lambda_j)-g(\lambda_i)}{\lambda_j-\lambda_i}H_{ij}$ for $\lambda_i\ne\lambda_j$ and $0$ otherwise, the Daleckii–Krein off-diagonal part. `schur hn hu negBlkφ H` with `negBlkφ a b = [a = b ∧ a < 0]` is $\sum_{\lambda<0}P_\lambda HP_\lambda$, entering with a minus sign. `specFn hn g (schur_isSymmetric hn hu zeroφ_symm hH)` is $g(P_0HP_0)$ with `zeroφ a b = [a = 0 ∧ b = 0]`. On the non-kernel diagonal blocks $g'(\lambda)$ is $0$ for $\lambda>0$ and $-1$ for $\lambda<0$, so $-\sum_{\lambda<0}P_\lambda HP_\lambda$ is the diagonal-block derivative there, and the kernel block, where $g$ is not differentiable, contributes $g(P_0HP_0)$ by positive homogeneity. This is the standard first-order expansion of a spectral function whose integrand has a kink at an eigenvalue: divided differences off the diagonal blocks, $g'(\lambda)P_\lambda HP_\lambda$ on the smooth blocks, and the directional derivative of $g$ applied to the compression on the kink block.
- **Hand check, $u=\operatorname{diag}(1,0,-1)$, $H=(h_{ij})$ symmetric.** Here $u_-=\operatorname{diag}(0,0,1)$ and $g(\lambda)=(0,0,1)$.
  - Claimed $D$. Off-diagonal $[K,u_-]_{ij}=\frac{g(\lambda_j)-g(\lambda_i)}{\lambda_j-\lambda_i}h_{ij}$: $(1,2)$: $\frac{0-0}{-1}=0$. $(1,3)$: $\frac{1-0}{-2}h_{13}=-\tfrac12h_{13}$. $(2,3)$: $\frac{1-0}{-1}h_{23}=-h_{23}$. The transposed entries agree ($(3,1)$: $\frac{0-1}{2}$, $(3,2)$: $\frac{0-1}{1}$), so $[K,u_-]$ is symmetric. Diagonal: $-\sum_{\lambda<0}P_\lambda HP_\lambda=\operatorname{diag}(0,0,-h_{33})$ and $g(P_0HP_0)=\operatorname{diag}(0,\max(-h_{22},0),0)$. So $D=\begin{pmatrix}0&0&-h_{13}/2\\0&\max(-h_{22},0)&-h_{23}\\-h_{13}/2&-h_{23}&-h_{33}\end{pmatrix}$.
  - Direct. The eigenvalues are simple, so $u+tH$ has eigenpairs $\mu_k=\lambda_k+th_{kk}+O(t^2)$ and $Q_k=e_ke_k^{\mathsf T}+tQ_k'+O(t^2)$ with $(Q_k')_{jk}=(Q_k')_{kj}=h_{jk}/(\lambda_k-\lambda_j)$ for $j\ne k$. For small $t$: $g(\mu_1)=0$. $g(\mu_3)=1-th_{33}+O(t^2)$, contributing $-h_{33}e_3e_3^{\mathsf T}+Q_3'$ to the derivative, and $(Q_3')_{13}=h_{13}/(-1-1)=-\tfrac12h_{13}$, $(Q_3')_{23}=h_{23}/(-1-0)=-h_{23}$. $\mu_2=th_{22}+O(t^2)$, so $g(\mu_2)=t\max(-h_{22},0)+O(t^2)$ (when $h_{22}=0$ the value is $O(t^2)$), contributing $\max(-h_{22},0)e_2e_2^{\mathsf T}$, while $g(\mu_2)Q_2'$ is $O(t^2)$. Summing gives exactly $D$ above, entry by entry. Passes.
- **`hW : ∀ t, (u + t • H).IsSymmetric`.** It follows from `hu` and `hH` (the proof builds the same fact for $H_{\mathrm{blk}}$ inline as `hWb`), so it adds no restriction. It is a `Prop`, so by proof irrelevance `specFn hn g (hW t)` does not depend on the proof chosen, and the argument exists only because `specFn` takes the symmetry proof as input. Harmless.
- **Proof route, checked.** With $K'$ the generator in `E →L[ℝ] E`, the proof sets $R(t)=U(t)\big(g(M(t))-g(u+tH_{\mathrm{blk}})\big)U(-t)$ and $\varphi(t)=U(t)(g(u)+tG_1)U(-t)$. `hdec` shows $g(u+tH)-g(u)-tD=R(t)+\varphi(t)-\varphi(0)-tD$ on $[0,t_0]$ via `specFn_eq_conj` and `specFn_blk`. `hRbound` bounds $\lVert R(t)\rVert\le\sqrt{\dim E}\,\lVert M(t)-u-tH_{\mathrm{blk}}\rVert$ for every $t$, which is $o(t)$ by `hasDerivAt_conjPath_blk`. `hφ` gives $\varphi'(0)=K'g(u)+G_1-g(u)K'=D$ by the product rule. Both pieces are $o(t)$ at $\mathcal N(0)$, restricted to $\mathcal N_{[0,\infty)}(0)$, and `hev` supplies the eventual window $0\le t\le t_0$. Sound.

## B. Supporting lemmas

- **`norm_specFn_sub_le`.** `norm_toCLM_le_sqrt_hs` gives $\lVert X\rVert\le\sqrt{\mathrm{hsSq}\,X}$ from $\lVert Xv\rVert^2\le\mathrm{hsSq}(X)\lVert v\rVert^2$ (`norm_apply_sq_le`). `hsSq_specFn_sub_le` at $L=1$ gives $\mathrm{hsSq}(g(B)-g(A))\le\mathrm{hsSq}(B-A)$, the `simpa` removing $1^2$. `sqrt_hs_le` gives $\sqrt{\mathrm{hsSq}\,Y}\le\sqrt{\dim E}\,\lVert Y\rVert$ from `hsSq_le_opNorm` ($\mathrm{hsSq}\,Y\le\dim E\cdot\lVert Y\rVert^2$, each basis column bounded by the operator norm). The chain is $\lVert g(B)-g(A)\rVert\le\sqrt{\dim E}\,\lVert B-A\rVert$, the factor correct and applied in the right direction at each step.
- **`norm_rot_le`.** $\langle U(t)x,U(t)x\rangle=\langle x,U(t)^*U(t)x\rangle=\langle x,U(-t)U(t)x\rangle=\langle x,x\rangle$ via `star_rot` and `rot_neg_mul_rot`, so $\lVert U(t)x\rVert=\lVert x\rVert$ and $\lVert U(t)\rVert\le1$. Correct, for any skew $K$.
- **`specFn_eq_conj`.** `specFn_conj` applied to the linear equivalence $U$ with forward map $U(t)$ and inverse $U(-t)$ gives $g(U(-t)\,T\,U(t))=U(-t)\,g(T)\,U(t)$. Conjugating back yields `specFn hn g hT = U(t) ∘ₗ specFn hn g (conj) ∘ₗ U(-t)`, i.e. $g(T)=U\,g(U^*TU)\,U^*$ with $U=U(t)$, $U^*=U(-t)$ (`star_rot`). Right orientation, and it is the one `hdec` needs, since $M(t)=U(t)^*(u+tH)U(t)$ is `conjPath`.
- **`conj_isSymmetric`** supplies the symmetry of $U^*TU$ through the adjoint identities for $U(\pm t)$. Correct.
