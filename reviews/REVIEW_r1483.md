## A. Fidelity of L17

- **Φ is the record's Φ.** $P$ is `Pcubic2` on `CurvOp2 (Fin n)`, evaluated at `u2 hn + t • H`. $\delta^2$ is `altNormSqOf (specFn hN negPartScalar (hW t))`, the model's $\lVert\mathrm{Alt}(W_-)\rVert^2$ of `uOp + t • opB H`, with $W_-=\tfrac12(\lvert W\rvert-W)$ as confirmed in [r1481](REVIEW_r1481.md). The layer has no native $\delta^2$ on `CurvOp2`, so the model's reading is the definition in force, and [BivModel.lean](../lean/HamiltonsCubic/Base/BivModel.lean) line 150 says so.
- **One path, by a proved equation that the statement does not cite.** `opB_add_smul` gives `opB (R + t • S) = opB R + t • opB S`, and `opB_u2` gives `opB (u2 hn) = uOp`, so `opB (u2 hn + t • H) = uOp + t • opB H`. Screen: that equation compiles in two rewrites. `opB` is an isometry onto the model (`inner_coords`, `coords_toSkew`, `toSkew_coords`), so the two arguments of $\Phi$ are one operator read in two coordinate systems.
- **The derivative is $2a\kappa F$ with the record's $F$.** `hP` gives $d^+P=0$, `L17_at_u` gives $d^+\delta^2=-2aF$, and `hP.sub (hδ.const_mul κ)` with `ring` gives $0-\kappa(-2aF)=2a\kappa F$, $a=1/(3\sqrt6)$. $F$ is `vec 1` and `vec 2` entries of `opB H` plus the ASD sliver of $(P_0HP_0)_-$, the record's $H_{22}+H_{33}+\operatorname{tr}(\Pi_{ASD}(P_0HP_0)_-)$ with `vec 0, 1, 2` the eigenvectors for $(2,-1,-1)/\sqrt6$ ([r1482](REVIEW_r1482.md) C). `HasDerivWithinAt … (Set.Ici 0) 0` is the one-sided $d^+$. The second conjunct follows from $F\ge0$, which `nlinarith` extracts from `L17_at_u`'s third conjunct.
- **The hypotheses are the lemma's.** `IsWeyl2 H` is $H\in\mathcal W_n$ on $\Lambda^2$ (self-adjoint, first Bianchi, Ricci-flat, the last unused). `innerOp2 H (u2 hn) = 0` is $\langle H,u\rangle=0$ with the $\Lambda^2$ pairing (`innerOp2_eq_trace` turns it into $\operatorname{tr}(Hu)=0$ for `L17_at_u`). Criticality is not a binder: it is `Wc_crit`, a theorem about the concrete $u$. $n\ge4$ is `hn`.
- **Binders that restrict nothing.** `hN : finrank ℝ (Biv n) = N` is a name for the dimension, satisfied by `rfl`. `κ` is universally quantified, so `L17` holds at $\kappa_n$ and every other value. `hW : ∀ t, (uOp + t • opB H).IsSymmetric` is a proof term the spectral function needs. Screen: `uOp_isSymmetric.add ((opB_isSymmetric hH.selfAdjoint).smul (by simp))` discharges it, so it restricts nothing, and by proof irrelevance the value of $\delta^2$ does not depend on it.
- **Gap A1, the path.** The statement's $\delta^2$ reads `uOp + t • opB H`, and the equation that makes it $\delta^2$ of `opB (u2 hn + t • H)` is proved (`opB_add_smul`, `opB_u2`) and used nowhere, including in `L17`. ## B. The P side

- **`sharpBil2_restrict` by `rfl`.** `sharpBil2 R X Y = ½(sharpQF2 R (X+Y) − sharpQF2 R X − sharpQF2 R Y)` and `sharpBil R X Y` is the same polarisation of `sharpQF`. The kernel accepts that `sharpQF2 (restrict hW) Z` unfolds to `sharpQF R ↑Z`, which is what `restrict_apply` (itself `rfl`) makes plausible. A definitional equation the kernel accepts is sound. It is stated for every `IsWeyl R`, not only `Wc`.
- **`inner2_Qop2_u2` is criticality on $\Lambda^2$.** `Qop2 R = compOp2 R + sharpMapCL R`, so $\langle Q(u)X,Y\rangle=\langle u^2X,Y\rangle+\langle u^\#X,Y\rangle$, the second term being `sharpBil2 (u2) X Y` by `inner2_sharpMap`, hence `sharpBil (Wc n) X Y` by `sharpBil2_restrict`. `Wc_crit` gives $\langle W^2X,Y\rangle+\langle W^\#X,Y\rangle=\sqrt{3/2}\,\langle WX,Y\rangle$ for skew $X,Y$. That is $Q(u)=\theta u$, $\theta=\sqrt{3/2}$, tested against every bivector, which by definiteness of `inner2` on $\Lambda^2$ is the operator equation. `innerOp2_Qop2_u2` sums it over the $\varphi$ frame.
- **`hasDerivAt_Pcubic2_u2`.** It calls `hasDerivAt_Pcubic2_line (u2 hn) H (isSelfAdjoint2_restrict (Wc_weyl hn))`, whose only hypothesis is self-adjointness of the base point, supplied from `IsWeyl`'s second clause. The value $3\langle H,Q(u)\rangle$ becomes $3\sqrt{3/2}\langle H,u\rangle$ and then $0$ by `hT`. Two-sided `HasDerivAt`, restricted to `Ici 0` in `L17`. Correct.

## C. The bridge

- **`coords_phi_pair`.** `coords φ_p` at `q` is `inner2 φ_p φ_q`, and with `inner2 = ½ tr(XᵀY)` the frame $\{\varphi_p\}_{i<j}$ is orthonormal, so it is $\delta_{pq}$. The case $j=k, i=l$ is excluded by `lt_asymm`. Correct.
- **`toSkew`, `coords_toSkew`, `toSkew_coords`.** `toSkew c = Σ_p c_p φ_p`, linear, landing in `skewSub`. `coords_toSkew` is orthonormality. `toSkew_coords` for skew $X$ is `skew_expand` folded to pairs by `sum_pairs`, using `phi i i = 0` and `phi_swap`. The pair is an isometric isomorphism $\Lambda^2\cong$ `Biv n`. Correct.
- **`opB`.** `coordsL ∘ subtype ∘ R ∘ toSkew`, so `opB R c = coords (R (toSkew c))`. `opB_isSymmetric` moves `inner_coords` across and applies self-adjointness. Correct.
- **`compOf_opB`.** `compOf X i j k l = ⟨coords φ_kl, X (coords φ_ij)⟩`, and for every $i,j$ (the unordered and diagonal ones included) `toSkew (coords φ_ij) = φ_ij` since $\varphi_{ij}$ is skew. So `compOf (opB R) i j k l = inner2 (R φ_ij) φ_kl`. Correct.
- **`alt4_opB_eq_zero`.** `alt4 T ijkl = ⅓(T_ijkl + T_iklj + T_iljk)`. With `compOf_opB` the three terms are `inner2 (Rφ_ij) φ_kl`, `inner2 (Rφ_ik) φ_lj`, `inner2 (Rφ_il) φ_jk`, which is `IsWeyl2.bianchi i j k l` term for term. Correct.
- **`om_eq`.** `om_entry a p q` states `om a q p = sq2⁻¹ · ⟨Emb q, J a · Emb p⟩ = sq2⁻¹ · J a q p` inside the block, the $(q,p)$ entry of $E J E^{\mathsf T}$ with no transpose. After `ext q p` the goal is at `(q, p)`, `sum_Emb` evaluates both contractions, and `pad_apply` with `J_cast`, `om6_eq_cast`, `Jz_eq_om6Z` (48 entries, by `decide`) matches `Jz a q p` to `om6Z a q p` in the same order. Outside the block both sides are zero. So $\omega_a=\mathrm{pad}(\omega^{(6)}_a)/\sqrt2$ with the orientation preserved. *A transposed version would flip the sign of each $\omega_a$, which `Wc` and `uOp` would not see, being quadratic in them.*
- **`opB_u2`.** `Wc X = Σ_a μ_a ⟨X, ω_a⟩ ω_a` with $\mu=(2,-1,-1)/\sqrt6$, and `uOp = Σ_γ λ_γ ⟨vec γ, ·⟩ vec γ` with $\lambda=(2,-1,-1,0,0,0)/\sqrt6$. `coords_om` gives `coords ω_a = vec a`, `hi` gives `inner2 (toSkew c) ω_a = ⟨vec a, c⟩`, and the three zero eigenvalues drop. Correct.
- **`innerOp2_eq_trace`.** `innerOp2 R S = ½ Σ_{i,j} ⟨Rφ_ij, Sφ_ij⟩`. The diagonal vanishes (`applyPhi R i i = 0`), the swap is even, so it is $\sum_{p}\langle R\varphi_p,S\varphi_p\rangle$, and in the basis `basisFun` that is $\sum_p\langle e_p,\mathrm{opB}R\,\mathrm{opB}S\,e_p\rangle$ after moving `opB R` across by symmetry. Self-adjointness of $R$ alone is needed. Correct.
