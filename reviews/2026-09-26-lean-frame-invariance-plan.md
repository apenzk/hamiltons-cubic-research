# Lean build plan: the frame change in the operator model

**Goal.** Several results are stated *in some orthonormal frame* or for *a four-dimensional $V\subseteq\mathbb R^n$*, and the layer proves them in the coordinate frame only: [L176](../findings/diagonal-half.md) for the coordinate-diagonal operator ([L176Op.lean](../lean/HamiltonsCubic/Results/L176Op.lean)), [L178](../findings/diagonal-half.md) for the coordinate $\Lambda^2\mathbb R^4$ ([L178Support.lean](../lean/HamiltonsCubic/Results/L178Support.lean)), and [I1](../plan.md)'s *coordinate-diagonal in no frame*, whose predicate [Frames.lean](../lean/HamiltonsCubic/Base/Frames.lean) defines through `conjOp` with no invariance proved. The build proves that `conjOp U` for orthogonal $U$ preserves the Weyl conditions and $\theta$, so each coordinate-frame theorem transfers to every frame.

**Route.** Every quantity the layer defines on an operator is a sum over the basis bivectors $\varphi_{ij}$, and every such sum is a trace, so it is unchanged when the basis is replaced by the conjugated one $U^{\mathsf T}\varphi_{ij}U$. That basis-independence is `phiSum_conj`, from the $\varphi$-expansion of a bivector and polarised Parseval. The build closed milestones 2 and 3 by shorter routes: milestone 2 through eigenframes, whose eigenvalues and structure constants a frame change preserves, and milestone 3 through the tensor law `C_conj`.

## Milestones

1. **The conjugated basis sums like the basis.** For $U$ orthogonal and $F,G$ linear on bivectors, $\sum_{ij}\langle F(U^{\mathsf T}\varphi_{ij}U),G(U^{\mathsf T}\varphi_{ij}U)\rangle=\sum_{ij}\langle F\varphi_{ij},G\varphi_{ij}\rangle$, with `inner2` and the bracket invariant under $X\mapsto U^{\mathsf T}XU$. **Proved** in [Results/FrameInv.lean](../lean/HamiltonsCubic/Results/FrameInv.lean) as `inner2_conj`, `br_conj`, `psi_pair` and `phiSum_conj`.
2. **$\lVert\cdot\rVert^2$ and $\theta$.** `normSq` and `theta` are unchanged by `conjOp U` at every Weyl operator, and `Pcubic` at every Weyl operator with an eigenframe. **Proved** in [Results/FrameTheta.lean](../lean/HamiltonsCubic/Results/FrameTheta.lean) by a shorter route than the sums: a frame change carries an eigenframe to one with the same eigenvalues and structure constants (`eigenFrame_conj`, `cst_conj`), so the frame identities give `invariants_conj_of_frame`, and every Weyl operator reaches an eigenframe through its linear extension $X\mapsto R(\mathop{\mathrm{skew}}X)$ (`linExt`), giving **`invariants_conj`**: $\lVert U\cdot R\rVert=\lVert R\rVert$ and $\theta(U\cdot R)=\theta(R)$ for every Weyl $R$ on $\mathbb R^n$.
3. **The Weyl conditions.** `IsWeyl (conjOp U R)` from `IsWeyl R`: Ricci and first Bianchi transform as tensors under $U$. **Proved** in [Results/FrameInv.lean](../lean/HamiltonsCubic/Results/FrameInv.lean) as `C_conj` (the curvature components transform with four factors of $U$, through `psi_expand`), `bianchi_conj`, `ric_conj` and **`isWeyl_conj`**.
4. **The transfers.** L178 for every four-dimensional $V$, and L176 in every orthonormal frame, from the coordinate statements and milestones 2 and 3. **Proved** in [Results/FrameTransfer.lean](../lean/HamiltonsCubic/Results/FrameTransfer.lean): `L178_frame`, $\theta(R)=\theta(U\cdot R)\le\sqrt{3/2}$ when the frame change $U$ carries $R$'s support onto the coordinate $\Lambda^2\mathbb R^4$ with $U\cdot R$'s coefficient matrix nonzero of rank at most three; `diagOp_eq_WD`, the layer's `diagOp w` is $W_w$ on bivectors; and `L176_someFrame`, L176 for [Frames.lean](../lean/HamiltonsCubic/Base/Frames.lean)'s `IsCoordDiagonalSomeFrame`, with `coeffOp_conj` reading $U\cdot R$'s matrix as $R$'s on the rotated orthonormal basis.

**Bound.** Two sessions, closed in one. Milestone 4 reads the rank of an operator in a rotated basis through `coeffOp_conj`.
