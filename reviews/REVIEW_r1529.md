## A. Fidelity

- **A-1. The $\psi$ argument** ([L136Sign.lean:36](../lean/HamiltonsCubic/Results/L136Sign.lean), line 42, line 67). `limit_nonneg` (nonnegative on $t>0$ implies a nonnegative limit of $\varphi(t)/t^k$) and `rightDeriv_nonneg` ($\varphi\ge0$ on $t>0$, $\varphi(0)=0$, one-sided derivative $d$ gives $d\ge0$, through the slope characterisation) are both correct and are exactly "from nothing else". **Gap.** r427 states the argument on $\varphi(t)=\mathop{\mathrm{tr}}(\Pi_A(D_s(t))_-)$ with $D_s=D-tC^{\mathsf T}\Lambda^{-1}C$, using $\Pi_AW_-(t)=t\,\Pi_A(D_s)_-$, so that the $t^2$-coefficient of $\mathop{\mathrm{tr}}(\Pi_AW_-)$ is $\varphi'(0^+)$ and $\varphi(0)=\mathop{\mathrm{tr}}(\Pi_AD_-)=0$ on the cone ([REVIEW_r427.md:87](REVIEW_r427.md)). The file's docstring instead names $\varphi(t)=\mathop{\mathrm{tr}}(\Pi_A(u+tH)_-)$ ([L136Sign.lean:19](../lean/HamiltonsCubic/Results/L136Sign.lean)), for which $\varphi(0)=0$ holds everywhere ($A\subseteq\ker u$) and the first-order form signs nothing about $X_2$. The chain `trA_nonneg` accordingly uses `limit_nonneg` at $k=2$, which is the same argument after dividing r427's identity by $t^2$, and is correct. `rightDeriv_nonneg` is never applied, yet the docstring ([L136Sign.lean:17](../lean/HamiltonsCubic/Results/L136Sign.lean)) and the row ([lean/INDEX.md:491](../lean/INDEX.md)) present it as the record's argument. The record's own paraphrase ([route-a.md:287](../findings/route-a.md), "$\varphi(t)=\mathop{\mathrm{tr}}(\Pi_A(\cdot)_-)$ along the path") is ambiguous between the two functions, which is where the slip comes from. The repair is one sentence: the chain signs $\mathop{\mathrm{tr}}(\Pi_A(u+tH)_-)/t^2$ directly, equivalent to r427's first-order form on the sliver.
- **A-2. The projection trace** ([L136Sign.lean:52](../lean/HamiltonsCubic/Results/L136Sign.lean)). $\mathop{\mathrm{tr}}(PN)=\mathop{\mathrm{tr}}(P^{\mathsf T}NP)$ from $P^2=P$ and cyclicity, then Mathlib's `conjTranspose_mul_mul_same`. Symmetric idempotent is exactly an orthogonal projection, and $(u+tH)_-=\tfrac12(\lvert u+tH\rvert-(u+tH))$ is symmetric PSD, so this is the right hypothesis set for $\mathop{\mathrm{tr}}(\Pi_A(u+tH)_-)\ge0$.
- **A-3. `hlim`** ([L136Sign.lean:65](../lean/HamiltonsCubic/Results/L136Sign.lean)). It asserts $\mathop{\mathrm{tr}}(\Pi_AN(t))/t^2\to\mathop{\mathrm{tr}}(\Pi_AX_2)$ as $t\downarrow0$. Convergence of that quotient forces the constant and linear orders of $\mathop{\mathrm{tr}}(\Pi_AN(t))$ to vanish, so the hypothesis honestly encodes "$X_2$ is the $t^2$-coefficient with lower orders vanishing", at the level of the $\Pi_A$-trace, which is all the chain uses. It is satisfiable ($N(t)=t^2X$ with $X\succeq0$), so the theorem is not vacuous. For the real $N(t)=(u+tH)_-$ it needs $\mathop{\mathrm{tr}}(\Pi_AD_-)=0$, and where that trace is positive the quotient diverges. **Gap.** That F = 0 is what makes the linear order vanish, through $F=0\Rightarrow\mathop{\mathrm{tr}}(\Pi_AD_-)=0$ ([route-a.md:289](../findings/route-a.md), the record's "$F=0$ forces $\Pi_AD_-=0$"), appears neither in the docstring ([L136Sign.lean:20](../lean/HamiltonsCubic/Results/L136Sign.lean), line 61) nor in the owed clause (line 27), which says "vanishing first order on the cone" without naming the mechanism.
- **A-4. $\star$ and $\Delta_2$** ([L136Sign.lean:72](../lean/HamiltonsCubic/Results/L136Sign.lean), line 82). On $E\oplus A\oplus K'$, $\star$ is the Hodge star of the distinguished $\Lambda^2\mathbb R^4$ extended by zero: $+1$ on the self-dual $E$, $-1$ on the anti-self-dual $A$, $0$ on $K'$, so $\star=\Pi_E-\Pi_A$ and $\lVert\star\rVert^2=3+3=6$, matching the Setting. For symmetric matrices $\langle\star,X_2\rangle=\mathop{\mathrm{tr}}(\star X_2)$, so `hΔ : Δ = g + 2 * a * ((PE - PA) * X₂).trace` is the Setting's $\Delta_2=\lVert g_1\rVert^2+2a\langle\star,X_2\rangle$ ([route-a.md:273](../findings/route-a.md)) with $g$ standing for $\lVert g_1\rVert^2$. **Gap.** That formula is a derived expansion (L135's straight-line coefficient, $\mathrm{Alt}$ an orthogonal projection, $g_0=a\star$), not a definition, and it enters both theorems as `hΔ`. Neither the file's owed paragraph ([L136Sign.lean:27](../lean/HamiltonsCubic/Results/L136Sign.lean)) nor the row's Owed ([lean/INDEX.md:491](../lean/INDEX.md)) lists it.
- **A-5. The box** ([L136Sign.lean:81](../lean/HamiltonsCubic/Results/L136Sign.lean)). With $\mathop{\mathrm{tr}}(\Pi_EX_2)=\tfrac{\sqrt6}3(B_{12}^2+B_{13}^2)=0$ and $\mathop{\mathrm{tr}}(\Pi_AX_2)=\mathcal K+\sqrt6(s_2+s_3)$, $\Delta=g-2a\mathcal K-2a\sqrt6(s_2+s_3)=g-2a\mathcal K-\tfrac23(s_2+s_3)\le g-\tfrac23(s_2+s_3)\le g$. The two identities are the record's ([route-a.md:279](../findings/route-a.md)), $2a\sqrt6=2\sqrt6/(3\sqrt6)=\tfrac23$ is `hc`, and $a\ge0$ is derived from `ha`. The boxed statement, reproduced.

"The weak box needs neither identity nor Hansen–Pedersen"** ([lean/INDEX.md:491](../lean/INDEX.md)). **Refuted as written.** The weak box is `cross_term_nonpos`, which takes $\mathop{\mathrm{tr}}(\Pi_EX_2)=0$ as `hE` ([L136Sign.lean:72](../lean/HamiltonsCubic/Results/L136Sign.lean)).
