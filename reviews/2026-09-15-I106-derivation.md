## Batch 1, the objective and the norm in traceless variables

Write $M_a=m_aI+X_a$ with $\operatorname{tr}X_a=0$, $\mu=\sum_am_a$, $X=\sum_aX_a$. Then $S=X-\tfrac12\mu I$, so $S$'s traceless part is $X$ and $\operatorname{tr}S=-\tfrac32\mu$. The Ricci condition at $a$ reads $3m_a+\sum_bK_{ab}=0$, frame-free.

Expanding L226's item 3 with $\operatorname{adj}(mI+X)=X^2-mX+(m^2-\tfrac12\lvert X\rvert^2)I$, $\det(X-cI)=\det X+\tfrac12c\lvert X\rvert^2-c^3$ and $\det X=\tfrac13\operatorname{tr}X^3$ for traceless $3\times3$ $X$:

- $\operatorname{tr}S^3+3\det S=2\operatorname{tr}X^3-\tfrac34\mu\lvert X\rvert^2-\tfrac34\mu^3$;
- $\operatorname{tr}M_a^3=3m_a^3+3m_a\lvert X_a\rvert^2+\operatorname{tr}X_a^3$;
- $3\operatorname{tr}(S\operatorname{adj}M_a)=3\operatorname{tr}(XX_a^2)-3m_a\operatorname{tr}(XX_a)+\tfrac34\mu\lvert X_a\rvert^2-\tfrac92\mu m_a^2$;
- $3K_{ab}\operatorname{tr}(M_aM_b)=9K_{ab}m_am_b+3K_{ab}\operatorname{tr}(X_aX_b)$.

So, with $C_{abc}=\operatorname{tr}(X_aX_bX_c)$ (totally symmetric for symmetric matrices) and $G_{ab}=\operatorname{tr}(X_aX_b)$,
$$P(W)=6\sum_aC_{aaa}+9\sum_{a\ne b}C_{aab}+12\sum_{a<b<c}C_{abc}+\tfrac32\sum_{a\ne b}\big(K_{ab}-m_a-m_b-\tfrac12\mu\big)G_{ab}+P_0,$$
$$P_0=-\tfrac34\mu^3+3\sum_am_a^3-\tfrac92\mu\sum_am_a^2+9\sum_{a<b}K_{ab}m_am_b+\sum_{a<b}K_{ab}^3+3\sum_{a<b<c}K_{ab}K_{ac}K_{bc},$$
**with no $\lvert X_a\rvert^2$ term at all**: its coefficient $3m_a-3m_a+\tfrac34\mu-\tfrac34\mu$ collects $3m_a$ from $\operatorname{tr}M_a^3$, $-3m_a$ from the diagonal of $-3m_a\operatorname{tr}(XX_a)$ (since $\operatorname{tr}(XX_a)=\sum_bG_{ab}$ includes $b=a$), and the two $\tfrac34\mu$ terms. *A first draft kept $3m_a$ here and failed the screen against the trusted $Q$ by exactly that term. The corrected formula agrees to ten digits at $n=5,6,7$.* The cubic coefficients come from $2\operatorname{tr}X^3=2\sum C_{aaa}+6\sum_{a\ne b}C_{aab}+12\sum_{a<b<c}C_{abc}$, $3\sum_a\operatorname{tr}(XX_a^2)=3\sum C_{aaa}+3\sum_{a\ne b}C_{aab}$ and $\sum_a\operatorname{tr}X_a^3$.

$$\lVert W\rVert^2=\sum_{a\ne b}G_{ab}+2\sum_a\lvert X_a\rvert^2+\tfrac34\mu^2+3\sum_am_a^2+\sum_{a<b}K_{ab}^2 .$$

**What depends on the frames.** For fixed spectra of the $X_a$ and fixed $(m,K)$, the value and the norm depend on the relative frames only through the Gram matrix $G$ and the cubic invariants $C$. The norm depends on them only through $\sum_{a\ne b}G_{ab}$. *At $\lvert J\rvert=2$ the analogous $C$ vanish identically and $P$ is linear in $G$, which is L224's rank-one mechanism. At $\lvert J\rvert=3$ the $C$ terms carry coefficients $6,9,12$ and do not vanish.*

**Check at $m=1$** ($n=4$): $3m_1=0$, $P=6\operatorname{tr}X_1^3$, $\lVert W\rVert^2=2\lvert X_1\rvert^2$, so $\theta\le6\cdot\tfrac1{\sqrt6}/2^{3/2}=\tfrac{\sqrt3}2=\Theta_4$ by L225, with equality at $X_1\propto\operatorname{diag}(2,-1,-1)$, the $S^2\times S^2$ tensor. So the $\lvert J\rvert=3$ step holds at $n=4$ with L225 alone, every block trivially commuting.

**Check at $m=2$** ($n=5$): $m_1=m_2=m$, $K_{12}=-3m$, and
$$P=6(\operatorname{tr}X_1^3+\operatorname{tr}X_2^3)+9\big(\operatorname{tr}X_1^2X_2+\operatorname{tr}X_1X_2^2\big)-18m\operatorname{tr}(X_1X_2)-72m^3,\qquad\lVert W\rVert^2=2(\lvert X_1\rvert^2+\lvert X_2\rvert^2)+2\operatorname{tr}(X_1X_2)+18m^2 .$$

## Batch 2, a maximiser on $\mathcal S_J$ is a critical point of $P$ on $\mathcal W_n$, and the criticality system

$Q$ is $O(n)$-equivariant and $\mathcal S_J\cap\mathcal W_n$ is the fixed space of $H_J$ in $\mathcal W_n$, so $Q$ maps it to itself. At a maximiser $W_*$ of $P$ on its unit sphere, $Q(W_*)-\theta W_*$ lies in $\mathcal S_J\cap\mathcal W_n$ and is orthogonal to $W_*^\perp$ there, hence is a multiple of $W_*$, and pairing with $W_*$ makes it zero. **So $Q(W_*)=\theta W_*$: a maximiser on the subalgebra is a genuine critical point of $P$ on $\mathcal W_n$**, and L219 applies to it at every reflection.

Read on the block $V_a$, $\tfrac13\partial P/\partial M_a=Q(W)\lvert_{V_a}+(\text{Ric}^*h)\lvert_{V_a}$ where $h$ is the Lagrange multiplier of the Ricci constraint and $(\mathrm{Ric}^*h)\lvert_{V_a}=H+h_aI$ with $H$ the $J$-block of $h$, **common to every $a$**. Differentiating item 3 of L226 gives, with $Z=S^2+\operatorname{adj}S+\sum_b\operatorname{adj}M_b$ absorbed into $H'=H-Z+\tfrac12(\operatorname{tr}Z)I$,
$$M_a^2+SM_a+M_aS-(\operatorname{tr}S)M_a-(\operatorname{tr}M_a)S+\sum_bK_{ab}M_b-\theta M_a=H'+h_a'I\quad\text{for every }a\in A .$$
Taking the commutator with $M_a$ kills the scalar and gives
$$[M_a^2,N]-(\operatorname{tr}M_a)[M_a,N]+\sum_bK_{ab}[M_a,M_b]=[M_a,H'] ,$$
one matrix equation per outer index, with one unknown symmetric $H'$ shared by all of them. **Commuting blocks solve it with $H'$ diagonal in the common frame.** Whether every solution at a maximiser has commuting blocks is the open question, and this system is where a direct attack would start.

## Batch 3, the reflection split at $e_1$ reduces the step to a quadratic form on $(\mathbb R^2)^m$

The sign change $\varepsilon_1$ normalises $H_J$, so it acts on $\mathcal S_J$, and its fixed space there is $\mathcal S_{\{2,3\}}$, a $\lvert J\rvert=2$ subalgebra where L224 holds. Split $W=E+O$ with $E\in\mathcal S_{\{2,3\}}$ and $O$ odd. By L219's parity argument (an automorphism fixing $E$ and reversing $O$), $P(W)=P(E)+3T(E,O,O)$ and $\lVert W\rVert^2=\lVert E\rVert^2+\lVert O\rVert^2$.

**The odd part.** In the frame $\varphi_{23},\varphi_{31},\varphi_{12}\leftrightarrow e_1,e_2,e_3$ of $\Lambda^2\mathbb R^J$, the entries of $S$ and of each $M_a$ carrying exactly one index $1$ are the off-diagonal entries of row $1$: $O$ is the tuple $x_a=((M_a)_{12},(M_a)_{13})\in\mathbb R^2$, $a\in A$, with $s=(S_{12},S_{13})=\sum_ax_a$ forced by $S=N-\tfrac12(\operatorname{tr}N)I$, and $\lVert O\rVert^2=2\lvert s\rvert^2+2\sum_a\lvert x_a\rvert^2$. The even part $E$ has $S^E=\operatorname{diag}(\sigma,S')$, $M_a^E=\operatorname{diag}(\mu_a,M_a')$ with $S',M_a'$ symmetric $2\times2$, and $K$.

**The quadratic form**, read off as the part of L226's item 3 quadratic in the odd entries, using $\operatorname{adj}\begin{pmatrix}\mu&x^{\mathsf T}\\x&M'\end{pmatrix}=\begin{pmatrix}\det M'&-(\operatorname{adj}M'\,x)^{\mathsf T}\\-\operatorname{adj}M'\,x&\mu\operatorname{adj}M'-(\lvert x\rvert^2I-xx^{\mathsf T})\end{pmatrix}$ and $\det\begin{pmatrix}\sigma&s^{\mathsf T}\\s&S'\end{pmatrix}=\sigma\det S'-\langle s,\operatorname{adj}S'\,s\rangle$:
$$3T(E,O,O)=3\sigma\lvert s\rvert^2+6\langle s,S'^0s\rangle+\sum_a\Big[3\mu_a\lvert x_a\rvert^2+3\langle x_a,M_a'x_a\rangle+3\langle x_a,(S'-(\operatorname{tr}S')I)x_a\rangle-6\langle s,\operatorname{adj}(M_a')\,x_a\rangle\Big]+6\sum_{a<b}K_{ab}\langle x_a,x_b\rangle ,$$
with $S'^0$ the traceless part of $S'$ (the $S'$ and $\operatorname{adj}S'$ contributions of $\operatorname{tr}S^3$ and $3\det S$ combining as $S'-\operatorname{adj}S'=2S'^0$). *To be screened against the trusted $Q$ before anything is built on it.*

**Its shape.** Writing $x$ as an $m\times2$ matrix with rows $x_a$, and $Y=\tfrac12(\operatorname{tr}Y)I+Y^0$ for the $2\times2$ blocks, the form is
$$3T(E,O,O)=\operatorname{tr}(x^{\mathsf T}Ax)+\operatorname{tr}(x^{\mathsf T}B_zx\,\sigma_z)+\operatorname{tr}(x^{\mathsf T}B_xx\,\sigma_x),\qquad\lVert O\rVert^2=\operatorname{tr}\big(x^{\mathsf T}(2I+2\mathbf 1\mathbf 1^{\mathsf T})x\big),$$
with $A,B_z,B_x$ symmetric $m\times m$ built from the scalars $(\sigma,\mu_a,\operatorname{tr}S',\operatorname{tr}M_a',K)$ and from the components of the traceless $S'^0,M_a'^0$ along $\sigma_z=\operatorname{diag}(1,-1)$ and $\sigma_x$. The rotation of the $(2,3)$-plane acts on $x$ by a single angle and on $(B_z,B_x)$ by the double angle.

**Where L224's mechanism lands, and where it stops.** *If every $x_a$ is parallel to one unit vector $u$, rotate the $(2,3)$-plane to put $u=e_2$: then every $(M_a)_{13}$ and $S_{13}$ vanish, $W$ is $\varepsilon_3$-even, so $W\in\mathcal S_{\{1,2\}}$ and L224 gives $P(W)\le\Theta_n\lVert W\rVert^3$ at once.* **So the $\lvert J\rvert=3$ step follows from L224 exactly when, for the $E$ of a maximiser, the pencil $\big(3T(E,\cdot,\cdot),\lVert\cdot\rVert^2\big)$ on $(\mathbb R^2)^m$ has a top eigenvector of rank one as an $m\times2$ matrix**, since then $W'=E+O'$ with $O'$ that eigenvector scaled to $\lVert O\rVert$ has $P(W')\ge P(W)$, $\lVert W'\rVert=\lVert W\rVert$, and $W'$ lies in a $\lvert J\rvert=2$ subalgebra. At $\lvert J\rvert=2$ the analogous form has $B_z=B_x=0$ (L224's $q_E(y)+q_E(x)$ is $\operatorname{tr}(x^{\mathsf T}Ax)$ with $A$ the form $q_E$), and a top eigenvector of $A\otimes I$ can always be taken of rank one. **With $B_z=0$ and $B_x\ne0$ one can still rotate $B_x$ into $B_z$ and the form is $ (A\pm B)\otimes$ the two coordinate lines, again rank one. With both $B_z$ and $B_x$ nonzero and not proportional as matrices, the top eigenvector need not have rank one**: for $A=0$, $B_z=\sigma_z$, $B_x=\sigma_x$ (so $m=2$), the vector $x=I_2$ gives value $4$ at norm $2$, while every rank-one $x=uv^{\mathsf T}$ gives at most $1$ per unit norm. *So the extension of L224's argument is not a formality: it needs a property of the $A,B_z,B_x$ that a Weyl $E\in\mathcal S_{\{2,3\}}$ produces, or a different comparison tensor.*

**The precise remaining statement.** Either (i) for every $E\in\mathcal S_{\{2,3\}}\cap\mathcal W_n$ the pencil above has a rank-one top eigenvector, which closes the step with L224, or (ii) there is a unit $W\in\mathcal S_J\cap\mathcal W_n$ with non-commuting blocks and $P(W)$ above every commuting configuration of the same norm, which is a fence post for R27 in this form, or (iii) neither, and the step needs the criticality system of batch 2 in addition to the split. **A screen at $n=5,6,7$ decides between (ii) and not-(ii) in seconds and is run next. It establishes nothing positive.**

**Screens, 2026-09-15** ([screen_I106.py](../scripts/misc/screen_I106.py)): the corrected formula and the norm agree with the trusted $Q$ at $n=5,6,7$ on random Weyl elements of $\mathcal S_J$, and batch 3's odd form equals $P(W)-P(E)$ to ten digits. A bounded random-restart ascent of $P/\lVert W\rVert^3$ on $\mathcal S_J\cap\mathcal W_n$ (60 starts, two minutes in all) reached $\Theta_5=0.942809$, $\Theta_6=1.054093$ and $\Theta_7=1.095445$ exactly, each at blocks commuting to $10^{-8}$ or better, the sphere products $S^2\times S^3$, $S^3\times S^3$ and $S^3\times S^4$. **It refutes nothing and proves nothing. It says the step is not false at small $n$ and that case (ii) of batch 3 did not appear.**

## Batch 4, the step at $n=5$ is PROVED, by a change of variables

At $m=2$ write $U=X_1+X_2$ and $V=X_1-X_2$, a bijection of pairs of traceless symmetric matrices. Then $\operatorname{tr}X_1^3+\operatorname{tr}X_2^3=\tfrac14\operatorname{tr}U^3+\tfrac34\operatorname{tr}(UV^2)$, $\operatorname{tr}X_1^2X_2+\operatorname{tr}X_1X_2^2=\tfrac14\operatorname{tr}U^3-\tfrac14\operatorname{tr}(UV^2)$ and $\operatorname{tr}(X_1X_2)=\tfrac14(\lvert U\rvert^2-\lvert V\rvert^2)$, so batch 1's $m=2$ formula becomes
$$P=\tfrac{15}4\operatorname{tr}U^3+\tfrac94\operatorname{tr}(UV^2)-\tfrac92m\lvert U\rvert^2+\tfrac92m\lvert V\rvert^2-72m^3,\qquad\lVert W\rVert^2=\tfrac32\lvert U\rvert^2+\tfrac12\lvert V\rvert^2+18m^2 .$$
**Fix $U$, $m$ and the spectrum of $V$. Every term but $\operatorname{tr}(UV^2)$ is then fixed, the norm included**, and $\operatorname{tr}(UV^2)=\langle U,V^2\rangle\le\sum_i\lambda_i^\downarrow(U)\lambda_i^\downarrow(V^2)$ by von Neumann, with equality at a $V$ diagonal in an eigenframe of $U$ with its eigenvalues ordered to match. That $V$ commutes with $U$, so $X_1=\tfrac12(U+V)$ and $X_2=\tfrac12(U-V)$ commute, and $W'$ built from them has $P(W')\ge P(W)$ and $\lVert W'\rVert=\lVert W\rVert$. A commuting configuration is a coordinate-diagonal tensor in a rotated frame of $\mathbb R^J$, so $P(W')\le\Theta_5\lVert W'\rVert^3$ by [L57](../findings/problem.md). **Hence $\sup P=\Theta_5$ on the unit sphere of $\mathcal S_J\cap\mathcal W_5$, attained at commuting blocks, and the $\lvert J\rvert=3$ step of R27 holds at $n=5$.** *Attained also at some non-commuting blocks whenever von Neumann's equality allows it, for instance $V$ with spectrum $(\nu,-\nu,0)$ rotated inside a two-dimensional eigenspace of $V^2$. The statement R27 needs is attainment, not uniqueness.* To be typed: the three trace identities in $U,V$ and the von Neumann step.

## Batch 5, what the change of variables does and does not give at $m\ge3$

Fix all blocks but $X_a,X_b$ and put $U=X_a+X_b$, $V=X_a-X_b$, $R'=\sum_{c\ne a,b}X_c$. The terms of $P$ whose value depends on the frame of $V$ at fixed $U$, fixed spectrum of $V$ and fixed other blocks are
$$\tfrac94\operatorname{tr}(UV^2)+\tfrac32\operatorname{tr}(V^2R')+\operatorname{tr}\Big(V\sum_{c\ne a,b}(\beta_{ac}-\beta_{bc})X_c\Big),\qquad\beta_{ab}=\tfrac32\big(K_{ab}-m_a-m_b-\tfrac12\mu\big),$$
and the norm depends on that frame not at all. *So a pairwise optimum aligns $V$ with $Z=\tfrac94U+\tfrac32R'$ when the linear term vanishes, which is alignment with a matrix other than $U$, and with a linear term present two alignments compete.* **This is the ordering question in explicit form: a quadratic alignment against $Z_{ab}$ and a linear one against $L_{ab}$, per pair.** It is a finite question about symmetric $3\times3$ matrices, and it is where a direct proof at $m\ge3$ would go next, together with batch 2's criticality system, which every maximiser satisfies.

**Screen 3** ([screen_I106.py](../scripts/misc/screen_I106.py)): for thirty random Weyl $E\in\mathcal S_{\{2,3\}}$ at each of $n=5,6,7$, the top eigenvector of batch 3's pencil had second singular value between $2\%$ and $27\%$ of the first, **never rank one**, while $\sup_t[P(E)+3\lambda(E)t]/(\lVert E\rVert^2+t)^{3/2}$ stayed below $\Theta_n$ on every sample (worst $0.94\,\Theta_5$, $0.82\,\Theta_6$, $0.78\,\Theta_7$). *So case (i) of batch 3 is false as a statement about every even part, case (ii) did not appear, and the step, if true at $m\ge3$, is true for a reason the split alone does not carry: the maximiser's own structure, or a change of variables as in batch 4.*

## Batch 6, the two structures a proof at $m\ge3$ can use

**The fluctuation form of the cubic.** Write $X_a=\tfrac1mX+Y_a$ with $\sum_aY_a=0$. Then, with $T=\sum_aY_a^2\succeq0$,
$$6\sum_aC_{aaa}+9\sum_{a\ne b}C_{aab}+12\sum_{a<b<c}C_{abc}=\frac{(2m+1)(m+1)}{m^2}\operatorname{tr}X^3+\Big(3+\frac3m\Big)\operatorname{tr}(XT)+\sum_a\operatorname{tr}Y_a^3,$$
and $\lVert W\rVert^2=(1+\tfrac1m)\lvert X\rvert^2+\sum_a\lvert Y_a\rvert^2+(\text{scalars})$, **frame-free**. At $m=2$ the last term vanishes ($Y_1=-Y_2$), which is batch 4. At $m\ge3$, $\sum_a\operatorname{tr}Y_a^3$ under $\sum_aY_a=0$ is the one cubic invariant of the fluctuations that no single alignment controls, and the Gram part $\sum_{a\ne b}\beta_{ab}\big[\tfrac1m\operatorname{tr}(X(Y_a+Y_b))+\operatorname{tr}(Y_aY_b)\big]$ adds the pairwise alignments. A common rotation of all $Y_a$ against $X$ is one von Neumann step, aligning $X$ with $(3+\tfrac3m)T+\tfrac2m\sum_a\gamma_aY_a$, $\gamma_a=\sum_{b\ne a}\beta_{ab}$.

**The Klein-four split.** The sign changes $\varepsilon_1,\varepsilon_2,\varepsilon_3$ on $J$ act on $\mathcal S_J$ with fixed space $\mathcal D_n$ and three nontrivial characters $\varepsilon_i\varepsilon_j$, so $\mathcal S_J\cap\mathcal W_n=\mathcal D_n\oplus O_{12}\oplus O_{13}\oplus O_{23}$ with $O_{ij}\cong\mathbb R^m$ the $(i,j)$ entries $x^{ij}_a=(M_a)_{ij}$ (and $S_{ij}=\sum_ax^{ij}_a$ forced), $\lVert O_{ij}\rVert^2=2\lvert x^{ij}\rvert^2+2(\sum_ax^{ij}_a)^2$, and $\mathcal D_n\oplus O_{ij}=\mathcal S_{\{i,j\}}$ is L224's subalgebra. Characters make
$$P(W)=P(D)+3\sum_{i<j}T(D,O_{ij},O_{ij})+6\,t(x^{12},x^{13},x^{23}),\qquad t(x,y,z)=\sum_ax_ay_az_a+\sum_a\big[\bar x\,y_az_a+\bar y\,x_az_a+\bar z\,x_ay_a\big]+2\bar x\bar y\bar z,$$
$\bar x=\sum_ax_a$, read off from L226's item 3 ($6M_{12}M_{13}M_{23}$ in $\operatorname{tr}M^3$, $2S_{12}S_{13}S_{23}$ in $\det S$, $S_{ij}M_{ik}M_{jk}$ in $\operatorname{tr}(S\operatorname{adj}M)$, equivalently $t=\sum_a\hat x_a\hat y_a\hat z_a-(m+1)\bar x\bar y\bar z$ with $\hat x_a=x_a+\bar x$). **L224 bounds each $P(D)+3T(D,O_{ij},O_{ij})$ by $\Theta_n(\lVert D\rVert^2+\lVert O_{ij}\rVert^2)^{3/2}$. The step at $\lvert J\rvert=3$ is the same bound for the sum of the three, with the trilinear $t$ coupling them, and $t$ cannot be discarded, since the equality set (an $O(3)$-rotation of a sphere product) has all three odd parts nonzero.** At a maximiser, L219 at each of the three reflections gives $L_{E_{ij}}O_{ij}=\tfrac\theta2O_{ij}$ with $E_{ij}$ the $\varepsilon_i\varepsilon_j$-even part, three equations the proof at $m\ge3$ can consume.

**Screen of batch 6** ([screen_I106_batch6.py](../scripts/misc/screen_I106_batch6.py), on Opus): the fluctuation form of the cubic and of the frame part of the norm agree exactly at $m=3,4,5$, and $P-P(D)-\sum_{i<j}3T(D,O_{ij},O_{ij})=6t$ at $n=5,6,7$ in both written forms of $t$. Both formulas are safe to build on. A screen, not a proof.

## Where this leaves R27, and the recommendation

- **The abandon condition is not met.** The $\lvert J\rvert=3$ step does not reduce to a decoupled bound: at $n=5$ it is proved by a change of variables that keeps the coupling ($\operatorname{tr}(UV^2)$ against a frame-free norm), and at $m\ge3$ every formulation found keeps a genuine coupling ($t$, or $\sum\operatorname{tr}Y_a^3$ under $\sum Y_a=0$). Nothing here is F67.
- **No ordering conflict was found.** The screens put the maximiser at commuting blocks at $n=5,6,7$, and the naive alignment counts of the sweep are replaced by explicit competing alignments ($Z_{ab}$ against $L_{ab}$ per pair) that a proof must reconcile rather than a fence post.
- **What is settled**: the step at $n=4$ (L225 alone) and at $n=5$ (batch 4), both attained at commuting blocks. **What is open**: $m\ge3$.
- **Recommended order.** (1) Number batch 4 as a lemma, type the two $U,V$ identities and the von Neumann step, Gate 2 with two readers: settles the $n=5$ node, grade $2\times3\times1\times1=6$. (2) I106 proper at $m\ge3$ via the Klein-four split plus L219 at three reflections and batch 2's system, the $m=3$ case first: the theorem is the step itself, grade $2\times2\times1\times1=4$. (3) Only if (2) meets a genuine obstruction, record it as the fence post the plan anticipated, with the pair $(Z_{ab},L_{ab})$ named. Fable's part ends here: batches 1 to 6 are on disk, and recording, typing and dispatch belong to Opus.
