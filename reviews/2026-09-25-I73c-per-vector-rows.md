# I73 (c): criticality is an operator identity, so the per-index rows hold at every vector of an eigenspace

*Working note, 2026-09-25. Derived in session; not yet audited. Screens by [screen_I73c_per_vector_ceiling.py](../scripts/misc/screen_I73c_per_vector_ceiling.py).*

## The ingredient the class system discards

$Q(\mathcal W_n)\subseteq\mathcal W_n$ ([L234](../findings/second-order.md), step (1)), so at a critical point $W^\#=\theta W-W^2$ holds as an **operator** identity on $\Lambda^2\mathbb R^n$. With $W=\sum_a\lambda_aP_a$ and $B_{ab}=P_a\#P_b\succeq0$ (a sum of $\tfrac12[\psi_\gamma,\psi_\delta]\otimes[\psi_\gamma,\psi_\delta]$ over $\gamma\in E_a$, $\delta\in E_b$):
$$\sum_{a,b}B_{ab}=(n-2)\,\mathrm{Id},\qquad\sum_{a,b}\lambda_bB_{ab}=-W,\qquad\sum_{a,b}\lambda_a\lambda_bB_{ab}=\sum_a\lambda_a(\theta-\lambda_a)P_a .$$
**Every right side is scalar on each eigenspace $E_c$.** The class system uses the traces of these identities against $P_c$, the per-index rows averaged over a basis; the identities themselves say that for EVERY unit $x\in E_c$ the numbers $b_{ab}(x)=\langle B_{ab}x,x\rangle\ge0$ satisfy the three rows with $E_c$'s right sides, and $\sum_bb_{ab}(x)\le\tfrac12\kappa_{m_a}(x)$ with $\kappa_r(x)$ the sum of the top $r$ eigenvalues of $-\mathrm{ad}_x^2$, **which depends on $x$**.

## Checked on a real critical point

At $S^5\times S^7$ (unit $W$, $\theta^2=\tfrac{264}{175}$), $W^\#=\theta W-W^2$ holds to $10^{-16}$, and at random unit $x$ in each eigenspace the three rows hold with constants $n-2$, $-\lambda_c$, $\lambda_c(\theta-\lambda_c)$. **The per-vector $\theta$-ceiling is tight there**: over 200 random unit $x$ per eigenspace the smallest ceiling is exactly $1.2282$, $S^5\times S^7$'s own $\theta$, in both extreme classes. *So the caps are sound on the one catalogued three-value critical point near the threshold, and sharp on it.*

## First consequence, on the symmetric family

At $\lambda=(-a,0,a)$, $a=1/\sqrt{2p}$ at unit $W$, rows (B) and (C) at unit $x\in E_0$ give $2b_{02}=b_{00}+b_{22}+\theta\sqrt{2p}+1$, so $x$ sends bracket mass at least $\theta\sqrt{2p}+1\approx7.06$ ($p=12$, $\theta\ge\theta_0$) from $E_0$ into $E_2$; $x$ lies in its own kernel, so the $E_0$-sourced mass is capped by $\kappa_{11}(x)$. **The per-vector ceiling in $E_0$ at $p=12$** (a screen of the six-variable linear programme over angle types): equal angles $1.0206$, rank four with equal angles $1.2247$, both below $\theta_0(12)=1.2360$; rank ten with equal angles $1.3472$; most other types a plateau $1.4289$. In $E_2$ every type allows $\theta\ge1.63$.

**So a symmetric three-value critical point at $p=12$ above $\theta_0(12)$ needs a negative eigenspace containing no equal-angle and no rank-four equal-angle element.** A necessary condition, not yet an exclusion: a twelve-dimensional subspace can a priori avoid those loci.

## The owed step: an averaged Ky Fan cap over a subspace

**Target.** For an $m$-dimensional subspace $V\subseteq\mathfrak{so}(n)$, a bound $\mathbb E_{x\in S(V)}\kappa_r(x)\le g(m,r)$, with $x$ uniform on the unit sphere of $V$, that falls below the global $\max\kappa_r$ once $m$ exceeds the largest dimension of a subspace of extremal elements. *Averaging the per-vector caps over a Haar-random orthonormal basis of $E_c$ then gives class-level caps $2\sum_{\alpha\in E_c}R_a(\psi_\alpha)\le m_c\,g(m_c,\cdot)$, linear in the class values and sharper than the global Ky Fan constants, which is what the 184 remaining survivors need.* **Why it can fall**: a linear space of decomposable bivectors has dimension at most $n-1$, so a twelve-dimensional eigenspace at $n=12$ must contain non-decomposable elements, whose $\kappa_r$ at large $r$ is smaller. *Not yet attempted beyond this sentence.*

**Two candidate geometric lemmas, screened.** *(a) Bounded rank*: a subspace of $\mathfrak{so}(12)$ whose elements all have rank at most $2$ has dimension at most $11$ (Meshulam's bound for alternating matrices), so a twelve-dimensional $E_0$ contains an element of rank at least $4$; **but that element can be nearly decomposable, and near-decomposable elements are good**, so rank alone decides nothing. *(b) Equal top angles*: the example $e_1\wedge\mathbb R^{11}\oplus\langle e_2\wedge e_3\rangle$ contains $(e_1\wedge e_4+e_2\wedge e_3)/\sqrt2$, with $s_1=s_2$ and bad; **but the largest ceiling over types with $s_1=s_2\ge$ the rest is $1.3077$** (at angles $\approx(0.49,0.49,0.41,0.41,0.41,0.11)$), above $\theta_0(12)$, so an element with equal top angles need not be bad. **What the bad set is**: the spread types, equal angles at full rank or at rank four. *So the joint statement has to be quantitative about spread, not a clean intersection with a locus.* Screens.

## The joint statement, as a concave envelope: spread averages are class variables

**The average spread of an eigenspace is class-level.** For $x=\sum_\alpha\xi_\alpha\Psi_\alpha$ with $\xi$ uniform on the unit sphere of $E_c$, $m=m_c$, the fourth moment $\mathbb E[\xi_\alpha\xi_\beta\xi_\gamma\xi_\delta]=(\delta_{\alpha\beta}\delta_{\gamma\delta}+\delta_{\alpha\gamma}\delta_{\beta\delta}+\delta_{\alpha\delta}\delta_{\beta\gamma})/(m(m+2))$ gives
$$\mathbb E\operatorname{tr}x^4=\frac{2G_{cc}+\sum_{\alpha,\beta}\operatorname{tr}(\Psi_\alpha\Psi_\beta\Psi_\alpha\Psi_\beta)}{m(m+2)}=\frac{3G_{cc}-\tau_{cc\cdot}}{m(m+2)},$$
using $\sum\Psi_\alpha^2=-A_c$ and $\operatorname{tr}(XYXY)=\operatorname{tr}(X^2Y^2)-\lVert[X,Y]\rVert^2$ in the record's norm. With angles $s_i$ of unit $x$, $\operatorname{tr}x^4=2\sum_is_i^4$, so $\mathbb E\sum_is_i^4=(3G_{cc}-\tau_{cc\cdot})/(2m(m+2))$. *At $S^5\times S^7$'s $\Lambda^2\mathbb R^5$ class this is $(240-60)/240=0.75$.*

**Why that closes the direction problem.** Every Ky Fan quantity is convex in $-\mathrm{ad}_x^2$, so averaging bounds it from below only. **But if $\kappa_r(x)\le\varphi_r\big(\sum_is_i^4\big)$ with $\varphi_r$ concave, Jensen runs the right way**: $\mathbb E_x\kappa_r(x)\le\varphi_r\big(\mathbb E\sum s_i^4\big)$, and the right side is class-level. Averaging the per-vector caps over a Haar-random orthonormal basis of $E_c$ then gives
$$2\sum_{\alpha\in E_c}R_a(\psi_\alpha)\ \le\ m_c\,\varphi_{r}\Big(\frac{3G_{cc}-\tau_{cc\cdot}}{2m_c(m_c+2)}\Big),$$
with the union version alike, and **each supporting line of the concave $\varphi_r$ is a constraint LINEAR in $(\tau,G)$**, which the class system does not have. *It is the averaged cap the previous section asked for, with the spread of the eigenspace, rather than its dimension, doing the work.*

**Owed**: $\varphi_r$, the concave upper envelope of $\kappa_r$ over angle types at fixed $\sum s_i^4$ (a six-parameter family, so a screen then a derivation), its supporting lines for the $r$ that occur, and a rerun of the survivors with the new constraints.

## L245 against the class system and the per-vector system: where it adds, and the lemma it needs

**Its trace consequences are already in the class system.** With $G_{ab}=\operatorname{tr}(A_aA_b)$, the rows $\sum_bG_{cb}=2(n-1)m_c$, $\sum_b\lambda_bG_{cb}=0$ and $G_{cb}\ge0$ put $(G_{c0},G_{c1},G_{c2})/2m_c$ on L245's own segment, so $G_{cc}\le2h_cm_c$ and every other traced form of $A_c\preceq h_cI$ follows from the rows. *L245 bites only pointwise, which no class variable carries.*

**Per vector it is weak.** For unit $x\in E_c$ with $\mathcal R_x=\tfrac12(-\mathrm{ad}_x^2)$ and $\mathcal S_x$ the anticommutator form $y\mapsto\tfrac14\lVert\{x,y\}\rVert_F^2$, the identity $\mathcal R_x+\mathcal S_x=D(xx^{\mathsf T})$ (the derivation extension of $xx^{\mathsf T}$ to $\Lambda^2$) gives $R_a(x)+S_a(x)=\operatorname{tr}(A_axx^{\mathsf T})=2t_a(x)$, with $t(x)$ a convex combination of segment points, hence on the segment, and $\sum_aS_a=n$, $\sum_a\lambda_aS_a=\lambda_c$. At the symmetric $p=12$ spectrum this caps $b_{02}\le11-2\sum_is_i^4$, far above the Ky Fan cap. *Checked on decomposable $x$ over all of $\Lambda^2$: $R=n-2$, $S=n$, sum $2(n-1)$.*

**Second moments cannot separate a star from a spread isotropy matrix.** $\mathbb E_{x\in S(V)}\sum_is_i^4=\dfrac{2\operatorname{tr}A_V^2+C_V}{2m(m+2)}$ with $C_V=\sum_{i,j}\operatorname{tr}(x_ix_jx_ix_j)$, $\lvert C_V\rvert\le4m$ (write $C_V=\operatorname{tr}(X^2F)$, $X=\sum_ix_i\otimes x_i$, $F$ the flip); star check $2(d^2+d)+2d=2d(d+2)$. With $\operatorname{tr}A_V^2\le2h\,m$ this gives only $m\le2(h+1)/q-2$ when every element has $\sum s^4\ge q$, which is $11$ at $q=1$, $h=5.5$: an $8$-dimensional star ($\lambda_{\max}=8$) and an $A_V$ with eigenvalue $5.5$ on three directions have the same trace data.

**The firing region of the general certificate at $\lambda_1=0$.** With $\lambda=(-a,0,b)$ and $\rho=a/b=\lvert\lambda_0\rvert/\lambda_2$, the constant of `general_identity` is $-\tfrac{a\,p_0}{2b(a+b)^2}\,Q$ with $Q=a^4+2a^3b+a^3\theta-(n-2)a^2b^2+2a^2b\theta-2b^3\theta$, so the certificate fires where $Q>0$, i.e.
$$\theta\,(\rho^3+2\rho^2-2)\ >\ b\,\rho^2\,(n-2-2\rho-\rho^2).$$
**At $\rho=1$ this is $\theta/a>n-5$, L247's band.** *For $\rho^3+2\rho^2-2>0$ (i.e. $\rho>0.839\ldots$) it is a lower bound on $\theta/b$ that grows like $\rho^2 n/(\rho^3+2\rho^2-2)$; for $\rho<0.839\ldots$ the left side is negative while the right is positive for $n\ge5$, so the certificate never fires: the extension reaches only spectra whose negative eigenvalue is not much smaller in size than the positive one.* With unit $W$ and $\operatorname{tr}W=0$ ($p_0a=p_2b$, $p_0a^2+p_2b^2=1$) the band sits, as before, where $p_0$ is near its maximum.
