## Claim 1: the classification

**1a, CONFIRMED.** [routes.md](../routes.md) line 22 carries R6 exactly as quoted: closed, padded $W_{\mathbb{CP}^2}$ a local max and Plücker-obstructed, "the record's lifting lemma renamed", criterion "separate a maximizer from an obstructed local max by something other than the value". [diagonality.md](../findings/diagonality.md) line 65 carries $\max\mathop{\mathrm{spec}}=0.5$, the $2$-dimensional $(-0.408248)$ eigenspace and $\min\lVert\omega\wedge\omega\rVert=0.5$. Candidate 1 (a decomposable vector in the top eigenspace) is R6's step "force a maximiser to be diagonalisable" at one eigenspace, and Plücker obstruction is exactly its failure, so the identification holds.

- **$t^2=\tfrac12$ in the maximal frame, checked by hand and against the record.** For $W=W^+$ on $\mathbb R^4$ and any frame, $e_{12},e_{34}$ share the self-dual component $\omega_1$, so $a_{12}=a_{34}=\tfrac12\langle W^+\omega_1,\omega_1\rangle$, likewise for the other two pairings, and $t^2=\tfrac12\sum_a\langle W^+\omega_a,\omega_a\rangle^2\le\tfrac12\lVert W^+\rVert^2=\tfrac12$, attained at the eigenbasis. Over $O(n)$ for the padded tensor, the record's frame-minimal off-diagonal mass $0.707$ ([diagonality.md](../findings/diagonality.md) line 21) gives $t^2=1-0.5=\tfrac12$. The value $\theta(W_{\mathbb{CP}^2})=\sqrt{3/2}$ exactly ([INDEX-results.md](../findings/INDEX-results.md) row 10) is what "(MG)'s $\sqrt{3/2}$ branch covers it" means, with equality.

**1b, REFUTED.** R6's criterion asks to tell apart two different tensors, a maximiser and an obstructed local maximum such as padded $W_{\mathbb{CP}^2}$. Frame maximality is a property of a frame, and every tensor has a globally maximal frame, padded $W_{\mathbb{CP}^2}$ and every other obstructed critical point included. So no frame condition, at any order, separates one tensor from another. What it does separate is the maximiser in its maximal frame from the same maximiser in a non-maximal frame. That is exactly [post F81](../findings/fence-posts.md)'s witness, which is the balanced product rotated by $\tfrac\pi4$ in $m$ planes, the maximiser itself, not an obstructed point.

- The sweep satisfies F81's "what a successor must clear" (use the maximal frame, at fourth order across several planes) and reports it as satisfying R6's criterion. They are two different requirements.
- Correction: the frame vehicle meets F81's requirement. Whether it meets R6's criterion depends on what the small-mass argument then does with the maximal frame. The only statement it can prove there is a bound on $\theta$ at critical points of small maximal-frame mass, which is a value statement over a locus, the form R6 says fails when obstructed points live on that locus.
- The Phase 3b tie-break ("I121 carries the one non-value separator on the table ... I73 carries none") rests on 1b and falls with it.

**1c, PLAUSIBLE with a correction.** The node is logically weaker than the lifting lemma. Lifting (the maximiser is coordinate-diagonal) gives $t=1$ in the maximal frame and so empties the small-mass node, while the node asks only that a maximiser with small maximal-frame mass have $\theta\le\sqrt{3/2}$. "The lifting lemma's small-mass part" is a fair gloss of that.

- **"R6's own obstruction ... is not a small-mass point" is true of padded $W_{\mathbb{CP}^2}$ and incomplete as an account of the node.** The record's other Plücker-obstructed critical points, $\mathbb{CP}^4$ and $\mathbb{CP}^5$ ([diagonality.md](../findings/diagonality.md) line 19), have frame-minimal off-diagonal mass $0.837$ and $0.866$ (line 21), so maximal-frame $t^2\approx0.30$ and $0.25$. This matches $t^2=\tfrac3{2(m+1)}=\tfrac3{n+2}$, consistent with $\kappa^2=\tfrac3{m+1}$ in [INDEX-values.md](../findings/INDEX-values.md) up to that file's normalisation, so $t\sim n^{-1/2}$. **The obstructed family lives at the node's own scale.**
- It does not threaten (MG), since $\theta^2(\mathbb{CP}^m)=\tfrac{(m+1)(2m-1)}{3m(m-1)}<\tfrac32$ at every $m\ge3$ ([INDEX-values.md](../findings/INDEX-values.md) line 51). What it shows is that the small-mass locus does contain obstructed critical points and they are cleared only by their value. The sentence should say so, instead of implying the node holds no obstructed points.

## Claim 2: the matching-rotation reduction

**Algebra CONFIRMED, derived here by hand.** On $\{e_{ik},e_{il},e_{jk},e_{jl}\}=\mathbb R^2_{\{i,j\}}\otimes\mathbb R^2_{\{k,l\}}$, with $\mathop{\mathrm{tr}}(P\otimes Q)^2=4$, the coefficients are $\alpha_{IX}=\tfrac12(x^f_i+x^f_j)$, $\alpha_{ZX}=\tfrac12(x^f_i-x^f_j)$ (with $x^f_i=W_{ik,il}$), and symmetrically $\alpha_{XI},\alpha_{XZ}$ from $x^e_k,x^e_l$. $\alpha_{XX}=\tfrac12(v+w)$ and $\alpha_{JJ}=\tfrac12(v-w)$. So a vanishing pair part kills exactly $\alpha_{IX},\alpha_{XI},\alpha_{ZX},\alpha_{XZ}$.

- A rotation by $\varphi$ in both planes acts on the block as $R(\varphi)\otimes R(\varphi)$, and $R Z R^{\mathsf T}=cZ+sX$ in both factors, so $\alpha_{IZ}\mapsto c\,\alpha_{IZ}$, $\alpha_{ZI}\mapsto c\,\alpha_{ZI}$, $\alpha_{ZZ}\mapsto c^2\alpha_{ZZ}+s^2\alpha_{XX}$, $\alpha_{II}$ fixed. With $u=s^2$, per block the change over $4$ is $-uS_{IZ}+2u\,\alpha_{ZZ}(\alpha_{XX}-\alpha_{ZZ})+u^2(\alpha_{XX}-\alpha_{ZZ})^2$, which sums to $u\,g(u)$ with $g$ exactly as written.
- The diagonal of the rotated operator on a block depends on that block alone, since the rotation preserves its span. The edge entries $a_e$ are fixed. With $M$ perfect every pair of indices is an edge or lies in exactly one block, so the sum over blocks is the whole change.
- $f(\varphi)\le f(0)$ for all $\varphi$ iff $g\le0$ on $(0,1]$ iff $g(0)\le0$ and $g(1)\le0$, $g$ being affine. $f-f(0)\approx16\varphi^2g(0)$, so $g(0)\le0$ is the second-order condition along $\xi_M$. $g(1)=\sum(\alpha_{XX}^2-\alpha_{IZ}^2-\alpha_{ZI}^2-\alpha_{ZZ}^2)$, and with $E(M)=4\sum\alpha_{XX}^2$, $\tfrac14s_{ef}^2=4\alpha_{II}^2$ and $t^2-\sum a_e^2=4\sum(\alpha_{II}^2+\alpha_{IZ}^2+\alpha_{ZI}^2+\alpha_{ZZ}^2)$ at $x=0$, $g(1)\le0$ is the matching inequality of [2026-09-28b](2026-09-28b-LIT-step8-sweep.md) line 93 at $x=0$, exactly.
- F81's witness: blocks have constant diagonal, so $\sum a_e^2+\tfrac14\sum s^2=t^2=\tfrac1n$, and the $\tfrac\pi4$ matching rotation returns the product's own frame, $f(\tfrac\pi4)=1$, so $E(M)=1-\tfrac1n=\tfrac{11}{12}$ at $n=12$. CONFIRMED. The fitted $\tfrac1n+(1-\tfrac1n)\sin^4 2\varphi$ of [r1833](REVIEW_r1833.md) is $4u\,g(u)$ with $g(0)=0$, which is why second order is blind to it.

**Corrections.**

- **Scope: pair-free only.** With a pair part, $\alpha_{IZ}\mapsto c\,\alpha_{IZ}\pm s\,\alpha_{IX}$ and $\alpha_{ZZ}$ gains $cs(\alpha_{ZX}+\alpha_{XZ})$, so $f$ is a degree-four polynomial in $(c,s)$ with odd terms, and "exactly two conditions" fails. The bold sentence "the fourth-order multi-plane separator, along matching rotations, is the matching inequality itself" must carry "for pair-free $W$". A maximiser in its maximal frame is not known to be pair-free.
- **Orientation.** "Rotate every plane of $M$ by the same angle" depends on an orientation of each plane. Reversing one plane's orientation flips $\alpha_{XX}$ on every block containing it and leaves $\alpha_{ZZ},\alpha_{IZ},\alpha_{ZI}$ fixed, so $S_{ZX}$, hence $g(0)$ and $g'$, change sign pattern, while $g(1)$ does not. The family is $2^{n/2-1}$ families, with one second-order condition $g_\varepsilon(0)\le0$ each and the one shared endpoint $g(1)\le0$.
- **Wording.** The fourth-order Taylor condition along $\xi_M$ binds only where $g(0)=0$, and there it is $g'(0)\le0$, equivalent to $g(1)\le0$. Where $g(0)<0$ the fourth-order condition is vacuous and $g(1)\le0$ is an independent finite-angle condition. So "along matching rotations, global maximality is the second-order condition plus the $\tfrac\pi4$ matching inequality" is exact, and "the fourth-order separator is the matching inequality" holds only on the stratum $g(0)=0$, which includes F81's witness.

## Claim 3: the screens

**REFUTED: the screen does not test the matching inequality.** [screen_I121_matching_feasible.py](../scripts/misc/screen_I121_matching_feasible.py) compares $V_M=E(M)+\sum(x^e_k+x^e_l)^2$ against $t^2$. The inequality it names is $V_M+\tfrac14\sum s_{ef}^2\le t^2-\sum_{e\in M}a_e^2$, and the dropped terms are a nonnegative share of $t^2$ itself, so the screen tests a strict relaxation.

- **Rerun, same seed, evaluating the full inequality at the returned frames** ([review_r1843_full_matching.py](../scripts/reviewers/review_r1843_full_matching.py)). At cap $0.1$ the relaxed numbers reproduce the sweep's exactly: $t^2=0.1093,0.0928,0.0800$ with relaxed margin $-0.0194,-0.0053,+0.0059$. The full inequality fails at all three frames, maximum excess $0.050$, $0.055$, $0.060$, on $24$, $30$, $42$ of the $105$ matchings. At cap $0.2$ the frames reached $t^2=0.192$ and $0.180$ (relaxed room $0.089$, $0.078$, the third start fell to $0.033$), and the full inequality fails at both, excess $0.016$ and $0.025$ on $7$ and $15$ matchings. The sweep's $t^2\approx0.20$ to $0.24$ with room $0.10$ to $0.13$ did not reproduce at cap $0.2$ with this seed.
- So "frames of the balanced product ... satisfying all 105 matching inequalities" is false for every frame the screen produced, and "$t^2\approx0.085\approx0.7/n$" is the floor of the relaxation.
- **The same descent on the full inequality** ([review_r1843_full_descent.py](../scripts/reviewers/review_r1843_full_descent.py), same seed, starts and step count) found no feasible frame at either cap: at cap $0.2$ the frames reached $t^2=0.175,0.188,0.169$ with full excess $0.012$, $0.013$, $0.011$, and at cap $0.1$ they reached $t^2=0.100,0.051,0.071$ with excess $0.036$ to $0.066$. This is a screen of $80$ steps and shows nothing positive: it does not show the matching family forces large mass. It leaves the question "how weak is the matching family" open, where the sweep reports it answered.
- **"Even at the maximiser" is wrong at $n=8$.** The screen's tensor is the balanced product $S^4\times S^4$. [problem.md](../findings/problem.md) item 11 names Fox's $\mathcal S^4(\alpha)$ the presumed global maximiser for $4\le n\le11$, and Conjecture A places the product at $n\ge12$. The logical point (the matching family cannot certify that a frame of a given tensor is maximal) does not need the tensor to be the maximiser, so the sentence should say "at a critical point whose maximal frame has $t=1$".
- **"Every family tried today ... lands on the same $n^{-1/2}$ barrier" is overstated.** For F81's witness the $\tfrac1n$ is proved. For the matching family there is one dimension, $n=8$, and one number, which cannot distinguish $c/n$ from any other law, and that number is the relaxation's. "$0.7/n$ at $n=8$" is a coincidence of one value with the frame-free floor's constant. "Only the full $O(n)$ maximality lies beyond it" has no support in the screens, which test no family beyond matchings.
- The screens are labelled screens in the sweep ("(a screen)"), which is correct. The sentences drawn from them state more than a screen of a relaxation shows.

## Claim 4: the recommendation's premise

**REFUTED as stated.** "The non-value separator has to be the full $O(n)$ maximality of the frame" inherits 1b's category error: full $O(n)$ maximality is satisfied by every tensor in its own maximal frame, so it separates frames of one tensor and never a maximiser from an obstructed local maximum. It is the right object for F81's requirement (exclude the maximiser seen in a bad frame), which is a different requirement from R6's.

- "No tractable finite family yet" stands: the matching family is the only finite family analysed, and unequal angles and overlapping planes are unanalysed.
- The premise's evidence, that the matching family is too weak, is no longer shown (claim 3). The recommendation's weakening should be stated as resting on the category correction, with the matching family's strength open.
- Corrected premise: I121's frame vehicle can exclude frame artefacts such as F81's witness without the value. The node then still needs a bound on $\theta$ at critical points of small maximal-frame mass, and the record's $\mathbb{CP}^m$ ($m\ge3$) show that locus holds obstructed critical points, all below $\sqrt{3/2}$.
