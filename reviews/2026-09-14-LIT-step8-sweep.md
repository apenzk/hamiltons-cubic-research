## Batch 1, direction 4: the literature on global maximisers of cubic forms

**Searched**: spectral norm of symmetric tensors and Banach's theorem, extremal cubics on spheres, optimality conditions for homogeneous polynomials on the sphere, Michel's theorem and maximal strata, extremal idempotents in metrised algebras. **Verdict: nothing reaches the root node analytically, and the one framework built for exactly this object is already on the shelf.**

- **Banach's theorem** (the norm of a symmetric multilinear form on a Hilbert space equals the norm of its polynomial): the record's own [tkachev-2019-clifford-extremal](../references/tkachev-2019-clifford-extremal/notes.md) carries it as Definition 2.5, a *normalised* inner product being $\langle x,x^2\rangle\le\lvert x\rvert^3$, and Definition 2.3 names a global maximiser an *extremal idempotent*, with Proposition 2.6 giving $L_c\le\tfrac12$ on $c^\perp$, which is [L24](../findings/second-order.md). *Its structure theorems (minimal algebras are Clifford-type Jordan, Propositions 2.8 to 3.2) need a unit, which $\mathcal W_n$ lacks.* **Consequence for the record, stated as a screen**: at a global maximiser $W^\ast$ every multiplication operator satisfies $\lVert L_x\rVert_{\mathrm{op}}\le M(n)\lvert x\rvert$, so $\mathop{\mathrm{spec}}(L_e)\subset[-1,1]$. The lower bound is a third constraint on the $L_e$ spectrum, which [R19](../routes.md)'s clearance asked for, **and it points the wrong way**: R19 needs a lower bound on the free eigenvalues' sum of squares and a box gives an upper one. *Recorded so the next sweep does not re-find it.*
- **Polynomial optimisation on the sphere** ([2111.01971](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2111.01971), [2010.07066](https://arxiv.org/abs/2010.07066), [1909.13270](https://arxiv.org/html/1909.13270)): optimality conditions are the record's H2 and H3, global optimality is NP-hard in general and certified by SOS hierarchies, which is [R1](../routes.md), analytic $0$. [2010.07066](https://arxiv.org/abs/2010.07066) characterises *generic* forms with no spurious local minimum by a resultant, and $P$ is the opposite case, padded $W_{\mathbb{CP}^2}$ being a spurious local maximum by [L3](../findings/problem.md)'s twin at $n=8$ ([L9](../results.md)).
- **Extremal cubics** ([2110.07946](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2110.07946)) classify the facial structure of the cubic norm ball on $\mathbb R^2$ and $\mathbb R^3$ only. Nothing transfers to an invariant cubic on a $1638$-dimensional sphere.
- **Michel's theorem** (critical points on maximal strata): covered by the [2026-09-13c](2026-09-13c-LIT-step8-sweep.md) sweep as route A, attackable $0$, and it constrains critical points rather than maximisers, so it consumes H2 alone and is refuted as a mechanism by the screening question.

## Batch 2, directions 1 and 2: improvement constructions and the great-circle form of H4

**Screened against [findings/](../findings/) by mechanism, not by name.**

- **Every subalgebra split is [L214](../findings/second-order.md).** For a subalgebra $V$ with projection $\pi$, $Q(\pi W)\in V$ kills the linear term, so $P(W)=P(\pi W)+3\langle L_{\pi W}N,N\rangle+P(N)$ with $N=W-\pi W$ exactly: the frame split is the general case with $V$ the diagonal subalgebra, and [post F67](../findings/fence-posts.md) fences the decoupled reading. *An improvement construction by projection onto a subalgebra is therefore [R24](../routes.md) again, and averaging two orbit points $(W^\ast+\sigma W^\ast)/2$ never improves, by the chord identity, which is [post F37](../findings/fence-posts.md)'s vacuity on the orbit.*
- **The great-circle form of H4 at $W^\ast$.** For every unit $X\perp W^\ast$ and every $t$: $m\cos^3t+3\cos t\sin^2t\,\alpha_X+\sin^3t\,\gamma_X\le m$, with $\alpha_X=\langle L_{W^\ast}X,X\rangle$, $\gamma_X=P(X)$, $m=M(n)$. *At $t\to0$ it is H3; at a $\tfrac12$-direction it forces $\gamma_X=0$, which [second-order.md](../findings/second-order.md) already records as "$P$ vanishes on $\ker\mathrm{Hess}$"; in general it bounds $\gamma_X\le m\,h(\alpha_X/m)$ with $h(\tfrac12)=0$ and $h(\tfrac13)=\tfrac{2}{\sqrt3}\cdot\tfrac23\approx0.77$.* **This is exactly [L20](../findings/route-a.md)'s engine read at the maximiser instead of at $\mathbb{CP}^2$**, and on the plane $\mathrm{span}\{D,N\}$ of a frame split it reduces, by L214's two exact relations, to a one-parameter condition on $(\theta,t,P(D))$ that a hand screen finds satisfiable with $t<1$ and $\theta>\Theta_n$. *So on its own it does not force diagonality; what it adds is a joint constraint on $(\alpha_X,\gamma_X)$ at every direction, untried with the frame-optimality conditions of the $t$-maximising frame.*

## Batch 3, direction 3: the value bootstrap at the balanced orbit, and THE RECORD MISSED ITS OWN DEVICE

**[L216](../findings/second-order.md)'s proof bounds the cubic remainder $\sin^3\sigma\,P(H)$ by [L143](../findings/bounds.md)'s $\lvert P\rvert\le1+\sqrt{n-2}$ and Bernstein's inequality, a loss of a factor $27(1+\sqrt{n-2})/\theta_0$, and [ATTEMPTS.md](../ATTEMPTS.md)'s tube entry records that "the record has no proved bound for $M^\ast=\sup\lvert P\rvert$" and files [I8](../plan.md) to build one.** *That is right for a critical point.* **For a GLOBAL MAXIMISER the bound is free and exact: $P(H)\le M(n)=P(W^\ast)$, which is what [L20](../findings/route-a.md) uses at the $\mathbb{CP}^2$ orbit and nobody ran at the balanced orbit.** Run there, with $u=W_{\mathrm{bal}}$, $V$ the unit normal at the nearest orbit point and the gap $\gamma_n=1/(n-2)$ of L216's corollary:
$$m\le\Theta_n\big(c^3+3cs^2(\tfrac12-\gamma_n)\big)+s^3m,\qquad\text{so}\qquad m\le\Theta_n\,\psi_{\gamma_n}(\varphi),\quad\psi_\gamma(\varphi)=\frac{c^3+(\tfrac32-3\gamma)cs^2}{1-s^3},$$
and $\psi_\gamma\le1$ exactly where $1-s^3-c-\tfrac12cs^2+3\gamma cs^2\ge0$, which holds for $s\lesssim3\gamma$: **at $n=12$ up to $\varphi\approx0.33$, against L216's $0.0132$.** *Inside, $m\le\Theta_n$, and with H1's $m\ge\Theta_n$ the maximiser has value exactly $\Theta_n$.* **So: every global maximiser within geodesic angle $\varphi_n\approx3/(n-2)$ of the balanced orbit has value $\Theta_n$, which is Conjecture A under a localisation hypothesis with an explicit radius, and the proof is L20's, audited three times, with one input swapped.** The same device at every sub-maximal gapped orbit (every $S^p\times S^q$, whose gaps L216's corollary gives in closed form) yields an EXCLUSION tube, $m\le\theta_{p,q}\psi_\gamma<\Theta_n$ being a contradiction, with a radius larger than the balanced one by the value ratio. *Screened by hand at $n=12$, $\gamma=0.1$: the defining polynomial is $+0.004$ at $s=0.25$ and $-0.0005$ at $s=0.33$.* **Not on the record**: `grep bootstrap` finds it only in L20's block and reviewer remarks, and L216's own note says the remainder needs $M^\ast$.

**What it does not do.** The far region, a maximiser outside every such tube, is untouched, and the tubes around the countably many rigid orbits do not cover the sphere. *It converts the localisation half from "within $\varepsilon_n$ of a rigid orbit", with $\varepsilon_n$ unknown, to "within $3/(n-2)$ of the balanced orbit", with the constant explicit, and it hands the exclusion mechanism to every gapped orbit at once.*
