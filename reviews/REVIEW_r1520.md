## A. Fidelity

The source is [diagonal-half.md:601](../findings/diagonal-half.md) to line 613, Lemma L178's statement and steps 1 to 5. The statement's equality clause takes the frame group O(n) so that the anti-self-dual mirror, which also attains the bound, is covered, and step 1 splits a rank-at-most-three W into W⁺ or W⁻. The mirror of steps 3 to 5 is therefore owed for Λ⁻ as well as Λ⁺.

- **A-1. K.** `Kz` ([L178Asd.lean:24](../lean/HamiltonsCubic/Results/L178Asd.lean) to line 27) is CP2Real's `Jz` ([CP2Real.lean:61](../lean/HamiltonsCubic/Results/CP2Real.lean)) with the lower-right 2 × 2 block negated, as the header says (line 9). As 2-forms ω = Σ_{i<j} K_ij e_i ∧ e_j these are −e₀₁ + e₂₃, −e₀₂ − e₁₃ and −e₀₃ + e₁₂. With vol = e₀₁₂₃, ⋆e₀₁ = e₂₃, ⋆e₀₂ = −e₁₃, ⋆e₀₃ = e₁₂, so each satisfies ⋆ω = −ω. CP2Real's `J` gives −e₀₁ − e₂₃, −e₀₂ + e₁₃ and −e₀₃ − e₁₂, each with ⋆ω = ω. So `K` is anti-self-dual and `J` self-dual, opposite orientations. Each `K a` is skew with K² = −1 (`Kz_skew`, `Kz_orth`), a complex structure. Correct.
- **A-2. asdFrame.** Lines 47 to 74 fill all eight fields of `SUFrame` ([L178Frame.lean:34](../lean/HamiltonsCubic/Results/L178Frame.lean) to line 43): s = −1 with s² = 1 by `norm_num`, and skew, orth, inner, br, cyc_eq, anti and cyc_cross each from a `decide` lemma over ℤ (lines 32 to 44) cast to ℝ. `Kz_br` (line 35) states the bracket as −2 Σ ε_abc K_c, and the `br` field restates it as 2·(−1) Σ ε K (line 64), so s = −1 is the sign `decide` found, the orientation reversal flipping the structure constants. The common cyclic sum is the Pfaffian identity ω_a ∧ ω_a = −vol shared by the anti-self-dual forms, and the vanishing mixed sum is ω_a ∧ ω_b = 0 for a ≠ b. Nothing is assumed: every field is a closed proof. Correct. *The header (line 10) and the plan say "every fact checked over ℤ by `decide`", while s_sq is `norm_num` on (−1)² = 1, a fact about the sign rather than the matrices. Not charged.*
- **A-3. The Λ⁻ half of steps 3 to 5.** `WMatA n M = WMatF asdFrame n M` (line 79), that is Σ_{b,c} M_bc ⟨X, ω⁻_b⟩ ω⁻_c with ω⁻_a = `omF asdFrame a`, the anti-self-dual forms of the first four coordinates padded into Λ²ℝⁿ. `WMatA_weyl` (line 82), `WMatA_bound` (line 86, P² ≤ (3/2)‖W‖⁶) and `WMatA_theta_le` (line 90, θ ≤ √(3/2)) instantiate the generic theorems at `asdFrame`, whose proofs run through `WMatF_spectral` and L178Frame.lean's `WF_*`, all of which take an arbitrary `SUFrame`. Step 2's γ² = 2 is sign-blind through `eps_sum_s` ([L178Frame.lean:226](../lean/HamiltonsCubic/Results/L178Frame.lean)), which is why s = −1 changes nothing, and steps 4 and 5 are `WF_P` (line 252) and the discriminant. As in r1519's A-3, `WMatF` over symmetric trace-free M is the general Weyl operator supported on the padded Λ⁻ℝ⁴ in one direction, the converse being milestone 4, and no equality clause is lifted to W_M or claimed. The hypotheses hn, hM, htr and h0 are those of r1519's A-5 and jointly satisfiable, M with zero diagonal and off-diagonal ones again a witness. Correct.
- **A-4. J_K_orth and J_K_cyc.** `J_K_orth` (line 99) is tr(J_aᵀ K_b) = 0 for all a, b, the Frobenius orthogonality of every self-dual structure to every anti-self-dual one, which is Λ⁺ ⊥ Λ⁻ on ℝ⁴. `J_K_cyc` (line 104) is cyc2(J_a, K_b) + cyc2(K_b, J_a) = 0 for all a, b, the symmetrised mixed cyclic sum, which is ω⁺_a ∧ ω⁻_b = 0. Both are `decide` over ℤ (lines 93 to 96) cast through `J_cast`. They are recorded for milestone 4 and used by nothing yet, which the header (lines 16 to 17) and the plan say. Correct.
