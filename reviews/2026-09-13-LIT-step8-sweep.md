# Literature sweep, 2026-09-13

**On Fable, reading only [the position file](2026-09-13-step8-position.md) of the record whole, and screening every candidate against [INDEX-literature.md](../findings/INDEX-literature.md) FIRST.** *The target, stated without the programme's vocabulary: given two points of a variety agreeing to second order in a natural filtration, what invariant separates them?*

## Batch 1: the target is the classifying problem of geometric invariant theory, and the record has a source for it read for the wrong line

**The maximiser question is a GIT question in disguise.** $P(W)=\langle W,Q(W)\rangle$ is a cubic on the unit sphere of $\mathcal W_n$, invariant under $SO(n)$, and its critical points are the idempotents $e=W/\theta$ of the Fox algebra, $Q(e)=\theta e$. *Distinguishing the balanced sphere product from a rival with the same low moments is distinguishing two critical points of a group-invariant functional*, which is exactly what the Kempf–Ness / Kirwan–Ness stratification does: a critical point of the norm of a moment map is a minimum WITHIN ITS STRATUM, and the stratum is labelled by the conjugacy class of the optimal destabilising one-parameter subgroup, a NON-equivariant invariant that two points of one low-moment fibre can differ on.

**[lauret-will-2006-einstein-solvmanifolds-moment-map](../references/lauret-will-2006-einstein-solvmanifolds-moment-map/notes.md) is this machinery already transported to a curvature-flavoured setting**, and the record read it for the SRG/diagonal line rather than for the maximiser. Its Theorem 2.5: a solvmanifold is Einstein iff the norm functional $F$ attains its minimum on the $\mathrm{GL}_n$ orbit at $\mu$. Its Theorem 3.1 and Proposition 3.4: the strata, and which carry the Einstein points. *This is the moment-map norm, its minima as the distinguished objects, and the stratification that separates them, in a setting one contraction away from ours.*

## Batch 2: the screen, by mechanism

**Against the fence posts.** The stratum is not a two-moment quantity, so [F65](../findings/fence-posts.md) does not reach it. It distinguishes orbit type, which is where the violation lives, so the equivariant-blindness post does not reach it. GIT delivers a structural statement (the maximiser is the minimal vector of the top stratum), not a machine certificate, so the analytic gate passes.

**Against [ATTEMPTS.md](../ATTEMPTS.md) by mechanism.** No entry applies the moment map, GIT, or the Kirwan–Ness stratification to $P$ or to the maximiser. The stratification worked in the record is the dimension count of the $C_5$ diagonal stabiliser, a different object.

**Against [INDEX-literature.md](../findings/INDEX-literature.md) FIRST.** The moment-map norm functional and its strata are Lauret–Will's Theorems 2.5, 3.1 and Proposition 3.4, on the shelf and cited nowhere in the maximiser work. The idempotent-variety and isospectral-algebra sources (krasnov-tkachev [2018](../references/krasnov-tkachev-2018-idempotent-variety/notes.md), [2022](../references/krasnov-tkachev-2022-medial-isospectral/notes.md)) carry the second and third candidates below.

## Batch 3: the two weaker candidates, screened the same way

**Isospectral algebra.** [krasnov-tkachev-2022](../references/krasnov-tkachev-2022-medial-isospectral/notes.md) classifies when all idempotents of a commutative nonassociative algebra share one spectrum (Corollaries 4.7–4.9, Theorem 2.6). *If the Fox algebra is NOT isospectral, the full spectrum of $L_e$ separates its idempotents.* The risk it runs straight into: the record already reads the $L_e$ spectrum block by block, and [F65](../findings/fence-posts.md) says two moments of it do not separate. The gain over that is the WHOLE spectrum rather than two moments, which is a finer numerical separator rather than a structural one, so it ranks below the GIT route.

**The idempotent variety and its discriminant.** [krasnov-tkachev-2018](../references/krasnov-tkachev-2018-idempotent-variety/notes.md) studies the variety of idempotents of a metrised algebra and the extremal ones (Propositions 6.6, 6.7: an extremal idempotent has $\sigma(c)\subset(-\infty,\tfrac12]$). *Two idempotents agreeing to second order are separated by a higher jet of the defining ideal, i.e. by the local ring at the point.* This is the target's literal reading, but the record has no handle on the idempotent variety's singularities and building one is a route of its own, so it ranks third.
