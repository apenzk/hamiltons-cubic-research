## A. Fidelity and use

- **A-1. The quaternion model** ([L178Quat.lean:27–28](../lean/HamiltonsCubic/Results/L178Quat.lean), [CP2Real.lean:36–39](../lean/HamiltonsCubic/Results/CP2Real.lean), [L178Asd.lean:24–30](../lean/HamiltonsCubic/Results/L178Asd.lean)). Checked column by column. $L_i$ sends $1,i,j,k$ to $i,-1,k,-j$, which is `J 0`. $L_j$ sends them to $j,-k,-1,i$, which is `J 1`. $L_k$ sends them to $k,j,-i,-1$, which is `J 2`. $R_i$ sends them to $i,-1,-k,j$, which is `K 0`, $R_j$ to $j,k,-1,-i$, which is `K 1`, and $R_k$ to $k,-j,i,-1$, which is `K 2`. `Lq p` is $p_0I+p_1J_0+p_2J_1+p_3J_2$ entry for entry, so it is $L_p$. `Lq_comm_K` is then associativity of $\mathbb H$, and `Lq_mul_transpose` is $\lvert p\rvert^2$.
- **A-2. The identities** ([L178Quat.lean:49–61](../lean/HamiltonsCubic/Results/L178Quat.lean)). `Lq_conj_J` is entrywise and kernel-checked, `rotq p b` being the coefficients of $pe_b\bar p$. At $p=(1+v_0,0,v_2,-v_1)$, which is $1-iu$ for $u=v_0i+v_1j+v_2k$, one has $(1-iu)u=u+i=i(1-iu)$ when $u^2=-1$, so $quq^{-1}=i$. `Lq_to_J0` is the unnormalised form, $(1-s)u+2s(1+v_0)J_0$, and at $s=1$ with $c^2=1/(2(1+v_0))$ it returns $J_0$, as `rot_to_J0` uses at lines 110–115.
- **A-3. What `rot_to_J0` gives milestone 2** ([L178Quat.lean:79–81](../lean/HamiltonsCubic/Results/L178Quat.lean)). Orthogonality is exported as $U^{\mathsf T}U=1$, and $UU^{\mathsf T}=1$ follows for a square matrix (Mathlib's [mul_eq_one_comm](https://leanprover-community.github.io/mathlib4_docs/Mathlib/Algebra/Group/Defs.html#mul_eq_one_comm), square matrices over a field being Dedekind-finite), which gives $UK_aU^{\mathsf T}=K_a$, so $\Lambda^-$ is fixed pointwise. **On the question asked: "$UJ_bU^{\mathsf T}\in\mathop{\mathrm{span}}J$" suffices, and orthonormality of the images is derivable, not an extra hypothesis.** $X\mapsto UXU^{\mathsf T}$ is a Frobenius isometry when $U$ is orthogonal, it maps the three-dimensional $\mathop{\mathrm{span}}J$ injectively into itself and hence onto itself, and an isometry preserving a subspace commutes with the orthogonal projection onto it. In coordinates, $w_{bd}=\tfrac14\langle UJ_bU^{\mathsf T},J_d\rangle$ and $\sum_dw_{bd}w_{b'd}=\tfrac14\langle UJ_bU^{\mathsf T},UJ_{b'}U^{\mathsf T}\rangle=\tfrac14\langle J_b,J_{b'}\rangle=\delta_{bb'}$ by the Frobenius-norm-$4$ fact, so $w$ is orthogonal and $\sum_b(UJ_bU^{\mathsf T})\otimes(UJ_bU^{\mathsf T})=\sum_bJ_b\otimes J_b$. **Gap.** The existential in `rot_to_J0` hides the witness $w=c^2\,$`rotq p b`, so milestone 2 must redo this derivation or reopen the proof. Exporting $w$ with $ww^{\mathsf T}=1$, or the projection invariance directly, is the clause the plan originally listed (C-7).
- **A-4. The $v_0=-1$ branch** ([L178Quat.lean:83–92](../lean/HamiltonsCubic/Results/L178Quat.lean)). $\lvert v\rvert=1$ and $v_0=-1$ force $v_1=v_2=0$, so $u=-J_0$. $U=L_j$ is orthogonal (`scaled_Lq` at $c=1$) and $jij^{-1}=-i$, read off `rotq (0,0,1,0)` row $0$ as $(-1,0,0)$, so $L_j(-J_0)L_j^{\mathsf T}=J_0$. Correct, and the case split at $-1\le v_0$ is exhaustive.
- **A-5. The plan's route** ([rigidity plan:5](2026-09-26-lean-L178-rigidity-plan.md), 10–12). Sound in outline. Rank at most three forces pure (anti-)self-duality ([diagonal-half.md:601](../findings/diagonal-half.md) step 1), and equality at $+\sqrt{3/2}$ puts the spectrum at $\tfrac1{\sqrt6}(2,-1,-1)$ up to positive scale. $t(2,-1,-1)$ is $t(3\omega\otimes\omega-P)$ with $\omega$ the unit top eigenform, and $U$ from `rot_to_J0` carries it to $t(3J_0\otimes J_0-P)$, the $\mathbb{CP}^2$ operator with $J_0$ distinguished. The reflection $D=\mathrm{diag}(1,1,1,-1)$ gives $DK_0D=J_0$, $DK_1D=J_1$, $DK_2D=-J_2$ by hand, so it carries $\Lambda^-$ onto $\Lambda^+$ and $P^-$ onto $P^+$. The sign on $J_2$ is harmless because $\omega\otimes\omega$ and $P$ are sign-blind and `rot_to_J0` takes any unit $v$, but milestone 2 must not treat $D\cdot K$ as the frame $J$ with the same bracket sign $s$. **Two steps the plan does not name, flagged before milestones 2–4 are built.**
  - *From a general coefficient block to $t(3vv^{\mathsf T}-I)$.* [L178_padded](../lean/HamiltonsCubic/Results/L178Reduce.lean) (L178Reduce.lean:223) acts on `WC n C` for a general symmetric $6\times6$ $C$ and carries no equality clause, and [WF_bound_eq_iff](../lean/HamiltonsCubic/Results/L178Frame.lean) (L178Frame.lean:267) is stated only for a weight $\mu$ diagonal in an `SUFrame`. Milestone 2 speaks of "WF of a rotated frame", but no milestone produces that frame from $C$. The route needs the block $A$ with a repeated eigenvalue to be written $t(3vv^{\mathsf T}-I)$ with $\lvert v\rvert=1$, which is Mathlib's spectral theorem ([Matrix.IsHermitian.spectral_theorem](https://leanprover-community.github.io/mathlib4_docs/Mathlib/Analysis/Matrix/Spectrum.html#Matrix.IsHermitian.spectral_theorem)), as `L178_n4_eq` in [L178Eq.lean](../lean/HamiltonsCubic/Results/L178Eq.lean) already uses for eigenvalues over the rational model, plus an equality clause for `L178_padded` itself.
  - *The sign $t>0$.* `WF_bound_eq_iff` is the squared form $P^2=\tfrac32\lVert W\rVert^6$, which also holds at $-W_{\mathbb{CP}^2}$. $t>0$ must come from $P>0$, i.e. from $\theta=+\sqrt{3/2}$, as `L178_n4_eq`'s `hpos` does. The plan's "equality puts the spectrum at $t(2,-1,-1)$" leaves the sign implicit, and the record's equality clause is "up to POSITIVE scale".
- **A-6. `normM2_CP_pos` and `CP_not_coordDiag`** ([L95.lean:477](../lean/HamiltonsCubic/Results/L95.lean), 496). `normM2_quarter` with `full4_std` gives $\lVert M\rVert_F^2=\tfrac14\cdot6n(n-2)(n+2)/(n-1)=\tfrac32n(n-2)(n+2)/(n-1)$, the docstring's value, and $6n(n+1)-18n/(n-1)=6n(n^2-4)/(n-1)$ checks by hand, positive at $n=2m\ge4$. `CP_not_coordDiag` rewrites the goal through `kappaSq_eq_iff` ([L94Statement.lean:502](../lean/HamiltonsCubic/Results/L94Statement.lean)) and `kappaSq_CP`, so it is $\neg\exists R$ with `IsFrame R` and `CoordinateDiagonal (rotate R (Wcp (stdJ m)))`, which is "coordinate-diagonal in no frame" with frames ranging over $O(n)$. That is exactly what the L94/L95 row ([lean/INDEX.md:422](../lean/INDEX.md)), the L95 row (line 424) and [bispectral.md:229](../findings/bispectral.md) now claim.
