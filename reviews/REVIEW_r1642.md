## Gaps

- **G1 (coverage), [I117Stable.lean:387-393](../lean/HamiltonsCubic/Results/I117Stable.lean).** On 𝔰𝔬(n) the block's "each component by `c − Cas_V/4`" has four scalars. `L265_I_sharp` states the trivial (`n − 2`) and Weyl (`−1`) ones as operator equations. The `Sym²₀ ℝⁿ` scalar `(n − 4)/2` (on `ρ∧g`, `ρ` traceless) and the `Λ⁴` scalar `2` exist only as ring identities in `so_sharp_scalars`. "Strictly" at `n ≥ 7` is also unstated: the file proves `≤`. The four-index layer makes both reachable. The `Λ⁴` value follows from `Ftri_id_tOf` at totally antisymmetric `T`, the `Sym²₀` value from `kn_pair`, `ric_kn` and `ric_cyc`, and strictness from `stab_ineq`'s slack `(m − 4)X` plus the `(n − 4)/2 < (n − 2)/2` margin.
- **G2 (prose), [I117Stable.lean:18](../lean/HamiltonsCubic/Results/I117Stable.lean).** "(four times the `Λ⁴` part)" should read "(three times the `Λ⁴` part)", as `cyc_sq`'s docstring at line 133 already says.
- **G3 (honesty), I117Stable.lean header, lines 252-264.** It should name what the file leaves out: the `Sym²₀` and `Λ⁴` eigen-clauses, strictness, and the general-𝔥 formula (quoted representation theory, as the INDEX row says).
