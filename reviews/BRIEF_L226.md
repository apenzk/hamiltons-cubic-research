## The claim under attack ([findings/second-order.md](../findings/second-order.md) lines 953-975)

#### Lemma L226, the cubic on the block subalgebra $\mathcal S_J$: every coupling carries the coefficient $3$, and at $\lvert J\rvert=3$ Ricci-flatness fixes $R_J$ as $N-\tfrac12(\mathop{\mathrm{tr}}N)I$

*Gate 2 owed. Derived 2026-09-15 for [I106](../plan.md), the coefficients the [2026-09-14c sweep](../reviews/2026-09-14c-LIT-step8-sweep.md) wrote unverified, in the coordinates of the [block note](../reviews/2026-09-14-I104-block-J.md). The operator and the bridge to $P$ are typed in [L226Statement.lean](../lean/HamiltonsCubic/Results/L226Statement.lean).*

**Setting.** Fix $J\subseteq[n]$ with $\lvert J\rvert=k\ge2$, outer set $A=[n]\setminus J$ and $m=n-k$. Put $V_a=\mathop{\mathrm{span}}\{\varphi_{pa}:p\in J\}$, identified with $\mathbb R^J$ by $x\mapsto\sum_px_p\varphi_{pa}$, and for $x,y\in\mathbb R^J$ put $x\wedge y=\sum_{p,q}x_py_q\varphi_{pq}\in\Lambda^2\mathbb R^J$. By the block note, $W\in\mathcal S_J$ is block-diagonal on $\Lambda^2\mathbb R^n=\Lambda^2\mathbb R^J\oplus\bigoplus_{a\in A}V_a\oplus\Lambda^2\mathbb R^A$: a symmetric $R_J$ on $\Lambda^2\mathbb R^J$, a symmetric $M_a$ on $V_a$ with $(M_a)_{pq}=W_{pa,qa}$, and $W\varphi_{ab}=K_{ab}\varphi_{ab}$. So $\lVert W\rVert^2=\lvert R_J\rvert^2+\sum_a\lvert M_a\rvert^2+\sum_{a<b}K_{ab}^2$. $\Lambda^2M$ is the operator $x\wedge y\mapsto Mx\wedge My$ on $\Lambda^2\mathbb R^J$.

**Statement.**
1. *For every $W\in\mathcal S_J$,* $$P(W)=P(R_J)+\sum_{a\in A}\Big(\mathop{\mathrm{tr}}M_a^3+3\mathop{\mathrm{tr}}\big(R_J\,\Lambda^2M_a\big)\Big)+\sum_{a<b}\Big(K_{ab}^3+3K_{ab}\mathop{\mathrm{tr}}(M_aM_b)\Big)+3\sum_{a<b<c}K_{ab}K_{ac}K_{bc},$$ *where $P(R_J)=\mathop{\mathrm{tr}}R_J^3+\langle R_J,R_J^\#\rangle$ is the cubic of $\Lambda^2\mathbb R^J=\mathfrak{so}(k)$.*
2. *$W\in\mathcal S_J$ lies in $\mathcal W_n$ exactly when $R_J$ satisfies the first Bianchi identity on $\mathbb R^J$, $\rho(R_J)+\sum_aM_a=0$ with $\rho(R_J)_{pq}=\sum_{r\in J}\langle R_J\varphi_{pr},\varphi_{qr}\rangle$, and $\mathop{\mathrm{tr}}M_a+\sum_{b\ne a}K_{ab}=0$ at every $a\in A$.*
3. *At $k=3$, write $R_J$ in the orthonormal frame $\varphi_{23},\varphi_{31},\varphi_{12}$ as a symmetric $S$. Then $P(R_J)=\mathop{\mathrm{tr}}S^3+3\det S$, $\mathop{\mathrm{tr}}(R_J\Lambda^2M)=\mathop{\mathrm{tr}}(S\mathop{\mathrm{adj}}M)$, $\rho(R_J)=(\mathop{\mathrm{tr}}S)I-S$, and Bianchi holds automatically. So on $\mathcal S_J\cap\mathcal W_n$, with $N=\sum_aM_a$, $S=N-\tfrac12(\mathop{\mathrm{tr}}N)I$ and* $$P(W)=\mathop{\mathrm{tr}}S^3+3\det S+\sum_a\Big(\mathop{\mathrm{tr}}M_a^3+3\mathop{\mathrm{tr}}(S\mathop{\mathrm{adj}}M_a)\Big)+\sum_{a<b}\Big(K_{ab}^3+3K_{ab}\mathop{\mathrm{tr}}(M_aM_b)\Big)+3\sum_{a<b<c}K_{ab}K_{ac}K_{bc}.$$

**Proof.**
1. **$P$ in an eigenbasis.** Let $v_\alpha$ be an orthonormal eigenbasis of $W$ with eigenvalues $\lambda_\alpha$, and $c_{\alpha\beta\gamma}=\langle[v_\alpha,v_\beta],v_\gamma\rangle$, totally antisymmetric. The index formula of [conventions.md](conventions.md), $(W^\#)_{\alpha\beta}=\tfrac12\sum c_{\alpha\gamma\delta}c_{\beta\epsilon\zeta}W_{\gamma\epsilon}W_{\delta\zeta}$, gives $\langle W,W^\#\rangle=\tfrac12\sum_{\alpha,\beta,\gamma}\lambda_\alpha\lambda_\beta\lambda_\gamma c_{\alpha\beta\gamma}^2$ over ordered triples, and $\mathop{\mathrm{tr}}W^3=\sum\lambda_\alpha^3$. *At $W=\mathrm{id}$ the first is $\tfrac12\cdot2(n-2)\dim\Lambda^2$, which is $\mathrm{id}^\#=(n-2)\mathrm{id}$.* $W\in\mathcal S_J$ is block-diagonal, so the eigenbasis is chosen block by block.
2. **Brackets between blocks.** From $[\varphi_{ab},\varphi_{cd}]=\delta_{ac}\varphi_{bd}+\delta_{bd}\varphi_{ac}-\delta_{ad}\varphi_{bc}-\delta_{bc}\varphi_{ad}$, for distinct $p,q\in J$ and distinct $a,b,c\in A$: $[\varphi_{pa},\varphi_{qa}]=\varphi_{pq}$, $[\varphi_{pa},\varphi_{pb}]=\varphi_{ab}$, $[\varphi_{pa},\varphi_{qb}]=0$, $[\varphi_{pa},\varphi_{ab}]=-\varphi_{pb}$, $[\varphi_{pa},\varphi_{bc}]=0$, $[\varphi_{ab},\varphi_{ac}]=\varphi_{bc}$, and $\Lambda^2\mathbb R^J$ commutes with $\Lambda^2\mathbb R^A$. So $[V_a,V_a]\subseteq\Lambda^2\mathbb R^J$ with $[x,y]=x\wedge y$, $[V_a,V_b]=\mathbb R\varphi_{ab}$ with $[x,y]=\langle x,y\rangle\varphi_{ab}$, $[\Lambda^2\mathbb R^J,V_a]\subseteq V_a$, $[V_a,\varphi_{ab}]\subseteq V_b$, and $\Lambda^2\mathbb R^J$ and $\Lambda^2\mathbb R^A$ are subalgebras. Hence $c_{\alpha\beta\gamma}\ne0$ only when the three blocks are, up to order, three copies of $\Lambda^2\mathbb R^J$, one of $\Lambda^2\mathbb R^J$ with two of one $V_a$, the three blocks $V_a,V_b,\mathbb R\varphi_{ab}$, or three pairs of $A$ forming a triangle.
3. **The four sums.** *Three copies of $\Lambda^2\mathbb R^J$*: the structure constants are those of $\mathfrak{so}(k)$, so these triples give $\langle R_J,R_J^\#\rangle$ and, with $\mathop{\mathrm{tr}}R_J^3$, $P(R_J)$. *$\Lambda^2\mathbb R^J$ with two of $V_a$*: the one $\Lambda^2\mathbb R^J$ vector takes $3$ positions in an ordered triple. For orthonormal eigenvectors $v_\beta,v_\gamma$ of $M_a$, $v_\beta\wedge v_\gamma$ is a unit eigenvector of $\Lambda^2M_a$ with eigenvalue $\lambda_\beta\lambda_\gamma$, and these span $\Lambda^2\mathbb R^J$ over $\beta<\gamma$, so for a unit eigenvector $\omega$ of $R_J$ with eigenvalue $\mu$, $\sum_{\beta,\gamma}\lambda_\beta\lambda_\gamma\langle v_\beta\wedge v_\gamma,\omega\rangle^2=2\langle\Lambda^2M_a\,\omega,\omega\rangle$. Summing, $\tfrac12\cdot3\cdot2\sum_\omega\mu\langle\Lambda^2M_a\omega,\omega\rangle=3\mathop{\mathrm{tr}}(R_J\Lambda^2M_a)$. *$V_a,V_b,\mathbb R\varphi_{ab}$*: $6$ orders, and $\sum_{\beta,\gamma}\lambda_\beta(M_a)\lambda_\gamma(M_b)\langle v_\beta,v_\gamma\rangle^2=\mathop{\mathrm{tr}}(M_aM_b)$, giving $\tfrac12\cdot6\,K_{ab}\mathop{\mathrm{tr}}(M_aM_b)$. *Triangles in $A$*: $6$ orders and $c^2=1$, giving $3K_{ab}K_{ac}K_{bc}$. With $\mathop{\mathrm{tr}}W^3=\mathop{\mathrm{tr}}R_J^3+\sum_a\mathop{\mathrm{tr}}M_a^3+\sum_{a<b}K_{ab}^3$ this is item 1.
4. **The Weyl conditions.** $W$ is symmetric and preserves $\Lambda^2$ by construction. An entry $W_{ij,kl}$ can be nonzero only when its index multiset lies in $J$, is $\{p,q,a,a\}$, or is $\{a,b,a,b\}$. A Bianchi sum with a repeated index follows from the pair symmetries, and one over four distinct indices not all in $J$ meets only entries of none of these shapes, so Bianchi reduces to $R_J$'s. For Ricci, $\mathop{\mathrm{Ric}}_{pq}=\rho(R_J)_{pq}+\sum_a(M_a)_{pq}$ and $\mathop{\mathrm{Ric}}_{aa}=\mathop{\mathrm{tr}}M_a+\sum_{b\ne a}K_{ab}$, while $\mathop{\mathrm{Ric}}_{pa}$ and $\mathop{\mathrm{Ric}}_{ab}$ pair vectors of different blocks and vanish. This is item 2.
5. **$k=3$.** The frame $\varphi_{23},\varphi_{31},\varphi_{12}\mapsto e_1,e_2,e_3$ carries $x\wedge y$ to $x\times y$, so the bracket of $\Lambda^2\mathbb R^J$ becomes the cross product, every distinct orthonormal triple has $c^2=1$, and $\langle R_J,R_J^\#\rangle=\tfrac12\cdot6\det S$. The cofactor identity $Mx\times My=\mathop{\mathrm{adj}}(M)(x\times y)$ for symmetric $M$ makes $\Lambda^2M$ the adjugate. Entry by entry, $\rho_{11}=S_{22}+S_{33}$ and $\rho_{12}=-S_{12}$, so $\rho(S)=(\mathop{\mathrm{tr}}S)I-S$. Every symmetric operator on $\Lambda^2\mathbb R^3$ satisfies Bianchi. Taking traces in $(\mathop{\mathrm{tr}}S)I-S=-N$ gives $\mathop{\mathrm{tr}}S=-\tfrac12\mathop{\mathrm{tr}}N$, hence $S=N-\tfrac12(\mathop{\mathrm{tr}}N)I$. $\square$

## The block subalgebra $\mathcal S_J$, derived

For $J\subseteq[n]$ let $H_J\subset\Gamma$ be the sign changes constant on $J$ and free off it, and $\mathcal S_J$ its fixed space, a subalgebra invariant under $O(J)$, which commutes with $H_J$. Reading characters entry by entry, a component $R_{pq,rs}$ survives exactly when its index multiset is trivial on $H_J$:

- $R_{pa,qa}$ with $p,q\in J$, $a\notin J$: for each $a$ a symmetric $\lvert J\rvert\times\lvert J\rvert$ matrix $M_a$;
- $R_{pq,rs}$ with all indices in $J$: the curvature-type block $R_J$ on $\Lambda^2\mathbb R^J$;
- $R_{ab,ab}$ with $a,b\notin J$: the outer sectional entries $K_{ab}$.

Every other entry carries a character nontrivial on $H_J$: $R_{pa,qb}$ ($\varepsilon_a\varepsilon_b$), $R_{pq,ra}$ ($\varepsilon_J\varepsilon_a$), $R_{ab,pq}$ ($\varepsilon_a\varepsilon_b$), and outer entries off the diagonal. So as an operator $W=\bigoplus_aM_a\oplus R_J\oplus\mathop{\mathrm{diag}}(K_{ab})$, and $O(J)$ conjugates every $M_a$ and $R_J$ at once. $\mathcal S_{\{i\}}=\mathcal D_n$ and $\mathcal S_{[n]}=\mathcal W_n$.
