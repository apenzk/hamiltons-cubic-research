## Gaps

- **G1 (fidelity, minor), [L127DnDim.lean:25](../lean/HamiltonsCubic/Results/L127DnDim.lean) and [lean/INDEX.md:495](../lean/INDEX.md).** The "1" of the sphere-weight line is claimed without a declaration. - **G2 (route, the owed item), [L127DnDim.lean:26-27](../lean/HamiltonsCubic/Results/L127DnDim.lean).** Directness follows by counting from what is already declared: dn_decomp gives that the six families span the weight space, the five declared dimensions plus 1 sum to n(n − 3)/2 = `finrank_weights`, and a spanning family of subspaces whose dimensions add to the ambient dimension is independent, by the rank of the sum map from the product of the families onto the weight space, which is surjective between spaces of equal finite dimension and so injective. The one missing input is that each family sits inside the weight space (zero row sums of `lamW S (dnK S) h` and of the cross and within families), which L127Dn's column-sum lemmas nearly give. An untracked L127DnDirect.lean was being compiled by a concurrent session during this review and was not reviewed here.

Commits are not amended, so the repair is a record line in the directness commit naming 59fca6d1's title as one step ahead of its proof.
