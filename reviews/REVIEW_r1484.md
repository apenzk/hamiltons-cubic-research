## A. `F_eq_zero_iff` and the closure lemmas

- **The characterization is the record's.** Route-a.md line 91 states $\{F=0\}=\{H\in T_uS:\Pi_{ASD}H\Pi_{\ker u}=0\}$. `F_eq_zero_iff` concludes `schur hN uOp_isSymmetric zeroφ H (asd γ) = 0` for $\gamma=0,1,2$. `zeroφ a b` is $1$ exactly when both eigenvalues are $0$ ([BlockValue.lean](../lean/HamiltonsCubic/Base/BlockValue.lean) line 38), so the Schur product is $P_0HP_0$, and since `uOp_asd` puts every ASD vector in $\ker u$, $P_0HP_0t=P_0Ht$. The condition is $\Pi_{\ker u}H\Pi_{ASD}=0$, the transpose of the record's, equivalent by symmetry of $H$.
- **Forward direction, checked step by step.** `L17_at_u` gives $F\ge\tfrac23\operatorname{tr}B_++\tfrac13\operatorname{tr}B_-$ with both traces nonnegative, so $F=0$ forces both traces to $0$, and `eq_zero_of_traces` gives $B=\Pi_{ASD}H\Pi_{ASD}=0$. Then each $\langle t,Ht\rangle=0$, `smooth_eq` (Bianchi through `pairing`, tangency through `trace_comp_rankSum`) gives $H_{22}+H_{33}=\tfrac23\sum\langle t,Ht\rangle=0$, so the sliver $\sum\langle t,Y_-t\rangle$ is $0$ with $Y=P_0HP_0$. Each term is nonnegative, so $Y_-t=0$ by `specFn_apply_eq_zero_of_inner`. `hdiag` gives $\langle t,Yt\rangle=\langle t,Ht\rangle=0$, so $\langle t,Y_+t\rangle=0$ and $Y_+t=0$, whence $Yt=Y_+t-Y_-t=0$. Correct.
- **Reverse direction.** $Yt=0$ gives $\langle t,Ht\rangle=0$ through `hdiag`, and $Y_-t=x_-(0)\,t=0$ by `specFn_apply_of_eigen`. Then $F=\tfrac23\cdot0+0=0$. Correct.
- **Hand test, the $2\times2$ kernel block.** $D=\begin{pmatrix}0&c\\c&0\end{pmatrix}$ on $A\oplus K'$, $c\ne0$, $A$ an ASD bivector, $K'\in\ker u$.
  - $D_-=\tfrac12(\lvert c\rvert I-D)$, so $\langle A,D_-A\rangle=\lvert c\rvert/2$. With $H_{22}=H_{33}=0$ (support in $\ker u$) this gives $F\ge\lvert c\rvert/2>0$, while $P_0DP_0A=cK'\ne0$. Both sides of the iff are false. Consistent.
  - *Admissibility needs care.* $\mathrm{Alt}(D)\propto A\wedge K'$. For $K'$ a second ASD bivector, $A\wedge K'=-\langle A,K'\rangle\mathrm{vol}=0$, and $A,K'$ anticommute as matrices so $D$ is Ricci-flat as well: $D$ is a tangent Weyl tensor. Then $B=D$, $\operatorname{tr}B_\pm=\lvert c\rvert$, $F=\lvert c\rvert/2+\lvert c\rvert/2=\lvert c\rvert$, which meets $\tfrac23\operatorname{tr}B_++\tfrac13\operatorname{tr}B_-=\lvert c\rvert$ with equality. For $K'$ a padded or mixed kernel direction, $A\wedge K'\ne0$ (the $e_{12}$ and $e_{34}$ parts of $A$ produce 4-forms on disjoint index sets), so the bare block is not Bianchi and a correction supported off $A$ is needed. The lemma then still predicts $F>0$, since any symmetric $Y$ with $\langle A,YA\rangle=0$ and $YA\ne0$ has $\langle A,Y_-A\rangle>0$. Expected $F>0$: confirmed in both readings.
- **Same $F$.** Screen 1: `Fval hN (opB_isSymmetric hH.selfAdjoint)` equals `L17`'s coefficient by `rfl`. Screen 2: every `IsWeyl2 H` with `innerOp2 H (u2 hn) = 0` yields `Admissible (opB H)`, from `opB_isSymmetric`, `alt4_opB_eq_zero`, and `opB_u2` with `innerOp2_eq_trace`. Both compiled.
- **`Admissible.add`, `Admissible.smul`, `schur_add_right`, `schur_smul_right`.** Entrywise linearity of the Schur product and of `alt4 ∘ compOf`, and linearity of the trace. Correct. `F_zero_add` and `F_zero_smul` rewrite through `F_eq_zero_iff` three times and use linearity of the Schur product. Correct.
- **Gap A1, `Admissible` is not the record's hypothesis set, and its docstring says it is.** [L17Cone.lean](../lean/HamiltonsCubic/Results/L17Cone.lean) line 176 reads *"The hypotheses of L17 are linear: symmetric, `Alt = 0` and tangent."* The record's set is $H\in\mathcal W_n$ tangent, which adds Ricci-flatness, and `L17` binds `IsWeyl2`, which carries it. `Admissible` is a strict superset (algebraic curvature operators with Ricci part, tangent).
