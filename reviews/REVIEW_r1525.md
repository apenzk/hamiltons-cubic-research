## A. Fidelity

- **A-1. L178_frame** ([FrameTransfer.lean:30](../lean/HamiltonsCubic/Results/FrameTransfer.lean)). The record's statement ([diagonal-half.md:603](../findings/diagonal-half.md)) is: W ≠ 0 Weyl, supported on Λ²V with dim V = 4, rank(W : Λ² → Λ²) ≤ 3, then θ(W) ≤ √(3/2). The theorem takes `IsOrth U`, `IsWeyl R`, and on U·R the hypotheses of `L178_supported` ([L178Support.lean:96](../lean/HamiltonsCubic/Results/L178Support.lean)): `hker` and `hran` say U·R = P(U·R)P for P the projection onto the coordinate Λ²ℝ⁴ spanned by `eF`, `hrank` and `h0` are rank ≤ 3 and nonzero read on the coefficient matrix on the orthonormal basis `eF` (r1522's A-rank reading). U·R has components of R in the frame of U's rows (r1524 A-1), so these say R is supported on Λ²V for V the span of U's first four rows. Every four-dimensional V is such a span for some orthogonal U by extending an orthonormal basis of V, which lean/INDEX.md:70 correctly records as untyped. The conclusion is θ(R), transported by `invariants_conj`'s second component and `isWeyl_conj`. Faithful to the inequality half.
- **A-2. diagOp_eq_WD** ([FrameTransfer.lean:41](../lean/HamiltonsCubic/Results/FrameTransfer.lean)). Both sides are expanded over `phi_expand X hX`: `hlin` pushes `diagOp w` (defined entrywise and linear in X) through the φ-sum, `diagOp_phi` gives w_ab φ_ab, and `WD` carries the matching ½ K_ab ⟨X, φ_ab⟩ φ_ab with the ½ absorbing the double count over ordered pairs. Symmetry of w is the only hypothesis and the only one `diagOp_phi` needs. Correct.
- **A-3. L176_someFrame** ([FrameTransfer.lean:69](../lean/HamiltonsCubic/Results/FrameTransfer.lean) to line 78). The proof rewrites U·R's φ-matrix to `coeffD w` and applies `L176_op`, with `IsWeight`'s row sums supplying Weyl through `WD_weyl_iff`. With `IsOrth U`, `coeffOp_conj` makes that matrix R's matrix on {Uᵀ φ_ab U}_{a<b}, an orthonormal basis of Λ², so its rank is rank(R : Λ² → Λ²), which is the right matrix. The sectional matrix of R in the frame of U's rows is w (⟨R ψ_ab, ψ_ab⟩ = ⟨(U·R) φ_ab, φ_ab⟩ = w_ab), so the degree clause is `card_nbr_zero_or_two_le` ([L176Statement.lean:67](../lean/HamiltonsCubic/Results/L176Statement.lean)) at w, whose `IsSectional` hypotheses `IsWeight` supplies. **Gap.** The signature takes neither `IsCoordDiagonalSomeFrame R` nor `IsOrth U` ([Frames.lean:52](../lean/HamiltonsCubic/Base/Frames.lean) asks both), so the theorem as typed is `L176_op` for `coeffD w` composed with the rewrite `hconj`, and holds for any U. The statement about R needs `coeffOp_conj` with `hU` and exists only in the docstring and in lean/INDEX.md:66. True and harmless, but no single declaration states that R's matrix on an orthonormal basis has rank at least four.
- **A-4. coeffOp_conj** ([FrameTransfer.lean:60](../lean/HamiltonsCubic/Results/FrameTransfer.lean)). `conjOp U R (φ_pq) = U R(ψ_pq) Uᵀ` by definition, and `inner2_move hU` moves the outer conjugation onto the second argument, turning φ_rs into ψ_rs. Correct, and it does need `hU`.
