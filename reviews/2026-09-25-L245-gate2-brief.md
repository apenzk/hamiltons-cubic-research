#### Lemma L245, the isotropy of each eigenspace is capped direction by direction, and a star eigenspace must be small

*Gate 2 owed. The scalar core is typed in [L245SymmetricIsotropy.lean](../lean/HamiltonsCubic/Results/L245SymmetricIsotropy.lean); the isotropy matrices enter as hypotheses.*

**Statement.** Let $W$ be a unit critical point with three eigenvalues $\lambda_0<\lambda_1<\lambda_2$ and isotropy matrices $A_c=\sum_{\psi\in E_c}\psi\psi^{\mathsf T}$ on $\mathbb R^n$ (the record's $A_a$, [the summed-pair note](../reviews/2026-09-23-I73c-summed-pairs.md), Setting). For every unit $x\in\mathbb R^n$ the weights $a_c=\langle A_cx,x\rangle$ satisfy
$$a_0+a_1+a_2=n-1,\qquad\lambda_0a_0+\lambda_1a_1+\lambda_2a_2=0,\qquad a_c\ge0,$$
so each $a_c$ is at most $h_c$, the largest value of that coordinate on the resulting segment. **In particular, if $E_c\supseteq e\wedge U$ for a unit $e$ and a subspace $U\perp e$, then $\dim U\le h_c$.** At a symmetric spectrum $(-a,0,a)$, $A_0=A_2$ and $h_0=h_2=\tfrac{n-1}2$.

**Proof.** $\sum_cA_c=\operatorname{Ric}(\mathrm{Id})=(n-1)I$ and $\sum_c\lambda_cA_c=\operatorname{Ric}(W)=0$ (section 3 of the note), and each $A_c\succeq0$; evaluate at $x$. For the star, $(e\wedge u)(e\wedge u)^{\mathsf T}e=e$ for unit $u\perp e$, so $\langle A_ce,e\rangle\ge\dim U$. $\square$

**What it does.** *The per-vector caps of [L244](../findings/second-order.md#lemma-l244-the-per-vector-ceiling-on-a-symmetric-three-value-critical-point) pass eigenspaces built from decomposable elements, and the two explicit all-good subspaces the approved screen met, $e_1\wedge U$ with $\dim U=8$ at $(8,50,8)$ and $e_1\wedge\mathbb R^{11}$ plus one direction at $(12,42,12)$, are stars with $\dim U>\tfrac{11}2$, so this lemma excludes both.* **Across the 190 survivors at $n=12$, $\theta=1.2361$, the smallest eigenspace is too large to be a star ($m_c>h_c$) at 175** (a screen of the exact $h_c$). *It is the first ingredient that constrains the eigenspaces JOINTLY, through $\operatorname{Ric}(W)=0$, which the per-vector caps do not see.* **Owed**: the combination, an all-good $E_c$ with $A_c\preceq h_cI$ as an operator inequality, beyond stars.
