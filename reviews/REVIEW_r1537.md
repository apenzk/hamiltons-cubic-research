## A. Fidelity

- **A-1. The statements** ([L178Rigid.lean:416–422](../lean/HamiltonsCubic/Results/L178Rigid.lean), 452–469, 474–489, against [diagonal-half.md:603](../findings/diagonal-half.md)). `conjOp U R` is $X\mapsto U\,R(U^{\mathsf T}XU)\,U^{\mathsf T}$ ([Frames.lean:34](../lean/HamiltonsCubic/Base/Frames.lean)), the operator read in the frame $U$, so `conjOp U R = t • Wc n` is "$W$ is $t\,W_{\mathbb{CP}^2}$ after the frame change $U$" with $t>0$ and $U$ orthogonal (`IsOrth` is both $U^{\mathsf T}U=1$ and $UU^{\mathsf T}=1$). The padded version equates the two maps on every matrix, which is stronger than needed and true because both sides are built from `inner2` against skew forms. The supported and frame versions compare only at skew $X$, and **that is the right reading**: the layer's operators are maps on all of $\mathfrak{gl}_n$, the record's $W$ is an operator on $\Lambda^2$, and `supported_facts` agrees with $W_C$ only on bivectors, so equality off $\Lambda^2$ would be a claim about an arbitrary extension. `theta_congr_skew` makes $\theta$ depend on the bivector values alone, which is what lets the converse run through the skew-only equality. The record's "nonzero" is absent from the hypotheses and harmless, since $\theta=\sqrt{3/2}$ forces $\lVert W\rVert>0$ (`WF_eq_top`, lines 286–291) and $t\,W_c$ with $t>0$ is nonzero. *Observation*: `L178_padded_eq`'s docstring (line 353) still says "a nonzero Weyl $W_C$" though the theorem carries no such hypothesis.
- **A-2. `Wc`** ([CP2Real.lean:172–178](../lean/HamiltonsCubic/Results/CP2Real.lean)). `Wc` is $\sum_a\mu_a\langle\cdot,\omega_a\rangle\omega_a$ with $\omega_a=J_a/\sqrt2$ padded (orthonormal by `om_inner`) and $\mu=(2,-1,-1)/\sqrt6$. So $\lVert W_c\rVert^2=\sum\mu_a^2=1$ and the $2$ sits on $J_0$ with a positive sign, and `Wc_theta` gives $\theta(W_c)=+\sqrt{3/2}$. That is the Kähler $\mathbb{CP}^2$ operator, $W^+$ with spectrum $\propto(2,-1,-1)$ and $W^-=0$, at unit norm and in the orientation that attains $+\sqrt{3/2}$, which is the record's $W_{\mathbb{CP}^2}$ once "up to positive scale" absorbs the normalisation.
- **A-3. Orientation** ([L178Rigid.lean:305–316](../lean/HamiltonsCubic/Results/L178Rigid.lean), 330–351). `D4` $=\mathrm{diag}(1,1,1,-1)$ has determinant $-1$, and `Upad n D4` extends it by the identity, so it too has determinant $-1$. `rigid_sd` supplies `Upad n U4` with $U_4$ a positive multiple of $L_q$, and $\det L_q=\lvert q\rvert^4>0$ (or $U_4=L_j$ on the $v_0=-1$ branch, determinant $1$). The composite `U * Upad n D4` returned by `rigid_rot_asd` is therefore orientation-reversing, as it must be: $SO(4)$ preserves $\Lambda^\pm$, so no rotation carries an anti-self-dual operator to $W_{\mathbb{CP}^2}$. `D4_K` gives $D_4K_bD_4^{\mathsf T}=(1,1,-1)_b\,J_b$, and the $-1$ on $J_2$ is absorbed into the coefficient matrix $O_{ab}\,\mathrm{sgnK}_b$ passed to `rigid_sd`, which asks only for orthonormality of the image triple (`horth`, from `inner2_conj'`), not for a bracket sign. This is exactly the care r1536 A-5 asked for.
- **A-4. The sign** ([L178Rigid.lean:256–301](../lean/HamiltonsCubic/Results/L178Rigid.lean)). $\theta=P/\lVert W\rVert^3=\sqrt{3/2}$, $\lVert W\rVert>0$ (a zero norm makes $\theta=0$ in Lean's division), so $P=\sqrt{3/2}\lVert W\rVert^3>0$. `WF_P` ([L178Frame.lean:252](../lean/HamiltonsCubic/Results/L178Frame.lean)) gives $P=9\mu_0\mu_1\mu_2$, so $\mu_0\mu_1\mu_2>0$. Squaring gives $P^2=\tfrac32N^3$, and `WF_bound_eq_iff` returns a repeated eigenvalue. In `top_of_eq`, with the pair $x,x$ and the third $-2x$ by trace zero, the product is $-2x^3>0$, so $x<0$ and $s=-x>0$ with the $2s$ on the odd index. All three cases check by hand. **So the sign comes from $\theta>0$, not from the squared identity**, closing r1536 A-5's second unnamed step.
- **A-5. `proj_inv`** ([L178Rigid.lean:59–96](../lean/HamiltonsCubic/Results/L178Rigid.lean)). With $\omega'_a=\sum_bc_{ab}\omega_b$ and both triples orthonormal, $\delta_{ab}=\langle\omega'_a,\omega'_b\rangle=\sum_dc_{ad}c_{bd}$, so $CC^{\mathsf T}=1$. Mathlib's [mul_eq_one_comm](https://leanprover-community.github.io/mathlib4_docs/Mathlib/Algebra/Group/Defs.html#mul_eq_one_comm) turns that into $C^{\mathsf T}C=1$, square matrices over a field being Dedekind-finite, which is the columns' orthonormality `hcol`. Expanding $\sum_a\langle X,\omega'_a\rangle\omega'_a=\sum_{d,e}(\sum_ac_{ad}c_{ae})\langle X,\omega_d\rangle\omega_e$ and applying `hcol` leaves $\sum_d\langle X,\omega_d\rangle\omega_d$. Correct, and it is the derivation r1536 A-3 described, now in the file.
- **A-6. The converse** ([L178Rigid.lean:389–412](../lean/HamiltonsCubic/Results/L178Rigid.lean), 420–422). `theta_smul_Wc` writes $t\,W_c$ as `WF stdFrame n (t • mu)`, and `WF_values` and `WF_P` give $N=t^2\sum\mu^2$ and $P=9t^3\mu_0\mu_1\mu_2$, so $\theta(tW_c)=t^3P_1/(\lvert t\rvert^3N_1^{3/2})=\theta(W_c)$ at $t>0$, and `Wc_theta` finishes. The `_eq_iff` converses rewrite $\theta(W)=\theta(U\cdot W)$ by `invariants_conj` (which needs `IsWeyl R`, a hypothesis of each theorem) and then transfer along the skew-only equality. Correct.
