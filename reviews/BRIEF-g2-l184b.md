#### Lemma L181, Ricci-flatness and first Bianchi are the two halves of one matrix, and together they empty the simultaneously-block-diagonal family

**Statement.** Fix an orthogonal decomposition $\mathbb R^n=P_1\oplus\dots\oplus P_m\oplus P_0$ with each $P_j$ a plane, and let $\varepsilon_j$ be the unit area-form of $P_j$. Call $W\in\mathcal W_n$ **simultaneously block-diagonal** for this splitting when every eigen-$2$-form with nonzero eigenvalue is a combination of the $\varepsilon_j$. **Then $W=0$.**

**Proof.** Write $\omega_i=\sum_{j=1}^mM_{ij}\varepsilon_j$ for the $r$ eigenforms with eigenvalues $\lambda_i\ne0$, and $\Lambda=\mathop{\mathrm{diag}}(\lambda)$. Orthonormality of the $\omega_i$ is $MM^{\mathsf T}=I_r$, the $\varepsilon_j$ being orthonormal.

*Ricci-flatness is the diagonal.* As a skew matrix $\varepsilon_j$ is supported on $P_j$, so $\omega_i\omega_i^{\mathsf T}$ is block-diagonal with $M_{ij}^2\,\mathrm{Id}_{P_j}$ on the $j$-th plane. By [L180](../findings/diagonal-half.md#lemma-l180-ricci-flatness-is-a-signed-psd-identity-and-it-names-the-support), Ricci-flatness is $\sum_i\lambda_i\omega_i^2=0$, which reads $$\textstyle\sum_i\lambda_iM_{ij}^2=0\quad\text{for each }j\qquad\Longleftrightarrow\qquad\mathop{\mathrm{diag}}\big(M^{\mathsf T}\Lambda M\big)=0.$$

*First Bianchi is the off-diagonal.* $\varepsilon_j\wedge\varepsilon_j=0$, so $\omega_i\wedge\omega_i=2\sum_{j<k}M_{ij}M_{ik}\,\varepsilon_j\wedge\varepsilon_k$, and the $\varepsilon_j\wedge\varepsilon_k$ are linearly independent in $\Lambda^4$. By [L179](../findings/diagonal-half.md#lemma-l179-the-support-of-a-low-rank-weyl-tensor-and-the-first-bianchi-identity-read-on-the-spectrum), first Bianchi is $\sum_i\lambda_i\,\omega_i\wedge\omega_i=0$, which reads $$\textstyle\sum_i\lambda_iM_{ij}M_{ik}=0\quad\text{for each }j<k\qquad\Longleftrightarrow\qquad\big(M^{\mathsf T}\Lambda M\big)_{j\ne k}=0.$$

*So $M^{\mathsf T}\Lambda M=0$.* Multiplying by $M$ on the left and $M^{\mathsf T}$ on the right and using $MM^{\mathsf T}=I_r$ gives $\Lambda=M\big(M^{\mathsf T}\Lambda M\big)M^{\mathsf T}=0$, contradicting $\lambda_i\ne0$ unless there are no such eigenvalues at all. $\square$

> **AND THE HALVES ARE EXACTLY A SYMMETRIC MATRIX'S ENTRIES.** *Ricci fixes the $m$ diagonal entries and Bianchi the $\binom m2$ off-diagonal ones, which together are the $\binom{m+1}2$ entries of $M^{\mathsf T}\Lambda M$ and nothing else.* **Neither condition is redundant, and Bianchi is load-bearing**: $\Lambda=\mathop{\mathrm{diag}}(1,-1)$ with $M=\tfrac1{\sqrt2}\begin{pmatrix}1&1\\1&-1\end{pmatrix}$ gives a nonzero block-diagonal trace-free $W$ whose Bianchi sum is $2\,\varepsilon_1\wedge\varepsilon_2\ne0$, *so Ricci alone does not empty the family.* **The one exception is $m=1$, where $\Lambda^4$ contributes nothing and the Ricci half closes it alone.** *Both from [r776](../reviews/REVIEW_r776.md).*

> **THE TWO CONDITIONS ARE COMPLEMENTARY HALVES OF ONE OBJECT, WHICH IS THE CONTENT.** *Ricci-flatness and the first Bianchi identity are usually carried as unrelated hypotheses and priced separately.* **On this family they are the diagonal and the off-diagonal of the single matrix $M^{\mathsf T}\Lambda M$**, so imposing both is imposing that one matrix vanishes, and a matrix congruent to $\Lambda$ through an isometry cannot vanish unless $\Lambda$ does. *No inequality, no spectrum, and no dimension enters: the statement holds at every $n$, every rank and every number of blocks.*

**What it does for [I38](../plan.md), stated exactly.** *[L180](../findings/diagonal-half.md#lemma-l180-ricci-flatness-is-a-signed-psd-identity-and-it-names-the-support) leaves $\dim V\ge6$ as the whole of what remains, and the first family one would build there is three forms sharing a block structure, the analogue of the balanced products that populate this record.* **That family is empty**, at $\dim V=6$ and at every larger even dimension. *What it does not reach is a triple with no common block splitting*, which is where a witness would now have to live.

**Two checks that it is not vacuous, both argued rather than asserted, and the reason a naming of particular forms cannot work is general.** *Every $2$-form is block-diagonal for **some** splitting, by the real skew normal form*, so no argument of the shape "this form is not a combination of area forms" can succeed for any choice of form. **The quantifier over all splittings has to be discharged by a count, and it is.**

*The span of the $\varepsilon_j$ is $m$-dimensional*, so the hypothesis needs $r\le m$ (which $MM^{\mathsf T}=I_r$ already forces) and $m\le\lfloor n/2\rfloor$. **$W_{\mathbb{CP}^2}$ escapes by that count alone**: at $n=4$ the span is at most two-dimensional while its three eigenforms span $\Lambda^+$, so no splitting can contain them. *This also fixes where the lemma has content at rank three: $r=3$ needs $m\ge3$, hence $n\ge6$, **exactly [I38](../plan.md)'s remaining case**, and nothing at $n=4$, which [L178](../findings/diagonal-half.md#lemma-l178-every-weyl-tensor-of-lambda2-rank-at-most-three-has-thetalesqrt32-with-equality-exactly-at-w_mathbbcp2) already settles.*

**A coordinate-diagonal $W$ escapes it too, and the reason is decomposability.** *If $e_{ab}=\sum_ja_j\varepsilon_j$ then, the $\varepsilon_j$ having orthogonal supports, a sum with two or more nonzero terms has rank at least four, so $e_{ab}=\pm\varepsilon_j$ for a single $j$.* **Two eigenforms sharing an index would then need their planes both orthogonal and intersecting**, and [L176](../findings/diagonal-half.md#lemma-l176-the-coordinate-diagonal-locus-has-minimum-degree-two-so-its-lambda2-rank-is-at-least-four) puts at least four edges in any support graph, whose minimum degree two makes two of them share a vertex.

## The block under audit, quoted verbatim from the file

#### Lemma L184, the coincident case at $\dim V=6$ is empty

*Derived 2026-09-08 while [L183](../findings/diagonal-half.md#lemma-l183-a-rank-three-weyl-tensor-is-an-orthonormal-triple-whose-images-are-collinear)'s Gate 2 ran. **Unaudited**, and the three cases below are separable, so a reviewer can attack them one at a time.*

**Statement, in the form both audits established rather than the one first written.** *No orthonormal triple $\omega_1,\omega_2,\omega_3$ of $2$-forms with a common six-dimensional support has both its squares and its self-wedges coincident.* **No Weyl hypothesis is used anywhere**, in particular there is no rank-three $W\in\mathcal W_n$ with $\dim V=6$ whose three images under $\Phi$ coincide, which is the corollary the programme wanted.

> **AND THE MECHANISM IS ONE THING IN ALL THREE CASES: THE HYPOTHESES FORCE $\omega_i=\pm\omega_1$, WHICH THREE ORTHONORMAL FORMS CANNOT SATISFY.** *Case 2 by $\star$ being injective, case 3 by the cross term, and case 1 by orthogonality alone once the wedge hypothesis is kept, equal wedges make $s_{ij}s_{ik}$ independent of $i$, so $\omega_i=\eta_i\omega_1$ and $\mathop{\mathrm{tr}}(\omega_1\omega_2)=\mp2\ne0$.* **That retires case 1's appeal to [L181](../findings/diagonal-half.md#lemma-l181-ricci-flatness-and-first-bianchi-are-the-two-halves-of-one-matrix-and-together-they-empty-the-simultaneously-block-diagonal-family) and with it the odd-$n$ question**, *which was never the lemma's anyway: the brief's rendering of L181 dropped its remainder summand $P_0$, and L181 as stated covers odd $n$, both of its own reviewers having found the remainder inert.* **Supplied by [r781](../reviews/REVIEW_r781.md) and [r782](../reviews/REVIEW_r782.md), independently.**

**Setup.** Write $A=-\omega_i^2$, common by hypothesis, positive definite of trace $2$ on $V$. *Each $\omega_i$ commutes with $\omega_i^2$, hence preserves every eigenspace of $A$*, and $A$'s eigenvalues come in equal pairs, $\omega_i$ being skew with eigenvalues $\pm\mathrm ic_j$. **So $A$ has spectrum $\{c_1^2,c_1^2,c_2^2,c_2^2,c_3^2,c_3^2\}$ and the three cases are set by how many of the $c_j^2$ agree.**

**Case 1, the $c_j^2$ pairwise distinct.** *$A$ is positive **definite** rather than merely semidefinite because $V=\mathop{\mathrm{im}}A$, which the setup uses and did not say; and its multiplicities are all even, which is exactly when such an $A$ is realisable.* *Case 1 no longer appeals to Given C at all, the orthogonality route above closing it directly.* *$A$'s eigenspaces are then three specific planes $P_1,P_2,P_3$, each $\omega_i$ preserves them, and a skew map on a plane is a multiple of its area form*, so $\omega_i=\sum_j\pm c_j\varepsilon_j$ for every $i$, **all three block-diagonal for one common splitting.** [Lemma L181](../findings/diagonal-half.md#lemma-l181-ricci-flatness-and-first-bianchi-are-the-two-halves-of-one-matrix-and-together-they-empty-the-simultaneously-block-diagonal-family) then gives $W=0$. $\square$

> **WHAT KILLS CASE 2 IS THE WEDGE ALONE, AND A WITNESS SHOWS IT.** *Three orthonormal forms on $\mathbb R^6$ with equal scalar squares **do exist**: take three pairwise disjoint perfect matchings of $\{1,\dots,6\}$ (say $\{12,34,56\}$, $\{13,25,46\}$, $\{14,26,35\}$) and let $J_k$ be the sum of the corresponding basis $2$-forms.* **Each is a complex structure** (block-diagonal with $2\times2$ rotation blocks, so $J_k^2=-\mathrm{Id}_6$ and $A_k=\mathrm{Id}_6$ for all three), **and they are pairwise trace-orthogonal**: disjointness of the matchings makes $(J_k)_{ab}(J_l)_{ba}$ vanish termwise, so $\mathop{\mathrm{tr}}(J_kJ_l)=0$. *Each has $\lVert J_k\rVert^2=3$, so $J_k/\sqrt3$ is a unit orthonormal triple.* **So neither orthonormality nor equal squares obstructs anything at $\mathbb R^6$** (*the whole force of case 2 is the wedge condition, which these three must therefore fail), **and both audits verified in closed form that they do**, the three self-wedges being $2(e_{1234}+e_{1256}+e_{3456})$, $2(-e_{1235}+e_{1346}-e_{2456})$ and $2(-e_{1246}-e_{1345}+e_{2356})$, with pairwise disjoint supports in $\Lambda^4$ and so pairwise orthogonal there.* **The construction also settles the dimension question outright**: three pairwise trace-orthogonal complex structures exist on $\mathbb R^6$, so nothing here wants $4\mid n$. *Supplied by [r780](../reviews/REVIEW_r780.md).*

**Case 2, all three equal, $A=cI_6$.** *Then $\omega_i/\sqrt c$ is orthogonal and skew, a complex structure, and unit norm forces $c=\tfrac13$, so $\omega_i^2=-\tfrac13\mathrm{Id}_6$ and $c$ is not a free parameter.* **The Hodge star here is $V$'s, not $\mathbb R^n$'s**: $\star(J\wedge J)$ lands in $\Lambda^2$ only in six dimensions, and read on $\mathbb R^n$ for $n>6$ the identity is false. *On $V$, every compatible complex structure satisfies $\star_V(J\wedge J)=2\epsilon_JJ$ with $\epsilon_J=\pm1$ by orientation.* **Equal $J_i\wedge J_i$ forces $J_i=\pm K$ for a single $K$**, *and nothing weaker survives, since $\star$ is injective, so equal wedges give $\omega_j=\pm\omega_1$ exactly.* **The contradiction is then linear independence rather than orthogonality**: $J_i=\pm K$ leaves the three forms spanning a line, so the rank cannot be three. *[r780](../reviews/REVIEW_r780.md) supplied both strengthenings; the trace condition $\mathop{\mathrm{tr}}(J_iJ_j)\ne0$ is not needed at all, and $\epsilon_J$ is well defined because $U(3)\subset SO(6)$ is connected.* **This closes nothing outside $\dim V=6$**, where alone $\star_V(J\wedge J)$ lands in $\Lambda^2V$, the dimension count $\binom{2m}4=\binom{2m}2$ holding at $2m=6$ and nowhere else. *The constraint is on $\dim V$ and not on $n$, which the block first conflated.* $\square$

**Case 3, exactly two agree, spectrum $(a,a,b)$ with $a\ne b$.** *$A$'s eigenspaces are a $4$-plane $Q$ and a plane $P$, both preserved, so $\omega_i=\sqrt a\,J_i\oplus\tau_i\sqrt b\,\varepsilon_P$ with $J_i$ a complex structure on $Q$ and $\tau_i=\pm1$.* **Equal wedges force $J_i=\sigma_iJ$ for one $J$**: the wedge splits into a $\Lambda^4Q$ part $a\,J_i\wedge J_i$ and a cross term $2\tau_i\sqrt{ab}\,J_i\wedge\varepsilon_P$ in $\Lambda^2Q\otimes\Lambda^2P$, the $\varepsilon_P\wedge\varepsilon_P$ term vanishing. **$J\wedge J=2\eta\,\mathrm{vol}_Q$ with $\eta=\pm1$ according to whether $J$'s orientation matches $Q$'s, NOT $+2$ always, which a first draft asserted.** *The cross term is what carries the conclusion*: equating it across $i$ gives $\tau_iJ_i$ all equal, hence $J_i=\sigma_iJ$ for one $J$, **and in fact $\sigma_i=\tau_i$** ([r782](../reviews/REVIEW_r782.md)), which makes $\rho_i=\sigma_i\tau_i=+1$ outright and retires the three-signs step: $2a=-b$ follows at once. **The $\Lambda^4Q$ condition is then automatic, but only in that order, and the sequencing is load-bearing.** *Read alone the $\Lambda^4Q$ equality says $\eta_1=\eta_2=\eta_3$, which is a real restriction; it becomes vacuous only after the cross term has already given $J_i=\sigma_iJ$, since $(-J)\wedge(-J)=J\wedge J$ leaves $\eta$ unchanged, *so the corrected sign costs the argument nothing, and the block had the right conclusion for a reason it stated wrongly.* **Then orthogonality is a sign identity that has no solution.** *With $\mathop{\mathrm{tr}}(J^2)=-4$ on $Q$ and $\varepsilon_P^2=-\mathrm{Id}_P$, $\mathop{\mathrm{tr}}(\omega_i\omega_j)=-4a\sigma_i\sigma_j-2b\tau_i\tau_j$, so orthogonality reads $4a\,\sigma_i\sigma_j=-2b\,\tau_i\tau_j$ at all three pairs. Setting $\rho_i=\sigma_i\tau_i$, the ratio $\sigma_i\sigma_j/(\tau_i\tau_j)=\rho_i\rho_j$ must be one constant, and three signs with all pairwise products equal force that constant to be $+1$*, **whence $4a=-2b$, impossible for $a,b>0$.** $\square$

> **WHAT IS ENTAILED, STATED WITHOUT INFLATION.** *At $\dim V=6$ the coincident case is empty, and since [L183](../findings/diagonal-half.md#lemma-l183-a-rank-three-weyl-tensor-is-an-orthonormal-triple-whose-images-are-collinear) leaves only all-coincide or all-distinct-and-collinear, what remains **there** is the distinct-and-collinear case.* **At $\dim V\ge8$ only case 1 survives**, the pairwise-distinct-$c_j^2$ sub-case, *cases 2 and 3 being strictly six-dimensional, the first because $\star(J\wedge J)$ lands in $\Lambda^2$ only at $n=6$ and the second because its splitting is $4+2$.* **A first version of this box read "one case left" while its own next sentence said nothing reaches $\dim V\ge8$; the two contradicted each other**, and [r782](../reviews/REVIEW_r782.md) caught it. *A ledger row taken from the inflated form would have been wrong, and rows are what later work is built on.*
