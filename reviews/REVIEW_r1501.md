## A. Fidelity

- **`blockR` is the block.** `Rp a b c d e f` is $\tfrac12\sum_{ij}B_{ij}w_iw_j^{\mathsf T}$ with $\lVert w_i\rVert^2=2$ and the $w_i$ orthogonal, so $\tfrac12w_k^{\mathsf T}(\mathrm{Rp})w_j=B_{kj}$, which is L178N4.lean's `blkP` (and `blkM` for `Rm`), the block in the orthonormal basis $w_i/\sqrt2$. `blockR a b c d e` is exactly $[[a,b,c],[b,d,e],[c,e,-a-d]]$ cast to ℝ, the argument pattern `Rp a b c d e (-a - d)` that `L178_n4_form` produces. `blockR_trace_sq` agrees with `normSq_Rp`, so $\lVert R\rVert^2=\mathop{\mathrm{tr}}B^2$ on both sides. **Faithful.**
- **The statement.** Step 5 says equality forces a repeated root and the roots $(2,-1,-1)/\sqrt6$ of $x^3-\tfrac12x-\tfrac1{3\sqrt6}$. `L178_n4_eq`'s conclusion is that spectrum for the normalised block, with the $2/\sqrt6$ eigenvalue of multiplicity exactly one (the `card = 1` clause), so the multiplicities are pinned and not only the value set. The hypothesis $P^2=\tfrac32\lVert R\rVert^6$ is scale-free and reads $\theta^2=\tfrac32$ at unit norm. **Faithful to the forward direction.**
- **Positive scale.** $P(R)=9\det B$ (`L178_theta_sd`/`_asd`), so $P>0$ is $\det B>0$, which selects the root $e_3=+\tfrac1{3\sqrt6}$. At $P<0$ the same argument gives $(-2,1,1)/\sqrt6$, which is $-W_{\mathbb{CP}^2}$, and no $O(4)$ frame change reaches it, since a frame change (orientation-reversing ones included, which swap $\Lambda^+$ and $\Lambda^-$) preserves the block spectrum. So `0 < Pcubic R` is the right reading of "positive scale", and the one-sided $\theta=+\sqrt{3/2}$ is the record's equality case of $\theta\le\sqrt{3/2}$. **Faithful.**
- **Rigidity owed.** The file header (lines 20 to 21) and the INDEX row both name the passage from spectrum to "$W_{\mathbb{CP}^2}$ up to an $O(n)$ frame change" as owed. *Note, no gap:* the stated requirement, "$SO(4)$ transitive on the orthonormal frames of $\Lambda^\pm$", is slightly short of what the step uses. $SO(4)$ acts on $\Lambda^+$ through $SO(3)$, which is transitive on oriented frames only (enough to diagonalise $B$, choosing the eigenframe's orientation), and carrying the $\Lambda^-$ case onto $W_{\mathbb{CP}^2}$ on $\Lambda^+$ needs an orientation-reversing element of $O(4)$ as well. Both are standard and the owed item stands as recorded.
- **Scope.** Rational $W$ only, as in `L178_n4`. The equality case is not vacuous there: `Rp 2 0 0 (-1) 0 (-1)` is rational with block $\mathop{\mathrm{diag}}(2,-1,-1)$. **CONFIRMED over rational $R$, forward direction.**

## B. Soundness

- **Trace lemmas.** `blockR_hermitian` is entrywise symmetry by `fin_cases`. `blockR_trace` sums the diagonal $a+d+(-a-d)=0$. `blockR_trace_sq` and `blockR_trace_cube` expand by `Matrix.mul_apply` over `Fin 3` and close by `ring`, the latter against $3\det3$, which is Newton's $p_3=3e_3$ at $e_1=0$ for the symmetric trace-free block. - **`L178_eq_block`, $\det B=t^3/(3\sqrt6)$.** Casting `heq` gives $81D^2=\tfrac32s^3$. With $t=\sqrt s$, $t^2=s$ and $(t^3/(3\sqrt6))^2=t^6/54=s^3/54$, so $D^2=(t^3/(3\sqrt6))^2$, the product $(D-x)(D+x)=0$ follows by `nlinarith`, and $D+x>0$ from $D>0$, $t>0$ kills the second factor. **Sound.**
- **The normalised traces.** $M=(1/t)B$. $\mathop{\mathrm{tr}}M=0$ by `trace_smul`. $\mathop{\mathrm{tr}}M^2=(1/t)^2s=1$. $\mathop{\mathrm{tr}}M^3=(1/t)^3\cdot3D=3/(3\sqrt6)=1/\sqrt6=(3-2)/\sqrt6$, closed by `field_simp; ring` after substituting $D$. **Sound.**

Hermitian proofs are propositions, so the eigenvalues do not depend on the witness. **Sound.**
