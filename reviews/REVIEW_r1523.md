## A. Fidelity

The source ([diagonal-half.md:557](../findings/diagonal-half.md)): W ≠ 0 a Weyl tensor coordinate-diagonal in some orthonormal frame {f_a}, sectional matrix K_ab = W_abab, G the graph of pairs with K_ab ≠ 0. Then every vertex has degree 0 or at least 2, G has at least four edges, and rank(W : Λ² → Λ²) ≥ 4. The proof's first line is the locus identity W(f_a ∧ f_b) = K_ab f_a ∧ f_b, giving rank = |E|, and Ricci-flatness on the locus is Σ_b K_ab = 0.

- **A-1. WD** ([L176Op.lean:30](../lean/HamiltonsCubic/Results/L176Op.lean)). The layer's `phi a b` is single b a 1 − single a b 1 and `inner2 X Y = ½ tr(XᵀY)` ([WeylReal.lean:39](../lean/HamiltonsCubic/Base/WeylReal.lean), line 45), so ⟨φ_ab, φ_cd⟩ = δ_ac δ_bd − δ_ad δ_bc (`inner2_phi_phi`, [Families.lean:84](../lean/HamiltonsCubic/Base/Families.lean)), and {φ_ab}_{a<b} is orthonormal. Since φ_ba = −φ_ab and φ_aa = 0, the ordered double sum ½ Σ_{a,b} K_ab ⟨X, φ_ab⟩ φ_ab equals Σ_{a<b} ½(K_ab + K_ba) ⟨X, φ_ab⟩ φ_ab, which for symmetric K is Σ_{a<b} K_ab ⟨X, φ_ab⟩ φ_ab: the self-adjoint operator diagonal in the coordinate basis of Λ² with eigenvalue K_ab on φ_ab. The diagonal of K never enters the operator. WD K also kills the symmetric part of any matrix, which is harmless since `IsWeyl` reads R only at skew arguments. Faithful, with the source's K_ab = W_abab read as ⟨W φ_ab, φ_ab⟩ in the layer's sign convention, immaterial here since the Weyl conditions are linear in K and the conclusion is sign-blind.
- **A-2. WD_phi** (line 47). W_K φ_cd = K_cd φ_cd for symmetric K, derived by collapsing the two Kronecker double sums (`dsum_ite`) and folding the (d, c) term through `phi_swap` and symmetry. It is the source's locus identity with f_a the coordinate vectors. Faithful.
- **A-3. WD_weyl_iff** (line 92). Checked by hand against the layer's definitions ([WeylReal.lean:106](../lean/HamiltonsCubic/Base/WeylReal.lean) to line 130). `Ric R a b = Σ_c ⟨R φ_ac, φ_bc⟩` gives Σ_c K_ac(δ_ab − δ_cb δ_ac) = δ_ab(Σ_c K_ac − K_aa), which is `ric_WD` (line 62) once hollowness removes K_aa. `bianchi R i j k l = ⟨Rφ_ij, φ_kl⟩ + ⟨Rφ_ik, φ_lj⟩ + ⟨Rφ_il, φ_jk⟩` becomes a signed sum of K_ij, K_ik, K_il over Kronecker conditions, and every configuration cancels: at i = k, j = l, i ≠ j the first term gives K_ij, the third −K_il = −K_ij, the second zero. So first Bianchi holds on the whole family with symmetry alone, the standard fact that a curvature operator diagonal in a coordinate bivector basis is always algebraic. Skewness is `WD_skew`, self-adjointness is `WD_inner` read both ways. Hollowness and symmetry are hypotheses of the iff, not conclusions, and must be: WD K sees only the symmetrised off-diagonal part of K. Correct. See C-66 for the row's wording.
- **A-4. coeffD** (line 114). Its entries are ⟨W_K φ_p, φ_q⟩ over p, q ∈ `Pr n`, the pairs a < b, and {φ_p} is an orthonormal basis of the skew matrices under `inner2`. So `coeffD K` is the matrix of W_K restricted to Λ² in an orthonormal basis, and its rank is rank(W_K : Λ² → Λ²). `coeffD_eq` (line 118) makes it `diagonal (K p.1.1 p.1.2)`, the off-diagonal case closing because p < and q < exclude the crossed match. `rank_coeffD` (line 135) counts the nonzero diagonal entries through Mathlib's `rank_diagonal` and a bijection onto `edges K`, which is `univ.filter (p.1 < p.2 ∧ K p.1 p.2 ≠ 0)` ([L176Statement.lean:41](../lean/HamiltonsCubic/Results/L176Statement.lean)). Correct. **Gap A-dict (minor).** Two dictionaries are elementary and untyped: that `(coeffD K).rank` is the operator rank on Λ² (the layer has no typed Λ² rank, exactly r1522's A-rank), and that an arbitrary Weyl operator R whose matrix on {φ_ab} is diagonal agrees with WD K on bivectors for K_ab = ⟨R φ_ab, φ_ab⟩ (the analogue of L178's `supported_eq_WC`, which the L178 chain did type). Row 70 now carries the first kind of note for L178 ("that reading … is not itself typed"). Row 66 ([lean/INDEX.md:66](../lean/INDEX.md)) states "`coeffD_eq` and `rank_coeffD`, the Λ²-rank of W_K is the number of edges" and "a nonzero Weyl operator diagonal in the coordinate frame" with neither dictionary named, so the same reading is by hand and unrecorded here.
- **A-5. L176_op** (line 148). For K symmetric and hollow with `IsWeyl (WD K)` and K ≠ 0, it gives 4 ≤ `(coeffD K).rank`, through `rank_coeffD` and `four_le_card_edges` fed the row sums from `WD_weyl_iff`. With K hollow, K ≠ 0 is W_K ≠ 0 on bivectors by `WD_phi`. So it is L176's edge bound and rank bound for W coordinate-diagonal in the coordinate frame, modulo A-dict. The degree clause is L176Statement.lean's `card_nbr_zero_or_two_le` (line 67), on the same K. Correct.
- **A-6. What is owed.** L176 is stated for some orthonormal frame {f_a}. With f_a = O e_a for O ∈ O(n), the conjugate of W by O is coordinate-diagonal in e with the same K, so the general case needs `IsWeyl` and the Λ²-rank invariant under that conjugation. [Frames.lean:34](../lean/HamiltonsCubic/Base/Frames.lean) defines `conjOp` and proves only `conjOp_one`, and a grep finds no invariance theorem for it, so row 66's "an O(n) conjugation whose invariance of the Weyl conditions and of rank the layer does not carry" is accurate. With A-dict, that is the whole of what separates `L176_op` from L176 as stated.
- **A-7. A witness.** At n ≥ 4 put K_12 = K_21 = c, K_23 = K_32 = −c, K_34 = K_43 = c, K_41 = K_14 = −c, zero elsewhere, c ≠ 0 (the source's four-cycle, [diagonal-half.md:567](../findings/diagonal-half.md)). K is symmetric and hollow, and each row sum cancels in pairs (row 1: c − c, row 2: c − c, row 3: −c + c, row 4: c − c). So `IsWeyl (WD K)` holds by `WD_weyl_iff`, K ≠ 0, and `coeffD K` has exactly four nonzero diagonal entries, rank 4. The hypotheses are satisfiable and the conclusion is sharp.
