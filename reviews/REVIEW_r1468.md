## A. weylPart_unique

The statement at [L2SphereWeyl.lean:131](../lean/HamiltonsCubic/Results/L2SphereWeyl.lean#L131) takes linear Weyl operators $W_1,W_2$ ([IsWeylOp](../lean/HamiltonsCubic/Base/FoxProduct.lean#L110) is `IsLinOp ∧ IsWeyl`), maps $K_1,K_2$ with `innerOp K R = 0` for every linear `R` with `Ric R = 0`, and $W_1X+K_1X=W_2X+K_2X$ at every matrix $X$. It concludes $W_1\varphi_{ij}=W_2\varphi_{ij}$.

- **$D$ is linear.** Additivity and homogeneity follow from those of $W_1$ and $W_2$ by `abel` and `smul_sub`. Correct.
- **$D$ is Ricci-flat.** [Ric](../lean/HamiltonsCubic/Base/WeylReal.lean#L106) is $\sum_c\langle R\varphi_{ac},\varphi_{bc}\rangle$, linear in the first slot through `inner2_sub_l`, so $\mathrm{Ric}(D)=\mathrm{Ric}(W_1)-\mathrm{Ric}(W_2)=0$ from the fourth `IsWeyl` clause of each. Correct. Only linearity and Ricci-flatness of $D$ are needed, because `hK` quantifies over exactly that class.
- **$\langle D,D\rangle=0$.** [innerOp](../lean/HamiltonsCubic/Base/WeylReal.lean#L135) is $\tfrac12\sum_{a,b}\langle R\varphi_{ab},S\varphi_{ab}\rangle$. Substituting $D\varphi_{ab}=K_2\varphi_{ab}-K_1\varphi_{ab}$ in the first slot gives $\langle K_2,D\rangle-\langle K_1,D\rangle$, and both vanish by `hK₁`, `hK₂` applied to $D$. Correct.
- **Definiteness.** `inner2 X X` is $\tfrac12\sum_{a,b}X_{ab}^2\ge0$ through `inner2_eq_sum`, so $\langle D,D\rangle=0$ forces each `inner2 (D φ_ab) (D φ_ab)` to vanish (`sum_eq_zero_iff_of_nonneg` twice), and then each entry of $D\varphi_{ij}$ (`mul_self_eq_zero`). Correct. The line `have := congrArg (fun Y => Y) hD0` at [L2SphereWeyl.lean:173](../lean/HamiltonsCubic/Results/L2SphereWeyl.lean#L173) is dead and harmless.
- **Non-vacuity.** `sphereProduct_weylPart` supplies $W=c\cdot$`diagOp(sphereWeight S)`, $K=$`diagOp(knWeight h)` meeting every hypothesis, once `isLinOp_diagOp` gives linearity. The conclusion is not free either: for any nonzero $D\in\mathcal W_n$, the pair $(W+D,K-D)$ satisfies every hypothesis but `hK`, which fails because $\langle K-D,D\rangle=-\langle D,D\rangle<0$.
- **"On bivectors" is enough.** Both $W_i$ are linear and the $\varphi_{ij}$ span the skew matrices, so the conclusion is equality on all of $\Lambda^2$. Off $\Lambda^2$ the two can differ, and `hsum` does not constrain it, since `hK` reads `K` only at the $\varphi_{ab}$. Every functional the layer applies to an operator (`innerOp`, `normSq`, `trCube`, `theta`) evaluates it only at the $\varphi_{ab}$, so comparison on $\Lambda^2$ is the layer's notion of equality. *Candidate withdrawn*: "the theorem concludes only on basis bivectors, not on every skew $X$". Located and exhibited, but the extension is one linearity step and nothing downstream reads the difference.

## B. sphereProduct_weylPart_unique

`#check` gives, at [L2SphereWeyl.lean:180](../lean/HamiltonsCubic/Results/L2SphereWeyl.lean#L180): for $p,q\ge2$, every `W K` with `IsWeylOp W`, `K` orthogonal to every linear Ricci-flat operator, and `diagOp (sectProd S) X = W X + K X` at every `X`, has `W (phi i j) = diagOp (cW S • sphereWeight S) (phi i j)`. The proof feeds `weylPart_unique` the split from `sphereProduct_weylPart` with `⟨isLinOp_diagOp _, h3⟩`. So the uniqueness half of r1467's G1 is a theorem now, and every split's Weyl summand is required to be an `IsWeylOp`.

**Gap G1 (minor): the sphere summand's `IsWeylOp` is proved and not stated.** *Location*: the docstring at [L2SphereWeyl.lean:177](../lean/HamiltonsCubic/Results/L2SphereWeyl.lean#L177), "The sphere product's Weyl part, as a LINEAR Weyl operator", and the commit message's "now as an IsWeylOp". *The failure*: no theorem in the file has `IsWeylOp (diagOp (cW S • sphereWeight S))` in its type. [sphereProduct_weylPart](../lean/HamiltonsCubic/Results/L2SphereWeyl.lean#L114) still exports the conjunct as `IsWeyl`, and `sphereProduct_weylPart_unique` quantifies over splits, so it would be true, and would say nothing about existence, if the sphere summand were not linear. "Is the Weyl part" needs existence and uniqueness together, and the existence half in the record's $\mathcal W_n$ lives only inside a proof term. *The opposite position*: `isLinOp_diagOp` is a public theorem in [SharpDiag.lean:291](../lean/HamiltonsCubic/Base/SharpDiag.lean#L291), the conjunction is one anonymous constructor, and the kernel has checked exactly that term. That is why the gap is minor. It stands because r1467 named this conjunct, the commit says it was fixed, and a reader of the two statements still has to assemble it.
