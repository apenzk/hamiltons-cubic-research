The three biconditionals are stated over naturals $p,q\ge2$, and since $\min(p,q)=2\iff p=2\lor q=2$ in that range the third is the block's clause. The proof multiplies `half_sub_mu` by $2(p-1)(q-1)>0$ and splits on $(p-2)(q-2)$ being $0$, $1$ or above $1$, the last from $4\le p$ or $4\le q$. This is exactly the display at [nondegeneracy.md:449](../findings/nondegeneracy.md) and its reading "fails only at $\min(p,q)=2$ and at $(3,3)$ … the two failures are different failures". Genuine content.
- **A-2. $(2,2)$** ([L11.lean:88](../lean/HamiltonsCubic/Results/L11.lean)). $\mu_{2,2}=2/2=1$. $\dim\mathcal W_4=4\cdot5\cdot6\cdot1/12=10=4+4+2$, and the trace $-4+2+2=0$.
