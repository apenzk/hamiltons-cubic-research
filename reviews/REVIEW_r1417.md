## 1. The objects

**The left side.** [conventions.md](../findings/conventions.md) defines $\theta=P(W)/\lVert W\rVert^3$, and the L52 block works at $\lVert M\rVert_F=1$. r1404 identified `Pcubic` with the record's $P=\langle W,Q(W)\rangle$ and r1415 identified `normSq R` with $\lVert M\rVert_F^2$ (it is `sum_mu_sq`'s right side). So `Pcubic R` under `normSq R = 1` is $\theta$.

**The $\varphi$ side.** `Kmat R a b = R4 R a b a b` is $W_{abab}$, zero on the diagonal by skew-symmetry, and the sum over `Pairs n` reads it as the sectional vector of length $\binom n2$. `L52_matrix_bound` is applied to `Mpair R`, whose diagonal entry at the pair $(a,b)$ is $M_{(ab),(ab)}=W_{abab}$, and the display's $K$ is written as `Kmat` directly. `Bw_diag_pair` is `phi_block_diag`, the record's $\langle\mathring W\varphi_{ab},\varphi_{ab}\rangle=W_{abab}$ with $\varphi_{ab}=(E_{ab}+E_{ba})/\sqrt2$.

**The $\psi$ side.** `Bw_diag_psi` is `diag_block` at $u=v=w_k$, $\langle\mathring W\mathop{\mathrm{diag}}w_k,\mathop{\mathrm{diag}}w_k\rangle=-w_k^{\mathsf T}Kw_k$, the record's polarised formula (bispectral.md line 495) without the restriction $u,v\perp\mathbf 1$, which `diag_block` does not need. At an orthonormal eigenbasis this is $-\kappa_k$, which `psi_block` states with the off-diagonal zeros as well.

**The extra zero.** The record's $\psi_k$ are an eigenbasis of $K$ on $\mathbf 1^\perp$, $n-1$ of them, so its $K'$ has $\binom n2+n-1=\dim S^2_0$ entries. `Bw` lives on $S^2$ with $\binom n2+n$. At Ricci-flat $R$, `K_row_sum` gives $K\mathbf 1=0$, $\mathbf 1^\perp$ is invariant because $K$ is symmetric, and the spectrum of $K$ on $\mathbb R^n$ is its spectrum on $\mathbf 1^\perp$ together with $0$. The multiset $\{-w_k^{\mathsf T}Kw_k\}$ at any orthonormal eigenbasis on $\mathbb R^n$ is minus that spectrum, so it is the record's $\psi$ diagonal plus one $0$, whether or not the chosen basis contains $\mathbf 1/\sqrt n$. The basis elements $\mathop{\mathrm{diag}}w_k$ need not be trace-free and so need not be the record's $\psi_k$, but only the diagonal values enter the display. $p_2$ and $p_3$ do not see an extra $0$. The header's last paragraph says exactly this, and the row marks it as prose, correctly.

**Complete families.** `hw` is $\sum_kw_k(i)w_k(j)=\delta_{ij}$, i.e. $W^{\mathsf T}W=I$ for the square matrix $W$ whose rows are the $w_k$. A square matrix with a left inverse has it as a right inverse, so $WW^{\mathsf T}=I$ and the rows are orthonormal. Hence the $\mathop{\mathrm{diag}}w_k$ are orthonormal, orthogonal to every off-diagonal $\varphi_{ab}$ by support, and the whole family `ew w` is orthonormal with $\dim S^2$ members. `expand_sym_w` proves the reconstruction formula, so spanning is machine-checked, and orthonormality is not. The downstream theorems do not need it: `Bw_comp` uses the expansion, `trace_Bw_eq` computes the diagonal block's trace directly from `hw`, and `sum_omega_w_sq` and `sum_omega_w_cube` go through those two. What orthonormality buys is the reading of `Bw`'s eigenvalues as the spectrum of $\mathring W$, and so the frame-independence of the constant $m$ in `hω`, which the record's conventions line requires. A `frob_ew` in the form of `frob_e` would close it.

## 2. Hypotheses and scope

`L52_record` takes `hR` (linear), `hskew` (bivector-preserving), `hsym` (self-adjoint on bivectors), first Bianchi, `normSq R = 1`, and the two spectral bounds. The first four with unit norm are what `L52_matrix_bound`'s power-sum hypotheses need, through `sum_mu_sq` ($\sum\mu^2=\lVert M\rVert_F^2=1$) and `sum_omega_w_sq` ($\sum\omega^2=3$), and what `L50_bispectral` needs. The record states L52 for $W\in\mathrm{Weyl}_n$, $n\ge4$. Every Weyl tensor satisfies the Lean hypotheses, so the record's statement is the Ricci-flat instance, and the $n\ge4$ restriction is not used by the inequality. The theorem is a correct generalisation. Off the Ricci-flat locus $K\mathbf 1\ne0$ in general and the "extra $0$" reading of 1c fails, but the displayed inequality with $K'$ the diagonal of `Bw` remains true, which is what the Lean states. The docstring and the row name the hypotheses without bivector preservation, and the header's "at a unit Weyl-type `R`" names the record's case rather than the theorem's.

`hω` bounds the $S^2$ spectrum of `Bw` below by $-m$. At Weyl $R$ that spectrum is the record's $\omega$ on $S^2_0$ plus one $0$ (r1416 item 2b), so `hω` is the record's $\omega_i\ge-m$ together with $0\ge-m$. The record's hypothesis already forces $m\ge0$, since $\mathop{\mathrm{tr}}_{S^2_0}\mathring W=0$ at a Weyl tensor puts $\min\omega\le0$. The two hypotheses are therefore equivalent at Weyl $R$, by an argument that is prose and that uses $\sum\omega=0$, owed in the L50 row. The L52 row should list it.

## 3. `frob_e`

The statement is Kronecker orthonormality of `e` over all of `S2Idx n`. The proof splits the four cases of `inl`/`inr` pairs and uses $\sqrt2\cdot\sqrt2=2$ against `symB`'s factor $\tfrac12$, which is the hand argument of r1416 section 1. With `expand_sym` and the count $\binom n2+n$ it makes `e` an orthonormal basis of $S^2$, and the L50 row's "in the orthonormal basis" is now a proved claim.
