## A. The near-critical equation

Quoted from [findings/problem.md](../findings/problem.md), line 667: "*Let $n\ge4$, $S\in\mathcal D_n$ with $\sum_{i<j}s_{ij}^2=1$, and $S\circ S=\lambda S+E$ with $E\in\mathcal D_n$ and $\lambda>\lvert E\rvert$, norms over unordered pairs.*"

**The product.** L223 does not define $\circ$ in its own block. Its proof, line 676, uses $\langle S\circ S,S\rangle=\tfrac12\sum_{i\ne j}s_{ij}^3+\tfrac12\operatorname{tr}S^3$, which is the unordered-pair pairing of the hollow array $P_{ab}=s_{ab}^2+(S^2)_{ab}$ ($a\ne b$) with $S$. That array is in $\mathcal D_n$ when $S$ is: its row sum at $a$ is $\sum_{b}s_{ab}^2-(S^2)_{aa}+(S\cdot S\mathbf 1)_a=0$ since $(S^2)_{aa}=\sum_b s_{ab}^2$ and $S\mathbf1=0$. So `hnear` drops no projection. It is L57's `hcrit` with the defect added, and there `diagP_eq_lam` makes $\lambda=\theta(S)$, which is why $\lambda$ is compared with $\bar\Theta$.

**$E$.** `hnear` fixes $E$ off the diagonal as $P-\lambda S$, symmetric with zero row sums once the diagonal is zero. So `hE0` alone is equivalent to $E\in\mathcal D_n$ given `hnear`, and item 1's upper bound, which takes `hE : IsWeight E`, uses only its `hollow` field.

## B to E. Items 1 to 3

Quoted, lines 668 to 670: items 1 to 3 as in the table. The Lean definitions match symbol for symbol: $\Theta=\sqrt{2(n-1)(n-2)}/n$, $Z_s=\sqrt{(n-2)/(2(n-1))}$, $\Delta=\Theta-\lambda+\max(0,-v^{\mathsf T}Ev)/\mu$, $\eta_1$ and `Zn` as in B.

**Which eigenvalue.** The record takes $\mu$ the top eigenvalue. `L223_items12` and `L223_item3` take any unit eigenvector of any $\mu>0$. That is a strengthening, and the proof never needs topness: step 1 is Cauchy–Schwarz and steps 2 and 3 use only $Sv=\mu v$ and $\sum v=0$ (from $\mu\ne0$).

**Item 1's upper bound, and where it is joined.** `L223_item1_upper` takes the top property as `htop` and an arbitrary unit $v$. For the record's item 1 one instantiates both theorems at the top unit eigenvector. That pair satisfies `hv`, `h2` and `htop`, and has $\mu>0$ ($\operatorname{tr}S=0$, $S\ne0$), but only the first three facts are typed: `exists_pos_eigen` ([L57Critical.lean](../lean/HamiltonsCubic/Results/L57Critical.lean) line 264) chooses $j$ maximising `hA.eigenvalues` and exports $\exists\mu\,v,\ 0<\mu\wedge Sv=\mu v\wedge\sum v^2=1$, dropping the maximality. So "item 1 as stated" is a conjunction of two theorems whose common instance is by reading.

**Item 3.** `sq_gap_le` gives $Z_s^2-Z_n^2\le2xZ_s^2$ from $Z_s(1-x)\le Z_n$, correctly handling $x>1$, and does not use $Z_n\ge0$. $\eta_3=\frac{2(n-2)}n(Z_s^2-Z_n^2)$ is the record's step 4 identity. `abs_sub_le` is $\big(\lvert t\rvert-s\big)^2\le(t^2-s^2)^2/s^2$, the record's coordinate bound at $s=1/\sqrt n$.

## F to H. Item 4

Quoted, line 671: "*If $4\sqrt{n-2}\sqrt{\Delta/\bar\Theta}<2(1-\Delta/\bar\Theta)$, then $p,q\ge2$ and $\lvert S-B\rvert\le\big(\sqrt2+\frac{4\sqrt{n-2}}{1-\Delta/\bar\Theta}\big)\sqrt{\Delta/\bar\Theta}$, where $B$ is the unit positive multiple of the projection of $(w_iw_j)$ onto $\mathcal D_n$, constant on the three classes of pairs of the sign partition and a positive multiple of the vertex matrix of $S^p\times S^q$*".

**`projD` is the orthogonal projection.** For hollow $X$, $\Pi X\in\mathcal D_n$ (`projD_isWeight`, which also needs $X$ symmetric) and $\langle X-\Pi X,Y\rangle=0$ for every $Y\in\mathcal D_n$ (`projD_orth`). These two facts determine the orthogonal projection. The formula $X_{ab}-(r_a+r_b)/(n-2)+T/((n-1)(n-2))$ is the record's $Z$ recipe applied to general $X$, and `projD_outer` recovers $Z(v)$ at $r_a=-v_a^2$, $T=-1$. Projecting `outer w` in place of the full $(w_iw_j)$ is the same, because a diagonal matrix is Frobenius-orthogonal to every hollow one.

**$B$ and the sphere product.** `projD_sign` computes $\Pi(ww^{\mathsf T})$ class by class, and `projD_sign_sphere` writes it as $c\,$`sphereWeight`$\,T$ with $c=\frac{4(p-1)(q-1)}{n(n-1)(n-2)}>0$ at $p,q\ge2$. `sphereWeight` is $q/(p-1)$ and $p/(q-1)$ in the two blocks and $-1$ across. For $S^p\times S^q$ with unit factors the sectional curvature is $1$ in-block and $0$ across, and subtracting the Schouten part leaves zero row sums, so the Weyl vertex matrix is positive in-block and negative across: the geometric sign, and the one [L57Sphere.lean](../lean/HamiltonsCubic/Results/L57Sphere.lean) already uses for L57's equality case. $B=W/\lvert W\rvert$ with $\lvert W\rvert>0$ established in the proof, so $B$ is the unit positive multiple, constant on the three classes because `sphereWeight` is.

**The proof's route.** $\lvert S-\hat Z\rvert^2=2-2\cos\alpha$, $\lvert Z-W\rvert\le\lVert v-w\rVert\le2\sqrt{n-2}\,Z_*\sqrt x$ (`projD_nonexp`, `outer_dist`, item 3), $\lvert\hat Z-\hat W\rvert\le2\lvert Z-W\rvert/\lvert Z\rvert$ (`nrmU_normalize`), and $\lvert Z\rvert\ge Z_*(1-x)$ (item 2). The smallness hypothesis gives $\lvert Z-W\rvert<\lvert Z\rvert$, hence $\lvert W\rvert>0$, hence $p,q\ge2$ by `projD_sign_zero`. This is the record's step 5 line for line.

**Non-vacuity (screen).** At the exact unit sphere products $S^2\times S^2$ and $S^3\times S^3$, a pure-Python screen finds $E=0$ to rounding, $\lambda=\bar\Theta$ ($0.866025$, $1.054093$), the top eigenvector the balanced sign vector, $x=0$, and the hypothesis $0<2$ holds, with $B=S$. At $S^2\times S^3$, $S^3\times S^4$, $S^4\times S^5$ it finds $E=0$, $x=0.0378$, $0.0101$, $0.0041$, and the hypothesis holds each time ($4\sqrt3\sqrt{0.0378}=1.35<1.92$ at $n=5$). This is a screen, not evidence for any claim above. It shows item 4's hypothesis has models at both parities.
