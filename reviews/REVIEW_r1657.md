## What the agreement statement would be

The record (findings/second-order.md, Lemma L127, "The table is forced") says L27's `O(p) × O(q)` decomposition of `L_e` and the table's sign-character block decomposition agree on the full spectrum with multiplicity. As a statement, at `p, q ≥ 3`: for every value `ν` in `{1, ½, λ_p, λ_q, −μ, μ, 0}`, the sum over the four rows of (block count × multiplicity of `ν` in that block) equals the sum of the dimensions of L27's summands on which `L_e` acts by `ν`. Two of those identities are the record's displays: at `λ_p`, `(p − 1)` from `𝒟ₙ` plus `binom p 2` within-factor tops equals `dim S²₀(ℝᵖ)`, and at the value L27's `trace_identity` places on `S²₀ ⊗ S²₀`, `binom p 2 · binom q 2 + (p − 1)(q − 1) + binom p 2 (q − 1) + binom q 2 (p − 1) = dim(S²₀ ⊗ S²₀)`. Both are proved in L127Statement.lean. What is untyped is the operator-level half, that both lists are the multiplicities of one operator `L_e` on the whole space, which needs L27's summands as `L_e`-eigenspaces, the part L27's row buckets as out. The Owed clause should say that, with the range.
