## 1. The sphere product

`sphereWeight S` puts $-1$ across the blocks and $q/(p-1)$, $p/(q-1)$ inside, which zero row sums force once the cross entry is fixed. The question for fidelity is the sign and the family, since $\theta$ is invariant only under positive scale. For $S^p(k_1)\times S^q(k_2)$ the curvature operator is diagonal on $\varphi_{ab}$ with $K_{ab}=k_1$, $k_2$ or $0$, and the Weyl weight is $K_{ab}-A_{aa}-A_{bb}$ with $A$ the Schouten tensor. Across the blocks this is
$$-(A_{aa}+A_{bb})=-\frac{(p-1)k_1(n-1-p)+(q-1)k_2(n-1-q)}{(n-1)(n-2)}=-\frac{(p-1)(q-1)(k_1+k_2)}{(n-1)(n-2)},$$
negative for spheres at every choice of radii (it vanishes only at $k_1=-k_2$, the conformally flat $S^p\times H^q$). The Weyl weight is therefore $c\cdot$`sphereWeight S` with $c>0$, and $\theta$ at the unit member is the product's $\theta$. `theta_sphereProduct` reads $\theta$ off `Pcubic` at unit norm and `sphereProductValue` supplies `Pcubic` $=$ `thetaSplit`, both on the standard three axioms, and `thetaSplitSq` is `thetaSpSq` by `ring`.

## 2. The competitor

`Wc n` is built from the three forms $J_a$, which satisfy the $\mathfrak{su}(2)$ relations (`J_br01`, `J_br02`, `J_br12`), embedded in the first four coordinates, with eigenvalues $(2,-1,-1)/\sqrt6$ on their span and $0$ elsewhere. That is the Kähler Weyl tensor of $\mathbb{CP}^2$, padded. The record's L2 statement names the competitor as Fox's $\mathcal S^4(\alpha)$, and its proof computes $\theta^2=3/2$ at $n=4$ and carries it to every $n\ge4$ by zero-padding, the construction `Wc` implements. The problem summary's item 4 distinguishes "literal $\mathbb{CP}^2$ zero-padded" from $\mathcal S^4(\alpha)$ while giving both $\theta=\sqrt{3/2}$, and L2's statement compares against the number $3/2$, so the Lean comparison against $\theta(W_c)^2$ is the record's comparison.

## 3. `L2_operators` against the record

The record's statement is about $\theta^{\rm sp}_{\max}(n)$, a maximum over finitely many splits. An upper bound on every admissible split is the upper half exactly, and one admissible split above $3/2$ is the lower half exactly. The first clause is the former. The second clause is the latter only if its witness is admissible, and the typed conclusion is
```
∃ (S : Finset (Fin n)) (t : ℝ), 0 < t ∧ normSq (diagOp (t • sphereWeight S)) = 1 ∧
    theta (Wc n) ^ 2 < theta (diagOp (t • sphereWeight S)) ^ 2
```
with no card bounds. The screen stated the record's max form at the operators, with $M$ bounded above by every admissible unit sphere product and attained by one, and derived it from `L2_operators`. The first half and the "no equality" conjunct closed. The second half closed in the branch where the witness has $2\le p$ and $2\le q$ and was left open in the others. The proof body has `hp'` and `hq'` in scope when it builds the witness, so the fix is to add `2 ≤ S.card ∧ 2 ≤ Sᶜ.card` to the existential. "No dimension sits at equality" then follows at the operators in two lines, as it does at `M` in `L2Statement.L2`.
