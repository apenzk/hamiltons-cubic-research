## 1. `ω ≻ d` and the extra zero

**The Lean objects.** `wK R` is `(Kmat_isHermitian R).eigenvectorBasis`, orthonormal and complete (`wK_norm`, `wK_complete`), with `Kmat R *ᵥ wK k = κ_k • wK k` (`wK_eigen`). `Bw w L u v = frob (L (ew w u)) (ew w v)`, and `ew w` is $\{\sqrt2\,\mathrm{symB}_{ab}\}_{a<b}\cup\{\mathop{\mathrm{diag}}w_k\}$ with $\sqrt2\,\mathrm{symB}_{ab}=(E_{ab}+E_{ba})/\sqrt2$, the record's $\varphi_{ab}$. r1417 settles that `ew w` is an orthonormal basis of $S^2$ for complete `w`, so the eigenvalues of `Bw (wK R) (ringW R)` are the spectrum of $\mathring W|_{S^2}$ with multiplicity.

**The spectrum.** At Ricci-flat $R$, `ringW_one` gives $(\mathring Wg)_{ij}=-\mathrm{Ric}_{ij}=0$, and `trace_ringW` gives $\mathop{\mathrm{tr}}\mathring Wh=-\langle\mathrm{Ric},h\rangle=0$, so $\mathring W$ maps $S^2$ into $S^2_0$ and kills $g$. With $S^2=S^2_0\oplus\mathbb Rg$ orthogonal, the Lean spectrum is $\omega\uplus\{0\}$. No choice of basis enters.

**The diagonal.** `Bw_diag_pair` gives $K_{ab}$ on the $\varphi$-block. `Bw_diag_psi` gives $-w_k^{\mathsf T}Kw_k$, which `wK_eigen` and `wK_norm` make $-\kappa_k$, $\kappa$ Mathlib's eigenvalue vector of $K$ on $\mathbb R^n$. Since $K$ is symmetric and $K\mathbf1=0$ (`K_row_sum`), $\mathbb R^n=\mathbf1^\perp\oplus\mathbb R\mathbf1$ is $K$-invariant, and the eigenvalue multiset on $\mathbb R^n$ is the multiset on $\mathbf1^\perp$ plus $\{0\}$. So the Lean diagonal is the record's $d$ plus one $0$.

**The repeated-zero case.** When $0$ has multiplicity at least $2$ in $K$, the $0$-eigenspace is at least two-dimensional and Mathlib may return an orthonormal basis of it that avoids $\mathbf1/\sqrt n$. Then several `diag (wK k)` carry trace, and `ew (wK R)` is not the record's basis with $g/\sqrt n$ adjoined. This does not matter: the spectrum above is basis-free, and the diagonal entries are the eigenvalues $-\kappa_k$ whatever the eigenvectors. Both multiset identities hold as stated.

**Cancellation.** For finite multisets, $x\uplus\{c\}\succ y\uplus\{c\}$ if and only if $x\succ y$. Totals differ by $c$ on both sides. The partial-sum condition is equivalent to $\sum_i(x_i-t)_+\ge\sum_i(y_i-t)_+$ for every real $t$ at equal totals, and the common entry adds $(c-t)_+$ to both sides. So `L54_omega_majorizes` specialises to exactly the record's $\omega\succ d$, and the record's own proof (Schur on $S^2_0$ in its basis) and the Lean proof (Schur on $S^2$) are the same theorem read in two ambient spaces.

**The header.** It says the $\psi$-block runs over "one more direction than the record's `𝟏^⊥`, with eigenvalue `0` there at a Ricci-flat `R` (`K_row_sum`), so `d` and `ω` each carry one extra `0`". The conclusion is right. `K_row_sum` gives the extra $0$ in $d$, and $\omega$'s comes from `ringW_one` and `trace_ringW`, which the sentence does not cite. "There" identifies the extra direction with $\mathbf1$, true only when Mathlib's basis contains $\mathbf1/\sqrt n$. A repair reads the extra $0$ as a multiset statement and cites both lemmas.

## 2. The sums

`tF R` is $\sum_a\sum_bK_{ab}^2$. `Kmat_diag` gives $K_{aa}=R_{aaaa}=0$, so this is $\sum_{a\ne b}K_{ab}^2$, the record's $t_F$. `sum_dphi_sq` is $\sum_{a<b}K_{ab}^2=t_F/2$, from `sum_pairs` with $K$ hollow and symmetric (`Kmat_symm`). `sum_d_sq` sums the diagonal of `Bw` over `S2Idx n`: the $\varphi$-block gives $t_F/2$ and the $\psi$-block gives $\sum_k\kappa_k^2=\mathop{\mathrm{tr}}K^2=t_F$ by `trace_pow_eq_sum_eigenvalues`, so the total is $\tfrac32t_F$. The record reaches the same $\mathop{\mathrm{tr}}K^2$ over $\mathbf1^\perp$ through step (0). The Lean sum over $\mathbb R^n$ needs no Ricci-flatness, and the two agree at Ricci-flat $R$ since the extra entry is $0$.

## 3. Rigidity

Elaborated signature: for $R$ with `IsLinOp`, `hskew`, `hsym`, first Bianchi at every index, `normSq R = 1` and `tF R = 2`, `SameSorted` holds for (`Mpair` eigenvalues, $(K_{ab})_{a<b}$) and for (`Bw (wK R) (ringW R)` eigenvalues, its diagonal). `SameSorted x y` is $\exists\sigma\in\mathrm{Perm},\ y_i=x_{\sigma i}$, equality of multisets, the record's "as multisets" and its $x^\downarrow=y^\downarrow$.

The proof is the record's (4): `L54_mu_majorizes` and `L54_omega_majorizes` give majorization, `sum_mu_sq` with `hunit` and `sum_dphi_sq` with `ht` give $\sum\mu^2=1=\sum d_\varphi^2$, `sum_omega_w_sq` (first Bianchi) with `hunit` and `sum_d_sq` with `ht` give $\sum\omega^2=3=\sum d^2$, and `sameSorted_of_majorizes_of_normSq_eq` closes both.

**Hypotheses.** The record assumes $W\in\mathrm{Weyl}_n$, $n\ge4$, $\lVert M\rVert_F=1$, $t_F=2$. A Weyl tensor is an algebraic curvature operator, so linearity, bivector preservation, self-adjointness and first Bianchi hold, and `normSq R` $=\sum\mu^2=\lVert M\rVert_F^2$ (`sum_mu_sq`). Ricci-flatness and $n\ge4$ are not assumed. The theorem is true without them as a statement about $\mathring W$ on $S^2$ and $K$ on $\mathbb R^n$, and at Ricci-flat $R$ the second conjunct is $\omega\uplus\{0\}=d\uplus\{0\}$ as multisets, which cancels to the record's $\omega=d$ (§1).
