# Review r357, Gate 2 on post F44 and the $T$-weighted contraction of Jacobi

**P1, the second-order criterion on single-character directions is blind to $R$: REFUTED as stated, CONFIRMED on the operator side.** $\langle X,L_WX\rangle$ is indeed a function of $\operatorname{diag}(M)$ alone; the criterion built from it is not, because $\kappa=\theta/2$ for the record's own [Lemma L24](../findings/second-order.md) and $\theta$ depends on $R$, and because the admissibility hypothesis $X\perp W$ reads $\langle X,R\rangle=0$ on a nontrivial character and fails. The post's "cannot detect the off-diagonal part $R$ at all" contradicts its own next paragraph. And the blindness is per-frame only: $\{\operatorname{diag}(M_F):F\in O(n)\}$ is the whole sectional-curvature function, which determines $W$.

**P2, the Killing collapse $\sum_{ab}c_{abg}A_aA_b=(n-2)A_g$: CONFIRMED.** All three steps and the constant, re-derived independently before reading the record and screened exactly at $n=5,6$.

**P3, the recorded contraction is vacuous: CONFIRMED, with its mechanism misattributed.** The left-hand side is $\sum_{abg}T_{abg}(W^\#)_{gg}=2(n-2)\operatorname{tr}(W^\#)$ as the brief states. The vacuity comes from the $g$-independence of the Killing marginal, relation (A) of [bounds.md](../findings/bounds.md), and what the contraction returns is $\operatorname{tr}(W^\#)=-\lVert W\rVert^2$, the $\lambda$-contraction of relation (B), which holds at **every** Weyl tensor with no criticality. So it is an artefact of the particular unweighted contraction, and the four sphere-product checks had no discriminating power: the identity cannot fail anywhere.

**P4, "any contraction pairing each $c$ with itself over both bracket slots loses all of Jacobi's content": REFUTED.** The escaping contractions are on the record already and are the backbone of [bounds.md](../findings/bounds.md): relations (B) and (C) are that very pairing, weighted by $\lambda_\beta$ and by $\lambda_\beta\lambda_\gamma$, and they return $-2W$ and $2W^\#$ rather than a multiple of the identity. The stated reason is false, squaring forces the Killing form only under an **ad-invariant**, i.e. constant, weight, and the post's own displayed escape terms pair over the *same* slots, contradicting its "pair across different index slots". The conclusion survives as **PLAUSIBLE** on a different mechanism, given below.

**P5, the convention trap: CONFIRMED, and no recorded result depends on the wrong one.** $\mathrm{ad}$ is a homomorphism for $(A_a)_{mn}=c_{anm}$ and an anti-homomorphism for $(A_a)_{mn}=c_{amn}$; [Q_full](../scripts/lib/adversarial_check1.py) uses the second and is unaffected because $W^\#$ is quadratic in $A$. One citation defect: the sentence is attributed to [conventions.md](../findings/conventions.md), which does not contain it.

## P2, the Killing collapse, step by step

**Step 1, the half-bracket.** $c_{abg}$ is antisymmetric in $(a,b)$, so $\sum_{ab}c_{abg}A_aA_b=\tfrac12\sum_{ab}c_{abg}(A_aA_b-A_bA_a)=\tfrac12\sum_{ab}c_{abg}[A_a,A_b]$. This needs only the antisymmetry, and it survives any weight $x_{ab}$ that is **symmetric** in $(a,b)$, the fact P4 turns on.

**Step 2, Jacobi.** $[A_a,A_b]=\mathrm{ad}([v_a,v_b])=\sum_dc_{abd}A_d$, which is the homomorphism property of $\mathrm{ad}$, equivalent to Jacobi, and holds in any representation.

**Step 3, the constant.** $\sum_{ab}c_{abg}c_{abd}=-\langle v_g,(\sum_aA_a^2)v_d\rangle$ by total antisymmetry and skewness of $A_a$; $\sum_aA_a^2$ is the Casimir of the adjoint in an orthonormal basis, so it is $\mu\,\mathrm{Id}$ with $\mu\dim=\operatorname{tr}\sum_aA_a^2=\sum_aK(v_a,v_a)$. With $K(X,Y)=(n-2)\operatorname{tr}(XY)$ and $\langle X,Y\rangle=-\tfrac12\operatorname{tr}(XY)$ ([conventions.md](../findings/conventions.md)) this is $K=-2(n-2)\langle\cdot,\cdot\rangle$, so $\mu=-2(n-2)$ and $\sum_{ab}c_{abg}c_{abd}=2(n-2)\delta_{gd}$. Together: $(n-2)A_g$. *Cross-checks:* the constant is relation (A) of [bounds.md](../findings/bounds.md) read at $g=d$, it is the pair-cap marginal of [conventions.md](../findings/conventions.md), and at $n=3$ it is $\sum_{ab}\varepsilon_{abg}\varepsilon_{abd}=2\delta_{gd}$ with $2(n-2)=2$.

*Screen:* $\lVert\sum_{ab}c_{abg}c_{abd}-2(n-2)\delta_{gd}\rVert_\infty=0$ and $\lVert\sum_{ab}c_{abg}A_aA_b-(n-2)A_g\rVert_\infty=0$ at $n=5,6$ in the homomorphism convention.

## P4, the counterexample, and what the mechanism should say

**The configuration P4 declares content-free is the configuration the record's row calculus is made of.** Write the pairing with a free slot on each factor and both other slots contracted, which is what "pairs each $c$ with itself over both bracket slots" means:

$$\Sigma_{gd}[x]\;=\;\sum_{a,b}x_{ab}\,c_{abg}\,c_{abd},\qquad x\ \text{symmetric in }(a,b).$$

At $x\equiv1$ this is $2(n-2)\delta_{gd}$, the Killing form, P4's case. At $x_{ab}=\lambda_a$ it is $-2\lambda_g\delta_{gd}$, the polarised form of relation (B), which [r331](REVIEW_r331.md) proves from Ricci-flatness and Bianchi. At $x_{ab}=\lambda_a\lambda_b$ it is $2(W^\#)_{gd}$, the polarised form of relation (C), which at a critical point is $2\lambda_g(\theta-\lambda_g)\delta_{gd}$. **Two of the three are not multiples of the identity, and both are informative**: (B) is the vector identity $R\lambda=-2\lambda$ whose $N-1$ non-$\lambda$ rows cut the transportation LP by $18\%$ at $n=6$ ([bounds-relaxations.md](../findings/bounds-relaxations.md), fence post F15), and (C) is the criticality data itself. So the quantifier "any contraction" is false, and the stated reason ("squaring forces the Killing form, which is a multiple of the identity"), is false wherever the weight is not constant.

**The Jacobi-derived identity itself carries a weight, by the same two steps.** Since $\lambda_a\lambda_b$ is symmetric in $(a,b)$, step 1 goes through unchanged and step 2 is untouched, so

$$\sum_{a,b}\lambda_a\lambda_b\,c_{abg}A_aA_b\;=\;\tfrac12\sum_d\Big(\sum_{ab}\lambda_a\lambda_bc_{abg}c_{abd}\Big)A_d\;=\;\sum_d(W^\#)_{gd}A_d\;\overset{\text{crit.}}{=}\;(\theta\lambda_g-\lambda_g^2)\,A_g,$$

the last equality in $W$'s eigenframe. This is P2 with one weight inserted, it is a genuine consequence of Jacobi, and its right-hand side is $W^\#$ (the object the whole problem is about) rather than a multiple of $A_g$ with a constant coefficient. *Screen, at $n=5,6$ on random diagonal $W$:* the weighted collapse matches $2W^\#$ to $4.4\times10^{-16}$ with diagonal spread $4.62$ and $5.77$ (so it is manifestly not a multiple of the identity), and the weighted operator identity has residual $2.2\times10^{-16}$.

**A second defect, in the words.** The post's escape terms are displayed as $\sum_{d\ne d'}c_{abd}c_{dce}c_{abd'}c_{d'ce}$, in which $c_{abd}$ and $c_{abd'}$ pair over the **same** two slots $a,b$ and differ only in the third index. The operative distinction is therefore *same index triple* versus *different index triple*, not *same slots* versus *different slots*, and the sentence as written says the opposite of what the formula shows.

**What the mechanism should say, and it still supports the conclusion.** Two repairs, both available:

- *Why the unweighted case collapses.* $\Sigma_{gd}[x]$ is $O(n)$-equivariant in $x$, so an $\mathrm{ad}$-invariant weight gives an intertwiner of the adjoint with itself, which Schur makes a multiple of the identity, and the only invariant weight expressible in $T$ alone is the constant one. **That is why the escape has to be $W$-weighted**, and it is exactly where relations (B) and (C) come from. *This has an $n\ge5$ hypothesis, the adjoint of $\mathfrak{so}(4)$ being reducible, which the record already flags in [bounds-relaxations.md](../findings/bounds-relaxations.md); the unweighted constant itself is a direct computation and needs no such hypothesis.*
- *Why no same-triple pairing constrains anything.* $T$-expressibility forces the two $c$'s of a monomial to carry the identical triple, so $\Sigma_{gd}[x]$ at $g=d$ is a **marginal of $T$** and is determined by $(T,\lambda)$ by definition. The relation it produces is therefore a definition rather than a constraint, whether the weight is constant or not, which is the true reason the recorded contraction returned an identity, and it covers the weighted cases the stated mechanism does not.

**What stays open, and it is where the record already puts it.** The case the repaired argument does not close is a Jacobi $c$ matched by a $c$ supplied by the weight: there the diagonal $e=e'$ part is $T$-expressible while the $e\ne e'$ part is a product of distinct $c$'s, and the identity relates the two. Nothing here shows those cross terms cancel, and nothing here shows they cannot. So **I15's question is not settled by post F44's argument**; what settles the linear case is [r331](REVIEW_r331.md)'s completeness theorem and the sign-realizability obstruction of [DERIVE-t7](DERIVE-t7.md), both of which stand independently.

## P3, the contraction, its left-hand side and the reason it is empty

**The left-hand side is as the brief describes.** Relation (A) gives $\sum_{ab}T_{abg}=2(n-2)$ for every $g$, so $\sum_{abg}T_{abg}(W^\#)_{gg}=2(n-2)\operatorname{tr}(W^\#)$. Because the weight does not depend on $g$, the weighted sum of the per-index criticality relations is $2(n-2)$ times their **unweighted** sum, and the unweighted sum is the trace of $Q(W)=\theta W$: $\operatorname{tr}(W^2)+\operatorname{tr}(W^\#)=\theta\operatorname{tr}W=0$.

**And that trace is not a consequence of criticality.** $\operatorname{tr}(W^\#)=\tfrac12\sum_{k\beta\gamma}T_{k\beta\gamma}\lambda_\beta\lambda_\gamma=\tfrac12\sum_\gamma\lambda_\gamma\big(\sum_{k\beta}\lambda_\beta T_{\gamma k\beta}\big)=-\sum_\gamma\lambda_\gamma^2=-\lVert W\rVert^2$ by relation (B) and the total symmetry of $T$, at every Weyl tensor. So the contraction returns a relation that holds off the critical set as well, and **the numerical check at four sphere products could not have failed**, it tested an identity valid at every unit-norm Weyl tensor. The vacuity is an artefact of the unweighted contraction, not of the sphere products and not of Jacobi: the same pairing at weights $\lambda_\beta$ and $\lambda_\beta\lambda_\gamma$ returns (B) and (C).

## P1, where $R$ enters, and the quantifier over frames

**The operator side of the post is right.** [Lemma L123](../findings/second-order.md)'s proof checks: $\varphi_\alpha\odot\varphi_\beta$ carries $\chi_{\alpha\triangle\beta}$ and $\alpha\triangle\beta=\emptyset$ iff $\alpha=\beta$, so the trivial character space is exactly the diagonal and a hollow $R$ has no trivial part; $\Gamma$ is abelian and acts on each character space by a scalar; $\circ$ is $O(n)$-equivariant, hence $\Gamma$-equivariant, so $R_\chi\circ X$ sits in $\chi\chi_X$; the trace pairing is $\Gamma$-invariant, so $\chi\chi_X=\chi_X$ forces $\chi=1$. Distinct even subsets give distinct characters of the **full** sign group, so the $n=4,6,8$ fusion of [Lemma L120](../findings/second-order.md) does not bite. Hence $\langle X,L_WX\rangle=\langle X,L_{\operatorname{diag}M}X\rangle$ on a single character.

**But the criterion is not the operator.** The post quantifies over criteria "of the form $\langle X,L_WX\rangle\le\kappa$", and the record's own instance is not of that form: [Lemma L24](../findings/second-order.md) reads $\langle X,L_eX\rangle\le\tfrac12$ with $e=W/\theta$, i.e. $\langle X,L_{\operatorname{diag}M}X\rangle\le\theta/2$, and $\theta=P(W)$ depends on $R$. A second channel is the admissibility: $\langle X,W\rangle=\langle X,R\rangle$ for $X$ in a nontrivial character, so $X\perp W$ is a condition **on $R$**, and when it fails the corrected bound is $\tfrac12(1+c^2)$ with $c=\langle X,R\rangle$, the record's own I12 correction, cited by the post three lines below the box it contradicts. So the criterion is a condition on the pair $(\operatorname{diag}M,\langle X,R\rangle)$ with an $R$-dependent constant, and "a criterion on $\operatorname{diag}(M)$ alone … cannot detect $R$ at all" is false as written.

**The quantifier over frames is the correction that matters.** Per frame the blindness is real. Across frames it is not: $\operatorname{diag}(M_F)_{ab}=W_{abab}$ is the sectional curvature of a coordinate $2$-plane of $F$, so $\{\operatorname{diag}(M_F):F\in O(n)\}$ is the sectional-curvature function on the whole Grassmannian, which determines an algebraic curvature tensor completely by polarisation under first Bianchi. **A family of single-character criteria taken over all frames is therefore not blind to $R$**, and the post's "what a successor must clear" (mixed characters, or a quantity that is not a Rayleigh quotient), omits a third option that its own next paragraph half-states when it says the four-index certificate is a condition on $M$ *in every frame*. The closing sentence "they never see $R$ because they cannot" understates [Lemma L121](../findings/second-order.md) and [Lemma L122](../findings/second-order.md) for the same reason: read over all frames, and with the projection defect restored, the $4$-set family is a constraint on all of $W$.

**Scope drawn correctly elsewhere.** The post's exclusions (mixed-character directions, third-order fusion, non-Rayleigh tests), are stated accurately, and its "the post is about a method, not about the problem" is right.

## P5, the convention, and one citation defect

$(A_a)_{mn}=\langle v_m,[v_a,v_n]\rangle=c_{anm}$ is the matrix of $\mathrm{ad}(v_a)$, so the homomorphism holds for that order; the other order is its negative, giving $[A'_a,A'_b]=-\sum_dc_{abd}A'_d$ and turning P2 into $-(n-2)A'_g$. *Screen at $n=5,6$:* $\lVert\sum_{ab}c_{abg}A'_aA'_b+(n-2)A'_g\rVert_\infty=0$, against $6$ and $8$ for the unflipped sign, the recorded "failed by a factor" is exactly this.

**Nothing recorded depends on the wrong one.** $(W^\#)_{ab}=-\tfrac12\operatorname{tr}(A_aWA_bW)$ is quadratic in $A$, so $A\mapsto-A$ leaves $Q$, $P$, $\theta$ and every $T_{abg}$ invariant; [Q_full](../scripts/lib/adversarial_check1.py) uses $(A_a)_{bg}=c_{abg}$, the anti-homomorphism order, and is correct. The trap is confined to arguments that use the bracket, which is P2 and its weighted analogue.

**The citation is wrong.** [bounds-relaxations.md](../findings/bounds-relaxations.md) attributes "$(A_a)_{bg}=c_{abg}$ with $A_a$ skew" to [conventions.md](../findings/conventions.md); that file carries no such line, the convention lives in the docstring of [Q_full](../scripts/lib/adversarial_check1.py). Under the record's own rule that conventions are quoted rather than restated, the line should either be added to [conventions.md](../findings/conventions.md) or cited to the implementation.
