## A. Statement fidelity

- **`schur`.** $\sum_i\sum_j c_{ij}\,e_i\langle e_j,\cdot\rangle$ with $c_{ij}=\varphi(\lambda_i,\lambda_j)\langle e_i,He_j\rangle$. Applied to $e_l$ it gives $\sum_i c_{il}e_i$, so the $(k,l)$ entry is $c_{kl}$, which `entry_schur` states. Correct.
- **`blkPart`.** $\varphi=[a=b]$ keeps exactly the entries with $\lambda_i=\lambda_j$, including pairs of distinct basis vectors inside one repeated eigenvalue, which is $\sum_\lambda P_\lambda HP_\lambda$ written in the eigenbasis. The file takes this entrywise form as the definition and proves no lemma naming the projections $P_\lambda$, which is a presentation choice and costs nothing downstream.
- **`rotGen` and the sign.** $\varphi(a,b)=[a\ne b]/(b-a)$, so the $(i,j)$ entry is $\langle e_i,He_j\rangle/(\lambda_j-\lambda_i)$, which is $P_{\lambda_i}HP_{\lambda_j}/(\lambda_j-\lambda_i)$, the record's $P_\lambda HP_\mu/(\mu-\lambda)$. `schur_commutator` gives $Ku-uK$ the multiplier $(b-a)\varphi(a,b)$, which is $1$ off the blocks and $0$ on them, hence $H-H_{\mathrm{blk}}$.
- **Hand check.** $u=\operatorname{diag}(1,-1)$, $e_1,e_2$ standard, $H=\begin{pmatrix}p&h\\h&q\end{pmatrix}$. Then $K_{12}=h/(-1-1)=-h/2$ and $K_{21}=h/(1-(-1))=h/2$, so $K=\begin{pmatrix}0&-h/2\\h/2&0\end{pmatrix}$, skew. $Ku=\begin{pmatrix}0&h/2\\h/2&0\end{pmatrix}$, $uK=\begin{pmatrix}0&-h/2\\-h/2&0\end{pmatrix}$, so $Ku-uK=\begin{pmatrix}0&h\\h&0\end{pmatrix}=H-\operatorname{diag}(p,q)=H-H_{\mathrm{blk}}$. Passes, with the convention $[K,u]=Ku-uK$ of `rotGen_commutator`.
- **`rotGen_commutator_specFn`.** Multiplier $(g(b)-g(a))\,\mathrm{rot}\varphi(a,b)$, so the $(i,j)$ entry is $\frac{g(\lambda_j)-g(\lambda_i)}{\lambda_j-\lambda_i}\langle e_i,He_j\rangle$ for $\lambda_i\ne\lambda_j$ and $0$ otherwise. This is the Daleckii–Krein first divided difference $g^{[1]}(\lambda_i,\lambda_j)$ restricted to the off-block entries, as the docstring says. Consistent with milestone 4's use: $u+tH=UMU^*$ gives $g(u+tH)\approx(1+tK)(g(u)+tG_1)(1-tK)=g(u)+t([K,g(u)]+G_1)$, the same sign.
- **`rotGen_isSkew`, `blkPart_isSymmetric`, `blkPart_comm_specFn`.** Skewness and symmetry follow from the (anti)symmetry of the multiplier and of $H$ entrywise, and commutation from $[a=b]f(b)=f(a)[a=b]$. Correct. Note that `rotGen_isSkew` needs `H.IsSymmetric` while `rotGen_commutator` does not, which matches the plan's wording.
- **`conjPath` and `star_rot`.** `conjPath K u H t = rot K (-t) * (u + t • H) * rot K t`. `star_rot` gives `star (rot K t) = rot K (-t)` under `star K = -K`, so the path is $U(t)^*(u+tH)U(t)$ for such $K$. Correct, and `rot_mul_rot_neg` with `rot_neg_mul_rot` then give $U^*U=UU^*=1$.
- **`hasDerivAt_conjPath`.** Product rule at $0$ with $U(0)=1$: $-Ku+H+uK=H-(Ku-uK)$. The theorem is stated for arbitrary `K u H : E →L[ℝ] E` with no skewness or relation between them, and is correct at that generality.
- **The $o(t)$ reading.** `HasDerivAt f f' 0` is, by definition, $f(t)=f(0)+tf'+o(t)$ in the norm of `E →L[ℝ] E`, and $f(0)=u$. So $M(t)=u+t(H-[K,u])+o(t)$ is a genuine consequence. It is $o(t)$ and not $O(t^2)$, see G2.
- **G1, the type mismatch.** `rotGen hn hu H`, `blkPart hn hu H` and `u` are `E →ₗ[ℝ] E`. `rot`, `conjPath` and `star_rot` take `E →L[ℝ] E`, where `*` is composition and `star` is `ContinuousLinearMap.adjoint`. The chain "$M'(0)=H-[K,u]=H_{\mathrm{blk}}$" needs (i) `rotGen_commutator` pushed through `LinearMap.toContinuousLinearMap`, and "$U(t)$ orthogonal" needs (ii) `star (toContinuousLinearMap (rotGen hn hu H)) = -toContinuousLinearMap (rotGen hn hu H)` from `rotGen_isSkew` via `ContinuousLinearMap.eq_adjoint_iff`. A grep finds neither `rotGen` nor `conjPath` in any other file, so neither bridge exists in the repository. **Screen, not evidence**: both bridges were typed in a scratch file and compiled, each in under ten lines, so the gap is routine to close. It is still a claim marked **Proved** in the record with no theorem behind it.
