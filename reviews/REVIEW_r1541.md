## A. Fidelity

- **A-1. `IsCritDet` and `det_expand`** ([L124Crit.lean:25–31](../lean/HamiltonsCubic/Results/L124Crit.lean), [:48–50](../lean/HamiltonsCubic/Results/L124Crit.lean)). Confirmed with gap. By hand: for a 3×3 V Cayley–Hamilton gives adj V = V² − (tr V)V + ½((tr V)² − tr V²)I, which at tr V = 0 is V² − ½(tr V²)I, so the derivative of det at V along X is tr(adj V · X) = tr(V²X) − ½(tr V²)(tr X) = tr(V²X) at trace-free X. `det_expand`'s linear coefficient says exactly this, and its quadratic coefficient tr(X²V) is the same identity with the roles swapped. On the unit sphere of trace-free symmetric matrices with the Frobenius pairing ⟨V, X⟩ = tr(VX), the tangent space at V is {X symmetric, tr X = 0, tr(VX) = 0}, and along the curve (V + tX)/‖V + tX‖ the derivative of det is tr(V²X) − 3 det V · tr(VX) = tr(V²X), so `IsCritDet` is the right definition. It is the right notion for the record: θ(W_V) = 9 det V/(tr V²)^{3/2} is homogeneous of degree 0 in V (`half_flat_values`), and V ↦ `WMat n V` is linear and injective, so a critical point of θ on the family is a ray whose unit representative is critical for det on the sphere. **Gap**: `crit_iff` never uses `det_expand`, and nothing typed connects `IsCritDet` to θ on the family. The definition is justified in the docstring, and the two reduction steps (the derivative of det on the sphere is the tangential tr(V²X), and θ's critical rays on `WMat n V` are det's critical points on the unit sphere) are modelling readings. The L124 row at [lean/INDEX.md:471](../lean/INDEX.md) lists only the general four-set under *Readings not typed*.
- **A-2. `crit_iff`** ([:57–118](../lean/HamiltonsCubic/Results/L124Crit.lean)). Confirmed, walked by hand. Set b = tr V³ and Y = V² − I/3 − bV. Y is symmetric, tr Y = 1 − 1 − 0 = 0, and tr(VY) = b − 0 − b = 0, so Y is a tangent. `trace_pow_four` gives tr V⁴ = (tr V²)²/2 = 1/2 (for x + y + z = 0, Σx⁴ = (Σx²)²/2), so tr(V²Y) = 1/2 − 1/3 − b² = 1/6 − b². Since tr Y = tr(VY) = 0, tr(Y²) = tr(V²Y), and `eq_zero_of_trace_sq` makes tr(Y²) = 0 force Y = 0. So b² = 1/6 ⟺ Y = 0 ⟺ V² = I/3 + bV. Critical implies b² = 1/6 by testing at X = Y. Conversely V² = I/3 + bV gives tr(V²X) = (tr X)/3 + b·tr(VX) = 0 on every tangent. For the record's quantities, Newton at trace zero gives e₃ = p₃/3, so tr V³ = 3 det V (L124Det's `trace_cube_eq_three_det`), and at unit norm θ = 9 det V = 3 tr V³, so (tr V³)² = 1/6 is θ² = 9/6 = 3/2, the equality value of `theta_sq_le_WMat`. V² = I/3 + bV makes the minimal polynomial of degree at most two, so V has at most two distinct eigenvalues, and at trace zero and unit norm exactly the spectrum ±(2, −1, −1)/√6.
