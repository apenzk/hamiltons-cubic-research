## The item

**I100.** *Theorem to aim at: a lower bound on $t=\max_Ft_F$, the diagonal mass in the best frame, at every global maximiser, from the joint constraint of H4 along great circles together with the first-order optimality of the $t$-maximising frame.* It **adds** the node that feeds L217 with a localisation. Grade $2\times2\times1\times1=4$.

**The chain it would need, as nodes.** (a) $t\ge1-\varepsilon_n$ at every maximiser. (b) A quantitative form of L57: a unit diagonal $d$ with $P(d)\ge\Theta_n-\delta$ lies within angle $\rho(\delta)$ of the balanced orbit. **The record carries no such stability estimate** (grep of [problem.md](../findings/problem.md) and [diagonal-half.md](../findings/diagonal-half.md) for stability and quantitative forms finds none). (c) Then $W^\ast$ lies within $\rho+\arcsin\sqrt{\varepsilon_n}$ of the orbit, and L217 closes if that is below $\arcsin\frac3{n-2}$. *So the far region splits into (a) and (b), and (b) is a finite-dimensional statement about the diagonal locus, where the record is strongest.*

## The identities in hand

**[L214](../findings/second-order.md), the frame split, Gate 2 passed.** In ANY frame, $W=D+N$ with $D$ its coordinate-diagonal part, **$t=\lVert D\rVert^2\in[0,1]$** (this block's $t$; [conventions.md](../findings/conventions.md)'s $t_F=\lVert K(F)\rVert_F^2\le2$ is twice it), $a=\langle L_DN,N\rangle$:
$$\theta t=P(D)+a,\qquad\theta(1-t)=2a+P(N),$$
and at a global maximiser $a\ge\tfrac\theta2t(1-t)$, equivalently $P(D)\le\tfrac\theta2t(1+t)$. *Input: $\langle D\circ D,N\rangle=0$, from L120's block structure at $\Gamma$-fixed $D$.*

**The maximising frame** ([second-order.md](../findings/second-order.md), "the gauge half has a repair"). *At $F$ maximising $t_F$, $t'(0)=-2\langle\mathrm{ad}_\xi W,D\rangle=0$ for every $\xi\in\mathfrak{so}(n)$, so $D\perp T_W\mathcal O$ and $N\perp T_W\mathcal O$.* **This is the only frame-optimality condition on file.** The second-order condition of that maximisation is untried.

**The great-circle form of H4** ([sweep](2026-09-14-LIT-step8-sweep.md), batch 2). For unit $X\perp W^\ast$ and every angle $s$: $m\cos^3s+3\cos s\sin^2s\,\alpha_X+\sin^3s\,\gamma_X\le m$, with $\alpha_X=\langle L_{W^\ast}X,X\rangle$ and $\gamma_X=P(X)$. It gives $\gamma_X\le m\,h(\alpha_X/m)$ with $h(\tfrac12)=0$. *On the plane $\mathrm{span}\{D,N\}$ it reduces by L214 to a one-parameter condition that a hand screen found satisfiable with $t<1$ and $\theta>\Theta_n$, so that plane alone does not force diagonality.*

**Others.** [L24](../findings/second-order.md): $L_W\le\tfrac\theta2$ on $W^\perp$ at a maximiser. At a local maximum $P$ vanishes on $\ker\mathrm{Hess}$ ([second-order.md](../findings/second-order.md), the result derived en route near its line 953). [L120](../findings/second-order.md) to [L122](../findings/second-order.md): $L_D$ block-diagonal, four-set spectrum $\pm\tfrac12\sqrt E$, pair block $\tfrac12[\operatorname{diag}(d)-\kappa+S]$.
