# Route R-A on [I73](../plan.md): sub-step (i), the identities on $W^\#$ and what they reduce it to

*Chosen at [step 9](2026-09-23-LIT-step9-sweep.md). Sub-step (i) is a grep of the record for every identity pairing $W^\#$ against a direction.*

## The reformulation is TRANSPARENT, not deep, and this says why

**Criticality gives $W^\#$ in closed form without any work.** $Q(W)=W^2+W^\#=\theta W$, so

$$W^\#=\theta W-W^2 .$$

**Hence $W^\#\in\mathop{\mathrm{span}}\{W,\mathrm{Id}\}\iff W^2\in\mathop{\mathrm{span}}\{W,\mathrm{Id}\}\iff$ the minimal polynomial has degree $2$.** *The three statements are one, and the sweep's framing of R-A as a reformulation rather than a theorem is confirmed from the record rather than asserted.* **What R-A buys is NOT a weaker statement. It is a different set of tools**, and sub-step (i) is about whether that set is non-empty.

## The identity that makes it computable

[second-order.md](../findings/second-order.md) line 88, from [L220](../findings/second-order.md)'s step 1:

$$\langle R^\#X,Y\rangle=-\tfrac12\mathop{\mathrm{Tr}}(\mathop{\mathrm{ad}}_XR\,\mathop{\mathrm{ad}}_YR),$$

and with $\Phi(A;B,C):=\sum_e\mathop{\mathrm{Tr}}(\mathop{\mathrm{ad}}_e\,B\,\mathop{\mathrm{ad}}_{Ae}\,C)$,

$$\langle S,R^\#\rangle_{\mathrm{op}}=-\tfrac12\Phi(S;R,R)\quad\text{for EVERY }S .$$

**So R-A reduces to a statement about the ad-trace form**:

$$\boxed{\ \Phi(S;W,W)=0\ \text{ for every }S\perp\mathop{\mathrm{span}}\{W,\mathrm{Id}\}\ }$$

*and equivalently, pairing criticality against $S\perp W$, $\langle S,W^2\rangle=\tfrac12\Phi(S;W,W)$, so the ad-form and $W^2$ carry the same information on $W^\perp$.*

## Why this is the right handle

**The record holds machinery for $\Phi$ and none for a spectrum.** *[L220](../findings/second-order.md) step 3 proves $\Phi$ fully symmetric at self-adjoint arguments, which is what makes $T$ symmetric; `trace_comm_sq` is typed in [L199Statement.lean](../lean/HamiltonsCubic/Results/L199Statement.lean) giving $\lVert[A,R]\rVert^2=2\mathop{\mathrm{tr}}(ARAR)-2\mathop{\mathrm{tr}}(A^2R^2)$ at skew $A$ and symmetric $R$, at every size and touching no entry.* **A spectrum has no such handle, which is the whole content of the reformulation.**

## What sub-step (ii) must now decide, stated exactly

**Does second-order optimality force $\Phi(S;W,W)=0$ on $\mathop{\mathrm{span}}\{W,\mathrm{Id}\}^\perp$?**

*The available second-order input is [L24](../findings/second-order.md): $\langle L_WX,X\rangle\le\tfrac\theta2\lVert X\rVert^2$ for $X\perp W$, which pairs $W$ against $X$ TWICE. The span condition pairs $W$ twice against $S$ ONCE, through $\Phi(S;W,W)$.* **These are different contractions of the same three-tensor**, and whether the first bounds the second is the question, not an assumption.

**A negative answer is informative and is the sweep's own falsifier**: if L24 does not reach $\Phi$, the smallest $S$ it fails to control is the object to look at, and whether such an $S$ is realisable at a critical point above $\sqrt{3/2}$ decides the route.

## Sub-step (ii): the span condition is ONE SCALAR EQUATION in the moments

**Write $W^2$ against the span it is asked to lie in.** With $W$ traceless of unit norm on an $N$-dimensional space, put

$$W^2=aW+\beta\,\mathrm{Id}+E,\qquad E\perp\mathop{\mathrm{span}}\{W,\mathrm{Id}\}.$$

*Tracelessness gives $\beta=\mathop{\mathrm{Tr}}(W^2)/N=1/N$; orthogonality to $W$ gives $a=\langle W^2,W\rangle=\mathop{\mathrm{Tr}}(W^3)$, which is the record's own $a$ at [second-order.md](../findings/second-order.md) line 146.* **Hence**

$$\boxed{\ \lVert E\rVert^2=\mathop{\mathrm{Tr}}(W^4)-\mathop{\mathrm{Tr}}(W^3)^2-\tfrac1N\ \ge\ 0,\ \text{ and }[I73](../plan.md)\text{ asks it to VANISH.}\ }$$

**So the span condition, hence the two-value spectrum, is a SINGLE SCALAR EQUATION between the third and fourth moments**, $\mathop{\mathrm{Tr}}(W^4)=\mathop{\mathrm{Tr}}(W^3)^2+\tfrac1N$.

**CRITICALITY IS NOT NEEDED FOR THE EQUIVALENCE.** *It is linear algebra about a traceless unit-norm symmetric operator: $E=0$ iff the minimal polynomial has degree $\le2$ iff the spectrum takes two values. Criticality enters only to connect $W^\#$ to $W^2$, which is sub-step (i).*

**Checked both ways.** *$\lVert E\rVert^2$ against $\mathop{\mathrm{Tr}}(W^4)-a^2-1/N$ agrees to machine zero on random spectra at $N=6$ and $N=12$, on two-value spectra at $(N,r)=(15,6)$ and $(28,12)$ where both vanish, and on a three-value spectrum where both give $0.0444$.* **It is strictly positive off the two-value locus**, which is what makes it a target rather than a restatement.

**Screened, in BOTH directions this time.** *[Post F23](../findings/fence-posts.md) is the nearest thing on the record: no LINEAR relation among $\mathop{\mathrm{tr}}M^4$, $\mathop{\mathrm{tr}}\mathring W^4$, $1$, $\langle W^\#,W^\#\rangle$, $\langle W^\#,W^2\rangle$ at $n\ge6$, rank $5$ of $5$. That is about linear relations among degree-four invariants of a general $W$; this is a non-linear condition characterising a spectrum, and the two do not meet.* **The underlying inequality is elementary**, a sum of squares in the spectrum's moments, and is not claimed as new. *What is new is the link: [I73](../plan.md) now has a NONNEGATIVE QUANTITY to be shown zero at a maximiser rather than a qualitative statement about a spectrum.*

**What this changes for sub-step (ii)'s question.** *The question was whether second-order optimality forces $\Phi(S;W,W)=0$ on a subspace. It is now whether it forces one scalar, $\mathop{\mathrm{Tr}}(W^4)-\mathop{\mathrm{Tr}}(W^3)^2-1/N$, to vanish.* **And the record's warning stands unchanged**: L24 is tight at $S^3\times S^3$ with excess $9$ and does not pin the structure there, so the third-order condition remains the place to look.
