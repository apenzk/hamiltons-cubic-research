### Reviewers 56 and 57: the diagonality split, and an enumeration that is not a search space

**They agree on all six verdicts.** Own implementations in [review_r56_*.py](scripts/) and [review_r57_core.py](scripts/review_r57_core.py) with six companions; both ran the repository's [review_r11_grading_slack.py](scripts/reviewers/r11/review_r11_grading_slack.py) and reconciled against it. Reports in [review_r56_report.md](agent_logs/review_r56_report.md) and [review_r57_report.md](agent_logs/review_r57_report.md).

**D1: the split is right and every number attached to it was the wrong statistic.** The figures $0.197$, $0.158$, $0.408$ are the **largest single off-diagonal entry** in the constructor's frame; the off-diagonal Frobenius masses are $0.837$, $0.866$, $0.707$, and those are already frame-minimal. The max-entry statistic even **reverses the ordering** ($\mathbb{CP}^5$ looks less non-diagonal than $\mathbb{CP}^4$ by entry and more by mass) and $0.408=1/\sqrt6$ contradicts [ATTEMPTS.md](ATTEMPTS.md)'s proof of the $1/\sqrt2$ floor for $\mathcal S^4(\alpha)$, which the record quotes for the same point elsewhere. The yes/no column survives, and both certified it properly rather than numerically: every eigenspace of $W_{\mathbb{CP}^2}$ has $\min\|\omega\wedge\omega\|=\tfrac12>0$, so the Plücker obstruction rules out any frame; the corresponding minima are $0.61$ and $0.63$ for $\mathbb{CP}^4$ and $\mathbb{CP}^5$. Both frame minimisers pass a positive control, driving a randomly rotated $S^5\times S^5$ from $0.98$ to $9.5\times10^{-6}$. **No script in the tree regenerated the table**, the commits carrying it touched only markdown.

**D2 CONFIRMED with a scope correction: the diagonal grant covers even $n\ge12$**, not all $n$.

**D4 REFUTED, by three counterexamples, one of them the record's own anchor.** Unitary isotropy is sufficient for non-diagonalisability and **never necessary**, and necessity is the direction the reduction used. Padded $W_{\mathbb{CP}^2}$, the $0.9909\,\theta_0$ row, is fixed by every single-coordinate reflection of the padding (residual $8.5\times10^{-16}$), each with odd $(-1)$-multiplicity, so its isotropy is not unitary, yet it is non-diagonalisable; the record's stated reason applies only in native dimension $4$. $\mathbb{CP}^m$ with $m$ odd fails too, complex conjugation lying in the isotropy with $(-1)$-multiplicity $m$. And **$W_{\mathbb{CP}^2}\oplus W_{S^4\times S^4}$ at $n=12$** is critical to $7\times10^{-16}$ with $S=0$ in both conventions, frame-minimal off-diagonal mass $0.483$, eight odd reflections in its isotropy, and $\theta=0.8367$ matching no enumerated $n=12$ point, a non-diagonal $S=0$ critical point **outside the enumeration**. The record's own "$\{S=0\}$ is closed under orthogonal direct sums", combined with the harmonic-square law, manufactures an infinite unlisted family of them: five verified at $n=12$, residuals $\le2.3\times10^{-16}$, $S$ structurally $0$ exactly, sitting at $0.65$–$0.70\,\theta_0$. So values are unharmed but **"the search space is already enumerated" is false**.

**D5 CONFIRMED in every row, and the omitted family agrees.** All $\theta$ values, ratios and slacks reproduce independently; the $\mathfrak{su}(m)$ Kähler excess is $5,9,14,20$ at $m=3,4,5,6$. The $r\ge8$ hyperkähler family that the repository script omits was rebuilt by both as a **six-dimensional** closed algebra, matching Fox's $\mathbb B_r$: exactly three $\theta$ magnitudes at each of $r=8$ and $r=12$, all three with $S=0$ at $r=8$ ($\theta/\theta_0=0.9737$, $0.8607$, $0.8552$) and one of three at $r=12$, with maximum $0.7483\,\theta_0(12)$. Nothing anywhere exceeds $\theta_0$ for $n\ge12$, and Fox at $0.9909$ remains the closest.

**D6 REFUTED as the residual statement, and the record already contradicted it.** [findings/INDEX.md](../findings/INDEX.md)'s own "third class of ungraded $S=0$ point" section states that "block sum or padding" is not exhaustive, and Reviewer 26 recorded that the crux phrasing needs a third entry, the crux, written later, does not have one. Completeness of an enumeration is both harder than needed and already false, and an isotropy-indexed list can never reach the symmetry-free points that the open-gaps list names as untouched. **What the route actually needs is a value bound**: $\sup\{\theta:\text{critical},\ S=0,\ \text{non-diagonal}\}\le\sqrt{3/2}$, which closes every even $n\ge12$ on its own.

**Two things the split was hiding.** The diagonal grant is a **value** statement, so it does not supply openness at the diagonal $S=0$ points, and the $C_5$ corrector test is already on record as blind, so openness there is still owed. And $S=0$ is one grading condition of three, so Cartan's ceiling does not apply on $\{S=0\}$ at all, only on the subalgebra family. One quantified caveat on null results: 193 random-start critical points at $n=6$ span $\theta\in[0.007,0.426]$ with no $S=0$ among them, but the positive control shows the high-$\theta$ $S=0$ basin has radius about $0.05$–$0.1$ in 84 dimensions, so no random sweep is evidence there.
