# I124: ascent at every non-diagonal critical point above $\theta_0$

*Working note for [I124](../plan.md), route [R57](../routes.md), the user's decision of 2026-09-28. Nothing here is numbered. The statement is typed as `I124` in [I124Statement.lean](../lean/HamiltonsCubic/Results/I124Statement.lean).*

**The object.** $W$ is a unit Weyl tensor with $Q(W)=\theta W$, $T$ the fully symmetric trilinear form with $T(x,x,x)=P(x)$, $x\circ y$ the product with $\langle x\circ y,z\rangle=T(x,y,z)$, and $L_e=\theta^{-1}W\circ\cdot$. Along a unit $v\perp W$ the great circle $\cos t\,W+\sin t\,v$ has second derivative $6\big(T(W,v,v)-\tfrac\theta2\lvert v\rvert^2\big)$ at $t=0$ ([second-order.md](../findings/second-order.md), the geodesic computation under the isolation radius), so **$v$ is an ascent direction exactly when $T(W,v,v)>\tfrac\theta2\lvert v\rvert^2$**. Orbit tangents $X\cdot W$ meet this with equality at every critical point (`L24_sharp_vec`), so an ascent direction is off the orbit.

**The frame split.** Fix an orthonormal frame, let $D$ be the Weyl tensors coordinate-diagonal in it, and write $W=a\hat A+b\nu$ with $a\hat A=\pi_DW$, $\hat A\in D$ and $\nu\perp D$ unit, $a^2+b^2=1$. $W$ non-diagonal means $b>0$ in every frame. **$D$ is closed under $Q$**: $W^2$ of a diagonal operator is diagonal, and $(\varphi_{ab}\varphi_{ab}^{\mathsf T})^\#(\varphi_{cd}\varphi_{cd}^{\mathsf T})=\tfrac12[\varphi_{ab},\varphi_{cd}][\varphi_{ab},\varphi_{cd}]^{\mathsf T}$ is a multiple of $\psi\psi^{\mathsf T}$ for $\psi$ the wedge on the symmetric difference of the two index pairs, or zero. $L_{\hat A}=\hat A\circ\cdot$ below is unscaled, as in R54's row. By polarisation $D\circ D\subseteq D$, so $T(x,x',y)=0$ whenever $x,x'\in D$ and $y\perp D$.

## Batch 1: the direction toward the diagonal part is a strict descent above $\Theta_n$ (derived, in Lean, Gate 2 [r1916](REVIEW_r1916.md), [r1918](REVIEW_r1918.md): L214 in circle coordinates, with one sharpening)

**Statement.** *In any frame with $a,b>0$, if $p=P(\hat A)\le\theta$, then $P$ restricted to the great circle through $\hat A$ and $\nu$ has second derivative at most $-3\theta(1-a)^2/b^2<0$ at $W$. By [L57](../findings/problem.md) ($P(\hat A)\le\Theta_n$ on the diagonal locus) this holds at every critical point with $\theta\ge\Theta_n$, in every frame where $W$ has a diagonal part, so the direction from $W$ toward its diagonal part is never an ascent direction there.*

**Proof.** *$T(\hat A,\hat A,\nu)=0$ by the closure, so $P(\cos\varphi\,\hat A+\sin\varphi\,\nu)=p\cos^3\varphi+3r\cos\varphi\sin^2\varphi+q\sin^3\varphi$ with $r=T(\hat A,\nu,\nu)$, $q=P(\nu)$. Its second derivative at $(\cos\varphi,\sin\varphi)=(a,b)$ is $3E$, $E=p(2ab^2-a^3)+r(2a^3-7ab^2)+q(2a^2b-b^3)$. The components of $Q(W)=\theta W$ along $\hat A$ and $\nu$ are $\theta a=a^2p+b^2r$ and $\theta b=2abr+b^2q$, and with $a^2+b^2=1$ they give $b^2E=2ap-\theta(1+a^2)$, which is at most $2a\theta-\theta(1+a^2)=-\theta(1-a)^2$ when $p\le\theta$.* $\square$ **Lean**: `circle_second`, `circle_descent` in [I124Circle.lean](../lean/HamiltonsCubic/Results/I124Circle.lean). The closure, the restriction of $P$ to the circle and the second derivative of that polynomial enter as the definition of $E$ and the two component equations.

- **Where it sits on the record.** The circle is [R54](../routes.md)'s (phase 3a of the [2026-09-28 sweep](2026-09-28-LIT-step8-sweep.md), `circle_cubic` in [CircleEndpoint.lean](../lean/HamiltonsCubic/Results/CircleEndpoint.lean)), the component equations are [L214](../findings/second-order.md)'s two relations, the circle direction is L214's test vector $tN-(1-t)D$ normalised, and $b^2E\le0$ is L214's inequality $P(D)\le\tfrac\theta2t(1+t)$, `circle_second` restating `L214_frame` in scalar form. **The sharpening**: with $p\le\theta$, $2a\le1+a^2$ makes L214's inequality strict at every critical point above $\Theta_n$, maximal or not, so above $\Theta_n$ it carries no information ([r1918](REVIEW_r1918.md)).
- **What it closes.** The simplest construction, moving $W$ toward its diagonal part. In every frame the ascent direction must leave $\operatorname{span}\{W,\pi_DW\}$. The hypothesis actually used is $P(\hat A)\le\theta$, which a global maximiser satisfies whatever its value.
- **What it leaves: the second variation's block form.** $W^\perp=\mathbb Rw_c\oplus(D\ominus\hat A)\oplus(D^\perp\ominus\nu)$ with $w_c=a\nu-b\hat A$. The circle direction couples to the other two blocks by $T(W,w_c,x)=ab\langle\nu\circ\nu-\hat A\circ\hat A,x\rangle$ and $T(W,w_c,y)=(a^2-b^2)\langle\hat A\circ\nu,y\rangle+ab\langle\nu\circ\nu,y\rangle$, generically nonzero ([r1916](REVIEW_r1916.md), [r1918](REVIEW_r1918.md); batch 3 is an instance). On $v=x+y$, $x\in D\ominus\hat A$, $y\in D^\perp\ominus\nu$, the closure makes the form
$$T(W,v,v)-\tfrac\theta2\lvert v\rvert^2=a\langle\hat A\circ x,x\rangle+2b\langle\nu\circ x,y\rangle+a\langle\hat A\circ y,y\rangle+b\langle\nu\circ y,y\rangle-\tfrac\theta2\lvert v\rvert^2,$$
with no $\nu$ term on the diagonal block and no $\hat A$ term across the blocks. Two candidate families follow: **the diagonal block alone**, ascent iff $a$ times the top of $L_{\hat A}$ compressed to $D\ominus\hat A$ exceeds $\tfrac\theta2$; and **the orbit tangents of the diagonal part**, $X\cdot\hat A\in D^\perp$, which are orthogonal to $W$ in a frame maximising $\lVert\pi_DW\rVert$ since that frame's first-order condition is $\langle X\cdot\hat A,\nu\rangle=0$ for every $X\in\mathfrak{so}(n)$.

## Batch 2: single-character directions see only the diagonal part (classified: agreement with L123 and F44)

**The fact.** The frame's full sign group $\Gamma$ fixes $D$ pointwise and acts on each character space $S_\chi$ by a scalar, and $\circ$ is $\Gamma$-equivariant, so $y\circ y\in D$ for $y$ in one character space and $T(\nu,y,y)=0$ since $\nu\perp D$. On $S_\chi\ominus\nu_\chi$ the second variation is therefore $a\langle\hat A\circ y,y\rangle-\tfrac\theta2\lvert y\rvert^2$, and $\nu$ couples only distinct characters. **This is [L123](../findings/second-order.md) with [post F44](../findings/INDEX-traps.md)**: $L_{\operatorname{diag}}$ is the block-diagonal part of $L_W$ and the off-diagonal part the off-block part. Batch 1's diagonal block is the trivial character.

**What it adds to batch 1.** $\theta a=a^2p+b^2r$ with $p\le\theta$ gives $r=\langle\hat A\circ\nu,\nu\rangle=a(\theta-ap)/b^2\ge\frac{a\theta}{1+a}$: above $\Theta_n$ the off-diagonal part carries a large $L_{\hat A}$-Rayleigh quotient, spread over the characters of $\nu$. A single-character direction ascends only if $a\lambda_{\max}(L_{\hat A}|_{S_\chi\ominus\nu_\chi})>\tfrac\theta2$, and $\frac{a\theta}{1+a}<\frac\theta{2a}$ at every $a<1$, so the bound on $r$ never forces it. Near a diagonal local maximum, where $L_{\hat A}\le\tfrac p2$ off $\hat A$, no single character ascends.

**Where I124 now stands.** The ascent direction mixes sign characters through $\nu$, in every frame, and is off $\operatorname{span}\{W,\pi_DW\}$. On two characters $\chi_1,\chi_2$ with $\chi_1\chi_2$ in $\nu$'s support, $v=\alpha y_1+\beta y_2$ ascends iff $b^2T(\nu,y_1,y_2)^2>d_1d_2$ with $d_i=a\langle\hat A\circ y_i,y_i\rangle-\tfrac\theta2\le0$, so what is owed is a lower bound on the coupling $T(\nu_{\chi_1\chi_2},y_1,y_2)$ against the two deficits that consumes $\theta\ge\theta_0$. *F44 names the same three exits: mix characters, leave Rayleigh quotients, or vary the frame.*

## Batch 3: the circle direction couples to the non-eigen part of $\nu$ with strength $\lvert u\rvert$ (derived, Gate 2 [r1925](REVIEW_r1925.md), [r1926](REVIEW_r1926.md), CONFIRMED)

**Statement.** *In any frame with $a,b>0$ let $w_c=a\nu-b\hat A$ (the unit circle direction at $W$), $r=\langle\hat A\circ\nu,\nu\rangle$ and $u=\hat A\circ\nu-r\nu\ne0$. Then $u\in D^\perp$, $u\perp W$, $u\perp w_c$, and $T(W,w_c,u)=-\lvert u\rvert^2$. So on $\operatorname{span}\{w_c,\hat u\}$ the second variation is $\begin{pmatrix}h_c&-\lvert u\rvert\\-\lvert u\rvert&h_u\end{pmatrix}$ with $h_c=\frac{2ap-\theta(1+a^2)}{2b^2}<0$ above $\Theta_n$ (batch 1) and $h_u=T(W,\hat u,\hat u)-\tfrac\theta2$, and it has an ascent direction iff $h_u>-\lvert u\rvert^2/\lvert h_c\rvert$.*

**Proof.** *$\hat A\circ x\in D$ for $x\in D$, so $\langle\hat A\circ\nu,x\rangle=\langle\hat A\circ x,\nu\rangle=0$ and $\hat A\circ\nu\in D^\perp$; $u\perp\nu$ by the choice of $r$, hence $u\perp\hat A,\nu,W,w_c$. The $D^\perp$ component of $W\circ W=\theta W$ is $2ab\,\hat A\circ\nu+b^2\pi_\perp(\nu\circ\nu)=\theta b\nu$, so $\langle b\,\nu\circ\nu,u\rangle=\langle\theta\nu-2a\hat A\circ\nu,u\rangle=-2a\lvert u\rvert^2$. Then $\langle W\circ\nu,u\rangle=a\lvert u\rvert^2-2a\lvert u\rvert^2=-a\lvert u\rvert^2$ and $\langle W\circ\hat A,u\rangle=a\langle Q(\hat A),u\rangle+b\lvert u\rvert^2=b\lvert u\rvert^2$, so $T(W,w_c,u)=-a^2\lvert u\rvert^2-b^2\lvert u\rvert^2$.* $\square$

- **What it gives.** $u=0$ exactly when $\nu$ is an $L_{\hat A}$-eigenvector, and otherwise the descent along $w_c$ can be traded for ascent along $w_c+s\hat u$ whenever $\hat u$'s own deficit is small against $\lvert u\rvert^2/\lvert h_c\rvert$. $w_c$ and $u$ live in the trivial character and $\nu$'s characters, so this is a mixed-character test in F44's sense. *Owed*: a bound on $T(W,\hat u,\hat u)=a\langle\hat A\circ\hat u,\hat u\rangle+b\,T(\nu,\hat u,\hat u)$, and the case $u=0$.

## Batch 4: the circle direction couples to at most two directions (derived, Gate 2 [r1925](REVIEW_r1925.md), [r1926](REVIEW_r1926.md): it is [L284](../findings/second-order.md) read as the second-variation row)

**Statement.** *With $R=Q(\hat A)-p\hat A\in D\ominus\hat A$ (the failure of $\hat A$ to be critical within $D$) and $u=\hat A\circ\nu-r\nu$ (batch 3), the row of the second variation at $w_c$ is $T(W,w_c,x)=-\tfrac ab\langle R,x\rangle$ on $D\ominus\hat A$ and $T(W,w_c,y)=-\langle u,y\rangle$ on $D^\perp\ominus\nu$. So $W^\perp=\mathbb Rw_c\oplus\operatorname{span}\{\hat R,\hat u\}\oplus V$ where $w_c$ is second-variation-orthogonal to $V$, and an ascent direction exists if the form on $\operatorname{span}\{w_c,\hat R,\hat u\}$ has a positive eigenvalue, the diagonal entry at $w_c$ being $h_c=\frac{2ap-\theta(1+a^2)}{2b^2}\le-\frac{\theta(1-a)}{2(1+a)}$ above $\Theta_n$.*

**Proof.** *The rows are $T(W,w_c,x)=\tfrac ba\langle Q(\nu),x\rangle$ and $T(W,w_c,y)=-\langle\hat A\circ\nu,y\rangle$ ([r1916](REVIEW_r1916.md); equal to r1918's forms by the two criticality components). The $D$ component of $W\circ W=\theta W$, $a^2Q(\hat A)+b^2\pi_D(\nu\circ\nu)=\theta a\hat A$, gives $\langle Q(\nu),x\rangle=-\tfrac{a^2}{b^2}\langle R,x\rangle$ on $D\ominus\hat A$, and $\langle\hat A\circ\nu,y\rangle=\langle u,y\rangle$ for $y\perp\nu$.* $\square$

- **Where it sits on the record.** L284's steps (3) and (4) at $z=-w_c$: its $\rho C$ is $w_c$'s row with the $w_c$ entry removed, $q_\perp=a^2R$, and its defect sum is $\lvert\text{row}\rvert^2=(a/b)^2\lvert R\rvert^2+\lvert u\rvert^2$. New is the reading as a row and the $2\times2$ and $3\times3$ ascent tests.
- **What it gives.** The descent along $w_c$ can be traded only through $\hat R$ (strength $\tfrac ab\lvert R\rvert$) and $\hat u$ (strength $\lvert u\rvert$). When $\hat A$ is critical within $D$ and $\nu$ is an $L_{\hat A}$-eigenvector, $w_c$ decouples entirely and the ascent must come from $V$, the part of $W^\perp$ the circle does not see. *Owed*: the entries $H(\hat R)$, $H(\hat u)$, $H(\hat R,\hat u)$, and the decoupled case.

## Batch 5: the decoupled case is a two-dimensional subalgebra, and padded $W_{\mathbb{CP}^2}$ is one (derived, one screen, Gate 2 [r1925](REVIEW_r1925.md), [r1926](REVIEW_r1926.md): the closed branch of the [I121 note](2026-09-28-I121-MG-audit.md))

**Statement.** *$R=0$ and $u=0$ hold exactly when $\hat A$ is a critical point of $P$ ($Q(\hat A)=p\hat A$, a diagonal one), $\nu$ is an $L_{\hat A}$-eigenvector with eigenvalue $r$, and then $\operatorname{span}\{\hat A,\nu\}$ is closed under $\circ$: $\hat A\circ\hat A=p\hat A$, $\hat A\circ\nu=r\nu$, $\nu\circ\nu=r\hat A+q\nu$. On it $P$ is the circle cubic, and $W$ is its critical point with $\theta a=a^2p+b^2r$, $\theta b=2abr+b^2q$.*

**Proof.** *$R=0$ is $Q(\hat A)=p\hat A$, and $u=0$ is $\hat A\circ\nu=r\nu$. The two components of criticality then give $\pi_D(\nu\circ\nu)=(\theta a-a^2p)\hat A/b^2=r\hat A$ and $\pi_\perp(\nu\circ\nu)=(\theta-2ar)\nu/b=q\nu$.* $\square$

- **Padded $W_{\mathbb{CP}^2}$ is decoupled** (screen, seconds, the cp2 screen, kept in the scratchpad, a check of a claim about to be recorded): in the coordinate frame of $\mathbb R^4$, $a=b=1/\sqrt2$, $\hat A$ is the Weyl tensor of $S^2\times S^2$ (weights $2,-1,-1,-1,-1,2$ on the pairs), $p=r=\sqrt3/2=\Theta_4$, $q=0$, and $R$, $u$ vanish to $10^{-15}$. The circle with $q=0$ has its interior critical point at $a^2=r/(3r-p)$ with $\theta=2ar$, so $r=p$ gives $\theta=\sqrt2\,p$, which is $\sqrt{3/2}$ here. With $r>0$ an interior critical point exists iff $r>\tfrac p2$ ($r=\tfrac p2$ gives $a=1$, $r\le\tfrac p3$ none). Both reviewers derived the decoupling by hand through the $\Lambda^\pm$ split, which holds at every $n\ge4$ after padding; in a generic frame $W_{\mathbb{CP}^2}$ is coupled, so the decoupled frames are its maximal ones. **The placement of $W_{\mathbb{CP}^2}$ in the closed branch is new to the record**; the equivalence and closure are [L284](../findings/second-order.md)'s $C=0$ case, the outside author's Conjecture 27.1 algebra with $(d,\tau,q)=(p,r,q)$, and for general $q$ the circle's criterion is [r1801](REVIEW_r1801.md)'s endpoint criterion in [R54](../routes.md)'s row.
- **So, in a maximal frame, the decoupled case is where I124 must consume $\theta\ge\theta_0$**: it contains the second-order-critical point below $\theta_0$. *Padded $W_{\mathbb{CP}^2}$ has no ascent in any frame, so an argument frame by frame must consume the value in the coupled case too.* Its whole content is a diagonal critical point $\hat A$ with an eigenvalue $r$ of $L_{\hat A}$ on $D^\perp$ large against $p$: batch 2 forces $r\ge\frac{a\theta}{1+a}$. At a diagonal local maximum $L_{\hat A}\le\tfrac p2$ off $\hat A$, and with $q=0$ this leaves no interior critical point ($a^2=r/(3r-p)>1$ for $\tfrac p3<r<\tfrac p2$). **Owed**: a bound on $L_{\hat A}$'s eigenvalues on $D^\perp$ at diagonal critical points ([L121](../findings/second-order.md), [L122](../findings/second-order.md) give the character blocks) that makes the circle's value fall below $\theta_0$, or an ascent in $V$ when it does not.

## Batch 6: in one sign character the decoupled case is $q=0$, and in a $4$-set character it is padded from $\mathbb R^4$ (derived, the scalar core in Lean; classified: agreement with the [I121 note](2026-09-28-I121-MG-audit.md), [L220](../findings/second-order.md) and [L224](../findings/second-order.md))

**Statement.** *Let $W$ be a unit critical point with $\theta\ge\Theta_n$, non-diagonal, and in a frame with $a,b>0$ let $Q(\hat A)=p\hat A$ ($R=0$). (1) If $\nu$ lies in one sign-character space, then $\nu\circ\nu=r\hat A$, $q=0$, $\theta=2ar$, $a^2(3r-p)=r$ and $\theta^2(3r-p)=4r^3$. (2) If that character is a $4$-set $\{i,j,k,l\}$, then $\hat A$ and $\nu$ are supported on $\Lambda^2\operatorname{span}\{f_i,f_j,f_k,f_l\}$, so $W$ is a padded Weyl tensor of $\mathbb R^4$ and $\theta\le M(4)=\sqrt{3/2}<\theta_0(n)$ at every $n\ge12$: no such point is in I124's range.*

**Proof.** *(1) The frame's sign group acts on $\nu$'s character by a scalar $\pm1$, so $\nu\circ\nu$ is fixed and lies in $D$ ([L123](../findings/second-order.md)'s equivariance). Then the $D^\perp$ component of criticality, $2ab\,\hat A\circ\nu+b^2\pi_\perp(\nu\circ\nu)=\theta b\nu$, has $\pi_\perp(\nu\circ\nu)=0$, so $q=\langle\nu\circ\nu,\nu\rangle=0$ and $\theta b=2abr$; and the $D$ component with $R=0$ reads $b^2\nu\circ\nu=a(\theta-ap)\hat A$, which is $r\hat A$ by $\theta a=a^2p+b^2r$. The scalars follow from the two component equations with $q=0$. (2) $\nu$ is a combination of $\varphi_{ij}\odot\varphi_{kl}$, $\varphi_{ik}\odot\varphi_{jl}$, $\varphi_{il}\odot\varphi_{jk}$; the squares of these and every bracket among $\varphi_{ij},\dots,\varphi_{kl}$ stay in $\Lambda^2\operatorname{span}\{f_i,f_j,f_k,f_l\}$, so $\nu\circ\nu$ is supported on the six pairs of the $4$-set. Since $\theta\ge\Theta_n\ge p$ and $a<1$, $\theta a-a^2p>0$, so $\hat A=b^2\nu\circ\nu/(a(\theta-ap))$ is supported there too, and so is $W$. A Weyl tensor of $\mathbb R^n$ supported on $\Lambda^2$ of a $4$-plane is a padded Weyl tensor of that plane, $\theta$ is invariant under padding, and $M(4)=\sqrt{3/2}$ ([conventions.md](../findings/conventions.md)); $\tfrac32<\theta_0(n)^2$ is $n^2-12n+8>0$, which holds at $n\ge12$ (`threshold_pos` in [L2.lean](../lean/HamiltonsCubic/Results/L2.lean)).* $\square$ **Lean**: `single_char_value` in [I124Circle.lean](../lean/HamiltonsCubic/Results/I124Circle.lean); the rest is prose.

- **What it closes.** The branch padded $W_{\mathbb{CP}^2}$ sits on: a decoupled point whose off-diagonal part is one $4$-set character is below $\theta_0$ at $n\ge12$, which is exactly how I124 is meant to consume $\theta\ge\theta_0$.
- **Where it sits on the record.** Part (1) is the I121 note's "a pure character is square-free, so $q\ne0$ needs cyclic support"; part (2)'s cap is [L220](../findings/second-order.md) (one four-set, $\theta\le\sqrt{3/2}$), and one pair block is capped at $\Theta_n$ by [L224](../findings/second-order.md), which a seconds-long screen at $n=8$ agreed with ($\nu\circ\nu$ diagonal and $u=0$ on every pair-character $x$ tried, $R\ne0$ except on the padded four-set). **So the closed branch of R57's frame split is the record's [I104](../plan.md), overlapping four-sets with $\tau>0$, now with $\theta\ge\theta_0$ in hand, and the coupled branch is a local form of (MG): the frame split carries R57 onto [I121](../plan.md#blocked)'s two halves, the difference being that I124 asks for an ascent direction only.**
- **What it leaves.** $\nu$ over several characters, in the decoupled and the coupled branch; a single pair character is closed in both branches by [L224](../findings/second-order.md) and a single four-set by [L220](../findings/second-order.md) ([r1943](REVIEW_r1943.md)).

## Batch 7: in the closed branch the orbit tangents of the diagonal part give a one-ratio test (derived, one screen, Gate 2 [r1930](REVIEW_r1930.md), [r1931](REVIEW_r1931.md), CONFIRMED)

**Statement.** *In a frame maximising $\lVert\pi_DW\rVert$, at a closed-branch point ($Q(\hat A)=p\hat A$, $\hat A\circ\nu=r\nu$), every $X\in\mathfrak{so}(n)$ gives a direction $X\cdot\hat A\perp W$ with*
$$T(W,X\cdot\hat A,X\cdot\hat A)-\tfrac\theta2\lvert X\cdot\hat A\rvert^2=-\frac{b^2r}{2a}\lvert X\cdot\hat A\rvert^2+b\big(r-\tfrac p2\big)\langle X\cdot\nu,X\cdot\hat A\rangle,$$
*so it ascends iff $(r-\tfrac p2)\langle X\cdot\nu,X\cdot\hat A\rangle>\frac{br}{2a}\lvert X\cdot\hat A\rvert^2$. Since $X\cdot W=aX\cdot\hat A+bX\cdot\nu$ lies in the kernel of the form on $W^\perp$, $F(X\cdot\nu)=\tfrac{a^2}{b^2}F(X\cdot\hat A)$ for the form $F$, so the orbit tangents of $\nu$ give no second test.*

**Proof.** *$X\cdot\hat A\perp\hat A$ by skewness and $\perp\nu$ by the frame's first-order condition. Differentiating $Q(g\hat A)=gQ(\hat A)$ once gives $\hat A\circ(X\cdot\hat A)=\tfrac p2X\cdot\hat A$, and twice gives $(X\cdot\hat A)\circ(X\cdot\hat A)=\tfrac p2X^2\cdot\hat A-\hat A\circ(X^2\cdot\hat A)$. So $\langle\nu,(X\cdot\hat A)\circ(X\cdot\hat A)\rangle=(\tfrac p2-r)\langle\nu,X^2\cdot\hat A\rangle=(r-\tfrac p2)\langle X\cdot\nu,X\cdot\hat A\rangle$, and $T(W,X\cdot\hat A,X\cdot\hat A)=\tfrac{ap}2\lvert X\cdot\hat A\rvert^2+b(r-\tfrac p2)\langle X\cdot\nu,X\cdot\hat A\rangle$; finally $\theta-ap=b^2r/a$ from $\theta a=a^2p+b^2r$.* $\square$ *The two derivatives of equivariance are the record's orbit identity and [post F37](../findings/INDEX-traps.md) applied at the diagonal critical point $\hat A$ rather than at $W$; pairing them with $\nu$ is new ([r1930](REVIEW_r1930.md), [r1931](REVIEW_r1931.md)).*

- **Screen** (seconds, a check of the identity before recording it): at $W_{\mathbb{CP}^2}$, $n=4$, the formula matches the direct second variation to $5\cdot10^{-14}$ on $200$ random $X$, and the ratio $\langle X\cdot\nu,X\cdot\hat A\rangle/\lvert X\cdot\hat A\rvert^2$ reaches $0.9965$ against the threshold $\frac{br}{2a(r-p/2)}=1$. **The supremum is exactly $1$, attained on the self-dual $\mathfrak{so}(3)$, where $X\cdot\hat A=X\cdot W/(a+b)$ is an orbit tangent of $W$** (both reviewers, in the $\Lambda^\pm$ model), so the equality is the kernel. **The same holds on every single-character closed-branch point**: for $g$ in the sign group flipping $\nu$'s character, $X\in\operatorname{stab}(g\cdot W)$ gives $aX\cdot\hat A=bX\cdot\nu$, so $X\cdot\hat A=X\cdot W/(2a)$, and the ratio $a/b$ meets the threshold exactly by batch 6's $a^2(3r-p)=r$.
- **What it leaves.** The ratio is well posed on $\mathfrak{so}(n)/\operatorname{stab}(\hat A)$, the numerator vanishing on $\operatorname{stab}(\hat A)$ by the first-order condition, and at $r=\tfrac p2$ no orbit tangent of $\hat A$ ascends. In a maximal frame, a closed-branch point ascends along an orbit tangent of its diagonal part as soon as the two quadratic forms $X\mapsto\langle X\cdot\nu,X\cdot\hat A\rangle$ and $X\mapsto\lvert X\cdot\hat A\rvert^2$ on $\mathfrak{so}(n)$ have a generalised eigenvalue beyond $\frac{br}{2a(r-p/2)}$ on the side of $r-\tfrac p2$'s sign. The directions attaining the threshold on one character are orbit tangents of $W$, so an ascent needs $\nu$ over several characters. The frame's second-order condition, $b^2\lvert\pi_DX\cdot\nu\rvert^2\le a^2\lvert X\cdot\hat A\rvert^2+ab\langle X\cdot\nu,X\cdot\hat A\rangle$, bounds the ratio below by $-a/b$ ([r1931](REVIEW_r1931.md)), which constrains the side $r<\tfrac p2$ and leaves $r>\tfrac p2$, I104's side, open. *Owed*: that eigenvalue at the I104 configurations (overlapping four-sets, $\tau=r>0$), where $\theta\ge\theta_0$ is to be consumed.

## Batch 8: where $\hat A$ is critical, $W$ lives on the indices $\nu$ touches, and five of them put it below $\theta_0$ (derived, Gate 2 [r1940](REVIEW_r1940.md), [r1941](REVIEW_r1941.md): the reduction CONFIRMED, the six-index clause refuted as first stated, for want of a ceiling on $M(6)$)

**Statement.** *Let $W$ be a unit critical point with $\theta\ge\Theta_n$, and in a frame with $a,b>0$ let $Q(\hat A)=p\hat A$ ($R=0$). Then $\hat A=\frac{b^2}{a(\theta-ap)}\pi_D(\nu\circ\nu)$, so $W$ is supported on $\Lambda^2\operatorname{span}\{f_i:i\in U\}$, $U$ the set of frame indices appearing in $\nu$'s expansion over $\varphi_\alpha\odot\varphi_\beta$, and $W$ is a padded Weyl tensor of $\mathbb R^{\lvert U\rvert}$. If $\lvert U\rvert\le5$, then $\theta\le\sqrt{3/2}<\theta_0(n)$ at every $n\ge12$; at $\lvert U\rvert=6$ the same follows from $M(6)<\theta_0(12)$, which the record does not hold. [I104](../plan.md)'s configuration $\mathcal D_n\oplus V_{Q_1}\oplus V_{Q_2}\oplus V_{Q_1\triangle Q_2}$ has $\lvert U\rvert\le6$, with equality when two blocks are nonzero.*

**Proof.** *The $D$ component of $W\circ W=\theta W$ with $Q(\hat A)=p\hat A$ reads $b^2\pi_D(\nu\circ\nu)=a(\theta-ap)\hat A$, and $\theta-ap>0$ above $\Theta_n\ge p$. $\nu$ is a symmetric operator on $\mathfrak{so}(U)=\Lambda^2\operatorname{span}\{f_i:i\in U\}$, a subalgebra, so $\nu^2$ and $\nu^\#\nu$, and with them $\nu\circ\nu$, stay there ([r1941](REVIEW_r1941.md)'s form of the step); hence $\hat A$ and $W=a\hat A+b\nu$ are supported on pairs inside $U$. A Weyl tensor of $\mathbb R^n$ supported on $\Lambda^2$ of a coordinate subspace is a padded Weyl tensor of it, and $\theta$ is invariant under padding ([L164](../findings/INDEX-results.md)), so $M$ is non-decreasing in the dimension. $M(4)=\sqrt{3/2}$ has a hand proof ([post F2c](../findings/fence-posts.md)) and $M(5)=\sqrt{3/2}$ the certificate of [sym3-blocks.md](../findings/sym3-blocks.md) ([L61](../findings/INDEX-results.md)), which covers every $\lvert U\rvert\le5$; $\mathcal W_m=0$ for $m\le3$; and $\tfrac32<\theta_0(n)^2$ at $n\ge12$ (`theta0Sq_sub_three_halves` with `threshold_pos`). **At $m=6$ the record proves only $M(6)\ge\sqrt{3/2}$** ([r649](REVIEW_r649.md)); the claim $M(6)=\sqrt{3/2}$ it carried in W5, in [conventions.md](../findings/conventions.md) and in [bispectral.md](../findings/bispectral.md) is corrected with this fold.* $\square$ **Lean**: the coefficient's positivity is `diag_coeff_pos` in [I124Circle.lean](../lean/HamiltonsCubic/Results/I124Circle.lean), and $\tfrac32<\theta_0(n)^2$ is `theta0Sq_sub_three_halves` with `threshold_pos` in [L2.lean](../lean/HamiltonsCubic/Results/L2.lean); the support argument is prose.

- **What it gives.** In the $R=0$ branch a non-diagonal critical point above $\theta_0$ has $\nu$ touching at least six frame indices, and at least seven once $M(6)<\theta_0(12)$. A pair-character component on $\{j,k\}$ touches $j$, $k$ and every $i$ carrying weight, and a four-set component its four indices. $R=0$ is a branch, not automatic. **I104's $\tau\le0$ half is already [L221](../findings/second-order.md) (b), $\theta^2\le\tfrac65$, with neither hypothesis; its $\tau>0$, $R=0$ half lies outside I124's range once $M(6)<\theta_0(12)$.** The reduction generalises batch 6 (2) and [L220](../findings/second-order.md)'s second step; the record has no padding argument in the closed branch ([r1941](REVIEW_r1941.md)).
- **What it does not reach.** Padded points from $\mathbb R^m$ with $6\le m\le11$, where no ceiling on $M(m)$ is on the record; a possible repair at I104's configuration that avoids $M(6)$, the joint chain of the [I104 note](2026-09-14-I104-tau-positive.md) with its $\rho=0$ on this branch ([r1940](REVIEW_r1940.md), not derived); $R=0$ points with $\lvert U\rvert\ge12$, the generic case, which padding cannot cap; and the branch $R\ne0$ (coupled, batch 4).

## Batch 9: with $\hat A$ critical and $\nu$ in one pair character, $x$ has two entries (derived, screens, the elimination in Lean, Gate 2 [r1942](REVIEW_r1942.md), [r1943](REVIEW_r1943.md): CONFIRMED, the value bound subsumed by [L224](../findings/second-order.md))

**Statement.** *Let $W$ be a unit critical point with $\theta\ge\Theta_n$, and in a frame with $a,b>0$ let $Q(\hat A)=p\hat A$ and $\nu$ lie in one sign-character space. Then $W$ is supported on $\Lambda^2$ of four frame vectors, so $\theta\le\sqrt{3/2}<\theta_0(n)$ at every $n\ge12$. For a four-set character this is batch 6 (2); the new case is a pair character.*

**Proof.** *Take the pair character $\{j,k\}$: $\nu=\sum_ix_if_i$, $f_i=\varphi_{ji}\odot\varphi_{ki}$, $\sum_ix_i=0$. Then $\nu\circ\nu$ is diagonal (batch 6 (1)) with vertex matrix $M_{ji}=M_{ki}=x_i^2$, $M_{jk}=-s$, $M_{ii'}=2x_ix_{i'}$, $s=\lvert x\rvert^2$, and by batch 8 $\hat A\propto M$. On the diagonal locus $Q$ acts by $Q(M)_{ab}=M_{ab}^2+(M^2)_{ab}$ ([L222](../findings/second-order.md) item 2's product and L224 step 2; the pattern $M(x)$ is L222 item 2's $G_x$, `Q_nuX` in [PairSquare.lean](../lean/HamiltonsCubic/Results/PairSquare.lean)), so $Q(\hat A)=p\hat A$ reads $M_{ab}^2+(M^2)_{ab}=\mu M_{ab}$. The $(j,k)$ row fixes $\mu=-(s^2+\sum x_i^4)/s$; the $(j,i)$ rows give $x_i^3+(s+\mu)x_i-2\sigma_3=0$ at every $x_i\ne0$, $\sigma_3=\sum x_i^3$; the $(i,i')$ rows give $\mu=3uv+2s-2(u^2+v^2)$ for every two indices carrying nonzero values $u,v$. Since $\sum x_i=0$, $x$ carries two distinct nonzero values $u\ne v$ at two indices, and then $u^2+uv+v^2+s+\mu=0$ with the pair row gives $3s=u^2-4uv+v^2$, while $s\ge u^2+v^2$; so $2(u+v)^2\le0$, $v=-u$ and $s=u^2+v^2$: $x$ is supported on exactly those two indices. $W$ then lives on $\{j,k,i_1,i_2\}$, a padded Weyl tensor of $\mathbb R^4$, and $M(4)=\sqrt{3/2}$ ([post F2c](../findings/fence-posts.md)).* $\square$ **Lean**: the elimination is `pair_char_supp` in [I124Circle.lean](../lean/HamiltonsCubic/Results/I124Circle.lean); the rows of the diagonal equation are prose.

- **Screens** (seconds, checks of claims about to be recorded, at $n=8$): $\pi_D(\nu\circ\nu)$ equals the pattern $M(x)$ to $10^{-15}$ at scale $1$; $Q$ on a random Ricci-flat diagonal tensor equals $M_{ab}^2+(M^2)_{ab}$ to $10^{-15}$ at scale $1$; and over fifteen random $x$ of supports $2$ to $6$, $R=0$ held exactly at support $2$ and nowhere else ($\lvert R\rvert\ge0.15$).
- **Where it sits on the record.** The value bound is subsumed: [L224](../findings/second-order.md) caps every unit $W\in\mathcal D_n\oplus V_{jk}$ at $\Theta_n$ with no criticality and no $R=0$, equality only at $(j,k)$-rotations of L57's maximisers, which are diagonal in another frame; [L220](../findings/second-order.md) caps every critical point in $\mathcal D_n\oplus V_Q$ with a four-set component at $\sqrt{3/2}$. At $n\ge12$, $\Theta_n>\sqrt{3/2}$, so the batch's branch is empty there ([r1942](REVIEW_r1942.md)). **New is the support statement**: under $R=0$ a single pair character has $x=(u,-u)$ on two indices.
- **What it gives.** **In either branch** a non-diagonal critical point above $\theta_0$ has $\nu$ over at least two sign characters (L220, L224), and in the $R=0$ branch over at least six frame indices (batch 8). *Owed*: $\nu$ over two or more characters, in both branches.

## Batch 10: the superposition bound, and what a counterexample to P must satisfy in every frame (derived, one screen, the averaging step in Lean, Gate 2 [r1944](REVIEW_r1944.md), [r1945](REVIEW_r1945.md): the bound CONFIRMED and new, (★) as a universal statement REFUTED)

**Statement.** *Let $n\ge12$ and $W$ be any unit Weyl tensor, not necessarily critical. In any frame write $W=a\hat A+N$, $a\hat A=\pi_DW$, $N=\sum_\chi N_\chi$ over the nontrivial sign characters, $b=\lVert N\rVert$, $w_\chi=\lVert N_\chi\rVert/b$. Then*
$$P(W)=\sum_\chi w_\chi^2\,P\big(a\hat A+b\hat N_\chi\big)+P(N),\qquad P(W)\le\Theta_n+P(N).$$
*So at even $n\ge12$, where $\Theta_n=\theta_0(n)$, a unit $W$ with $P(W)>\theta_0$ has $P(N_F)\ge P(W)-\theta_0>0$ in every frame. At the balanced product every frame has $P(N)\ge0$, with $N=0$ in its diagonal frames. The cases $b=0$ (then $W=\hat A$ and L57 applies) and $a=0$ (any unit diagonal tensor serves as $\hat A$) are included.*

**Proof.** *$T(\hat A,\hat A,N)=\langle Q(\hat A),N\rangle=0$ since $D$ is closed under $Q$; $T(\hat A,N_\chi,N_\psi)=\langle\hat A\circ N_\chi,N_\psi\rangle=0$ for $\chi\ne\psi$, as $\hat A\circ N_\chi$ carries $\chi$; and $P(\hat N_\chi)=\langle\hat N_\chi\circ\hat N_\chi,\hat N_\chi\rangle=0$ since $\hat N_\chi\circ\hat N_\chi\in D$ ([L123](../findings/second-order.md)'s equivariance). So $P(W)=a^3p+3a\sum_\chi\lVert N_\chi\rVert^2T(\hat A,\hat N_\chi,\hat N_\chi)+P(N)$, and each unit $a\hat A+b\hat N_\chi$ has $P=a^3p+3ab^2T(\hat A,\hat N_\chi,\hat N_\chi)$; weighting by $w_\chi^2$, $\sum w_\chi^2=1$, gives the identity. Each $a\hat A+b\hat N_\chi$ is a unit tensor in $\mathcal D_n\oplus V_\chi$: for a pair character [L224](../findings/second-order.md) caps it at $\Theta_n$; for a four-set $Q$, $\mathcal D_n\oplus V_Q$ is closed under $\circ$, so the maximum of $P$ on its sphere is a critical point of $P$ on $\mathcal W_n$, which is diagonal ($\le\Theta_n$ by [L57](../findings/problem.md)) or has $\theta\le\sqrt{3/2}$ ([L220](../findings/second-order.md)), and $\sqrt{3/2}<\Theta_n$ at $n\ge12$. The weighted average of values at most $\Theta_n$ is at most $\Theta_n$. At the balanced product $P(W)=\Theta_n$, so $P(N)\ge0$ in every frame.* $\square$ **Lean**: the averaging step is `superposition_bound` in [I124Circle.lean](../lean/HamiltonsCubic/Results/I124Circle.lean); the identity and the single-character caps are prose on the cited lemmas.

- **Screen** (seconds, a check of the identity before recording it): on a random unit Weyl tensor at $n=6$, in the coordinate frame, $P(W)$ and the right side agree exactly, with $\sum w_\chi^2=1$ over the characters present.
- **The universal form is false** ([r1945](REVIEW_r1945.md)): the Weyl tensor of $\mathbb{CP}^6$ at $n=12$ has $P=0.925<\Theta_{12}$ and $P(N_F)\ge0.085$ in every frame, since its diagonal mass is at most $3/(n+2)$ in every frame and $P(N_F)=\theta(1-3t)+2t^{3/2}p$ at a critical point. **At a global maximiser the condition "some frame has $P(N_F)\le0$" is equivalent to P** ([r1944](REVIEW_r1944.md), [r1945](REVIEW_r1945.md)), so the bound's content is a necessary condition on a counterexample: positive off-diagonal cubic in every frame, of size at least its excess over $\theta_0$.
- **Where it sits on the record.** New as a bound (both readers): the per-character computation is on record in L220's corollary and in `L224_cubic`, and keeping the terms coupled per character avoids [post F67](../findings/fence-posts.md). At a critical point it reads $3\theta a^2-2a^3p\le\Theta_n$ in every frame, [L214](../findings/second-order.md)'s first relation with the new cap, valid whatever $\theta$; the cap $\Theta_n\frac{1+2a^3}{3a^2}$ is never below $\Theta_n$, so it excludes nothing alone, and it strictly sharpens [R51](../routes.md)'s $\frac{2a^3}{3a^2-1}$ for $a<1$. L220 carries an $n\ge12$ sup clause that states the four-set cap directly.
- *Owed*: an argument that uses $P(W)>\theta_0$ or maximality to produce a frame with $P(N_F)\le0$, exact at the balanced product: Haar-averaged frames give $P(N_F)\in[1.03,1.18]$ there against $P=1.236$ ([r1944](REVIEW_r1944.md), a screen), so a spread-out average cannot work.
