## A. SubFermat.lean

- `fermat_sub` is [WeylMaximiser.lean:103](../lean/HamiltonsCubic/Results/WeylMaximiser.lean#L103)'s `innerOp2_Qop2_eq_zero_weyl` with the Weyl sphere replaced by $\{\lVert R\rVert^2=1\}\cap S$. The only new obligation is that the sphere curve stays in $S$. [SphereCurve.lean:61](../lean/HamiltonsCubic/Results/SphereCurve.lean#L61) defines it as $(1+t^2\lVert y\rVert^2)^{-1/2}(a+ty)$, and [SubFermat.lean:41](../lean/HamiltonsCubic/Results/SubFermat.lean#L41) discharges membership by `smul_mem`, `add_mem` and `smul_mem`. The curves at $y$ and $-y$ give $\pm3\langle y,Q(a)\rangle\le0$. Correct.
- `crit_sub` sets $z=Q(a)-\langle a,Q(a)\rangle a$, which lies in $S$ by `hSQ` and is orthogonal to $a$ at $\lVert a\rVert=1$. Fermat at $y=z$ gives $\langle z,Q(a)\rangle=0$, so $\lVert z\rVert^2=0$. The eigenvalue is $P(a)$ by `Pcubic2_eq_innerOp2`, $P(R)=\langle R,Q(R)\rangle$ ([Bivectors2.lean:837](../lean/HamiltonsCubic/Base/Bivectors2.lean#L837)). The hypotheses (self-adjoint elements, $Q$-closure, local maximum on the sphere of $S$) are exactly what step 1 uses: step 1 goes from "subalgebra" to "the maximum satisfies $Q(W)=\theta W$", and closure under $\circ$ gives closure under $Q$ through `foxProd2_self`.
- `SW t` is $\{R: R\text{ Weyl},\ R=D+\nu,\ D\text{ of character }1,\ \nu\text{ of character }\chi_t\}$. The components are not required to be Weyl, and [BlockSub.lean:201](../lean/HamiltonsCubic/Results/BlockSub.lean#L201)'s `weyl_fixed_iff` supplies that they are (as `comp ∅` and `comp t`), so `SW t` is the record's $\mathcal S_Q$ at $t=Q$ for nonempty $t$.

## C. L221a.lean

- `multi_split_zero`: for each $i$ it takes $p\in Q_i$ and the single flip at $p$. Disjointness makes $\chi_{Q_j}(\varepsilon_p)=1$ for $j\ne i$ ([L221a.lean:73](../lean/HamiltonsCubic/Results/L221a.lean#L73)), so conjugating the sum gives $A_0-A_i+\sum_{j\ne i}A_j=0$ beside $A_0+A_i+\sum_{j\ne i}A_j=0$, hence $A_i=0$, then $A_0=0$. At $m=0$ the sum over `Fin 0` is empty, `hsum` reads $A_0+0=0$ and the second conjunct is vacuous. At $m=1$ the flip at any $p\in Q_0$ separates $\chi_{Q_0}$ from $1$. Both boundaries are sound. The theorem needs only pairwise disjoint nonempty sets: no Weyl, no cardinality.

Its cross terms vanish by `disjoint_prod_zero`, which rests on [L120Support.lean:63](../lean/HamiltonsCubic/Results/L120Support.lean#L63)'s `block_card`. ## Observations

1. **O1, local maximiser is a strengthening of "the maximum".** [SubFermat.lean:93](../lean/HamiltonsCubic/Results/SubFermat.lean#L93) takes `IsLocalMaxOn` on $\{\lVert R\rVert^2=1\}\cap\mathcal S_t\cap\mathcal W_n$. A global maximum on that set is a local one (`IsMaxOn.localize`), so every maximum of [second-order.md:1536](../findings/second-order.md#L1536) satisfies the hypothesis. Existence of the maximum (compactness) is not typed. The record presupposes it and does not claim it in step 1.
2. **O2, the index set is unrestricted.** `L220_step1_crit` holds at every `t`, including $t=\varnothing$ where $\mathcal S_\varnothing=\mathcal D_n$. The identification with the record's $\mathcal S_Q$ via `weyl_fixed_iff` needs $t$ nonempty ([BlockSub.lean:201](../lean/HamiltonsCubic/Results/BlockSub.lean#L201)). The four-set case is inside that range.
3. **O3, maximiser input where L221 says critical point.** L221's statement quantifies over "every unit critical point of $P$ in $\mathcal S$" ([second-order.md:1557](../findings/second-order.md#L1557), (b) likewise). Read as $Q(W)=\theta W$ in $\mathcal W_n$ with $W\in\mathcal S$, this is `L221b_split` and `L221a_split` directly. Read as a critical point of $P$ restricted to the unit sphere of $\mathcal S$, the passage to $Q(W)=\theta W$ needs the first-order condition at a non-maximal critical point: $\langle y,Q(W)\rangle=0$ for $y\in\mathcal S$, $y\perp W$, then $Q$-closure. `crit_sub` covers maximisers only. The module docstring's "step 1 in full" ([L221Crit.lean:7](../lean/HamiltonsCubic/Results/L221Crit.lean#L7)) is exact under the first reading and short by that first-order lemma under the second. The downstream use at $n\ge12$ concerns maximisers, so nothing there depends on it. The record should say which reading it means, or the Lean should type the critical-point form of `crit_sub`, which is `crit_sub`'s argument with the Fermat step replaced by the hypothesis.
4. **O4, the Weyl identification of the Klein and m-block spaces is neither proved nor listed as owed.** `SK` ([L221Crit.lean:79](../lean/HamiltonsCubic/Results/L221Crit.lean#L79)) and `SM` ([L221a.lean:176](../lean/HamiltonsCubic/Results/L221a.lean#L176)) require $R$ Weyl and its character components arbitrary. The record's $\mathcal D_n\oplus V_{Q_1}\oplus\cdots$ is built from blocks of L120's decomposition of $\mathcal W_n$, so its components are Weyl. The two spaces coincide: the components are unique by `klein_zero` and `multi_split_zero`, hence equal to `comp u R`, which `comp_isWeyl2` makes Weyl. For $\mathcal S_t$ this is `weyl_fixed_iff`, and there is no Klein or $m$-block analogue. It matters downstream: step 2's coordinates $a+b+c=0$ on $V_Q$ and L121's spectrum are Weyl facts about $\nu_i$, and `L221b_step1` and `L221a_crit` hand out $\nu_i$ with no Weyl clause. It belongs on the owed lists of [INDEX.md:150](../lean/INDEX.md#L150) and [second-order.md:1554](../findings/second-order.md#L1554). The statements as typed are correct, so this is an omission from the owed list, not a defect.
5. **O5, step 3 for (a) is listed as owed but is proved.** [INDEX.md:150](../lean/INDEX.md#L150) and [second-order.md:1554](../findings/second-order.md#L1554) owe "L220's steps 3 to 9 on each block" for (a). Step 3 on block $i$ is $D\circ D+\sum_j\nu_j\circ\nu_j=\theta D$ and $2D\circ\nu_i=\theta\nu_i$, which is `L221a_split`. The owed range is steps 4 to 9. The error is conservative.
6. **O6, the L220 row omits the $n\ge12$ clause and the operator-level equality case from its owed list.** [INDEX.md:149](../lean/INDEX.md#L149) owes the inputs to `theta_sq_le`. The statement's last sentence (at $n\ge12$, $\sup P=\Theta_n$ on $\mathcal S_Q$, attained only on $\mathcal D_n$) also needs L57 and Nienhaus Proposition 2.25 at the operators, and "equality exactly at padded $W_{\mathbb{CP}^2}$" is typed only as a scalar witness. Neither is claimed proved, so the row overstates nothing. It is incomplete as a list of what is owed.
7. **O7, what the rows claim proved is exact.** Every theorem the new parts of rows 149 and 150 name exists with the described statement: `fermat_sub`, `crit_sub`, `SW`, `SW_Qop2`, `L220_step1_crit`, `InK`, `K_mul`, `SK`, `SK_Qop2`, `L221b_step1`, `multi_split_zero`, `L221a_split`, `InM`, `M_mul`, `SM`, `L221a_crit`.
