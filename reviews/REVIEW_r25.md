### Reviewer 25: the subalgebra ("Casimir") family, the closed form, the dimension cap, and its scope

**Four claims confirmed, one over-sold, three corrections.** Everything was rebuilt from the definitions (structure constants, $\#$, $Q$, $P$, Ricci, the $\Lambda^4$ projection and the Weyl subspace), with nothing imported from the code under review ([review_r25_core.py](scripts/review_r25_core.py), [review_r25_tests.py](scripts/reviewers/r25/review_r25_tests.py), [review_r25_subalgebras.py](scripts/reviewers/r25/review_r25_subalgebras.py), [review_r25_table.py](scripts/reviewers/r25/review_r25_table.py); full report in [review_r25_report.md](agent_logs/review_r25_report.md)).

**CONFIRMED.** $\theta=\tfrac2n\sqrt{rN/(N-r)}$ and $S_{\mathfrak{mmm}}=(N-r)[2(n-2)-4(n-1)r/N]$, both exact on all twelve members to $10^{-15}$ and $10^{-13}$; hence $S_{\mathfrak{mmm}}\ge0\Rightarrow r\le n(n-2)/4\Rightarrow\theta\le\theta_0(n)$, algebraically airtight. Independently verified: $\kappa=2(n-2)$ at $n=3\ldots9$ identically for every index, $Q(\mathrm{Id})=(n-1)\mathrm{Id}$, and (the step that legitimises the block-scalar reduction), **$Q$ preserves the Weyl space** to $3.1\times10^{-16}$ at $n=5\ldots8$, with $\dim$ matching $n(n+1)(n+2)(n-3)/12$. The elimination was redone symbolically by hand and reproduces $S_{\mathfrak{hmm}}=2\sigma r(N-r)/N$ and $\theta=\sigma u r/(N-r)$.

**No counterexample exists.** Every candidate with $r>n(n-2)/4$ is killed by exactly one hypothesis, and all three are load-bearing: $\mathfrak g_2\subset\mathfrak{so}(7)$ ($r=14$ against a cap of $8.75$) and $\mathfrak{spin}(7)\subset\mathfrak{so}(8)$ ($r=21$ against $12$) satisfy (F1) and (F2) and die **only** on (F3); $\mathfrak{so}(n-1)\subset\mathfrak{so}(n)$ dies **only** on (F2); $\mathfrak u(m)$, $\mathfrak{su}(m)$, $\mathfrak{sp}(m)+\mathfrak{sp}(1)$ and the unbalanced splits die on (F1).

**Correction 1, $K$ is the full normaliser $N_{O(n)}(\mathfrak h)$, not the connected group.** With the connected group, $\mathfrak{so}(4)+\mathfrak{so}(4)\subset\mathfrak{so}(8)$ fails both (F1), each $\mathfrak{so}(4)$ splits self-dual $\oplus$ anti-self-dual, and (F3), the two block volume forms, so the equality case is **empty at $n=8$**. The reflections of $O(4)\times O(4)$ and the block swap restore $F1=F2=1$, $F3=0$.

**Correction 2, criticality is a consequence, not a hypothesis.** (F2) gives $\mathrm{Ric}(\Pi_{\mathfrak h})$ scalar hence $W_{\mathfrak h}$ Ricci-flat; (F3) gives $\mathrm{Alt}(W_{\mathfrak h})=0$; so $W_{\mathfrak h}$ is Weyl, $Q$ preserves that space, $Q(W_{\mathfrak h})$ is $K$-invariant and traceless, and that space is $\mathbb R\,W_{\mathfrak h}$.

**Correction 3, the equality case needs $n$ even, and two hypotheses were unstated**: $0<r<N$ (the elimination divides by $u$ and $v$) and $\mathfrak h\not\cong\mathfrak m$ as $K$-modules. For odd $n$, $n(n-2)/4$ is not an integer, so $\theta<\theta_0$ strictly, $0.828$ against $0.980$ at $n=5$, $1.027$ against $1.107$ at $n=7$, $1.127$ against $1.176$ at $n=9$.

**The scope verdict, and it deflates the result.** The slack has a closed form on the family, $S=\tfrac{r}{nN}[4(N-r)-n^2]$, verified against the definition on all twelve members with $\delta^2=0$ throughout. Since $4(N-r)-n^2\ge0\iff r\le n(n-2)/4\iff S_{\mathfrak{mmm}}\ge0$, **the dimension cap is the already-proven slack inequality restricted to this family**, a clean sum-of-squares re-derivation and a genuine cross-check, but not independent leverage. The family also has **no near-extremal member other than the extremum**: $\theta/\theta_0$ runs $1.000$ at the four balanced two-block products, then $0.845$, $0.730$, $0.671$, $0.655$, $0.612$, $0.598$, $0.507$, $0.471$ (a gap of more than $15\%$) and the ungraded frontier points that make the conjecture hard are absent entirely. Finally the maximum is unique **only within the family**: $W_{\mathbb{CP}^3}$ at $n=6$ is critical at residual $5.1\times10^{-16}$ with $\theta=1.054093=\theta_0(6)$ exactly, tying $S^3\times S^3$ from outside.

**One framing worth adopting.** The subalgebras that beat the cap are exactly the Berger special-holonomy list, $\mathfrak u(m)$, $\mathfrak{su}(m)$, $\mathfrak{sp}(m)+\mathfrak{sp}(1)$, $\mathfrak g_2$, $\mathfrak{spin}(7)$, each carrying a parallel $4$-form. So (F3) is not ad hoc: it says $\mathfrak h$ is not a special-holonomy algebra, and the theorem reads as the Lie-theoretic statement *an irreducible non-special-holonomy subalgebra of $\mathfrak{so}(n)$ has dimension at most $n(n-2)/4$, with equality only for the balanced split*. No statement of it was found in [references/](references/) or by search.

**Two non-members exceed $\theta_0$ and are harmless**: $\mathfrak{so}(3)+\mathfrak{so}(5)\subset\mathfrak{so}(8)$ at $1.151371$ and $\mathfrak{so}(4)+\mathfrak{so}(6)\subset\mathfrak{so}(10)$ at $1.203766$. Without (F2) the operator is not Ricci-flat ($\|\mathrm{Ric}\|=1.04$ and $0.93$), so it is not a Weyl operator, (F2) is what puts $W_{\mathfrak h}$ in the arena.
