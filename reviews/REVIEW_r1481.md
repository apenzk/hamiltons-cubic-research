## A. Statement fidelity of hasDerivWithinAt_defect_u

- **The competitor.** `uOp = rankSum lam6 vec` with `lam6 = (2, −1, −1, 0, 0, 0)/√6` on `vec δ = coords(pad ω_δ)/√2`. `vec_orthonormal` proves the six vectors orthonormal in `Biv n` (the layer's `inner2` gives $\lVert\omega_\delta\rVert^2=2$ by `inner2_om6`). So `uOp` has spectrum $(2,-1,-1)/\sqrt6$ on the span of $a,b,c$ and $0$ on its complement, and its Hilbert–Schmidt norm is $\sqrt{(4+1+1)/6}=1$. That is the record's unit padded competitor. The file does not state the unit norm as a theorem, which the identity does not need.
- **Self-dual triple.** `om6 0..2` are the layer's `bivA, bivB, bivC` ($\varphi_{01}+\varphi_{23}$, $\varphi_{02}+\varphi_{31}$, $\varphi_{03}+\varphi_{12}$), all three carrying the same orientation sign, so they span one of the two 3-spaces of $\Lambda^2\mathbb R^4$, and `om6 3..5` span the other. Since $u$ is supported on the first triple, the second is $\ker u\cap\Lambda^2\mathbb R^4$, which is the record's ASD space whichever orientation is chosen.
- **Tie to the layer.** `defectSq_u_eq` rewrites the layer's `defectSq lamU omU` through `defectSq_eq_of_orthogonal` with $c=2$, the rescaled presentation being `(2·lamU, omU/√2)`, and `hop` shows that rank sum is `uOp` term by term ($2\,\mathrm{lamU}_\gamma=\mathrm{lam6}_\gamma$ and `lam6 3..5 = 0`). This discharges [r1480](REVIEW_r1480.md)'s G1 (the rescaling 5c owed). `altNormSq_uNeg` then gives $\delta^2(u)=\tfrac19$ from `defect_u`, the record's value.
- **The function.** The derivative is of `t ↦ altNormSqOf(specFn negPartScalar (uOp + t•H))` within `Ici 0` at `0`, which is $\delta^2(u+tH)$ by the bridge [r1480](REVIEW_r1480.md) confirmed. `hW` is a provable proposition (sum of symmetric maps) carried as an argument, as in `hasDerivWithinAt_defect`.
- **The value.** $-2\cdot\tfrac1{3\sqrt6}\cdot\big(\langle v_1,Hv_1\rangle+\langle v_2,Hv_2\rangle+\sum_{\delta\in\{3,4,5\}}\langle v_\delta,(P_0HP_0)_-v_\delta\rangle\big)$, with $v_1=b/\sqrt2$, $v_2=c/\sqrt2$ the unit eigenvectors of $u$'s eigenvalue $-1/\sqrt6$, and $(P_0HP_0)_-$ = `specFn negPartScalar (schur zeroφ H)`, where `zeroφ a b = [a=0][b=0]` makes the Schur product $P_0HP_0$. The ASD sum over an orthonormal basis of the ASD 3-space is $\mathop{\mathrm{tr}}(\Pi_{ASD}(P_0HP_0)_-)$. This is exactly $-2aF(H)$.

- **Hand check, H = −u.** $H_{22}=H_{33}=+\tfrac1{\sqrt6}$, $P_0(-u)P_0=0$, so $F=\tfrac2{\sqrt6}$ and $-2aF=-\tfrac{4}{3\cdot6}=-\tfrac29$, matching $\delta^2((1-t)u)=(1-t)^2/9$. **H = u** gives $+\tfrac29$ and the record's $F(u)=-\tfrac2{\sqrt6}$ (line 83).
- **Hand check, a tangent direction.** $H=-v_3v_3^\top$ with $v_3=a'/\sqrt2$ is symmetric with $\langle H,u\rangle=0$. Here $u+tH$ has commuting, orthogonal parts, so $(u+tH)_-=u_-+tv_3v_3^\top$ exactly for $t\ge0$, and $\delta^2$ has slope $2\langle\mathrm{Alt}(u_-),\mathrm{Alt}(v_3v_3^\top)\rangle=2a\cdot s_3=-2a$, using the record's $\mathrm{Alt}(u_-)=a\star$ (checked independently in B at the $(0,1,2,3)$ component). ## B. pairing and alt4_uNeg

- `alt4 T ijkl = ⅓(T ijkl + T iklj + T iljk)`, and `compOf(rankSum lamNeg vec) ijkl = Σ_δ lamNeg_δ · ½ · ω_δ,ij ω_δ,kl` (`compOf_rankSum_vec`). The three cyclic products are $\omega_{ij}\omega_{kl}$, $\omega_{ik}\omega_{lj}$, $\omega_{il}\omega_{jk}$, exactly the pattern of `cyclic_eq_star`'s left side, over $\delta=1,2$ (the support of `lamNeg`). So `alt4_uNeg` is ⅓ · (1/√6) · ½ · LHS $=\tfrac1{6\sqrt6}\cdot$RHS. The integer identity is the right one.
- **Hand check of `cyclic_eq_star` at $(0,1,2,3)$.** $b$: $b_{01}b_{23}+b_{02}b_{31}+b_{03}b_{12}=0+(-1)(-1)+0=1$. $c$: $0+0+(-1)(-1)=1$. LHS $=2$. RHS: $a_{01}a_{23}=1$ against $a'_{01}a'_{23}=-1$, all other products $0$, so $1-(-1)=2$. Both sides are $2\varepsilon_{0123}$. With the normalisation this gives $\mathrm{Alt}(u_-)_{0123}=\tfrac2{6\sqrt6}=a$ and $\star_{0123}=\sum_\delta s_\delta\tfrac12\omega_{\delta,01}\omega_{\delta,23}=1$, so $\mathrm{Alt}(u_-)=a\star$ at that component.
- **Off-block cases.** The `by_cases` on "all four indices below 4" and the three nested `not_and_or` splits cover every pattern. In each branch the chosen `pad_zero` rewrites hit an index that appears in every product on both sides (each of the three cyclic products contains every one of $i,j,k,l$ exactly once per factor pair). The kernel checked the branches.
- **Non-symmetric X.** `pairing` uses `alt4_selfAdjoint` and `alt4_idem`, both stated for arbitrary four-index arrays, and `inner_coords_apply`, which expands $\langle\mathrm{coords}\,\Omega,X\,\mathrm{coords}\,\Omega\rangle$ for any linear $X$. No symmetry of $X$ enters. Constant: $\tfrac14\sum\omega\omega\,\mathrm{compOf}X=2\langle v,Xv\rangle$, so the pairing is $\tfrac1{6\sqrt6}\cdot2=\tfrac1{3\sqrt6}=a$ times $\sum s_\delta\langle v_\delta,Xv_\delta\rangle$. Correct.
- **Consistency check.** $X=u_-$ gives $a\cdot\tfrac2{\sqrt6}=\tfrac19=\delta^2(u)$, agreeing with `altNormSq_uNeg`.
