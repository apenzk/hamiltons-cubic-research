## A. Fidelity

- **A-1. The lift** ([L173Lift.lean:115](../lean/HamiltonsCubic/Results/L173Lift.lean)). `tLift cls t p q r` is `t (cls p) (cls q) (cls r)` when $q\ne p$, $r\ne p$ and $q\ne r$, else $0$. This is the $G$-invariant hollow tensor L104's proof pairs with a function on class-multisets ([bounds-relaxations.md:363](../findings/bounds-relaxations.md)), read in the direction L173 needs: from the ten value-type numbers to a tensor on $\binom{12}2=66$ indices. The class map `clsW` ([L173Lift.lean:131](../lean/HamiltonsCubic/Results/L173Lift.lean)) has sizes $(4,3,59)$ by `msz_clsW`, so the index-level spectrum is $\lambda_q=a\,\nu'_{\mathrm{cls}(q)}$ with the record's multiplicities. Right.
- **A-2. The count** ([L173Lift.lean:54](../lean/HamiltonsCubic/Results/L173Lift.lean), line 87). For $p$ in class $i$, the ordered pairs $(q,r)$ with $q,r,p$ pairwise distinct and classes $(j,k)$ number $\mu_j\mu_k$ when $j\ne k$ and $\mu_j(\mu_j-1)$ when $j=k$, with $\mu_j=m_j-\delta_{ij}$, which checks by hand. `wC` ([L173Lift.lean:34](../lean/HamiltonsCubic/Results/L173Lift.lean)) is that expression and `wC_clsW` identifies it with L173Statement's `w` ([L173Statement.lean:148](../lean/HamiltonsCubic/Results/L173Statement.lean)) at `clsW`. Summed over ordered $(j,k)$, $\mu_j\mu_k$ counted twice for $j\ne k$ reproduces L104's $\sum_{j<k}2\mu_j\mu_k$. The proof through `inner_count` and the split at $q=p$ is honest.
- **A-3. The three relations** ([L173Lift.lean:143](../lean/HamiltonsCubic/Results/L173Lift.lean) to line 146). (A) is $\sum_{q,r}T_{pqr}=20=2(n-2)$ at $n=12$, the ordered-pair reading of [bounds.md:264](../findings/bounds.md), and (C) is $\sum_{q,r}\nu'_q\nu'_rT_{pqr}=2\nu'_p(7-\nu'_p)$, both in the record's form in the scaled variable L173Statement fixes ($\theta'=\theta/a=7$). **Gap A-3.** (B) is typed as $\sum_{q,r}T_{pqr}(\nu'_q+\nu'_r)/2=-2\nu'_p$, while the record's (B) is $\sum_{\beta\gamma}\lambda_\beta T_{k\beta\gamma}=-2\lambda_k$. The two agree because `tLift clsW T p q r = tLift clsW T p r q`, which holds by `T_symm`'s second conjunct and the symmetric guard, and that is not a Lean statement. The (B) conjunct at [L173Lift.lean:145](../lean/HamiltonsCubic/Results/L173Lift.lean) is therefore the record's (B) only after a by-hand step. L173Statement's class-level `relBlhs` uses the same half-sum, which r757 and r758 accepted at the class level where `T_symm` is proved alongside it.
- **A-4. The pair cap** ([L173Lift.lean:178](../lean/HamiltonsCubic/Results/L173Lift.lean)). For $q\ne p$, $\sum_rT_{pqr}$ equals `capLhs (clsW p) (clsW q)`, whose coefficient $m_k-\delta_{ik}-\delta_{jk}$ is L104's $\mu^{(ij)}_k$, and `caps_hold` bounds it by $2$. At $q=p$ every term is $0$ by the guard, so the record's $\sum_cT_{abc}\le2$ holds at every pair. The hypothesis $q\ne p$ is the only case with content.
- **A-5. The statement.** L173 asserts a totally symmetric hollow $T\ge0$ ([bounds-relaxations.md:555](../findings/bounds-relaxations.md)). Hollowness of `tLift` is the guard itself. Total symmetry needs `T_symm` plus the guard's invariance under the six permutations of $(p,q,r)$, and nonnegativity is `T_nonneg` at the guard's true branch. **Gap A-5.** None of the three is stated for `tLift clsW T`, and the L173 row reads "proved" on `witness_index_level` and `witness_cap` alone ([lean/INDEX.md:231](../lean/INDEX.md)). Each is a short lemma, so this is a record gap, not a mathematical one, but with A-3 the symmetry is load-bearing.
- **A-6. The move to proved.** L173's content is that the witness satisfies the relaxation and that $\theta=7a>\theta_0(12)$, the latter typed as `theta_closed_form` and `theta_exceeds_target`. That (A), (B), (C) hold at critical points is an input the lemma does not assert, and the record says so: *"the lemma needs only soundness of the relaxation, never realizability of the witness"* ([bounds-relaxations.md:563](../findings/bounds-relaxations.md)). So typing the relations as definitions and calling L173 proved is right, once A-5 is closed. The quoted addenda (non-realizability, the family at every $n\ge8$, exact maximality) sit outside the Statement and the row does not claim them.
