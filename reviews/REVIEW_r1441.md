## A. L120

Quoted from [findings/second-order.md](../findings/second-order.md), line 975: "*and at every diagonal $M$, criticality is unused; $\Gamma$-equivariance needs only that $M$ is $\Gamma$-fixed, the operator $L_M$ preserves each summand.*" Line 981: "*The equivariance route also works, but only with the full sign group, and it is machine-checked: `L120_block_diag` in L120Block.lean, $L_M$ preserving every sign-isotypic subspace at a diagonal $M$.*"

**The action and the isotypic subspace.** `act g R` is $Z\mapsto gR(g^{\mathsf T}Zg)g^{\mathsf T}$, which on $\Lambda^2$ is the $O(n)$ action on curvature operators. $\Gamma=\{\operatorname{diag}(\varepsilon):\varepsilon_i=\pm1\}$ is `signMat ε` with `ε i * ε i = 1`, and `signMat_orth` gives $g^{\mathsf T}g=gg^{\mathsf T}=1$. `hX`, $\texttt{act}(\operatorname{diag}\varepsilon)X=c(\varepsilon)X$ for every $\varepsilon$, is membership of $X$ in the isotypic subspace of the character $c$. Here $c$ is any function. When $X\ne0$ it is forced to be a character, so nothing is lost. The full sign group is used, the one the record says the argument needs.

**The conclusion.** `L120_block` states $g\,(M\circ X)(g^{\mathsf T}Zg)\,g^{\mathsf T}=c(\varepsilon)\,(M\circ X)(Z)$ for every $Z$. That is `act (signMat ε) (M ∘ X) = c ε • (M ∘ X)` unfolded through `act_apply`, so $M\circ X$ lies in the same isotypic subspace. The proof is `foxProd_act` plus `hM` and `hX` plus bilinearity (`foxProd_smul_right`). `hM` (at `L120_block`) and `hε` are used, and `hX` is used at the given $\varepsilon$.
