## What I derived before opening the section

Writing $\varphi(x)=(x-\nu_r)^2=\nu_r^2-2\nu_rx+x^2$, the multiplier weights are $(y^A,y^B,y^C)=(\nu_r^2,-2\nu_r,1)$, and $\psi(x,y)=y^A+y^B(x+y)/2+y^Cxy=\nu_r^2-\nu_r(x+y)+xy=(x-\nu_r)(y-\nu_r)$, which does satisfy $\psi(x,x)=\varphi(x)$. Reading the three reduced rows of [Lemma L104](../findings/bounds-relaxations.md) off their displayed forms, the coefficient of $t_{ijk}$ in $(\mathrm A_i)$ is $2\mu_j\mu_k$ for $j<k$ and $\mu_j(\mu_j-1)$ for $j=k$; in $(\mathrm B_i)$ it is $\mu_j\mu_k(\nu_j+\nu_k)=2\mu_j\mu_k\cdot\frac{\nu_j+\nu_k}2$ and $\mu_j(\mu_j-1)\nu_j$; in $(\mathrm C_i)$ it is $2\mu_j\mu_k\nu_j\nu_k$ and $\mu_j(\mu_j-1)\nu_j^2$. So each row's coefficient is (orbit count) times the corresponding monomial in $\psi$, and the composite coefficient is (orbit count) times $\psi(\nu_j,\nu_k)$ exactly, with the $j=k$ case falling out because $\psi(\nu_j,\nu_j)=\varphi(\nu_j)$. The brief's description of the multiplier calculus is therefore correct as stated, and the right-hand side is $y^A\cdot2(n-2)+y^B\cdot(-2\nu_i)+y^C\cdot2\nu_i(\theta-\nu_i)=2\big[(n-2)\nu_r^2+2\nu_r\nu_i+\nu_i\theta-\nu_i^2\big]$.

I checked the calculus against a case the record already holds before using it on a new one: the mixed root $\varphi(x)=(x-\nu_1)(x-\nu_2)$ at row $1$ reproduces, term by term including every sign, the display in [DERIVE-sp3c](DERIVE-sp3c.md)'s addendum, $-\mu_1\mu_2(\nu_1-\nu_2)^2t_{112}-\mu_1\mu_3(\nu_1-\nu_2)(\nu_1-\nu_3)t_{113}+\mu_2\mu_3(\nu_1-\nu_2)(\nu_2-\nu_3)t_{123}+\mu_3(\mu_3-1)(\nu_1-\nu_3)(\nu_2-\nu_3)t_{133}=2\nu_1[(n-1)\nu_2+\theta]$, so the polarisation convention I am using is the record's.

## R1, CONFIRMED, with one admissibility caveat that only strengthens the claim

Row $i$ carries exactly six types, the multisets $\{i,j,k\}$ over the six unordered pairs $jk$ drawn from $\{1,2,3\}$. The three pairs containing $r$ are annihilated, since $\psi(\nu_j,\nu_k)=(\nu_j-\nu_r)(\nu_k-\nu_r)$ vanishes iff $j=r$ or $k=r$ and the $\nu$ are distinct; the three pairs drawn from the two-element set $\{1,2,3\}\setminus\{r\}$ survive. That is $6-3=3$ in every one of the nine row-root combinations, with no exception, because the count $3=\binom{2+1}{2}$ never depends on which row or which root is taken. Enumerating them gives nine *distinct* three-subsets, all nine listed correctly by the record's construction: $\{122,123,133\}$, $\{111,113,133\}$, $\{111,112,122\}$, $\{222,223,233\}$, $\{112,123,233\}$, $\{112,122,222\}$, $\{223,233,333\}$, $\{113,133,333\}$, $\{113,123,223\}$ for $(i,r)=(1,1),(1,2),(1,3),(2,1),(2,2),(2,3),(3,1),(3,2),(3,3)$. The three with $r=i$ are exactly the three carrying the clean right-hand side, so "three of the nine" is right.

**The one caveat.** The count $3$ presumes all ten types admissible, i.e. every $m_i\ge3$, the same hypothesis the ten-variable, dimension-four setting already carries from [Lemma L104](../findings/bounds-relaxations.md), whose own admissibility paragraph makes it load-bearing. If some $m_i=2$ the type $\{iii\}$ does not exist and the two rows $(i,r)$ with $r\ne i$ lose it, leaving **two** survivors and forbidding a *pair* rather than a triple, a strictly stronger cut, so no row-root combination degenerates in the weakening direction. The three $r=i$ identities are untouched by this, needing only $m_j,m_k\ge2$ for the two classes other than $i$. The claim is therefore safe, and the caveat is worth writing into the section because the ten-variable framing is stated without it here.

**Screen.** A symbolic expansion of $\nu_r^2(\mathrm A_i)-2\nu_r(\mathrm B_i)+(\mathrm C_i)$ over all nine $(i,r)$, with $\mu_j=m_j-\delta_{ij}$ symbolic, returns coefficient $=$ (orbit count) $\times(\nu_j-\nu_r)(\nu_k-\nu_r)$ in all $54$ type slots and the predicted right-hand side in all nine, with the survivor triples as listed. This screens my hand derivation; it is not offered as evidence for the identity, which is proved above.

## R2, CONFIRMED

The logic is sound and slightly stronger than the record states. The annihilated types have coefficient identically zero, so their $t$ values never enter; the identity restricted to a row therefore reads (three nonzero coefficients) $\cdot\,t=\mathrm{RHS}$ with no residue. If those three $t$ vanish the left side is $0$ exactly, so $\mathrm{RHS}=0$; contrapositively $\mathrm{RHS}\ne0$ forbids the three-subset. Note this forbids *every* vanishing set containing the three-subset, not merely the four-element ones, which matters, because [DERIVE-sp3c](DERIVE-sp3c.md) records that a vertex has *at least* four zeros and that an enumeration built on exactly four cannot see the degenerate ones. The three-subset form of the cut is immune to that objection in a way the first family's four-subset form was not, and that is a point in the section's favour which it does not currently make.

The count of seven is right: a four-subset containing a fixed three-subset is determined by its fourth element, chosen from the $10-3=7$ remaining types.

## R3, REFUTED as stated; the arithmetic is right and the scoping is not

**The arithmetic.** At $r=i$ the right-hand side is $2[(n-2)\nu_i^2+2\nu_i^2+\nu_i\theta-\nu_i^2]=2[(n-1)\nu_i^2+\nu_i\theta]=2\nu_i[(n-1)\nu_i+\theta]$, exactly as claimed, in all three cases. Confirmed by hand and by the screen.

**Row $\nu_1$, unconditional, and by less than the record spends.** $\nu_1=\lambda_{\max}>0$ at every nonzero traceless $W$, with no kernel-freeness hypothesis needed; with $\theta>0$ the bracket $(n-1)\nu_1+\theta$ is strictly positive. So $\{122,123,133\}$ is forbidden using only $\theta>0$. The record's "on a kernel-free spectrum" is over-strong here.

**Row $\nu_2$, NOT unconditional.** Nothing in the $d=3$ setting forces $\nu_2>0$. The setting fixes $\nu_1>\nu_2>\nu_3$ and tracelessness gives $\nu_1>0>\nu_3$, but $\nu_2<0$ is a live sign pattern: it means the negative eigenspace has dimension $m_2+m_3\ge6$, so [Lemma L35](../findings/stratum-two-negative.md) (which caps $\theta$ only when the negative eigenspace has dimension at most two), does not touch it, and I found nothing else on the record that excludes it. With $\nu_2<0$ the right-hand side $2\nu_2[(n-1)\nu_2+\theta]$ vanishes exactly at $\theta=(n-1)\lvert\nu_2\rvert$, which is **strictly inside** the $\Pi$-bound $\theta\le(n-1)\lvert\nu_3\rvert$ of [Lemma L107](../findings/bounds.md), since $\lvert\nu_2\rvert<\lvert\nu_3\rvert$. So the degeneracy is not excluded by $\Pi$-strictness and the cut fails there.

**This is a regression, not a new discovery.** [DERIVE-sp3c](DERIVE-sp3c.md) already carries the correction as its recorded error (ii): *"Vanishing exactly at $\Pi$-saturation is false when $\nu_2<0$, where the degeneracy sits at $\theta=(n-1)\lvert\nu_2\rvert$, strictly inside the bound."* The double-root section restates the corrected form of the slip on a new family. Under the brief's own record-discipline clause this is the failure mode it names.

**The repair, and it is cheap.** Add the hypothesis $\theta\ne(n-1)\lvert\nu_2\rvert$, automatic when $\nu_2>0$, and the exact analogue of the $\Pi$-strictness already assumed at $\nu_3$. Call it *second-$\Pi$-strictness*. It repairs, in one clause, four separate items that all degenerate at the same point: the $\nu_2$ double-root cut, the two clean four-subsets $\{112,113,123,133\}$ and $\{113,123,133,233\}$ whose right-hand sides carry $(n-1)\nu_2+\theta$, and the §G3 pair cut of [DERIVE-sp3c](DERIVE-sp3c.md), whose right-hand side is $2\nu_1[(n-1)\nu_2+\theta]$ and which was derived under the explicit hypothesis $\nu_1>\nu_2>0>\nu_3$. Recommended wording for the table: the $\nu_1$ row *unconditional*, the $\nu_2$ row *unless second-$\Pi$ attained*, the $\nu_3$ row *unless $\Pi$ attained*, three tiers, not two.

**Row $\nu_3$, correct as stated.** $\nu_3<0$ always, so $\nu_3\ne0$ needs no hypothesis, and the vanishing condition $\theta=(n-1)\lvert\nu_3\rvert$ is $\Pi$-saturation exactly.

## R4, CONFIRMED, with the credit misassigned

I recounted the chain from scratch. Cut one removes the six clean four-subsets, $210-6=204$. Cut two is [DERIVE-sp3c](DERIVE-sp3c.md)'s §G3 pair cut, forbidding every four-subset containing both $123$ and $133$: there are $\binom82=28$ of them, of which three ($\{112,113,123,133\}$, $\{123,133,223,233\}$, $\{113,123,133,233\}$), are already among the six, so $28-3=25$ are new and $204-25=179$. Cut three: the four-subsets containing $\{122,123,133\}$ all contain $\{123,133\}$, so **all seven were already removed** and this three-subset contributes **nothing**; the four-subsets containing $\{112,123,233\}$ number seven, of which one ($+133$) contains $\{123,133\}$ and none is among the six, so six are new and $179-6=173$. Every figure reproduces, and the combinatorial screen agrees: $204$, $179$, $173$, and $130$ under all nine.

**The finding this recount produces.** The two "unconditional" three-subsets do not split the work, the truly unconditional one does *none* of it, and every one of the six removals comes from the $\nu_2$ one, which is the one that is not unconditional. So the honest figures are: **$179$ under $\{\theta>0$, kernel-free, $\Pi$ strict$\}$, and $173$ only after adding second-$\Pi$-strictness**, which the $179$ itself also needs, via the §G3 pair cut, so the *chain* is internally consistent and it is the labelling that is wrong. Restarting the count on the $\nu_2<0$ stratum with only the genuinely unconditional cuts available gives $187$, or $178$ with $\Pi$-strictness at $\nu_3$; those are the figures that hold with no sign assumption on $\nu_2$.

**The control.** $\{113,123,223,333\}$ contains none of $\{122,123,133\}$, $\{112,123,233\}$ or $\{123,133\}$ and is none of the six, so it survives the unconditional cut, as the record claims and as the control requires. But it **does** contain the third three-subset $\{113,123,223\}$, so it is *not* in the $130$, correctly, because that three-subset's escape clause is $\Pi$-saturation, which is exactly where the split symmetric pair sits. This is a genuine internal-consistency success and the section should say so: **$130$ is a count valid only strictly below $\Pi$-saturation, and the conjectured extremal configuration is one of the $43$ it removes.** As written, "all nine would take it to $130$" reads as an unqualified improvement on $173$; it is not, and a successor who reaches $130$ and then looks for the extremum inside it will not find it.

**One caveat carried forward, from [post F43](../findings/bounds-relaxations.md) rather than from me.** These are counts of four-subsets in $\Pi(\lambda)$, which drops the proved pair cap; restoring the cap breaks the four-zeros vertex count. So $173$ bounds a candidate list, not $\theta$, and the section is right to call it a cut rather than a bound.

## R5, REFUTED. The check could not have failed on any claim the section makes

**The identity is criticality, rewritten.** [bounds.md](../findings/bounds.md) settles this in its own words: $(\mathrm A)$ is the Killing marginal and $(\mathrm B)$ is $W\#I=-W$, *"both holding on all of $\mathcal W_n$"*, published as [Böhm–Wilking](../references/boehm-wilking-2008-space-forms/notes.md) Lemma 2.1, while *"$(\mathrm C)$ is the diagonal of $W^\#=\theta W-W^2$ and holds only at critical points."* The double-root identity at row $a$ is $\nu_r^2(\mathrm A_a)-2\nu_r(\mathrm B_a)+(\mathrm C_a)$. The $(\mathrm C)$ weight is $y^C=1\ne0$, so the map from $\{(\mathrm A_a),(\mathrm B_a),(\mathrm C_a)\}$ to $\{(\mathrm A_a),(\mathrm B_a),\text{identity}\}$ is invertible: **the identity carries exactly the content of criticality at row $a$, no more and no less.** It holds at every $\theta$-critical Weyl tensor, at every $n$, every $d$, every spectrum, every sign pattern, every root $\nu_r$, and indeed at every *real number* $\nu_r$, whether or not it is an eigenvalue.

**So the three test points test criticality.** $S^3\times S^4$, $S^3\times S^5$ and $S^4\times S^5$ were reported as critical with residual $\le4\times10^{-16}$; the identity's residual at $10^{-9}$ is that same number, propagated through a fixed linear map, in different coordinates. The record measured the same quantity twice and read the second as corroboration of the first's *interpretation*. This is the shape the record itself already named three paragraphs earlier in the same file, in the Jacobi-contraction section: *"the collapse comes from relation (A)'s $g$-independence, and what comes out follows from (B) at every Weyl tensor, so the four sphere-product checks could not have failed, whatever the claim."* The double-root check is the same construction with $(\mathrm C)$ included instead of excluded.

**What the check *could* have caught, exhaustively.** (i) A transcription slip in $\psi$, a wrong sign or factor in $(y^A,y^B,y^C)$, which would break the identity at generic $(\nu,\theta)$. (ii) A slip in the closed-form right-hand side $2[(n-2)\nu_r^2+2\nu_r\nu_i+\nu_i\theta-\nu_i^2]$, *provided* the two sides were computed by independent routes rather than by the same expression. (iii) The orbit counts $\mu_j\mu_k$ and $\mu_j(\mu_j-1)$, and with them the class-constancy of $T$ at the test points, *but only if* the left side was assembled from the three reduced class variables rather than summed over all index pairs. That is the whole list, and every item is a one-line algebraic check on three published relations.

**What it had no power over, which is everything the section asserts.** The three-subset forbidding logic; the sign reasoning that makes a right-hand side nonzero; the $\nu_2>0$ scoping defect found under R3, which is a statement about which spectra exist and cannot be probed by evaluating an identity that holds at all of them; the counts $179\to173\to130$; the vertex reading; the relation to $\Pi$-saturation. **A numerical evaluation of an identity cannot test a hypothesis under which the identity is not being applied.** Had the $\nu_2$ row been labelled unconditional in error (which it was), three points agreeing to $10^{-9}$ would have looked exactly the same.

**Two further weaknesses in the check as reported.** The three points are one family (unbalanced sphere products, all symmetric spaces, all diagonal, all $S=0$), at $n=7,8,9$, below the $n\ge12$ regime the conjecture concerns; even a check with real power would have sampled one orbit type. And no run script for it is committed: `grep` over [scripts/](../scripts/) finds no reference to the double-root family anywhere, while the neighbouring enumeration has [run_jacobi_closure_d3.py](../scripts/runs/run_jacobi_closure_d3.py) on file. A reader cannot determine which of the three failure modes above was live, because the artefact that would say is not in the record.

**Where this leaves the section.** The identity is *correct*, I derived it independently, by hand and by symbolic screen, and it needs no numerical support at all, as the section's own "needs no check beyond one line" paragraph already says of the annihilation. The defect is the sentence claiming the check discriminates: *"the check is against the definition of record rather than against the code that produced the formula, the two failures this record carries from earlier in the same session."* It does not repair those two failures; it reproduces them. **Recommended: withdraw the verification paragraph, or restate it as what it is, a transcription check on a one-line algebraic combination, with the note that the identity is a consequence of $(\mathrm A)$, $(\mathrm B)$, $(\mathrm C)$ and could not have failed at any critical point.**

## Offered to the section, two of the six quadratic right-hand sides are sign-determined, using no hypothesis on $\nu_2$

The section leaves the six $r\ne i$ right-hand sides as "quadratic conditions in $(\nu,\theta)$, not analysed here". Two of them admit a one-line sign argument from tools already on the record, and neither mentions $\nu_2$, so both survive the R3 defect intact. Write $s=\nu_1>0$ and $m=\lvert\nu_3\rvert>0$.

**At $(i,r)=(3,1)$**, the three-subset is $\{223,233,333\}$ and the right-hand side is $2[(n-2)s^2-2sm-m^2-m\theta]$. The $\Pi$-bound $\theta\le(n-1)m$ ([Lemma L107](../findings/bounds.md)) gives $-m\theta\ge-(n-1)m^2$, so it is at least $2[(n-2)s^2-2sm-nm^2]$, whose positive root in $s/m$ is $[1+\sqrt{1+n(n-2)}]/(n-2)=n/(n-2)$. **So $\nu_1>\frac n{n-2}\lvert\nu_3\rvert$ forbids $\{223,233,333\}$.** The threshold being exactly the balanced product's ratio $u/w=n/(n-2)$ is a reassuring degeneracy: at the balanced ratio with $\Pi$ attained, the right-hand side is zero on the nose.

**At $(i,r)=(1,3)$**, the three-subset is $\{111,112,122\}$ and the right-hand side is $2[(n-2)m^2-2sm-s^2+s\theta]$. With $\theta>0$ it is at least $2[(n-2)m^2-2sm-s^2]$, whose positive root in $s/m$ is $\sqrt{n-1}-1$. **So $\nu_1<(\sqrt{n-1}-1)\lvert\nu_3\rvert$ forbids $\{111,112,122\}$.**

**The two ranges overlap for every $n\ge7$**, since $\sqrt{n-1}-1>n/(n-2)$ there, $2.317>1.2$ at $n=12$, and $\nu_1/\lvert\nu_3\rvert$ is confined to $(0,n-2]$ by [Lemma L73](../findings/bounds.md). **Hence at every $d=3$ spectrum with $n\ge12$, at least one of $\{111,112,122\}$ and $\{223,233,333\}$ is forbidden, whatever the sign of $\nu_2$.** This does not lower $173$, which is a spectrum-uniform count, but the LP it feeds is posed *at a fixed spectrum*, which is the currency that matters: the per-spectrum candidate list drops from $173$ to at most $166$, and to $159$ where both conditions hold. I mark this **PLAUSIBLE rather than proved** on one point only, I have derived it in full above, but it rests on the $\Pi$-bound and Lemma L73 being applicable at $d=3$ without further hypothesis, which I read off their statements rather than re-deriving. Everything else in it is elementary.

## Summary of what the section should change

The identity, the nine three-subsets, the seven-fold consequence and the counts $179$, $173$, $130$ all stand and are reproduced here independently. Three things need editing: the $\nu_2$ row is **not** unconditional and needs second-$\Pi$-strictness, which is the correction [DERIVE-sp3c](DERIVE-sp3c.md) already recorded once and this section reintroduced; the credit for the $179\to173$ cut belongs entirely to that conditional identity, the unconditional one contributing zero; and the verification paragraph claims discrimination the check cannot have. The last is the one that matters most, because the section presents the check as the answer to the session's two prior vacuity failures, and it is a third instance of them.
