## A. L70Attain against L70's attainment sentence

**The record** (bounds.md line 224): saturation forces $\dim E_{\min}\ge n/2$ and $\dim E_{\max}\ge(n-2)/2$, "and both are attained exactly at $W_{\mathbb{CP}^2}$ at $n=4$, where they read $2$ and $1$".

**`WCP2` is the record's $W_{\mathbb{CP}^2}$.** The pair basis is `basis4` $=(\varphi_{01},\varphi_{02},\varphi_{03},\varphi_{12},\varphi_{13},\varphi_{23})$, each $\varphi_{ij}$ of `inner2` norm $\tfrac12\cdot2=1$ and pairwise orthogonal, so the matrix of a self-adjoint operator in it is symmetric with the operator's spectrum. With $a=(1,0,0,0,0,1)$, $b=(0,1,0,0,-1,0)$, $c=(0,0,1,1,0,0)$:
- $aa^{\mathsf T}$ puts $1$ at $(0,0),(0,5),(5,0),(5,5)$.
- $-\tfrac12bb^{\mathsf T}$ puts $-\tfrac12$ at $(1,1),(4,4)$ and $+\tfrac12$ at $(1,4),(4,1)$.
- $-\tfrac12cc^{\mathsf T}$ puts $-\tfrac12$ at $(2,2),(2,3),(3,2),(3,3)$.

That is `WCP2` entry for entry. $a,b,c$ are orthogonal of norm $\sqrt2$, so the spectrum is $2$ on $a$, $-1$ on $b,c$, $0$ on the orthogonal complement. With orientation $e_{0123}$, $\ast e_{01}=e_{23}$, $\ast e_{02}=-e_{13}$, $\ast e_{03}=e_{12}$, so $a,b,c$ span $\Lambda^+$ and the operator vanishes on $\Lambda^-$. `WCP2_normSq` gives $\lVert W\rVert^2=6$, and dividing by $\sqrt6$ gives the record's unit spectrum $(2,-1,-1)/\sqrt6$. **Up to scale, it is the record's object**, and eigenspace dimensions are scale-invariant.

**`WCP2_eqs`**: row $i$ of `WCP2` against $x$, read off the matrix. Correct.

**`eigen_minus_span`, by hand.** $Wx=-x$: rows 0 and 5 give $2x_0+x_5=0$ and $x_0+2x_5=0$, so $x_0=x_5=0$. Row 1 gives $\tfrac12x_1+\tfrac12x_4=0$, so $x_4=-x_1$ (row 4 agrees). Row 2 gives $x_3=x_2$ (row 3 agrees). So $x=x_1(0,1,0,0,-1,0)+x_2(0,0,1,1,0,0)=x_1v_1+x_2v_2$. **`eigen_plus_span`, by hand.** $Wx=2x$: rows 0 and 5 give $x_5=x_0$. Rows 1 and 4 give $x_4=5x_1$ and $x_1=5x_4$, so $x_1=25x_1=0$. Rows 2 and 3 give $x_3=-5x_2$ and $x_2=-5x_3$, so $x_2=0$. So $x=x_0\,(1,0,0,0,0,1)$, and `WCP2_eigen_plus` shows the line is an eigenline. $E_2$ is exactly a line.

**`WCP2_spectrum`.** The six `linear_combination` certificates are polynomial identities, for instance row 0: $(\mu+1)(\mu-1)(x_0+x_5-\mu x_0)+(\mu+1)(x_0+x_5-\mu x_5)$ has $x_5$ coefficient $(\mu+1)(\mu-1)+(\mu+1)(1-\mu)=0$ and $x_0$ coefficient $(\mu+1)\big[(\mu-1)(1-\mu)+1\big]=(\mu+1)(2\mu-\mu^2)=-\mu(\mu+1)(\mu-2)$, which is the target exactly (each equation entering as $\mu x_i-(Wx)_i$). ## C. L107Chord against the chord paragraph

**The record** (bounds.md lines 573 to 577): $T$ vanishes on repeated indices, so in row $a$ the variable $X=\tfrac{\lambda_b+\lambda_c}2$ ranges over means of two distinct eigenvalues away from $a$, inside $[\ell_a,\sigma_a]$, the means of the two smallest and the two largest eigenvalues over indices $\ne a$. Then $\theta\le\lambda_{\max}-\ell_a-\sigma_a-(n-2)\ell_a\sigma_a/\lambda_{\max}$ at $a=\arg\max$, dominating the $\Pi$ display unconditionally.

**The hypothesis is implied by the record's definition.** `cst v γ a b` $=\langle[v_\gamma,v_a],v_b\rangle$. It vanishes at $a=\gamma$ since $[v_\gamma,v_\gamma]=0$, at $b=\gamma$ since $\langle[v_\gamma,v_a],v_\gamma\rangle=-\langle v_a,[v_\gamma,v_\gamma]\rangle=0$ by ad-invariance, and at $a=b$ since $\langle[v_\gamma,v_a],v_a\rangle=-\langle v_\gamma,[v_a,v_a]\rangle=0$. So a pair carrying mass has $a\ne b$, both $\ne\gamma$, and its mean lies between the mean of the two smallest and the mean of the two largest eigenvalues over indices $\ne\gamma$. `hsupp` holds at $\ell=\ell_\gamma$, $\sigma=\sigma_\gamma$. **Implied, and the Lean form is the weaker hypothesis** (any interval holding the support means), so the theorem widens the record's.

**`L107_chord_row`, by hand.** Pointwise on the support, $\lambda_a\lambda_b\le X^2\le(\ell+\sigma)X-\ell\sigma$ from $(X-\ell)(\sigma-X)\ge0$, times $c^2\ge0$. Summing: $\sum c^2=2(n-2)$ (row A, `killing_frame`), $\sum_{ab}\tfrac{\lambda_a+\lambda_b}2c^2=-2\lambda_\gamma$ (row B, `aW2_frame`, `aW2_mid`), and $\sum\lambda_a\lambda_bc^2=2\lambda_\gamma(\theta-\lambda_\gamma)$ (row C, `crit_frame`). So $2\lambda_\gamma(\theta-\lambda_\gamma)\le-2(\ell+\sigma)\lambda_\gamma-2(n-2)\ell\sigma$, the stated row. It is the record's display with $\lambda_{\min},\lambda_{\max}$ replaced by $\ell,\sigma$.

**`L107_chord`.** At $\lambda_\gamma=M>0$, divide by $M$: $\theta\le M-\ell-\sigma-(n-2)\ell\sigma/M$. **Verbatim the record's display**, $n$ being `Fintype.card ι`.

**`chord_dominates` is the record's domination claim.** The record's reason: for nested intervals the difference of the chords is affine and nonnegative at the endpoints of the smaller. By hand, $\big[(m+M)X-mM\big]-\big[(\ell+\sigma)X-\ell\sigma\big]$ is affine in $X$, equal to $(\ell-m)(M-\ell)$ at $X=\ell$ and $(\sigma-m)(M-\sigma)$ at $X=\sigma$, both $\ge0$ when $m\le\ell\le\sigma\le M$. Integrated against $c^2$ with rows (A) and (B) this is the stated row-level comparison, and at $\lambda_\gamma=M>0$ dividing by $M$ gives the comparison of the two $\theta$ bounds. The Lean form is the row level, one division short of the $\theta$ level, and the division is by a positive number. "Unconditionally" is right: at the record's $m=\lambda_{\min}$, $M=\lambda_{\max}$, the side conditions $m\le\ell_a$, $\sigma_a\le M$ always hold.

**Padded $W_{\mathbb{CP}^2}$, by hand.** Spectrum $(2\varepsilon,-\varepsilon,-\varepsilon,0,\dots,0)$ on $N=\binom n2$ entries, top row $a$ at $2\varepsilon$.
- $n=4$: the other entries are $-\varepsilon,-\varepsilon,0,0,0$. Two smallest $-\varepsilon,-\varepsilon$, so $\ell_a=-\varepsilon$. Two largest $0,0$, so $\sigma_a=0$.
- $n\ge5$: the other entries are $-\varepsilon,-\varepsilon$ and $N-3\ge7$ zeros, the same $\ell_a=-\varepsilon$, $\sigma_a=0$.
- The bound is $2\varepsilon+\varepsilon-0-0=3\varepsilon$ at every $n$, and $\lVert W\rVert^2=6\varepsilon^2=1$ gives $3\varepsilon=3/\sqrt6=\sqrt{3/2}$.
- Cross-check on the actual support: $v_a=\omega_1$ brackets $\omega_2$ to $\omega_3$ (mean $-\varepsilon$) and, at $n\ge5$, mixed bivectors to mixed ones (mean $0$), so $[\ell,\sigma]=[-\varepsilon,0]$ is the tightest interval at $n\ge5$. At $n=4$ the support is $\{(\omega_2,\omega_3)\}$ alone, and $[-\varepsilon,-\varepsilon]$ also returns $4\varepsilon-2\varepsilon^2/(2\varepsilon)=3\varepsilon$.
