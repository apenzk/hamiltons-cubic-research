**One-line summary: CONFIRMED WITH GAP.** `L52_matrix_bound` is L52's display for any two real symmetric matrices under exactly the record's spectral bounds and normalisations, with the range condition derived and the standard three axioms, and the gaps are an Owed list still missing the two norm identities and L50's trace-to-spectrum bridge, a Karamata attribution that fits only the "only if" half, and an L54Statement sentence that makes $\mu=d_\varphi$ wait on $\mathring W$.

## 1. The theorem against the display

The record's display is $\theta\le\tfrac32[p_3(K)+3M_+(1-p_2(K))]-\tfrac12[p_3(K')+3m(p_2(K')-3)]$ with $\theta=\tfrac32p_3(\mu)-\tfrac12p_3(\omega)$ by L50. The elaborated type of `L52_matrix_bound` has left side `3/2 * ∑ j, hA.eigenvalues j ^ 3 - 1/2 * ∑ j, hB.eigenvalues j ^ 3` and right side as in the table, so the two agree once $K$ is read as the diagonal of `A` and $K'$ as the diagonal of `B`. Mathlib's `IsHermitian.eigenvalues` is indexed by the eigenvector basis, so each spectrum is counted with multiplicity and the index sets of diagonal and spectrum coincide, which Jensen row by row needs.

The proof is the record's own. Concavity of $h(t)=t^3-3M_+t^2$ with $K=P\mu$ gives $\sum h(\mu_j)\le\sum h(K_i)$, which with $\sum\mu^2=1$ is $p_3(\mu)\le p_3(K)+3M_+(1-p_2(K))$. Convexity of $g(t)=t^3+3mt^2$ with $K'=Q\omega$ gives $\sum g(K'_i)\le\sum g(\omega_j)$, which with $\sum\omega^2=3$ is $p_3(\omega)\ge p_3(K')+3m(p_2(K')-3)$. The weighted difference is the display.

**Hypotheses.** The record's conventions line fixes $W\in\mathrm{Weyl}_n$, $n\ge4$, $\lVert M\rVert_F=1$ and the two constants. The Lean statement carries the constants' defining bounds and the two power sums the unit norm gives, and it drops Weyl, $n\ge4$ and $\sum\mu=\sum\omega=0$, none of which the inequality uses. Nothing the argument needs is missing. Negative `Mp` or `m` are admitted, and the statement is then either true by the same proof or vacuous, since `gap_factors`'s last factor stays nonnegative on $[-m,\infty)$ for every real $m$.

**The link to $\theta$.** L50Spectra.lean proves `L50_bispectral`, $P=\tfrac32\operatorname{tr}R^3-\tfrac12\operatorname{tr}_{S^2}\mathring W^3$, and `L50_norm`, $\operatorname{tr}_{S^2}\mathring W^2=3\lVert R\rVert^2$, as traces of operators. Its docstring states that the eigen-decompositions are not typed. So reading the left side of `L52_matrix_bound` as $\theta$ at the record's objects needs the trace of the cube of `Mpair R` and of the $\mathring W$ matrix to be the cube sums of their eigenvalues, and the $S^2$ trace to equal the $S^2_0$ trace (the docstring's `ringW_one` argument). The L52Bound docstring's "the left side being $\theta$ by L50" is a correct pointer and not a typed link.

## 2. `convexOn_g`, `concaveOn_h`, and where the ranges come from

`convexOn_g m : ConvexOn ℝ (Set.Ici (-m)) (CubicConvexity.g m)` with `CubicConvexity.g m t = t ^ 3 + 3 * m * t ^ 2`. The proof reads convexity off `gap_factors`, the exact identity whose last factor is $x+y+(ax+by)+3m$, nonnegative when all three points are at least $-m$. `concaveOn_h Mp : ConcaveOn ℝ (Set.Iic Mp) (fun t => t ^ 3 - 3 * Mp * t ^ 2)` applies `convexOn_g Mp` at $-x,-y$, and $h(t)=-g_{M_+}(-t)$ turns convexity on $[-M_+,\infty)$ into concavity on $(-\infty,M_+]$. Both are the record's claims on the record's sets.

**The range condition is derived.** The record says "both diagonals lie in those ranges". In Lean, `L52_matrix_bound` takes hypotheses on the eigenvalues only. `sum_convexOn_diag` and `sum_concaveOn_diag` take `hx : ∀ j, x j ∈ S` for the eigenvalues and invoke `ConvexOn.map_sum_le` on each row, whose conclusion is stated at the weighted sum and whose proof places it in `S` by `Convex.sum_mem`. So the diagonal entries are in range because they are convex combinations of the spectrum, which is the record's reason. The doubly stochastic `P` is `diag_doublyStochastic`, proved in SchurMajor.lean, so the representation r1413 found discharged is now consumed.

## 3. The L52 row of lean/INDEX.md

**Owed list.** The row reads: "the instantiation at $A=M$ (`Mpair`, built) and $B=\mathring W$ in the basis $\{\varphi_{ab}\}\cup\{\psi_k\}$ of $S^2_0$ (not built), and the equality clause, which needs Karamata". The two instantiations are correctly described. To state L52 at the record's $W$ the layer still needs:

1. $\sum\mu^2=1$ as a sum over `Mpair R`'s eigenvalues at unit norm (r1411 gives the Frobenius norm, the eigenvalue sum is unstated).
2. $\sum\omega^2=3$ over the eigenvalues of $\mathring W$ on $S^2_0$ (`L50_norm` is a trace over $S^2$).
3. L50 with power sums of eigenvalues in place of traces, which is what makes the left side $\theta$.
4. That $\{\varphi_{ab}\}\cup\{\psi_k\}$ is an orthonormal basis of $S^2_0$ with the $\psi_k$ an eigenbasis of $K$ on $\mathbf1^\perp$, which the L54 row names and which the $\mathring W$ matrix presupposes.

Items 1 to 3 were r1413's items 2 to 4 and are still absent from the row.

**"which needs Karamata".** The record splits the equality clause. "The 'if' half is false without the $\psi$ stipulation above", and it is elementary once the stipulation holds, since both operators are then diagonal in one basis and each $P$ is a permutation. "The 'only if' half does need Karamata, and needs two strictnesses rather than one", because Jensen's equality case pins the variable on each row's support and not the weight, which fails at degenerate spectra. So "needs Karamata" is right for "only if" and should say so. The same row's earlier clause, "the strict convexity of $t^3+3mt^2$ the equality case invokes is closed in CubicConvexity ... and Karamata is no longer needed at all", sits in a sentence about the equality case and reads as denying what the new Owed clause asserts. The record's "The inequality needs no Karamata" is the intended scope and the row should state it.

**Tag.** Moving this row to **statement: typed** is supported by L52Bound.

## 4. L54Statement.lean's docstring

The new "The record's statement" paragraph summarises items 1 to 4 accurately, including the sorted (multiset) equalities of item 4. The verbatim quotation of a superseded text and the paragraph saying `M` and `K(F)` have no Lean layer, both flagged by r1412, are removed, and no em dash or `---` remains.

"Where the geometric half is typed" is accurate on what is built: `ringW` in SecondKind.lean, `phi_block_diag`, `M_diag`, `diag_block`, `psi_block`, `tF_le` and `tF_eq_iff` in L54Basis.lean, `L54_mu_majorizes` in L54Schur.lean. Two defects:

- **The $\mu$ half of the cheaper route does not need $\mathring W$.** The record's direct argument reads "by (3) $M$ is diagonal in the pair basis, so its spectrum is $d_\varphi$". At $t_F=2$, `tF_eq_iff` gives `IsCoordDiag R`, the off-diagonal entries of `Mpair R` then vanish, and a diagonal symmetric matrix's eigenvalues are its diagonal as a multiset. None of that touches $\mathring W$. Only "$\mathring W$ diagonal in the eigenbasis, so its spectrum is $d$" needs the $\mathring W$ matrix. The sentence should attribute the $\mathring W$ dependence to $\omega\succ d$ and to $\omega=d$ as vectors.
- **Name.** `ψ_block` should be `psi_block`, the declaration's name in L54Basis.lean.
