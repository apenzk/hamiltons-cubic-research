## A. Fidelity

- **A-1. conjOp** ([Frames.lean:34](../lean/HamiltonsCubic/Base/Frames.lean)). `conjOp U R X = U R(UᵀXU) Uᵀ`. With ψ_ab = Uᵀφ_abU, `psi_expand` ([FrameInv.lean:181](../lean/HamiltonsCubic/Results/FrameInv.lean)) gives ψ_ab = Σ_pq U_ap U_bq φ_pq, which is f_a ∧ f_b for the orthonormal frame f_a = Σ_p U_ap e_p (the rows of U), and `inner2_back` (line 190) moves the outer U Uᵀ onto the second slot. So ⟨(U·R)φ_ab, φ_cd⟩ = ⟨R(f_a ∧ f_b), f_c ∧ f_d⟩: the components of U·R in the coordinate frame are the components of R in the frame of U's rows. `IsOrth` asks both UᵀU = 1 and UUᵀ = 1, redundant over a field but harmless. This is the passive frame change the record means by *in some orthonormal frame*. Faithful.
- **A-2. isWeyl_conj** ([FrameInv.lean:280](../lean/HamiltonsCubic/Results/FrameInv.lean)). Hypotheses are exactly `IsOrth U` and `IsWeyl R`. Skewness goes through `isSkew_conj` and `isSkew_back`. Self-adjointness moves U across the pairing with `inner2_move` (line 213) and uses R's self-adjointness at the skew arguments UᵀXU, UᵀYU. Bianchi and Ricci vanish because `bianchi_conj` and `ric_conj` write them as U-contractions of R's own, which vanish. Correct, no hidden hypothesis.
- **A-3. invariants_conj** ([FrameTheta.lean:114](../lean/HamiltonsCubic/Results/FrameTheta.lean)). Hypotheses are `IsOrth U` and `IsWeyl R` on `Fin n`. The restriction to `Fin n` is inherited from `eigenFrame_exists` ([EigenFrameExist.lean:146](../lean/HamiltonsCubic/Results/EigenFrameExist.lean)), whose section is over `Fin n`, and costs nothing since the record's objects live on ℝⁿ. The conclusion is normSq and θ, not P. When ‖R‖ = 0 both θ are 0/0 = 0 in Lean and the statement stays true. Correct and honestly stated.
- **A-4. linExt** ([FrameTheta.lean:72](../lean/HamiltonsCubic/Results/FrameTheta.lean)). `IsWeyl` carries no linearity clause, and `eigenFrame_exists` needs `IsLinOp`, so R is replaced by R' = R ∘ skewPart. `skewPart` is linear and is the identity on skew matrices (`skewPart_of_skew`, line 68), so R' agrees with R on bivectors (`linExt_agree`), and R' is linear on all matrices because R is linear on bivectors, which `weyl_lin` derives from self-adjointness and skewness ([L41Statement.lean:140](../lean/HamiltonsCubic/Results/L41Statement.lean)). All four `IsWeyl` clauses read R only at skew arguments or at φ_ab, so `linExt_isWeyl` is immediate. The detour is closed on both ends by `theta_congr_skew` (line 48) and `innerOp_congr_skew` ([L24Sharp.lean:36](../lean/HamiltonsCubic/Results/L24Sharp.lean)): normSq and trCube evaluate R at φ_ab and at R(φ_ab), which is skew, and `sharpOp_congr_on_skew` ([FoxBridge.lean:136](../lean/HamiltonsCubic/Results/FoxBridge.lean)) covers R#, whose quadratic form evaluates R at φ_ab and brackets of skew matrices. The hypothesis `hR`, that R preserves skewness, is exactly what makes the second evaluation R(R φ) land on a bivector. On the conjugated side, `hagc` (line 122) holds because UᵀXU is skew whenever X is. Legitimate.
- **A-5. C_conj, bianchi_conj, ric_conj** ([FrameInv.lean:201](../lean/HamiltonsCubic/Results/FrameInv.lean), line 231, line 250). The statements are verbatim the docstrings: four factors of U on the components, four on Bianchi, two on Ricci with the contraction closed by the column orthonormality Σ_c U_cq U_cs = δ_qs taken from UᵀU = 1 (line 254). The `IsWeyl R` hypothesis is used only for linearity through `weyl_lin2`, which the docstrings do not mention and the signatures show. Correct.
- **A-6. An example** (by hand). n = 4, U the quarter turn in the (0,1)-plane: U₀₁ = −1, U₁₀ = 1, U₂₂ = U₃₃ = 1. By `psi_expand`, ψ₀₁ = φ₀₁, ψ₂₃ = φ₂₃, ψ₀₂ = −φ₁₂, ψ₁₂ = φ₀₂, ψ₀₃ = −φ₁₃, ψ₁₃ = φ₀₃. Take R = `diagOp w` with w the four-cycle w₀₁ = w₂₃ = 1, w₁₂ = w₀₃ = −1, w₀₂ = w₁₃ = 0, a Weyl operator by [r1523](REVIEW_r1523.md)'s `WD_weyl_iff` reading. Then U·R = `diagOp w'` with w' obtained by swapping w₀₂ ↔ w₁₂ and w₀₃ ↔ w₁₃: w'₀₁ = w'₂₃ = 1, w'₀₂ = w'₁₃ = −1, w'₁₂ = w'₀₃ = 0. Every row of w' sums to zero, so U·R is Weyl as `isWeyl_conj` says, it is the four-cycle 0–1–3–2–0 up to relabelling, and ‖U·R‖² = ‖R‖² = 4 with equal θ, as `invariants_conj` says. Both hypotheses are jointly satisfiable and the conclusions are non-vacuous.

For milestone 4** (not a gap of this commit). `IsCoordDiagonalSomeFrame` ([Frames.lean:52](../lean/HamiltonsCubic/Base/Frames.lean)) asks `conjOp U R = diagOp w` as functions on all matrices, while `IsWeyl` constrains R only on bivectors. The transfer will need the bivector-only reading, which `linExt` and `innerOp_congr_skew` now supply.
