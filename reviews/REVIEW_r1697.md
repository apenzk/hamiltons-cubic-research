## Findings

- **F1 (step 9, wording, no effect on the result).** The prose says the numerator is "a positive constant times (2Sm − 2m + 1)²Q" and the denominator is negative. The certificate's actual factorisation of Φ(S, S²/m) − c_0 over √2 is numerator −1 · (2Sm−2m+1)² · Q and denominator 8(m−2)(2m−3)(m−1)²S²m²(2m−1)² · P(S, m), P the quadratic in S from D. The sign conclusion is the same, and the certificate checks the actual structure. The prose should state the certificate's form.
- **F2 (step 9 certificate, gap in the certificate's logic, harmless).** sign_on counts a factor as positive when all its Bernstein coefficients are non-negative, which proves ≥ 0 and not > 0. For the denominator factor P this leaves a zero unexcluded by the certificate itself. P > 0 follows from D > 0 (step 6, E > 0 in step 8), so the result stands, but the proof should say where P's strict positivity comes from.
- **F3 (step 6, unstated hypothesis, true).** "c'²s_o < 1" is asserted without the reason. It follows from c' < 1 (certified) and s_o ≤ max π_i · S ≤ ½. It is also what makes φ concave globally on {c'x < 1}: φ'' = 2(c'²s_o − 1)/(b0 u³) with u = 1 − c'x.
- **F4 (step 5, terminology).** ψ̂ is called the concave envelope of ψ̃. The argument only uses that it is a concave majorant on [0, 1], which it is. Whether it is the least one is immaterial.
- **F5 (preamble, superfluous side condition).** "Entries of size 1/n on the γ = 1 branch at π = ½" is certified, but step 3 does not use it: fh switches at 7/32 and every uniform entry σ/n ≤ 1/6 < 7/32 lies on psiL by definition. It can be dropped or re-labelled.
- **F6 (steps 2, 4, degenerate cases, unstated).** At π_0 = 0 the q_0i are undefined. Row 0 and every u_i then vanish, steps 1 and 2 contribute 0, and the Cauchy–Schwarz of step 4 runs over old rows only (π_i's column mass is then Σ_{k≠i} π_k q_ki = π_i). Rows with π_i = 0 drop out likewise. The prose is silent on both. The Lean statement handles them by the x/0 = 0 convention.
