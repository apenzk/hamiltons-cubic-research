## A. `foxTri_hder` and the action

**The record** ([route-a.md](../findings/route-a.md) line 413): *"For $A\in\mathfrak{so}(n)$ write $A\!\cdot\!x=[\rho(A),x]$ for the infinitesimal conjugation action on $\mathcal W_n$."* The proof (line 421): *"$T$ is $SO(n)$-invariant, so differentiating along $\exp(tA)$ gives $T(A\!\cdot\!x,y,z)+T(x,A\!\cdot\!y,z)+T(x,y,A\!\cdot\!z)=0$."*

**The action and its derivative, by hand.** [WeylAction.lean](../lean/HamiltonsCubic/Results/WeylAction.lean) line 20 defines `act g R Z = g * R (gᵀ * Z * g) * gᵀ`, and `foxProd_act` in [WeylFoxEquivariance.lean](../lean/HamiltonsCubic/Results/WeylFoxEquivariance.lean) is the finite equivariance under this `act`. Put $g=e^{tA}$ with $A^T=-A$, so $g^T=e^{-tA}$. At $t=0$: $\frac{d}{dt}(g^TZg)=A^TZ+ZA=-[A,Z]$, and $\frac{d}{dt}(g\,Y\,g^T)=AY+YA^T=[A,Y]$. By the product rule on linear $R$,
$$\tfrac{d}{dt}\big\vert_0\,\mathrm{act}(e^{tA})R\,(Z)=[A,R(Z)]-R([A,Z]),$$
which is `Dop A R Z` with the same sign. With $\rho(A)=\mathrm{ad}_A$ on $\Lambda^2$, $[\rho(A),R](Z)=\rho(A)R(Z)-R(\rho(A)Z)$ is the same expression. So `Dop A` is the record's $A\!\cdot$, and the Lean derives the differentiated identity directly (through $F_4$ and `basis_rot`) instead of differentiating `foxProd_act`, which is legitimate and gives the identity at every linear $p,q$, a range wider than $\mathcal W_n$.

**The Lean.** `foxTri_hder`: at skew $A$, linear $p,q,r$ and $r$ bivector-valued, $T(Dp,q,r)+T(p,Dq,r)+T(p,q,Dr)=0$. The assembly checks by hand: by `foxTri_split` each $T$ is $\tfrac14(J(pq,r)+J(qp,r)+\tfrac12M)$, `Jd_inv` at $(p,q)$ and at $(q,p)$ kills the two Jordan triples (the product rule $D(pq)=(Dp)q+p(Dq)$ plus `pair_skew`), and `Mx_inv` kills the mixed triple. `Dop_skew` supplies bivector-valuedness of $Dr$ so that `foxTri_split` applies in the third term.

**Against L101Statement's `hder`.** `hder : ∀ p q r, T (D p) q r + T p (D q) r + T p q (D r) = 0` over an abstract inner product space $E$. `foxTri_hder` is that at $T=$ `foxTri`, $D=$ `Dop A`, on the elements satisfying `IsLinOp` (all three) and bivector-valuedness (third slot). Both are clauses of `IsWeylOp`, so on a bundled $\mathcal W_n$ closed under $D$ the hypotheses discharge and `hder` holds unconditionally. The closure is the owed item the L101 row names. `hsym` is `foxTri_comm`.

**Hypothesis use.** `hA` enters through `trL_ad_comm` and `basis_rot` (both expand $[A,\varphi_{ij}]$ as a skew matrix and use $\langle[A,X],Y\rangle=-\langle X,[A,Y]\rangle$). `hp`, `hq` enter `foxTri_split` (through `foxProd_eq`) and `Jd_inv`. `hr` enters `pair_skew` and `G_inv`, `H_inv` (linearity of $r$ in `basis_rot`'s bilinear form). `hrs` enters `foxTri_split` through `mixOp_pair`, which needs $r(\varphi_{ij})$ skew. None is redundant: `hr` and `hrs` are independent conditions.

## B. `innerOp_Dop_skew` and `pair_skew`

**The record** (line 421): *"the derivation $X\mapsto[\rho(A),X]$ is Frobenius-skew"*. **The Lean**: `innerOp (Dop A F) G = -innerOp F (Dop A G)` at skew $A$ and linear $F,G$. L101Statement's `hskew : ∀ v w, ⟪D v, w⟫ = -⟪v, D w⟫` is this verbatim with $\langle\cdot,\cdot\rangle=$ `innerOp`.

**By hand.** $\langle[\rho,F],G\rangle+\langle F,[\rho,G]\rangle=\langle\rho F,G\rangle-\langle F\rho,G\rangle+\langle F,\rho G\rangle-\langle F,G\rho\rangle$. The first and third cancel since $\rho=\mathrm{ad}_A$ is skew for `inner2` at skew $A$. The second and fourth are $-\sum_{ij}[\langle F\rho\varphi,G\varphi\rangle+\langle F\varphi,G\rho\varphi\rangle]$, the derivative of $\sum\langle Fg\varphi,Gg\varphi\rangle$ under rotation of the orthonormal basis, which is `basis_rot` at $B(X,Y)=\langle FX,GY\rangle$. That is `pair_skew`'s proof. Both hypotheses `hF`, `hG` are consumed as the bilinearity of $B$. $F,G$ need not be bivector-valued, because `inner2` is the Frobenius pairing on all matrices.

## C. The route lemmas

- **`trL_ad_comm`**: $\mathrm{tr}_{\Lambda^2}(\mathrm{ad}_A\circ F)=\mathrm{tr}_{\Lambda^2}(F\circ\mathrm{ad}_A)$ at skew $A$, linear $F$, with $\mathrm{tr}_{\Lambda^2}F=\tfrac12\sum\langle F\varphi_{ij},\varphi_{ij}\rangle$ the compressed trace. The proof expands $[A,\varphi_{ij}]$ in the $\varphi$ basis with coefficients $c_{ijkl}$ antisymmetric under $(ij)\leftrightarrow(kl)$ and relabels. $F$ need not map into $\Lambda^2$, since only the pairing against $\varphi$ is read.
- **`F4_invariance`**: $F_4(R,S,\eta,\zeta)=-\tfrac14\sum\langle[\eta,R[\zeta,S\varphi]],\varphi\rangle$, and `sharpQF_F4`, `mixQF_F4` identify $F_4(R,R,\eta,\eta)$ with `sharpQF` and $F_4(A,B,\eta,\eta)$ with `mixQF` by `rfl`. The four first-order terms add to $[\mathrm{ad}_A,G]$ with $G=\mathrm{ad}_\eta\circ R\circ\mathrm{ad}_\zeta\circ S$ (`four_terms`, Jacobi twice), and its compressed trace vanishes by `trL_ad_comm`.
- **`basis_rot`**: $\sum_{ij}[B([A,\varphi],\varphi)+B(\varphi,[A,\varphi])]=0$ for bilinear $B$, the same antisymmetry argument over pairs of pairs.
- **`mixOp_pair`**, the constant. `mixOp R S X` $=\tfrac12\sum_{ij}\big(\tfrac12(\mathrm{mixSym}(X+\varphi)-\mathrm{mixSym}X-\mathrm{mixSym}\varphi)\big)\varphi_{ij}$, and `mixSym_polar` makes the bracket $\tfrac12P(X,\varphi_{ij})$. So $\langle\mathrm{mixOp}\,X,W\rangle=\tfrac12\sum_{ij}\tfrac12P(X,\varphi_{ij})\langle\varphi_{ij},W\rangle$. `skew_expand` gives $W=\tfrac12\sum\langle W,\varphi_{ij}\rangle\varphi_{ij}$ for skew $W$, so by linearity of $P(X,\cdot)$ (`lin_expand`), $P(X,W)=\tfrac12\sum\langle W,\varphi_{ij}\rangle P(X,\varphi_{ij})$. Hence $\langle\mathrm{mixOp}\,X,W\rangle=\tfrac12P(X,W)$, the Lean's constant. The skewness of $W$ is load-bearing: `skew_expand` fails off $\Lambda^2$.
- **`foxTri_split`**, the constant. `foxProd_eq` gives $p\circ q=\tfrac12(pq+qp+\mathrm{mixOp}\,p\,q)$. Then $T(p,q,r)=\tfrac12\sum\langle(p\circ q)\varphi,r\varphi\rangle=\tfrac12\sum\tfrac12\big(\langle pq\varphi,r\varphi\rangle+\langle qp\varphi,r\varphi\rangle+\tfrac12P(\varphi,r\varphi)\big)=\tfrac14\big(J(pq,r)+J(qp,r)+\tfrac12M\big)$, the Lean's constants, using `mixOp_pair` at $W=r\varphi_{ij}$, which is where `hr` (bivector-valued) enters.
