## The two exact obstructions, as requirements on any route to P

1. **Exactness on the products.** The unbalanced products $S^{m-1}\times S^{m+1}$ are three-value critical points with $\theta^2/\theta_0^2=1-2/m^3+O(m^{-4})$ ([L232](../findings/diagonal-half.md), [28k](2026-09-28k-LIT-step8-sweep.md)). *Any estimate a route uses on its way to $\theta\le\theta_0$ loses less than a relative $n^{-3}$ there, or never passes through them.*
2. **Global, not local.** Padded $W_{\mathbb{CP}^2}$ is a second-order-critical point at $\sqrt{3/2}<\theta_0$ with $t_{\max}^2\ge\tfrac12$ and a non-product diagonal part ([28h](2026-09-28h-LIT-step8-sweep.md), [F73](../findings/INDEX-traps.md)). *A route that uses only criticality and second-order information cannot exclude it, so it must read $\theta\ge\theta_0$ through a comparison.*

## The only tool on record meeting requirement 1

[L57](../findings/problem.md), the diagonal theorem: $P(A)\le\Theta_n\lVert A\rVert^3$ for $A$ diagonal in an orthonormal frame, exact on every sphere product. **So every route that meets 1 reduces a maximiser to diagonality**, as (MG) ([I121](../plan.md#blocked)), I73 with L105, and [I36](../plan.md) do, each by a different mechanism, and each is blocked at its non-diagonal part by requirement 2 or by circularity.
