## 1. The frame

L30Statement's `Eigenframe` is its own structure, separate from L41Statement's `EigenFrame` which r1425 checked, and differs only in phrasing completeness as span membership. The file closes that clause from `frameVec_complete` by `Submodule.sum_mem`, `smul_mem` and `subset_span`, which is the literal content. The eigenvalues are `Mpair`'s, which r1425 item 1b identified with the spectrum of $W$ on $\Lambda^2$ with multiplicity, and `frameVec_eigen` ties each to its vector, so no reindexing enters.

## 2. The negative support is intrinsic

Let $N\subseteq\Lambda^2$ be the sum of the eigenspaces of $W$ with negative eigenvalue. For any orthonormal eigenframe, the $v_\alpha$ with $\lambda_\alpha<0$ lie in $N$, and they span it: for $X\in N$ the expansion $X=\sum_\alpha\langle X,v_\alpha\rangle v_\alpha$ has $\langle X,v_\alpha\rangle=0$ whenever $\lambda_\alpha\ge0$, since eigenvectors of a self-adjoint operator at distinct eigenvalues are orthogonal. Hence $\operatorname{im}X\subseteq\sum_{\lambda_\alpha<0}\operatorname{im}v_\alpha$, and each $v_\alpha$ is itself in $N$, so `negSupport` $=\sum_{X\in N}\operatorname{im}X$, a function of $W$ alone. A repeated negative eigenvalue changes which vectors Mathlib picks inside the eigenspace and not their span, so it does not change $U$. The same subspace is the image of $\sum_{\lambda_\alpha<0}\lvert\lambda_\alpha\rvert v_\alpha^{\mathsf T}v_\alpha$, because a sum of positive semidefinite terms has image the sum of their images and $\operatorname{im}(v^{\mathsf T}v)=\operatorname{im}v^{\mathsf T}=\operatorname{im}v$ for skew $v$. This matches the record's "$U=\sum_{\lambda_\alpha<0}\operatorname{im}v_\alpha$, the sum over all negative eigenvectors", whose scope note already warns that individual ranks are frame-dependent under repetition while $\dim U$ is not. The equality is untyped in Lean, and nothing downstream needs it, since `L30_padding` accepts any `Eigenframe`.

## 3. The padding and the support clause

The record's conclusion "$W$ is a padding of a $\mathrm{Weyl}_{\dim U}$ tensor" has two halves: $W$ agrees with $W'$ on $\Lambda^2U$, which `L30_padding_exists` gives, and $W$ vanishes on the complement, which is L30's first clause and lives in `L30_support`. That theorem is still stated at an assumed `Eigenframe`, and `L30_support_exists` would be the same one-line application of `eigenframe30_exists`. The file's docstring says "the padding of L30 and L164 holds", which reads as both halves. `hW0` is carried and, per the L30 (statement) row, unused, so the Lean statement is the record's at every nonzero $W$ and loses only the trivial point $W=0$, where L164's $\theta$ is undefined anyway.

## 4. The results.md sentence

"The support claim is proved, and so is the norm-and-$\theta$ clause that makes the reduction useful" is accurate for L30: at $U$ the negative support, L164's three equalities are proved in the record (Gate 2 PROVEN) and in the kernel. The fix is a qualifier: "kernel-checked as `L30_padding` at L30's $U$". The two INDEX rows are accurate and already say this on the L164 row.
