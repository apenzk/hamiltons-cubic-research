## B. Soundness

- **`dform_mul_transpose`** (line 49). With $\omega^{\mathsf T}=-\omega$, $\omega\omega^{\mathsf T}=-(uv^{\mathsf T}-vu^{\mathsf T})^2$. `vecMulVec_mul_vecMulVec` gives $(uv^{\mathsf T})(uv^{\mathsf T})=(v\cdot u)\,uv^{\mathsf T}=0$, $(uv^{\mathsf T})(vu^{\mathsf T})=(v\cdot v)\,uu^{\mathsf T}=uu^{\mathsf T}$, and symmetrically, so $\omega\omega^{\mathsf T}=uu^{\mathsf T}+vv^{\mathsf T}=\Pi$. **Sound.** `proj_idem`, `proj_symm`, `proj_trace` ($\operatorname{tr}\Pi=\lvert u\rvert^2+\lvert v\rvert^2=2$) likewise.
- **`eq_of_anticomm`** (line 72). $(A-B)^{\mathsf H}(A-B)=(A-B)^2=A-AB-BA+B=(A+B)-(AB+BA)=0$ from symmetry, idempotence and the anticommutator, and `conjTranspose_mul_self_eq_zero` over $\mathbb R$ (a real star-ordered ring, so $M^{\mathsf T}M=0\Rightarrow M=0$) gives $A=B$. **Sound.**
- **`anticomm_of_sum`** (line 84). From $\lambda_0P_0=-(\lambda_1P_1+\lambda_2P_2)$, squaring and $P_0^2=P_0$: $(\lambda_1P_1+\lambda_2P_2)^2=\lambda_0^2P_0=-\lambda_0(\lambda_1P_1+\lambda_2P_2)$. The left side is $\lambda_1^2P_1+\lambda_2^2P_2+\lambda_1\lambda_2\{P_1,P_2\}$. With $\lambda_0=-\lambda_1-\lambda_2$ the right side is $(\lambda_1^2+\lambda_1\lambda_2)P_1+(\lambda_2^2+\lambda_1\lambda_2)P_2$, so $\lambda_1\lambda_2(\{P_1,P_2\}-P_1-P_2)=0$, entrywise a linear combination of `e`, as the `linear_combination e` closes. $\lambda_1\lambda_2\ne0$ cancels. No sign grouping is needed, as the commit says. **Sound.**
- **Ricci-flatness to $\sum\lambda_i\Pi_i=0$** (lines 113 to 117). `ric_specOp` ([L180Statement.lean](../lean/HamiltonsCubic/Results/L180Statement.lean) line 150, hypothesis: every `om i` skew, supplied by `dform_skew`) rewrites `Ric (specOp λ ω)` as `ricci λ ω`, and `ricci_flat_iff` (line 130) turns $\forall a\,c$ vanishing into $\sum\lambda_i\,\omega_i\omega_i^{\mathsf T}=0$. `dform_mul_transpose` then gives $\sum\lambda_i\Pi_i=0$. The trace gives $2(\lambda_0+\lambda_1+\lambda_2)=0$, hence `hl0` and `hl2`. **Sound.**
- **The planes.** `e01` applies `anticomm_of_sum` to the reordered sum $\lambda_2\Pi_2+\lambda_0\Pi_0+\lambda_1\Pi_1=0$ with $\lambda_2=-\lambda_0-\lambda_1$ and $\lambda_0\lambda_1\ne0$, then `eq_of_anticomm`, giving $\Pi_0=\Pi_1$. **Sound.**

With $\Pi_1=\Pi_0$ and `proj_mulVec`, $u_1=a u_0+b v_0$, $v_1=c u_0+d v_0$, $a=u_0\cdot u_1$, $b=v_0\cdot u_1$, $c=u_0\cdot v_1$, $d=v_0\cdot v_1$. `dot` computes inner products in the orthonormal frame $(u_0,v_0)$, giving $a^2+b^2=1$, $c^2+d^2=1$, $ac+bd=0$. `dform_comb` gives $u_1\wedge v_1=(ad-bc)\,u_0\wedge v_0$, a ring identity checked entrywise. **Sound.**
