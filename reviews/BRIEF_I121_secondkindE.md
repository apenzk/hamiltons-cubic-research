## $E$ is the second-kind cubic, and $E\le\tfrac32\sqrt{2(n-1)/n}\,t_{\max}$ on the whole unit sphere (derived)

*The frame-free target of the previous section holds for every Weyl tensor, critical or not, with a constant uniform in $n$. The frame it needs is the eigenframe of the bottom eigenvector of $\mathring W$.*

- **$E=-\tfrac12p_3(\omega)$.** With $\omega=\mathop{\mathrm{spec}}(\mathring W)$ on $S^2_0$ in Li's $iklj$ order ([conventions.md](../findings/conventions.md)), [L50](../findings/bispectral.md) reads $P=\tfrac32\mathop{\mathrm{tr}}M^3-\tfrac12p_3(\omega)$ on all of $\mathrm{Weyl}_n$, so $E=P-\tfrac32\mathop{\mathrm{tr}}M^3=-\tfrac12p_3(\omega)$. *Agreement with the record: this note's $E$ is L50's second-kind term, written in another notation.* Independently, $I_3=\mathop{\mathrm{tr}}_{M_n}(\mathring W^3)$ by relabelling indices, and the skew half of that trace is $\mathop{\mathrm{tr}}M^3$ by first Bianchi, which reproduces the identity.
- **Trace positivity.** Let $\mu=-\min\omega\ge0$ (as $\mathop{\mathrm{tr}}\mathring W=0$). Then $\mathop{\mathrm{tr}}\mathring W^3+\mu\mathop{\mathrm{tr}}\mathring W^2=\sum_s\langle(\mathring W+\mu)\mathring Ws,\mathring Ws\rangle\ge0$ over an orthonormal basis $s$ of $S^2$, so with $\sum\omega^2=3\lVert W\rVert^2$, $\ E\le\tfrac32\mu\lVert W\rVert^2$.
- **The bottom of $\mathring W$ is a frame quantity: agreement with the record.** For symmetric $h$, $-\langle\mathring Wh,h\rangle=\sum W_{abcd}h_{ac}h_{bd}$, the pairing of $W$ with $\Lambda^2h$. In the eigenframe $F$ of $h$, with eigenvalues $e$, this is $e^{\mathsf T}K_Fe$ with $K_F$ the hollow sectional matrix of $F$, and [bispectral.md](../findings/bispectral.md) already carries $\mu=v^{\mathsf T}K^Fv\le\mu_1(K^F)\le\sqrt{t_F(n-2)/(n-1)}$ frame by frame, with $t_F=\lVert K^F\rVert_F^2$ (Gate 2 [r555](REVIEW_r555.md), [r556](REVIEW_r556.md)), where it is used with $t_F\le2$ for the absolute cap $\tfrac{n-1}n\mu\le\theta_0(n)$. **Read with $t_F\le\kappa^2=2t_{\max}^2$ instead** ([L94](../findings/bispectral.md)), it gives $\mu\le\sqrt{2(n-2)/(n-1)}\;t_{\max}$. The Lean below proves the slightly weaker $\mu\le\sqrt{2(n-1)/n}\;t_{\max}$, by a Cauchy–Schwarz against the off-diagonal part of $e\otimes e$ that uses hollowness alone: $(e^{\mathsf T}K_Fe)^2\le\lVert K_F\rVert_F^2\big((\sum e^2)^2-\sum e^4\big)\le\tfrac{n-1}n\lVert K_F\rVert_F^2\lVert h\rVert^4$. The record's cap also spends $K_F\mathbf1=0$ and $\sum e=0$, and the two constants differ by $0.3\%$ at $n=14$.
- **So, for every $W\in\mathrm{Weyl}_n$,** $$E(W)\le\tfrac32\sqrt{\tfrac{2(n-2)}{n-1}}\;t_{\max}(W)\,\lVert W\rVert^2\le\tfrac32\sqrt{\tfrac{2(n-1)}n}\;t_{\max}(W)\,\lVert W\rVert^2<\tfrac3{\sqrt2}\,t_{\max}(W)\,\lVert W\rVert^2,$$ the first by the record's cap and the second as typed. **What is new is the pairing of the two halves**: trace positivity turns the bottom of $\mathring W$ into a bound on $E$, and the frame cap read at $t_{\max}$ turns that into the node's frame-free target. Against the calibration, the balanced product has $E/t_{\max}\to\sqrt2$ and the constant is $2.12$. The loss is the trace-positivity step: at the product one eigenvalue $-\mu$ carries $\mu^2\approx2$ of $\sum\omega^2=3$ and $E\approx\tfrac12\mu^3$.
- **At the node.** At a unit critical point with $\theta\ge\theta_0(n)$, $\theta^2\le\tfrac32+\theta E$ ([r1849](REVIEW_r1849.md), [r1850](REVIEW_r1850.md), `crit_E_bound`) gives $E\ge\varepsilon_n$, hence $$t_{\max}^2\ \ge\ \frac29\cdot\frac n{n-1}\,\varepsilon_n^2,$$ which is $1.22\times10^{-4}$ at $n=12$, $1.27\times10^{-3}$ at $n=14$, $2.86\times10^{-3}$ at $n=16$, and tends to $\tfrac1{36}$. **No critical point at or above $\theta_0$ has maximal-frame mass below this**, a constant uniform in $n$ where the single-rotation and matching constraints gave $n^{-1/2}$. It sits far below the $\mathbb{CP}^m$ scale $\tfrac3{n+2}$ at the dimensions in play ($0.0029$ against $0.167$ at $n=16$), so the range between it and the product step stays open.
- **Why it clears the $n^{-1/2}$ barrier and [post F81](../findings/fence-posts.md).** The frame is chosen from $W$ as a whole, the eigenframe of $\mathring W$'s bottom eigenvector, where the earlier constraints came from rotations in one plane or along one matching. And the bound is on $t_{\max}$, which F81's rotated product carries at $1$.
- **Gate 1** ([g1_I121_E_second_kind.py](../scripts/misc/g1_I121_E_second_kind.py)): on random Weyl tensors at $n=6,8,10$ and at $S^3\times S^3$, $-\tfrac12p_3(\omega)$ equals $E$ to every printed digit, $\sum\omega^2=3$, $\mu=e^{\mathsf T}K_Fe$ in the bottom eigenvector's eigenframe, and $\mu\le\sqrt{2(n-1)/n}\,t_F$ there ($1.2649\le1.2910$ at $S^3\times S^3$).
- **Lean** ([SecondKindE.lean](../lean/HamiltonsCubic/Results/SecondKindE.lean)): `E_eq_ring` ($E=-\tfrac12\mathop{\mathrm{tr}}_{S^2}\mathring W^3$ from `L50_bispectral`), `trS2_cube_ge` and `E_le` (trace positivity), `neg_frob_ringW` (the $\Lambda^2h$ pairing), `eigen_expand`, `quad_eigen`, `frob_eigen` and `secK_diag` (the eigenframe, from Mathlib's spectral theorem), `hollow_quad_sq_le` and `sumsq_sq_le` (the scalar Cauchy–Schwarz), **`E_le_frame`: if every orthonormal frame has $\sum K_{pq}^2\le B$, then $E\le\tfrac32\sqrt{(n-1)/n}\sqrt B\,\lVert W\rVert^2$**, its instance `E_le_tmax` at $B=2t^2$, and `t_ge_of_crit`, the threshold from `crit_E_lt`. The least such $B$ is $\kappa^2=2t_{\max}^2$ by definition.

## Next: the range above the uniform threshold

- **The block equations are closed**: summed isotropically they are $\tfrac32$ times Euler, and per matching they give nothing past the matching cap ([2026-09-28e sweep](2026-09-28e-LIT-step8-sweep.md)).
- **The frame-free bound appears to have the right order in $t_{\max}$** (a calibration, unproved). The normalised sums of $k$ products in generic frames have $E\approx\theta_0/\sqrt k$ with $t_{\max}\gtrsim1/\sqrt k$; if $t_{\max}$ is also of order $1/\sqrt k$ there, $E/t_{\max}$ stays of order $\theta_0$ as $t_{\max}\to0$, no bound of higher order in $t_{\max}$ holds on the unit sphere, and frame-free the constant can move only from $2.12$ toward $\theta_0$. The balanced product alone fixes that floor, since there $E/t_{\max}\to\theta_0$ at $t_{\max}=1$.
- **So raising the threshold toward the $\mathbb{CP}^m$ scale uses criticality beyond $\theta^2\le\tfrac32+\theta E$.** Two inputs are on the record in the second-kind variables this bound runs on: the lift of the critical equation to $S^2_0$ at the bottom eigenvector, $\theta\mu=-\langle(W^2)^{\circ}h,h\rangle-\langle(W^\#)^{\circ}h,h\rangle$ ([target.md](../findings/target.md)), and the Schur–Horn majorisation $\omega\succ d$ in a frame ([bispectral.md](../findings/bispectral.md)). *Their reach at small $t_{\max}$ is the next derivation.*

- **L94** ([bispectral.md](../findings/bispectral.md)): **Statement.** For $W$ antisymmetric in each index pair, acting on $\Lambda^2$ as $M$, let $t_F=\lVert K(F)\rVert_F^2=\sum_{a\ne b}W_{abab}(F)^2$ in the frame $F$ and $\kappa^2=\max_Ft_F$ over orthonormal frames. Then the maximum is attained, $\kappa^2\le2\lVert M\rVert_F^2$ with $\lVert M\rVert_F$ the Frobenius norm of the operator on $\Lambda^2$, and $\kappa^2=2\lVert M\rVert_F^2$ exactly when $W$ is coordinate-diagonal in some frame. *The $4$-tensor norm would make the bound slack by a factor $4$.*
- **The reduction** (Gate 2 r1849, r1850): at a unit critical point with value $\theta>0$, $\theta^2\le\tfrac32+\theta E$ with $E=P-\tfrac32\mathop{\mathrm{tr}}W^3$. Lean: `crit_E_bound`, `crit_E_lt` in `lean/HamiltonsCubic/Results/SmallMassE.lean`. $\varepsilon_n=\theta_0-\tfrac3{2\theta_0}$, $\theta_0(n)=\sqrt{2(n-1)(n-2)}/n$.
- **$t_{\max}$**: $t_{\max}^2=\max_F\sum_{p<q}K_F(p,q)^2$ over orthonormal frames, the diagonal mass in the $\Lambda^2$ norm, so $\kappa^2=2t_{\max}^2$.
