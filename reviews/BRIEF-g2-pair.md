## Conventions

- $\Lambda^2\mathbb R^n$ has $\{f_a\wedge f_b\}_{a<b}$ orthonormal; a $2$-form is also a skew matrix, $\lVert\omega\rVert^2=-\tfrac12\mathop{\mathrm{tr}}(\omega^2)$, and its **support** is that matrix's image. $\langle\alpha,\beta\rangle=-\tfrac12\mathop{\mathrm{tr}}(\alpha\beta)$.
- **Orthonormal pair**: $\lVert\omega_1\rVert=\lVert\omega_2\rVert=1$ and $\langle\omega_1,\omega_2\rangle=0$.
- $\mathcal W_n$: algebraic Weyl tensors. **Nothing below should need them; say so if a step does.**
- **The conjecture's range is even $n\ge12$**, threshold $\sqrt{3/2}$, crossover $6+2\sqrt7$. **None should appear; say so if one does.**

## The claim under audit

**Pair statement.** *No orthonormal **pair** $\omega_1,\omega_2$ of $2$-forms with a common six-dimensional support has both $\omega_1^2=\omega_2^2$ and $\omega_1\wedge\omega_1=\omega_2\wedge\omega_2$.*

**The traced argument, as the block records it.** Writing $A=-\omega_i^2$, common by hypothesis, positive definite of trace $2$ on the common support $V$: each $\omega_i$ commutes with $\omega_i^2$ so preserves $A$'s eigenspaces, and $A$'s eigenvalues are doubled, giving spectrum $\{c_1^2,c_1^2,c_2^2,c_2^2,c_3^2,c_3^2\}$. Then

- **Pairwise distinct $c_j^2$:** $\omega_i=\sum_js_{ij}c_j\varepsilon_j$ with $s_{ij}=\pm1$; equal wedges force $s_{1j}s_{1k}=s_{2j}s_{2k}$, hence $\omega_2=\eta\omega_1$, and $\mathop{\mathrm{tr}}(\omega_1\omega_2)=-2\eta\ne0$.
- **All equal, $A=cI$:** each $\omega_i/\sqrt c$ is a complex structure, $\star_V$ is injective, so equal wedges give $\omega_2=\pm\omega_1$.
- **Exactly two equal, $(a,a,b)$:** $\omega_i=\sqrt a\,J_i\oplus\tau_i\sqrt b\,\varepsilon_P$; the cross term forces $\tau_iJ_i$ equal, hence $\omega_2=\tau_2\tau_1\omega_1$.

In each case $\omega_2=\pm\omega_1$, which an orthonormal pair cannot satisfy.

## The block it was traced from, quoted verbatim

#### Lemma L184, the coincident case at $\dim V=6$ is empty

*Derived 2026-09-08 while [L183](../findings/diagonal-half.md#lemma-l183-a-rank-three-weyl-tensor-is-an-orthonormal-triple-whose-images-are-collinear)'s Gate 2 ran. **Unaudited**, and the three cases below are separable, so a reviewer can attack them one at a time.*

**Statement, in the form both audits established rather than the one first written.** *No orthonormal triple $\omega_1,\omega_2,\omega_3$ of $2$-forms with a common six-dimensional support has both its squares and its self-wedges coincident.* **No Weyl hypothesis is used anywhere**, in particular there is no rank-three $W\in\mathcal W_n$ with $\dim V=6$ whose three images under $\Phi$ coincide, which is the corollary the programme wanted.

> **AND THE MECHANISM IS ONE THING IN ALL THREE CASES: THE HYPOTHESES FORCE $\omega_i=\pm\omega_1$, WHICH THREE ORTHONORMAL FORMS CANNOT SATISFY.** *Case 2 by $\star$ being injective, case 3 by the cross term, and case 1 by orthogonality alone once the wedge hypothesis is kept, equal wedges make $s_{ij}s_{ik}$ independent of $i$, so $\omega_i=\eta_i\omega_1$ and $\mathop{\mathrm{tr}}(\omega_1\omega_2)=\mp2\ne0$.* **That retires case 1's appeal to [L181](../findings/diagonal-half.md#lemma-l181-ricci-flatness-and-first-bianchi-are-the-two-halves-of-one-matrix-and-together-they-empty-the-simultaneously-block-diagonal-family) and with it the odd-$n$ question**, *which was never the lemma's anyway: the brief's rendering of L181 dropped its remainder summand $P_0$, and L181 as stated covers odd $n$, both of its own reviewers having found the remainder inert.* **Supplied by [r781](../reviews/REVIEW_r781.md) and [r782](../reviews/REVIEW_r782.md), independently.**

**Setup.** Write $A=-\omega_i^2$, common by hypothesis, positive definite of trace $2$ on $V$. *Each $\omega_i$ commutes with $\omega_i^2$, hence preserves every eigenspace of $A$*, and $A$'s eigenvalues come in equal pairs, $\omega_i$ being skew with eigenvalues $\pm\mathrm ic_j$. **So $A$ has spectrum $\{c_1^2,c_1^2,c_2^2,c_2^2,c_3^2,c_3^2\}$ and the three cases are set by how many of the $c_j^2$ agree.** *Every input to that split is Weyl-free: the doubling is skewness, positive definiteness is the common six-dimensional support, and $\mathop{\mathrm{tr}}A=2$ is the unit norm, so the three cases exhaust the possibilities for any three positive reals and the split survives the strengthened statement, where there is no tensor to constrain the spectrum.* **And the corollary follows because a rank-three Weyl tensor's eigenforms are exactly such a triple**, *equal squares already force a common support, so no reconciliation step is needed and nothing is lost by dropping the Weyl hypothesis.* **The bridge from [L183](../findings/diagonal-half.md#lemma-l183-a-rank-three-weyl-tensor-is-an-orthonormal-triple-whose-images-are-collinear)'s language is that $\Phi(\omega)=(\omega^2,\omega\wedge\omega)$, so "the images coincide" IS "the squares and the self-wedges coincide"**, *a definition this block used and did not carry, which both reviewers recorded as debt rather than guessing at.*

**Case 1, the $c_j^2$ pairwise distinct.** *$A$ is positive definite on $V$ because $V=\mathop{\mathrm{im}}A$, and its multiplicities **on $V$** are all even, off $V$ the zero eigenvalue has multiplicity $n-6$, so the evenness is a statement about $A|_V$ and was written as though about $A$.* **$A$'s eigenspaces are then three planes $P_1,P_2,P_3$, each preserved by every $\omega_i$, and a skew map on a plane is a multiple of its area form**, so $\omega_i=\sum_js_{ij}c_j\varepsilon_j$ with $s_{ij}=\pm1$. *Equal wedges force $s_{ij}s_{ik}c_jc_k$ to be independent of $i$, and $c_jc_k>0$ cancels, so $s_{ij}s_{ik}$ is independent of $i$; setting $\eta_i=s_{i1}s_{11}$ gives $s_{ij}=\eta_is_{1j}$ for **every** $j$, hence $\omega_i=\eta_i\omega_1$ exactly.* **Then $\mathop{\mathrm{tr}}(\omega_1\omega_2)=-2\eta_2=\mp2\ne0$, against orthogonality.** *Individual $s_{ij}$ is not canonical, each $\varepsilon_j$ being fixed up to sign, but the independence claim and $\eta_i$ both are.* **This closes case 1 with no Weyl input and no appeal to [L181](../findings/diagonal-half.md#lemma-l181-ricci-flatness-and-first-bianchi-are-the-two-halves-of-one-matrix-and-together-they-empty-the-simultaneously-block-diagonal-family)**, *whose conclusion is about a $W\in\mathcal W_n$ and so cannot reach a bare triple at all, which is why the appeal had to go rather than merely being improved on.* $\square$

**Case 2, all three equal, $A=cI_6$.** *Then $\omega_i/\sqrt c$ is orthogonal and skew, a complex structure, and unit norm forces $c=\tfrac13$, so $\omega_i^2=-\tfrac13\mathrm{Id}_6$ and $c$ is not a free parameter.* **The Hodge star here is $V$'s, not $\mathbb R^n$'s**: $\star(J\wedge J)$ lands in $\Lambda^2$ only in six dimensions, and read on $\mathbb R^n$ for $n>6$ the identity is false. *On $V$, every compatible complex structure satisfies $\star_V(J\wedge J)=2\epsilon_JJ$ with $\epsilon_J=\pm1$ by orientation.* **Equal $J_i\wedge J_i$ forces $J_i=\pm K$ for a single $K$**, *and nothing weaker survives, since $\star$ is injective, so equal wedges give $\omega_j=\pm\omega_1$ exactly.* **The contradiction is then linear independence rather than orthogonality**: $J_i=\pm K$ leaves the three forms spanning a line, so the rank cannot be three. *[r780](../reviews/REVIEW_r780.md) supplied both strengthenings; the trace condition $\mathop{\mathrm{tr}}(J_iJ_j)\ne0$ is not needed at all, and $\epsilon_J$ is well defined because **$SO(6)$** is connected, the block first credited $U(3)$, which is the wrong group for the argument ([r784](../reviews/REVIEW_r784.md)).* **This closes nothing outside $\dim V=6$**, where alone $\star_V(J\wedge J)$ lands in $\Lambda^2V$, the dimension count $\binom{2m}4=\binom{2m}2$ holding at $2m=6$ and nowhere else. *The constraint is on $\dim V$ and not on $n$, which the block conflated twice, once in the case body and again in the entailment box below, the second surviving the first repair.* $\square$

**Case 3, exactly two agree, spectrum $(a,a,b)$ with $a\ne b$.** *$A$'s eigenspaces are a $4$-plane $Q$ and a plane $P$, both preserved, so $\omega_i=\sqrt a\,J_i\oplus\tau_i\sqrt b\,\varepsilon_P$ with $J_i$ a complex structure on $Q$ and $\tau_i=\pm1$.* **Equal wedges force $J_i=\sigma_iJ$ for one $J$**: the wedge splits into a $\Lambda^4Q$ part $a\,J_i\wedge J_i$ and a cross term $2\tau_i\sqrt{ab}\,J_i\wedge\varepsilon_P$ in $\Lambda^2Q\otimes\Lambda^2P$, the $\varepsilon_P\wedge\varepsilon_P$ term vanishing. **$J\wedge J=2\eta\,\mathrm{vol}_Q$ with $\eta=\pm1$ according to whether $J$'s orientation matches $Q$'s, NOT $+2$ always, which a first draft asserted.** *The cross term is what carries the conclusion*: equating it across $i$ gives $\tau_iJ_i$ all equal, hence $J_i=\sigma_iJ$ for one $J$, **and $\rho_i=\sigma_i\tau_i$ is constant**, which retires the three-signs step and gives $2a=-b$ at once. *Writing that as "$\sigma_i=\tau_i$" is choice-dependent ($J$ and $-J$ swap it) and $\rho_i$ constant is the invariant form ([r783](../reviews/REVIEW_r783.md)).* **With $J:=\tau_1J_1$ the two parts carry the same sign, so $\omega_i=\tau_i\tau_1\omega_1$ and case 3 exhibits the common mechanism too**, in a line the case itself did not take. **The $\Lambda^4Q$ condition is then automatic, but only in that order, and the sequencing is load-bearing.** *Read alone the $\Lambda^4Q$ equality says $\eta_1=\eta_2=\eta_3$, which is a real restriction; it becomes vacuous only after the cross term has already given $J_i=\sigma_iJ$, since $(-J)\wedge(-J)=J\wedge J$ leaves $\eta$ unchanged, *so the corrected sign costs the argument nothing, and the block had the right conclusion for a reason it stated wrongly.* **Then orthogonality is a sign identity that has no solution.** *With $\mathop{\mathrm{tr}}(J^2)=-4$ on $Q$ and $\varepsilon_P^2=-\mathrm{Id}_P$, $\mathop{\mathrm{tr}}(\omega_i\omega_j)=-4a\sigma_i\sigma_j-2b\tau_i\tau_j$, so orthogonality reads $4a\,\sigma_i\sigma_j=-2b\,\tau_i\tau_j$ at all three pairs. Setting $\rho_i=\sigma_i\tau_i$, the ratio $\sigma_i\sigma_j/(\tau_i\tau_j)=\rho_i\rho_j$ must be one constant, and three signs with all pairwise products equal force that constant to be $+1$*, **whence $4a=-2b$, impossible for $a,b>0$.** $\square$

> **AN OPEN OBSERVATION, RECORDED AND DELIBERATELY NOT FOLDED INTO THE STATEMENT.** *[r783](../reviews/REVIEW_r783.md) noticed that every contradiction above uses only a **pair** and flagged it for its own audit rather than adopting it, which is the discipline this round was convened to enforce, so it is written here as an observation and not as a strengthening.* **Traced through: case 1's equal wedges give $s_{1j}s_{1k}=s_{2j}s_{2k}$, hence $\omega_2=\eta\omega_1$ and $\mathop{\mathrm{tr}}(\omega_1\omega_2)=-2\eta\ne0$; case 2's $\star$-injectivity gives $\omega_2=\pm\omega_1$; case 3's cross term gives $\omega_2=\tau_2\tau_1\omega_1$.** *Each closes on two forms, and the case split needs only the common square.* **So the lemma appears to prove the strictly stronger pair statement**, *no orthonormal **pair** of $2$-forms with a common six-dimensional support has both its squares and its self-wedges coincident*, **and that is unaudited.** *It is not what the block claims and must not be cited as though it were; the triple statement above is what two rounds have checked.*

> **WHAT IS ENTAILED, STATED WITHOUT INFLATION.** *At $\dim V=6$ the coincident case is empty, and since [L183](../findings/diagonal-half.md#lemma-l183-a-rank-three-weyl-tensor-is-an-orthonormal-triple-whose-images-are-collinear) leaves only all-coincide or all-distinct-and-collinear, what remains **there** is the distinct-and-collinear case.* **At $\dim V\ge8$ the case split does not even apply**, being a trichotomy on the three values $c_1^2,c_2^2,c_3^2$ that six dimensions supply, *at $\dim V=8$ there are four, and a pattern such as $(a,a,b,b)$ falls outside all three cases entirely.* **Only case 1's argument transfers, to the pairwise-distinct stratum**, and the rest is untouched, *cases 2 and 3 being strictly six-dimensional, the first because $\star(J\wedge J)$ lands in $\Lambda^2$ only at $n=6$ and the second because its splitting is $4+2$.* **A first version of this box read "one case left" while its own next sentence said nothing reaches $\dim V\ge8$; the two contradicted each other**, and [r782](../reviews/REVIEW_r782.md) caught it. *A ledger row taken from the inflated form would have been wrong, and rows are what later work is built on.*
