## 1. The spectrum is the record's $\mu$

L50's statement lets $W$ act on $\Lambda^2\mathbb R^n$ as $M$ with $\lVert M\rVert_F=1$ and spectrum $\mu$. In the layer, `phi i j = E_ji - E_ij` and `inner2 X Y = ½ tr(XᵀY)` give $\langle\varphi_{ij},\varphi_{kl}\rangle=\delta_{ik}\delta_{jl}-\delta_{il}\delta_{jk}$, the record's pairing (r1401). `Pairs n` is $\{(a,b):a<b\}$, so $\{\varphi_{ab}\}$ is an orthonormal basis of $\Lambda^2$ and `Mpair R p q = R4 R p.1 p.2 q.1 q.2` is the matrix of $M$ in it. A matrix in an orthonormal basis has the operator's spectrum, and `IsHermitian.eigenvalues` indexes that spectrum by the eigenvector basis, so each eigenvalue appears with its multiplicity and the index set has $\binom n2$ elements.

`normSq R` is `innerOp R R`, which `innerOp_R4` writes as $\tfrac14\sum_{ijkl}W_{ijkl}^2=\sum_{a<b,c<d}W_{abcd}^2$. That is $\lVert M\rVert_F^2$, the square of the norm the record fixes at $1$. `sum_mu_sq` therefore reads $\sum\mu^2=\lVert M\rVert_F^2$, and the record's $\sum\mu^2=1$ is its instance at unit norm. The proof takes $\mathop{\mathrm{tr}}(\mathrm{Mpair}^2)$, expands the four-index sum into ordered pairs `A2`, and halves twice with `sum_pairs'` using antisymmetry in each slot. Every step is an identity of finite sums.

`sum_mu_cube` equates $\sum\mu^3$ with `trCube R`, which r1404 identified as the record's $\mathop{\mathrm{tr}}M^3$. The route is `trCube_eq_alpha` ($\alpha=8\mathop{\mathrm{tr}}M^3$) and a threefold halving of $\alpha$ to the pair-indexed triple sum $\mathop{\mathrm{tr}}(\mathrm{Mpair}^3)$. So the theorem is $p_3(\mu)=\mathop{\mathrm{tr}}M^3$ exactly.

## 2. `L50_mu_spectral` and the L50 row

The theorem rewrites `L50_bispectral`'s $\mathop{\mathrm{tr}}M^3$ by `sum_mu_cube` and so states $P=\tfrac32p_3(\mu)-\tfrac12\mathop{\mathrm{tr}}_{S^2}\mathring W^3$. Against the record's display this is the first term in spectral form and the second in trace form, over $S^2$ rather than $S^2_0$. At unit norm $P=\theta$. The hypotheses are those of `L50_bispectral` and drop Ricci-flatness, which the record's $\mathrm{Weyl}_n$ carries, so the Lean statement is weaker in its conclusion and wider in its range, a partial reading and a faithful one.

The Owed list names what is left: the $S^2\to S^2_0$ passage, $p_3(\omega)$ from a matrix of $\mathring W$ on $S^2_0$, the two trace-zero conditions, the $n\le5$ collapse through link (b), and the $n\ge6$ converse. The earlier clause "the reading of the two traces as $p_3(\mu)$ and $p_3(\omega)$" is correctly cut to the $\omega$ side. The row names `sum_mu_sq` and `L50_mu_spectral`, and `sum_mu_cube` sits inside the latter.
