## A. L153

Quoted from [findings/bounds.md](../findings/bounds.md), Lemma L153, **Statement**: "For an orthonormal basis $\{v_\beta\}$ of $\Lambda^2\mathbb R^n=\mathfrak{so}(n)$ and **any** $v\in\mathfrak{so}(n)$, $\sum_\beta\lVert[v,v_\beta]\rVert^2=2(n-2)\lVert v\rVert^2$, at every $n\ge2$. Taking $v=v_\alpha$ a basis element gives $\sum_\beta\lVert[v_\alpha,v_\beta]\rVert^2=2(n-2)$."

The Lean statement quantifies over an `EigenFrame W v lam`, whose fields are skewness, orthonormality under `inner2`, the eigen equation $W v_\gamma=\lambda_\gamma v_\gamma$ and completeness on skew matrices. Orthonormality with completeness is the record's "orthonormal basis", and the eigen field is vacuous for the caller, who may pick $W=0$ and $\lambda=0$. The file's docstring says so. The norm is `inner2`, half the Frobenius trace, the record's convention ($\mathop{\mathrm{tr}}(v^2)=-2\lVert v\rVert^2$). The proof moves the frame sum to the $\varphi$-basis sum by `innerOp_frame`, which needs only linearity of $Y\mapsto[X,Y]$ (`br_sum_r`), and `killing_phi` evaluates that sum entrywise with $X$ skew. The Lean has no lower bound on $n$ and needs none.

## D. L57's equality case as a sphere product

**The constant.** $c=-(xy+\frac{x^2+y^2}{n-2}-\frac1{(n-1)(n-2)})$ is minus the cross-class value of $Z(v)$, the scale that turns the $-1$ cross entry of `sphereWeight` into $Z_{xy}$. At the balanced split it is $\frac{n-2}{n(n-1)}$, and at $(p,q)=(\frac{n-1}2,\frac{n+1}2)$ it is $\frac{n(n-3)}{(n^2-1)(n-2)}$, the constants r1435 and r1436 found by hand.

**The sizes.** The even theorem exports $2\lvert T\rvert=n$ and the odd one $(\lvert T\rvert-\lvert T^c\rvert)^2=1$ in $\mathbb Z$, the record's balanced and $(\lfloor n/2\rfloor,\lceil n/2\rceil)$ splits, with the side left open by the symmetry $\texttt{sphereWeight}\,T=\texttt{sphereWeight}\,T^c$ (the in-block entries of the two sides swap roles).

**The sign.** `sphereWeight` is positive in-block and negative across. Families.lean identifies it with the record's $S^p\times S^q$ weight ($\frac1{p(p-1)}$ in, $-\frac1{pq}$ across, times $pq$ at the balanced split), and the record's $V$ is its negative (r1436 C2), so $c\,t>0$ is the record's geometric Weyl tensor.
