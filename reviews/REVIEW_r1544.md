## A. Fidelity

- **A-1. `F_eq_Fn`** ([L182Bridge.lean:21–29](../lean/HamiltonsCubic/Results/L182Bridge.lean)). Confirmed, traced by hand. L182's `theta0 n` is √(2(n−1)(n−2)/n²) ([L182Statement.lean:22–25](../lean/HamiltonsCubic/Results/L182Statement.lean)), and `hθ` rewrites it to L162's `q n / n` = √(2(n−1)(n−2))/n by `Real.sqrt_div'` and √(n²) = n, which needs n ≥ 0 and has n > 2. `cp`, `rhoEff` and `w0` ([L182Statement.lean:31–33](../lean/HamiltonsCubic/Results/L182Statement.lean)) are 3θ₀/(n−2), √6 − θ₀(n+16)/(2(n−2)) and (n−4)/n, so after `hθ` their combination c_p + ρ_eff w₀² is `Sfun n` ([L162Mono.lean:24–25](../lean/HamiltonsCubic/Results/L162Mono.lean)) syntactically. L182's `lam` ([L182Statement.lean:28](../lean/HamiltonsCubic/Results/L182Statement.lean)) and L162's `lam` ([L162.lean:126–127](../lean/HamiltonsCubic/Results/L162.lean)) unfold to the same term, so `hl` is `rfl`. `Cn` = 2(2n−3)/(n−1) and `beta` = λ/(2C) are the constants `Fn` ([L162Limit.lean:21–22](../lean/HamiltonsCubic/Results/L162Limit.lean)) inlines, and s₀ = √S in both. So F and `Fn` agree term for term, and both are the record's F(w₀) ([second-order.md:2328–2332](../findings/second-order.md)).
