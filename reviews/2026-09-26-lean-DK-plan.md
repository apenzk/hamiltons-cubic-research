# Lean build plan: the one-sided derivative of the negative part, for L17

**Goal.** Discharge [L17Statement.lean](../lean/HamiltonsCubic/Results/L17Statement.lean)'s hypothesis `hderiv`, the record's $d^+\delta^2(u;H)=-2aF(H)$ at the padded $\mathbb{CP}^2$ competitor $u$ ([route-a.md](../findings/route-a.md), Lemma L17), so that L17 on Figure 2's live route becomes unconditional. The same machinery serves L136, L140 and L141.

**What Mathlib provides** (scouted 2026-09-26): the spectral theorem for symmetric operators, Rayleigh quotients with their extremal characterisation, and continuity of the continuous functional calculus in its argument. **What it lacks**: Courant–Fischer min–max, Weyl's eigenvalue inequality, and any derivative of spectral projections or of $W\mapsto f(W)$ at a non-smooth $f$.

**Bound.** Multi-session, of the order of a thousand lines or more. The record itself notes that Daleckii–Krein does not apply at its own object, since $x\mapsto x_-$ fails $C^1$ at $0\in\operatorname{spec}(u)$, so the derivative is assembled from two branches rather than quoted.

## Milestones

1. **Eigenvalue control.** Weyl's inequality $\lvert\lambda_k(A+tH)-\lambda_k(A)\rvert\le\lvert t\rvert\lVert H\rVert$ for symmetric operators on a finite-dimensional real inner product space, from the two Rayleigh bounds in Mathlib's eigenbasis and a rank count. **Proved** in [Base/WeylIneq.lean](../lean/HamiltonsCubic/Base/WeylIneq.lean): `abs_eigenvalue_add_smul_sub_le`, resting on `abs_eigenvalue_add_sub_le` and `abs_inner_self_le`.
2. **Spectral functions, characterised on eigenvectors.** $g(T)=\sum_i g(\lambda_i)\,e_i\langle e_i,\cdot\rangle$, with the uniqueness lemma that any operator sending each eigenvector $v$ of $T$ (eigenvalue $\mu$) to $g(\mu)v$ equals $g(T)$. Three consequences follow from uniqueness alone: $g(U^{\top}TU)=U^{\top}g(T)U$ for orthogonal $U$, $g(cT)=c\,g(T)$ for $c\ge0$ when $g$ is positively homogeneous, and $g(T)=-T$ when every eigenvalue lies where $g=-\mathrm{id}$. **Then the mixed-basis identity**: in eigenbases $e$ of $A$ and $f$ of $B$, $\langle e_i,(g(B)-g(A))f_j\rangle=(g(\nu_j)-g(\lambda_i))\langle e_i,f_j\rangle$ and $\langle e_i,(B-A)f_j\rangle=(\nu_j-\lambda_i)\langle e_i,f_j\rangle$, which makes $g$ $L$-Lipschitz in the Hilbert–Schmidt norm whenever it is $L$-Lipschitz on $\mathbb R$. **Proved** in [Base/SpectralFn.lean](../lean/HamiltonsCubic/Base/SpectralFn.lean): `specFn_unique`, `specFn_conj` (for every invertible $U$ with $U^{-1}TU$ symmetric), `specFn_smul`, `specFn_neg`, `inner_sub_eigen`, `inner_specFn_sub` and `hsSq_specFn_sub_le`, with `norm_apply_sq_le` and `hsSq_le_opNorm` comparing the Hilbert–Schmidt and operator norms.
3. **Block reduction by a rotation.** With $K=\sum_{\lambda\ne\mu}P_\lambda HP_\mu/(\mu-\lambda)$ (skew, solving $[K,u]=H-H_{\mathrm{blk}}$) and $U(t)=\exp(tK)$ (orthogonal, $U'(0)=K$), the path $M(t)=U(t)^{*}(u+tH)U(t)$ has $M'(0)=H_{\mathrm{blk}}$, so $M(t)=u+tH_{\mathrm{blk}}+o(t)$ where $H_{\mathrm{blk}}=\sum_\lambda P_\lambda HP_\lambda$. **Proved** in [Base/Schur.lean](../lean/HamiltonsCubic/Base/Schur.lean) (`rotGen_commutator`, `rotGen_isSkew`, `blkPart_comm_specFn`, and `rotGen_commutator_specFn` giving $[K,g(u)]$ as the Daleckii–Krein off-diagonal part) and [Base/BlockRotation.lean](../lean/HamiltonsCubic/Base/BlockRotation.lean) (`star_rot`, `rot_mul_rot_neg`, `hasDerivAt_conjPath`, and the bridge `star_rotGen` and `hasDerivAt_conjPath_blk` carrying the generator into `E →L[ℝ] E`).
4. **The block-diagonal value is exact.** For small $t\ge0$, $g(u+tH_{\mathrm{blk}})=g(u)+t\,G_1$ with $G_1=-\sum_{\lambda<0}P_\lambda HP_\lambda+(P_0HP_0)_-$ for $g=x_-$: on a block $\lambda\ne0$ every eigenvalue stays on the side of $0$ that $\lambda$ is on once $t\le\gamma/(2(\lVert H_{\mathrm{blk}}\rVert_{HS}^2+1))$, $\gamma$ the least nonzero $\lvert\lambda\rvert$ (or $1$ when $u=0$), so $g$ is affine there, and on the kernel block $g(tP_0HP_0)=t\,(P_0HP_0)_-$ by homogeneity. **Proved** in [Base/BlockValue.lean](../lean/HamiltonsCubic/Base/BlockValue.lean): `specFn_blk`, for any $g$ vanishing on $(0,\infty)$, equal to $-x$ on $(-\infty,0)$ and positively homogeneous. With 2 and 3, $(u+tH)_-=u_-+t\big([K,u_-]+G_1\big)+o(t)$ as $t\to0^+$ in the operator norm, **proved** in [Base/NegPartDeriv.lean](../lean/HamiltonsCubic/Base/NegPartDeriv.lean) as `hasDerivWithinAt_specFn_negPart`.
5. **Assembly at $u$**, in three parts.
   - **5a, the bivector model.** $E=\mathbb R^{\binom n2}$ with orthonormal basis $e_{ij}$ ($i<j$), the coordinates of a skew matrix $X$ being $\langle X,\varphi_{ij}\rangle$ under the layer's `inner2`. A spectral operator `specOp λ ω` with skew $\omega$ acts on $E$ with entries `comp` (`comp_specOp_eq`), and at a skew presentation orthogonal with a common norm the operator of `negPartOp` is `specFn` of the negative part at the operator of `specOp`. This gives the presentation-free $\delta^2(W)=\lVert\mathrm{Alt}(W_-)\rVert^2$ on symmetric operators of $E$, agreeing with `defectSq` at every such presentation, the layer's own included (they have norm² $2$). **Proved** in [Base/BivModel.lean](../lean/HamiltonsCubic/Base/BivModel.lean): `inner_coords` (Parseval), `specFn_rankSum`, `defectSq_eq` (orthonormal) and `defectSq_eq_of_orthogonal` (common norm $c>0$). *Both hypotheses are needed: a repeated or non-skew $\omega$ makes the two sides differ.*
   - **5b, the chain rule.** $\delta^2$ is the square of a linear image of $W_-$, so milestone 4's derivative gives $d^+\delta^2(u;H)=2\langle\mathrm{Alt}(u_-),\mathrm{Alt}(D)\rangle$ with $D=[K,u_-]-\sum_{\lambda<0}P_\lambda HP_\lambda+(P_0HP_0)_-$. **Proved** in the same file as `hasDerivWithinAt_defect`.
   - **5c, the evaluation.** $\mathrm{Alt}(u_-)=a\star$ (the value of `defect_u` in [DefectCP2.lean](../lean/HamiltonsCubic/Results/DefectCP2.lean) and its $\star$ form), so the pairing is $a$ times the trace of $D$ against the Hodge star on $\Lambda^2\mathbb R^4$. The star preserves each eigenspace of $u$, which kills the off-block term $[K,u_-]$. It is $+1$ on the negative eigenspace $\langle b,c\rangle$, which gives $-(H_{22}+H_{33})$, and $-1$ on the anti-self-dual part of $\ker u$, which gives $-\operatorname{tr}(\Pi_{ASD}(P_0HP_0)_-)$. The total is $-2aF(H)$, which discharges `hderiv`. **Proved** in [Results/L17Deriv.lean](../lean/HamiltonsCubic/Results/L17Deriv.lean) as `hasDerivWithinAt_defect_u`, for every symmetric $H$ and $n\ge4$, with `pairing` for $\langle\mathrm{Alt}(u_-),\mathrm{Alt}(X)\rangle=a\operatorname{tr}(\star X)$ and `defectSq_u_eq` identifying the competitor with the layer's.

6. **Closure.** L17's chain with its three inputs discharged: the compression $\operatorname{tr}(B_-)\le\operatorname{tr}(\Pi_{ASD}(P_0HP_0)_-)$ for any orthogonal projection and symmetric operator, Bianchi through `pairing`, tangency through the trace against $u$. **Proved** in [Base/Compression.lean](../lean/HamiltonsCubic/Base/Compression.lean) and [Results/L17Close.lean](../lean/HamiltonsCubic/Results/L17Close.lean) as `L17_at_u`.

7. **The $\Phi$ half.** $d^+P(u;H)=3\langle H,Q(u)\rangle=3\sqrt{3/2}\langle H,u\rangle=0$ for tangent $H$, from `Wc_crit` and the layer's `hasDerivAt_Pcubic2_line`, and the map `opB` from `CurvOp2` to the bivector model with `opB_u2`. **Proved** in [Results/L17Phi.lean](../lean/HamiltonsCubic/Results/L17Phi.lean) as `L17` (the identity along the one path $u+tH$, and $d^+\Phi\le0$ at $\kappa\le0$) and `L17_ge12` ($\kappa=\kappa_n$, $n\ge12$). The converse half of the record's "exactly those $n$" is [Results/L17Converse.lean](../lean/HamiltonsCubic/Results/L17Converse.lean)'s `L17_converse`, with the padded $\overline{\mathbb{CP}}{}^2$ tensor as witness. [Results/L17Cone.lean](../lean/HamiltonsCubic/Results/L17Cone.lean) proves $\{F=0\}$ a linear subspace.

**Stop condition.** If a milestone turns out to need contour integration of matrix resolvents, which Mathlib lacks, the build pauses and records that as the blocking piece. Route 2 to 4 above uses only finite sums, eigenbases and the exponential series, so none of them should reach that point.
