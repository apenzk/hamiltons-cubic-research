## A. Fidelity

- **A-1. $\theta$ is the operator's** ([L161Link.lean:31](../lean/HamiltonsCubic/Results/L161Link.lean), line 34). The left side is `theta (diagOp (t • sphereWeight S)) ^ 2`, `theta` being [WeylReal.lean:180](../lean/HamiltonsCubic/Base/WeylReal.lean)'s `Pcubic R / norm R ^ 3`. The proof's first rewrite is `theta_sq_sphere` ([L2Operators.lean:54](../lean/HamiltonsCubic/Results/L2Operators.lean)), which turns it into `thetaSpSq S.card Sᶜ.card`, and only then does `thetaSq_cast` (line 25, a `push_cast; ring` between two identical closed forms) bring in L161's rational `thetaSq`. `theta_sq_sphere` rests on `theta_sphereProduct` ([Families.lean:655](../lean/HamiltonsCubic/Base/Families.lean)) applied to `sphereProductValue` ([SphereValue.lean:327](../lean/HamiltonsCubic/Base/SphereValue.lean)), a theorem, so no hypothesis stands in for the value. The owed item "θ(W_{p,q})² taken as a definition" is discharged.
- **A-2. The operator is $W_{p,q}$, and the sign is positive** ([Families.lean:308](../lean/HamiltonsCubic/Base/Families.lean)). `sphereWeight S` puts $q/(p-1)$ inside $S$, $p/(q-1)$ inside $S^c$ and $-1$ across. The record's weight ([diagonal-half.md](../findings/diagonal-half.md), quoted at [Families.lean:39–43](../lean/HamiltonsCubic/Base/Families.lean)) is $1/(p(p-1))$ inside and $-1/(pq)$ across at $p=q$, and its unbalanced continuation is forced by zero row sums. Multiplying by $pq>0$ gives exactly `sphereWeight`, and $t>0$ keeps the scale positive, so the operator is a positive multiple of $W_{p,q}$ and not of $-W_{p,q}$. `theta_sphereProduct` then gives $\theta=$ `thetaSplit` $=\sqrt{\cdot}>0$, the orientation in which $e=W/\theta$ has $L_e=1$ on $\langle W\rangle$ as L161 reads it. $\theta$ is odd, so $\theta(-W)^2=\theta(W)^2$ and the square would hide a flip, but nothing here flips, and L161's $\kappa_n=\theta^2\mathop{\mathrm{tr}}(L_e^2)$ is itself invariant under $W\mapsto-W$ since $L_{-e}^2=L_e^2$. The sign cannot matter to L161.
- **A-3. $n=p+q$** ([L161Link.lean:33](../lean/HamiltonsCubic/Results/L161Link.lean), lines 38–39). $p$ and $q$ are `S.card` and `Sᶜ.card`, their sum is $n$ by `Finset.card_add_card_compl` and `Fintype.card_fin`, and `hn'` replaces $p+q$ by $n$ in `kappa_identity`'s right side. $n$ enters nowhere else, and $n\ge4$ follows from the two card hypotheses. Correct.
- **A-4. The range $p,q\ge2$** ([L161Link.lean:14–15](../lean/HamiltonsCubic/Results/L161Link.lean), lines 28–29). **Gap.** The hypotheses are `2 ≤ S.card` and `2 ≤ Sᶜ.card`, matching `theta_sq_sphere`, and `kappa_identity` needs only nonzero denominators, so the equation holds there. But `trLe2 p q` is $\mathop{\mathrm{tr}}(L_e^2)$ only where [L27](../findings/nondegeneracy.md)'s ten-summand decomposition holds, $p,q\ge3$ ([curvature-invariants.md:114](../findings/curvature-invariants.md), "the ten-summand decomposition needs $p,q\ge3$"), and [L27.lean](../lean/HamiltonsCubic/Results/L27.lean)'s `overcount_two` and `overcount_one` show the formula-dimensions miscount at $q\le2$. The module docstring calls the result "L161's identity" at every unit sphere product with $p,q\ge2$ (lines 14–15) and the theorem docstring (line 28) writes $\mathop{\mathrm{tr}}(L_e^2)$. At $p=2$ or $q=2$ the second factor is the closed form and not the trace of any $L_e$. The theorem is correct as stated in Lean. The prose should say the right factor is L161's closed form `trLe2`, and that it reads $\mathop{\mathrm{tr}}(L_e^2)$ at $p,q\ge3$, or the hypotheses should be raised to 3.
- **A-5. The row** ([lean/INDEX.md:531](../lean/INDEX.md)). The owed list after the edit is Fox's Theorem 1.3 and $L_e$'s spectrum on the ten summands. That is complete: with $\theta^2$ discharged, the only remaining imports are $W$-independence and the spectrum that makes `trLe2` the trace, and the Lean docstring says so at [L161Link.lean:17](../lean/HamiltonsCubic/Results/L161Link.lean). **Gap.** The new sentence says `kappa_at_operator` "gives $\theta(W)^2\mathop{\mathrm{tr}}(L_e^2)=(n-1)(2n-1)/4$ with $\theta$ the unit sphere-product operator's own". Only $\theta$ is the operator's. The $\mathop{\mathrm{tr}}(L_e^2)$ factor is still L161's closed form, which the sentence does not say, and which a reader of "at the operator" is likely to take as the operator's trace too. The sentence also inherits A-4's range. Honest wording: "gives $\theta(W)^2\cdot$`trLe2`$(p,q)=(n-1)(2n-1)/4$, $\theta$ the operator's own and `trLe2` still L161's closed form". A minor placement point: the sentence follows the **Owed** list directly and could be read as a third owed item. It would read better before **Owed**.
