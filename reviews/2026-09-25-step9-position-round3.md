# Step 9 position, round 3 (2026-09-25): I73 (c) at every n

**Where the project stands.** P reduces to [I73](../plan.md) (a global maximiser has two eigenvalues) through [L105](../findings/bounds-relaxations.md). (a) is settled, (b) is blind at [F39](../findings/fence-posts.md), and (c), three values above $\theta_0(n)$, was worked by census at $n=12$ until the user redirected it to every $n$. Taken at every $n$, the symmetric family ($\lambda=(-a,0,a)$) has stars excluded by [L246](../findings/second-order.md) and equal-isotropy halvings excluded as padded two-value points (L105). The per-eigenspace ingredients are bounded by [F77](../findings/fence-posts.md). The general symmetric $E_0$ is open, and so is the asymmetric majority.

**The open nodes.**
1. **Stability of near-decomposable subspaces**: a subspace $V\subseteq\mathfrak{so}(n)$ whose every element has a dominant top angle ($s_1^2\ge\tau>\tfrac12$) has an isotropy $A_V=\sum\psi^{\mathsf T}\psi$ with an eigenvalue near $\dim V$. The exact case ($\tau=1$) is the classical classification (stars or $\Lambda^2\mathbb R^3$).
2. **Three-value critical points at every $n$**: any argument excluding $\theta>\theta_0(n)$ that does not run through a census of spectra.
3. **Weyl tensors with $W^2\propto\mathrm{Id}$**: one exists on $\mathbb R^5$ with $Q(W)=0$ ([screen](../scripts/misc/screen_weyl_square_identity.py)); their classification bears on which symmetric configurations exist.

**Fence posts bearing on them.** [F77](../findings/fence-posts.md) (per-eigenspace ingredients leave a window at $n\ge19$), [F76](../findings/fence-posts.md) (the class-level summed pair test cannot close (c)), [F39](../findings/fence-posts.md) (the Hessian supplies no test direction at the saddle), and the averaging barrier in [the note](2026-09-25-I73c-per-vector-rows.md) (second moments cannot separate a star from a spread isotropy).
