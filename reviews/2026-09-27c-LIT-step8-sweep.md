## Phase 3a, the screen: global comparison along $Q$-subalgebras

**Derived here, by hand.** Let $V\subseteq\mathcal W_n$ be closed under $Q$ (every fixed space $\mathcal W_n^H$, $H\subseteq O(n)$, and the coordinate-diagonal locus of any frame), $\theta_V$ the maximum of $P$ on $V$'s unit sphere, and $W$ a unit global maximiser with $Q(W)=\theta W$. With $D=\Pi_VW$, $F=W-D$, $d=\lVert D\rVert^2$: $\langle Q(D),F\rangle=0$, so pairing criticality with $D$ and with $W$ gives $P(F)=\theta(1-3d)+2P(D)$, and comparing $W$ with the unit vector $D-F$ gives $P(F)\ge0$. So
$$\theta(3d-1)\le2P(D)\le2\theta_Vd^{3/2},\qquad\text{i.e.}\qquad 3d-2r\,d^{3/2}\le1,\quad r=\theta_V/\theta .$$
At $r=1$ this is $(1-\sqrt d)^2(1+2\sqrt d)\ge0$ and says nothing; at $r<1$ it caps $d$ strictly below $1$ ($r=0.9$ gives $d\le0.64$). **A global maximiser above a $Q$-subalgebra's top value is far from that subalgebra.** *Classified against the record: the identity is [L214](../findings/second-order.md)'s frame split ($\theta t=P(D)+a$, $\theta(1-t)=2a+P(N)$) at the diagonal locus, which gives $P(N)=\theta(1-3t)+2P(D)$; L214 pairs it with the second-order bound $P(D)\le\tfrac\theta2t(1+t)$, an upper bound, and the reflection gives the lower bound $2P(D)\ge\theta(3t-1)$ from global maximality. Agreement on the identity; the global comparison is [L219](../findings/second-order.md)'s step 4 at a coordinate reflection (below), so what is new is the generality of $V$.* On a one-dimensional $V$ spanned by a critical point it reduces to $1-3c^2+2rc^3\ge0$, which is the plane through $W$ and $V$ and adds nothing; the content is in large $V$.

**What it does to (b)**: nothing is asked of a saddle by a global argument, so (b), the pair test's blindness at a saddle, is a limit of the instrument and not of the theorem, and retires under any route that argues from global maximality. **What it does to the asymmetric spectra**: nothing directly. The lemma speaks of distance to subalgebras, not of the number of values, and no subgroup $H$ adapted to a three-value $W$ was found whose fixed space has a known top below $\theta_0$ and must carry a large share of $W$.

**Its pairing with the diagonal locus is fenced, by [F67](../findings/fence-posts.md) and [trap 26](../findings/INDEX-traps.md).** The coordinate-diagonal locus of any frame is a $Q$-subalgebra with top $\Theta_n=\theta_0(n)$ at even $n$ by [L57](../findings/problem.md). Read as a bound on the value, the lemma there is $\theta\le\theta_0\,g(t)$ with $g(t)=\frac{2t^{3/2}}{3t-1}$, and $g(t)-1=\frac{(1-\sqrt t)^2(1+2\sqrt t)}{3t-1}>0$ at every $t\in(\tfrac13,1)$: exact at the diagonal end and above $\theta_0$ everywhere below it, which is trap 26's shape. **So a lower bound on the diagonal mass that does not depend on $\theta$ closes nothing through it.** Closing needs $t\ge d^*(\theta_0/\theta)$, and from $3(1-\sqrt t)^2\approx2(1-\theta_0/\theta)$ near $t=1$, $d^*\approx1-2\sqrt{2(1-\theta_0/\theta)/3}$, a diagonal mass tending to $1$ as $\theta\downarrow\theta_0$. **And (MG) supplies no mass bound at all**: it is the value dichotomy $\rho\le\max\{\sqrt{3/2},P(A)/\lVert A\rVert^3\}$, which with L57 closes at even $n\ge12$ by itself.

**The mechanism is the record's, at a coordinate reflection.** [L219](../findings/second-order.md)'s step 4 is the same global comparison: at a reflection $P(F)=0$ by parity, so the competitor $D-F$ gives nothing, and the competitor $-D/\lVert D\rVert$ gives $\lVert c\rVert^2\le\tfrac34$ (Lean `odd_mass_max`). R51 is that comparison at every $Q$-subalgebra. [R26](../routes.md), whose calculus L219 is, closed on the reflection lemma's scalar forms being F67, and the diagonal-locus pairing above is the same closure. **What survives is a subalgebra whose top $\theta_V$ lies strictly below $\theta_0$**: a fixed share $t_0$ of it closes whenever $\theta_V\,g(t_0)\le\theta_0$, and no such subalgebra carrying a provable share is known.
