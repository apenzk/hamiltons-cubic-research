## A. Is the competitor right?

**The record's $u$.** [route-a.md:182](../findings/route-a.md#L182): *"$u=W_{\mathcal S^4(\alpha)}$ the $\mathbb{CP}^2$-type competitor of unit norm, $\theta^*=P(u)=\sqrt{3/2}$"* and *"At $u$, $\delta^2=\tfrac19$"*. **The Lean object.** [Families.lean:826](../lean/HamiltonsCubic/Base/Families.lean#L826) defines `WCP2 X = proj bivA 1 X + proj bivB (−½) X + proj bivC (−½) X` with `proj v c X = (c ⟨v,X⟩) • v`. `bivA = φ₀₁+φ₂₃`, `bivB = φ₀₂+φ₃₁`, `bivC = φ₀₃+φ₁₂` are self-dual ($\star e_{02}=e_{31}$ and $\star e_{03}=e_{12}$ since $(0,2,3,1)$ and $(0,3,1,2)$ are even), mutually orthogonal, each of `inner2`-norm² $2$ (`inner2` is $\tfrac12\mathop{\mathrm{tr}}X^\top Y$). So `WCP2` $=2P_{\hat a}-P_{\hat b}-P_{\hat c}$ on unit $\hat a,\hat b,\hat c$, norm² $4+1+1=6$, matching `WCP2_normSq`. `WCP2_specOp` identifies it with `specOp lamCP2 omCP2`. `lamU = (√6)⁻¹ · lamCP2` and `omU = pad ∘ omCP2` then present $\tfrac1{\sqrt6}(2P_{\hat a}-P_{\hat b}-P_{\hat c})$ padded, which is the record's spectrum. [RelationC.lean:62](../lean/HamiltonsCubic/Base/RelationC.lean#L62) gives $P(W)^2\cdot2=3\cdot6^3$, so $P^2=324/216=\tfrac32$ at unit norm and $\theta^*=\sqrt{3/2}$.

**The presentation is orthogonal but not normalised, and `defectSq` still reads the true $W_-$.** An eigenbivector $\omega$ of `inner2`-norm² $2$ carries eigenvalue $2\lambda$, and `negPartScalar` is positively homogeneous, so $\mathrm{negPart}(2\lambda)\hat\omega\otimes\hat\omega=\mathrm{negPart}(\lambda)\,\omega\otimes\omega$, which is `negPartOp`'s term. The same holds for the appended arc presentation, which is orthogonal because the supports are disjoint.

**$\delta^2(W_{\mathbb{CP}^2})=\tfrac23$ by hand.** With the convention $W_-=\tfrac12(\lvert W\rvert-W)$, $W_-=P_{\hat b}+P_{\hat c}$. At $n=4$, `alt4` of $\hat\omega_{ij}\hat\omega_{kl}$ at $(0,1,2,3)$ is $\tfrac13(\hat\omega_{01}\hat\omega_{23}+\hat\omega_{02}\hat\omega_{31}+\hat\omega_{03}\hat\omega_{12})=\tfrac13\cdot\tfrac12=\tfrac16$ for $\hat b$ and for $\hat c$, and Alt is the multiple of $\varepsilon$ with that coefficient, in agreement with $\langle P_{\hat\omega},\star\rangle/\lVert\star\rVert^2=1/6$. So $\mathrm{Alt}(W_-)=\tfrac13\varepsilon$, and `fourNormSq` $\varepsilon=\tfrac14\cdot24=6$ gives $\delta^2=\tfrac19\cdot6=\tfrac23$. At unit norm $a=\tfrac1{3\sqrt6}$ and $a^2\cdot6=\tfrac19$, the record's line. `comp_negPart` is exactly this: `negPartScalar` sends $(1,-\tfrac12,-\tfrac12)$ to $(0,\tfrac12,\tfrac12)$, and $\tfrac12 b_{ij}b_{kl}=\hat b_{ij}\hat b_{kl}$.

**Observation O1, not a gap.** No Lean statement says `normSq (specOp lamU omU) = 1` in $\mathbb R^n$, though [DefectCP2.lean:162](../lean/HamiltonsCubic/Results/DefectCP2.lean#L162)'s docstring and the L134 row call it "the unit competitor". *Screen, not evidence:* the scratch file proves `normSq (specOp lamCP2 omCP2) = 6` at $n=4$ from `WCP2_specOp` and `WCP2_normSq` in one rewrite, and it compiles. *Arguing the opposite:* no theorem under review has unit norm as a hypothesis or a conclusion, $\delta^2$'s value depends only on the scale, which is pinned by `WCP2_normSq`, and padding by zeros preserves `normSq` term by term.
