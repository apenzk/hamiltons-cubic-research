## A. L70Sharp against Lemma L70's excluded-index blockquote

**The mass.** `bAB ψ cls a b x` $=\tfrac12\sum_{\gamma\in a}\sum_{\delta\in b}\langle[\psi_\gamma,\psi_\delta],x\rangle^2$, the record's $\tfrac12c^2_{vab}$ aggregated over the two classes. `rowA_sum` gives $\sum b_{ab}=n-2$, the record's $\tfrac12\sum c^2_{vab}=\tfrac12\cdot2(n-2)$. `rowC_sum` gives $\sum\lambda_a\lambda_bb_{ab}=\lambda_{\min}(\theta-\lambda_{\min})$, the record's "$\lambda_{\min}^2+\langle v,W^\#v\rangle=\theta\lambda_{\min}$" in class form.

**`sharp_eq`.** $D_1=\sum(\lambda_a\lambda_b-m_1)b_{ab}=\lambda_{\min}(\theta-\lambda_{\min})-(n-2)m_1$. At $m_1=\lambda_{\min}\lambda_{\max}$ this is the record's $D(v)$, and dividing by $\lambda_{\min}<0$ gives $\theta=\lambda_{\min}+(n-2)\lambda_{\max}-D/\lvert\lambda_{\min}\rvert$ exactly (screen 1, which checks the algebra only). At general $m_1$ it is the blockquote's "the sharp version subtracts $m_1$".

**Is the Lean $m_1$ the record's?** The record's $m_1$ is "the minimum over the support", the support of $\mathrm{ad}(v)$ being the pairs with $c_{vab}\ne0$. The Lean takes any $m_1$ with $m_1\le\lambda_a\lambda_b$ on every class pair with `bAB ≠ 0`. `bAB` is a sum of squares, so it is nonzero exactly when some index pair inside the two classes carries mass, and $\lambda_a\lambda_b$ is constant on that class pair (`hWP`). The set of products on class pairs carrying mass is therefore the set of products on index pairs carrying mass, and the largest admissible $m_1$ is the record's. Every smaller $m_1$ gives a weaker true bound. The equality clause stays correct at a non-sharp $m_1$: there $D_1>0$ whenever mass is present, and the right side "every pair carrying mass has $\lambda_a\lambda_b=m_1$" is false, so both sides fail together.

**Sign at $\lambda_{\min}<0$.** `sharp_bound` rewrites the target as $\theta\le(\lambda_{\min}^2+(n-2)m_1)/\lambda_{\min}$ and uses `le_div_iff_of_neg`, giving $\theta\lambda_{\min}\ge\lambda_{\min}^2+(n-2)m_1$, which is $D_1\ge0$. The direction is right. `sharp_le_boxed`: $m_1/\lambda_{\min}\le\lambda_{\max}\iff m_1\ge\lambda_{\min}\lambda_{\max}$ at $\lambda_{\min}<0$ (`div_le_iff_of_neg`), multiplied by $n-2\ge0$. Right. `L70_sharp_equality`'s two directions are $\theta\lambda_{\min}=\lambda_{\min}^2+(n-2)m_1\iff D_1=0$, with $\lambda_{\min}\ne0$ consumed by `field_simp`.

**The equality condition.** `sharp_zero_iff` at the sharpest $m_1$ is "every pair carrying mass achieves $m_1$", the blockquote's "ad(v) concentrate on the pairs achieving $m_1$".

**A widening, not a defect.** `lmin` is any eigenvalue with a unit skew eigenvector `x` and `lmin < 0`, never required to be the least. So `L70_sharp_bound` holds at every negative row, which is the negative half of the per-row family at bounds.md line 583 ($\theta\le\lambda_a+(n-2)m_a/\lambda_a$ at $\lambda_a<0$) with $m_a$ replaced by the support minimum, which is at least $m_a$ and so at least as strong. The docstring's "unit `λ_min`-eigenvector" is narrower than the theorem.

**Two observations.** The `sharp_le_boxed` docstring's "equal at $m_1=\lambda_{\min}\lambda_{\max}$, which is the case whenever a pair $(\lambda_{\min},\lambda_{\max})$ carries mass" is prose, not a typed clause, and it holds when $\lambda_{\min}\le0\le\lambda_{\max}$ are the extreme eigenvalues, which on $\mathcal W_n$ tracelessness supplies. Separately, bounds.md uses $m_1$ for two objects, the support minimum here (line 222) and the first moment $m_1=-4\theta$ of the L107 to L108 block (lines 477 and 497). The Lean uses the first sense throughout, so nothing is misread, but the record carries the collision.

## B. L24Hess against Lemma L24 and L134(a)

**`Pcubic2_circle`.** For $c\ne0$, $ca+sv=c(a+\tfrac scv)$, so $P=c^3P(a+\tfrac scv)=c^3\big(P(a)+3\tfrac{s^2}{c^2}\langle a,Q(v)\rangle+\tfrac{s^3}{c^3}P(v)\big)$ by `Pcubic2_line`, which needs only self-adjointness and $\langle v,Q(a)\rangle=0$. The $c=0$ case is $s^3P(v)$. Both match the displayed formula.

**The constants by hand.** Near $0$: $\cos^3t=1-\tfrac32t^2+O(t^4)$, $3\cos t\sin^2t=3t^2+O(t^4)$, $\sin^3t=O(t^3)$. So $f''(0)=-3P(a)+6\langle a,Q(v)\rangle$, the $P(v)$ term dropping out. `circle_deriv2` states exactly this, and `L24_hessian`'s $6(\langle a,Q(v)\rangle-\tfrac12P(a))$ is the same number. The `deriv (deriv f)` is genuine: `hf` rewrites $f$ everywhere by `funext`, and the closed form is differentiable on all of $\mathbb R$ (`circle_hasDerivAt` at every $t$).

**Is it the Riemannian Hessian?** For $P$ cubic, $\nabla P(W)=3Q(W)$ and $\nabla^2P_W(v,v)=6\langle W,Q(v)\rangle=6\langle W\circ v,v\rangle$ by full symmetry of $T$. On the unit sphere of $\mathcal W_n$ at unit $a$, the Riemannian Hessian is $\nabla^2P_a(v,v)-\langle\nabla P(a),a\rangle\lVert v\rVert^2=6\langle a,Q(v)\rangle-3P(a)\lVert v\rVert^2$, and for $\gamma(t)=\cos t\,a+\sin t\,v$ with $v$ unit and $\perp a$, a unit-speed geodesic, $(P\circ\gamma)''(0)$ equals it. With $\theta=P(a)$ and $e=a/\theta$, $6\langle a,Q(v)\rangle=6\theta\langle L_ev,v\rangle$, so the Hessian is $6\theta(\langle L_ev,v\rangle-\tfrac12)$, second-order.md line 61's $6\theta(L_e-\tfrac12\mathrm{Id})$ read as a quadratic form, and its half is route-a.md line 186's $3\theta^*(\langle L_eH,H\rangle-\tfrac12)$ at $u$ with $\theta^*=P(u)$. `hLe` computes $\langle L_ev,v\rangle=P(a)^{-1}\langle a,Q(v)\rangle$ through `foxProd2_smul_left` and `innerOp2_foxProd2_self`, and `hθ` is consumed by `field_simp`.

**Norm-free?** Yes. No step uses $\lVert a\rVert$ or $\lVert v\rVert$: `Pcubic2_circle` needs self-adjointness and $\langle v,Q(a)\rangle=0$, the latter from `hcrit` and `hperp`. The docstring's claim is also right in its other half: away from unit $a$, $v$ the curve leaves the unit sphere, $P(a)$ is not the record's $\theta=P/\lVert a\rVert^3$, and at non-unit $v$ the right side carries $-\tfrac12$ where the Hessian carries $-\tfrac12\lVert v\rVert^2$. So the identity holds at every norm and reads as the Hessian at unit $a$, $v$ only, which is what lines 91 to 93 say. `L24_hessian`'s docstring says "a unit critical Weyl `a`" while the theorem assumes no norm, a narrower description than the statement (observation).

**Criticality.** `hcrit : Qop2 a = lam • a` is the record's $Q(W)=\theta W$ ([conventions.md](../findings/conventions.md) line 151, as r1448 A found) and is consumed only to give $\langle v,Q(a)\rangle=\lambda\langle v,a\rangle=0$. L134 applies (a) at $u=W_{\mathcal S^4(\alpha)}$, a unit critical point with $\theta^*=P(u)$, so the hypotheses are the Setting's.
