#### Lemma L244, the per-vector ceiling on a symmetric three-value critical point

*Gate 2 owed. The linear-arithmetic core is typed in [L244SymmetricPerVector.lean](../lean/HamiltonsCubic/Results/L244SymmetricPerVector.lean), the per-vector rows entering as hypotheses. Working note: [the per-vector rows](../reviews/2026-09-25-I73c-per-vector-rows.md).*

**Setting.** $W$ a unit critical point, $W^2+W^\#=\theta W$, with three eigenvalues; $P_a$ the eigenprojections, $B_{ab}=P_a\#P_b\succeq0$, and for unit $x$, $b_{ab}(x)=\langle B_{ab}x,x\rangle=\tfrac12\sum_{\gamma\in E_a,\delta\in E_b}\langle[\psi_\gamma,\psi_\delta],x\rangle^2$. $\kappa_r(x)$ is the sum of the top $r$ eigenvalues of $-\mathrm{ad}_x^2$.

**Statement.** (i) **The per-vector rows.** Since $Q(\mathcal W_n)\subseteq\mathcal W_n$ ([L234](../findings/second-order.md#lemma-l234-the-projected-pair-quotient-in-closed-form-at-every-weyl-e), step (1)), $W^\#=\theta W-W^2$ is an operator identity, so for every unit $x\in E_c$:
$$\sum_{a,b}b_{ab}(x)=n-2,\qquad\sum_{a,b}\lambda_b\,b_{ab}(x)=-\lambda_c,\qquad\sum_{a,b}\lambda_a\lambda_b\,b_{ab}(x)=\lambda_c(\theta-\lambda_c),$$
with source caps $2\sum_bb_{ab}(x)\le\kappa_{m_a}(x)$, and $\kappa_{m_c-1}(x)$ for $a=c$ since $x\in\ker\mathrm{ad}_x$. (ii) **The symmetric family.** If the eigenvalues are $(-a,0,a)$, $a=1/\sqrt{2p}$, with multiplicities $(p,N-2p,p)$, then every unit $x$ in the negative eigenspace gives
$$\theta\ \le\ \frac{\min\big(\kappa_{p-1}(x),\ \kappa_p(x)-2,\ n-4\big)-1}{\sqrt{2p}},\qquad\text{in particular}\qquad\theta\le\frac{n-5}{\sqrt{2p}} .$$

**Proof.** (i) Pair $\sum_{a,b}B_{ab}=I\#I=(n-2)I$, $\sum_{a,b}\lambda_bB_{ab}=I\#W=-W$ (row (B), a Weyl identity) and $\sum_{a,b}\lambda_a\lambda_bB_{ab}=W^\#=\theta W-W^2$ with $x$; each right side is $(\cdot)\,x$ on $E_c$. The caps: $\sum_{\gamma\in E_a}\lVert[x,\psi_\gamma]\rVert^2=2\sum_bb_{ab}(x)$ by invariance, and Ky Fan bounds the trace of $-\mathrm{ad}_x^2$ over the $m_a$-dimensional $E_a$, or over $E_c\cap x^\perp$ for $a=c$. (ii) At $x\in E_0$, row (B) divided by $a$ is $b_{12}+b_{22}-b_{00}-b_{01}=1$, and row (C) divided by $a^2$ is $2b_{02}-b_{00}-b_{22}=K$ with $K=\theta/a+1$. Row (A) then gives $K=(n-4)-4b_{00}-4b_{01}-b_{11}\le n-4$; the $E_0$ cap gives $K\le2b_{02}\le2(b_{00}+b_{01}+b_{02})\le\kappa_{p-1}(x)$; the $E_2$ cap gives $K+2\le2b_{02}+2(b_{12}+b_{22})\le\kappa_p(x)$. $\square$

**Checks.** *The operator identity and the per-vector rows hold at $S^5\times S^7$ to $10^{-16}$ at random eigenspace vectors, and the per-vector ceiling there is tight*, its smallest value over random vectors of the extreme classes being $S^5\times S^7$'s own $\theta=1.2282$ (a screen of the general six-variable programme, [screen_I73c_per_vector_ceiling.py](../scripts/misc/screen_I73c_per_vector_ceiling.py), which (ii) reproduces in closed form).

**What it does to (c) at $n=12$.** The $x$-free bound $\theta\le7/\sqrt{2p}$ excludes every symmetric three-value critical point above $\theta_0(12)$ with $p\ge17$ ($\tfrac{49}{34}<\tfrac{220}{144}$), and not at $p=16$. **Below that, an equal-angle $x\in E_0$ caps $\theta$ at $5/\sqrt{24}\approx1.0206$ and a rank-four equal-angle one at $6/\sqrt{24}\approx1.2247$ at $p=12$**, both below $\theta_0(12)$, so the negative eigenspace of such a point contains neither. *[L242](../findings/bounds-relaxations.md#lemma-l242-the-capped-three-value-class-system-reaches-above-sqrt32-under-every-valid-seven-frame-cap)'s spectrum is consistent with the $x$-free bound ($1.361\le1.4289$).* **Owed**: the same derivation at a general three-value spectrum, where the 138 asymmetric survivors live.
