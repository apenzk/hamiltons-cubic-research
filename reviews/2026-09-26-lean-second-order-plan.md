# Lean build plan: the one-sided second-order expansion of the defect, for L134(b)

**Goal.** [route-a.md](../findings/route-a.md), Lemma L134(b), takes $c_2(H)$ to be the $t^2$ coefficient of the one-sided expansion of $\delta^2$ along the great circle $\cos t\,u+\sin t\,H$ at the padded $\mathbb{CP}^2$ competitor, and cites Rellich's theorem for it. The Lean statement is the expansion itself: for every symmetric $H$ there are $c_1,c_2$ with $\delta^2(\cos t\,u+\sin t\,H)=\tfrac19+c_1t+c_2t^2+o(t^2)$ as $t\to0^+$. With `L17` this gives $c_1=-2aF(H)$, so on $\{F=0\}$ the expansion is the record's $\tfrac19+c_2t^2+o(t^2)$.

**Route, which replaces Rellich.** It is the first-order build of [2026-09-26-lean-DK-plan.md](2026-09-26-lean-DK-plan.md) run one order further. It rests on two facts already proved there: the negative part's one-sided derivative holds at every symmetric operator, not only at $u$, and the value on a block-diagonal perturbation is exact.

## Milestones

1. **The exact block value, uniformly.** `specFn_blk` for a block-diagonal $X$ holds on $[0,t_0]$ with $t_0=\gamma/(2(\lVert X\rVert_{HS}^2+1))$. For the family $X(t)=B+tB_2$ the bound $C=2\lVert B\rVert_{HS}^2+2\lVert B_2\rVert_{HS}^2$ serves every $t\in[0,1]$, with $t_0$ capped at $1$, so $g(u+tB+t^2B_2)=g(u)+t\big(-\sum_{\lambda<0}P_\lambda(B+tB_2)P_\lambda+g(P_0(B+tB_2)P_0)\big)$ exactly for small $t\ge0$. **Proved** in [Base/SecondOrder.lean](../lean/HamiltonsCubic/Base/SecondOrder.lean) as `specFn_blk_family`, on [Base/BlockValue.lean](../lean/HamiltonsCubic/Base/BlockValue.lean)'s `specFn_blk_bound`.
2. **The kernel block to second order.** By linearity of Schur multipliers (`schur_add_op`, `schur_smul_op`), $P_0(B+tB_2)P_0=D+tD_2$ with $D=P_0BP_0$. The one-sided derivative of [NegPartDeriv.lean](../lean/HamiltonsCubic/Base/NegPartDeriv.lean), applied at $D$ rather than at $u$, gives $g(D)+t\,d^+g(D;D_2)+o(t)$. **Proved** as `exp2_negPart_blk` in [Base/SecondAssembly.lean](../lean/HamiltonsCubic/Base/SecondAssembly.lean).
3. **The rotation to second order.** $U(t)=\exp(tK_1)\exp(t^2K_2)$, with $[K_1,u]$ removing the off-diagonal part of $H$ and $[K_2,u]$ that of the $t^2$ coefficient. This gives $U(t)^*W(t)U(t)=u+tB+t^2B_2+o(t^2)$ with $B$, $B_2$ block-diagonal. The expansion of $\exp$ to second order comes from its power series. **Proved** in [Base/Exp2.lean](../lean/HamiltonsCubic/Base/Exp2.lean): the calculus `Exp2` of one-sided expansions $a+tb+t^2c+o(t^2)$ with sums and products (`Exp2.mul`), `exp2_rot`, `exp2_rot_sq`, and **`exp2_conj`**, $R(t)^*W(t)R(t)=u+t(H-[K,u])+t^2(M_2-[K_2,u])+o(t^2)$ with $M_2$ explicit. The choice of $K$, $K_2$ as the generators of [Schur.lean](../lean/HamiltonsCubic/Base/Schur.lean), so that both coefficients are block-diagonal, is `commutator_toCLM` in [Base/SecondAssembly.lean](../lean/HamiltonsCubic/Base/SecondAssembly.lean).
4. **Assembly.** Conjugating back, $g(W(t))=g(u)+tA_1+t^2A_2+o(t^2)$ for $t\ge0$. The $o(t^2)$ terms are carried by the Hilbert–Schmidt Lipschitz bound of [SpectralFn.lean](../lean/HamiltonsCubic/Base/SpectralFn.lean). **Proved** in [Base/SecondAssembly.lean](../lean/HamiltonsCubic/Base/SecondAssembly.lean) as `exp2_negPart_path`, for every path of symmetric operators $u+tH+t^2G+o(t^2)$. Milestones 2 and 3 are proved inside it.
5. **The defect.** $\delta^2=\lVert\mathrm{Alt}(g(W))\rVert^2$ is a quadratic image, so $\delta^2(W(t))=\delta^2(u)+t\,2\langle\mathrm{Alt}\,A_0,\mathrm{Alt}\,A_1\rangle+t^2\big(\lVert\mathrm{Alt}\,A_1\rVert^2+2\langle\mathrm{Alt}\,A_0,\mathrm{Alt}\,A_2\rangle\big)+o(t^2)$, which is L134(b)'s existence claim. **Proved** in [Results/L134b.lean](../lean/HamiltonsCubic/Results/L134b.lean) as `L134b` along the great circle $\cos t\,u+\sin t\,H$, through `exp2_cos`, `exp2_sin` and `exp2_altNormSqOf`.
6. **The display.** The circle and the line $u+tH$ agree to first order and the negative part is Lipschitz, so the two expansions of $g$ share their first coefficient, and $c_1$ is $\delta^2$'s one-sided derivative along the line, $-2aF(H)$. With $P$'s exact expansion along the circle, $\Phi(\cos t\,u+\sin t\,H)=(P(u)-\kappa/9)+2a\kappa F(H)\,t+\big(3\langle u,Q(H)\rangle-\tfrac32P(u)-\kappa c_2\big)t^2+o(t^2)$, whose $t^2$ coefficient is $\tfrac12\mathrm{Hess}(P|_S)_u[H,H]-\kappa c_2$ at unit $H$. **Proved** in [Results/L134bPhi.lean](../lean/HamiltonsCubic/Results/L134bPhi.lean) as `L134b_first` and `L134b_Phi`.

**Bound.** Multi-session, of the order of the first-order build. Milestones 1 and 2 reuse [BlockValue.lean](../lean/HamiltonsCubic/Base/BlockValue.lean) and [NegPartDeriv.lean](../lean/HamiltonsCubic/Base/NegPartDeriv.lean). Milestone 3 is the new analysis.

**What it does not give.** A value for $c_2$, or the bound $c_2\le0.3591$ that I3 needs. Those are L134(d) and L140, research items.
