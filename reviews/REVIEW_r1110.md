## 1. The lemma

- **The identity.** The record's pairing $\langle Z,Z'\rangle=\tfrac12\operatorname{tr}(Z^{\mathsf T}Z')$ is Ad-invariant, so $\mathrm{ad}_{X_0}$ is skew-adjoint and $\lVert[X_0,X_j]\rVert^2=\langle X_j,-\mathrm{ad}_{X_0}^2X_j\rangle$ with $-\mathrm{ad}_{X_0}^2\succeq0$ symmetric. Ky Fan's maximum principle gives $\sum_{j=1}^r\langle X_j,AX_j\rangle\le\lambda_1(A)+\dots+\lambda_r(A)$ for orthonormal $X_1,\dots,X_r$, and the maximum over unit $X_0$ is $\kappa_r$.
- **What is consumed.** Only $\lVert X_0\rVert=1$ and orthonormality of $X_1,\dots,X_r$ among themselves. Orthogonality to $X_0$ is never used. Restricting $A$ to $X_0^\perp$ deletes one zero eigenvalue ($X_0\in\ker\mathrm{ad}_{X_0}$) and leaves the top $r$ unchanged whenever $r$ is at most the number of nonzero eigenvalues, so it buys nothing. The stated hypothesis (all of $X_0,\dots,X_r$ orthonormal) is correct and harmlessly stronger than needed, and it is what eigenvectors of $W$ supply.
- **$r=1$** is L31's cap: $\lambda_1(-\mathrm{ad}_X^2)=(s_1+s_2)^2\le2$.

## 2. The spectrum and the constants

- **Multiplicities.** Each root pair $\pm(e_a\pm e_b)$ and, at odd $n$, $\pm e_a$ spans a two-dimensional real root space, on which $-\mathrm{ad}_X^2$ acts by $(s_a\pm s_b)^2$ or $s_a^2$. The count closes: $m+4\binom m2=\binom{2m}2$ and $m+4\binom m2+2m=\binom{2m+1}2$, the $m$ Cartan directions carrying $0$ (not stated in the note, harmless). So "each slot value with multiplicity 2" is right and $\kappa_{2k}=2\max(\text{sum of }k\text{ distinct slots})$.
- **The quadratic form.** $(s_a\pm s_b)^2=s_a^2+s_b^2\pm2s_as_b$, so a slot sum is $s^{\mathsf T}(D+A_+-A_-+\Lambda)s$ and its maximum over the unit sphere is $\lambda_{\max}(M)$. Signs of $s_a$ are free (flipping $s_a$ swaps the $+$ and $-$ slots at $a$), so the maximum over angles is the maximum over $s\in S^{m-1}$.
- **$k=1,2$ checked by hand.** $k=1$: an edge gives $2$, a loop $1$. $k=2$: $P_3$ gives $3$; edge with an endpoint loop $\tfrac{3+\sqrt5}2$; $\pm$ pair, two disjoint edges and edge plus a distant loop give $2$; two loops $1$. So $\kappa_2=4$, $\kappa_4=6$.
- **$k=3$, the enumeration.** Screen (exhaustive over three distinct slots on six indices, up to index permutation and sign flips of $s$): **19 configuration classes, maximum exactly 4**, attained only by the triangle and the star $K_{1,3}$. Values: triangle $4$, star $4$, $P_3$ plus centre loop $2+\sqrt3$, $\pm$ pair plus edge $2+\sqrt2$, $P_4$ $2+\sqrt2$, $P_3$ plus endpoint loop $3.247$ (root of $\lambda^3-5\lambda^2+6\lambda-1$), then five classes at $3$, two at $\tfrac{3+\sqrt5}2$, four at $2$, three loops at $1$. The note's sentence is correct and complete in content.
- **The edge bound, one step supplied.** $q(G)\le\max_{uv\in E}(d_u+d_v)$ is a theorem for the signless Laplacian of a simple graph, and every simple three-edge graph has $d_u+d_v\le4$ on each edge. **The configurations here are signed** ($A_+-A_-$), and the unbalanced triangle $(s_a+s_b)^2+(s_a+s_c)^2+(s_b-s_c)^2$ is not a signless Laplacian. The step is $s^{\mathsf T}(D+A_\pm)s\le\lvert s\rvert^{\mathsf T}(D+\lvert A\rvert)\lvert s\rvert$, one line, which the note should carry. (That class has index $3$.)

At that $s$ the top two eigenvalues sum to $\tfrac83<4$ and the top four to $\tfrac{16}3<6$.
