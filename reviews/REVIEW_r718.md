## The coefficient, checked against [Lemma L27](../findings/nondegeneracy.md) rather than against the symbol

**The convex-combination reading is right, and the coefficient is the minimum rather than an average.** [Lemma L27](../findings/nondegeneracy.md) makes $L_e$ a scalar on each of ten $O(m)\times O(m)$-summands, so $\tfrac12\mathrm{Hess}$ is diagonal there and the margin is $3\theta_0\sum_j(\tfrac12-\lambda_j)\lVert H_j\rVert^2$ with $\sum_j\lVert H_j\rVert^2=1$. The eight summands the domain admits carry, at $p=q=m$:

| summand | $\lambda$ | margin $3\theta_0(\tfrac12-\lambda)$ |
| --- | --- | --- |
| $S^2_0(\mathbb R^m)$, two copies, this is $H_s$ | $\tfrac{m-2}{2(m-1)}$ | $\dfrac{3\theta_0}{2(m-1)}=\dfrac{3\theta_0}{n-2}=c_p$ |
| $\Lambda^2\!\otimes\!\Lambda^2$ | $+\mu$ | $3\theta_0\dfrac{m-3}{2(m-1)}$, **the smallest off-$H_s$ margin** |
| $S^2_0\!\otimes\!S^2_0$ | $-\mu$ | $3\theta_0(\tfrac12+\mu)$ |
| $\mathrm{Weyl}_m$ (twice), $X_m\!\otimes\!\mathbb R^m$ (twice) | $0$ | $\tfrac32\theta_0$ |

*with $\tfrac12-\mu=\tfrac{(m-2)^2-1}{2(m-1)^2}=\tfrac{m-3}{2(m-1)}$ at $p=q=m$.* **The two exclusions are load-bearing and both hold.** $\langle W\rangle$ carries $\lambda=1$ and so margin $-\tfrac32\theta_0<0$; it is removed by $T_{W^*}S=W^{*\perp}$, which is [Lemma L141](../findings/second-order.md)'s "the radial direction must be excluded by hand". The orbit summand $\mathbb R^m\!\otimes\!\mathbb R^m$ carries margin $0$ and is removed by $T_{W^*}\mathcal O^\perp$, [Lemma L27](../findings/nondegeneracy.md) making $E(\tfrac12)$ **equal** to the orbit tangent. So $c(H)\ge(1-w^2)c_p+w^2c_{\min,r}$ with $c_{\min,r}=3\theta_0\tfrac{m-3}{2(m-1)}$, and

$$\rho_n=c_{\min,r}-c_p=3\theta_0\frac{m-4}{2(m-1)}=3\theta_0\frac{n-8}{2(n-2)}\qquad(m=n/2),$$

**the substitution being exact rather than approximate.** *Checked by quantity: $\theta_0(n)^2=2(n-1)(n-2)/n^2$ is dimension-dependent, so $\rho_{12}=3(1.236033)(4)/20=0.741620$ and $\rho_{14}=3(1.261680)(6)/24=0.946260$, both of [r716](REVIEW_r716.md)'s figures reproduce, and the apparent $\tfrac34\theta_0$ at $n=14$ is a different quantity from $\rho_{14}$.*

> **THE PENALTY LANDS ON THE SUMMAND THAT SUPPLIES THE COEFFICIENT, AND $\rho_n$ IS WRITTEN AS THOUGH IT DID NOT.** The $c$ in this chain is the constant of [Lemma L141](../findings/second-order.md)(b), $Q(H)=\tfrac12\mathrm{Hess}-\kappa_n\lVert D(\mathrm{Alt}\,W_-)[H]\rVert^2\le-c\lVert H\rVert^2$, the Hessian **and** the quartic-tail penalty. L141(b)'s own binding display,
> $$\frac Q{\lVert H\rVert^2}=3\theta_0\big(2\mu s-\mu-\tfrac12\big)+\tfrac29\lvert\kappa_n\rvert s,$$
> puts the whole defect response on the $(2,2)$ four-sets and, within them, increasing in $s$ up to $s=1$, the $+\mu$ eigendirection, which is $\Lambda^2\!\otimes\!\Lambda^2$, **the very summand whose margin $\rho_n$ is built from**. Reading the margin off at $s=1$ gives $c_{\min,r}^{\mathrm{eff}}=3\theta_0(\tfrac12-\mu)-\tfrac29\lvert\kappa_n\rvert$ and hence
> $$\rho_n^{\mathrm{eff}}=3\theta_0\frac{n-8}{2(n-2)}-\frac29\lvert\kappa_n\rvert,\qquad \lvert\kappa_n\rvert=9\big(\theta_0(n)-\sqrt{3/2}\big),$$
> which is $0.71904$ at $n=12$, $0.87239$ at $n=14$, $0.98564$ at $n=16$ and $\to\tfrac32\sqrt2-2(\sqrt2-\sqrt{3/2})=1.74238$. **[Lemma L149](../findings/second-order.md) charges itself with this in the paragraph "The penalty term, which had no account" (with the cruder coefficient $1$ in place of $\tfrac29$) and then the blockquote four lines above ignores it.** *Every verdict here survives the correction: $\rho_n^{\mathrm{eff}}>0$ at every even $n\ge12$ and increases. What does not survive is the exactness of C3.*

## $w^*$ leaves the interval, and it leaves it at the two dimensions the blockquote quotes

**The derivative is C2's, and I re-derived it.** For $g=\sqrt{c+\rho w^2}/(A+Bw)$,
$$\tfrac{d}{dw}g^2=\frac{2\big[\rho Aw-Bc\big]}{(A+Bw)^3},\qquad w^*=\frac{Bc}{\rho A},\qquad g(w^*)=\frac{\sqrt c}{\sqrt{A^2+B^2c/\rho}},$$
each step exact, and C3's $\kappa/\rho_n=2n/(n-8)$ likewise: $\kappa=3\theta_0\tfrac n{n-2}$ and $\rho_n=3\theta_0\tfrac{n-8}{2(n-2)}$ share $3\theta_0/(n-2)$, leaving $n\big/\tfrac{n-8}2$. Substituting into $g(w^*)$ with $A^2=n$ and $B^2c/\rho=n\kappa/\rho=2n^2/(n-8)$ gives $A^2+B^2c/\rho=n\tfrac{3n-8}{n-8}$ and C3's closed form. **All of that is right.**

> **THE STATIONARY POINT IS OUTSIDE $[0,1]$ FOR EVERY EVEN $n<16$, WHICH IS $n=10,12,14$, THE WHOLE OF THE RANGE THE REPAIR WAS MADE FOR.** With the lemma's own $c=\kappa/n$, $A=n^{1/2}$, $B=n$,
> $$w^*=\frac{Bc}{\rho A}=\frac\kappa{\rho\sqrt n}=\frac{2n}{(n-8)\sqrt n}=\frac{2\sqrt n}{n-8},$$
> and $w^*\le1$ reads $n-8\ge2\sqrt n$, i.e. $(\sqrt n-4)(\sqrt n+2)\ge0$, i.e. **$n\ge16$**. *Values: $3.16228$ at $n=10$, $1.73205$ at $n=12$, $1.24722$ at $n=14$, exactly $1$ at $n=16$, $0.84853$ at $n=18$.* **Below $n=16$ the sign of $g'$ is negative throughout $[0,1]$, so $g$ is strictly decreasing and its minimum over the admissible range is $g(1)$, not $g(w^*)$.** *The repaired form is worse in this respect than the one it replaces, dividing by $\rho_n<1$ pushes $w^*$ further out at exactly $n=12$ and $n=14$.*

**The defect is inherited, and two audits passed over it.** The unrepaired proof's $w^*_{\text{old}}=Bc/A=\kappa n^{-1/2}$ is $1.42302$ at $n=10$, $1.28452$ at $n=12$, $1.18019$ at $n=14$ and stays above $1$ for even $n\le18$. [r452](REVIEW_r452.md) row 4 and [r453](REVIEW_r453.md) row 6 each record "single interior minimum at $w^*=Bc/A$" as CONFIRMED, both re-derived the sign of $g'$ correctly, and neither compared the root with the endpoint of the interval $w$ is confined to. *[r452](REVIEW_r452.md) came closest (its row 10 observes that $w^*$ is "a weight in a different decomposition") and read the mismatch as a conflation of quantities rather than as a range violation.* **[Lemma L158](../findings/second-order.md) already prints $1.2845$ for this number and treats it as an attribution error rather than as an inadmissible minimiser.**

## What the minimum actually is, at the three readings of the denominator now on the record

*Screen, at $n=12$ and $n=14$; $g$ evaluated on a uniform grid of $[0,1]$ at $2\times10^4$ points, the ratios being repaired-over-unrepaired so every $w$-free prefactor cancels.*

| denominator | $n=12$: minimiser, ratio | $n=14$: minimiser, ratio |
| --- | --- | --- |
| $A+Bw$, $A=n^{1/2}$ (**the lemma's own**) | $w=1$, $0.9008$ | $w=1$, $0.9794$ |
| $B\min\!\big(\tfrac AB\sqrt{1-w^2}+w,1\big)$ ([r716](REVIEW_r716.md)'s admissible envelope, same $A$) | $w=0.7560$, $0.9215$ | $w=0.6832$, $0.9840$ |
| the same with $A/B=\sqrt{2/(n-2)}$, [Lemma L146](../findings/second-order.md)'s constant | $w=0.5976$, $0.9393$ | $w=0.5222$, $0.9877$ |

**C4's $0.882$ and $0.978$ appear in none of the three**, because they are $\sqrt{\kappa(n-8)/(3n-8)}\big/\sqrt{\kappa/(1+\kappa)}$, a ratio of two unconstrained infima taken at $w^*=1.732$ and $w^*_{\text{old}}=1.285$. *The direction C4 asserts is right and the effect is real: the repair does lower the constant at $n=12$ and $n=14$, by about $10\%$ and $2\%$ rather than by $12\%$ and $2.2\%$.* **The asymptote is sound**: $w^*=2\sqrt n/(n-8)\to0$, so the formula becomes attained, and the ratio runs $1.0384$ at $n=16$, $1.1552$ at $n=24$, $1.3187$ at $n=1000$, $\to1.32042$.

**The kink and $w^*$ do not merely coexist, $w^*$ is on the wrong side of it.** [Lemma L158](../findings/second-order.md)'s $w_0=(n-4)/n$ is $\tfrac23$ at $n=12$ and $\tfrac57$ at $n=14$; $w^*$ is $1.732$ and $1.247$ under the lemma's $A$, and $1.118$ and $0.8165$ under [Lemma L146](../findings/second-order.md)'s. *In all four cases $w^*>w_0$, so the stationary point sits inside the region where $\lVert H\rVert_{op}\le1$ is the operative bound, the denominator is flat in $w$, and $g$ is increasing.* **Where the envelope saturates, C2's formula prices a denominator that is not there**, and the true minimiser is $\min(w^*,w_0)$ or below.

> **AND THE LEMMA'S $A=n^{1/2}$ IS ITSELF A LOWER BOUND STATED UPWARD.** [Lemma L146](../findings/second-order.md) gives $\lVert H_s\rVert_{op}\le\sqrt{2/(n-2)}=1/\sqrt{m-1}$, so the constant term in $n\lVert H\rVert_{op}$ is $n\sqrt{2/(n-2)}=\sqrt{2n^2/(n-2)}$, which is $\sqrt{2n/(n-2)}=1.5492$ times $n^{1/2}$ at $n=12$. *A smaller $A$ is a smaller denominator and so a larger $g$*, which is the direction [Lemma L157](../findings/second-order.md) has twice been corrected for. **It is also what makes $w^*$ read differently in the two places the record computes it**, $1.2845$ against $0.82916$, a discrepancy [Lemma L158](../findings/second-order.md) records without naming its cause.

## The range, and whether it can go to zero

**$\rho_n$ is strictly increasing in even $n$ with $\rho_8=0$ and $\sup\rho_n=3\sqrt2/2=2.121320$, unattained.** Both factors of $3\theta_0(n)\tfrac{n-8}{2(n-2)}$ increase, $\theta_0(n)\uparrow\sqrt2$ and $\tfrac{n-8}{n-2}\uparrow1$. So:

- **$\rho_n>2.11$ from $n\approx1406$ onwards**, and C4's closed interval fails there. The correct statement is $\rho_n\in[\rho_{n_{\min}},\,3\sqrt2/2)$.
- **$0.45$ is $\rho_{10}$, i.e. $m=5$, and $m=5$ is outside the range of the lemma this coefficient is built on.** [Lemma L141](../findings/second-order.md)(b) is stated at $n=2m\ge12$ and [Lemma L133](../findings/second-order.md) clears $\kappa_n$ "for every $n\ge12$"; at $n=10$ the penalty even changes sign, $\theta_0(10)=1.2<\sqrt{3/2}$ making $\kappa_{10}=+0.2227$. *Over $n\ge12$ the pure-Hessian interval is $[0.74162,\,2.12132)$ and the penalised one $[0.71904,\,1.74238)$.* **[Lemma L149](../findings/second-order.md)'s $m\ge5$ comes from its own $c_0\ge\tfrac34\theta_0$ range condition and is a weaker hypothesis than its inputs carry.**
- **The lower bound is the one that does the work, and C4 is right to name it.** $n\,g(w^*)=\sqrt{\kappa/(1+\kappa/\rho)}$, which tends to $0$ as $\rho\to0^+$; at $\rho=0$ the minimum over $[0,1]$ is $g(1)=\sqrt c/(A+B)=\Theta(n^{-3/2})$. *So $\Theta(n^{-1})$ genuinely requires $\rho_n$ bounded away from $0$, which $\rho_n\ge0.71904$ supplies. The upper bound enters nothing.*

**Odd $n$: the lemma claims nothing there, and the $n$-form invites the claim.** [Lemma L149](../findings/second-order.md) lives at $p=q=m$, $n=2m$, so $n$ is even throughout and $n-8=2(m-4)$, $n-2=2(m-1)$. Written in $n$ alone, $\rho_n$ evaluates at odd $n$ and returns a number, but [conventions.md](../findings/conventions.md) records that $\theta_0(n)$ at odd $n$ is **not** the diagonal maximum, $\Theta_n<\theta_0(n)$ there, and that this exact gloss is what regrew [Lemma L0](../findings/problem.md)'s refuted constant. *The $n$-form should carry "even $n$" on its face.*

## What I did not settle

- **Whether $\tfrac29$ or $1$ is the right penalty coefficient in $\rho_n^{\mathrm{eff}}$.** [Lemma L141](../findings/second-order.md)(b) derives $\tfrac29$ from [Lemma L133](../findings/second-order.md)'s support table; [Lemma L149](../findings/second-order.md)'s own penalty paragraph uses $1$ together with the coarser margin $\tfrac32\theta_0$ and reaches $0.416$ asymptotically. *Both leave $\rho^{\mathrm{eff}}>0$ at every even $n\ge12$, so no verdict here turns on the choice; the record carrying two accountings of one quantity is itself worth a line.*
- **The true minimiser once $(1-\eta^*)^2$ is inside the minimisation.** [r714](REVIEW_r714.md) and [r716](REVIEW_r716.md) disagree with [r713](REVIEW_r713.md) on this, and the blockquote I audit is written for the prefactor-free $g$. *My ratios cancel every $w$-free factor, so they are unaffected; the absolute minimiser is not mine to settle.*
- **$\lvert\kappa_n\rvert$ at finite $n$ against its asymptote.** I used $\lvert\kappa_n\rvert=9(\theta_0(n)-\sqrt{3/2})$ throughout, [Lemma L133](../findings/second-order.md)'s closed form, giving $0.101594$ at $n=12$ and $1.705220$ in the limit, the finite-$n$ reading [Lemma L158](../findings/second-order.md) settles in favour of.

**Inherited without re-derivation**: [Lemma L27](../findings/nondegeneracy.md)'s ten-summand decomposition and its multiplicity-freeness; [Lemma L141](../findings/second-order.md)(b)'s block additivity and its $s$-display; [Lemma L133](../findings/second-order.md)'s support table and $\kappa_n=9(\sqrt{3/2}-\theta_0)$; [Lemma L146](../findings/second-order.md)'s $1/\sqrt{m-1}$; [Lemma L148](../findings/second-order.md)'s vanishing of the defect on $H_s$; [Lemma L158](../findings/second-order.md)'s $w_0=(n-4)/n$ and [r716](REVIEW_r716.md)'s homogeneous envelope. **Re-derived here**: the per-summand margin table and $\tfrac12-\mu=\tfrac{m-3}{2(m-1)}$ at $p=q$; the $m\mapsto n$ substitution; $\mathrm{sign}\,g'$, $w^*$ and $g(w^*)$ for general $\rho$; $\kappa/\rho_n=2n/(n-8)$ and the closed form; $w^*=2\sqrt n/(n-8)$ and its threshold $n\ge16$; $\rho_n^{\mathrm{eff}}$; the monotonicity and supremum of $\rho_n$; and $n\,g(w^*)=\sqrt{\kappa/(1+\kappa/\rho)}$.

3. **Withdraw $0.882$ and $0.978$** and record $0.9008$ and $0.9794$ against the lemma's own denominator, or $0.9215$ and $0.9840$ against [r716](REVIEW_r716.md)'s admissible envelope, naming which denominator each is read at. **Keep $1.32$** with "for $n\ge16$" beside it.
