### Reviewers 48 and 49: the block-sum law, and a bound that was circular

**They agree on all five verdicts, found the same counterexample independently, and both ran the repository's code alongside their own rebuilds.** Own implementations in [review_r48_core.py](scripts/review_r48_core.py) and [review_r49_core.py](scripts/review_r49_core.py) with six and eleven drivers. Reports in [review_r48_report.md](agent_logs/review_r48_report.md) and [review_r49_report.md](agent_logs/review_r49_report.md). Both reproduced [b_block_sum_law.py](scripts/b_block_sum_law.py) digit for digit, including its exactly-zero residual: **the repository code does what its docstring says.**

**B1 CONFIRMED, with the hypothesis mis-stated.** Additivity is exact ($0.0$) but requires **disjoint index blocks**, not orthogonality as operators. Frobenius-orthogonal pairs sharing coordinates break it: $\langle W_{S^3\times S^3},W_{\mathbb{CP}^2}\rangle=0$ at $n=6$ while the cross term is $0.2152$. Proved analytically from the $c_{anm}$ triangle condition, which forces $A\cap B\ne\varnothing$ for a nonvanishing cross term. Coordinate-disjointness is **sufficient but not necessary**, a $\Lambda^+/\Lambda^-$ pair on the *same* four coordinates also has $W_A\circ W_B=0$ exactly.

**B2 CONFIRMED** at twelve and thirteen held-out block sums between them, three- and four-block sums, unequal dimensions, decomposable blocks, scattered coordinates, ambient padding, predicted against measured to $\le9\times10^{-16}$ with criticality residuals $\le4\times10^{-16}$.

**B3 REFUTED as written.** Strictness fails on the negative-$\theta$ stratum: $(+\mathrm{Fox})\oplus(-\mathrm{Fox})$ at $n=8$ gives $\theta=+0.866$ against $\min_i\theta_i=-1.2247$. The correct statement is $|\theta|<\min_i|\theta_i|$. The $k$-equal-blocks form $\theta_1/\sqrt k$ holds, verified at $k=2,3,4$ to $10^{-15}$.

**B4 REFUTED on two independent grounds, both of which the brief asked about.** First, **the hypothesis is the conjecture**: "$\theta_i\le\sqrt{3/2}$ for $n_i\le11$" is the open low-dimensional half of Conjecture A. One reviewer proved it outright at $n_i=4$ ($P=9(\det W^++\det W^-)$ on $\mathrm{Weyl}_4$, exact to $10^{-13}$, giving $\max P=\sqrt{3/2}$) and found that for $5\le n_i\le11$ the record's own word is "presumed", the only unconditional statement being a dichotomy at $8\le n\le11$, with Nienhaus 4.19 recorded as conditional. So the bound was circular. Second, **the constant is false from $n=16$**: $S^6\times S^6$ on twelve coordinates plus $W_{\mathbb{CP}^2}$ on four gives $\theta=0.869988927484>\sqrt3/2$, criticality residual $2.7\times10^{-16}$, Ricci $1.4\times10^{-16}$, Bianchi $0$, found independently by both. A second witness at $n=20$, $S^8\times S^8\oplus\mathrm{Fox}$, reaches $0.885202$. **The $\sqrt3/2$ bound holds only for $n\le15$**, four dimensions of the conjecture's range. The Fox-plateau agreement is a tautology, since the plateau *is* two $W_{\mathbb{CP}^2}$ blocks, the law evaluated at its own example.

**The replacement is better than the claim, and needs saying as an induction.** $|\theta|\le\max_iM(n_i)/\sqrt2<1<\theta_0(n)$ for every $n\ge12$, with $D/\theta_0\le0.706$, requiring only $\theta_i<\sqrt2$, far weaker than $\sqrt{3/2}$. It is an induction on $n$, which the original phrasing concealed.

**B5 PLAUSIBLE but insufficient, and it exposes two errors in the record.** The recorded claim that every ungraded $S=0$ point sits at $\theta/\theta_0\le0.53$, capped at $0.89$, is **false**: padded $W_{\mathbb{CP}^2}$ is on that list at $0.9909\,\theta_0(12)$ and above $\theta_0$ at $n=8,10$, with $S=0$ exactly and ungradedness reproduced independently (leaks $16/24/32$). Padded Fox, padded $S^4\times S^4$ and padded $S^3\times S^3$ sit at $0.9909$, $0.9269$ and $0.8528$. **Padding does not lower $\theta$, so the law never covered that half**, the $0.89$ figure belongs to the degenerate-point statement, and the law's own cap at $n=12$ is $0.7006$. Separately, the direct-sum saddle lemma already on record is verified correct ($\mathrm{Hess}=+3\theta\|H\|^2$) and already excludes decomposable *maximizers* unconditionally, so the law's distinct value is over the whole decomposable critical set rather than over maximizers.

**A third class the taxonomy is missing.** "Decomposable" was equated with "coordinate block sum", and the real condition is $W_A\circ W_B=0$. At $n=8$, $228$ of $300$ Levenberg–Marquardt starts found critical companions of padded $W_{\mathbb{CP}^2}$ carrying $12$–$73\%$ of their mass on cross-block planes, and one such pair obeys the harmonic-square law exactly ($0.337831962346$ predicted and measured). $\ker L_X$ runs $148$–$285$ dimensions where the coordinate picture predicts $0$–$15$. **So "block sum or padding" is not an exhaustive taxonomy of the ungraded $S=0$ points, and the crux's phrasing needs a third entry.**

**Reconciliation.** No verdict conflicts, and the two reports differ only in which extra material each pursued, one the low-dimensional status of the hypothesis and the $\mathrm{Weyl}_4$ determinant identity, the other the cross-block companions and the $\ker L_X$ dimensions.
