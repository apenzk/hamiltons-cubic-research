## H1. The competitor step is even-$n$ only

**The two mechanical steps are unimpeachable and I confirm them independently.** [Lemma L107](../findings/bounds.md) is derived at the $\lambda_{\max}$ row of the pinned calculus: $\mathbb E_{\mu_a}[Y]\le\mathbb E_{\mu_a}[X^2]\le(\lambda_{\min}+\lambda_{\max})\mathbb E_{\mu_a}[X]-\lambda_{\min}\lambda_{\max}$ with $\mathbb E_{\mu_a}[X]=-\lambda_a/(n-2)$; at $a=\arg\max$ the $\lambda_{\max}^2$ terms cancel and division by $\lambda_{\max}>0$ leaves $\theta\le(n-1)m$. [Lemma L70](../findings/bounds.md) is the same system at the $\lambda_{\min}$ row, using $\lambda_b\lambda_c\ge\lambda_{\min}\lambda_{\max}$ (valid because $\lambda_{\min}<0\le\lambda_{\max}$, which tracelessness supplies) and dividing by $\lambda_{\min}<0$. Both use row (C), which is the diagonal of $W^\#=\theta W-W^2$ and therefore holds at critical points; a maximiser is critical because the unit sphere of $\mathcal W_n$ is compact, $dP(W)[V]=3\langle Q(W),V\rangle$ by the symmetry of the trilinear form, and $Q$ maps $\mathcal W_n$ into $\mathcal W_n$ ([problem.md](../findings/problem.md), from [Böhm–Wilking](../references/boehm-wilking-2008-space-forms/notes.md) eq. (6)). So **"are L70 and the $\Pi$-bound unconditional at a maximiser", yes, both, with no further hypothesis.** The algebra is also right: $\theta_0/(n-1)=\tfrac1n\sqrt{2(n-2)/(n-1)}$ and $n\theta_0/((n-1)(n-2))=\sqrt{2/((n-1)(n-2))}$, and these are exactly the $w$ and $u=\tfrac n{n-2}w$ that [Lemma L105](../findings/bounds-relaxations.md)'s proof derives for the balanced product at $r=n(n-2)/4$. The normalisation is consistent throughout, $\theta$ is scale-invariant, $h$ and $m$ are not, and both the note and L105 work at $\sum\lambda^2=1$, so the $\sqrt2$ trap flagged in [conventions.md](../findings/conventions.md) does not bite.

**What fails is the one step the note says is the whole of maximality's contribution.** $S^{n/2}\times S^{n/2}$ does not exist at odd $n$, and the record does not merely leave the odd case open, it contradicts the note. [conventions.md](../findings/conventions.md) fixes $\Theta_n$, the diagonal maximum, as $\theta_0(n)$ at even $n$ and $\Theta_n^2=2(n-1)(n-3)/((n+1)(n-2))$ at odd $n$, with [bispectral.md](../findings/bispectral.md) tabulating $\Theta_5=0.9428090$ against $\theta_0(5)=0.9797959$ and stating $\Theta_n<\theta_0(n)$ strictly at odd $n$. [Lemma L71](../findings/bounds.md) says the same thing from the other side: *no critical point saturates [Lemma L70](../findings/bounds.md) at odd $n\ge5$*, because $\dim\mathfrak m=n^2/4\notin\mathbb Z$. So at odd $n$ the note's chain has no competitor, and its conclusion clause ("attained, simultaneously and exactly, at the balanced sphere product"), names an object that does not exist. **The theorem is not merely unproved at odd $n$; as written it does not parse there.**

**The repair is one substitution and costs nothing.** Replace $\theta_0(n)$ by $\Theta_n$ throughout: the record's diagonal theorem ([problem.md](../findings/problem.md)) gives $\langle Q(W),W\rangle\le\Theta_n\lvert W\rvert^3$ with equality exactly at $S^{\lfloor n/2\rfloor}\times S^{\lceil n/2\rceil}$ at every $n\ge4$, and that product is a genuine Einstein product of round spheres, hence a unit competitor in $\mathcal W_n$ at every $n\ge5$ (unbalanced products are catalogued critical points on this record, $S^3\times S^5$ and $S^5\times S^7$ carry recorded L70 slacks of $1.115547$ and $0.614120$). The theorem then reads $m\ge\Theta_n/(n-1)$ and $(n-2)h\ge\Theta_n\cdot\tfrac n{n-1}$ at every $n\ge5$, coinciding with the note at even $n$ and remaining true at odd $n$. As a bonus the odd case acquires a strictness statement: equality in the half-plane forces L70-saturation, which [Lemma L71](../findings/bounds.md) forbids at odd $n$, so $(n-2)h-m>\Theta_n$ strictly there.

**The screen line is vacuous at half its sample.** "Both bounds equal the balanced product's own $m$ and $h$ to twelve digits at $n=5,6,8,12,20,50$" compares closed forms to a closed form; at $n=5$ there is no balanced product to have an $m$ and an $h$. That is exactly the failure mode the screening allowance is meant to catch and did not, because nothing in the screen was an object.

**And "exactly" is proved for one of the two bounds only.** Equality in the $h$-bound forces equality in $(n-2)h\ge\theta+m$, i.e. L70-saturation at a critical point, and [Lemma L71](../findings/bounds.md) then makes $W$ the balanced product, that is a real rigidity corollary and the note is entitled to it. Equality in the $m$-bound gives only $\theta=\theta_0(n)$ together with $\Pi$-saturation, and [Lemma L105](../findings/bounds-relaxations.md) records that $\Pi$-saturation holds at *every* member of the subalgebra family (the $n=8$ torus at $0.540062$, $\mathrm{ad}(\mathfrak{su}(3))$ at $0.836660$), while [r323](REVIEW_r323.md)'s sharpening says L70-saturation is $\Pi$-saturation *plus* the ratio $h/m=n/(n-2)$, which $m$-equality does not supply. So the $m$-bound's equality case is **PLAUSIBLE, unproved from the cited lemmas**; the claim that the balanced product attains both is true and is what the sentence should say.

**One hypothesis the note could have dropped and did not.** [Lemma L71](../findings/bounds.md) carries a live caveat that the boxed L70 is the *weakened* member of the per-row family and that its equality case matches the sharp one only when $\lambda_{\min}$ has multiplicity $\ge2$. That caveat is automatically satisfied here: $D(v)=0$ forces every unit of $\mathrm{ad}(v)$'s $2(n-2)$ units of bracket mass onto pairs with $\lambda_a\lambda_b=\lambda_{\min}\lambda_{\max}$, and since $c_{vab}$ excludes the row index, a simple $\lambda_{\min}$ would leave no such pair and force $\mathrm{ad}(v)=0$, impossible in $\mathfrak{so}(n)$ for $n\ge3$. So saturation implies multiplicity $\ge2$ for free, and the uniqueness half of H1 is not exposed to that caveat.

## H2. True, weaker, and the comparison is right

**The stated form is derivable and I reconstruct its route.** [Lemma L73](../findings/bounds.md) gives $m\ge h/(n-2)$ on all of $\mathcal W_n$; feeding that into $(n-2)h\ge\theta+m\ge\theta_0+h/(n-2)$ gives $h\big[(n-2)-\tfrac1{n-2}\big]\ge\theta_0$, i.e. $h\ge\theta_0(n)(n-2)/((n-2)^2-1)$. That is [plan-maximizer](plan-maximizer.md)'s route step 2 exactly, so the item's (ii) is sound. **The numbers check**: $\theta_0(12)=\sqrt{220}/12=1.2360331$, $\times\,10/99=0.1248518$, against $\sqrt{2/110}=0.1348400$, the note's $0.12485$ and $0.13484$, and a ratio of $1.0800$, the claimed $8\%$.

**The improvement has a closed form the note did not state, and it is worth stating.** New over old is $\tfrac{n}{(n-1)(n-2)}\big/\tfrac{n-2}{(n-1)(n-3)}=\tfrac{n(n-3)}{(n-2)^2}$, which exceeds $1$ exactly when $n>4$ (so the new bound is strictly stronger at every $n$ in scope) and tends to $1$ like $1+2/n$: $8.00\%$ at $n=12$, $2.00\%$ at $n=50$. **The mechanism of the gain is that $\Pi$ beats L73 as the source of a lower bound on $m$**: at the balanced product $\theta_0/(n-1)=0.11237$ against $h/(n-2)=0.01348$, a factor of $8.3$.

## H3. Both halves wrong

**The numerical claim is false, and the bound it disparages is exactly sharp.** At $n=12$ the balanced product has $m=w=\tfrac1{12}\sqrt{20/11}=0.11236664$, $h=1.2\,w=0.13483997$, multiplicities $r=n(n-2)/4=30$ positive and $n^2/4=36$ negative (check: $30h^2+36m^2=0.545455+0.454545=1$). Then $m(h+m)=0.11236664\times0.24720661=1/36$ exactly, so $q\ge1/(m(h+m))$ reads $q\ge36$, the true value. The companion $p\ge1/(h(h+m))$ reads $p\ge30$, also exact. **Both are sharp at the balanced product**, for the structural reason that a two-eigenvalue spectrum makes every inequality in the chain $\sum\lambda^2\le h\sum_+\lambda+m\sum_-\lvert\lambda\rvert$ an equality. The sentence "weaker than $3$ at the balanced product" is off by an order of magnitude and inverts the bound's standing. **The honest statement is different**: to *lower*-bound $q$ one needs an *upper* bound on $m(h+m)$, and the record supplies none beyond $h^2+m^2\le1$, under which $\max m(h+m)=\tfrac12+\tfrac{\sqrt2}2=1.2071$ and the bound degrades to $q\ge1$. That is why the counting does not deliver three, not because it is weak at the extremum, but because it is unusable away from it.

**And three negative eigenvalues is a theorem on this record.** [plan-maximizer](plan-maximizer.md)'s I25 route step 3 reads: "[Lemma L35](../findings/stratum-two-negative.md) (domain: every $W$, dimension-free): at most two negative eigenvalues caps $\theta$ at $\sqrt{3/2}<\theta_0(n)$ for $n\ge12$; hence (iii) at a maximizer for $n\ge12$." L35 is canonical in [stratum-two-negative.md](../findings/stratum-two-negative.md), indexed in [results.md](../results.md) line 156, and carries its own Gate 2 (Reviewers 62 and 63). The arithmetic closes: $\theta_0(n)^2>3/2\iff4(n^2-3n+2)>3n^2\iff n^2-12n+8>0\iff n\ge12$ (at $n=11$, $-3<0$; at $n=12$, $+8>0$), so $\theta\ge\theta_0(n)>\sqrt{3/2}$ at every maximiser with $n\ge12$ even, and $q\le2$ is excluded. **The threshold at which the argument switches on is exactly the conjecture's own $n\ge12$**, which is a fact worth recording rather than a coincidence to be passed over. The note's "what is owed" section attacks a route the item never proposed, and asserts the negation of a consequence of an indexed lemma, the precise failure the brief's record-discipline paragraph names.

**A short argument the note missed, covering the range L35 does not.** At **every** critical point with $\theta>0$, $q\ge2$. Take $a=\arg\min$. Row (C) says $\mathbb E_{\mu_a}[\lambda_b\lambda_c]=\lambda_a(\theta-\lambda_a)/(n-2)$, which is strictly negative since $\lambda_{\min}<0<\theta-\lambda_{\min}$. But $T$ vanishes on repeated indices ([conventions.md](../findings/conventions.md); the record states the consequence explicitly at [Lemma L73](../findings/bounds.md), "in row $a$ the pairs satisfy $b\ne c$, $b\ne a$, $c\ne a$"), so $\mu_a$ is supported away from $a$; if $\lambda_{\min}$ were the only negative eigenvalue, every $\lambda_b,\lambda_c$ in the support would be $\ge0$ and the expectation would be $\ge0$. Contradiction, so a second negative index exists. Row (A) makes $\mu_a$ a probability measure, so the support is nonempty and the argument has no gap. **This needs no maximality, no $n\ge12$, and no upper bound on anything**, and it is the statement the counting was reached for. It does not extend to three: pushing the same row against the pair cap $\sum_cT_{a_1a_2c}\le2$ gives only $2m_2h\ge m(\theta+m)$, which at $n=12$ is satisfied throughout the admissible range.

## H4. The classification is incomplete, the family and the grade assignment are right

**The assignment is correct and I re-derived it rather than reading it off.** With $V_1,\dots,V_4$ the joint eigenspaces of the two lifted involutions, signs $(+,+),(+,-),(-,+),(-,-)$, the block $\mathrm{Hom}(V_i,V_j)$ carries the grade $(s_is_j,t_it_j)$. Blocks $(i,i)$ give $(+,+)$; $(1,2)$ and $(3,4)$ both give $(+,-)$; $(1,3)$ and $(2,4)$ both give $(-,+)$; $(1,4)$ and $(2,3)$ both give $(-,-)$. So $\mathfrak g_{01}=\mathrm{Hom}(V_1,V_2)\oplus\mathrm{Hom}(V_3,V_4)$, $\mathfrak g_{10}=\mathrm{Hom}(V_1,V_3)\oplus\mathrm{Hom}(V_2,V_4)$, $\mathfrak g_{11}=\mathrm{Hom}(V_1,V_4)\oplus\mathrm{Hom}(V_2,V_3)$, exactly as displayed, and the dimensions $\sum_i\binom{n_i}2$, $n_1n_2+n_3n_4$, $n_1n_3+n_2n_4$, $n_1n_4+n_2n_3$ sum to $\binom n2$ identically.

**"So it is a splitting" is the false step, and the error is in the lifting.** An automorphism of $\mathfrak{so}(n)$ is $\mathrm{Ad}_g$ for $g\in O(n)$ (for $n\ne8$; $\mathrm{Aut}=O(n)/\{\pm I\}$, since $-I$ acts trivially). $\mathrm{Ad}_g$ is an involution iff $g^2$ is central in $O(n)$, i.e. $g^2=\pm I$, **not** $g^2=I$. The case $g^2=-I$ is a complex structure $J$, and $\mathrm{Ad}_J$ is a genuine involutive automorphism with fixed algebra $\mathfrak u(m)$: for $X$ commuting with $J$, $JXJ^{-1}=-JXJ=X$; for $X$ anticommuting, $JXJ^{-1}=-X$. The note's premise from [r347](REVIEW_r347.md) (that the grading group must be elementary abelian $2$), is about the *group*, and the note silently reads it as a statement about the *lifts*.

**A counterexample at $n=6$, small enough to check by hand.** Take $J$ a complex structure on $\mathbb R^6=\mathbb C^3$ and $\sigma=I_{2,4}$ for a splitting $\mathbb R^6=\mathbb R^2\oplus\mathbb R^4$ that $J$ preserves; then $J\sigma=\sigma J$ and $\mathrm{Ad}_J,\mathrm{Ad}_\sigma$ generate a faithful $\mathbb Z_2\times\mathbb Z_2$ in $\mathrm{Aut}(\mathfrak{so}(6))$ (fixed algebras $\mathfrak u(3)$ of dimension $9$, $\mathfrak{so}(2)\oplus\mathfrak{so}(4)$ of dimension $7$, and $\mathfrak g_{00}\oplus\mathfrak g_{11}$ of dimension $7$, none of them all of $\mathfrak{so}(6)$). The grades are $\mathfrak g_{00}=\mathfrak u(1)\oplus\mathfrak u(2)$ of dimension $5$, then $9-5=4$, $7-5=2$ and $8-4=4$, summing to $15$. **No splitting of $\mathbb R^6$ into four parts has $\sum_i\binom{n_i}2=5$**: the attainable values over the partitions $6,\,51,\,42,\,411,\,33,\,321,\,3111,\,222,\,2211$ are $15,10,7,6,6,4,3,3,2$. So this grading is outside the stated family, and the family is not all of them.

**A second gap the brief names and the note does not answer.** At $n=8$, $\mathrm{Aut}(\mathfrak{so}(8))=PO(8)\rtimes S_3$; the record itself uses triality at $n=8$ ([target.md](../findings/target.md), the $\mathbb{HP}^2$/$S^3\times S^5$ and $\mathbb{CP}^4$/$S^2\times S^6$ isospectral pairs, "it exists only at $n=8$"). A $\mathbb Z_2\times\mathbb Z_2$ generated with an order-two outer automorphism is not induced by any element of $O(8)$ and so cannot be a splitting.

**Containment of the damage.** Nothing downstream in the note actually needs completeness. H5's zero-count is proved below from the group alone; H6 is an existence claim that more gradings only help. What must be re-labelled are the two counts, "$74$ distinct multiplicity triples" and "eighteen splittings" are counts over splitting-type gradings, hence lower bounds, and the note's conclusion "it does not shorten the list" is strengthened, not weakened, by the correction.

## H5. Six zeros confirmed by hand and for all gradings; "degenerate vertex" is not

**I recompute the vanishing sets from the symmetric condition, without using the splitting.** In $\mathbb Z_2\times\mathbb Z_2$ write the three nonzero elements $u,v,w$ with $u+v=w$ and $u+v+w=0$. Merging $\mathfrak g_{00}$ with $\mathfrak g_w$ and labelling $E_1=\{0,w\}$, $E_2=\{u\}$, $E_3=\{v\}$: type $112$ needs $x+y=u$ with $x,y\in\{0,w\}$, and $x+y\in\{0,w\}$, so it vanishes; likewise $113$; $222,223,233,333$ vanish because $u,v,u+v+v=u$ and so on are all nonzero. The survivors are $111$ ($0+0+0$), $122$ ($0+u+u$), $123$ ($w+u+v$) and $133$ ($0+v+v$). Vanishing set $\{112,113,222,223,233,333\}$, the note's first row. Merging $\mathfrak g_u$ with $\mathfrak g_v$ and labelling $E_1=\{u,v\}$, $E_2=\{0\}$, $E_3=\{w\}$: survivors are $112$ ($u+u+0$), $113$ ($u+v+w$), $222$ ($0+0+0$) and $233$ ($0+w+w$); vanishing set $\{111,122,123,133,223,333\}$, the note's second row. **Six each, six merges, two patterns, and the derivation uses only the group**, so it covers the $\mathfrak u(m)$-type and triality gradings that H4 leaves out. A screen over all six merges reproduces both rows exactly. *One thing the table should say and does not*: both rows are written in the labelling $E_1=$ the merged eigenspace; under the other labelling of the second row the set is $\{111,113,122,123,223,333\}$, the same pattern relabelled, and a reader matching a concrete point against the table needs the convention.

**"Exactly six" is really "at least six".** The group condition is *necessary* for nonvanishing and not sufficient: $x+y+z=0$ permits $[\mathfrak g_x,\mathfrak g_y]\perp\mathfrak g_z$ to hold anyway, and a reduced variable $t_{ijk}$ aggregates many index triples. So what is proved is that the grading *forces* six zeros; whether the four survivors are nonzero at a given point is a further check that the note's screen over abstract grade-labels cannot make.

**The vertex reading does not follow, and the count points the other way from what the note infers.** At $d=3$ with every multiplicity $\ge3$, [Lemma L104](../findings/bounds-relaxations.md) gives $V=10$ types and a feasible affine set of dimension $V+1-(3d-2)=4$ inside the $11$ coordinates $(t,\theta)$. Imposing six coordinate vanishings on a four-dimensional affine set is **six conditions on four dimensions**, whose generic outcome is that the face is **empty**. So the correct reading is the opposite of the note's: the $\mathbb Z_2\times\mathbb Z_2$ zero-pattern over-determines the fixed-spectrum system by two, and therefore *cuts the spectrum* (a solution exists only on a codimension-$\le2$ locus of $(\nu,\theta)$) rather than certifying that some point is a vertex. And even where a point does exist, vertexhood needs the six active constraints to have rank $4$ on the four-dimensional direction space; at rank $r<4$ the point sits on a face of dimension $4-r$ and is not a vertex at all. **That rank check is a computation this brief does not authorise and the note did not perform.** What the run would decide: whether the six-zero face of $\Pi(\lambda)$ at a $(30,20,16)$ spectrum is empty, a vertex, or a positive-dimensional face, respectively excluding the pattern at that spectrum, confirming the note's word, or refuting it. Recorded and not run.

**And the analogy to $d=2$ is drawn in the wrong direction.** The arithmetic of the analogy is fine, at $d=2$, $V=4$ and the feasible set is a segment, the balanced product has $t_{112}=t_{222}=0$, one more than a vertex of a segment requires. But what makes that degeneracy *mean* something in [Lemma L105](../findings/bounds-relaxations.md) is that the two binding facets are the $\Pi$-bound and the tangent bound and they cross exactly at $\theta_0(n)$. At $d=3$ nothing of the kind is shown, and the record already holds a warning attached to these very multiplicities: [DERIVE-t11](DERIVE-t11.md) §5's kernel-free witness has multiplicities $(30,20,16)$ and passes every per-row condition at $\theta=1.2917>\theta_0(12)=1.2360$, which is why [post F43](../findings/bounds-relaxations.md) declares the per-row family spent at $d\ge3$. So calling the $\mathbb Z_2\times\mathbb Z_2$ point "the $d=3$ analogue of the phenomenon that singles out the maximiser" asserts a direction of travel that the record's own witness at the same multiplicities points against. The claim to make is the neutral one: **the grading forces six of the ten types to vanish, which is two more than the affine dimension, and what that face contains is undetermined.** *Two further caveats, both from [Lemma L104](../findings/bounds-relaxations.md) itself*: the dimension $4$ presumes every multiplicity $\ge3$, which the merge does not guarantee in general; and $\Pi(\lambda)$ drops the proved pair cap, which at $d=3$ is six further inequalities and which [post F43](../findings/bounds-relaxations.md) records as breaking the analogous zero-count.

## H6. Right, and a strictly better witness sits inside the note's own count

**The arithmetic.** $(n_1,n_2,n_3,n_4)=(0,2,2,8)$ gives $\mathfrak g_{00}=0+1+1+28=30$, $\mathfrak g_{01}=0\cdot2+2\cdot8=16$, $\mathfrak g_{10}=0\cdot2+2\cdot8=16$, $\mathfrak g_{11}=0\cdot8+2\cdot2=4$, summing to $66=\binom{12}2$; merging $\mathfrak g_{01}$ with $\mathfrak g_{11}$ gives $20$ and the triple $(30,20,16)$. **Confirmed.**

**A $V_i$ of dimension zero still gives a genuine grading.** The test is that $\mathbb Z_2\times\mathbb Z_2\to\mathrm{Aut}(\mathfrak{so}(12))$ be injective, i.e. that none of $\sigma,\tau,\sigma\tau$ be $\pm I$. With $V_1=0$: $\sigma=(+\text{ on }V_2,\ -\text{ on }V_3\oplus V_4)$, $\tau=(+\text{ on }V_3,\ -\text{ on }V_2\oplus V_4)$, $\sigma\tau=(+\text{ on }V_4,\ -\text{ on }V_2\oplus V_3)$, and each has both signs present because $V_2,V_3,V_4$ are all nonzero. All four graded pieces are nonzero, $(30,16,16,4)$. **So the objection the brief invites does not land**, one vanishing $V_i$ is harmless, and only two vanishing would collapse the group to $\mathbb Z_2$.

**But the note need not have relied on that, and its own count says so.** Screened over all compositions of $12$ into four parts and all six merges: **eighteen ordered $4$-tuples** give the triple $(30,20,16)$, which is the note's eighteen, and they are twelve arrangements of $\{0,2,2,8\}$ **and six of $\{2,2,4,4\}$**. The second multiset has no zero part: $\mathfrak g_{00}=1+1+6+6=14$, $\mathfrak g_{01}=2\cdot2+4\cdot4=20$, $\mathfrak g_{10}=\mathfrak g_{11}=2\cdot4+2\cdot4=16$, summing to $66$; merging $\mathfrak g_{00}$ with either $16$ gives $(30,20,16)$. Here all four $V_i$ are honest positive-dimensional subspaces and the two involutions are ordinary reflections, so **the withdrawal of the earlier exclusion stands on a splitting with nothing to object to.** The note picked the one witness exposed to an attack and left the clean one implicit in a count.

**The remaining figure checks.** $74$ distinct multiplicity triples at $n=12$ with every multiplicity $\ge3$, screened, and $86$ if the multiplicity floor is dropped. And the scope disclaimer at the end of the note is the right one and should be kept verbatim: this bears on multiplicities only, and whether any Weyl tensor carries that spectrum, let alone critically, is untouched. *That disclaimer covers the withdrawal; it does not cover the vertex claim of §H5, which is where the note over-reaches.*
