## Questions for this sweep

1. **A comparison principle for cubic forms on representations.** Is there a theorem that, for an invariant cubic on an irreducible module, bounds the value at any unit vector by the value at a vector of prescribed isotropy plus a defect that vanishes only on that orbit (a stability or Łojasiewicz-type inequality at the maximiser, globalised)?
2. **Rearrangement or two-point symmetrisation for invariant polynomials.** Does any symmetrisation on the Weyl module (averaging over a reflection, a polarisation along a hyperplane of $\mathbb R^n$) not decrease $P$ and push toward the product's isotropy?
3. **The pinching constant through a flow.** Hamilton's ODE $\dot W=Q(W)$ has the maximiser as its fastest self-similar blow-up; does comparison of blow-up times, or a monotone quantity along the flow, give the sharp constant elsewhere?
