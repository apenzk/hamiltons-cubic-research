## A. Fidelity

- **A-1. K on 𝟏⊥** ([L54Spectrum.lean:107–116](../lean/HamiltonsCubic/Results/L54Spectrum.lean)). Confirmed. `g'` is `toLp 2 (fun _ => 1)`, the all-ones vector in EuclideanSpace ℝ (Fin n), so `(ℝ ∙ g')ᗮ` is 𝟏⊥ with the standard inner product, which is the record's. The left side maps `hB.eigenvalues` (Mathlib's spectrum of `Kmat R`, the same `hB` L54Rigid's `wK` is built from) over `Finset.univ`, and the right side is `0 ::ₘ` the eigenvalues of `T.restrict` on 𝟏⊥. That is the record's "the spectrum of K on ℝⁿ is that on 𝟏⊥ plus one 0", and the record's d_ψ = −(eigenvalues of K on 𝟏⊥) in (1) is stated against exactly that restricted spectrum. g' ≠ 0 is read off coordinate ⟨0, hn⟩.
- **A-2. W̊ on S²₀** ([L54Spectrum.lean:119–152](../lean/HamiltonsCubic/Results/L54Spectrum.lean)). Confirmed. `frob_ew` ([L52Frame.lean:108](../lean/HamiltonsCubic/Results/L52Frame.lean)) makes `ew w` orthonormal under the Frobenius pairing, with √2·symB = (E_ab + E_ba)/√2 on the pair block and diag(w_k) on the diagonal block, and `expand_sym_w` (line 84) makes it a basis of S². So h ↦ (frob h (ew w u))_u is an isometry S² → ℝ^{S2Idx n}, and ⟨c, gCoord w⟩ = frob(Σ c_u ew w u, 1) = tr h. Orthogonality to `gCoord w` in coordinates is therefore tracelessness, and `(ℝ ∙ toLp (gCoord w))ᗮ` is S²₀. `Bw_gCoord` is (Bw·gCoord)_u = Σ_v frob(W̊ e_u, e_v) frob(1, e_v) = frob(W̊ e_u, 1) = frob(e_u, W̊ 1) = 0 by `expand_sym_w` at h = 1, `ringW_selfAdjoint` and `ringW_one` at Ric = 0, traced line by line. Since `Bw` is symmetric (`Bw_isHermitian`), `toEuclideanLin (Bw w (ringW R))` is W̊ transported by that isometry whatever the row/column convention, so its restriction to g'⊥ has W̊ on S²₀'s spectrum. *The isometry itself is assembled here by hand from `frob_ew` and `expand_sym_w`, not typed as a LinearIsometryEquiv. This is the layer's standing identification of `Bw` with W̊ and carries nothing new.*
- **A-3. Restricted operator.** Confirmed. `(hT.restrict_invariant hW).eigenvalues rfl` is Mathlib's spectrum of the self-adjoint restriction to the invariant subspace, indexed by `Fin (finrank ℝ g⊥)`, so the right side has n − 1 (resp. dim S²₀) entries, as the record's 𝟏⊥ and S²₀ spectra do. The `∃ hW` wrapper is harmless: the invariance proof is a Prop and the eigenvalues do not depend on it up to proof irrelevance, and the proof supplies `null_perp_invariant`.
- **A-4. Hypotheses.** Confirmed. hR, hskew and hsym feed `Kmat_isHermitian`, `Bw_isHermitian` and `ringW_selfAdjoint`, hRic feeds `K_one` and `ringW_one`, hn makes g ≠ 0 in both instances, and hw feeds `Bw_isHermitian`, `expand_sym_w` and the nonvanishing of `gCoord w`. `K_spectrum_split` does not carry hw (declared after it). The record's n ≥ 4, unit norm and first Bianchi are not needed for this passage, so their absence is a generalisation. The build reports no unused-variable warning in this file.
- **A-5. The owed item and the tag** ([lean/INDEX.md:273](../lean/INDEX.md)). Confirmed with gap. The partial tag is right: the record's (4) on S²₀ needs both ω and d on S² to be the record's plus one shared 0, and the d half is not assembled. The owed statement itself (the record's d on 𝟏⊥ is the layer's d less one 0) is accurate and is a passage the record needs rather than states, since the record works on S²₀ throughout. **Gap 1**: its precondition "which needs an eigenbasis of K containing 𝟏/√n" is not needed. `psi_block` ([L54Basis.lean:123](../lean/HamiltonsCubic/Results/L54Basis.lean)) takes any orthonormal eigenfamily w : m → ι → ℝ over any index m, so the eigenvectors of the restriction on 𝟏⊥, coerced to ℝⁿ, give the record's ψ-block as diag(−κ_k) with κ the restricted eigenvalues; the layer's ψ-diagonal at `wK` is −(`hB.eigenvalues`) by `Bw_diag_psi` ([L52Frame.lean:249](../lean/HamiltonsCubic/Results/L52Frame.lean)); and `K_spectrum_split` relates the two multisets with no condition on the eigenbasis. [L54Rigid.lean:21–22](../lean/HamiltonsCubic/Results/L54Rigid.lean) already says the extra 0 appears "whatever eigenbasis is chosen", so the row now contradicts the file it summarises. The route the owed item names is one construction, not a requirement, and a reader planning the next round would build more than is needed.
