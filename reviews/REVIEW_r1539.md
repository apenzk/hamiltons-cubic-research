## A. Fidelity

- **A-1. `eq_iff_identity_unnormalised`** ([L225Identity.lean:150–191](../lean/HamiltonsCubic/Results/L225Identity.lean)). Confirmed. Hypotheses $c>0$, $c^2=k(k-1)$, $k\ge2$ and $\operatorname{tr}M=0$ are the record's $c=\sqrt{k(k-1)}$, $k\ge2$, trace-free. The equivalence is $\operatorname{tr}M^3=\frac{k-2}c\sqrt{\operatorname{tr}M^2}^{\,3}$ exactly when $M=\sqrt{\operatorname{tr}M^2}\,(\tfrac kc uu^{\mathsf T}-\tfrac1cI)$ with $\sum u_j^2=1$, the record's clause verbatim. For symmetric $M$, $\operatorname{tr}M^2=\sum_{ij}M_{ij}M_{ji}=\sum_{ij}M_{ij}^2$, so $\sqrt{\operatorname{tr}M^2}$ is the Frobenius norm, and `eq_zero_of_trace_sq` proves the same identity en route. **The $M=0$ edge** ([:160–165](../lean/HamiltonsCubic/Results/L225Identity.lean)): both sides hold, the right with $u=e_{i_1}$ and scale $0$, which matches the record's heading "equality exactly at $M^0=0$ and …". The positive case normalises by $s=\sqrt{\operatorname{tr}M^2}>0$ and reduces to `eq_iff_identity`, whose converse `identity_attains` goes through $P=uu^{\mathsf T}$, $P^2=P$, $\operatorname{tr}P=1$, which I checked by hand: $\operatorname{tr}M=\tfrac kc-\tfrac kc=0$, $\operatorname{tr}M^2=\tfrac{k^2-2k+k}{c^2}=1$, $\operatorname{tr}M^3=\tfrac{k^3-3k^2+3k-k}{c^3}=\tfrac{k(k-1)(k-2)}{c^3}=\tfrac{k-2}c$. The forward half reads $u$ off the row $O_{i_0}$ of the eigenframe at the unique eigenvalue $(k-1)/c$ of `matrix_cube_eq`, and $\operatorname{diag}(\lambda)=-\tfrac1cI+\tfrac kc E_{i_0i_0}$ is right.
- **A-2. `IsHermitian`.** Confirmed. Over $\mathbb R$ the star is trivial, so $M^{\mathsf H}=M$ is $M^{\mathsf T}=M$, and the file uses exactly that at [:135–137](../lean/HamiltonsCubic/Results/L225Identity.lean) and [:170](../lean/HamiltonsCubic/Results/L225Identity.lean). `sym_spectral_gen` ([:29–38](../lean/HamiltonsCubic/Results/L225Identity.lean)) turns Mathlib's unitary eigenvector matrix into a real orthogonal $O$ with $M=O^{\mathsf T}\operatorname{diag}(\lambda)O$, at every finite index.
- **A-3. `exists_orth_to_single`** ([:208–255](../lean/HamiltonsCubic/Results/L225Identity.lean)). Confirmed by hand. At $u=e$ it returns $H=1$ ([:212](../lean/HamiltonsCubic/Results/L225Identity.lean)). Otherwise $w=u-e\ne0$, $d=\lvert w\rvert^2=2(1-u_{i_1})>0$, and $H=I-\tfrac2d ww^{\mathsf T}$ is symmetric with $H^2=I-\tfrac4d ww^{\mathsf T}+\tfrac4{d^2}d\,ww^{\mathsf T}=I$, so $H^{\mathsf T}H=1$ is a genuine orthogonality. $Hu=u-\tfrac2d w\,(w\cdot u)$ with $w\cdot u=1-u_{i_1}=d/2$, so $Hu=u-w=e$. The antipodal $u=-e$ is covered by the general branch ($d=4$).
- **A-4. `k3_clause`** ([:259–316](../lean/HamiltonsCubic/Results/L225Identity.lean)). Confirmed. At $c=\sqrt6$, $(k-2)/c=1/\sqrt6$, and $\operatorname{diag}(2,-1,-1)=3e_0e_0^{\mathsf T}-I$ ([:269](../lean/HamiltonsCubic/Results/L225Identity.lean)). "Some orthonormal frame" as $M=t\,O^{\mathsf T}DO$ with $O^{\mathsf T}O=1$ is right: the rows of $O$ are the frame, and $M$ has matrix $tD$ in it. No $\det O=1$ is needed or claimed, which is correct for "orthonormal frame". The forward half conjugates by the Householder $H$ with $H^{\mathsf T}e_0=u$ and $t=s/\sqrt6\ge0$. The converse reads $u=O^{\mathsf T}e_0$, unit from $OO^{\mathsf T}=1$, and $\operatorname{tr}M^2=6t^2$ with $t\ge0$ gives $s=\sqrt6\,t$. *No gap*: the $k=3$ inequality itself, $\operatorname{tr}M^3\le\tfrac1{\sqrt6}\lvert M\rvert^3$, is `matrix_cube_le_unnormalised` at `Fin 3` rather than a separate theorem, a direct specialisation.
