## A. `br_phi_expand` and `Dop_coord`

**Convention.** [WeylReal.lean](../lean/HamiltonsCubic/Base/WeylReal.lean) line 45: `phi i j = single j i 1 - single i j 1`, so $(\varphi_{ij})_{ab}=\delta_{aj}\delta_{bi}-\delta_{ai}\delta_{bj}$, that is $\varphi_{ij}=e_je_i^{\mathsf T}-e_ie_j^{\mathsf T}$. `br X Y = XY - YX`.

**By hand.** $Ae_j=\sum_mA_{mj}e_m$ and $e_i^{\mathsf T}A=\sum_mA_{im}e_m^{\mathsf T}=-\sum_mA_{mi}e_m^{\mathsf T}$ at skew $A$. So
$$[A,e_je_i^{\mathsf T}]=\sum_m\big(A_{mj}\,e_me_i^{\mathsf T}+A_{mi}\,e_je_m^{\mathsf T}\big),\qquad[A,e_ie_j^{\mathsf T}]=\sum_m\big(A_{mi}\,e_me_j^{\mathsf T}+A_{mj}\,e_ie_m^{\mathsf T}\big).$$
The difference is $\sum_mA_{mj}(e_me_i^{\mathsf T}-e_ie_m^{\mathsf T})+A_{mi}(e_je_m^{\mathsf T}-e_me_j^{\mathsf T})=\sum_m(A_{mj}\varphi_{im}+A_{mi}\varphi_{mj})$, which is `br_phi_expand` term for term. Skewness of $A$ is used once, at $A_{im}=-A_{mi}$, and the Lean's `rw [hA' b i, hA' b j]` is that step.

**`Dop_coord`.** $\langle DR\varphi_{ij},\varphi_{kl}\rangle=\langle[A,R\varphi_{ij}],\varphi_{kl}\rangle-\langle R[A,\varphi_{ij}],\varphi_{kl}\rangle$. The first is $-\langle R\varphi_{ij},[A,\varphi_{kl}]\rangle$ by `inner2_br_skew` (Frobenius skewness of $\mathrm{ad}_A$ at skew $A$, valid at all matrices), and expanding $[A,\varphi_{kl}]$ gives $-\sum_m(A_{mk}R_{ijml}+A_{ml}R_{ijkm})$. The second, by linearity of $R$ and the expansion of $[A,\varphi_{ij}]$, is $\sum_m(A_{mi}R_{mjkl}+A_{mj}R_{imkl})$. Together: $-\sum_m(A_{mk}R_{ijml}+A_{ml}R_{ijkm}+A_{mi}R_{mjkl}+A_{mj}R_{imkl})$, the stated formula. `Dop_coord` needs only linearity of $R$, not bivector-valuedness, which matches its hypotheses.

## B. The four clauses and `Dop_weylOp`

**Bianchi.** WeylReal line 110: `bianchi R i j k l` $=R_{ijkl}+R_{iklj}+R_{iljk}$, cyclic in the last three slots with the first fixed. Expanding the three terms of `bianchi (Dop A R)` by `Dop_coord` gives twelve terms under one minus sign. Grouped by the $A$ factor:
- $A_{mi}$: $R_{mjkl}+R_{mklj}+R_{mljk}$ = `bianchi R m j k l`
- $A_{mj}$: $R_{imkl}+R_{iklm}+R_{ilmk}$ = `bianchi R i m k l`
- $A_{mk}$: $R_{ijml}+R_{imlj}+R_{iljm}$ = `bianchi R i j m l`
- $A_{ml}$: $R_{ijkm}+R_{ikmj}+R_{imjk}$ = `bianchi R i j k m`.

That is the Lean's `e`, and `hb` closes it.

**Ricci.** `Ric R a b` $=\sum_cR_{ac,bc}$. By `Dop_coord` at $(i,j,k,l)=(a,c,b,c)$, $\mathrm{Ric}(DR)_{ab}=-\sum_{c,m}(A_{mb}R_{acmc}+A_{mc}R_{acbm}+A_{ma}R_{mcbc}+A_{mc}R_{ambc})$. The first is $\sum_mA_{mb}\mathrm{Ric}(R)_{am}$ and the third $\sum_mA_{ma}\mathrm{Ric}(R)_{mb}$ (the Lean's `h1`, `h2`). Renaming $c\leftrightarrow m$ in the fourth gives $\sum A_{cm}R_{acbm}=-\sum A_{mc}R_{acbm}$, which cancels the second (the Lean's `h3`). So $\mathrm{Ric}(DR)$ is $A$ against $\mathrm{Ric}(R)$ and vanishes with it.

**Pair symmetry.** At skew $X,Y$: $\langle DRX,Y\rangle=-\langle RX,[A,Y]\rangle-\langle[A,X],RY\rangle$, using `inner2_br_skew` and `hsym` at the skew pair $([A,X],Y)$. $\langle X,DRY\rangle=-\langle[A,X],RY\rangle-\langle RX,[A,Y]\rangle$ by the same two moves in the other order. Equal. `sym_Dop` uses `hsym` at $([A,X],Y)$ and $(X,[A,Y])$, which is why it needs `br_isSkew_real`.

**Coverage.** `IsWeylOp R` is `IsLinOp R ∧ IsWeyl R` ([FoxProduct.lean](../lean/HamiltonsCubic/Base/FoxProduct.lean) line 110), and `IsWeyl` has four clauses (WeylReal lines 126 to 130). `Dop_weylOp` destructures five and supplies five: `Dop_lin`, `Dop_skew`, `sym_Dop`, `bianchi_Dop`, `ric_Dop`. **Against `act_mem`.** [WeylAction.lean](../lean/HamiltonsCubic/Results/WeylAction.lean) line 123: `act g R ∈ weylModuleS ι` for orthogonal $g$. r1446 item A established that `Dop A R` is $\tfrac{d}{dt}\big\vert_0\mathrm{act}(e^{tA})R$, sign included, so preservation of $\mathcal W_n$ by `Dop A` is the tangent statement of `act_mem`. ## D. OrbitFree: the contraction and `weyl_moves`

**`phi_col`.** $(\varphi_{pq})_{ms}=\delta_{mq}\delta_{sp}-\delta_{mp}\delta_{sq}$, so $\sum_m(\varphi_{pq})_{ms}X_m=[s=p]X_q-[s=q]X_p$. Matches.

**`contract`, by hand.** Take `Dop_coord` at $A=\varphi_{pq}$, $k=p$, and sum over $p$. With $A_{ms}=(\varphi_{pq})_{ms}$:
- $\sum_mA_{mp}R_{ijml}=R_{ijql}-[p=q]R_{ijpl}$, summed over $p$: $nR_{ijql}-R_{ijql}=(n-1)R_{ijql}$
- $\sum_mA_{ml}R_{ijpm}=[l=p]R_{ijpq}-[l=q]R_{ijpp}$, summed: $R_{ijlq}-[l=q]\sum_pR_{ijpp}$
- $\sum_mA_{mi}R_{mjpl}=[i=p]R_{qjpl}-[i=q]R_{pjpl}$, summed: $R_{qjil}-[i=q]\sum_pR_{pjpl}$
- $\sum_mA_{mj}R_{impl}=[j=p]R_{iqpl}-[j=q]R_{ippl}$, summed: $R_{iqjl}-[j=q]\sum_pR_{ippl}$.

Under the overall minus sign these are the seven terms of `contract`, including the three conditional traces.

**`contract_weyl`, by hand.** At a Weyl $u$: $R_{ijpp}=0$ ($\varphi_{pp}=0$). $\sum_pR_{pjpl}=\sum_pR_{jplp}=\mathrm{Ric}_{jl}=0$ (two sign flips). $\sum_pR_{ippl}=-\sum_pR_{iplp}=-\mathrm{Ric}_{il}=0$. $R_{ijlq}=-R_{ijql}$. $R_{qjil}=R_{ilqj}$ by pair symmetry. The bracket becomes $(n-2)R_{ijql}+R_{ilqj}+R_{iqjl}$. `bianchi u i q j l` is $R_{iqjl}+R_{ijlq}+R_{ilqj}=0$, so $R_{ilqj}+R_{iqjl}=-R_{ijlq}=R_{ijql}$, and the bracket is $(n-1)R_{ijql}$. The contraction is $-(n-1)R_{ijql}$, as stated. The Lean's `e1` to `e5` and `hB := hb i q j l` are these steps.

**The $n\ge2$ hypothesis.** `weyl_invariant_zero` uses it for $n-1\ne0$ only. At $n\le1$ every skew matrix is $0$, so the conclusion $uX=0$ holds by linearity without it. The hypothesis is consumed and costs nothing. (At $n\le3$, $\mathcal W_n=0$ and the theorem is vacuous in content, which is also harmless.)

**Is "$u$ vanishes on bivectors" the right $u=0$?** Yes. `IsWeylOp` constrains $u$ only through its values on skew inputs, and every quantity the layer builds reads $u$ only there: `innerOp` evaluates at the $\varphi_{ij}$, `Qop` composes $u$ with itself on bivector values, and `sharpQF` feeds brackets of skew matrices. So $u\vert_{\Lambda^2}=0$ is `innerOp u u = 0`, which is the record's $u=0$ in $\mathcal W_n\subseteq\mathrm{End}(\Lambda^2)$. The reconstruction step `hphi` uses bivector-valuedness (`hu.2.1`) to apply `skew_expand` to $u\varphi_{ij}$, which is where that clause enters. The hypothesis `hD` is required only on bivectors and only at $X=\varphi_{ij}$, so the theorem is stronger than the record's "$Du=0$ for every $A$".

**`weyl_moves`.** Contrapositive of `weyl_invariant_zero`, with $A=\varphi_{pq}$ skew by `phi_isSkew`, so the $A$ it produces is an admissible input to `orbit_half_weyl`. Its conclusion "$Du\,Y\ne0$ at a bivector $Y$" gives `innerOp (Du) (Du)` $>0$ by `skew_expand` and positivity of `inner2` on nonzero matrices, a one-line bridge the layer does not type (item F).
