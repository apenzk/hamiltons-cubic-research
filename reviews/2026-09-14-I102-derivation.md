## (b) The first-order condition of the minimising reflection

**Claim.** *For $W\in\mathcal W_n$ and a unit $u\in\mathbb R^n$ let $s^2(u)=\lVert c_u\rVert^2$, $c_u$ the $\sigma_u$-odd part. Then $u$ is a critical point of $s^2$ on the unit sphere exactly when*
$$\langle c_u,\ \xi\cdot b_u\rangle=0\qquad\text{for every }\xi=v\wedge u,\ v\perp u,$$
*where $\xi\cdot$ is the action of $\mathfrak{so}(n)$ on $\mathcal W_n$ and $b_u$ the even part.*

**Proof.** $c_u=\tfrac12(W-\sigma_uW)$, so $s^2(u)=\tfrac12\big(\lVert W\rVert^2-\langle W,\sigma_uW\rangle\big)$ and it suffices to differentiate $f(u)=\langle W,\sigma_uW\rangle$. A curve $u(\varepsilon)=R_\varepsilon u$ with $R_\varepsilon=\exp(\varepsilon\xi)$, $\xi=v\wedge u$, has $\sigma_{u(\varepsilon)}=R_\varepsilon\sigma_uR_\varepsilon^{-1}$, so $f(u(\varepsilon))=\langle R_\varepsilon^{-1}W,\sigma_uR_\varepsilon^{-1}W\rangle$ and, $\sigma_u$ being self-adjoint,
$$f'(0)=-2\langle\xi\cdot W,\sigma_uW\rangle=-2\langle\xi\cdot b+\xi\cdot c,\ b-c\rangle .$$
**$\xi$ is $\sigma_u$-odd in $\mathfrak{so}(n)$** ($\sigma_u\xi\sigma_u=-\xi$, since $\xi$ exchanges $u$ with $v\in u^\perp$), so $\xi\cdot b$ is odd and $\xi\cdot c$ is even. The pairings of opposite parity vanish and $f'(0)=-2\big(\langle\xi\cdot c,b\rangle-\langle\xi\cdot b,c\rangle\big)=4\langle\xi\cdot b,c\rangle$, using that $\xi$ acts skew-adjointly. Every tangent direction at $u$ is such a curve. $\square$

**What it says.** *The odd part at a minimising $u$ is orthogonal to the odd half of the orbit tangent along $u\wedge u^\perp$.* The orbit tangent there is $\xi\cdot W=\xi\cdot b+\xi\cdot c$ with $\xi\cdot b$ odd, and [F37](../findings/fence-posts.md) makes $\xi\cdot W$ a $\tfrac\theta2$-eigenvector of $L_W$ at every critical point. **So minimising $u$ removes from $c$ exactly the $n-1$ orbit directions that would otherwise make the lemma's $\tfrac\theta2$-eigenvector condition, L219's $2b\circ c=\theta c$, hold vacuously along the orbit.** *This is the same shape as the frame condition $D,N\perp T_W\mathcal O$ of [I100](../plan.md), with $n-1$ conditions in place of $\binom n2$, which is what an induction one vector at a time should cost.*

**Screened** (sweep batch 3): $\min_us^2(u)=10^{-15}$ at padded $W_{\mathbb{CP}^2}$ and $S^4\times S^4$ at $n=8$, so the condition is met there with $c=0$. **The formula itself screened at $n=8$** on a random unit $W$, random $u$ and $v$: the finite-difference derivative of $f$ along $\exp(\varepsilon\,v\wedge u)u$ is $-0.03016673$ against $4\langle\xi\cdot b,c\rangle=-0.03016673$, and $\xi\cdot b$ is odd to $2\times10^{-11}$.

## (c) The lemma, restated, and its decoupled form fails as L218 predicts

**Restatement.** Write $\mathcal A_k=\mathrm{Fix}(\sigma_{e_1},\dots,\sigma_{e_k})$. Since $-I\in O(n)$ acts trivially on $\mathcal W_n$ (even degree), $\sigma_{e_n}=-\sigma_{e_1}\cdots\sigma_{e_{n-1}}$ acts as their product, so $\mathcal A_{n-1}=\mathcal D_n$. **The root node is therefore $n-1$ steps of: $\sup P$ on the unit sphere of $\mathcal A_k$ equals $\sup P$ on that of $\mathcal A_{k+1}$, and every maximiser on $\mathcal A_k$ is even under some reflection $\sigma_u$ with $u\perp e_1,\dots,e_k$.** *$\mathcal A_k$ is a subalgebra, so its maximisers are critical points of $P$ on $\mathcal W_n$, L219 applies inside it, and (b) applies with $u$ restricted to $\{e_1,\dots,e_k\}^\perp$, since the $\sigma_{e_i}$ commute with $\sigma_u$ there.*

**The decoupled step, and why it fails.** With $m_{k+1}=\sup_{\mathcal A_{k+1}}P$ and $s=\lVert c\rVert$, L219 gives $P(W)\le m_{k+1}(1-s^2)^{3/2}+3\kappa\sqrt{1-s^2}\,s^2$ for $\kappa=\sup\{\langle L_bc,c\rangle\}$ over unit even $b$ and odd $c$. **That closes exactly when $\kappa\le m_{k+1}/2$**, since $(1-x)(1+\tfrac x2)^2=1-\tfrac34x^2-\tfrac14x^3\le1$ makes $m\sqrt{1-x}(1+\tfrac x2)\le m$. **And $\kappa\ge\tfrac{\sqrt3}2>\tfrac{\Theta_n}2\ge\tfrac{m_{k+1}}2$ once $m_{k+1}\le\Theta_n$**: take $b$ the padded $S^2\times S^2$ on a four-set $Q\ni u$ of its own frame, which is diagonal and so even, and $c$ the top eigenvector of $L_b$ on $V_Q$, which is odd under $\sigma_u$ since $u\in Q$. *That is [L218](../findings/second-order.md)'s equality case, $\langle L_bc,c\rangle=\tfrac{\sqrt3}2$.* **So the decoupled step is post [F67](../findings/fence-posts.md) at the reflection level, the fourth time, and it fails for the same reason L218 recorded**: the coupling's maximiser (padded $S^2\times S^2$ with a four-set direction) and $P$'s maximiser (the balanced product) are different even parts.

**What survives for a joint argument.** At a critical point L219 fixes $\langle L_bc,c\rangle=\tfrac\theta2s^2$ exactly, which is the decoupled bound's equality value, and $P(b)=\theta(1-\tfrac32s^2)$, so **a maximiser with $c\ne0$ has $P(\hat b)=\theta\frac{1-3s^2/2}{(1-s^2)^{3/2}}=\theta(1-\tfrac38s^4+O(s^6))$, a fourth-order deficit**, and its even part carries a coupling of exactly half its own value. *The joint statement needed is therefore about pairs $(b,c)$ with $2b\circ c=\theta c$ and $b\circ b+c\circ c=\theta b$: whether such a pair with $c\ne0$ and $\theta\ge\Theta_n$ exists with $c\perp\xi\cdot b$ along $u\wedge u^\perp$.* **Padded $W_{\mathbb{CP}^2}$ is such a pair at $\theta=\sqrt{3/2}$ for a generic $u$, and has $c=0$ at its padding directions**, so the criterion must use H1 or H4 against it, as the root position said.

**The second variation in $u$, derived.** With $W(\varepsilon)=R_\varepsilon^{-1}W=W-\varepsilon\,\xi\cdot W+\tfrac{\varepsilon^2}2\xi^2\cdot W+O(\varepsilon^3)$ and $f=\langle W(\varepsilon),\sigma_uW(\varepsilon)\rangle$,
$$f''(0)=2\langle\xi\cdot W,\sigma_u\,\xi\cdot W\rangle+2\langle\xi^2\cdot W,\sigma_uW\rangle=4\big(\lVert\xi\cdot c\rVert^2-\lVert\xi\cdot b\rVert^2\big),$$
using that $\xi\cdot$ flips parity, $\xi^2\cdot$ keeps it, and $\langle\xi^2\cdot x,x\rangle=-\lVert\xi\cdot x\rVert^2$. **So at a minimising $u$, $\lVert\xi\cdot c\rVert\le\lVert\xi\cdot b\rVert$ for every $\xi\in u\wedge u^\perp$**, a statement about the orbit geometry of $W$ alone: it uses neither criticality nor the value. *Screened at $n=8$ on a random unit $W$: the second finite difference of $f$ is $+0.148760$ against $4(\lVert\xi\cdot c\rVert^2-\lVert\xi\cdot b\rVert^2)=+0.148760$.*

**Verdict on the joint form.** Every condition available at a minimising $u$ is one of three kinds: the two scalar relations of L219 ($\langle L_bc,c\rangle=\tfrac\theta2s^2$, $P(b)=\theta(1-\tfrac32s^2)$), which are satisfied by every critical point and are the frame split's scalar system again, the first and second variations in $u$, which are orbit geometry and hold for any $W$, and H3 on the plane, which is automatic. **None consumes H1 or H4 beyond the plane, so padded $W_{\mathbb{CP}^2}$ at a generic $u$ and every catalogued critical point pass all of them.** *This is post [F67](../findings/fence-posts.md)'s mechanism in joint form: the scalar data at a reflection are consistent with $c\ne0$ at any value, as they were at the frame split ([I100 derivation](2026-09-14-I100-derivation.md), batch 2).* **R26's abandon condition is met as the plan wrote it**: the lemma reduces, with the tools on the record, to bounds of the coupling and the even part that F67 fences. *What R26 leaves that is durable is L219 itself and the nested-subalgebra restatement, which any later route may use.*

**Status: (c) closed as a dead end for R26**, the joint form being F67 by the argument above. *The route's sequence continues with [I101](../plan.md).*
