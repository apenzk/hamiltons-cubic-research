## 1. The ten summands and their sum

The record's display lists $\langle W\rangle$, $\mathrm{Weyl}_p$, $\mathrm{Weyl}_q$, $\mathbb R^p\otimes\mathbb R^q$, $S^2_0(\mathbb R^p)$, $S^2_0(\mathbb R^q)$, $S^2_0\otimes S^2_0$, $\Lambda^2\otimes\Lambda^2$, $X_p\otimes\mathbb R^q$, $X_q\otimes\mathbb R^p$, and `tenSum` adds their dimensions in that order. `dim_sum` is stated over $\mathbb Q$ with no hypothesis, which is what "identically in $p,q$" means. Reading the formulas at $m=3$ as a sanity check: $\dim\mathrm{Weyl}_3=0$, $\dim X_3=5$, $\dim S^2_0=5$, $\dim\Lambda^2=3$, all correct.

## 2. The bottom end

The record: "the ten dimensions over-counting by $2$ at $q=2$ and by $1+p$ at $q=1$", and elsewhere "at $(3,2)$, $n=5$, the sum reads $37$ against $\dim\mathrm{Weyl}_5=35$". `tenSumTrue2` replaces the formula values $\dim\mathrm{Weyl}_2=-2$ and $\dim X_2=0$ by $0$, and `tenSumTrue1` replaces $\dim\mathrm{Weyl}_1=-1$ and $\dim X_1=-1$ by $0$. The resulting excesses are $2$ and $0+1+p\cdot1=1+p$. The substitution at $q=2$ changes only the Weyl slot, since the formula already gives $X_2=0$. So the prose "$\dim\mathrm{Weyl}_q$ and $\dim X_q$ read negative" is right at $q=1$ and half right at $q=2$. The `overcount_two` docstring ("`dim Weyl_2` reads `−2`") has it right.

## 3. The scalars

L26 states $\mu=\frac{n-2}{2(p-1)(q-1)}$. Then $(p-1)(q-1)-(p+q-2)=pq-2p-2q+3=(p-2)(q-2)-1$, which is L27's display, so `L27.mu` is L26's $\mu$ off $p=1$, $q=1$. The file's docstring for `mu` cites only L27's display, and the equality with L26 lives in `L26Statement.half_sub_mu` for a different constant of the same name. A one-line theorem `mu p q = L26Statement.mu p q` for $p,q\ge2$ would close this.

The trace identity is the only arithmetic step of the argument not typed. It is what fixes the sign on $S^2_0\otimes S^2_0$, and without it the file's values are a list of nine derived scalars and one asserted. The screen compiled by `field_simp; ring` from `lam`, `mu`, `dimS`, `dimL`, so typing it costs one theorem.
