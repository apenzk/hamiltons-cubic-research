## 1. [brS](../lean/HamiltonsCubic/Base/Bivectors2.lean#L337), [sharpQF2](../lean/HamiltonsCubic/Base/Bivectors2.lean#L343) and [sharpBil2](../lean/HamiltonsCubic/Base/Bivectors2.lean#L348) against the record's

**The three definitions, side by side.** [sharpQF](../lean/HamiltonsCubic/Base/WeylReal.lean#L102) is `-(1/4) * ∑ i : ι, ∑ j : ι, inner2 (br η (R (br η (R (phi i j))))) (phi i j)`, and [sharpQF2](../lean/HamiltonsCubic/Base/Bivectors2.lean#L343) is the same string with `br` replaced by [brS](../lean/HamiltonsCubic/Base/Bivectors2.lean#L337) and `phi i j` packaged with its membership proof. **The constant is `-(1/4)` in both, the summation is over the same `univ × univ` of ORDERED pairs in both, and the two brackets carry `η` in the SAME slot, first, in both.** [brS](../lean/HamiltonsCubic/Base/Bivectors2.lean#L337) is `br` on the coercions with the membership proof attached, so `↑(brS X Y) = br ↑X ↑Y` holds by `rfl`, which I checked as a one-line theorem. [sharpBil2](../lean/HamiltonsCubic/Base/Bivectors2.lean#L348) is `(1/2) * (sharpQF2 R (X + Y) - sharpQF2 R X - sharpQF2 R Y)`, which is [sharpBil](../lean/HamiltonsCubic/Base/WeylReal.lean#L147) with the addition taken in the submodule, and `↑(X + Y) = ↑X + ↑Y` is [Submodule.coe_add](https://leanprover-community.github.io/mathlib4_docs/Mathlib/Algebra/Module/Submodule/Basic.html#Submodule.coe_add).

**Is `sharpQF2 R η` the record's `sharpQF` of the same operator read as a bare function, at every `η ∈ Λ²`? Yes, and I proved it rather than compared strings.** The statement is `sharpQF Rb ↑η = sharpQF2 R η` for every bare `Rb` extending `R`, and the proof is `congr 1` on the constant, `Finset.sum_congr` twice, then the extension hypothesis applied at `⟨phi i j, _⟩` and again at `brS η (R ⟨phi i j, _⟩)`, with `↑(brS X Y) = br ↑X ↑Y` closing each bracket. **The second application of the hypothesis is where the change of domain earns something**: it needs `brS η (R φ_ij)` to be in `Λ²`, which on `CurvOp2` is the codomain plus [br_mem_skewSub](../lean/HamiltonsCubic/Base/Bivectors2.lean#L298) and on the bare type is not available at all. The theorem compiles. `sharpBil Rb ↑X ↑Y = sharpBil2 R X Y` follows from it in three rewrites and a `norm_num`, and also compiles.

**The control.** I wrote `sharpQF2'`, identical to [sharpQF2](../lean/HamiltonsCubic/Base/Bivectors2.lean#L343) except that the `-(1/4)` is a `-(1/2)`, and ran the IDENTICAL proof script against `sharpQF Rb ↑η = sharpQF2' R η`. It is REJECTED, the `congr 1` on the two constants leaving a stuck goal. So the check is sensitive to exactly the constant the brief names as the failure mode, and a check that passed under either normalisation would have tested nothing.

**Verdict: CONFIRMED.** The `¼`, the `½`, both summation ranges and the order of the two brackets are the record's.

## 2. [sharpMap](../lean/HamiltonsCubic/Base/Bivectors2.lean#L369) against [sharpOp](../lean/HamiltonsCubic/Base/WeylReal.lean#L153)

**The positive claim holds.** `sharpOp Rb ↑X = ↑(sharpMap R X)` at every bare `Rb` extending `R` and every `X : skewSub ι`, which I proved by pushing the coercion through the outer `½`, through the two sums by [AddSubmonoidClass.coe_finsetSum](https://leanprover-community.github.io/mathlib4_docs/Mathlib/Algebra/Group/Submonoid/Membership.html) and through each scalar by [Submodule.coe_smul](https://leanprover-community.github.io/mathlib4_docs/Mathlib/Algebra/Module/Submodule/Basic.html#Submodule.coe_smul), then applying section 1's agreement at the second slot. The theorem compiles. So `sharpMap` is the record's `sharpOp` restricted, with the same outer `½` paying for the same ordered-pair double count.

**The CONTRAST the docstring draws is false, and this is the item's finding.** [Bivectors2.lean:367](../lean/HamiltonsCubic/Base/Bivectors2.lean#L367) reads *Unlike the record's version this lands in `Λ²` by construction, the `φ` being bivectors, so no skewness side condition is needed*. The record's version lands in `Λ²` by construction too, by the same argument and at EVERY matrix argument, skew or not: [sharpOp](../lean/HamiltonsCubic/Base/WeylReal.lean#L153) is a scalar multiple of a double sum of multiples of `φ_ij`, so [Submodule.smul_mem](https://leanprover-community.github.io/mathlib4_docs/Mathlib/Algebra/Module/Submodule/Defs.html#Submodule.smul_mem) and [Submodule.sum_mem](https://leanprover-community.github.io/mathlib4_docs/Mathlib/Algebra/Module/Submodule/Defs.html#Submodule.sum_mem) close it. I proved `sharpOp Rb X ∈ skewSub ι` for an ARBITRARY bare `Rb` and an ARBITRARY matrix `X`, with no hypothesis whatever, and it compiles in one term. **There is no skewness side condition anywhere on [sharpOp](../lean/HamiltonsCubic/Base/WeylReal.lean#L153) to be dropped**, and the only place [WeylReal.lean](../lean/HamiltonsCubic/Base/WeylReal.lean) does carry one is [skew_expand](../lean/HamiltonsCubic/Base/WeylReal.lean#L79), which is about the coordinates FAITHFULLY resolving their argument rather than about the value landing anywhere.

**Does dropping it change what the map is on the record's own domain? No, and the question has a sharper answer than "no".** On `Λ²` the two maps agree, by the theorem above. Off `Λ²` the record's map is still defined and still lands in `Λ²`, and what it is NOT there is the `#` of anything, since [skew_expand](../lean/HamiltonsCubic/Base/WeylReal.lean#L79) is what licenses reading `sharpBil R X (φ_ij)` as the `φ_ij` coordinate of `R# X`, and that reading needs `X` skew. **So the difference the change of domain makes at `sharpMap` is that the arguments where the coordinate reading is unlicensed have been removed from the type**, which is a real gain and is not the one the docstring claims.

**The opposite reading, argued.** One could read *no skewness side condition is needed* as being about the bundling into a `CurvOp2` rather than about the value. That reading does not rescue the sentence: [sharpMap](../lean/HamiltonsCubic/Base/Bivectors2.lean#L369) is NOT bundled, it is a bare `CurvOp2 ι → skewSub ι → skewSub ι`, and what bundling would need is linearity in `X`, which is [WeylReal.lean:263](../lean/HamiltonsCubic/Base/WeylReal.lean#L263)'s standing debt and is not a skewness condition.

**Verdict: CONFIRMED in substance, the contrast REFUTED.** The repair is one sentence: `sharpMap` lands in `Λ²` by construction, as the record's `sharpOp` does, and what the domain change buys is that its argument is licensed rather than that its value is.

## 3. [sharpPair2](../lean/HamiltonsCubic/Base/Bivectors2.lean#L374) and [Pcubic2](../lean/HamiltonsCubic/Base/Bivectors2.lean#L380)

**Both are the record's, and I proved both agreements.** [sharpPair](../lean/HamiltonsCubic/Base/WeylReal.lean#L167) is `innerOp R (sharpOp R)`, which expands to `(1/2) * ∑ i ∑ j ⟨R φ_ij, sharpOp R φ_ij⟩`, and [sharpPair2](../lean/HamiltonsCubic/Base/Bivectors2.lean#L374) is that expansion verbatim with `applyPhi` and `sharpMap` in the two slots. `sharpPair Rb = sharpPair2 R` compiles from section 2's agreement and the extension hypothesis. [Pcubic2](../lean/HamiltonsCubic/Base/Bivectors2.lean#L380) is `trCube2 R + sharpPair2 R`, which is [Pcubic](../lean/HamiltonsCubic/Base/WeylReal.lean#L170)'s `trCube R + sharpPair R`, and `Pcubic Rb = Pcubic2 R` compiles too. **The composite step needs the extension hypothesis applied TWICE and the second application needs `R X ∈ Λ²`**, which the codomain supplies, so `hc : Rb (Rb ↑X) = ↑(R (R X))` is a consequence of the extension hypothesis rather than an extra assumption. That is a small correction to [r1007](REVIEW_r1007.md)'s section 4, which reads the second application as unavailable on the bare type: it is unavailable for an arbitrary bare function and it is available for one that extends a `CurvOp2`, because then `Rb φ_ij` is `↑(R φ_ij)` and skew by the codomain.

**The one correction, and it is a missing declaration rather than a wrong one.** [sharpPair2](../lean/HamiltonsCubic/Base/Bivectors2.lean#L374) INLINES the pairing rather than calling [innerOp2](../lean/HamiltonsCubic/Base/Bivectors2.lean#L276), because [sharpMap](../lean/HamiltonsCubic/Base/Bivectors2.lean#L369) is not a `CurvOp2` and `innerOp2` takes two. **So the layer's own coherence obligation has no analogue here**: [Pcubic_eq_inner_Qop](../lean/HamiltonsCubic/Base/WeylReal.lean#L249) states `P = ⟨R, Q(R)⟩` on the bare type, and on this domain there is no `Qop2` to state it against, [Pcubic2_eq](../lean/HamiltonsCubic/Base/Bivectors2.lean#L384) being `rfl` and carrying no content beyond the definition. That is the same debt section 6 names, seen from the other side.

**Verdict: CONFIRMED**, with the inlining and the absent `Qop2` recorded.

### 4b. The registered norm is not the record's, and the two unit spheres differ

**What the registrations supply is the SUP operator norm.** [ContinuousLinearMap.toNormedAddCommGroup](https://leanprover-community.github.io/mathlib4_docs/Mathlib/Analysis/Normed/Operator/Basic.html) carries `‖f‖ = sInf {c | ∀ x, ‖f x‖ ≤ c * ‖x‖}`, where the record's is Hilbert-Schmidt, `normSq R = ½ ∑_i ∑_j ⟨R φ_ij, R φ_ij⟩`.
