## 1. Result 5(a)'s three blocks, re-derived, and the $P_-$ consistency question

Write $W=W_+-W_-$, $\lvert W\rvert=W_++W_-$, $G:=W_+^\#+W_-^\#$, $C:=W_+\#W_-$. Bilinearity and symmetry of $\#$ give, with no hypothesis on $W$,

$$\lvert W\rvert^\#=G+2C,\qquad W^\#=G-2C,\qquad\text{so }\lvert W\rvert^\#=W^\#+4C\ \text{ and }\ 2G=\lvert W\rvert^\#+W^\#.$$

At a critical point $W^\#=\theta W-W^2$ is a function of $W$, so it is block-diagonal in $P_\pm,P_0$ with $P_+W^\#P_+=\theta W_+-W_+^2$, $P_-W^\#P_-=-\theta W_--W_-^2$, $P_0W^\#=0$. Substituting into the definition $X_{\mathrm{inv}}=\lvert W\rvert^\#-P_+W^\#P_++P_-W^\#P_-$ and using $W\lvert W\rvert=W_+^2-W_-^2$ gives $X_{\mathrm{inv}}=\lvert W\rvert^\#+(W-\theta)\lvert W\rvert$, Result 5(a)'s closed form. Compressing:

- **$++$ block.** $P_+\lvert W\rvert^\#P_+=P_+W^\#P_++4P_+CP_+=\theta W_+-W_+^2+4P_+CP_+$; adding $P_+(W-\theta)\lvert W\rvert P_+=W_+^2-\theta W_+$ leaves $4P_+CP_+$. PSD since $C\succeq0$ by Hamilton's $A\#B\succeq0$ for $A,B\succeq0$.
- **$--$ block, both forms.** Via $\lvert W\rvert^\#=2G-W^\#$: $P_-X_{\mathrm{inv}}P_-=2P_-GP_--P_-W^\#P_-+(-W_-^2-\theta W_-)=2P_-GP_-+(\theta W_-+W_-^2)-(W_-^2+\theta W_-)=2P_-GP_-$. Via $\lvert W\rvert^\#=W^\#+4C$: $P_-X_{\mathrm{inv}}P_-=(-\theta W_--W_-^2)+4P_-CP_-+(-W_-^2-\theta W_-)=4P_-CP_--2\theta W_--2W_-^2$, Theorem A's form. **The two agree exactly**, their difference being $P_-(2G-4C)P_-+2\theta W_-+2W_-^2=2P_-W^\#P_-+2\theta W_-+2W_-^2=0$. PSD is visible only in the $G$ form ($G\succeq0$ as a sum of sharp squares of PSD operators); Theorem A's form is a signed difference and carries no sign on its own, which is why the lemma is right to cite Result 5(a) for positivity and Theorem A only for the support argument.
- **$00$ block.** $\lvert W\rvert P_0=0$ kills the second summand, leaving $P_0\lvert W\rvert^\#P_0$, PSD since $\lvert W\rvert\succeq0$.

So the three block identities are right and each block is PSD. The compression $P_0W^\#=0$ needs exactly $Q(W)=\theta W$ as an operator on $\Lambda^2$ and nothing more: $W^\#=\theta W-W^2$ and $P_0W=0$. [conventions.md](../findings/conventions.md) line 119 defines a critical point as that operator equation, and batch 1 (4) records why the Euler–Lagrange projection onto $\mathcal W_n$ costs nothing ($Q$ preserves $\mathcal W_n$). No further hypothesis is consumed.

## 2. The trace-zero argument, step by step

With $h=\lambda\,\mathrm{id}$, batch 1 (2) gives $H(e_i\wedge e_j)=(\eta_i+\eta_j)e_i\wedge e_j=2\lambda\,e_i\wedge e_j$, so $H=2\lambda\,\mathrm{Id}$ and $J_5=\langle X_{\mathrm{inv}},H\rangle=2\lambda\mathop{\mathrm{tr}}X_{\mathrm{inv}}$. Cross-check from the other side of the adjoint: $J_5=\langle\mathrm{Ric}(X_{\mathrm{inv}}),\lambda\,\mathrm{id}\rangle=\lambda\sum_{i\ne j}(X_{\mathrm{inv}})_{ijij}=2\lambda\mathop{\mathrm{tr}}X_{\mathrm{inv}}$, the same number, which is also the record's $\mathop{\mathrm{scal}}(X)=2\mathop{\mathrm{tr}}X$ ([ATTEMPTS.md](../ATTEMPTS.md) line 3962, $S=\tfrac12\mathop{\mathrm{tr}}X_{\mathrm{inv}}$ and $\mathop{\mathrm{scal}}=4S$). **The pairing constant is $2$ and positive under every convention in [conventions.md](../findings/conventions.md)**: a rescaling of either Frobenius pairing multiplies $J_5$ by a positive number, so no sign hides there. The lemma's displayed $J_5=\lambda\mathop{\mathrm{tr}}X_{\mathrm{inv}}$ contradicts its own $H=2\lambda\,\mathrm{Id}$ by that factor; [post F54](../findings/second-order.md) carries the complementary slip ($H=\lambda\,\mathrm{Id}$ with $J_5=\lambda\mathop{\mathrm{scal}}$). The consistent triple is $H=2\lambda\,\mathrm{Id}$, $J_5=\lambda\mathop{\mathrm{scal}}(X_{\mathrm{inv}})=2\lambda\mathop{\mathrm{tr}}X_{\mathrm{inv}}$.

$\lambda>0$: $\mathop{\mathrm{tr}}h=\sum_{i\ne j}\lvert W\rvert_{ijij}=2\mathop{\mathrm{tr}}_{\Lambda^2}\lvert W\rvert$, and $\lvert W\rvert\succeq0$ with $\lvert W\rvert=0$ iff $W=0$. So $\lambda=2\mathop{\mathrm{tr}}\lvert W\rvert/n>0$ exactly when $W\ne0$; the lemma should say so, since $W=0$ is a critical point of the operator equation at every $\theta$.

The equality argument. $\mathop{\mathrm{tr}}X_{\mathrm{inv}}=\sum_b\mathop{\mathrm{tr}}(P_bX_{\mathrm{inv}}P_b)$ with each summand the trace of a PSD operator, so $\mathop{\mathrm{tr}}X_{\mathrm{inv}}=0$ forces each block to vanish. For PSD $A$ and a projection $P$, $PAP=0$ gives $\lVert A^{1/2}Px\rVert^2=\langle PAPx,x\rangle=0$, hence $AP=0$ and by symmetry $PA=0$: this is the "zero diagonal block kills the row" step, and it is correct. Applied to $C\succeq0$ with $P_+$: $P_+C=CP_+=0$. Applied to $\lvert W\rvert^\#\succeq0$ with $P_0$: $P_0\lvert W\rvert^\#=0$; then $4P_0C=P_0\lvert W\rvert^\#-P_0W^\#=0$. So $C=(P_++P_-+P_0)C(P_++P_-+P_0)=P_-CP_-$. Theorem A's $X_{\mathrm{inv}}=4C-2\theta W_--2W_-^2$ then has every term supported in $\mathop{\mathrm{ran}}P_-$, so $X_{\mathrm{inv}}=P_-X_{\mathrm{inv}}P_-$, which is the vanished $--$ block. The converse is trivial. **Every step holds; the lemma's equality characterisation is proven.** It uses criticality three times (the block forms, $P_0W^\#=0$, Theorem A) and cannot be lifted to non-critical $W$ in this form.

## 3. The isotropy sentence, and which reading of "isotropy group" it needs

$h=\mathrm{Ric}(\lvert W\rvert)$ is $O(n)$-equivariant: functional calculus commutes with conjugation by $\Lambda^2g$, and $\mathrm{Ric}$ is a contraction. So $h$ commutes with every $g$ in the stabiliser $K=\mathrm{Stab}_{O(n)}(W)$. Real Schur's lemma on an irreducible $K$-module gives only that the commutant is $\mathbb R$, $\mathbb C$ or $\mathbb H$ (for $K=U(m)\subset SO(2m)$ the complex structure $J$ commutes and is not scalar) so "equivariant on an irreducible forces scalar" is incomplete as stated. **What closes it is that $h$ is symmetric**: it has a real eigenvalue, the eigenspace is $K$-invariant and nonzero, hence all of $\mathbb R^n$. The statement is true; the proof needs that one sentence.

On the reading: if *any* subgroup of $K$ acts irreducibly on $\mathbb R^n$ then $K$ does, and the argument above needs only a subgroup; so the two readings give the same hypothesis and no ambiguity survives. Irreducibility is over $\mathbb R$. This is the multiplicity-one special case of [ATTEMPTS.md](../ATTEMPTS.md) Theorem B's isotropy argument (line 928), and it is immune to the defect [LEDGER.md](../findings/LEDGER.md) records against that argument's general form ("scalar on each isotypic factor" fails where an irreducible appears with multiplicity $m$, giving an $m\times m$ block): a single irreducible summand has multiplicity one. *The general form still stands uncorrected at that line of ATTEMPTS.md; that is outside this brief, and I note it as a neighbour.*

Consistency against the catalogue, all by representation theory and none by computation: $SU(3)$ acting by $\mathrm{Ad}$ on $\mathfrak{su}(3)=\mathbb R^8$ is irreducible, and the record has $h$ scalar there; $Sp(2)Sp(1)$ on $\mathbb R^8$ is irreducible, and $h$ is scalar at $\mathbb{HP}^2$; the balanced $S^m\times S^m$ has the block swap in its full stabiliser, under which $\mathbb R^m\oplus\mathbb R^m$ is irreducible, and $h$ is scalar there with $\kappa=1$; the unbalanced $S^p\times S^q$ is reducible and the record's $\kappa=q/p\ne1$ ([post F54](../findings/second-order.md)) says $h$ is not scalar. The lemma's hypothesis draws exactly the line the record already observes.

## 5. The consequence paragraph, what the lemma plus r697/r698 actually prove

Sorting the record's sharp points by the status of "$X_{\mathrm{inv}}=0$": every $S^p\times S^q$ is **proven** by hand, and by the reviewers rather than by this lemma (the unbalanced ones are outside its hypothesis anyway). $\mathbb{HP}^2$ satisfies the hypothesis analytically, so the lemma proves $X_{\mathrm{inv}}(\mathbb{HP}^2)=0\iff J_5(\mathbb{HP}^2)=0$, but $J_5(\mathbb{HP}^2)=0$ is on the record as a $10^{-14}$ residual (Result 4), so the vanishing of $X_{\mathrm{inv}}$ there is **numerical**, now with two independent numerical witnesses (the $J_5$ residual and the batch's direct $1.4\times10^{-15}$) and one analytic equivalence between them. $\mathcal S^4(\alpha)$ is numerical as before. So "at every recorded point where the grant is sharp, $X_{\mathrm{inv}}=0$" is an accurate description of the record; "the lemma explains it on the irreducible class rather than observing it" overreaches, the lemma explains why the two vanishings *coincide* there, and observes neither. An analytic $X_{\mathrm{inv}}(\mathbb{HP}^2)=0$ would need $\mathop{\mathrm{tr}}X_{\mathrm{inv}}$ evaluated by hand from the $\mathbb{HP}^2$ spectrum, which the batch did not do and this brief did not ask for.
