# Literature sweep, 2026-09-14c, on Fable: the cubic on block subalgebras after L224

**Position**: [2026-09-14c-step8-position.md](2026-09-14c-step8-position.md). **Question**: when is the maximum of an $O(k)$-invariant cubic on a tuple of symmetric $k\times k$ matrices, coupled through quadratic terms, attained at a commuting tuple? At $k=2$ this is [L224](../findings/second-order.md); at $k=n$ it is the root node.

## Batch 1, the DDVV and Böttcher--Wenzel family

Searched: DDVV proof and equality case (Ge–Tang, Lu), the 2024 survey, BW generalisations (weighted, three-matrix, $q$-deformed). Filed [ge-tang-2008-ddvv-proof](../references/ge-tang-2008-ddvv-proof/) and [ge-li-tang-zhou-2024-ddvv-survey](../references/ge-li-tang-zhou-2024-ddvv-survey/). **Outcome: nothing cubic.** Every inequality in the family bounds commutator norms, quadratic in each matrix, and every equality case is a maximally non-commuting pair. The family measures non-commutativity from above and cannot say that a cubic peaks at commuting tuples. *The 09-13c sweep's use of BW as the pair-mass ceiling stands and is all this literature gives.*

## Batch 2, the per-matrix cubic

Searched: sharp bounds on $\mathop{\mathrm{tr}}\mathring A^3$ for trace-free matrices, Newton's inequalities, the $\lambda$-method (Okumura-type lemmas). Filed [trace-free-cubic-2023-newton](../references/trace-free-cubic-2023-newton/). **Outcome: the tool for the block cubic, with its equality case.** $\mathop{\mathrm{tr}}\mathring A^3\le\frac{n-2}{\sqrt{n(n-1)}}\lvert\mathring A\rvert^3$, equality exactly at an eigenspace of dimension $n-1$; at $3\times3$, $\mathop{\mathrm{tr}}(M^0)^3\le\tfrac1{\sqrt6}\lvert M^0\rvert^3$ at $\mathop{\mathrm{diag}}(2,-1,-1)$. *The proof is two lines from sharp Newton applied to $\mathring A+\lambda I$ and is worth having on the record in its own right: it is the same mechanism as L57's fourth-moment step, one matrix at a time.* It bounds each block separately and leaves the coupled frames untouched, so it is a lemma for the route and not the route.

## Batch 3, cubic invariants of tuples and Horn-type convexity

Searched: maxima of invariant cubics on tuples of symmetric matrices, simultaneous diagonalisability at extrema, Kostant convexity and Horn's problem for $\mathop{\mathrm{tr}}(A+B)^3$, Chern-conjecture cubic traces. Filed [chern-cubic-trace-2026](../references/chern-cubic-trace-2026/) as not relevant. **Outcome: no theorem of the needed shape.** The only general principle surfaced is the first-order condition on a unitary orbit, $[A,\nabla F(A)]=0$, which is criticality and not maximality. Horn's problem gives the spectrum of $A+B$ as a polytope whose vertices are the commuting pairs, but $\sum\mu_i^3$ is not convex, so a maximum at a vertex is not automatic even for two matrices. *Nothing found: the cubic-on-tuples question is not a named problem in this literature.*

## Derivation, screened against the record: the polynomial on $\mathcal S_J$ at $\lvert J\rvert=3$

On $\mathcal S_J$ with $\lvert J\rvert=3$, from the [block note](2026-09-14-I104-block-J.md)'s coordinates and the bracket relations $[e_{pa},e_{qa}]=\pm e_{pq}$, $[e_{pa},e_{pb}]=\pm e_{ab}$, $[e_{pa},e_{qb}]=0$ for $p\ne q$, $a\ne b$:
- **Bianchi is automatic on $\mathcal S_J$** (every cyclic sum involves an entry of a nontrivial character), and **Ricci-flatness reads** $\sum_aM_a=-\rho(R_J)$ on $J$, where $\rho$ is the Ricci contraction of the $3$-dimensional curvature block, a bijection in dimension three, and $\mathop{\mathrm{tr}}M_a+\sum_bK_{ab}=0$ at each outer $a$. So $R_J$ is determined by $\sum_aM_a$.
- **The cubic**: $P=\sum_a\mathop{\mathrm{tr}}M_a^3+\sum_a\langle\mathop{\mathrm{cof}}M_a,R_J\rangle+\sum_{a<b}K_{ab}\mathop{\mathrm{tr}}(M_aM_b)+\mathop{\mathrm{tr}}R_J^3+(\text{terms in }K\text{ and }K\cdot\mathop{\mathrm{diag}}M_a)$, up to the record's normalisations, where $\mathop{\mathrm{cof}}M_a=\Lambda^2M_a$ on $\Lambda^2\mathbb R^J\cong\mathbb R^3$. *The two couplings between different frames are $K_{ab}\mathop{\mathrm{tr}}(M_aM_b)$, an inner product, and $\langle\mathop{\mathrm{cof}}M_a,R_J\rangle$ with $R_J$ linear in $\sum_bM_b$.*
- **What "commuting" would need.** The per-$a$ terms $\mathop{\mathrm{tr}}M_a^3+\langle\mathop{\mathrm{cof}}M_a,R_J\rangle$ are each maximised in $R_J$'s eigenframe when $M_a$ is two-valued (Case–Tyrrell's equality case aligned with $R_J$), and $\mathop{\mathrm{tr}}(M_aM_b)\le\sum\lambda_i(M_a)\lambda_i(M_b)$ (von Neumann) is maximised at aligned frames. **Both estimates are attained at simultaneously diagonal $M_a$, so the obstruction is only that the per-$a$ alignment with $R_J$ and the pairwise alignment through $K_{ab}$ may want different orderings.** *This is the precise remaining question, and it is a finite one about orderings of three eigenvalues, not an open-ended one.* Screened against the record: no findings file states $P$ on $\mathcal S_J$, and the [block note](2026-09-14-I104-block-J.md) stops before the cofactor term.

**Coefficients unverified.** The normalisations of the cofactor and $K_{ab}$ terms are not derived here, and the constant terms in $K$ are omitted. A derivation cycle owes them, from $\langle R^\#,R\rangle$ with the record's sharp formula, before anything is proved.
