## A. Fidelity

- **A-1. The product and its normalisation.** `sharpCross X Y Z = ½(sharpOp (X+Y) Z − sharpOp X Z − sharpOp Y Z)` ([FoxProduct.lean:264](../lean/HamiltonsCubic/Base/FoxProduct.lean)) is the polarisation of the quadratic map $R\mapsto R\#R$, which is Böhm–Wilking's bilinear $A\#B=\tfrac12((A+B)\#(A+B)-A\#A-B\#B)$. `sharpOp` reassembles $R\#X$ from the polarised quadratic form `sharpQF` ([WeylReal.lean:102](../lean/HamiltonsCubic/Base/WeylReal.lean), line 153). The anchor `sharpOp_id` ([StructureConstants.lean:217](../lean/HamiltonsCubic/Base/StructureConstants.lean)) gives $I\#I=(n-2)I$, which is Böhm–Wilking's value: their $\mathrm{Ric}(R)\wedge\mathrm{id}=R+R\#\mathrm{id}$ at $R=\mathrm{id}$, with $\mathrm{Ric}(\mathrm{id})=(n-1)\mathrm{id}$, forces it. So `sharpCross W (fun X => X)` is the record's $W\#I$ with the record's constant.
- **A-2. `sharpCross_id`** ([L131Sharp.lean:104](../lean/HamiltonsCubic/Results/L131Sharp.lean)). Hypotheses `IsWeyl W` and `IsSkew Z`, conclusion `sharpCross W (fun X => X) Z = -W Z`. This is consequence (ii)'s $W\#I=-W$ ([isotropy-cap.md:435](../findings/isotropy-cap.md)), read on $\Lambda^2$ as the record reads operators. The proof polarises `cross_QF` at $Z+\varphi_{ij}$, $Z$ and $\varphi_{ij}$ to get each $\varphi_{ij}$ coordinate of the cross term as $-2\langle WZ,\varphi_{ij}\rangle$, and `phi_expand` reassembles $-WZ$. The constants check by hand: $\tfrac12\cdot\tfrac12\cdot(-2)\cdot\sum_{ij}\langle WZ,\varphi_{ij}\rangle\varphi_{ij}=-\tfrac12\sum\langle WZ,\varphi_{ij}\rangle\varphi_{ij}=-WZ$.
- **A-3. `swap_ad`** ([L131Sharp.lean:28](../lean/HamiltonsCubic/Results/L131Sharp.lean)). With $\mathrm{ad}=\mathrm{ad}_\eta$ skew on $\Lambda^2$, the left side is $\mathop{\mathrm{tr}}(\mathrm{ad}^{\mathsf T}W\,\mathrm{ad})=-\mathop{\mathrm{tr}}(\mathrm{ad}\,W\,\mathrm{ad})$ and the right side is $\mathop{\mathrm{tr}}(\mathrm{ad}^{\mathsf T}\mathrm{ad}\,W)=-\mathop{\mathrm{tr}}(W\mathrm{ad}^2)$, equal by cyclicity. The docstring's $\mathop{\mathrm{tr}}(W\mathrm{ad}^2)=\mathop{\mathrm{tr}}(\mathrm{ad}\,W\,\mathrm{ad})$ names the two sides in the reverse order, which an equation permits. Correct.
- **A-4. `cross_QF`** ([L131Sharp.lean:67](../lean/HamiltonsCubic/Results/L131Sharp.lean)). Expanding `sharpQF` at $W+I$, which is linear on bivectors by `weyl_lin`, leaves two cross sums, $-\tfrac14\sum(\langle[\eta,W[\eta,\varphi]],\varphi\rangle+\langle[\eta,[\eta,W\varphi]],\varphi\rangle)=\tfrac14(S_1+S_2)$ by `adinv`, with $S_1=S_2$ by `swap_ad` and $\tfrac12S_1=-2\langle W\eta,\eta\rangle$ by `aW2`. So the cross term is $-2\langle W\eta,\eta\rangle$, which is $2\langle(W\#I)\eta,\eta\rangle$ at $W\#I=-W$. Consistent.
