## A. Fidelity

**The record's objects**, quoted from [bispectral.md:457](../findings/bispectral.md#L457): *"the $\Lambda^2$ Weyl spectrum of $\mathbb{CP}^m$, and $p_3(\mu)\vert_{\mathbb{CP}^m}=\frac{4m^2+2m-11}{3\sqrt{3m(m^2-1)(2m-1)}}$, $p_3(\omega)\vert_{\mathbb{CP}^m}=-\frac9{\sqrt{3m(m^2-1)(2m-1)}}$"*. [L53.lean:37-40](../lean/HamiltonsCubic/Results/L53.lean#L37) defines `p3muNum m = 4m² + 2m − 11` and `Rsq m = 3m(m² − 1)(2m − 1)`, [L53.lean:84](../lean/HamiltonsCubic/Results/L53.lean#L84) `thetaSq m = (m + 1)(2m − 1)/(3m(m − 1))`, and [L53.lean:87](../lean/HamiltonsCubic/Results/L53.lean#L87) `p3muSq m = p3muNum m ^ 2 / (9 * Rsq m)`.

**$W_{\mathbb{CP}^m}$ is the record's tensor.** Independently: the Fubini–Study curvature at holomorphic sectional curvature $4$ is $R=(\delta\delta-\delta\delta)+J_{ik}J_{jl}-J_{il}J_{jk}+2J_{ij}J_{kl}$, Einstein with $\mathrm{Ric}=(2m+2)g$ and scalar curvature $2m(2m+2)$. Its Weyl part subtracts $\frac{\mathrm{scal}}{n(n-1)}(\delta\delta-\delta\delta)=\frac{2m+2}{2m-1}(\delta\delta-\delta\delta)$, which leaves the coefficient $1-\frac{2m+2}{2m-1}=-\frac3{2m-1}$, the tensor at [L70CPm.lean:14-15](../lean/HamiltonsCubic/Results/L70CPm.lean#L14). Reading $(RX)_{ij}=\tfrac12\sum_{kl}R_{ijkl}X_{kl}$ on skew $X$ sends the three terms to $X$, $JXJ^{\mathsf T}$ (both $J$-products give it, halved) and $\langle J,X\rangle_F\,J$, which is `WCPm` at [L70CPm.lean:107](../lean/HamiltonsCubic/Results/L70CPm.lean#L107). Conjugation by $J$ is $+1$ on the $J$-invariant bivectors ($\mathbb RJ$, and the primitive $(1,1)$ part of dimension $m^2-1$) and $-1$ on the $(2,0)+(0,2)$ part (dimension $m(m-1)$), and the last term acts on $J$ alone with $\langle J,J\rangle_F=2m$. So the spectrum is $c+1+2m=\frac{4(m^2-1)}{2m-1}$ once, $c+1=\frac{2(m-2)}{2m-1}$ with multiplicity $m^2-1$, and $c-1=-\frac{2(m+1)}{2m-1}$ with multiplicity $m(m-1)$, for $c=-\frac3{2m-1}$. The multiplicities fill $\dim\Lambda^2=m(2m-1)$. At $m=2$ this is $(4,0,0,0,-2,-2)$, twice the $(2,0,0,0,-1,-1)$ of the layer's $W_{\mathbb{CP}^2}$ with $\lVert W\rVert^2=6$. The layer also proves `WCPm_isWeylOp` and `WCPm_critical` with multiplier $2m+2>0$, so the orientation is the one with $P>0$. My position agrees with r1459 and r1460.

**`p3mu_WCPm` is L53's form.** Its type, printed by `#check`, is `trCube WCPm ^ 2 * Rsq m = (p3muNum m / 3) ^ 2 * normSq WCPm ^ 3` at `1 ≤ m`. `trCube` is $\langle R^2,R\rangle=\sum\mu^3$ and `normSq` is $\sum\mu^2$ in the $\varphi$ basis, which is orthonormal for `inner2` with the ordered-pair doubling paid by `innerOp`'s $\tfrac12$. Dividing by $\lVert W\rVert^6 R^2$ gives $p_3(\mu)^2=(\operatorname{tr}M^3/\lVert W\rVert^3)^2=N^2/(9R^2)$ = `p3muSq`, exactly. At $m=1$ both sides are $0$ (`Rsq 1 = 0` and `normSq = 0`), so the hypothesis $m\ge1$ admits nothing false.

**The sign.** The square loses it. $p_3(\mu)=N/(3R)$ with $N=4m^2+2m-11\ge9$ at $m\ge2$, so $p_3(\mu)>0$ there. The record needs it: L53.lean's `bispectral_numerator` combines $\tfrac32\cdot\tfrac N3-\tfrac12(-9)$ with the signed value, and the square of $\tfrac32p-\tfrac12q$ is not determined by $p^2$. *Arguing the opposite*: `trCube_WCPm` states $\operatorname{tr}M^3=8(m^2-1)\,m(2m-1)N/(2m-1)^3$, manifestly positive at $m\ge2$, and `normSq_WCPm` is positive there, so the signed $p_3(\mu)$ is one division away from proved theorems, and the row claims only the squared identity. **Withdrawn as a gap**, recorded as a note.

**`thetaSq_WCPm` is L53's $\theta^2(\mathbb{CP}^m)$.** Its type is `Pcubic WCPm ^ 2 = thetaSq m * normSq WCPm ^ 3` at `2 ≤ m`, which is $\theta^2=P^2/\lVert W\rVert^6$ with L53.lean's `thetaSq`. ## B. Proofs

**`frob_W_W`** ([L53CPm.lean:59](../lean/HamiltonsCubic/Results/L53CPm.lean#L59)). With $WX=cX+CX+fJ$, $f=\langle J,X\rangle_F$ and $C=\mathrm{conj}$: $\langle CX,CX\rangle_F=\langle X,X\rangle_F$ by `frob_conj` and `conj_conj`, $\langle X,J\rangle_F=\langle CX,J\rangle_F=f$ by `frob_J_conj`, and $\langle J,J\rangle_F=2m$. So $\lvert WX\rvert^2=(c^2+1)\lvert X\rvert^2+2c\langle CX,X\rangle+f^2(2m+2c+2)$, the stated form.

**`frob_W2_W`** ([L53CPm.lean:70](../lean/HamiltonsCubic/Results/L53CPm.lean#L70)). `WCPm_sq` gives $W^2X=aX+bCX+gfJ$ with $a=c^2+1$, $b=2c$, $g=2c+2+2m$. Pairing with $cX+CX+fJ$: the $\lvert X\rvert^2$ coefficient is $ac+b$, the $\langle CX,X\rangle$ coefficient is $a+bc$, and the $f^2$ coefficient is $a+b+g(c+1+2m)$. All three match lines 71 to 73.

**The three sums, under the layer's normalisation.** `innerOp` is $\tfrac12\sum_{ij}$ of `inner2`, and `inner2` is $\tfrac12\operatorname{tr}A^{\mathsf T}B=\tfrac12\langle A,B\rangle_F$ (`inner2_eq_frob`), so each trace is $\tfrac14$ of the ordered sum.
- `sum_frob_phi_phi`: $\langle\varphi_{ij},\varphi_{ij}\rangle_F=2$ off the diagonal, so the sum is $2n^2-2n$ with $n=2m$, and $\tfrac14$ of it is $m(2m-1)=\operatorname{tr}\mathrm{Id}$.
- `sum_frob_conj_phi`: $\langle C\varphi_{ij},\varphi_{ij}\rangle_F=2J_{ij}^2$, summing to $4m$, so $\operatorname{tr}C=m$, which agrees with $(+1)(m^2)+(-1)m(m-1)$.
- `sum_J_sq` with `frob_phi`: $\langle J,\varphi_{ij}\rangle_F^2=4J_{ij}^2$, summing to $8m$, so $\operatorname{tr}\Pi=2m$ for $\Pi X=\langle J,X\rangle_FJ$, which is $2m$ times the orthogonal projection onto $J$ since $\mathrm{inner2}(J,J)=m$. *Note, not a gap*: the module docstring at [L53CPm.lean:16](../lean/HamiltonsCubic/Results/L53CPm.lean#L16) calls `sum_J_sq` the trace of the "`J`-projection". $\Pi$ is $2m$ times a projection, and the raw sum $\sum J_{ij}^2=2m$ equals $\operatorname{tr}\Pi$ only numerically.

**The closed forms by hand.** From the spectrum in A: $(2m-1)^2\sum\lambda^2=16(m^2-1)^2+4(m^2-1)(m-2)^2+4m(m-1)(m+1)^2=4(m^2-1)(6m^2-3m)$, so $\lVert W\rVert^2=12m(m^2-1)/(2m-1)$. And $(2m-1)^3\sum\lambda^3=8(m^2-1)\big(8(m^2-1)^2+(m-2)^3-m(m+1)^2\big)=8(m^2-1)(8m^4-24m^2+11m)$, with $8m^4-24m^2+11m=m(2m-1)(4m^2+2m-11)$.
- $m=2$, $c=-1$: $\lVert W\rVert^2=16+4+4=24=12\cdot2\cdot3/3$. $\operatorname{tr}M^3=64-8-8=48=8\cdot3\cdot54/27$. Through `frob_W2_W`: $-4\cdot6+4\cdot2+16\cdot4=48$. $P=6\cdot24=144$, $\theta^2=144^2/24^3=3/2$ = `thetaSq 2`. $p_3(\mu)^2=48^2/24^3=1/6=81/(9\cdot54)$ = `p3muSq 2`.
- $m=3$, $c=-3/5$: eigenvalues $32/5$ once, $2/5$ eight times, $-8/5$ six times. $\lVert W\rVert^2=(1024+32+384)/25=288/5$, and $\operatorname{tr}M^3=(32768+64-3072)/125=29760/125=64\cdot465/125$ with $465=3\cdot5\cdot31$. $P=8\cdot288/5$, so $\theta^2=64/(288/5)=10/9$ = `thetaSq 3`. $p_3(\mu)^2=(29760/125)^2/(288/5)^3=961/3240=31^2/(9\cdot360)$ = `p3muSq 3`.

**`Pcubic_eq_innerOp_Qop`** ([L53CPm.lean:111](../lean/HamiltonsCubic/Results/L53CPm.lean#L111)). $P=\langle R^2,R\rangle+\langle R,R^\#\rangle$ by definition, and $\langle R,Q(R)\rangle=\langle R,R^2\rangle+\langle R,R^\#\rangle$ by additivity in the first slot after the two commutations. Correct for any operator, with no linearity used, as [WeylReal.lean](../lean/HamiltonsCubic/Base/WeylReal.lean)'s `sharpPair` docstring anticipates.

**`innerOp_Qop_of_critical`** ([L53CPm.lean:118](../lean/HamiltonsCubic/Results/L53CPm.lean#L118)). `IsCritical R th` is $Q(R)\varphi_{ij}=\theta R\varphi_{ij}$ on every basis bivector, and `innerOp` only evaluates on the basis, so $\langle R,Q(R)\rangle=\theta\lVert R\rVert^2$. Correct.

**`Pcubic_WCPm`** ([L53CPm.lean:126](../lean/HamiltonsCubic/Results/L53CPm.lean#L126)) composes the two with `WCPm_critical` at $\theta=2m+2$. Correct.
