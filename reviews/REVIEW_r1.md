**2. Independent re-derivations, all CONFIRMED.**

- **Crossover equation.** Re-derived from scratch with `sympy`: $f(r):=|\mathcal S^r(\alpha)|^{-2}=\tfrac{(r+2)(r-1)}{6r(r-2)}$ has $f'(r)=-\tfrac{3r^2-4r+4}{6r^2(r-2)^2}$ (discriminant of $3r^2-4r+4$ is $16-48<0$, confirmed negative, so $f$ strictly decreasing, $r=4$ uniquely maximizes, $f(4)=3/8$, matches). Balanced-split maximization: $\tfrac{d}{dx}\log[(x-1)(n-x-1)/(x(n-x))]=0$ solves to the unique real root $x=n/2$ (`sympy.solve` confirms). Substituting $p=q=n/2$ into $\tfrac{(n-1)(p-1)(q-1)}{2(n-2)pq}$ and setting equal to $3/8$ gives exactly $n^2-12n+8=0$, roots $n=6\pm2\sqrt7\approx11.2915$ or $0.7085$, **matches FINDINGS.md exactly**, independently confirmed by direct symbolic computation, not just re-read.
- **$P(\text{idempotent }e)=1/|x|$.** Re-derived from first principles: for $x*x=x$ (equivalently $Q(x)=x$), write $x=|x|e$; homogeneity $Q(te)=t^2Q(e)$ gives $|x|^2Q(e)=|x|e \Rightarrow Q(e)=e/|x|$, so $P(e)=\langle e,Q(e)\rangle=\langle e,e\rangle/|x|=1/|x|$ (using $|e|=1$). **CONFIRMED**, elementary and correct.
- **Jordan-algebra idempotent classification.** Solved $X\times X=X$ directly with `sympy` for a general diagonal trace-free $3\times3$ matrix $(\lambda_1,\lambda_2,\lambda_3)$, $\sum\lambda_i=0$, under $A\times B=\tfrac12(AB+BA)-\tfrac13\mathop{\mathrm{tr}}(AB)I$: the only solutions are $(0,0,0)$ and permutations of $(2,-1,-1)$, **CONFIRMED exactly**, matching the document's Step 2 derivation. The "single $O(3)$-orbit" claim is legitimately justified (not merely asserted): every real symmetric matrix is orthogonally diagonalizable, $\times$ is $O(3)$-equivariant under conjugation, so the eigenvalue classification transports to *all* (not just diagonal) nontrivial idempotents, and the stabilizer of a $(2,-1,-1)$-pattern under conjugation is $O(1)\times O(2)$, giving orbit space $\mathbb{RP}^2$ exactly as claimed. This reasoning is sound.
- **Second-variation formula.** Independently re-derived via Taylor expansion along $x(t)=\cos(t)x^*+\sin(t)H$: with $f(t)=T(x(t),x(t),x(t))$, $f'(t)=3T(x'(t),x(t),x(t))$, $f''(t)=3T(x'',x,x)+6T(x',x',x)$; at $t=0$ ($x=x^*$, $x'=H$, $x''=-x^*$): $f''(0)=-3T(x^*,x^*,x^*)+6T(x^*,H,H)=6T(x^*,H,H)-3\lambda$. Cross-checked independently via the ambient-Hessian-minus-shape-operator formula: with $g(x)=|x|^2-1$, Lagrange multiplier $\kappa$ from $\nabla P(x^*)=\kappa\nabla g(x^*)$ gives $\kappa=3\lambda/2$ (since $\nabla P=3Q(x^*)=3\lambda x^*=\kappa\cdot2x^*$), and $\mathrm{Hess}_{\text{sphere}}(H,H)=\mathrm{Hess}_{\text{amb}}P(H,H)-\kappa\cdot\mathrm{Hess}_{\text{amb}}g(H,H)=6T(x^*,H,H)-\tfrac{3\lambda}2\cdot2|H|^2=6T(x^*,H,H)-3\lambda|H|^2$. **Both methods independently give $-3\lambda$, not $-2\lambda$, CONFIRMED**, the document's correction is right.
