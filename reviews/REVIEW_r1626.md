## Checks, and the object that would break each claim

- **`FourForm` is exactly the permutations of $(i,j,k,l)$.** The breaking object is a permutation missing from the six `PP` disjuncts, or a disjunct admitting a non-permutation. The three pairings each appear with either pair first (`PP i j k l`, `PP k l i j`, and likewise for $ik|jl$, $il|jk$), and `PP` allows both orientations in each slot, giving $3\cdot2\cdot2\cdot2=24$ tuples. Each is a permutation, and the 24 are distinct at distinct indices, so they are all $4!$ permutations. At distinct indices every permutation already has $a\ne b$, $c\ne d$, so that constraint removes nothing.
- **The slot chase.** The breaking object is a nonzero coordinate at a non-permutation position. `four_parity` follows from `coord_flip` and cancellation at `hne`. `four_occurs` evaluates at $m=i$: `flip i` has $-1$ exactly at $i$ (`sgn m x = if x = m then -1 else 1`), so if $i$ is absent the left side is $1$ and the right is $-1\cdot1\cdot1\cdot1$, contradiction. Applied to $i,j,k,l$ (by reordering the character with `ring_nf`), all four occur among four slots, hence each exactly once by counting. The Lean proof does not state uniqueness, and does not need it: the 256 `rcases` branches substitute and close either by a distinctness contradiction or by `simp` landing on a `FourForm` disjunct. `hab` and `hcd` from `cd_self12`/`cd_self34` are available in context for the contradictions. The kernel accepted all branches.
- **Vanishing propagation.** `pp_coord_zero` covers the four orientations with `cd_sw12` and `cd_sw34` and the pair swap with `cd_pair`, which needs `hD`. `fourForm_coord_zero` routes $ij|kl$ to `hA`, $ik|jl$ to `hB`, $il|jk$ to `hC`. The breaking object would be a pairing routed to the wrong base coordinate, and each of the six branches matches its disjunct.
- **Membership and the basis.** `spanVec i j k l x y z = x·rk2(φ_ij,φ_kl) + y·rk2(φ_ki,φ_jl) + z·rk2(φ_il,φ_jk)` ([L126GenBridge.lean:30](../lean/HamiltonsCubic/Results/L126GenBridge.lean#L30)), with `rk2 U V X = ⟨V,X⟩U + ⟨U,X⟩V`, and `spanVec_isWeyl` ([L126GenBridge.lean:101](../lean/HamiltonsCubic/Results/L126GenBridge.lean#L101)) gives `weylModule` at $x+y+z=0$, the Ricci trace vanishing by disjointness. Precomposition with `skewProj` adds `KillsSym` via `comp_skewProj_mem`.
- **The pairing coordinates and the signs.** By `coord R a b p q = ⟨R φ_ab, φ_pq⟩` and `inner2_phi_phi` ($\langle\varphi_{ij},\varphi_{kl}\rangle=[i{=}k,j{=}l]-[i{=}l,j{=}k]$): at $(i,j,k,l)$ only the $x$ term survives, $1\cdot1$. At $(i,k,j,l)$ only $\langle\varphi_{ki},\varphi_{ik}\rangle\langle\varphi_{jl},\varphi_{jl}\rangle=-1$ survives, so the coordinate is $-y$. At $(i,l,j,k)$ only $\langle\varphi_{il},\varphi_{il}\rangle\langle\varphi_{jk},\varphi_{jk}\rangle=1$ survives, $z$. This is the breaking object for the sign bookkeeping, and it agrees with the `simp` proof.
- **The Bianchi cut.** `cd_bianchi R hR i j k l` reads $R_{ijkl}+R_{iklj}+R_{iljk}=0$ and `cd_sw34 R i k l j` rewrites $R_{iklj}=-R_{ikjl}$, so $R_{ijkl}+(-R_{ikjl})+R_{iljk}=0$, which is $x+y+z=0$ at $y=-R_{ikjl}$. Consistent with the basis above: at $R=$ the span operator, $-R_{ikjl}=-(-y)=y$.
- **`four_isotypic`.** $D=R-S$ is Weyl and flip-isotypic with the same character (`act_sub'`, `fourS_iso`), vanishes at the three base coordinates by `coord_fourS_pairings`, so every nonzero coordinate would sit at a `FourForm` position where `fourForm_coord_zero` makes it zero. `cd_ext` gives $D=0$. The breaking object is a flip-isotypic $R$ with support off the pairings, excluded by `four_coord_form`.
- **`fourS_iso` and `fourS_iso_signMat`.** Both compare coordinates through `eq_of_coord` with both sides in `weylModuleS` (`act_mem` with `flip_orth` or `signMat_orth`, and `smul_mem`). At a nonzero coordinate `fourS_form` gives a `FourForm` position, derived from the six product terms of `coord_fourS` via `phi_ip_ne`. I checked the index shuffles at z3 and z4 (the $b$ term, with pairs $\{k,i\}$ and $\{j,l\}$): z3 maps to `PP j l i k` using `h2.symm` to reorient $\{k,i\}$ as $\{i,k\}$, and z4 maps to `PP i k j l` using `h1.symm`. Both correct. `fourForm_parity_sign` holds for any $\varepsilon$ since every position is a permutation, with no need for $\varepsilon^2=1$.
- **Two-sidedness at $\Gamma$.** `fourSummand_mem` forward is `fourS_mem` and `fourS_iso_signMat`. Backward specialises $\varepsilon=$ `sgn m` through `sgn_mul_self`, relying on `flip m` and `signMat (sgn m)` agreeing definitionally, which the kernel accepted (as in L120PairSign, r1625). The hypothesis $\forall i,\ \varepsilon_i\varepsilon_i=1$ over real $\varepsilon$ is exactly $\Gamma$. The witness $(R_{ijkl},-R_{ikjl})$ has third parameter $-R_{ijkl}+R_{ikjl}=R_{iljk}$ by the cut.
- **Dimension.** `fourMap` is linear (`module` after unfolding `spanVec`). Injectivity reads $x$ from the $(i,j,k,l)$ coordinate and $-y$ from $(i,k,j,l)$, so a nonzero $(x,y)$ in the kernel is impossible. `finrank_range_of_inj` and `finrank_prod` give $2$. This matches the record's "first Bianchi cuts each $4$-set block from $3$ to $2$", the three coordinates of the block being $R_{ijkl},R_{ikjl},R_{iljk}$ up to the 24 symmetries, with Ricci-flatness empty (`spanVec_isWeyl`'s Ricci clause needs no constraint).
