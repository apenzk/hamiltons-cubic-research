**Falsifying object and search.** The Lean statements would differ from the record's claim if there existed a pair of skew $X,Y$ with $\lVert X\rVert=\lVert Y\rVert=1$ that satisfies one side of the iff in one formulation and not the other: concretely (i) a skew rank-4 $X$ with all nonzero singular values $1/\sqrt2$ whose $X^{\mathsf T}X$ is not $\tfrac12P_{\operatorname{im}X}$ (would break the record-to-Lean direction of the converse), or (ii) a $Q$-carried pair for which the Lean's $Q$ does not pin $\operatorname{im}X$ (would break the Lean-to-record direction of the forward case). I looked for both analytically. (i) is excluded by the spectral theorem for the symmetric $X^{\mathsf T}X$ and $\operatorname{im}X^{\mathsf T}=\operatorname{im}X$ for skew $X$. (ii) is excluded by $\ker X=\ker X^{\mathsf T}X=\ker Q$ when $c>0$. Non-vacuity screen by hand at $n=4$: $X=\tfrac1{\sqrt2}(\varphi_{12}+\varphi_{34})$, $Y=\tfrac1{\sqrt2}(\varphi_{13}-\varphi_{24})$ have $X^{\mathsf T}X=Y^{\mathsf T}Y=\tfrac12I_4$, $Q=I_4$, and anticommute, so `cap_eq_converse` applies to the record's own witness. No such object exists.

**Verdict: CONFIRMED.** L31 is proved in full in Lean, cap and both directions of the equality case, matching the record at its normalisation. The file's module docstring is stale and should be updated.
