## 1. The hypothesis, rebuilt, and what L70's dimension theorem adds

**The translation from L70's equality case is sound, and the strict sign is what makes it exact.** $D(v)=\tfrac12\sum_{a,b}(\lambda_a\lambda_b-\lambda_{\min}\lambda_{\max})c_{vab}^2$ with every summand nonnegative, so $D(v)=0$ forces $c_{vab}=0$ unless $\lambda_a\lambda_b=\lambda_{\min}\lambda_{\max}$. That product is *only* attained at the extreme pair: for $\lambda_a,\lambda_b$ of one sign the product is $\ge0>\lambda_{\min}\lambda_{\max}$, and for $\lambda_a<0<\lambda_b$ we have $\lvert\lambda_a\lambda_b\rvert\le\lvert\lambda_{\min}\rvert\lambda_{\max}$ with equality only at both extremes; a zero eigenvalue gives product $0$, again strictly larger. The strictness $\lambda_{\min}<0<\lambda_{\max}$ comes from $\mathop{\mathrm{tr}}W=0$ at $W\ne0$, which L70's block now states. With $c_{abc}$ totally antisymmetric ([conventions.md](../findings/conventions.md)), reading $\langle[v,x],y\rangle=c_{vxy}$ over the three placements of the excluded index gives the three relations verbatim:

$$[\mathfrak m,\mathfrak m]\subseteq\mathfrak h,\qquad[\mathfrak m,\mathfrak h]\subseteq\mathfrak m,\qquad[\mathfrak m,\mathfrak r]=0 .$$

**Item (1), what the new dimension theorem gives L71.** [L70](../findings/bounds.md)'s audit proved that saturation forces $\dim E_{\min}\ge n/2$ and $\dim E_{\max}\ge(n-2)/2$ from [Lemma L31](../findings/stratum-two-negative.md) and the Killing marginal alone. **At $n\ge5$ it is a corollary of L71 rather than an input to it**, L71 concludes $\dim\mathfrak m=n^2/4$ and $\dim\mathfrak h=n(n-2)/4$, which dominate it by a factor of order $n$. It shortens nothing: the two facts L71 needs about dimensions, $\mathfrak m\ne0$ and $\mathfrak h\ne0$, already follow from $\mathop{\mathrm{tr}}W=0$ at $W\ne0$, which the block needs anyway for the strict sign.

**It earns a sentence for two other reasons, and the block should carry both.**

- **It removes an ambiguity in L71's own hypothesis.** L70's excluded-index blockquote establishes that the boxed bound is the weakened member of a family whose sharp member subtracts $m_1$, and that the two equality cases agree **when $\lambda_{\min}$ has multiplicity $\ge2$**. The dimension theorem supplies $\dim E_{\min}\ge n/2\ge3$ at every $n\ge5$, so "saturating Lemma L70" means the same thing under either reading. Without it, L71's hypothesis is one of two conditions and the block does not say which.
- **It makes the $n=4$ exception extremal rather than merely exhibited.** At $W_{\mathbb{CP}^2}$ the spectrum on $\Lambda^2\mathbb R^4$ is $\lambda_{\min}$ at multiplicity $2$, $\lambda_{\max}$ at multiplicity $1$ and $0$ at multiplicity $3$, so $\dim E_{\min}=2=n/2$ and $\dim E_{\max}=1=(n-2)/2$: **both bounds are attained, simultaneously, at exactly the point the lemma excludes.** The block currently justifies the exception by the non-simplicity of $\mathfrak{so}(4)$ alone, which says why the proof stops and not why the point sits where it does.

## 2. The ideal argument, CONFIRMED, rebuilt independently

I take the relations of §1 as the hypothesis and use nothing else.

**$\mathfrak g'=\mathfrak m+[\mathfrak m,\mathfrak m]$ is a subalgebra.** $[\mathfrak m,\mathfrak m]\subseteq\mathfrak h$ and $\mathfrak m\perp\mathfrak h$ make the sum direct. $[[\mathfrak m,\mathfrak m],\mathfrak m]\subseteq[\mathfrak h,\mathfrak m]\subseteq\mathfrak m$. For the top bracket, take generators $X=[m_1,m_2]$ and $Y\in[\mathfrak m,\mathfrak m]$: Jacobi gives $[X,Y]=[m_1,[m_2,Y]]-[m_2,[m_1,Y]]$, and $[m_i,Y]\in[\mathfrak m,[\mathfrak m,\mathfrak m]]\subseteq[\mathfrak m,\mathfrak h]\subseteq\mathfrak m$, so both terms lie in $[\mathfrak m,\mathfrak m]$; linearity extends this from generators to the span. *The block's displayed chain $[[\mathfrak m,\mathfrak m],[\mathfrak m,\mathfrak m]]\subseteq[\mathfrak m,[\mathfrak m,[\mathfrak m,\mathfrak m]]]$ is not a containment of sets as written*, this is [r328](REVIEW_r328.md)'s presentational point, still unrepaired in the text, and it is repaired by the generator expansion just given.

**$\mathfrak r$ centralises $\mathfrak g'$.** $[\mathfrak r,\mathfrak m]=0$ by hypothesis, and $[\mathfrak r,[\mathfrak m,\mathfrak m]]\subseteq[[\mathfrak r,\mathfrak m],\mathfrak m]+[\mathfrak m,[\mathfrak r,\mathfrak m]]=0$.

**$\mathfrak h$ normalises $\mathfrak g'$.** $[\mathfrak h,\mathfrak m]\subseteq\mathfrak m\subseteq\mathfrak g'$, and $[\mathfrak h,[\mathfrak m,\mathfrak m]]\subseteq[[\mathfrak h,\mathfrak m],\mathfrak m]+[\mathfrak m,[\mathfrak h,\mathfrak m]]\subseteq[\mathfrak m,\mathfrak m]$, using $[\mathfrak h,\mathfrak m]\subseteq\mathfrak m$ in both terms. **Item (3): the Jacobi identity proves this containment outright.** The parenthetical *"measured off-$[\mathfrak m,\mathfrak m]$ residual $\le7\times10^{-16}$ at the balanced products and $0$ at $W_{\mathbb{CP}^2}$"* is load-bearing nowhere, and it is weaker than corroboration: the balanced products and $W_{\mathbb{CP}^2}$ are precisely the points at which the conclusion is already known, so the measurement cannot discriminate between the containment holding and its failing anywhere it might. It belongs outside the derivation, in the "Verified structure" paragraph that already reports the same figure.

**$\mathfrak g'$ is an ideal, and the conclusion follows.** $\mathfrak{so}(n)=\mathfrak m\oplus\mathfrak h\oplus\mathfrak r$ as vector spaces and $\mathrm{ad}$ is linear in its first slot, so checking the three summands separately settles $[\mathfrak{so}(n),\mathfrak g']\subseteq\mathfrak g'$. $\mathfrak m\ne0$ because $\mathop{\mathrm{tr}}W=0$ at $W\ne0$ forces $\lambda_{\min}<0$. With $\mathfrak{so}(n)$ simple, $\mathfrak g'=\mathfrak{so}(n)$; since $\mathfrak g'\subseteq\mathfrak m\oplus\mathfrak h$ and the three eigenspace summands are mutually orthogonal, $\mathfrak r=0$ and $[\mathfrak m,\mathfrak m]=\mathfrak h$. Then $\mathfrak{so}(n)=\mathfrak h\oplus\mathfrak m$ with $[\mathfrak h,\mathfrak h]=[\mathfrak h,[\mathfrak m,\mathfrak m]]\subseteq[\mathfrak m,\mathfrak m]=\mathfrak h$, $[\mathfrak h,\mathfrak m]\subseteq\mathfrak m$, $[\mathfrak m,\mathfrak m]\subseteq\mathfrak h$ (a symmetric pair) and $W$ has exactly two eigenvalues because $\mathfrak r$ was defined as every other eigenspace, kernel included. **All of it CONFIRMED.**

**Item (2), where simplicity is invoked, and why $n\ge5$ rather than $n\ge3$.** Simplicity is invoked once, at "$\mathfrak g'=\mathfrak{so}(n)$", and it is **asserted with no citation**, the block states "$\mathfrak{so}(n)$ is simple for $n\ge5$" as a bare fact. The statement is true. **$n=3$ is excluded by vacuity and by nothing else**: $\mathfrak{so}(3)$ is simple, so the argument would run, but $\dim\mathcal W_3=\tfrac{n(n-3)(n+1)(n+2)}{12}=0$, so there is no unit $W$ to be critical. The substantive exclusion is $n=4$ alone. **The block should say so**, because "$n\ge5$" as written invites the reading that simplicity fails below it, which is false at $n=3$.

**Criticality is used nowhere in §2**, a point both [r327](REVIEW_r327.md) and [r328](REVIEW_r328.md) made and the block still does not state. Everything above holds for any traceless symmetric $W\ne0$ on $\Lambda^2\mathbb R^n$ whose $\mathrm{ad}(v)$, $v\in E_{\min}$, is supported on $E_{\min}\otimes E_{\max}$. Criticality enters only twice: to make "saturating L70" equivalent to the scalar condition $\theta=\lambda_{\min}+(n-2)\lambda_{\max}$, and in §4.

## 3. DEFECT, LIVE AND ALREADY GATE 2, the triality sentence does not close the step

> *"$\dim\mathfrak g^\theta$ is an $\mathrm{Aut}$-invariant, so triality produces no new dimension at $n=8$."*
>, [bounds.md](../findings/bounds.md), in the uniqueness paragraph and again as defect (iv); and in [results.md](../results.md)'s canonical statement as "triality produces no new dimension at $n=8$".

**The sentence is true and it does not do the job.** Cartan's classification enumerates involutions up to $\mathrm{Aut}(\mathfrak{so}(n))$. What the lemma consumes is that $\mathfrak h$ is conjugate to the standard $\mathfrak{so}(n/2)\oplus\mathfrak{so}(n/2)$ **under $O(n)$**, because $W$ is recovered from $\mathfrak h$ as an operator on $\Lambda^2\mathbb R^n$ and only $O(n)$ acts on $\mathcal W_n$. For $n\ge5$, $n\ne8$, $\mathrm{Aut}(\mathfrak{so}(n))=PO(n)$ and the two agree. At $n=8$ triality is outer and not induced by $O(8)$, so excluding a new *dimension* leaves open a second $O(8)$-class *at the same dimension*, which is exactly what would break "$W$ is the balanced sphere product".

**The record contains a witness that this failure mode is real at $n=8$, one dimension away.** [graded-half.md](../findings/graded-half.md) records that the triality automorphism $\tau$ preserves $\mathcal W_8$, criticality and $\theta$, and sends $S^3\times S^5$ (isotropy $\mathfrak{so}(3)\oplus\mathfrak{so}(5)$, dimension $13$), to a graded critical point whose $13$-dimensional isotropy $\mathfrak{sp}(1)\oplus\mathfrak{sp}(2)$ acts **irreducibly** on $\mathbb R^8$, "so no element of $O(8)$ carries it to a sphere product or a $\mathbb{CP}^m$". **Two $O(8)$-classes at one fixed-point dimension.** The block's argument would have "proved" they are the same. So the sentence is not merely under-argued; the record already knows the pattern of reasoning is invalid at exactly this $n$, and [results.md](../results.md)'s [Lemma L69](../results.md) block scopes its own enumeration as "complete for $n\ne8$" while L71's does not.

**The conclusion nonetheless survives, by either of two repairs, both already filed.**

- **[r329](REVIEW_r329.md)'s.** $\mathrm{Inn}$ is normal in $\mathrm{Aut}$, so the triality image of an inner involution is inner. The inner involutions of $\mathfrak{so}(8)$ have fixed algebras $\mathfrak{so}(p)\oplus\mathfrak{so}(8-p)$ with $p$ even, and $\mathfrak u(4)$; their dimensions are $16$ ($p=2$), $12$ ($p=4$) and $16$ ($\mathfrak u(4)$). Only $p=4$ gives $12$, and all splittings $\mathbb R^8=\mathbb R^4\oplus\mathbb R^4$ are $SO(8)$-conjugate. One class.
- **[r330](REVIEW_r330.md)'s.** $\mathfrak{so}(4)\oplus\mathfrak{so}(4)\cong\mathfrak{su}(2)^{\oplus4}$ acts on all three $8$-dimensional representations of $\mathrm{Spin}(8)$ as a sum of two $(2,2)$ blocks on complementary pairs of factors, so its triality images act on $\mathbb R^8$ as $\mathbb R^4\oplus\mathbb R^4$ again and are $O(8)$-conjugate to it.

**I verify both independently and they agree**, and I note the contrast that explains why $12$ is safe and $13$ is not: $\mathrm{Ad}(\mathrm{diag}(-1_4,1_4))$ has determinant $+1$ and is inner, while $\mathrm{Ad}(\mathrm{diag}(-1_3,1_5))$ has determinant $-1$ and is not, which is precisely why the $13$-dimensional class splits under triality and the $12$-dimensional one does not. **That contrast is the sentence the block owes**, and neither repair as filed states it.

**Verdict: REFUTED AS STATED; the lemma's conclusion REPAIRED.** The defect is a Gate 2 (found independently by two reviewers on identical briefs) that was filed on 2026-09-01 and never applied to the text it corrects, in two files.
