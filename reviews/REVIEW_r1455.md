## A. The classification

**Convention.** The generalised Kronecker delta is $\delta^{a_1\cdots a_6}_{b_1\cdots b_6}=\sum_{\sigma\in S_6}\mathop{\mathrm{sgn}}\sigma\prod_i\delta^{a_i}_{b_{\sigma(i)}}$, with no $1/6!$. Contracting against $W_{a_1a_2}{}^{b_1b_2}W_{a_3a_4}{}^{b_3b_4}W_{a_5a_6}{}^{b_5b_6}$ sets $b_j=a_{\sigma^{-1}(j)}$. The script uses `inv`, that is $\sigma^{-1}$, and Lean's `LLterm` uses `x (σ j)`. Since $\mathop{\mathrm{sgn}}\sigma=\mathop{\mathrm{sgn}}\sigma^{-1}$ and $\sigma\mapsto\sigma^{-1}$ is a bijection, both are the same sum. In the Euclidean frame upper and lower indices coincide. The normalisation $-1/64$ is then fixed by the count below.

**Self-edges.** Label $a_u$ sits in exactly one lower slot (factor $\lfloor u/2\rfloor$) and one upper slot. If factor $k$'s upper slot carries a label from its own lower pair, that label is summed inside factor $k$ alone, so the factor contributes $\sum_aW_{ab\,a c}$, $\sum_aW_{ab\,ca}$, $\sum_bW_{ab\,bc}$ or $\sum_bW_{ab\,cb}$, each $\pm$ a Ricci contraction of $W$, which is zero. When both upper slots carry the own pair, the factor is $\sum W_{abab}$ or $\sum W_{abba}$, a further trace of a Ricci contraction, zero again. The script's test `owner(u)==k for u in up` catches both cases. The record says "Ricci-flatness", which is this program's name for $R=W$. The vanishing is the trace-freeness of $W$.

**Counting survivors by hand.** Let $n_{kl}$ be the number of factor $k$'s upper slots carrying labels of factor $l$. No self-edge means $n_{kk}=0$, and rows and columns sum to $2$. The solutions are the two cyclic matrices ($n_{01}=n_{12}=n_{20}=2$ and its reverse) and the matrix with every off-diagonal entry $1$.
- Cyclic: factor $k$ places the two labels of its target in $2$ orders, so $2^3=8$ permutations per cycle, $16$ in all.
- Mixed: each lower pair sends one label to each of the other two factors ($2$ ways per pair) and each factor orders its two upper labels ($2$ ways per factor), $2^3\cdot2^3=64$.

So $80$ survivors and $640$ traces, matching the script's `Counter`.

**The $\alpha$ class and its sign.** The cyclic permutation sending pair $0$ to pair $1$ to pair $2$ is a product of two $3$-cycles, sign $+1$, and its term is $W_{12}{}^{34}W_{34}{}^{56}W_{56}{}^{12}=\alpha$. Swapping the two upper labels in one factor changes $\mathop{\mathrm{sgn}}\sigma$ by $-1$ and the factor by $-1$ (`anti34`), so all $8$ terms of the cycle are $+\alpha$. The reverse cycle gives $W_{12}{}^{56}W_{34}{}^{12}W_{56}{}^{34}$, which pair symmetry turns into $\alpha$ again. **Weight $+16$.**

**The $\gamma$ class and its sign.** Take upper labels $(b_1,\dots,b_6)=(a_3,a_5,a_1,a_6,a_2,a_4)$, the map $j\mapsto$ label being $(13)(25)(46)$, sign $-1$. The term is $W_{1235}W_{3416}W_{5624}$. With $a,b,c,d=1,2,3,5$ the first factor is $W_{abcd}$. Pair symmetry gives $W_{3416}=W_{1634}=W_{aecf}$ with $e=6$, $f=4$, and $W_{5624}=W_{2456}=W_{bfde}$. So the term is $-\gamma$. The $64$ mixed permutations form one orbit under relabellings of the six dummies that preserve the pairing, composed with within-factor upper swaps, and each of those moves preserves $\mathop{\mathrm{sgn}}\sigma$ times the term. **Weight $-64$.**

**Canonicalisation.** `canon` applies a factor order, one of the eight symmetries $\{1,\text{anti12},\text{anti34},\text{both}\}\times\{1,\text{pair}\}$ per factor with its sign, then relabels by first occurrence and takes the least key. Each move is an identity of the summed term, so equal keys mean equal terms up to the tracked sign. A class that is minus itself would make the stored sign arbitrary, and the output rules that out here: the class totals are $16$ and $-64$, equal in absolute value to the class sizes, so every term of a class entered with the same sign. The $-64$ key `((0,1,2,3),(0,4,2,5),(1,5,3,4))` is $W_{abcd}W_{aecf}W_{bfde}=\gamma$. *Screen, reproducing the hand count, not evidence beyond it.*

**Normalisation.** $16\alpha-64\gamma=16\alpha-64\beta+64T=16\alpha-64\beta+16\alpha=-64(\beta-\tfrac\alpha2)$, the record's display with $-1/64$.

## B. The Bianchi steps

First Bianchi on the last three indices, $W_{abcd}+W_{acdb}+W_{adbc}=0$ (Lean's `WeylC.bianchi`).

**$\gamma=\beta-T$.** On the third factor, $W_{bfde}+W_{bdef}+W_{befd}=0$, so $W_{bfde}=-W_{bdef}+W_{bedf}$. Multiplying by $W_{abcd}W_{aecf}$ and summing, $\gamma=\beta-T$. This is `gamma_eq`'s pointwise step.

**$4T=\alpha$.**
1. $W_{bdef}$ is antisymmetric in $e,f$, so $T=\tfrac12\sum W_{abcd}(W_{aecf}-W_{afce})W_{bdef}$. Bianchi on $W_{a\,e\,c\,f}$: $W_{aecf}+W_{acfe}+W_{afec}=0$, so $W_{aecf}-W_{afce}=W_{aecf}+W_{afec}=-W_{acfe}=W_{acef}$. Hence $T=\tfrac12Q$, $Q=W_{abcd}W_{acef}W_{bdef}$.
2. Bianchi on the first factor, $W_{abcd}=W_{acbd}-W_{adbc}$. Relabelling $b\leftrightarrow c$ turns $W_{acbd}W_{acef}W_{bdef}$ into $W_{abcd}W_{abef}W_{cdef}=\alpha$. So $Q=\alpha-R$ with $R=W_{adbc}W_{acef}W_{bdef}$.
3. Relabelling $b\leftrightarrow c$ in $R$ gives $\sum W_{adcb}\,W_{abef}W_{cdef}$, and $W_{abef}W_{cdef}$ is antisymmetric in $a,b$ and in $c,d$. The $(ab)(cd)$-antisymmetric part of $W_{adcb}$ is $\tfrac14(W_{adcb}-W_{acdb}-W_{bdca}+W_{bcda})$. Bianchi gives $W_{adcb}-W_{acdb}=W_{adcb}+W_{acbd}=-W_{abdc}=W_{abcd}$, and $-W_{bdca}+W_{bcda}=W_{bdac}+W_{bcda}=-W_{bacd}=W_{abcd}$. So the part is $\tfrac12W_{abcd}$ and $R=\tfrac12\alpha$.
4. $Q=\tfrac\alpha2$ and $T=\tfrac\alpha4$.

Lean's `T_eq` runs the same two antisymmetrisations in a different order (relabel $b\leftrightarrow d$ with `bianchi_diff` on the first factor to reach $S$, then $e\leftrightarrow f$ and $b\leftrightarrow c$ with `bianchi_diff` again). `bianchi_diff`, $W_{abcd}-W_{adcb}=W_{acbd}$, is Bianchi with `anti34` twice: $W_{abcd}=-W_{acdb}-W_{adbc}=W_{acbd}+W_{adcb}$. Correct.
