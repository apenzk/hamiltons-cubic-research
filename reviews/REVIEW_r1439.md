## A. L50's splitting

Quoted from [findings/bispectral.md](../findings/bispectral.md), Lemma L50, line 133: "Let $\mathring W$ be the curvature operator of the second kind on trace-free symmetric $2$-tensors, $(\mathring Wh)_{ij}=\sum_{k,l}W_{iklj}h_{kl}$, with spectrum $\omega$." And line 139: "*The splitting $S^2=S^2_0\oplus\mathbb Rg$ at a Ricci-flat $W$ is kernel-checked too: $\mathring W$ on $S^2$ is its compression to $g^\perp=S^2_0$ and kills $g$ (`Bmat_compress`, `Bmat_zero_eigen` in [L50Split.lean](../lean/HamiltonsCubic/Results/L50Split.lean)), so its spectrum on $S^2$ is $\omega$ with one extra $0$.*"

**The objects.** `S2Idx n` $=$ `Pairs n ⊕ Fin n`, and the printed definition of `e` is `√2 • symB a b` on `inl` and `symB a a` on `inr`, with `symB a b` $=\frac12(E_{ab}+E_{ba})$. So the `inr` vectors are $E_{aa}$, and `gvec`, $0$ on `inl` and $1$ on `inr`, is $\langle g,e_u\rangle$ for every $u$. `ringW R h i j` $=\sum_{kl}R_{iklj}h_{kl}$ is the record's $iklj$ order, and `Bmat` is its Gram matrix in `e`.

**What `Bmat_mulVec_g` really says.** The proof computes $\sum_v\langle\mathring We_u,e_v\rangle g_v=\operatorname{tr}(\mathring We_u)$, which is the statement that $\mathring W$ lands in $S^2_0$. The docstring's "$\mathring Wg=0$ in coordinates" is the same vector read through the symmetry of `Bmat`, and `vecMul_Bmat_g` makes that explicit. Both readings are correct.

**What the spectrum sentence needs.** With $P$ an orthogonal projector of rank $N-1$, `Bmat` $=P\,$`Bmat`$\,P$ and `Bmat`$\,g=0$, choose an orthonormal basis of $g^\perp$ and complete it by $g/\sqrt n$: `Bmat` is block-diagonal $\mathrm{diag}(B_0,0)$, $B_0$ is $\mathring W|_{S^2_0}$ in that basis, and the multiset of eigenvalues is $\mathrm{spec}(B_0)\cup\{0\}=\omega\cup\{0\}$. Every step is elementary, and none is in the file. `Bmat_zero_eigen` is strictly weaker: at a $W$ with $0\in\omega$ it is already witnessed by $\omega$ alone. For the power sums the record uses ($\sum\omega^2=3$, $p_3(\omega)$) the extra $0$ is invisible, so [SecondKindMatrix.lean](../lean/HamiltonsCubic/Results/SecondKindMatrix.lean)'s `L50_spectral` over all of $S^2$ is the record's formula once the multiset statement is granted.

## B. L70 in one frame

Quoted from [findings/bounds.md](../findings/bounds.md), Lemma L70, line 216: "*at every critical point and for every unit $v\in E_{\min}(W)$*", with the boxed identity. Line 224: "*Saturation forces $\dim E_{\min}\ge n/2$ and $\dim E_{\max}\ge(n-2)/2$*". Line 226: "*So equality holds exactly when $\mathrm{ad}(v)$ is supported on $E_{\min}\otimes E_{\max}$ ... for every unit $v\in E_{\min}$.*" Line 228: "*There $W=\Pi_{\mathfrak h}-\tfrac rN I$, so $E_{\max}=\mathfrak h$, $E_{\min}=\mathfrak m=\mathfrak h^\perp$ ... saturation $\iff[\mathfrak m,\mathfrak m]\perp\mathfrak m$*".

**The bracketing hypotheses.** `hlo` and `hγ` together say $\lambda_{\min}$ is the minimum and is attained at $\gamma_0$. `hhi` says $\lambda_{\max}$ bounds the spectrum above, without attainment. `hneg`, `hpos` are the strict signs, which the record derives on $\mathcal W_n$ from $\operatorname{tr}W=0$ at $W\ne0$ and which the L70 row lists as typed in L70b.lean. **Nothing here narrows the record**: the minimum of a finite spectrum is attained, and the extra freedom in $\lambda_{\max}$ only admits hypotheses that are contradictory.

**Every $v\in E_{\min}$ against a frame vector.** For the dimension theorem the record's quantifier is idle, since saturation is a statement about $(\lambda,\theta)$ (line 739 says so: "the hypothesis 'saturates Lemma L70' is the scalar spectral equation"). For the equality case the record's "for every $v$" is recovered from all frame vectors at $\lambda_{\min}$ by linearity of $\langle[x,y],v\rangle$ in $v$, and the basis-free form "supported on $E_{\min}\otimes E_{\max}$" from the fact that each eigenspace is spanned by its frame vectors. Both are by reading and both are correct.

**The two-valued setting.** `hlam` fixes $H$ as the $\lambda_{\max}$ index set, so the frame vectors indexed by $H$ span $E_{\max}$ whatever orthonormal basis Mathlib picked, and `hclosed` is then the basis-free statement that $E_{\max}$ is a subalgebra. The record's normalisation $\lambda_{\max}-\lambda_{\min}=1$ is dropped, which is harmless since saturation is homogeneous.

## C. L134 against L135

Quoted from [findings/route-a.md](../findings/route-a.md), Lemma L134, line 182: "$\Phi=P-\kappa_n\delta^2$ with $\kappa_n=9(\sqrt{3/2}-\theta_0(n))<0$ for $n\ge12$". Lemma L135(b), line 224: "*the candidate $c_2\le\tfrac5{36}$ is exactly $\Delta_2\le\tfrac14$*".

**The record's reading is the Lean's.** (d)'s "or any bound below $0.3591$" and (b)'s "candidate" both make $\tfrac5{36}$ a sufficient condition, and `candidate_implies_target` is precisely that implication on the range where L134 applies. Route-a line 77 fixes $\theta_0(n)$ at odd $n$ to the repaired $\Theta_n$, which is `ThetaSqOdd`, so $\lvert\kappa_n\rvert=9(\Theta_n-\theta^*)$ at both parities from $n\ge12$ on.

**Where the record still says "target".** L135's heading, "and the target is the record's own universal ceiling", and line 240, "it says why $\tfrac14$ is the right target", name $\Delta_2\le\tfrac14$ (equivalently $c_2\le\tfrac5{36}$) as the target. The L140 row of [lean/INDEX.md](../lean/INDEX.md) says "by [L135] that is **exactly** $c_2\le5/36$, I3's target". I3's target is $c_2\le\theta^*/(2\lvert\kappa_n\rvert)$ (L135(b)'s own parenthesis), and L134.lean's whole point is that the two differ at every $n\ge12$.

**The quantifier "at every $n$".** Below $12$ the sign of $\kappa_n$ flips, $\lvert\kappa_n\rvert=9(\theta^*-\Theta_n)$, and L134's reduction is not stated there. The Lean's `hlo` encodes $n\ge12$ implicitly, and the crossover that discharges it at $\Theta_n$ is typed in [L2.lean](../lean/HamiltonsCubic/Results/L2.lean) and not composed.
