## 1. The frame expansion

Write $u_{\alpha\beta}=[v_\alpha,v_\beta]$ and $\omega_{\alpha\beta\gamma}=\langle u_{\alpha\beta},v_\gamma\rangle$, with $\{v_\alpha\}$ an orthonormal eigenbasis of all of $\Lambda^2$, the kernel included. Bilinearity and rank-one positivity give $W^\#=\tfrac12\sum_{\alpha,\beta}\lambda_\alpha\lambda_\beta u_{\alpha\beta}u_{\alpha\beta}^{\mathsf T}$ and $\lvert W\rvert^\#=\tfrac12\sum_{\alpha,\beta}\lvert\lambda_\alpha\lambda_\beta\rvert u_{\alpha\beta}u_{\alpha\beta}^{\mathsf T}$. Pairing with $v_\gamma$ and $v_\delta$:

- $(\lvert W\rvert^\#)_{\gamma\delta}=\tfrac12\sum\omega_{\alpha\beta\gamma}\omega_{\alpha\beta\delta}\lvert\lambda_\alpha\lambda_\beta\rvert$.
- $(P_+W^\#P_+)_{\gamma\delta}=[\gamma,\delta\in+]\cdot\tfrac12\sum\omega_{\alpha\beta\gamma}\omega_{\alpha\beta\delta}\lambda_\alpha\lambda_\beta$, entering with a minus sign.
- $(P_-W^\#P_-)_{\gamma\delta}=[\gamma,\delta\in-]\cdot\tfrac12\sum\omega_{\alpha\beta\gamma}\omega_{\alpha\beta\delta}\lambda_\alpha\lambda_\beta$, entering with a plus sign.

The sum is `Xinv lam ω γ δ` with `wt lam (lam α * lam β) γ δ`, exactly. On the diagonal the weight is $\lvert x\rvert-x\ge0$ on $+$, $\lvert x\rvert+x\ge0$ on $-$ and $\lvert x\rvert$ on the kernel, which is Result 5(a)'s three PSD blocks. The identity check of conventions.md passes: at $W=\mathrm{id}$ the trace is $\tfrac12 N\cdot2(n-2)=(n-2)N$, so the factor is the record's and a display without it would be twice the record's $\#$.

## 2. `Graded`

The three inclusions put every bracket of nonzero eigendirections into $\mathfrak p$ or $\mathfrak m$, so the closure against $\mathfrak k$ comes with them, and the record's "three inclusions plus closure" are four readings of one condition. `Graded` encodes that condition coefficient by coefficient. It does not use antisymmetry of $\omega$, so it does not collapse the $++$ and $--$ conditions the way the record's "two conditions, not three" remark does, and it needs no such collapse.

## 3. The two theorems

Both hold for arbitrary arrays, so they are stronger than any reading restricted to genuine bracket coefficients, and they carry no hypothesis the record's lemmas lack. L154's criticality appears in the record's first proof (through Theorem A) and not in the L155 route the record names as the shorter proof, which is the route formalised.
