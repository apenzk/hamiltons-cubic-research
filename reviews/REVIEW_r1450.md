## A. L54Direct against Lemma L54 (4), "And directly"

**The objects.** `ew w (inl p)` $=\sqrt2\cdot\tfrac12(E_{ab}+E_{ba})=\varphi_{ab}$ and `ew w (inr k)` $=\operatorname{diag}(w_k)$. `Bw w L u v` $=\langle L(\mathrm{ew}\,u),\mathrm{ew}\,v\rangle_{\mathrm{frob}}$, so `Bw wK (ringW R)` is $\mathring W$ in Li's $iklj$ order (r1401) as a matrix in $\{\varphi_{ab}\}\cup\{\operatorname{diag}(w_k)\}$. `Mpair R p q` $=R_{abcd}$, the record's $M$ in the pair basis. `IsCoordDiag R` is $R_{ijkl}=0$ unless $(k,l)\in\{(i,j),(j,i)\}$, the record's "$W_{iklj}\ne0$ only when $\{i,k\}=\{l,j\}$" after relabelling.

**The two identities by hand.** Off the diagonal of `Mpair`, $p\ne q$ with $a<b$, $c<d$ excludes $(c,d)=(a,b)$ and $(c,d)=(b,a)$, so $R_{abcd}=0$. On the diagonal it is $K_{ab}$ (`Mpair_diag`). So `Mpair R` $=\operatorname{diag}(d_\varphi)$, the record's "$M$ is diagonal in the pair basis". For `Bw_pair_off`, $\langle\mathring W\varphi_{ab},\varphi_{cd}\rangle$ expands (`frob_symB_gen`, `ringW_symB`) into $R_{cabd}$, $R_{cbad}$, $R_{dabc}$, $R_{dbac}$, and `cd_zero1` to `cd_zero4` kill each when $(a,b)\ne(c,d)$ with both ordered. `Bw_pair_psi` pairs $\mathring W\varphi_{ab}$ against a diagonal matrix, which reads only the entries $(\mathring W\varphi_{ab})_{ii}\propto R_{iabi}+R_{ibai}$, zero by `cd_zero_diag` since $a\ne b$. ## C. L107Balanced against Lemma L107's tightness paragraph

**`sphereWeight` is the sphere product.** For the Einstein product with $\mathrm{Ric}=c\,g$, the sectional curvatures in the frame are $c/(p-1)$ in the first block, $c/(q-1)$ in the second, $0$ across. The Weyl part of a frame-diagonal operator at an Einstein metric subtracts $c/(n-1)$ from each, leaving $cq/((p-1)(n-1))$, $cp/((q-1)(n-1))$ and $-c/(n-1)$. Scaled by $-(n-1)/c$ on the cross value, these are $q/(p-1)$, $p/(q-1)$, $-1$, the entries of `sphereWeight` (Families.lean line 308). Row sums vanish: $(p-1)\tfrac q{p-1}-q=0$.

**$\lambda_{\min}=-t$.** `diagOp w X` is the Hadamard product $w\circ X$ and `inner2 X X` $=\tfrac12\sum X_{ab}^2$, so $\langle WX,X\rangle+t\langle X,X\rangle=\tfrac t2\sum_{a,b}(w_{ab}+1)X_{ab}^2\ge0$ at $t\ge0$ because every weight is $\ge-1$ (`sphereWeight_ge`: $0$, $\tfrac q{p-1}$, $\tfrac p{q-1}$ are nonnegative at $p,q\ge2$, and the cross value is $-1$). The bound covers every matrix, so in particular every bivector. Equality at $\varphi_{ab}$, $a\in S$, $b\notin S$, comes from `diagOp_phi`, $W\varphi_{ab}=-t\,\varphi_{ab}$, an eigen-equation. So $-t$ is an eigenvalue on $\Lambda^2$ and every eigenvalue $\mu$ with bivector eigenvector $X$ satisfies $\mu\langle X,X\rangle=\langle WX,X\rangle\ge-t\langle X,X\rangle$. **This is the record's $\lambda_{\min}$, the least eigenvalue of $W$ on $\Lambda^2$.** The widening to all matrices is harmless, since the minimiser is a bivector. The step from Rayleigh minimum to "least eigenvalue" is the one line above and is not stated in Lean, which the docstring's "as a Rayleigh minimum" says honestly.

**The normalisation.** `diagOp_normSq` gives $\lVert W\rVert^2=\sum_{a<b}w_{ab}^2$, the record's $\lVert W\rVert_F^2$ on $\Lambda^2$. `sphereWeight_sumSq` is $\sum_{i,j}w_{ij}^2=pq\big(\tfrac q{p-1}+\tfrac p{q-1}+2\big)=pq(n-1)(n-2)/((p-1)(q-1))$, the numerator being $(p+q)^2-3(p+q)+2$. Unit norm gives $t^2\,pq(n-1)(n-2)=2(p-1)(q-1)$, which is `sphereWeight_scale`. At $p=q=m$: $t^2=(m-1)/(m^2(2m-1))=2(n-2)/(n^2(n-1))=$ `lamMinSq n`, and at $n=6$ this is $2/45$, L67's $(2A)^2$ at $m=3$. So `balanced_lamMinSq` is right, and `balanced_tight` is `tightness` times it.

**`thetaSplitSq` is $\theta_{p,q}^2$.** From the scale, $((n-1)t)^2=2(n-1)(p-1)(q-1)/(pq(n-2))$, which is `thetaSplitSq p q` (Families.lean line 485) verbatim. At $p=q=m$ it is $(2m-1)(m-1)/m^2=\theta_0(2m)^2$. That this formula is $\theta$ at the product is `theta_sphereProduct_value`, $\theta(\mathrm{diagOp}(t\cdot w))=\sqrt{\text{thetaSplitSq}}$ at $t>0$, standard three axioms. So the record's "equality holds at every Einstein $S^p\times S^q$", $\theta=(n-1)\lvert\lambda_{\min}\rvert$, is the conjunction of `sphere_lamMin`, `sphere_tight` and `theta_sphereProduct_value` at $t>0$ with square roots taken on nonnegative sides. The conjunction is not stated as one theorem. bounds.md line 565 cites the first two, which is accurate for what they say.

**Observation, sign of $t$.** `sphere_tight` and `balanced_tight` assume no sign on $t$, and their docstrings read $t$ as $\lvert\lambda_{\min}\rvert$. *Screen*: the scratch file instantiates `sphere_tight` at $t<0$ on `Fin 6` and it compiles, so the identity holds there, as a squared identity must. At $t<0$ the operator is $-\lvert t\rvert W_{p,q}$, whose least eigenvalue is $-\lvert t\rvert\max\big(\tfrac q{p-1},\tfrac p{q-1}\big)$, not $-\lvert t\rvert$. The theorems are correct, and the docstring reading holds at $t\ge0$ only, where `sphere_lamMin` applies.
