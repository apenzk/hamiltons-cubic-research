## A. L70 on L23's family

Quoted from [findings/bounds.md](../findings/bounds.md), Lemma L70, line 228: "There $W=\Pi_{\mathfrak h}-\tfrac rN I$, so $E_{\max}=\mathfrak h$, $E_{\min}=\mathfrak m=\mathfrak h^\perp$, and $v\in\mathfrak m$. The both-indices-in-$\mathfrak h$ mass is $\langle v,[\mathfrak h,\mathfrak h]\rangle=0$ automatically, since $\mathfrak h$ is a subalgebra and $\mathfrak m\perp\mathfrak h$. **The only surviving obstruction is the $\mathfrak m\times\mathfrak m$ mass, so saturation $\iff[\mathfrak m,\mathfrak m]\perp\mathfrak m\iff(\mathfrak{so}(n),\mathfrak h)$ is a symmetric pair.**"

The Lean follows the argument step for step. `hh_mass_zero` is the first sentence, the two mixed cases of `L70_support_iff_mm`'s mpr are the pairs $(\lambda_{\max},\lambda_{\min})$ and $(\lambda_{\min},\lambda_{\max})$ that the support condition allows, and the $\mathfrak m\times\mathfrak m$ case is the one obstruction. The mp direction uses `hne`: a nonzero $\mathfrak m\times\mathfrak m$ entry would need $\lambda_{\min}=\lambda_{\max}$.

**Which support condition.** `L70_equality_support` (L70Unit.lean) is stated on class masses $b_{ab}(x)=\frac12\sum_{\gamma\in a,\delta\in b}\langle[\psi_\gamma,\psi_\delta],x\rangle^2$ over a frame $\psi$ on `Fin r`. `saturation_dims` (L70Dims.lean) and `support_of_saturation` (L70Compose.lean) state it on frame indices, `cst v γ₀ a b ≠ 0`. L70Symmetric.lean uses the second, identically. The two forms agree because a class mass vanishes iff each of its squared terms does, and $\langle[\psi_\gamma,\psi_\delta],x\rangle=\mathrm{cst}\,\gamma\,\delta\,\gamma_0=\mathrm{cst}\,\gamma_0\,\gamma\,\delta$ by `cst_cyclic`. That agreement is by reading.

**What "saturation" costs.** Saturation is $\theta=\lambda_{\min}+(n-2)\lambda_{\max}$ at a critical Weyl point. `support_of_saturation` gives saturation to support in the spectral-theorem frame, and the class-form `L70_equality_support` gives the converse. To make line 228's sentence literal, one would take a critical Weyl $W$ whose spectral frame has the two-valued spectrum and $\mathfrak h$ spanned by its top eigenvectors, and compose. The file does not do this, and the record's sentence says it does.

**Every $v\in\mathfrak m$ versus every frame vector.** The record's L70 is "for every unit $v\in E_{\min}$", the Lean quantifies over frame vectors $v_{\gamma_0}$, $\gamma_0\notin H$. For the equivalence this is enough: $[\mathfrak m,\mathfrak m]\perp\mathfrak m$ implies the support condition at every $v\in\mathfrak m$ by linearity in $v$, and the converse needs only the frame vectors.

## B. L57's equality clause in both directions

Quoted from [findings/problem.md](../findings/problem.md), Lemma L57: "$\langle Q(W),W\rangle\le\Theta_n\|W\|^3$ for every diagonal $W$ at every $n\ge4$, $\Theta_n^2=\frac{2(n-1)(n-2)}{n^2}$ ($n$ even), $\Theta_n^2=\frac{2(n-1)(n-3)}{(n+1)(n-2)}$ ($n$ odd), with equality exactly at $S^{n/2}\times S^{n/2}$ for even $n$ and at $S^{\lfloor n/2\rfloor}\times S^{\lceil n/2\rceil}$ for odd $n$, at the *geometric* Weyl tensor of that product, which is $-V/\lVert V\rVert$ under the record's $V$ convention."

`unit_join` is two lines of glue, and it is the right glue: `diagOp_normSq` converts $\lVert\mathrm{diag}\,w\rVert=1$ to the record's $\operatorname{tr}M^2=2$, and `diagCubicFormula` is the record's $\operatorname{tr}W^3=\frac12\langle M^{\circ2},M\rangle$, $\langle W,W^\#\rangle=\frac12\langle M^2,M\rangle$. The only-if directions feed `L57_unit_sphere` and `L57_unit_sphere_odd` (r1437 D4) the `diagP` equation obtained from the $\theta$ hypothesis, and the if directions evaluate $\theta$ at $c\,\texttt{sphereWeight}\,T$ by `theta_sphereProduct_value` and reduce $\texttt{thetaSplit}$ at the balanced and the $(m,m+1)$ split. No direction takes the other as input. The sign is the geometric one, since `sphereWeight` is positive in-block (r1437 D2) and $c>0$ is in both directions.

## C. L154's adjoint

Quoted from [findings/bounds.md](../findings/bounds.md), line 69: "$\mathrm{Ric}^*(h)X=hX+Xh$ is proved the adjoint of $\mathrm{Ric}$ at symmetric $h$ (`ricStar_adjoint`), which ties `J5` to $\langle\mathrm{Ric}(X_{\mathrm{inv}}),h\rangle$."

The proof expands `innerOp` over the $\varphi$ basis, evaluates each $\langle A\varphi_{ij},h\varphi_{ij}+\varphi_{ij}h\rangle$ entrywise by `inner2_ricStar_phi`, swaps $i\leftrightarrow j$ in one half using $A\varphi_{ji}=-A\varphi_{ij}$, and matches the result with $\sum_{a,b}\sum_c\langle A\varphi_{ac},\varphi_{bc}\rangle h_{ab}$ via `inner2_phi`. The left side is the Frobenius pairing of symmetric matrices with no $\frac12$, the right the $\Lambda^2$ pairing with its $\frac12$ over ordered pairs, and the two screens in C2 confirm the constants. The record's $J_5=\langle X_{\mathrm{inv}},H\rangle$ is `J5` in frame form, $\sum_{\gamma\delta}X_{\gamma\delta}\langle Hv_\gamma,v_\delta\rangle$, and C3's gap is that nothing connects it to `ricStar_adjoint`.
