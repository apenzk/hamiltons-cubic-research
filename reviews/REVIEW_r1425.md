## 1. The eigenframe

`U k p` is coordinate `p` of Mathlib's `eigenvectorBasis k` for the Hermitian matrix `Mpair R`, and `U_cols` and `U_rows` are the two orthogonality relations of the eigenvector unitary, read off `mem_unitaryGroup_iff'` and `mem_unitaryGroup_iff`. Setting $v_k=\sum_p U_{kp}\varphi_p$ over ordered pairs, `inner2_frameVec` reads coordinates by pairing, and orthonormality of the $\varphi_p$ (`inner2_phi_pairs`, whose proof excludes the swapped pair because $a<b$ and $b<a$ cannot both hold) turns `U_cols` into orthonormality of the $v_k$. `R_phi_pairs` expands $R\varphi_p=\sum_q\langle R\varphi_p,\varphi_q\rangle\varphi_q$ and uses `hsym` to write the coefficient as `Mpair R q p`, so linearity and `U_eigen` give $Rv_k=\lambda_kv_k$. Completeness is the ordered-pair expansion of a skew $X$ followed by `U_rows`. Since the $\varphi_p$ are an orthonormal basis of $\Lambda^2$ of size $N$, `Mpair R` is the matrix of $W\vert_{\Lambda^2}$ and the $N$ values `eigenvalues k` are its spectrum with multiplicity.

## 2. $\lvert W\rvert$ and the projections

The record's $\lvert W\rvert$ and $P_\pm$ are spectral functions of $W$, so they are the same operators whichever orthonormal eigenbasis is used inside each eigenspace. `absOp` is $\lvert W\rvert$ written on the built frame, and `XinvRec`'s two indicator factors are $P_\pm$ on that frame. `L155_exists` therefore speaks about the record's $X_{\mathrm{inv}}$, and the frame enters only as the coordinates in which it is read. The reading of "every frame entry zero" as $X_{\mathrm{inv}}=0$, and of `Graded` on a frame as the three subspace inclusions with closure, is by bilinearity and was accepted in [r1423](REVIEW_r1423.md) and [r1424](REVIEW_r1424.md).
