## A. Fidelity

- **A-1. The family** ([L124Half.lean:10–12](../lean/HamiltonsCubic/Results/L124Half.lean)). Confirmed with gap. Mathematically the identification is right and complete. A curvature operator supported on Λ²⟨S⟩ is a symmetric 6×6 block in the Λ²₊ ⊕ Λ²₋ splitting. Half-flatness kills the Λ²₋ columns, and symmetry then kills the Λ²₋ rows, leaving one symmetric 3×3 block V on Λ²₊. First Bianchi on Λ²ℝ⁴ equates the traces of the two diagonal blocks, so tr V = tr 0 = 0, and Ricci-flatness in ℝⁿ adds nothing, since contraction over indices outside S vanishes and a self-dual block is Ricci-flat in dimension four. So the half-flat family is exactly `WMat n V` with V symmetric trace-free, and there is no wider half-flat operator the parametrisation misses. **Gap**: the file asserts this in its docstring, and no theorem connects the record's definition (a Weyl operator supported on the four-set that annihilates Λ²₋) to `WMat n V`. The layer already has the parts: [L178Reduce.lean:112](../lean/HamiltonsCubic/Results/L178Reduce.lean)'s `weyl_blocks` gives the vanishing off-diagonal block and the trace-free diagonal blocks for any Weyl `WC n C`, and `WC_split` at [:212](../lean/HamiltonsCubic/Results/L178Reduce.lean) splits `WC n C` into `WMat n A + WMatA n D`, exactly as [L178Rigid.lean:357–369](../lean/HamiltonsCubic/Results/L178Rigid.lean) uses them. Half-flatness is D = 0 in that split. The L124 row at [lean/INDEX.md:471](../lean/INDEX.md) names "the family at the coordinate four-set being `WMat n V`" as a fact rather than as an untyped modelling step. *A note, no gap*: the file works at the coordinate four-set {0, 1, 2, 3}. A general S is a coordinate permutation away, and the row says "coordinate four-set", which is honest.
- **A-2. The ω basis.** Confirmed by hand from [CP2Real.lean:36–39](../lean/HamiltonsCubic/Results/CP2Real.lean) and `phi i j = single j i 1 − single i j 1`. `J 0` has −1 at (0,1), +1 at (1,0), −1 at (2,3), +1 at (3,2), so `J 0 = φ₀₁ + φ₂₃`. `J 1` has −1 at (0,2) and +1 at (1,3), so `J 1 = φ₀₂ − φ₁₃`. `J 2` has −1 at (0,3) and at (1,2), so `J 2 = φ₀₃ + φ₁₂`. With `om a = J a/√2` and (i, j, k, l) = (0, 1, 2, 3) these are the record's ω₁ = (e_ij + e_kl)/√2, ω₂ = (e_ik − e_jl)/√2, ω₃ = (e_il + e_jk)/√2 with the record's signs, not merely up to sign. The basis is self-dual under ⋆e₀₁ = e₂₃, ⋆e₀₂ = e₃₁, ⋆e₀₃ = e₁₂. `pair_sums_diag` ([:130–141](../lean/HamiltonsCubic/Results/L124Half.lean)) sums (0,1)+(2,3) to V₀₀, (0,2)+(1,3) to V₁₁, (0,3)+(1,2) to V₂₂, which is A, B, C in the record's order.
- **A-3. `Msec`** ([:111–112](../lean/HamiltonsCubic/Results/L124Half.lean)). Confirmed. `inner2 X Y = ½ tr(XᵀY)` gives `inner2 (phi i j) (phi i j) = 1`, so φᵢⱼ is the unit bivector e_ij and ⟨Wφᵢⱼ, φᵢⱼ⟩ is the sectional value with no stray factor. `Msec_WMat` expands it to Σ V_bc ω_b(j,i) ω_c(j,i), and at each coordinate pair exactly one ω_a has a nonzero (j,i) entry, ±1/√2, so Mᵢⱼ = ½V_aa, the record's M_ij = M_kl = ½(V₊)₁₁. The sign of ω₂'s (3,1) entry enters squared, as the brief anticipated.
- **A-4. `half_flat_values`** ([:29–46](../lean/HamiltonsCubic/Results/L124Half.lean)). Confirmed. ρ² is ‖W|Λ²⟨S⟩‖², and on this family W is zero off Λ²⟨S⟩, so ρ² = `normSq (WMat n V)`, and the theorem's `tr V²` is ‖V₊‖² in the orthonormal ω basis (`omF_inner`). P = 9 det V passes through the eigenframe with det O² = 1 read off OᵀO = 1, so orientation does not matter. The constant is the record's: at V = diag(2, −1, −1)/√6, det V = 1/(3√6) and 9 det V = √(3/2) at tr V² = 1.
- **A-5. The equality point** ([:53–93](../lean/HamiltonsCubic/Results/L124Half.lean)). Confirmed. θ is scale-invariant, so "exactly at ±W_CP²" is a statement about rays up to frame, and the rendering ∃ orthogonal U, t > 0 with U·W = t·W_c (resp. t < 0) is right. `IsOrth` admits reflections, which is harmless: a reflection carries W_CP² to an anti-self-dual tensor with the same θ, never to −W_CP², and θ(t·W_c) has the sign of t, so the two theorems cannot both fire. `half_flat_eq_neg` reduces through θ(W₋ᵥ) = −θ(Wᵥ), which follows from P odd and ‖W‖ even, and through `WMat_neg`. V = 0 gives θ = 0 in Lean's division, so neither side holds, correctly. The forward direction is `rigid_rot_sd`, reviewed at [r1537](REVIEW_r1537.md), the converse `theta_smul_Wc` with `invariants_conj`.
- **A-6. `sigma_le_rho`** ([:144–162](../lean/HamiltonsCubic/Results/L124Half.lean)). Confirmed. The left side is the record's Σ with A, B, C the diagonal entries, `sigma_of_sum_zero` gives Σ = (3/2)ΣA², and tr V² = ΣA² + 2Σ_{a<b}V_ab² gives the bound with equality iff the off-diagonal vanishes. The record's "the three opposite-pair planes are W-invariant" is that condition: span{e_ij, e_kl} = span{ω₁, ω̄₁}, W kills ω̄₁, so the plane is invariant iff Wω₁ ∝ ω₁, a zero off-diagonal in V's first row. That reading lives in the docstring, which is acceptable for a plane-invariance paraphrase.
