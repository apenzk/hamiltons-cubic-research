### Reviewers 36 and 37: whether Lemma L27's constant is forced by symmetry

**They agree on all six verdicts, and both killed the main claim for the same reason, reached independently.** Own implementations in [review_r36_*.py](scripts/) and [review_r37_core.py](scripts/review_r37_core.py) with ten companions; neither imported the code under review. Reports in [review_r36_report.md](agent_logs/review_r36_report.md) and [review_r37_report.md](agent_logs/review_r37_report.md).

**K4 REFUTED: $\mathrm{sharp\_bilin}(I_p,\cdot)$ is not an endomorphism of $\mathrm{Weyl}_n$.** $I_p$ satisfies the first Bianchi identity but has $\mathrm{Ric}\ne0$, so it is an algebraic curvature operator lying outside $\mathrm{Weyl}_n$, and the map sends $\mathrm{Weyl}_n$ into $S^2(\Lambda^2)$ rather than into itself. For generic unit $V\in\mathrm{Weyl}_n$, $44$–$50\%$ of the image lies outside, roughly $20\%$ violating Bianchi and $40$–$47\%$ violating Ricci-flatness, at five splits. The simplest counterexample is $V=W$: $\mathrm{sharp\_bilin}(I_p,W)=0.8\,W+R$ with $\|R\|$ running from $0.40$ at $(3,4)$ to $1.15$ at $(5,5)$, entirely outside $\mathrm{Weyl}_n$. Schur therefore gives a scalar multiple of the inclusion only under multiplicity-one **in the target**, and the granted multiplicity-freeness is about $\mathrm{Weyl}_n$ and does not transfer. Everything else asserted in K4 holds: equivariance to $2.5\times10^{-16}$ with reflections included, $I_p$ invariant, the operator self-adjoint.

**K5 CONFIRMED for the projected operator and REFUTED for the raw one.** The "scalar to $10^{-15}$" evidence measured $\Phi_p=P_{\mathrm{Weyl}}\circ\mathrm{sharp\_bilin}(I_p,\cdot)$, which genuinely is scalar on all ten summands; the raw operator deviates by $0.23$–$0.79$ on the very eigenspaces called scalar. The merged-eigenspace pattern reproduces exactly, $1.928$, $3.752$, $4.535$ on the zero eigenspaces and $1.414$ at the $(4,4)$ $\lambda=\tfrac13$, and does track summand-merging, of the projected operator. One reviewer explained why the out-of-Weyl part vanishes on $X_p\otimes\mathbb R^q$ specifically: that module does not occur in $(\mathrm{Weyl}_n)^\perp\cong\Lambda^4\oplus S^2$, where every $p$-factor carries label $[1^a]$, $[2]$ or $[0]$ and never $[2,1]$, decomposed independently at six splits, with the Casimir signature never appearing.

**K1 CONFIRMED, and the moral drawn from it was wrong.** The generic $V$ was a fair representative and the identity does fail on the raw support block. But the brief concluded that any proof must therefore use the Weyl conditions, and that is false: the raw $(pp,\mathrm{mix})$ block is **invariant** under $\mathrm{sharp\_bilin}(I_p,\cdot)$, the operator is exactly symmetric there, and the block splits as $\Lambda^2(\mathbb R^p)\otimes\mathbb R^p\otimes\mathbb R^q=(\Lambda^3\oplus X_p\oplus\mathbb R^p)\otimes\mathbb R^q$ with eigenvalues $1$, $-\tfrac12$ and $\tfrac{p-2}2$, verified $p=3\ldots7$. So a support-block computation does reach the constant, with Schur applied in the ambient block and nothing needed about $\mathrm{Weyl}_n$. The Weyl conditions then cut that block **exactly along the isotypic split**: $\Lambda^3\otimes\mathbb R^q$ carries all the Bianchi obstruction, $\mathbb R^p\otimes\mathbb R^q$ all the Ricci obstruction, and $X_p\otimes\mathbb R^q$ is precisely their intersection.

**K2 CONFIRMED and strengthened, the kernel is redundant.** $X_p\otimes\mathbb R^q=\{V\in\mathrm{Weyl}_n:\mathop{\mathrm{supp}}V\subseteq(pp,\mathrm{mix})\}$ at all twelve splits to $n=15$, so the summand has a description with no representation theory and no $\ker L_e$ in it. Dimensions confirmed at 17 splits, $n=6\ldots11$.

**K3 CONFIRMED**, over full orthonormal bases to $6.7\times10^{-15}$, pushed to $n=15$.

**K6: the conclusion stands and the stated route dies with K4, and the collapse is worse than the brief claimed.** The $X_q\otimes\mathbb R^p$ companion is genuinely free by $p\leftrightarrow q$ relabelling, run in both orders at seven split pairs. But "no additional hypothesis" is false twice over. Repairing through the ambient block needs multiplicity-freeness of $\Lambda^2(\mathbb R^p)\otimes\mathbb R^p\otimes\mathbb R^q$, cheaper than the $\mathrm{Weyl}_n$ grant, but a different fact. And repairing through Schur **couples** the two debts instead of removing one: Schur is what carries the value off a single representative, and Schur *is* Remark 4.7(ii) at $p\in\{3,4\}$, so where a failure of 4.7(ii) previously left the constant standing, the two now fall together.

**The way out, and it needs no grant at all.** The constant is **local**: one explicit $V$ ($R_{12,3m}=1$, $R_{23,1m}=-1$), works at every split, with $\mathrm{sharp\_bilin}(I_S,V)=-V/2$ exactly for every index set $S\supseteq\{0,1,2\}$ with $m\notin S$. There is no $p$, $q$ or $n$ dependence to prove. So a direct Bianchi-plus-Ricci index computation on the support subspace, with both conditions demonstrably needed, would discharge the constant without Schur and without 4.7(ii). Also recorded: on that summand $\Phi_p=-\tfrac12$, $\Phi_q=0$, $\Phi_{\mathrm{mix}}=-\tfrac12$, so $L_{e'}V=0$ for **any** three-block diagonal $e'$ and criticality is never used.

**A side result bearing on the remaining grant.** $X_p\otimes\mathbb R^q$ is $O(p)\times O(q)$-irreducible of real type but $SO(4)\times SO(q)$-**reducible**, commutant $1$ against $2$ at $(4,3)$, $(4,4)$ and $(4,5)$, and $1$ at every $p=3$ split. That re-derives the $p=4$ half of Nienhaus's Remark 4.7(ii) for this summand independently, and shows the $O$-versus-$SO$ distinction is load-bearing rather than cosmetic.

**Reconciliation.** No verdict conflicts. The two repairs are complementary rather than competing: one routes through the ambient block and needs a cheaper grant, the other observes the constant is local and points at an index computation needing none. Neither has been carried out.
