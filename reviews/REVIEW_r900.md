## 1. The premise, and it is the whole risk

**The premise is true, both halves, with the constant exactly $1$.** I establish it twice, once from the structure constants and once from the record's Lean definitions read as arithmetic.

**Setting.** Write $\varphi_{ij}$ for [WeylReal.lean](../lean/HamiltonsCubic/Base/WeylReal.lean)'s basis bivector, $+1$ at $(j,i)$ and $-1$ at $(i,j)$, and note $\langle\varphi_{ij},\varphi_{ij}\rangle=1$ under that file's `inner2`, whose $\tfrac12$ is exactly what buys this. A pair-diagonal $W$ is the operator $X\mapsto K\odot X$ on skew matrices, equivalently $W\varphi_{ij}=K_{ij}\varphi_{ij}$, and the $\Lambda^2$-diagonal entry at the pair $(ab)$ is $K_{ab}$.

**The square half is immediate.** $W(W\varphi_{ab})=K_{ab}^2\varphi_{ab}$, so $W^2$ is pair-diagonal with entry $K_{ab}^2$, which is $(W^2)_{ab}=K_{ab}^2$.

**The sharp half.** `sharpQF R η` is $-\tfrac14\sum_{i,j}\langle[\eta,R[\eta,R\varphi_{ij}]],\varphi_{ij}\rangle$. The ordered sum double counts an orthonormal family, so this is $-\tfrac12\operatorname{tr}_{\Lambda^2}(\mathrm{ad}_\eta R\,\mathrm{ad}_\eta R)$. Take $\eta=\varphi_{ab}$ and use $[\varphi_{ab},\varphi_{ac}]=\varphi_{bc}$ and $[\varphi_{ab},\varphi_{bc}]=-\varphi_{ac}$, computed from $\varphi_{ij}=E_{ji}-E_{ij}$. Then $\mathrm{ad}_\eta$ kills $\varphi_{ab}$ and every $\varphi_{cd}$ with $c,d\notin\{a,b\}$, and on each $2$-plane $\mathrm{span}\{\varphi_{ac},\varphi_{bc}\}$, $c\notin\{a,b\}$, it is a quarter turn. Hence

$$\mathrm{ad}_\eta W\,\mathrm{ad}_\eta W\,\varphi_{ac}=-K_{ac}K_{bc}\varphi_{ac},\qquad \mathrm{ad}_\eta W\,\mathrm{ad}_\eta W\,\varphi_{bc}=-K_{ac}K_{bc}\varphi_{bc},$$

so the trace is $-2\sum_{c\notin\{a,b\}}K_{ac}K_{bc}$ and

$$\langle W^\#\varphi_{ab},\varphi_{ab}\rangle=\texttt{sharpQF}(W,\varphi_{ab})=\sum_{c\notin\{a,b\}}K_{ac}K_{cb}=(K^2)_{ab},$$

the last step by $K_{aa}=K_{bb}=0$ and symmetry. **And $W^\#$ is pair-diagonal**: $(W^\#)_{\alpha\beta}=\tfrac12\sum_{\gamma\delta}c_{\alpha\gamma\delta}c_{\beta\gamma\delta}K_\gamma K_\delta$ at diagonal $W$, and $\{\gamma,\delta\}$ must be a triangle pair for $\alpha=(ab)$ and for $\beta$ at once, which forces $\beta=\alpha$ by a two-line index check on $\{(ae),(eb)\}=\{(af),(fd)\}$. `sharpBil` is the polarisation of a quadratic form, and `sharpOp` reassembles from `inner2` coordinates exactly as `skew_expand` licenses, so `sharpOp` is $W^\#$ and carries no factor of its own.

**The normalisation is pinned three ways, independently.** *First*, the record's own anchor: my screen gives $\texttt{sharpQF}(\mathrm{id},\varphi_{01})=n-2$ at $n=4,5,6$, so the $-\tfrac14$ over ordered pairs is the constant that makes $\mathrm{id}^\#=(n-2)\mathrm{id}$, which is what [findings/stratum-two-negative.md](../findings/stratum-two-negative.md) says pins the $\tfrac12$ of the index formula. *That anchor is the one [WeylReal.lean](../lean/HamiltonsCubic/Base/WeylReal.lean) records as absent from its own layer, and this screen supplies it numerically.* *Second*, the sharp operator read off the Lean definitions is exactly pair-diagonal, to $0$ in floating point, and equals $K^2$ on the pair diagonal to $10^{-15}$, at $K_{ss}$, at $K_{ss}$ rescaled to unit norm, at random hollow symmetric zero-row-sum $K$ at $n=4,5,6$, and at a hollow symmetric $K$ with row sums $1$. *Third*, the record's $C_5$ pentagon: the same machinery returns $W^\#=-\mathrm{Id}$ and $W^2=\mathrm{Id}$ at $n=5$, hence $Q(W)=0$, which is what [findings/diagonal-half.md](../findings/diagonal-half.md) records from [r866](REVIEW_r866.md) and [r867](REVIEW_r867.md) at a different object entirely.

**The brief's factor worry resolves in the lemma's favour, and a factor would have been fatal.** With $(W^\#)_{ab}=\lambda(K^2)_{ab}$ the row sum of the two halves is $(1-\lambda)\sum_cK_{ac}^2$, so any $\lambda\ne1$ leaves a nonzero residual proportional to the row mass. `sharpQF`'s $\tfrac14$ against `sharpOp`'s $\tfrac12$ is the pair of factors the brief flags, and they play different roles: the $\tfrac14$ converts an ordered double sum into the $\Lambda^2$ trace with the standard $-\tfrac12$, and the $\tfrac12$ is `skew_expand`'s, the coefficient that makes the $\varphi$ family resolve a skew matrix over ordered pairs. **Neither is free, and both check out against the $\mathrm{id}^\#$ anchor.**

**One citation defect.** The premise is derived on the record exactly once, at [findings/diagonal-half.md](../findings/diagonal-half.md) in the $C_5$ block, where computing $W^\#$ for coordinate-diagonal $W$ from the triangle support of the structure constants is credited to [r866](REVIEW_r866.md) as a general theorem. The same file states the premise a second time as an aside, and [findings/diagonal-half.md](../findings/diagonal-half.md) elsewhere records the criticality equation $M^{\circ2}+M^2=\theta M+\operatorname{diag}(s)$ as holding at **fitted** residual $10^{-16}$. **The L208 block asserts the premise flatly and cites neither**, so a reader who doubts it has nowhere to go inside the block, and the Lean file names it as left out without saying where it lives. *The fix is one clause naming the $C_5$-block derivation.*

## 2. Ricci-flatness on the locus

**Confirmed, and the diagonal conditions are the whole of it.** `Ric R a b` is $\sum_c\langle R\varphi_{ac},\varphi_{bc}\rangle$. At pair-diagonal $R$ this is $\sum_cK_{ac}\langle\varphi_{ac},\varphi_{bc}\rangle$, and $\langle\varphi_{ac},\varphi_{bc}\rangle$ vanishes unless $\{a,c\}=\{b,c\}$, which for $a\ne b$ is impossible and for $a=b$ needs only $c\ne a$. So

$$\operatorname{Ric}(W)_{ab}=0\ \ (a\ne b)\ \text{ identically on the locus},\qquad \operatorname{Ric}(W)_{aa}=\sum_{c\ne a}K_{ac}=(K\mathbf 1)_a .$$

**Ricci-flatness on the locus therefore is $K\mathbf 1=0$, with no residual off-diagonal content**, confirmed to $0$ in floating point at every $K$ I tried. *Two consequences worth recording.* The locus is exactly $\mathcal D_n$ as [findings/problem.md](../findings/problem.md) defines it, symmetric, hollow, zero row sums, and every member is a genuine Weyl tensor, because a pair-diagonal operator satisfies the first Bianchi identity automatically: in `bianchi`'s three terms each $\langle R\varphi_{ij},\varphi_{kl}\rangle$ needs $\{i,j\}=\{k,l\}$, which fails for four distinct indices and cancels in pairs when two coincide.

**An unstated step in the block.** The lemma's conclusion is $\operatorname{Ric}(Q(W))=0$, a matrix statement, and the argument's one display covers the $aa$ entries only. The off-diagonal entries vanish for the reason just given, that $Q(W)$ is again pair-diagonal, and the block never says so. *True as written, and carried by a step the reader has to supply.*

## 3. The cancellation

**Confirmed, every step, and every hypothesis it uses is stated.** From $K\mathbf 1=0$, $K^2\mathbf 1=K(K\mathbf 1)=0$, so $\sum_{c\ne a}(K^2)_{ac}=(K^2\mathbf 1)_a-(K^2)_{aa}=-(K^2)_{aa}$. Then $(K^2)_{aa}=\sum_cK_{ac}K_{ca}=\sum_cK_{ac}^2$ by symmetry, and $\sum_{c\ne a}K_{ac}^2=\sum_cK_{ac}^2$ by $K_{aa}=0$. The two sums are $+\sum_cK_{ac}^2$ and $-\sum_cK_{ac}^2$. **This is the part of the lemma least likely to be wrong and it is right.**

**Each of the three hypotheses is load-bearing, which the brief asks me to name.** Drop hollowness while keeping symmetry and zero row sums and the sum is $-K_{aa}^2$ rather than $0$: at $K=\begin{pmatrix}1&-1\\-1&1\end{pmatrix}$, row sums zero and $K_{00}=1$, the sum is $-1$. Drop zero row sums and the sum is $(K\mathbf1)$-dependent: at the hollow symmetric $K$ with row sums $1$ my screen returns $\lvert\operatorname{Ric}(Q(W))\rvert_{\max}=1$. Drop symmetry and $(K^2)_{aa}=\sum_cK_{ac}K_{ca}$ is no longer a sum of squares. **The Lean file's three hypotheses are therefore exactly the needed set, none redundant.**

## 5. The witness

**$K_{ss}$ is the object it is named after.** [Families.lean](../lean/HamiltonsCubic/Base/Families.lean)'s `sphereWeight` at $p=q=2$ and $n=4$ is $2$ inside each block and $-1$ across, and $K_{ss}$ is exactly $\tfrac13$ of it. The record's [findings/diagonal-half.md](../findings/diagonal-half.md) scale, $1/(p(p-1))$ inside and $-1/(pq)$ across, is $\tfrac12$ and $-\tfrac14$, which is $\tfrac34$ of $K_{ss}$. **All three are the same ray**, and $K_{ss}$ is the geometric representative: the Weyl tensor of the product of two unit round $2$-spheres has $R_{1212}=R_{3434}=1$ and mixed $0$, and subtracting the Einstein constant-curvature part $s/(n(n-1))=\tfrac13$ gives $\tfrac23$ and $-\tfrac13$ entry for entry.

**Its $\theta$ is $\sqrt3/2$, which is $\theta_0(4)$.** At this scale $Q(W)=W$, checked entrywise, $\tfrac49+\tfrac29=\tfrac23$ at a pair and $\tfrac19-\tfrac49=-\tfrac13$ at a mixed pair, so `normSq` $=\sum_{a<b}K_{ab}^2=\tfrac43$, `Pcubic` $=\tfrac43$, and $\theta=\tfrac43/(\tfrac43)^{3/2}=\sqrt3/2=0.866025404$, which my screen returns to nine digits. *Since $\theta$ is invariant under positive rescaling, the same value comes out at unit norm, where `normSq` $=1$ and `Pcubic` $=\sqrt3/2$.* **And a second, independent check from a different findings block**: the unit-norm $\Lambda^2$ spectrum of $K_{ss}$ is the multiset of its entries over unordered pairs, $(2,2,-1,-1,-1,-1)/\sqrt{12}$ to nine digits, which is exactly the spectrum [findings/curvature-invariants.md](../findings/curvature-invariants.md)'s fence post F19 records for Euclidean Schwarzschild at $\theta=\sqrt3/2$. `Kss_sq_pair` and `Kss_sq_mixed` are right, $\tfrac29$ and $-\tfrac49$, and $\operatorname{Ric}(W^2)=\tfrac23I$ at this scale matches the closure block's own number.

**One precision defect, of scale rather than of identity.** The file writes *"the sectional matrix of $S^2\times S^2$ at $n=4$"* with a definite article, and $\lVert K_{ss}\rVert_F^2=\tfrac83$ against the $t=\lVert K\rVert_F^2=2$ that [findings/conventions.md](../findings/conventions.md) attaches to a unit Weyl tensor on the locus, so $K_{ss}$ is not the unit-norm representative and the file says nothing about which of the record's three scales it holds. *Nothing in the lemma notices, the three hypotheses being scale-free, and the non-vacuity claim stands.* **The fix is one clause, that this is the geometric scale at which $Q(W)=W$, hence $\theta=1/\lVert W\rVert=\sqrt3/2$.**

## 6. What the lemma does not reach

**The block's list, the off-diagonal case and the Bianchi half, is wrong in both directions at once.**

*Overstated, the Bianchi half.* On the diagonal locus $Q(W)$ is pair-diagonal, as section 1 shows, and a pair-diagonal operator satisfies the first Bianchi identity automatically, as section 2 shows. **So on this locus the Bianchi half is free and the closure statement is complete, not half complete**: $W\in\mathcal D_n$ with $K\mathbf 1=0$ gives $Q(W)\in\mathcal D_n$ with zero row sums, hence $Q(W)\in\mathcal W_n$. *What is owed is the off-locus case, both halves of it, and the block should say so.*

*Understated, how little the locus adds.* The diagonal locus is precisely the domain of the conjecture's granted diagonal case, up to the $O(n)$ action that makes "coordinate-diagonal in the given frame" and "coordinate-diagonal in some frame" the same set. **So the lemma contains no Weyl tensor the granted case does not already cover, and what it buys is that $\mathcal D_n$ is closed under $Q$**, which legitimises the criticality equation $M^{\circ2}+M^2=\theta M+\operatorname{diag}(s)$ of the diagonal half as an equation inside $\mathcal W_n$. *By bilinearity it extends at no cost to the polarised product: for $X,Y\in\mathcal D_n$ both Ricci-flat, applying the lemma to $X+Y$, $X$ and $Y$ and using linearity of `Ric` gives $\operatorname{Ric}(X\circ Y)=0$, so the whole Fox product closes on the locus and not only the square.* **What it does not reach is every load-bearing use of closure**: $L_e=e\circ(\cdot)$ runs over all of $\mathcal W_n$, so [findings/nondegeneracy.md](../findings/nondegeneracy.md)'s ten-summand decomposition and the $\kappa_n$ apparatus need one argument off the locus, and the bi-spectral relaxation at $t<2$ and the $\mathbb{CP}^2$-type competitor are off it entirely. *The block's sentence "This is the Ricci half of closure" reads larger than the locus it is scoped to, and [r896](REVIEW_r896.md)'s general identity, which the block itself names as subsuming this lemma, is where the closure the citations need actually comes from.*

## 7. What would break it, and what I did to look

**The single object that would overturn the lemma is `sharpOp`'s constant.** Any $\lambda\ne1$ in $(W^\#)_{ab}=\lambda(K^2)_{ab}$ leaves the residual $(1-\lambda)\sum_cK_{ac}^2$, nonzero at every $W\ne0$, and the lemma is then false rather than vacuous. I looked for such a factor four ways: the structure-constant derivation of section 1, the $\mathrm{id}^\#=(n-2)\mathrm{id}$ anchor evaluated on the very definition the file uses at three dimensions, the direct evaluation of `sharpOp` at five different $K$ at three dimensions, and the record's $C_5$ pentagon where $Q(W)=0$ is recorded independently by two earlier reviewers and reproduces here exactly. **All four agree on $\lambda=1$.**

**The second candidate is the $\tfrac12$ in `inner2`, which is the record's own $\sqrt2$ trap, and it cannot reach this lemma.** Rescaling the $\Lambda^2$ inner product leaves $\#$ alone, because $\mathrm{ad}$ involves no metric and the trace is basis-free, and it moves $\lVert W\rVert$ and hence $\theta$. *A statement that some Ricci tensor vanishes is immune to an overall rescaling of the pairing, so the trap lands on $\theta$ and on item 5's numbers, never on item 3's cancellation.*

**The third is hollowness of $K$, and the record's definition supplies it.** $K_{ab}=W_{abab}$ has no diagonal to speak of, the entry $K_{aa}$ being $W_{aaaa}=0$ by antisymmetry, so the hypothesis is a property of the object rather than a restriction on it.

**What I did not do**: compile the Lean file from source, the toolchain being present at Lean 4.33.1 and a full build being the kind of long run the standing analysis-only rule reserves. *I checked the already-built artefact instead, with a two-second live axiom query over a current `.olean`, which settles the absence of `sorry` without the rebuild.*
