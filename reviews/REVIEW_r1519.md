## A. Fidelity

The source is [diagonal-half.md:603](../findings/diagonal-half.md) to line 613, Lemma L178's statement and steps 1 to 5, read verbatim.

- **A-1. SUFrame and stdFrame.** The structure ([L178Frame.lean:31](../lean/HamiltonsCubic/Results/L178Frame.lean) to line 41) asks for a sign with s² = 1, three skew orthogonal 4 × 4 matrices of pairwise Frobenius inner product 4δ, brackets 2s ε, a common cyclic sum, anticommutation of distinct members and a vanishing mixed cyclic sum. Together skew and orth give F_a² = −1, and with `br` and `anti` they give F_aF_b = s F_c on cyclic triples (`mulF`, [L178Rot.lean:74](../lean/HamiltonsCubic/Results/L178Rot.lean)), the quaternion relations. These are consistent, and `stdFrame` ([L178Frame.lean:63](../lean/HamiltonsCubic/Results/L178Frame.lean) to line 73) inhabits the structure with F = J and s = 1, reusing CP2Real's `J_skew`, `J_orth`, `J_inner`, `J_br` and `cyc_eq`, with the two new facts `J_anti` and `J_cyc_cross` obtained from `Jz_anti` and `Jz_cyc_cross` (lines 43 to 48) by `decide` over ℤ and cast. The common cyclic sum is the Pfaffian form ωₐ ∧ ωₐ = vol shared by self-dual forms, which is where `WF_weyl`'s Bianchi case spends Σμ = 0 (line 181). The construction `WF` (line 160) and its invariants copy L178Pad.lean with `cst_omF` (line 151) carrying the sign, and `eps_sum_s` (line 224) shows s enters ⟨W, W#⟩ only through s² = 1, so step 2's γ² = 2 is sign-blind. Correct.
- **A-2. rotFrame.** `rotF A O a = Σ_b O_ab A_b` ([L178Rot.lean:109](../lean/HamiltonsCubic/Results/L178Rot.lean)) takes the rows of O. `cof_eps` (line 53), Σ_{c,d} O_ac O_bd ε_cde = det O Σ_f ε_abf O_fe, says the cross product of rows a and b of an orthogonal O is det O times the row indexed by a × b, which follows from adj O = det O · Oᵀ (`adj_eq`, line 45) and `adjugate_fin_three`. Then [A'_a, A'_b] = 2s Σ O_ac O_bd ε_cde A_e = 2s det O Σ_f ε_abf A'_f, which is `rotF_br` (line 167), so the sign s·det O (line 226) is right, and s_sq holds because det O² = 1 (`det_sq`, line 39). Orthogonality, inner products and anticommutation follow from `rotF_mul` (line 119) and the row relations, and the cyclic facts (lines 192 and 207) are the quadratic expansions with the row identities as the linear-combination coefficients. Correct.
- **A-3. WMat.** `WMat n M X = Σ_{b,c} M_bc ⟨X, ω_b⟩ ω_c` (line 255) with ω = CP2Real's `om`, the orthonormal self-dual basis of the first four coordinates padded into Λ²ℝⁿ. A self-adjoint operator on Λ²ℝⁿ with image in Λ⁺ℝ⁴ and kernel containing its complement is exactly such a sum with M symmetric, and its Ricci contraction is (tr M) times a fixed nonzero matrix (the symmetrised ω_bω_c is −δ_bc on the first four coordinates), so among these the Weyl ones are exactly tr M = 0. The family `WMat` over symmetric trace-free M is therefore the general self-dual Weyl operator supported on Λ⁺ℝ⁴ ⊂ Λ²ℝⁿ for the first four coordinates. Lean proves the forward direction (`WMat_weyl`, line 296). The converse, that every such Weyl operator is a `WMat`, is not stated here and belongs to milestone 4, and the row does not claim it. `WF_rot_eq` (line 260) checks (Oᵀ diag μ O)_bc = Σ_a O_ab μ_a O_ac against the expansion of WF over the rotated frame. `sym_spectral` (line 270) comes from Mathlib's `IsHermitian.spectral_theorem` with the eigenvector unitary transposed, and `WMat_spectral` (line 285) reads Σμ = tr M = 0 off `trace_mul_cycle`. Correct.
- **A-4. Steps 3 to 5.** `WMat_bound` (line 302) is P(W_M)² ≤ (3/2)‖W_M‖⁶ and `WMat_theta_le` (line 308) is θ(W_M) ≤ √(3/2), both in the layer's operator model at every n ≥ 4. Step 2's γ² = 2 comes from the layer's # through `cst_omF`, step 3 holds on this family by direct computation through the eigenframe of `WF_frame`, step 4 is `WF_P`'s P = 9e₃, and step 5 is `disc_identity`'s discriminant. This is steps 3 to 5 for the self-dual half on the first four coordinates. Step 5's equality clause is not lifted to W_M (there is no `WMat_bound_eq_iff`), and none is claimed.
- **A-5. Hypotheses and witness.** `hn : 4 ≤ n`, `hM : Mᵀ = M`, `htr : M.trace = 0` and `h0 : M ≠ 0` are jointly satisfiable, and each of hM and htr is load-bearing (the spectral theorem and Σμ = 0). `h0` is not needed for truth (θ = 0/0 = 0 in Lean at M = 0) and is harmless. Witness, n = 4: M with zero diagonal and every off-diagonal entry 1 is symmetric and trace-free with spectrum (2, −1, −1), p₂ = Σ M_bc² = 6 and det M = 2, so P = 18, P² = 324 = (3/2)·6³ and θ = 18/6^{3/2} = √(3/2), the CP² value attained at a matrix that is not diagonal in the standard basis. A strict case: M = ((1, 1, 0), (1, 0, 0), (0, 0, −1)) has p₂ = 4 and det M = 1, so P = 9 and P² = 81 < (3/2)·4³ = 96.
