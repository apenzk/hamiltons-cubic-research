One line: at every $\Gamma$-fixed unit critical Weyl operator with $P\ne0$, the Riemannian Hessian of $P$ on the Weyl sphere is kernel-checked to preserve every sign-isotypic subspace of the tangent space, and what remains of "block-diagonal, checkable block by block" is that these subspaces sum to $W^\perp$.

## Attack line 1: is `SignChar` the right notion

- **The definition.** `HasChar c S R := ∀ Z, IsSkew Z → S * extend R (Sᵀ * Z * S) * Sᵀ = c • extend R Z`. On skew inputs `extend R` is `R` (TensorBridge's `extend_apply`), and $S^{\mathsf T}ZS$ is skew whenever $Z$ is (`skew_conj`, true for every $S$). So `HasChar` is exactly $(g\cdot R)(Z)=c\,R(Z)$ on $\Lambda^2$ for the action $(g\cdot R)(Z)=g\,R(g^{\mathsf T}Zg)\,g^{\mathsf T}$, the same action WeylAction.lean's `act` uses on bare operators. At $g=\operatorname{diag}(\varepsilon)$ it multiplies the coordinate $R_{ijkl}$ by $\varepsilon_i\varepsilon_j\varepsilon_k\varepsilon_l$, which is the record's $\Gamma$-action on $\mathcal W_n$. `SignChar c R` asks this for every $\varepsilon$ with $\varepsilon_i^2=1$, i.e. every element of $\Gamma$.
- **The character is an arbitrary function.** `c : (ι → ℝ) → ℝ` is not required to be a homomorphism $\Gamma\to\{\pm1\}$. This only enlarges the family of subspaces considered: for a $c$ that is not a character, the subspace it names is $\{0\}$ (a nonzero $R$ forces $c$ multiplicative), and the invariance statement is then trivially true. It restricts nothing.
- **Agreement with L120Block (O1).** `act_extendL` gives `HasChar c S R → act S (extendL R) = c • extendL R` on all matrices. The converse is immediate by restricting the equation to skew $Z$, since `extendL R Z` is `extend R Z` definitionally. So `SignChar c R` holds iff `extendL R` satisfies L120Block's `hX`/`hM` shape for every $\varepsilon$. This is the equivalence the brief asked for, stated as one direction in Lean and the other by definition.
- **`skewProj_conj` for every $S$.** $\mathrm{skewProj}(S^{\mathsf T}ZS)=\tfrac12(S^{\mathsf T}ZS-(S^{\mathsf T}ZS)^{\mathsf T})=\tfrac12(S^{\mathsf T}ZS-S^{\mathsf T}Z^{\mathsf T}S)=S^{\mathsf T}\,\tfrac12(Z-Z^{\mathsf T})\,S$. Only $(AB)^{\mathsf T}=B^{\mathsf T}A^{\mathsf T}$ and bilinearity are used. The Lean proof is that rewrite chain. `hasChar_all` then reads: `extend R (SᵀZS) = extend R (skewProj(SᵀZS)) = extend R (Sᵀ skewProj(Z) S)`, and applies `HasChar` at the skew input `skewProj Z`, then folds `extend R (skewProj Z) = extend R Z`. No orthogonality of $S$ enters, so `act_extendL` holds for every $S$. Orthogonality enters only inside `L120_block` through `signMat_orth`, where `foxProd_act` needs it.

## Attack line 2: `hessOp_char`

- **What it states.** For `v : weylPerpR a` with `SignChar c (tanV v)`, `SignChar c (tanV (hessOp … v))`. With $U_c=\{v\in W^\perp:\ \mathrm{SignChar}\ c\ v\}$ (a subspace, by `hasChar_smul`/`hasChar_sub` and the zero case), this is $\mathrm{Hess}(U_c)\subseteq U_c$ for every $c$. That is what "the Hessian is block-diagonal over the sign characters" means once one knows $W^\perp=\bigoplus_c U_c$, since an operator is block-diagonal for a direct-sum decomposition exactly when it preserves each summand.
- **O2: the decomposition half is owed, and the row says so.** $W^\perp=\bigoplus_c U_c$ over $\Gamma$'s characters is standard (a finite abelian group of commuting involutions acting on a real space), and on the bare side [L120.lean](../lean/HamiltonsCubic/Results/L120.lean) and its siblings type the summands and their dimensions. The `CurvOp2` statement that the $U_c$ exhaust $W^\perp$ is not in L120Hess.lean, and the row's first owed item is exactly this ("*checkable block by block* as a statement about $W^\perp$'s decomposition into these subspaces on `CurvOp2`"). The file's docstring and the row say "maps each sign-isotypic subspace of $W^\perp$ into itself", which is precisely what is proved. The row then quotes the record's "block-diagonal in the same way" as the clause this addresses, which is a fair reading given the owed item beside it.
- **The proof.** `hessOp_apply` unfolds to $(6P(a))\bigl(L_e v-\tfrac12 v\bigr)$. `LeLin_char` gives $L_e v\in U_c$ from `foxProd2_char` with $a$ replaced by $P(a)^{-1}a$ (still $\Gamma$-fixed by `hasChar_smul`). `foxProd2_char` feeds `extendL a` and `extendL v` to `L120_block` with `hM` from `act_extendL (ha ε' hε')` and `one_smul`, and `hX` from `act_extendL (hv ε' hε')`. The bridge `foxProd (extend a) (extend v) = foxProd2 a v` on skew inputs is `foxProd_extend`, used at $Z$ and at $\operatorname{diag}(\varepsilon)^{\mathsf T}Z\operatorname{diag}(\varepsilon)$ (skew by `skew_conj`). Closure under subtraction and scaling finishes it. Every step reads correctly.
- **O3: the hypothesis on $a$.** `hfix : SignChar (fun _ => 1) a` is $\Gamma$-fixedness. The record's parenthesis says "criticality is unused; $\Gamma$-equivariance needs only that $M$ is $\Gamma$-fixed", and the record's "diagonal" is $\Gamma$-fixed because $\mathcal D_n$ is exactly the $\Gamma$-fixed subspace (the record's own reasoning at the L214 block). The remaining binders `ha`, `hWa`, `hcrit`, `hθ` are the arguments `hessOp` needs to exist as an endomorphism of $W^\perp$ ($L_e$ preserves $W^\perp$ only at a unit critical point with $P\ne0$, `LeLin_mapsTo`). The character argument itself never touches them: `LeLin_char` and `foxProd2_char` carry no such hypothesis. So the record's "criticality is unused" holds for the $L_M$ clause as typed, and the Hessian clause needs criticality only because a Hessian on the sphere is the critical-point object. That the record's "diagonal" implies `SignChar (fun _ => 1)` for a `CurvOp2` diagonal operator is the row's second owed item, and it is a short composition of `act_signMat_diag` with `act_extendL`'s converse and `restrict_diag_eq`.
- **Claimed beyond proved.** Nothing. The docstrings for `hessOp_char` and the header claim invariance and nothing else. The commit body's "the record's 'the Hessian of P on the sphere is block-diagonal in the same way'" is an identification of which clause is addressed, with the decomposition listed as owed in the same row.

## Attack line 3: non-vacuity

- **O4.** No declaration in the layer instantiates `hessOp_char` with a concrete $a$ and a $v$ of nontrivial character. I searched for uses of `SignChar`/`HasChar` (only L120Hess.lean, plus unrelated names in L120.lean, L120Dim.lean, L120Cut.lean) and for typed critical diagonal instances on `CurvOp2`. What exists:
  - [L126Main.lean](../lean/HamiltonsCubic/Results/L126Main.lean)'s `sphere_isCritical2`: the sphere product `restrict (diagOp (t • sphereWeight T))` satisfies `Qop2 a = λ • a` on `CurvOp2`, and L126Final.lean derives `Pcubic2 M ≠ 0` for its $M$. Unit norm is a choice of $t$.
  - [L120Cut.lean](../lean/HamiltonsCubic/Results/L120Cut.lean)'s `pair_mem_weylPerp` and `four_mem_weylPerp`: pair-isotypic and four-set-isotypic Weyl operators, restricted to `CurvOp2`, lie in `weylPerp (restrict (diagOp w))`, with characters $\varepsilon_j\varepsilon_k$ and $\varepsilon_i\varepsilon_j\varepsilon_k\varepsilon_l$ stated through single flips `act (flip m) B`.
  - `diag_in_trivial` and L120Block's `act_signMat_diag`: a diagonal operator is $\Gamma$-fixed on the bare side.
- **What is missing is only the translation**: from `act (flip m) B = χ(m) • B` on `CurvLin` for every single flip to `SignChar χ (restrict hB)` for every $\varepsilon$ (multiplicativity over products of flips, then `restrict`/`extend` agreement on skew inputs), and the same for the diagonal $a$. Mathematically the hypotheses are satisfiable and the characters nontrivial (e.g. the balanced sphere product at $n=4$ with a four-set $v$ of character $\varepsilon_1\varepsilon_2\varepsilon_3\varepsilon_4$), so the theorem is not vacuous. The layer does not hold that instance typed. The row records the diagonal half of this gap, and the $v$ half is part of its decomposition item.
