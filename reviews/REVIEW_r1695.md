One line: at every unit critical diagonal Weyl operator with $P\ne0$, the Hessian of $P$ on the sphere is kernel-checked to send each four-set-summand operator of $W^\perp$ to an operator of the same character $\varepsilon_i\varepsilon_j\varepsilon_k\varepsilon_l$, and the operators it quantifies over are ones the layer already builds.

## Attack line 1: the character and `four_act_signMat`

- **The record's character.** [second-order.md](../findings/second-order.md), Lemma L120, proof of the decomposition: $e_{ij}\odot e_{kl}$ has character $\varepsilon_i\varepsilon_j\varepsilon_k\varepsilon_l$, and with $\{i,j\}\cap\{k,l\}=\varnothing$ this is *"a four-index character on the three pairings of a $4$-subset"*. The three pairings $ij|kl$, $ik|jl$, $il|jk$ all carry the same product. So $\varepsilon_i\varepsilon_j\varepsilon_k\varepsilon_l$ is the record's character.
- **The hypothesis matches L120Cut.** `four_act_signMat`'s `hiso` is, verbatim, `∀ m, act (flip m) B = (sgn m i * sgn m j * sgn m k * sgn m l) • B`, the hypothesis of `four_mem_weylPerp` and `four_perp_diag`. With `sgn m i = if i = m then -1 else 1` (WeylInvariantZero.lean), at the flip of $m$ this is $\varepsilon_i\varepsilon_j\varepsilon_k\varepsilon_l$ at $\varepsilon=$ the indicator of $m$.
- **The proof.** `signMat_eq_diag_set` writes $\operatorname{diag}(\varepsilon)$ as `diagSet s` with $s=\{i:\varepsilon_i=-1\}$, `act_diag_set` (read and confirmed in [r1694](REVIEW_r1694.md)) multiplies the flip characters over $s$, three `prod_mul_distrib` split the four-factor product, four `prod_sgn` give $[-1]_{i\in s}$ per index, and four `sign_of_set` turn each into $\varepsilon_\cdot$. `sign_of_set` is the pair file's inline `hv` lifted to a lemma: from $\varepsilon_i^2=1$, $(\varepsilon_i-1)(\varepsilon_i+1)=0$, so $\varepsilon_i=\pm1$ and the indicator agrees.
- **Every sign vector.** The quantifier is over all $\varepsilon$ with $\varepsilon_i^2=1$, which is exactly $\{\pm1\}^n$. Cases checked by hand: $\varepsilon\equiv1$ (empty set, product $1$), one of $i,j,k,l$ flipped ($-1$), two ($+1$), three ($-1$), all four ($+1$), and flips outside $\{i,j,k,l\}$ contributing nothing. All agree with $\varepsilon_i\varepsilon_j\varepsilon_k\varepsilon_l$.
- **Distinctness.** `four_act_signMat` asks none, and is still true without it (at $i=j$ it gives $\varepsilon_k\varepsilon_l$, a pair character). This is a harmless generalisation: the distinctness the record needs enters in `hessOp_four` through `four_mem_weylPerp`.

## Attack line 2: `hessOp_four`

- **Hypotheses side by side with `hessOp_pair`.** Identical: `IsWeight w`, `normSq2 a = 1`, `Qop2 a = lam • a`, `Pcubic2 a ≠ 0` at $a=$ `restrict (diagOp_isWeyl hwt)`, and $B\in$ `weylModuleS`. Differing only in the summand: six distinctness hypotheses on $i,j,k,l$ in place of $j\ne k$, and the four-index flip character in place of the two-index one. These are exactly `four_mem_weylPerp`'s, which places $B$'s restriction in $W^\perp=$ `weylPerp M`.
- **Conclusion.** `SignChar (fun ε => ε i * ε j * ε k * ε l)` of `tanV (hessOp … ⟨restrict B, four_mem_weylPerp …⟩)`. The proof is the pair proof with `four_act_signMat` in place of `pair_act_signMat`: `hessOp_char` (L120Hess.lean) fed `signChar_diag` for $a$ and `hasChar_restrict` on the evaluated `four_act_signMat` for $B$.
- **Is it the record's claim.** The record: *"the Hessian of $P$ on the sphere is block-diagonal in the same way"*, so on $V_{ijkl}\subset W^\perp$ the Hessian stays in $V_{ijkl}$. `hessOp_four` proves the image has character $\varepsilon_i\varepsilon_j\varepsilon_k\varepsilon_l$. As for the pair ([r1694](REVIEW_r1694.md) O3), identifying the character on `CurvOp2` with membership in $V_{ijkl}$ is the owed decomposition. On the bare side `fourSummand_mem` already characterises $V_{ijkl}$ as the Weyl operators of this character under the whole sign group, and the full sign group separates the even-subset characters at every $n$ (the record's block on $\Gamma\cap SO(n)$), so the identification is the right one to owe.
- **The $B$ exists, nonzero at $n\ge4$.** `fourS_mem` and `fourS_iso` (L120Four.lean) give, for every $(x,y,z)$ with $x+y+z=0$, the operator `fourS i j k l x y z` in `weylModuleS` with `∀ m, act (flip m) _ = (sgn m i * sgn m j * sgn m k * sgn m l) • _`, the hypothesis form of `hessOp_four` word for word. `fourPart_mem` (L120Span.lean) packages the four-set part of any Weyl $R$ the same way. At $x=1$, $y=-1$, $z=0$, `coord_fourS_pairings` gives the coordinate $(i,j,k,l)$ equal to $1$, so $B\ne0$. Equivalently `fourSummand_finrank` gives dimension $2$. Distinct $i,j,k,l$ in `Fin n` exist exactly at $n\ge4$, and below that the theorem is vacuous, as the record's summand is empty there. The $a$ side is jointly satisfiable as recorded in [r1693](REVIEW_r1693.md) O4.
