### Reviewers 52 and 53: multi-block products, and a slack that was measured on the wrong scale

**They agree on all five verdicts. M4 (the cycle's headline), is refuted three separate ways, and the audit turned up the first indecomposable $S=0$ critical points on record.** Own implementations in [review_r52_core.py](scripts/review_r52_core.py) and [review_r53_core.py](scripts/review_r53_core.py); both ran the repository's code alongside. Reports in [review_r52_report.md](agent_logs/review_r52_report.md) and [review_r53_report.md](agent_logs/review_r53_report.md).

**M1 CONFIRMED and made exact.** The Ricci diagonal of `sphere_product_W` at $k$ blocks is exactly $(2-k)/p$, $0$ at two blocks in `Fraction` arithmetic, exactly $-1/p$ at three. One restatement: the $|\mathrm{Ric}|\approx0.45$ recorded is the per-index RMS, while $\|\mathrm{Ric}\|_F$ is $1.43$–$1.65$.

**M2 CONFIRMED and upgraded to a proof at $k=3$.** The block-symmetric locus is three-dimensional, so criticality is three quadrics; solved over $\mathbb Q$ it yields exactly **seven nonzero rays**, the Bézout bound of eight minus the origin, so nothing is missing. A 4000-start independent solver returns exactly the repository's 60-start solution set. Two caveats: the driver's zero-entry filter discards genuine $S=0$ critical points, and the sorted-multiset deduplication could in principle collide.

**M3 CONFIRMED to the last digit**, with two facts the record missed. The orbit tangent is *contained* in $E(\tfrac12)$, all 27 principal cosines $1$. More importantly, **$\lambda_{\max}(L_e|_{W^\perp})=1$, not $\tfrac12$**, so $(S^3)^3$ is a saddle rather than a near-maximiser. And the degeneracy is **generic at $k\ge3$**, excess $24$–$162$ across seven products, not an analogue of the $(3,3)$ anomaly.

**M4 REFUTED on three independent counts.**

*The number was measured on the wrong scale.* $S=81$ in the natural normalisation; the recorded $3969$ comes from [b_padded_s3s3.py](scripts/b_padded_s3s3.py)'s helper, which brackets in the **adjoint representation** and so carries a spurious factor $(n-2)^2=49$, ratios $16,36,49,64,81,100,121,144$ measured at $n=6\ldots14$. In route B's own $\theta$-identity convention the value is $0.75$, and on the convention FINDINGS uses elsewhere for its $8.94$ and $16.7$ figures it is $12.73$. **The recorded figure over-reports by about $312\times$.**

*The two slacks are related, and the relation is exactly where the subtlety lives.* $S_\theta=v^2\,S_{\mathrm{struct}}$ **precisely where $\mathfrak h$ is a subalgebra**, nine points, deviation $\le1.3\times10^{-9}$. Where $\mathfrak h$ is not a subalgebra they separate, and the witness matters: the **$C_5$ blow-up at $n=10$, blocks $(2,2,2,2,2)$ with ratios $4:-2:+1$**, is exactly critical (residual $1.3\times10^{-16}$) with $S_{\mathrm{struct}}=0$ and $S_\theta=10/9$. Both repository implementations reproduce both answers. **The crux needs the structural one.**

*The logic is a non sequitur.* $S$ is not a distance to $\{S=0\}$: perturbing padded $W_{\mathbb{CP}^2}$ (an exact $S=0$ critical point), by $10^{-6}$ gives $S_{\mathrm{struct}}\in[11.3,22.2]$, the same order as $\mathfrak{su}(3)$'s $40$. Openness fails through a **sequence**, so the size of $S$ at a single point is not a topological statement at all, and the record's own "$S$ is lower semicontinuous" permits exactly the bad case. **The working exclusion is the $\theta$ one the claim discarded**: $P$ is constant on connected components of $\mathrm{Crit}(P)$, and $\sup\theta/\theta_0$ over $k$-block products is $1/\sqrt{k-1}$, so every three-block product sits below $0.7071\,\theta_0$ (largest found $0.6804$). The recorded phrase "cleaner than the $\theta$ bound" is backwards.

**The most consequential find: indecomposable $S=0$ critical points exist.** Scanning 3767 multi-block criticals at $n=9\ldots16$, $k=3,4,5$ turned up **41 indecomposable ones with $S_{\mathrm{struct}}=0$**, all at $k=5$, all of $C_5$ blow-up type, and all exactly where the two slack conventions disagree. None at $k=3$ or $k=4$, with a structural reason: the negative block graph must be connected, triangle-free and non-bipartite, and $C_5$ is the smallest such graph. There is also a closed form for the sphere-product case, $S_{\mathrm{struct}}=3e_3(n_1,\ldots,n_k)$, verified on 62 partitions to $n=34$, so **no multi-block sphere product has $S=0$**, proved rather than swept, while the blanket claim over all multi-block criticals is false. Separately, $S_\theta$ is not bounded below: along $(2,2,m)$ it decays from $0.468$ to $0.165$ at $n=34$ while $S_{\mathrm{struct}}=12m$ diverges.

**M5 PLAUSIBLE but narrower than claimed.** Three-block *sphere products* are closed (by $\theta$, not by slack) and all tested are degenerate at $\theta/\theta_0\le0.671$. Openness at $(S^3)^3$ itself holds vacuously and was checked anyway: 52 Gauss–Newton correctors from all 54 excess $E(\tfrac12)$ directions plus random controls all return to $\theta=0.7698$, $S=81$, with no nearby $S=0$ critical point. Still untested: the non-sphere-product multi-block class at $(3,3,3,3)$ with $\theta=0.7745087$, $S=81$ and $\mathfrak h$ not a subalgebra; the subalgebra family beyond two members, where $\mathfrak{su}(3)$ is nondegenerate but **$\mathfrak{so}(5)$ is degenerate with excess 35**, which is new; cross-block companions; and indecomposable points generally.
