## Attack line 1: `signChar_diag`

- **What it states.** For any $w$ with `hW : IsWeyl (diagOp w)`, `SignChar (fun _ => 1) (restrict hW)`: for every $\varepsilon$ with $\varepsilon_i^2=1$ and every skew $Z$, $D\,\operatorname{extend}(\operatorname{restrict}\,hW)(DZD)\,D=\operatorname{extend}(\operatorname{restrict}\,hW)(Z)$ with $D=\operatorname{diag}(\varepsilon)=D^{\mathsf T}$.
- **`hasChar_restrict` is sound.** On skew $X$, `extend (restrict hW) X = R X` by `extend_apply` then `restrict_apply`. Both arguments in `HasChar` are skew ($Z$ by hypothesis, $S^{\mathsf T}ZS$ by `skew_conj`, true for every $S$), so the goal rewrites to the bare identity $S\,R(S^{\mathsf T}ZS)\,S^{\mathsf T}=c\,R(Z)$ on skew $Z$, which is the hypothesis `h`. Nothing is lost: the bare identity is only needed on skew inputs.
- **The diagonal instance.** `act_signMat_diag` (L120Block) gives `act (signMat ε) (diagLin w) = diagLin w` as linear maps on all matrices, and `diagLin_eq` makes `diagLin w` definitionally `diagOp w`. By hand: $(\varepsilon_a(w_{ab}\,\varepsilon_aZ_{ab}\varepsilon_b)\varepsilon_b)=w_{ab}Z_{ab}$. Evaluated at $Z$ this is `h` with $c=1$, the `one_smul` rewrite matching the scalar.
- **Is this the record's "diagonal $M$".** The record writes a diagonal $M$ as $M\varphi_{ab}=w_{ab}\varphi_{ab}$ with $w$ a symmetric hollow weight ([Families.lean](../lean/HamiltonsCubic/Base/Families.lean)'s docstring on `diagOp`, the Hadamard product $X\mapsto w\circ X$). `restrict (diagOp w)` on $\Lambda^2$ is exactly that operator. **O1: `signChar_diag` is more general than the record needs**, asking only `IsWeyl (diagOp w)` and none of `IsWeight`'s symmetry, hollowness or row sums. The proof uses no hypothesis beyond typing `restrict`, which matches the record's *"$\Gamma$-equivariance needs only that $M$ is $\Gamma$-fixed"*.

## Attack line 2: the flip-to-sign translation

- **`act_diag_set`**, induction on $s$. Empty case: `diagSet ∅ = 1` (every `if i ∈ ∅` is false), `act 1 B = B`, empty product $1$. Insert case, $a\notin s$: `diagSet (insert a s) = flip a * diagSet s` entrywise, at $i=a$ giving $(-1)\cdot1$ since $a\notin s$, at $i\ne a$ giving $1\cdot[\pm1]_{i\in s}$. Then `act_comp` (`act g (act h R) = act (g*h) R`) peels the flip, `ih`, `act_smul'` (linearity of `act` in $R$, proved from `Matrix.mul_smul`/`smul_mul`) and `h a` give $\chi_a\prod_s\chi_i$, and `prod_insert` reorders. Sound for every finite $s$ and every $\chi$.
- **`signMat_eq_diag_set`.** From $\varepsilon_i^2=1$, $(\varepsilon_i-1)(\varepsilon_i+1)=0$, so $\varepsilon_i\in\{1,-1\}$, and `diagonal ε` agrees with `diagSet {i : εᵢ = -1}` entrywise. For $\varepsilon\equiv1$ the set is empty, `diagSet ∅ = 1`, and the character computed below is $1=\varepsilon_j\varepsilon_k$. For $\varepsilon$ with only $j$ flipped the set is $\{j\}$ and the character is $-1$, only $k$ flipped also $-1$, both flipped $+1$. All match $\varepsilon_j\varepsilon_k$.
- **`prod_sgn` and the character.** `sgn m i = if i = m then -1 else 1` (WeylInvariantZero.lean), so $\prod_{i\in s}\operatorname{sgn}_i(j)$ has exactly one factor $-1$ when $j\in s$ and none otherwise, which is `Finset.prod_ite_eq`. `pair_act_signMat` splits $\prod_{i\in s}\operatorname{sgn}_i(j)\operatorname{sgn}_i(k)$ with `prod_mul_distrib` into $[j\in s]\cdot[k\in s]$ signs, and `hv` identifies $[-1]_{i\in s}$ with $\varepsilon_i$. The result is $\varepsilon_j\varepsilon_k$ for every sign vector.
- **Is $\varepsilon_j\varepsilon_k$ the record's character.** The record gives $e_{ij}\odot e_{ik}$ the character $\varepsilon_i\varepsilon_j\varepsilon_i\varepsilon_k=\varepsilon_j\varepsilon_k$, and L120Cut's `hiso` at the single flip $m$ reads $\operatorname{sgn}_m(j)\operatorname{sgn}_m(k)$, which is $\varepsilon_j\varepsilon_k$ at $\varepsilon=$ the indicator of $m$. Correct. The character is only evaluated on sign vectors, since `SignChar` quantifies over $\varepsilon$ with $\varepsilon_i^2=1$.

## Attack line 3: `hessOp_pair`

- **What it states.** Hypotheses: `IsWeight w`, unit norm, criticality $Q(a)=\lambda a$ and $P(a)\ne0$ for $a=$ `restrict (diagOp w)`, a $B\in$ `weylModuleS` with $j\ne k$ and flip character $\operatorname{sgn}_m(j)\operatorname{sgn}_m(k)$. Conclusion: `SignChar (fun ε => ε j * ε k)` of the Hessian's image of $B$'s restriction, placed in $W^\perp$ by `pair_mem_weylPerp`. The proof feeds `hessOp_char` with `signChar_diag` for $a$ and, for $B$, `hasChar_restrict` on the evaluated `pair_act_signMat`. Every step is a composition of results read above or in [r1693](REVIEW_r1693.md).
- **O3: it is a character statement.** It says the image has character $\varepsilon_j\varepsilon_k$, which is the record's "the Hessian preserves $V_{jk}$" once the $\varepsilon_j\varepsilon_k$-isotypic part of $W^\perp$ on `CurvOp2` is identified with $V_{jk}$. The full sign group separates the characters (the record's block on $\Gamma\cap SO(n)$ explains why the full group is needed), and on the bare side `pairSummand_mem` and `pair_isotypic_signMat` characterise $V_{jk}$ by this character. The `CurvOp2` side of that identification is the row's owed decomposition item. The row says "to one with the same character", which is exactly what is proved.
- **Is it vacuous if no $B$ exists.** It would be, but $B$ exists. `pairS_mem hjk c hsum` and `pairS_iso hjk c hsum` (L120Pair.lean) give, for every coefficient vector $c$ with $\sum_{y\notin\{j,k\}}c_y=0$, the operator `pairLin j k c ∘ₗ skewProj` in `weylModuleS` with precisely `hiso`'s flip character, stated in the same form `∀ m, act (flip m) _ = (sgn m j * sgn m k) • _`. `pairPart_mem` (L120Span.lean) packages the same pair of facts for the pair part of any Weyl $R$, and `pairSummand_mem` (L120PairDim.lean) proves these are all such $B$. **Nonzero ones exist at $n\ge4$**: take $y\ne y'$ outside $\{j,k\}$ and $c=\delta_y-\delta_{y'}$, then `coord_pairS_diag` gives the coordinate $(j,y,k,y)$ equal to $1$. Equivalently, `pairSummand_finrank` gives dimension $n-3\ge1$. (The zero operator satisfies the hypotheses trivially at every $n$.) On the $a$ side, [r1693](REVIEW_r1693.md) O4 located the sphere product's criticality on `CurvOp2` (`sphere_isCritical2`) with `sphereWeight_isWeight` supplying `IsWeight`, so `hessOp_pair`'s hypotheses are jointly satisfiable from typed parts.
- **O2: the row's sentence is inaccurate.** The eb7b4e30 row text reads *"its hypothesis on the operator $B$ is L120Cut's, and no explicit such $B$ is constructed."* The layer constructs such $B$ in the exact hypothesis form, in three files the same row already lists. **What is untyped is a single assembled instance** of `hessOp_pair` (a concrete $w$, $B$ and $j,k$ fed to it). Suggested replacement: *"its hypothesis on $B$ is L120Cut's, met by every `pairLin j k c ∘ₗ skewProj` on the Ricci cut (`pairS_mem`, `pairS_iso`), nonzero at $n\ge4$. No assembled instance with a concrete critical $w$ is typed."* This is a row understatement, and it changes no Lean claim.

## What would break the claims, and what I did to look for it

- **A sign vector on which the flip product is not $\varepsilon_j\varepsilon_k$**, e.g. $\varepsilon\equiv1$ (empty flip set) or $\varepsilon$ flipping exactly one of $j,k$. I traced `diagSet`, `prod_sgn` and `hv` through each of the four cases for $(\varepsilon_j,\varepsilon_k)$ and found the character correct in all.
- **A diagonal Weyl operator that is not $\Gamma$-fixed on $\Lambda^2$.** The entrywise computation $\varepsilon_a\varepsilon_b\cdot w_{ab}\cdot\varepsilon_a\varepsilon_b=w_{ab}$ holds for every $w$, so none exists, and the transfer to $\Lambda^2$ only restricts the domain.
- **A hypothesis on $B$ that no operator satisfies, making `hessOp_pair` vacuous.** I searched the L120 files for constructions in `hiso`'s form and found `pairS_mem`/`pairS_iso`, `pairPart_mem` and `pairSummand_mem`, with a nonzero witness via `coord_pairS_diag`. This search is what exposed O2.
- **A mismatch between the record's "diagonal $M$" and `diagOp`.** Families.lean defines `diagOp w` as the Hadamard product and documents it as the record's $M\varphi_{ab}=w_{ab}\varphi_{ab}$, and `restrict_diag_eq` (L120Cut) identifies the two readings on bivectors.
