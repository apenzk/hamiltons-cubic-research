## Phase 1, the two structure-constant identities, and $\kappa$. **CONFIRMED, both, including the $1$ / $2$ coefficients.**

**Setup fixed from [conventions.md](../findings/conventions.md).** Orthonormal basis $\varphi_{ab}=E_{ab}-E_{ba}$, $a<b$, of $\Lambda^2\mathbb R^n$ under $\langle X,Y\rangle=\tfrac12\mathop{\mathrm{tr}}(X^{\mathsf T}Y)$, that inner product makes $\varphi_{ab}$ unit ($\mathop{\mathrm{tr}}(v^2)=-2$), which is the row conventions.md means; its neighbouring line $\lVert\varphi_{ab}\rVert^2=2$ is the plain-Frobenius reading of the same object and is a second normalisation, not a contradiction. Structure constants $c_{\alpha\beta\gamma}=\langle[\varphi_\alpha,\varphi_\beta],\varphi_\gamma\rangle$ are totally antisymmetric, so $T_{\alpha\beta\gamma}=c_{\alpha\beta\gamma}^2$ is **totally symmetric**, the fact that carries both identities.

**$\kappa=2(n-2)$, derived twice.** With the formula $[E_{ij},E_{kl}]=\delta_{jk}E_{il}-\delta_{li}E_{kj}$ one gets $[\varphi_{12},\varphi_{1c}]=-\varphi_{2c}$ and $[\varphi_{12},\varphi_{2c}]=+\varphi_{1c}$ for $c\ge3$, and $[\varphi_{12},\varphi_{ab}]=0$ when $\{a,b\}\cap\{1,2\}=\emptyset$. So $\sum_\beta\lVert[\varphi_\alpha,\varphi_\beta]\rVert^2=(n-2)+(n-2)=2(n-2)$. This agrees with the Killing form $B(X,Y)=(n-2)\mathop{\mathrm{tr}}(XY)$ of conventions.md via $B(v,v)=-\sum_\beta\lVert[v,\varphi_\beta]\rVert^2=-2(n-2)$ at $\mathop{\mathrm{tr}}(v^2)=-2$. **$\kappa=2(n-2)$ is right in the record's normalisation.**

**Identity 1.** For $\alpha\in\mathfrak h$, split $\sum_{\beta\gamma}T_{\alpha\beta\gamma}=\kappa$ over the four $\mathfrak h/\mathfrak m$ quadrants. $\mathfrak h$ a subalgebra kills $c_{\alpha\beta\gamma}$ with $\alpha,\beta\in\mathfrak h$, $\gamma\in\mathfrak m$; total symmetry kills the mirrored quadrant too. Summing over $\alpha\in\mathfrak h$: $S_{\mathfrak{hhh}}+S_{\mathfrak{hmm}}=\kappa r$, with $S_{\mathfrak{hmm}}:=\sum_{\alpha\in\mathfrak h}\sum_{\beta,\gamma\in\mathfrak m}T_{\alpha\beta\gamma}$. **Coefficient $1$ is right: only one of the four quadrants survives.**

**Identity 2.** For $\alpha\in\mathfrak m$: the $(\mathfrak h,\mathfrak h)$ quadrant vanishes, the $(\mathfrak h,\mathfrak m)$ and $(\mathfrak m,\mathfrak h)$ quadrants are **two distinct ordered quadrants each equal to $S_{\mathfrak{hmm}}$** by total symmetry of $T$, and the $(\mathfrak m,\mathfrak m)$ quadrant gives $S_{\mathfrak{mmm}}$. Summing over $\alpha\in\mathfrak m$: $2S_{\mathfrak{hmm}}+S_{\mathfrak{mmm}}=\kappa(N-r)$. **The coefficient $2$ is exactly the two ordered quadrants, and it is right.** The asymmetry between the two identities is real, not a slip: in identity 1 the repeated-subspace index is the *summation* index $\alpha$, in identity 2 it is one of the two free slots.

**Arithmetic witness, $n=6$, $\mathfrak h=\mathfrak{so}(3)\oplus\mathfrak{so}(3)$** (computed by hand, not by script). Each $\mathfrak{so}(3)$ has $c_{abc}=\pm1$ on all $3!$ ordered triples of $(\varphi_{12},\varphi_{13},\varphi_{23})$, so $S_{\mathfrak{hhh}}=6+6=12$; direct count gives $S_{\mathfrak{hmm}}=6\cdot6=36$ and $S_{\mathfrak{mmm}}=0$. Against the identities: $12+36=48=\kappa r=8\cdot6$ ✓ and $2\cdot36+0=72=\kappa(N-r)=8\cdot9$ ✓.

## Phase 2, the closed forms. **CONFIRMED, and the $W^\#$ normalisation is independently anchored.**

**The $W^\#$ constant the block's two scalar equations encode** is $W^\#_{\alpha\alpha}=\tfrac12\sum_{\gamma\delta}T_{\alpha\gamma\delta}\lambda_\gamma\lambda_\delta$ for $W$ diagonal in the basis. Block-averaging that over $\mathfrak h$ reproduces $\tfrac1{2r}(S_{\mathfrak{hhh}}u^2+S_{\mathfrak{hmm}}v^2)$ and over $\mathfrak m$ reproduces $\tfrac1{2(N-r)}(2S_{\mathfrak{hmm}}uv+S_{\mathfrak{mmm}}v^2)$, the block's equations are the two block-traces of $Q(W)=\theta W$, hence genuine necessary conditions, and the $uv$ term correctly carries the same factor $2$ as identity 2. **The $\tfrac12$ is confirmed against the record's own anchor $\mathop{\mathrm{tr}}(W^\#)=-\lVert W\rVert^2$** ([problem.md](../findings/problem.md), item (C)): at $n=6$ balanced, $u^2=1/10$, $v^2=1/(45/2)\cdot\ldots$, numerically $u=0.3162278$, $v=-0.2108185$, giving $\tfrac12(12u^2+36v^2)+\tfrac12(2\cdot36\,uv)=1.4-2.4=-1$ exactly. A different constant in $W^\#$ would break that.

**The elimination, redone.** Substituting the two identities into the block equations and writing $\sigma=1+\kappa/2=n-1$ gives $\sigma u^2+\tfrac{S_{\mathfrak{hmm}}}{2r}(v^2-u^2)=\theta u$ and $\sigma v+\tfrac{S_{\mathfrak{hmm}}}{N-r}(u-v)=\theta$. With $v=-\rho u$, $\rho=r/(N-r)$ (this is tracelessness), equating the two and cancelling the common factor $u(1+\rho)>0$ leaves $\sigma=S_{\mathfrak{hmm}}\big[\tfrac1{N-r}+\tfrac{1-\rho}{2r}\big]=S_{\mathfrak{hmm}}\tfrac{N}{2r(N-r)}$, i.e. $S_{\mathfrak{hmm}}=2\sigma r(N-r)/N$ ✓ as stated. Back-substituting, $\theta=\sigma u\big[1-\tfrac{N-2r}{N-r}\big]=\sigma ur/(N-r)$ ✓ as stated. Unit norm $ru^2+(N-r)v^2=u^2rN/(N-r)=1$ gives $u=\sqrt{(N-r)/(rN)}$, so $\theta=(n-1)\sqrt{r/(N(N-r))}$, and since $n(n-1)/2=N$ this is **identically** $\tfrac2n\sqrt{rN/(N-r)}$ ✓.

**$S_{\mathfrak{mmm}}$** $=\kappa(N-r)-2S_{\mathfrak{hmm}}=(N-r)\big[2(n-2)-\tfrac{4(n-1)r}{N}\big]$ ✓ exactly as stated.

**The hand case the brief asked for.** $\mathfrak h=\mathfrak{so}(n/2)\oplus\mathfrak{so}(n/2)$ has $r=2\binom{n/2}2=n(n-2)/4$, so $N-r=n^2/4$ and $\theta=\tfrac2n\sqrt{\tfrac{n(n-2)}4\cdot\tfrac{n(n-1)}2\cdot\tfrac4{n^2}}=\tfrac{\sqrt{2(n-1)(n-2)}}n=\theta_0(n)$ ✓ (conventions.md: $\theta_0^2=2(n-1)(n-2)/n^2$). And $[\mathfrak m,\mathfrak m]\subseteq\mathfrak h$ for the block split, so $S_{\mathfrak{mmm}}=0$ ✓, matching the closed form at $r=n(n-2)/4$. At $n=6$ the numbers are $\theta=1.0540926=\theta_0(6)$, hand-computed.

**One unstated normalisation.** The block writes $u=c(1-r/N)>0$, i.e. it silently takes $c>0$. $Q$ is quadratic, so $W$ and $-W$ are both critical with $\theta$ of opposite sign; conventions.md's "normalise $W$ to $\theta>0$" is what fixes the branch, and the Statement does not say so. Minor, and it changes nothing downstream.

## Phase 3, the cap, monotonicity, the equivalence chain. **CONFIRMED, with one sense-of-"graded" caveat carried to Phase 6.**

- **$S_{\mathfrak{mmm}}\ge0\Rightarrow r\le n(n-2)/4$**: $N-r>0$ and $2(n-2)\ge 4(n-1)r/N$ gives $r\le\tfrac{(n-2)N}{2(n-1)}=\tfrac{n(n-2)}4$ ✓.
- **Monotonicity over the whole admissible range**: $\theta(r)=\tfrac2n\sqrt{rN/(N-r)}$ and $r\mapsto r/(N-r)$ is strictly increasing on $(0,N)$, so $\theta$ is strictly increasing and **injective** there. So $r\le n(n-2)/4\iff\theta\le\theta_0(n)$, and **no second branch exists**, the brief's worry is unfounded. $S_{\mathfrak{mmm}}$ is affine in $r$ with negative slope, so $S_{\mathfrak{mmm}}=0$ has the single root $r=n(n-2)/4$. The three conditions $S_{\mathfrak{mmm}}=0$, $r=n(n-2)/4$, $\theta=\theta_0$ are therefore mutually equivalent **given the eigen-equation** ✓.
- **$S_{\mathfrak{mmm}}=0\iff[\mathfrak m,\mathfrak m]\subseteq\mathfrak h$**: $S_{\mathfrak{mmm}}=\sum_{a,b\in\mathfrak m}\lVert\Pi_{\mathfrak m}[\varphi_a,\varphi_b]\rVert^2$, a sum of squares over an orthonormal basis, so it vanishes iff every $\Pi_\mathfrak m[\varphi_a,\varphi_b]=0$. **An identity, no argument needed** ✓.
- **$[\mathfrak m,\mathfrak m]\subseteq\mathfrak h\iff$ symmetric pair**: needs one step the block does not write. $[\mathfrak h,\mathfrak m]\subseteq\mathfrak m$ is *automatic* from $\mathfrak m=\mathfrak h^\perp$ and ad-invariance ($\langle[h,m],h'\rangle=-\langle m,[h,h']\rangle=0$). With that plus $[\mathfrak h,\mathfrak h]\subseteq\mathfrak h$ and $[\mathfrak m,\mathfrak m]\subseteq\mathfrak h$, the map $\pm1$ on $\mathfrak h/\mathfrak m$ is an involutive automorphism, which **is** the definition of a symmetric pair. So this arrow is a definition once the free step is supplied; the free step is genuinely free but is omitted.
- **"$W_\mathfrak h$ is $\mathbb Z_2$-graded"** is the one arrow that is *not* settled inside the block, because conventions.md records that **"graded" carries three inequivalent senses (C) pair, (A) sign, (B) slice, with (C) and (A) incomparable**. The block names none. Carried to Phase 6.

## Phase 4, the even-$n$ classification, $\mathfrak u(m)$, and the odd case. **Conclusion right; two supporting claims in the block are false, and the classification is quoted without the caveat the record itself mandates.**

**$\mathfrak u(m)$ does not break the cap, and the block's own arithmetic is the reason.** $\mathfrak u(m)\subset\mathfrak{so}(2m)$ is a symmetric pair (so $S_{\mathfrak{mmm}}=0$ genuinely) with $r=m^2$ against a cap of $m(m-1)$ at $n=2m$, it exceeds by exactly $m$. The apparent contradiction with "$S_{\mathfrak{mmm}}\ge0$" resolves at the **hypothesis**, not at any of the three conditions: putting $S_{\mathfrak{mmm}}=0$ into identity 2 gives $S_{\mathfrak{hmm}}=(n-2)(N-r)$, and matching that against the derived $S_{\mathfrak{hmm}}=2(n-1)r(N-r)/N$ forces $r=n(n-2)/4$. **So for a symmetric pair, $Q(W_\mathfrak h)=\theta W_\mathfrak h$ holds only at the cap**; $\mathfrak u(m)$ simply fails L23's hypothesis. L23 as *stated* is therefore safe, and the record already holds this ([nondegeneracy.md](../findings/nondegeneracy.md), the r320 "two inequalities pinching" note, with $r-\mathrm{cap}=m$ for $\mathfrak u(m)$ and $(p-q)^2/4$ for the splits). **This is the point the brief asked me to locate: the exclusion happens at the eigen-equation, not at $(\Lambda^4)^K=0$.**

**Consequence the block obscures.** *Every* unbalanced $\mathfrak{so}(p)\oplus\mathfrak{so}(q)$ is a symmetric pair strictly **over** the cap ($r-\mathrm{cap}=(p-q)^2/4$). So the cap is **not** a bound on symmetric pairs, and any reading of L23 as "symmetric pairs are capped" is backwards.

**FINDING 4a, "Each carries a parallel $4$-form" is FALSE, and contradicts the sentence before it.** The block lists as cap-exceeding: $\mathfrak g_2$, $\mathfrak{spin}(7)$, $\mathfrak{so}(n-1)$, $\mathfrak u(m)$, $\mathfrak{su}(m)$, $\mathfrak{sp}(m)\oplus\mathfrak{sp}(1)$, and the unbalanced splits, then says "Each carries a parallel $4$-form, so the third condition says precisely that $\mathfrak h$ is not a special-holonomy algebra."

- **$\mathfrak{so}(n-1)\subset\mathfrak{so}(n)$ carries none for $n\ge6$.** $\Lambda^4(\mathbb R^{n-1}\oplus\mathbb R)=\Lambda^4\mathbb R^{n-1}\oplus\Lambda^3\mathbb R^{n-1}$, and $(\Lambda^k\mathbb R^{n-1})^{SO(n-1)}=0$ for $0<k<n-1$; invariants appear only at $n-1\in\{3,4\}$. So $(\Lambda^4\mathbb R^n)^K=0$ and **(F3) holds** for it, while $r=\binom{n-1}2$ beats the cap by a factor $2(n-1)/n$. It is excluded by $(\mathrm{Sym}^2\mathbb R^n)^K$ being $2$-dimensional, **(F2)**.
- **Unbalanced splits carry none either**, generically: for $\mathfrak{so}(5)\oplus\mathfrak{so}(7)\subset\mathfrak{so}(12)$ ($r=31$ against a cap of $30$), $(\Lambda^4\mathbb R^{12})^{O(5)\times O(7)}=0$, $\Lambda^4\mathbb R^p$ has no $O(p)$-invariant, $\Lambda^2\otimes\Lambda^2$ has none for $p,q\ne2$, and the odd-degree pieces have none. Excluded by **(F1)/(F2)**.
- **The record contradicts itself in one paragraph.** The parallel passage at [ungraded-slack.md](../findings/ungraded-slack.md) line 97 says $\mathfrak{so}(n-1)$ is "failing only (F2)" and then says "Every one of them carries a parallel $4$-form". Those cannot both hold: a parallel $4$-form *is* a failure of (F3).
- **So the equivalence "(F3) says exactly that $\mathfrak h$ is not a special-holonomy algebra" is wrong in both directions.** Forwards it is not exclusive, over-cap subalgebras are killed by (F1) and (F2) as often as by (F3). Backwards it is not necessary either: the block's own $\mathfrak{so}(4)\oplus\mathfrak{so}(4)$ example fails (F3) through two block volume forms with no special holonomy in sight.

**FINDING 4b, $\mathfrak{sp}(m)\oplus\mathfrak{sp}(1)$ is listed as cap-exceeding and exceeds the cap only at $m=2$.** In $\mathfrak{so}(4m)$, $r=m(2m+1)+3=2m^2+m+3$ against a cap of $4m^2-2m$. At $m=2$: $13$ against $12$, over ✓. At $m=3$: $24$ against $30$, **under**. At $m=4$: $39$ against $56$, **far under**, and the gap widens quadratically. The claim is false for every $m\ge3$.

**FINDING 4c, the "Lie-theoretic bound" restatement is unsupported, and self-contradictory under the natural reading of "irreducible".** The block ends: "an irreducible non-special-holonomy subalgebra of $\mathfrak{so}(n)$ has dimension at most $n(n-2)/4$, with equality only for the balanced split." Read "irreducible" as **irreducible on $\mathbb R^n$**, the standard reading, and the one "non-special-holonomy" presupposes, and the **equality case fails its own hypothesis**: $\mathfrak{so}(n/2)\oplus\mathfrak{so}(n/2)$ acts on $\mathbb R^{n/2}\oplus\mathbb R^{n/2}$, reducibly. Read it as (F1), $K$-irreducibility of $\mathfrak h$ *and* $\mathfrak m$, and the sentence is a restatement of L23 under (F1)–(F3) in which "non-special-holonomy" is not equivalent to (F3) (Finding 4a) and so is doing no honest work. The block adds "No statement of it was located in [references/](../references/)", on this evidence that is unsurprising, because the statement as written is not one.

**FINDING 4d, the even-$n$ classification quotes an enumeration the record has flagged as incomplete at $n=8$, with no caveat.** The proof line "Among symmetric pairs, $\binom p2+\binom q2=n(n-2)/4$ ... and $\mathfrak u(m)$ has $r=m^2\ne m(m-1)$" tacitly assumes the $\mathbb Z_2$-gradings of $\mathfrak{so}(n)$ are $\mathfrak{so}(p)\oplus\mathfrak{so}(q)$ and $\mathfrak u(n/2)$ only. **Trap W2** ([INDEX-traps.md](../findings/INDEX-traps.md)) says that list is complete only for $n\ne8$, triality adding $\mathfrak{spin}(7)$ ($r=21$) and $\mathfrak{sp}(1)\oplus\mathfrak{sp}(2)$ ($r=13$), and that "every use of it must carry" the caveat. **The conclusion survives**: the cap at $n=8$ is $12$, and $21\ne12\ne13$, so $\mathfrak{so}(4)\oplus\mathfrak{so}(4)$ is still the unique equality case. But the argument as written is incomplete at exactly the dimension the record singles out, and the repair is one clause.

**The algebra the block does assert is right.** $\binom p2+\binom q2=\frac{n^2+(p-q)^2-2n}4$, so $=\frac{n(n-2)}4$ iff $(p-q)^2=0$ ✓. And $m^2\ne m(m-1)$ for $m\ge1$ ✓.

**Odd $n$.** $n(n-2)$ is a product of two odds, hence odd, so $n(n-2)/4\notin\mathbb Z$ and $r\le n(n-2)/4$ is strict ✓. **Stronger than the block says, and the block does not say it:** since $S_{\mathfrak{mmm}}=0$ forces $r=n(n-2)/4$, **at odd $n$ the family contains no symmetric pair at all**, every member has $S_{\mathfrak{mmm}}>0$ and $\theta<\theta_0(n)$ strictly. The block states the strictness but names no equality case and no odd-$n$ maximiser; that is a coverage gap, not an error.

## Phase 5, "Producing members". **The logic is sound and the dimension count is exactly the one the brief suspected; one hypothesis is missing, and the block's own verification paragraph says it was found and then dropped.**

**The chain, made explicit.** $K=N_{O(n)}(\mathfrak h)\subseteq O(n)$ acts orthogonally on $\Lambda^2\mathbb R^n$ preserving $\mathfrak h$, hence $\mathfrak m=\mathfrak h^\perp$; $W_\mathfrak h$ is $K$-invariant by construction; $Q$ is $O(n)$-equivariant, so $Q(W_\mathfrak h)$ is $K$-invariant.
1. **(F2) $\Rightarrow$ Ricci-flat.** $\mathrm{Ric}$ is equivariant, so $\mathrm{Ric}(W_\mathfrak h)\in(\mathrm{Sym}^2\mathbb R^n)^K=\mathbb R\,\mathrm{Id}$; its trace is the scalar curvature, $\propto\mathop{\mathrm{tr}}W_\mathfrak h=0$; so $\mathrm{Ric}(W_\mathfrak h)=0$ ✓.
2. **(F3) $\Rightarrow$ Bianchi-free.** $\mathrm{Alt}$ is the orthogonal projection onto $\Lambda^4$ ([conventions.md](../findings/conventions.md)) and is equivariant, so $\mathrm{Alt}(W_\mathfrak h)\in(\Lambda^4\mathbb R^n)^K=0$ ✓. Together, $W_\mathfrak h\in\mathcal W_n$.
3. **The count.** With $\mathfrak h,\mathfrak m$ irreducible **and inequivalent** as $K$-modules, $\mathrm{End}_K(\Lambda^2)$ is block-diagonal and the invariant *symmetric* forms are $\dim S^2(\mathfrak h)^K+\dim S^2(\mathfrak m)^K=1+1$, **exactly $2$, spanned by $\Pi_\mathfrak h$ and $\Pi_\mathfrak m$, equivalently by $\mathrm{Id}$ and $\Pi_\mathfrak h$.** (The count is $1$ per summand whatever the summand's real/complex/quaternionic type, since the extra endomorphisms $i,j,k$ are *anti*symmetric against the invariant metric.) Every element of $\mathcal W_n$ is traceless, and the traceless part of that $2$-space is the single line $\mathbb R W_\mathfrak h$. So $\mathcal W_n^K\subseteq\mathbb R W_\mathfrak h$, and $=$ by step 2. Hence $Q(W_\mathfrak h)\in\mathbb R W_\mathfrak h$ ✓.

**So the brief's hypothesis is right and the block's wording is right**: the invariant space is $2$-dimensional spanned by $\Pi_\mathfrak h$ and $\mathrm{Id}$, its traceless part is one line, and "the $K$-invariant traceless Weyl operators are $\mathbb R W_\mathfrak h$" is the correct sentence.

**FINDING 5a, the criterion as stated omits $\mathfrak h\not\cong\mathfrak m$, and the block records that omission against itself.** Step 3 needs the two summands **inequivalent** as $K$-modules. If $\mathfrak h\cong\mathfrak m$ (possible only at $r=N/2$), $\mathrm{End}_K(\Lambda^2)\cong M_2(\mathbb R)$, the invariant symmetric forms number $3$, their traceless part is a **plane**, and $Q(W_\mathfrak h)\parallel W_\mathfrak h$ is no longer forced by equivariance. The block's own **Verification** paragraph says Reviewer 25 "supplied the normaliser correction, the $0<r<N$ and $\mathfrak h\not\cong\mathfrak m$ hypotheses", so the hypothesis was found by the audit and is **absent from the criterion it corrects**. It is a gap in a *sufficient* condition, so it cannot make L23's Statement false; it can make a member produced by the criterion unjustified. **Repair: add "and $\mathfrak h\not\cong\mathfrak m$ as $K$-modules" to (F1).**

**The block's $n=8$ warning checks out.** For $\mathfrak h=\mathfrak{so}(4)\oplus\mathfrak{so}(4)\subset\mathfrak{so}(8)$ with $K_0=SO(4)\times SO(4)$: $(\mathrm{Sym}^2\mathbb R^8)^{K_0}$ is $2$-dimensional (one identity per block, the $\mathbb R^4\otimes\mathbb R^4$ piece contributing none), so (F2) fails; $(\Lambda^4\mathbb R^8)^{K_0}$ contains both block volume forms, so (F3) fails; and each $\mathfrak{so}(4)$ splits self-dual $\oplus$ anti-self-dual, so (F1) fails too. Adjoining the $O(4)$ reflections kills the volume forms and fuses SD with ASD, and the block swap collapses the two $\mathrm{Sym}^2$ identities to one. **"Fails two of the three" is an undercount (it fails all three under $K_0$**) and the repair by the full normaliser is correct. Here $r=12\ne16=N-r$, so $\mathfrak h\not\cong\mathfrak m$ holds and Finding 5a does not bite at the equality case.

## Phase 6, verification, and whether the 29 citations overreach. **No step of L23 rests on numbers; the concession is consistent with the citations.**

**Nothing in the Statement or Proof is numerical.** I re-derived $\kappa$, both identities, both closed forms, the cap, the monotonicity and the equality case by hand above; the script and Reviewer 25 confirm what an argument already gives. The one value the block quotes as content, $0.836660$ for the $\mathfrak{su}(3)$ bi-invariant, is the closed form at $n=8$, $r=8$, $N=28$: $\tfrac28\sqrt{8\cdot28/20}=0.8366600$, hand-checked, so it is a corollary, not evidence. **The only genuinely numerical claims are in "What it does not reach"**, the $\theta/\theta_0$ ladder $0.845,0.730,0.671,0.655$ and "no near-extremal member other than the extremum". Those are census statements about the family's *population*, they are labelled as such, and no part of the lemma leans on them.

**Where the audit line is weaker than it reads.** "Reviewer 25 ... found no counterexample" is a search result, not a proof, and the same sentence records a hypothesis (Finding 5a) that never reached the criterion. So the Verification paragraph over-reports: it presents an audit as closing the block while itself naming a correction the block did not absorb.

**The concession is consistent with the 29 citations, checked, not assumed.** I read every citing line. The pattern is the opposite of overreach: the record fences L23 harder than L23 fences itself.
- **Guards.** [support-dichotomy.md](../findings/support-dichotomy.md) says extending the cap off the family "misreads Lemma L23"; [post F40](../findings/fence-posts.md) proves the $\dim E_+$ extension **false**; [graded-half.md](../findings/graded-half.md)'s labelling table tags L23 as sense **(C)** and says (C) and (A) "part company off it"; [nondegeneracy.md](../findings/nondegeneracy.md) calls the $\{S=0\}$ collapse "nearly a tautology" and states the reach is "modest".
- **Scope-accurate uses.** [results.md](../results.md) line 38 says only "the subalgebra family is capped at $\theta_0(n)$ by a dimension count".
- **One genuinely independent re-derivation, which strengthens the case rather than borrowing from it.** [Lemma L105](../findings/bounds-relaxations.md) obtains the *same* cap $r\le n(n-2)/4$ and the same crossing at $\theta_0(n)$ on the two-eigenvalue stratum from the LP facet $t_{222}\ge0$, "with no sum of squares, no Lie theory and no hypothesis that $E_+$ is closed under bracket". That is a second, hypothesis-free route to L23's conclusion, and it also supplies the structural reading L23 lacks: $t_{112}=0$ says $E_{\max}$ is a subalgebra and $t_{222}=0$ says $E_{\min}$ is bracket-orthogonal to itself, so **L23's two hypotheses are exactly the two facets**, and the balanced split is where both bind.

**FINDING 6a, the "graded" arrow in the equality chain names no sense.** Conventions.md and trap **W3** record three inequivalent senses, (C) pair, (A) sign, (B) slice, with (C) and (A) incomparable, and require every claim to name one. The Statement's chain reads "... when $W_\mathfrak h$ is $\mathbb Z_2$-graded, and when $(\mathfrak{so}(n),\mathfrak h)$ is a symmetric pair" with no label. [graded-half.md](../findings/graded-half.md)'s labelling table settles it, L23 is **(C)**, and on this family (C) and (A) coincide because $\mathfrak h,\mathfrak m$ genuinely are $E_\pm$, so the arrow is true in both senses *here*. But the Statement does not say so, and the record's own trap W3 exists because that omission is how the senses get conflated downstream.

**Does the following section undercut L23?** No. "The graded critical set is strictly wider than the subalgebra family" shows $\mathbb{CP}^3$, Jensen's point and $S^5\times S^7$ are graded, critical and **not Casimirs**, three or more eigenvalues, or (for $S^5\times S^7$) an actual $\theta$ that misses the family's value at its $r$. Every one of them fails L23's hypothesis that $W$ is a two-eigenvalue Casimir with $Q(W)=\theta W$, so none is a counterexample. What it does undercut is any reading of L23 as classifying the graded critical set, and it says so.
