## 2. The circularity at source, independently

**Quoted from [hessian_fast.py:151](../scripts/lib/hessian_fast.py#L151):**

```
    Hess = 6 * M - 3 * lam_P * np.eye(Dt)
```

with `M = Etanflat @ Qform_sym @ Etanflat.T` twelve lines above it and `lam_P = float(wunit_flat @ Qform_sym @ wunit_flat)` at [line 126](../scripts/lib/hessian_fast.py#L126). **The fold's sentence is exactly right.** $\mathrm{Hess}/(6\theta)+\tfrac12=(6M-3\theta\,\mathrm{Id})/(6\theta)+\tfrac12=M/\theta$, an identity in $M$ and $\theta$ with no residue, so the inversion returns the compression of $L_e$ whatever constant the code carries and whatever the true constant is. **It is understated in two ways.** The coefficient is an input the record has edited: [hessian_pipeline_b.py:97](../scripts/lib/hessian_pipeline_b.py#L97) carries the identical line under the comment `# CORRECTED coefficient: -3 lambda, not -2 lambda`. **What the inversion does test, stated positively.** `Qform_sym` is built from the $\mathfrak{so}(n)$ structure constants and $W^*$'s entries, so the spectrum of $M/\theta$ is a computation of $L_e$ that never reads a closed form. ## 6. The `Proof` paragraph and the L27 citation

**No finding, and the swap the brief proposes would introduce an error.** The paragraph needs *an upper bound on the whole spectrum*, $\lambda_{\max}(L_e\vert_{W^\perp})=\tfrac12$ attained exactly on the orbit tangent, which is [Lemma L27](../findings/nondegeneracy.md)'s Statement and holds in L27's range. **[Lemma L207](../findings/nondegeneracy.md) states a different quantity**: its Statement is $\dim E_{1/2}(L_e)=pq+\binom p2\binom q2[\mu=\tfrac12]$ for $p,q\ge2$, an excess count, and it supersedes L27's scope *for that count*, which is what [nondegeneracy.md:466](../findings/nondegeneracy.md#L466) claims and all it claims.

**At $\min(p,q)=2$ the clause L3 needs is false.** [nondegeneracy.md:482](../findings/nondegeneracy.md#L482) records $S^2\times S^{n-2}$ as *a saddle, not a maximiser*, carrying $\binom{n-2}2$ strictly positive Hessian directions, because $\mu>\tfrac12$ there, and [nondegeneracy.md:449](../findings/nondegeneracy.md#L449) says the two failures are different failures, equality at $(3,3)$ and $\mu>\tfrac12$ at $\min(p,q)=2$. **So a citation of L207 for the quoted clause would assert $\lambda_{\max}=\tfrac12$ at a point where it is $\mu>\tfrac12$**, and the wider scope is wider only for the statement L3 does not use.

**Nothing in the paragraph depends on the narrower scope in a way that binds.** L3's hypothesis is even $n\ge8$ balanced, so $p=q=n/2\ge4$, inside L27's range with both exclusions clear, and the box below the proof already handles $n=6$ as L27's excluded $(3,3)$. **The one addition available is additive rather than corrective**: L207 now supplies $\dim E_{1/2}=pq$ at the balanced point by a closed form rather than by L27's multiplicity-free decomposition, so citing both would give the paragraph a second support. That is an improvement and not a defect.
