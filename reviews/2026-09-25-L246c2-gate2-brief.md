# Gate 2 brief: L246 Corollary 2, the support bound (r1136, r1137)

**Rule for reviewers**: analysis-only. Derive by hand; seconds of computation on objects you construct yourself only to screen a claim, reported as a screen. Model: opus.

## Givens (take as proved)

- **Setting.** $W$ a self-adjoint operator on $\Lambda^2\mathbb R^n$ (inner product $\tfrac12\operatorname{tr}(x^{\mathsf T}y)$ on skew matrices) with three eigenvalues $\lambda_0<\lambda_1<\lambda_2$, eigenspaces $E_c$, isotropy $A_c=\sum_{\psi\in E_c}\psi^{\mathsf T}\psi$ over an orthonormal basis of $E_c$, so $\operatorname{tr}A_c=2\dim E_c$.
- $\sum_cA_c=(n-1)I$ and $\sum_c\lambda_cA_c=0$ (Ricci-flatness).
- **L246** (Gate 2 CONFIRMED by two reviewers): for any subspace $F\subseteq\Lambda^2$ and any eigenvector $e$ of $A_F$ with eigenvalue $\beta_j$, $\beta_j\le\sum_{i\ne j}\min(1,\beta_i)$ over the other eigenvalues of $A_F$.

## The claim under audit, verbatim

**Corollary 2, the support bound** (*Gate 2 owed*). At $\lambda_1=0$, with $r=\lvert\lambda_0\rvert/\lambda_2$, if $E_0\subseteq\Lambda^2S$ with $\dim S=s$ (equivalently $\operatorname{rank}A_0\le s$) and $\dim E_0=p$, then $s(s-1)\ge2(1+r)p$: an extreme eigenspace fills at most a $\tfrac1{1+r}$ share of any $\Lambda^2S$ containing it, so $E_0=\Lambda^2\mathbb R^s$ is excluded at every $n$. *Proof*: $A_2=rA_0$, so the pair $(0,2)$ has $\beta=(1+r)\alpha$ and the inequality reads $\alpha_j\le\sum_{i\ne j}\min(\tfrac1{1+r},\alpha_i)$; at most $s-1$ other eigenvalues are nonzero, so $\alpha_{\max}\le\tfrac{s-1}{1+r}$, and $2p=\operatorname{tr}A_0\le s\,\alpha_{\max}$.

## Attack items

1. Is each step valid: $A_2=rA_0$ at $\lambda_1=0$; the pair form $\alpha_j\le\sum_{i\ne j}\min(\tfrac1{1+r},\alpha_i)$; $\operatorname{rank}A_0\le s$ iff $E_0\subseteq\Lambda^2S$ with $\dim S=s$; $\alpha_{\max}\le\tfrac{s-1}{1+r}$; $2p\le s\,\alpha_{\max}$?
2. Is "$E_0=\Lambda^2\mathbb R^s$ is excluded at every $n$" right, and for which $s$ (including $s=n$, where $E_0$ would be all of $\Lambda^2$)?
3. Is the bound attained or near-attained by any configuration, and is it implied by anything simpler (e.g. $\dim E_0\le\dim\Lambda^2S$)?
4. Does "fills at most a $\tfrac1{1+r}$ share" state the inequality faithfully (share of $\dim\Lambda^2S=\tfrac{s(s-1)}2$)?
