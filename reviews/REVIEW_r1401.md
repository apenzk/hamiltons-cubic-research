**Verdict: CONFIRMED.** `R4` is the record's $W_{ijkl}$ in sign, scale and norm, `ringW` is Li's $iklj$ operator and gives $+\mathrm{Id}$ on $S^2_0$ at the round sphere, and the four proved facts are correct under the right hypotheses. The row's "Not typed" list is right in content but should be stated on $S^2$, with the $\Lambda^2$ block $-M$ and the $\beta$ dictionary named beside it.

**The refuting object** would be a symmetric trace-free $h$ with `ringW id h` $\ne h$ (the $ikjl$ order or a sign flip in `phi`), or an index quadruple where `R4 id` differs from $\delta_{ik}\delta_{jl}-\delta_{il}\delta_{jk}$ (a factor $2$ from the unnormalised $\phi$ or the $\tfrac12$ in `inner2`). I looked for it by computing `inner2 (phi i j) (phi k l)` entrywise from the definitions in WeylReal.lean, substituting into `ringW` at $R=\mathrm{Id}$, and checking the trace part against `Ric`. None exists.
