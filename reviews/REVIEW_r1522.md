**Summary: L178Support.lean soundly proves θ(R) ≤ √(3/2) for every Weyl R on ℝⁿ supported on the coordinate Λ²ℝ⁴ whose coefficient matrix C_ij = ⟨R e_i, e_j⟩ is nonzero of rank at most three (6/6 on the standard three axioms, no `sorry`), discharging r1521's owed identification with W_C and its gap C-dh, with two minor gaps: the equivalence of `C.rank ≤ 3` and `C ≠ 0` with the source's operator rank and nonvanishing is untyped and no longer named anywhere, and row 70 still carries an unqualified "Owed: the padding (step 3)" beside its own "STEP 3 IS PROVED".**

## A. Fidelity

The source is [diagonal-half.md:603](../findings/diagonal-half.md), Lemma L178: a nonzero W ∈ 𝒲_n supported on Λ²V for a four-dimensional V ⊆ ℝⁿ with rank(W : Λ² → Λ²) ≤ 3 has θ(W) ≤ √(3/2), with equality exactly at W_CP² up to positive scale and an O(n) frame change.

- **A-1. eF_inner** ([L178Support.lean:32](../lean/HamiltonsCubic/Results/L178Support.lean)). The diagonal blocks reuse `omF_inner` for `stdFrame` and `asdFrame`. The mixed case writes (eF (inl a))ᵀ eF (inr b) as ½ · Emb (J_aᵀ K_b) Embᵀ through `sandwich`, moves Emb round the trace by `trace_mul_comm`, collapses Embᵀ Emb = 1 by `Emb_orth`, and closes by `J_K_orth`. With `inner2 X Y = ½ tr(XᵀY)` this is ⟨eF i, eF j⟩ = δ_ij, and six orthonormal elements of the six-dimensional coordinate so(4) span it, so span e = Λ²ℝ⁴ ⊂ Λ²ℝⁿ. Correct, and it types the orthonormality that r1521 A-1 checked by hand.
- **A-2. The support hypotheses** (line 50 and line 51). `hker` says R X = 0 for every bivector X orthogonal to all e_i, which is W vanishing on (Λ²V)^⊥. `hran` says ⟨R X, Y⟩ = 0 for every bivector X and every bivector Y ⊥ e, and since R X is a bivector by `IsWeyl`'s first clause, this is R(Λ²) ⊆ Λ²V. Together they are W = P W P with P the orthogonal projection onto Λ²V, the standard meaning of "supported on Λ²V". *Note, not charged:* `hran` follows from `hker` and `IsWeyl`'s symmetry clause, since ⟨R X, Y⟩ = ⟨X, R Y⟩ = 0 when Y ⊥ e, so it is redundant but harmless, and every supported W satisfies it. Faithful.
- **A-3. Rank.** `supported_eq_WC` (line 49) proves R X = W_C X on bivectors with C_ij = ⟨R e_i, e_j⟩, by `extend_frame` completing e to an orthonormal basis (e, g) of Λ²ℝⁿ, `weyl_lin` for linearity on bivectors, `hker` to kill the g-part of X, and `hran` to kill the g-part of each R e_i. With e orthonormal (A-1), R restricted to Λ² is Σ C_ij ⟨·, e_i⟩ e_j, whose rank as a map Λ² → Λ² is rank C. So `hrank : C.rank ≤ 3` (line 99) is the source's rank hypothesis, and `h0 : C ≠ 0` (line 100) is the source's W ≠ 0, since a supported R vanishes on bivectors exactly when C = 0. **Gap A-rank (minor).** The layer's R is a bare function on matrices and has no typed rank on Λ², so stating the hypothesis on C is the natural typing. The translation from rank(W : Λ² → Λ²) and W ≠ 0 to the two hypotheses on C is elementary linear algebra and true, but it is untyped, and r1521 listed it as part of the owed identification with W_C. Now that `supported_eq_WC` discharges the identification, the records drop the item entirely: [lean/INDEX.md:70](../lean/INDEX.md) says "with coefficient matrix of rank at most three", which is accurate, but neither row 70's "Still owed" nor [diagonal-half.md:613](../findings/diagonal-half.md) ("states it for the operator") says that the operator-rank reading is by hand.
- **A-4. L178_supported** (line 96). For `4 ≤ n`, `IsWeyl R`, `hker`, `hran`, `C.rank ≤ 3` and `C ≠ 0`, it concludes `theta R ≤ √(3/2)`, with `theta` the layer's P/‖R‖³. Under A-2 and A-3 this is L178's inequality on ℝⁿ for V the first four coordinates, stated for the operator rather than for W_C. The equality clause is not claimed. The sign-closed |θ| ≤ √(3/2) of the source follows by applying the theorem to −R, since θ is odd, and is not typed. Correct.
- **A-5. θ reads R only on bivectors** (lines 119 to 129). `innerOp` sums `inner2 (R (phi i j)) (S (phi i j))`, so `innerOp_congr_skew` ([L24Sharp.lean:36](../lean/HamiltonsCubic/Results/L24Sharp.lean)) needs agreement on skew arguments only, and gives `normSq R = normSq (WC n C)`. `trCube = innerOp (compOp R) R` needs `compOp` to agree on bivectors, R(R X) = W_C(W_C X), which line 121 gets by rewriting R X = W_C X and then R at the bivector W_C X, a bivector by `hWC.1`. `sharpPair = innerOp R (sharpOp R)` needs `sharpOp` to agree on bivectors, which `sharpOp_congr_on_skew` ([FoxBridge.lean:136](../lean/HamiltonsCubic/Results/FoxBridge.lean)) gives from R preserving bivectors and R = W_C there. `theta` and `norm` unfold to `Pcubic` and `normSq`, so θ(R) = θ(W_C). Correct.
- **A-6. IsWeyl (WC n C)** (lines 106 to 117). Bivector preservation and symmetry are R's, transported by `hRC` at bivector arguments (the rewrite `← hRC` turns W_C X back into R X). `bianchi` and `Ric` evaluate the operator only at `phi` arguments, which are skew (`phi_isSkew`), so `simp only [hRC _ (phi_isSkew _ _)]` rewrites R's equations into W_C's. Every clause of `IsWeyl` ([WeylReal.lean:126](../lean/HamiltonsCubic/Base/WeylReal.lean)) is covered. Correct. `coeff_symm` (line 89) reads C's symmetry from the symmetry clause, as `L178_padded` requires.
- **A-7. A witness.** R = W_C with C = fromBlocks diag(2, −1, −1) 0 0 0. By `WC_split` and `WMat_weyl` it is Weyl. It satisfies `hker` since W_C X = Σ C_ij ⟨X, e_i⟩ e_j vanishes when X ⊥ e, and `hran` since ⟨W_C X, Y⟩ = Σ C_ij ⟨X, e_i⟩ ⟨e_j, Y⟩ vanishes when Y ⊥ e. Its coefficient matrix is ⟨W_C e_i, e_j⟩ = Σ C_kl δ_ik δ_lj = C by `eF_inner`, nonzero of rank 3. So the theorem is not vacuous, and this R is the equality case.
- **A-8. What stays owed.** Against the source, the Lean leaves (i) a general four-dimensional V, which needs an O(n) change of frame carrying V to the first four coordinates with θ, `IsWeyl`, support and rank transported along it, and (ii) the equality clause, rigidity up to positive scale and an O(n) frame. Both are named in row 70, the plan's "What it does not give" and diagonal-half.md's note. Accurate, apart from A-rank. *Note, not charged:* row 70 and the plan call the O(n) frame change something "the layer does not build". [Frames.lean:34](../lean/HamiltonsCubic/Base/Frames.lean) does build the action `conjOp` with `IsOrth`. What is missing is the invariance of θ, `IsWeyl`, support and rank under it, which is the precise owed item.
