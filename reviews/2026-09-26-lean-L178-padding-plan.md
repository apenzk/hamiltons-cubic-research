# Lean build plan: L178 on ℝⁿ, the padding step and the self-dual family

**Goal.** [diagonal-half.md](../findings/diagonal-half.md), Lemma L178, bounds $\theta\le\sqrt{3/2}$ for every Weyl tensor on $\mathbb R^n$ supported on $\Lambda^2V$ for a four-dimensional $V$ with $\Lambda^2$-rank at most three. [L178Real.lean](../lean/HamiltonsCubic/Results/L178Real.lean) proves it at $V=\mathbb R^4=\mathbb R^n$ in the pair-basis coordinate model. Step 3 (padding) and the link from that coordinate model to the operator model of [WeylReal.lean](../lean/HamiltonsCubic/Base/WeylReal.lean), where $\theta$ at general $n$ lives, are owed.

**Route.** [CP2Real.lean](../lean/HamiltonsCubic/Results/CP2Real.lean) already computes $\theta$ in the operator model at every $n\ge4$ for the padded $W_{\mathbb{CP}^2}=\sum_a\mu_a\langle\cdot,\omega_a\rangle\omega_a$, through an eigenframe and the frame identities for $\lVert W\rVert^2$, $\mathop{\mathrm{tr}}W^3$ and $\langle W,W^\#\rangle$. Its proofs use the specific $\mu$ only at the end. The build generalises it to every trace-free weight on every orthonormal basis of $\Lambda^\pm\mathbb R^4$, which is the whole rank-at-most-three family by step 1.

## Milestones

1. **Every trace-free weight on the standard self-dual basis.** $W_\mu=\sum_a\mu_a\langle\cdot,\omega_a\rangle\omega_a$ with $\sum\mu=0$ is Weyl on $\mathbb R^n$, with $\lVert W_\mu\rVert^2=\sum\mu^2$, $\mathop{\mathrm{tr}}W_\mu^3=\sum\mu^3$ and $\langle W_\mu,W_\mu^\#\rangle=6\mu_0\mu_1\mu_2$, so $P=9\mu_0\mu_1\mu_2$ and $P^2\le\tfrac32\lVert W_\mu\rVert^6$ with equality exactly at a repeated weight. **Proved** in [Results/L178Pad.lean](../lean/HamiltonsCubic/Results/L178Pad.lean) as `Wm_weyl`, `Wm_values`, `Wm_P`, `Wm_bound`, `Wm_bound_eq_iff` and `Wm_theta_le`.
2. **Every orthonormal basis of $\Lambda^+\mathbb R^4$.** *The construction is generic*: [Results/L178Frame.lean](../lean/HamiltonsCubic/Results/L178Frame.lean) runs milestone 1 on any `SUFrame`, a triple of skew orthogonal $4\times4$ matrices, pairwise orthogonal and anticommuting, with brackets $2s\,\varepsilon$ and a common cyclic sum whose mixed part vanishes, and `stdFrame` is CP2Real's. **Proved** in [Results/L178Rot.lean](../lean/HamiltonsCubic/Results/L178Rot.lean): `rotFrame`, the rotated triple is an `SUFrame` with sign $s\det O$ (the bracket through `cof_eps`, the rows' cross product from $\mathop{\mathrm{adj}}O=\det O\,O^{\mathsf T}$); `sym_spectral` and `WMatF_spectral`, every symmetric trace-free $M$ is $O^{\mathsf T}\mathop{\mathrm{diag}}(\mu)O$ with $W_M$ the rotated diagonal operator; and `WMat_weyl`, `WMat_bound`, `WMat_theta_le`, so $\theta(W_M)\le\sqrt{3/2}$ for every nonzero symmetric trace-free $M$. For $O\in O(3)$ the rotated forms $\omega'_a=\sum_bO_{ab}\omega_b$ are orthonormal with brackets $\sqrt2\det O\,\varepsilon$, and milestone 1 runs on them unchanged. By the spectral theorem on a symmetric trace-free $3\times3$ matrix $M$, this covers $W_M=\sum_{b,c}M_{bc}\langle\cdot,\omega_b\rangle\omega_c$.
3. **The anti-self-dual mirror**, the same with the anti-self-dual forms. **Proved** in [Results/L178Asd.lean](../lean/HamiltonsCubic/Results/L178Asd.lean): `asdFrame`, the anti-self-dual structures are an `SUFrame` with $s=-1$ (every matrix fact by `decide` over $\mathbb Z$), so `WMatA_weyl`, `WMatA_bound` and `WMatA_theta_le` hold on $\Lambda^-$. The generic statements are `WMatF_weyl`, `WMatF_bound` and `WMatF_theta_le` in [Results/L178Rot.lean](../lean/HamiltonsCubic/Results/L178Rot.lean). `J_K_orth` and `J_K_cyc` record $\Lambda^+\perp\Lambda^-$ and the vanishing mixed cyclic sum, for milestone 4.
4. **The reduction.** A Weyl operator on $\mathbb R^n$ supported on $\Lambda^2\mathbb R^4$ with rank at most three is $W_M$ on $\Lambda^+$ or on $\Lambda^-$, by step 1 read in the operator model. **Proved** in [Results/L178Reduce.lean](../lean/HamiltonsCubic/Results/L178Reduce.lean) for $W_C=\sum_{i,j}C_{ij}\langle\cdot,e_i\rangle e_j$ over the basis $e=(\omega^+,\omega^-)$ with $C$ symmetric: `weyl_blocks`, Ricci-flatness at the sixteen embedded pairs and Bianchi at $(0,1,2,3)$ force the mixed block to vanish and both diagonal blocks to be trace-free; `one_block_zero`, $\mathop{\mathrm{rank}}C\le3$ then leaves one block, through a nonzero principal $2\times2$ minor in each (`minor_ne`); and **`L178_padded`**, $\theta(W_C)\le\sqrt{3/2}$. **And for the operator itself** in [Results/L178Support.lean](../lean/HamiltonsCubic/Results/L178Support.lean): `supported_eq_WC`, a Weyl operator killing the bivectors orthogonal to $e$ and taking values in $\mathop{\mathrm{span}}e$ is $W_C$ on bivectors, for $C_{ij}=\langle Re_i,e_j\rangle$; and `L178_supported`, $\theta(R)\le\sqrt{3/2}$ for such a nonzero $R$ whose coefficient matrix has rank at most three, $\theta$ reading $R$ only on bivectors.

**Bound.** Two to three sessions. Milestones 1 and 3 adapt CP2Real's proofs, milestone 2 is one polynomial identity for $\varepsilon$ under a $3\times3$ matrix, and milestone 4 carries the argument.

**What it does not give.** A general four-dimensional $V$ rather than the first four coordinates, which is an $O(n)$ frame change the layer does not build, and the rigidity of the equality case up to frame.
