## Item 1

- **The trace identity holds.** $uv=\tfrac12[u,v]+\tfrac12\{u,v\}$, and $\{u,v\}$ is symmetric for skew $u,v$. For symmetric $S$ and skew $w$, $\mathop{\mathrm{tr}}(Sw)=\mathop{\mathrm{tr}}(w^{\mathsf T}S^{\mathsf T})=-\mathop{\mathrm{tr}}(Sw)$, so it vanishes. Also $\mathop{\mathrm{tr}}([u,v]w)=2\mathop{\mathrm{tr}}(uvw)$, since $\mathop{\mathrm{tr}}(vuw)=\mathop{\mathrm{tr}}(w^{\mathsf T}u^{\mathsf T}v^{\mathsf T})=-\mathop{\mathrm{tr}}(uvw)$.
- **The rows see only brackets.** $b_{ab}(x)=\tfrac12\sum\langle[\psi_\gamma,\psi_\delta],x\rangle^2$ is built from brackets by definition, and the identity shows that writing it through products $\mathop{\mathrm{tr}}(\psi_\gamma\psi_\delta x)$ changes nothing. $\{u,v\}=-2h_{uv}$ matches the note's $h_v=-\tfrac12\{U,V\}$. Correct, and close to a tautology.
- **Correction: identities pairing anticommutators already stand in the same note.** Line 91 holds $\mathcal R_x+\mathcal S_x=D(xx^{\mathsf T})$, so $R_a(x)+S_a(x)=\mathop{\mathrm{tr}}(A_axx^{\mathsf T})$: the per-vector anticommutator mass $S_a(x)$ is fixed by the bracket mass $R_a(x)$, which the rows control, together with the isotropy $A_a$. Line 197's $H\preceq\tfrac{n-2}2I$ from $G\succeq0$ is the orthogonal split of the pair products $uv$ into bracket and anticommutator parts. Line 193's $Q_c=\tfrac12C_c-\mathop{\mathrm{tr}}(A_ch^2)$ pairs $h$ with commutators and isotropy. **Accurate form**: the rows together with isotropy reach anticommutator *masses*. The *$W$-weighted* form $\mathring W(h,h)$ that the Ricci correction needs comes only from criticality evaluated on symmetric directions (line 137).

## Item 2

- **The action is linear.** For a symmetric operator $T=\sum_{k,l}T_{kl}\varphi_k\otimes\varphi_l$ on $\Lambda^2$, set $\mathring T(h,h)=\sum_{k,l}T_{kl}\mathop{\mathrm{tr}}(\varphi_kh\varphi_lh)$. The pairing $\mathop{\mathrm{tr}}(\varphi_kh\varphi_lh)$ is symmetric in $k,l$ by cyclicity, so this is a basis-independent contraction and linear in $T$. With $\mathring P_c=Q_c$ it reproduces the record's $\sum_c\lambda_cQ_c=\mathring W(h,h)$, and $\mathring{\mathrm{Id}}=\lvert h\rvert^2-(\mathop{\mathrm{tr}}h)^2$ fixes the normalisation: $\psi$ orthonormal for $\tfrac12\mathop{\mathrm{tr}}(X^{\mathsf T}Y)$, which is the normalisation of Casimir.phi $=E_{ji}-E_{ij}$ in [Casimir.lean](../lean/HamiltonsCubic/Results/Casimir.lean).
- **Each term is right.** $W^2=\sum\lambda_c^2P_c$ gives $(W^2)^\circ=\sum\lambda_c^2Q_c$. $W^\#=\sum_{a,b}\lambda_a\lambda_bB_{ab}$ by bilinearity of $\#$, which gives $(W^\#)^\circ=\sum\lambda_a\lambda_b\mathring B_{ab}$, and $\mathring B_{ab}$ is as stated from $B_{ab}=\tfrac12\sum[\psi_\gamma,\psi_\delta]\otimes[\psi_\gamma,\psi_\delta]$.
- **The symmetric-family form follows with the stated signs.** At $\lambda=(-a,0,a)$, index $1$ drops out and $\mathring B_{02}=\mathring B_{20}$. So $a^2(\mathring B_{00}+\mathring B_{22}-2\mathring B_{02})=\theta a(Q_2-Q_0)-a^2(Q_0+Q_2)$. Dividing by $\theta a$ gives the displayed identity, with $a/\theta=1/(\theta\sqrt{2p})=1/\kappa$.
- **Consistency with the Ricci term.** In $B_{\mathrm{Ric}}$'s convention $\langle h,\mathring eh\rangle=\tfrac a\theta(Q_0-Q_2)$ (line 191), so $x^{\mathsf T}B_{\mathrm{Ric}}x=\dfrac{Q_0+Q_2+\mathcal B}{2(n-2)\kappa^2}$ with $\mathcal B=(\mathring B_{00}+\mathring B_{22}-2\mathring B_{02})(h_x,h_x)$. The $1/\kappa^2$ is the claimed extra $1/\kappa$.

## Item 3

- **The bound holds.** For any $X$, $\lvert\mathop{\mathrm{tr}}(XhXh)\rvert=\lvert\langle(Xh)^{\mathsf T},Xh\rangle_F\rvert\le\lVert Xh\rVert_F^2=\mathop{\mathrm{tr}}(h^2X^{\mathsf T}X)$ by Cauchy–Schwarz. Summing with the factor $\tfrac12$ gives $\lvert\mathring B_{ab}(h,h)\rvert\le\tfrac12\mathop{\mathrm{tr}}(h^2A_{ab})$. This is the same step as line 191's $\lvert Q_c\rvert\le\mathop{\mathrm{tr}}(h^2A_c)$.
- **The constant holds.** $\sum_{a,b}A_{ab}=\sum_{\gamma,\delta}[\psi_\gamma,\psi_\delta]^{\mathsf T}[\psi_\gamma,\psi_\delta]$ over a full orthonormal basis does not depend on the basis. $O(n)$-conjugation maps one orthonormal basis to another, so the sum is $cI$. casimir_skew_lt gives $\sum_\delta\lVert[\psi_\gamma,\psi_\delta]\rVert_F^2=2(n-2)\lVert\psi_\gamma\rVert_F^2=4(n-2)$. The sum over $\binom n2$ values of $\gamma$ is $2n(n-1)(n-2)=cn$, so $c=2(n-1)(n-2)$. The screen agrees.
- **The conclusion follows.** The bound gives $\lvert\mathcal B\rvert\le\tfrac12\mathop{\mathrm{tr}}\big(h^2(A_{00}+A_{22}+2A_{02})\big)\le(n-1)(n-2)\lvert h\rvert^2$, which is $O(n^2)$. That returns $Q_2-Q_0=O(n)\lvert h\rvert^2$, the same order as the direct bound $\tfrac12\lvert C_2-C_0\rvert\le n\lvert h\rvert^2$. So the prefactor is spent, as claimed.
