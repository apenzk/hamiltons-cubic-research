## 1. L24 at source: derivation or assertion

**It derives.** The Argument, quoted whole from [second-order.md](../findings/second-order.md):

> **Argument.** $P$ is a cubic form, so $\nabla P=3Q(W)$ and the second differential is $6\,\theta\,L_e$; the constraint $\|W\|^2=1$ contributes $-3\theta\,\mathrm{Id}$ through its multiplier, leaving $6\theta(L_e-\tfrac12\mathrm{Id})$ on the tangent space. Two steps the first version left implicit. **$L_e$ is self-adjoint**, so $\lambda_{\max}$ is defined at all: $T(X,Y,Z)=\langle X\circ Y,Z\rangle$ is fully symmetric, the $W^2$ part as the symmetrisation of $\operatorname{tr}(XYZ)$ and the $\#$ part because permuting the arguments permutes the slots of both totally antisymmetric $c$'s, sign $(-1)^2$. **And $W^\perp$ is $L_e$-invariant**, since $L_ee=e$ by criticality and self-adjointness then carries the complement, so the restriction is a genuine operator rather than a compression.

**Both steps the brief asks about are complete.** With $T$ fully symmetric and $P(W)=T(W,W,W)$, the first differential is $3T(W,W,\cdot)=3\langle Q(W),\cdot\rangle$ and the second is $6T(W,\cdot,\cdot)=6\langle L_W\,\cdot,\cdot\rangle$, which is $6\theta L_e$ because $L_W=\theta L_e$ for $e=W/\theta$. The multiplier is not free either: at a critical point of $P$ restricted to the unit sphere, $\nabla P=3Q(W)=3\theta W$, so the Lagrangian is $P-\tfrac{3\theta}2(\lVert W\rVert^2-1)$ and its Hessian on $T_WS=W^\perp$ is $6\theta L_e-3\theta\,\mathrm{Id}$. The $-3\theta$ is $\langle\nabla P,W\rangle$, which criticality plus $\theta=P(W)$ fixes, so no convention is left open. **Nothing about the constant was owed, and W1 stands.**

**Two observations that strengthen W1 rather than weaken it.** The Statement itself already says the ambient and Riemannian forms differ by *exactly* the multiplier term, so the identity is asserted at statement level and argued below it. And the record holds a second, independent derivation of the same constant at [nondegeneracy.md](../findings/nondegeneracy.md), in the r83/r84 Gate 2 passage, which calls $c=6\lambda-3$ *"an identity, since $P$ is cubic so $\mathrm{Hess}\,P=6F_W$ and the constrained Hessian is $6F_W-3\theta$"*, adds that it is *"already written in the project's own code"*, and rules that *"reading it off a table row was fitting something free"*. **That passage is the withdrawal's own finding, recorded earlier against an earlier cycle, and neither the claim nor the withdrawal cites it.**

## 7. Was the numerical confirmation sound

**The arithmetic is exact and the inference from it is void.** Taking [algebra.md](../findings/algebra.md)'s $n=12$ row and reading $\lambda=(c+3)/6$ off it gives $\tfrac12^{(36)}$, $\tfrac25^{(40)}$, $\tfrac15^{(225)}$, $0^{(936)}$, $-\tfrac15^{(400)}$, which is the quoted spectrum character for character. The multiplicities sum to $1637$, which is $\dim\mathcal W_{12}-1$, and the full-space trace is $1+18+16+45+0-80=0$, so the two internal checks pass.

**The check tests no constant, because the pipeline hard-codes the constant being tested.** [hessian_fast.py](../scripts/lib/hessian_fast.py#L151) builds the tangent Hessian as

```
Hess = 6 * M - 3 * lam_P * np.eye(Dt)
```

where its docstring defines $\Phi(H)=\langle W_{\mathrm{unit}},Q(H)\rangle$, so `M` is the compression of $L_W$ to the tangent space, $M=E\,Q_{\mathrm{form}}\,E^{\mathsf T}$ with $\langle X,Q_{\mathrm{form}}Y\rangle=T(W,X,Y)$, and `lam_P` is $\theta$. **Then $\mathrm{Hess}/(6\theta)+\tfrac12=M/\theta=L_W/\theta=L_e$ identically, at any $\theta$ and whatever the true constant is.** The claim *"a wrong constant would rescale all of them"* fails: a wrong constant would sit in the pipeline and in its inversion at once and cancel exactly. The trace detector fails for the same reason, since $\operatorname{tr}(\mathrm{Hess}/(6\theta)+\tfrac12)$ over the tangent space is $\operatorname{tr}(L_e)-1$ by construction, so what $\operatorname{tr}L_e=0$ tests is Fox's identity against the computed multiplication operator.

**On the provenance question the brief asks, the answer is worse than a tie.** [algebra.md](../findings/algebra.md) calls its own subject *"the Hessian spectrum at $W^*$"*, its table column is literally $(6F-3\theta)/\theta$, which is the Hessian in units of $\theta$, and the file says the five families *"were fitted on $n=6\ldots14$ and confirmed on a held-out $n=16$"* with *"Every recorded row is reproduced"* by the closed forms. The rows came first and the forms were fitted to them, and the rows at $n=6$ to $12$ are the full-Hessian numerics that [ATTEMPTS.md](../ATTEMPTS.md) records as [hessian_fast.py](../scripts/lib/hessian_fast.py)'s output. **So the agreement at $S^6\times S^6$ is that pipeline's output at $n=12$ against a table row whose origin is that pipeline's output at $n=12$**, and *"that table came from Casimir arithmetic with no Hessian in its derivation"* is false of the rows.

**What survives, stated at its real strength.** Reviewer 84's master formula $\lambda=1-\mathrm{Cas}_p/(4(p-1))-\mathrm{Cas}_q/(4(q-1))$ at [nondegeneracy.md](../findings/nondegeneracy.md) produces the $L_e$ eigenvalue directly from Casimir data, and it was derived rather than fitted. **Evaluating those families at $k=6$ and comparing them with the numerically computed multiplication operator is a genuine independent check of the block table's values and multiplicities**, which is worth having and is what should be recorded. It says nothing about the Hessian normalisation. **W3 therefore collapses in its stated form and survives in a weaker one**, so the withdrawal under-corrected on the one sentence it chose to keep.

## 8. What would break the withdrawal, and how I looked for it

**The single sentence is the middle clause of L24's Argument**: *"the constraint $\|W\|^2=1$ contributes $-3\theta\,\mathrm{Id}$ through its multiplier"*. Had the Argument asserted the $-3\theta$ with a citation, or reached $6\theta L_e$ and stopped at the ambient second differential, then the conversion from the ambient form to the constrained one would have been unargued, R16's need would have been live, and the withdrawal would be wrong. **A second sentence would do it too**, the earlier criterion paragraph's *"verified through the geodesic second derivative"*, which is a verification rather than a derivation. Had that been the record's only account, the constant would have stood as measured and not derived, which is exactly the status the rubric tells an item to inherit.

**How I looked.** I read the L24 heading whole rather than the criterion paragraph, then every line of [second-order.md](../findings/second-order.md) that cites L24, to see whether any consumer treats the constant as owed, and none does: [L128](../findings/second-order.md)(ii)'s proof and L3's proof both use $6\theta(L_e-\tfrac12\mathrm{Id})$ as given. I then searched the findings folder for an independent derivation and for a contrary one, and found the r83/r84 passage at [nondegeneracy.md](../findings/nondegeneracy.md) deriving the same constant a second way and calling a table-row reading of it *"fitting something free"*. **Nothing on the record says the constant was open, and two places derive it**, so the withdrawal's central claim has two independent supports rather than one.
