# Lean build plan: L178's equality case

**Goal.** [L178](../findings/diagonal-half.md) states $\theta(W)\le\sqrt{3/2}$ on the rank-$\le3$ Weyl tensors supported on a four-dimensional $V$, *with equality exactly when $W$ is $W_{\mathbb{CP}^2}$ up to positive scale and an $O(n)$ frame change*. The layer proves the bound on every $V$ ([FrameTransfer.lean](../lean/HamiltonsCubic/Results/FrameTransfer.lean)'s `L178_frame`) and the equality case in spectral form (`WF_bound_eq_iff`: two equal eigenvalues). This build proves the rigidity: equality forces $W=t\cdot U\cdot W_{\mathbb{CP}^2}$ with $t>0$ and $U$ orthogonal.

**Route.** In the layer's frame the self-dual structures $J_a$ are left multiplication by $i,j,k$ on $\mathbb H=\mathbb R^4$ and the anti-self-dual $K_a$ are right multiplication. So left multiplication $L_q$ by a unit quaternion rotates $\Lambda^+$ by $u\mapsto quq^{-1}$ and fixes $\Lambda^-$ pointwise, and the rotation taking a unit $u$ to $i$ is $q\propto1-iu$. Equality puts the spectrum at $t(2,-1,-1)$, so $W=t(3\,\omega\otimes\omega-P_{\Lambda^+})$ with $\omega$ the top eigenform, and $L_q$ carries $\omega$ to $J_0$ while fixing $P_{\Lambda^+}$. The anti-self-dual case goes through the reflection $\mathrm{diag}(1,1,1,-1)$, which carries $K_a$ into $\Lambda^+$.

## Milestones

1. **Quaternion rotations of $\Lambda^+$.** $L_q$ as a $4\times4$ matrix, $L_qL_q^{\mathsf T}=\lvert q\rvert^2$, $L_qJ_bL_q^{\mathsf T}=\sum_cR(q)_{bc}J_c$ (the orthogonality of $R(q)$ is derived where it is used, in milestone 2's `proj_inv`, from conjugation preserving the inner product), $L_qK_a=K_aL_q$, and for every unit $v\in\mathbb R^3$ an orthogonal $U$ with $U(\sum_bv_bJ_b)U^{\mathsf T}=J_0$. **Proved** in [Results/L178Quat.lean](../lean/HamiltonsCubic/Results/L178Quat.lean): `Lq_mul_transpose`, `Lq_comm_K`, `Lq_conj_J`, `Lq_to_J0` (the exact identity $L_puL_p^{\mathsf T}=(1-s)u+2s(1+v_0)J_0$ at $p=(1+v_0,0,v_2,-v_1)$, $s=\lvert v\rvert^2$) and `rot_to_J0`, 8/8 on the standard three.
2. **The equality operator.** At a spectrum $t(2,-1,-1)$, `WF` of a rotated frame is $t(3\,\omega_a\otimes\omega_a-P)$ with $P=\sum_b\omega_b\otimes\omega_b$ the frame-independent projection onto $\Lambda^+$. **Proved** in [Results/L178Rigid.lean](../lean/HamiltonsCubic/Results/L178Rigid.lean): `Wtrip`, `conjOp_Wtrip`, `proj_inv` (an orthonormal triple's coefficient matrix has orthonormal rows, hence columns) and `Wtrip_top`.
3. **Padding.** $U\oplus I_{n-4}$ is orthogonal and conjugates `omF` blockwise. **Proved** there: `Upad`, `Upad_mul`, `Upad_orth`, `Upad_conj`.
4. **Assembly.** Equality in `L178_padded` gives $\exists\,U,\ t>0$ with $U\cdot W=t\,W_c$, and `FrameTransfer` carries it to every four-dimensional $V$. **Proved** there: `WMatF_spectral` reduces a general coefficient block to a rotated frame operator, `WF_eq_top` takes the sign $t>0$ from $\theta=+\sqrt{3/2}$, `rigid_sd` and `rigid_rot_asd` handle the two blocks, and `L178_padded_eq_iff`, `L178_supported_eq_iff` and `L178_frame_eq_iff` state the equality clause as an equivalence, 30/30 on the standard three.

**Bound.** Two sessions, closed in one. Milestone 1 is polynomial identities in $q$ checked entrywise, and the rest reuses `SUFrame`, `WF` and `conjOp`.
