## Claim 1, the fence post holds, on a repaired derivation

**What the paper actually says.** Definition 1.4 reads: a freely-described element of a consistent sequence $\{(V_n,\varphi_n)\}$ is a sequence $\{v_n\in V_n^{G_n}\}$ of invariants with $\varphi_n^*(v_{n+1})=v_n$. Since $\varphi_n$ is an isometric embedding (Definition 1.3(b)), $\varphi_n^*$ is the *orthogonal projection* $P_{V_n}$, so free description is **restriction compatibility**, and nothing else. Their own worked instance is the identity: "extracting the top left $n\times n$ submatrix of $I_{n+1}$ yields $I_n$". For spaces of degree-$d$ polynomials on $V_n$ the orthogonal projection onto the sub-block is exactly restriction of the function to $V_n\subseteq V_{n+1}$, so free description of a polynomial sequence means $p_{n+1}\vert_{V_n}=p_n$.

**Step (i), "a freely described element is pinned by equivariance from $n_0$" is the right conclusion attributed to the wrong mechanism.** Equivariance pins nothing. The pinning is Proposition 2.9: for $U$ a $V$-module presented in degree $k$, $P_{U_n}\colon U_{n+1}^{G_{n+1}}\to U_n^{G_n}$ is an *isomorphism* for all $n\ge k$; Theorem 3.5 cites exactly this for "there is a unique extension of $u_{n_0}$ to a freely-described element $\{u_n\}$", under its standing hypothesis $n_0\ge k$. So uniqueness is real, and "carries the scalar coefficient unchanged" follows: if $A$, $B$, $C$ are each freely described, then any fixed-coefficient combination is restriction-compatible, and by the isomorphism it is *the* extension. The step survives; its stated reason does not, and the correction matters because it exposes the load-bearing hypothesis $n_0\ge k$ that the file never mentions.

**Step (ii), $A$, $B$, $C$ are freely described, and the file is right for a reason it does not state.** Checked in T1/T2 of the script. A random $W\in\mathrm{Weyl}_n$ ($n=5,6$) zero-padded to $m=n+1,n+2$ stays trace-free ($\lvert\mathrm{Ric}\rvert_{\max}\le 9\times10^{-16}$), so $\mathrm{Weyl}_n\subset\mathrm{Weyl}_{n+1}$ really is a consistent sequence; and $A=\lvert W\rvert^6$, $B=\langle Q(W),W\rangle^2$, $C=\lvert W\rvert^2\lvert Q(W)\rvert^2$ all reproduce to $\le 8\times10^{-16}$ relative error. The mechanism is that $Q=W^2+W^\#$ is block-local, which the record already had as "$\theta$ is padding-invariant".

**The dependency the file misses.** This works only for the *unprojected* $Q$, which is what [curvature_operator.py](../scripts/curvature_operator.py) implements. Planting the violation, recompute with $Q$ replaced by its Weyl projection, whose projector carries $1/n$ and $1/(n-2)$ factors, makes the check fire at once: $C$ moves by $2.7\times10^{-2}$ from $n=5$ to $m=6$ and $5.4\times10^{-2}$ to $m=7$, while $B$ stays put to $6\times10^{-16}$. So $B$ is robust to the choice and $C$ is not, and the file's blanket "each defined by the same contraction at every $n$, so each is freely described" is true only under a convention it never fixes.

**Step (iii), the conclusion is right but the file's derivation of it has a hole, which is closable.** The file argues that the free extension asserts $\theta\le\Theta_{n_0}$ and that this is false at $n>n_0$. As written that skips the multiplier: the free extension is $\Theta_{n_0}^2A-B+\mu(C-B)$, and $C-B=\lvert W\wedge Q(W)\rvert^2\ge0$ by Cauchy–Schwarz, so a priori a large enough $\mu$ could compensate the deficit and leave the extension nonnegative after all. The hole closes because $C-B$ vanishes *exactly* at the witness. T3 confirms it: at the balanced sphere product of dimension $n$, normalised to $\lvert W\rvert=1$, $Q(W)=\theta W$ to $\le1.4\times10^{-16}$ with $\theta=\Theta_n$ to twelve digits, and $C-B\le7\times10^{-16}$, at $n=6,8,10,12$. So the free extension evaluates there to $\Theta_{n_0}^2-\Theta_n^2<0$ **for every $\mu$**, which is the strongest form of the objection, since $\mu$ is precisely the free variable $x\in V_n$ of (ConicSeq) and is allowed to be chosen afresh at each $n$. The description set is not merely wrong at $n>n_0$; it is **empty**. [plan.md](../plan.md) line 382 already records $W\wedge Q(W)=0$ at the extremiser, so the file had the missing ingredient in hand and did not use it.

**A cleaner statement of the same obstruction, which the file could have made.** Given step (ii), $G_{\mu,n+1}\vert_{\mathrm{Weyl}_n}=G_{\mu,n}+(\Theta_{n+1}^2-\Theta_n^2)A\ne G_{\mu,n}$, so $\{G_{\mu,n}\}$ **fails Definition 1.4 outright**. No witness and no monotonicity direction is needed for that half; the witness is needed only for the sharper conclusion that the extension is *false*, not merely *different*.

**Two smaller defects.** The file uses $\Theta_n^2=2(n-1)(n-2)/n^2$ throughout, but [problem.md](../findings/problem.md) records the constant as parity-dependent, with $2(n-1)(n-3)/((n+1)(n-2))$ at odd $n$, and a consistent sequence steps through every $n$, odd ones included. T4 checks the parity-correct sequence and finds it strictly increasing for all $n\in[4,400)$, so the file's monotonicity conclusion survives on the right formula; the formula it prints is wrong at odd $n$. Second, the file's closing loop `assert 3*n - 4 > 0` for `n in range(2, 500)` is a tautology about integers and checks nothing about $\Theta_n$; the derivative claim it advertises is correct but unverified by that line.

**What the fence post does not close.** $2-\Theta_n^2>0$ at every $n$ (T8: $17/36$, $34/77$, $20/49$, $35/144$, $149/2500$ at $n=12,13,14,24,100$), so $2A-B+\mu(C-B)$ has constant coefficients, is freely described, and is nonnegative everywhere. Theorem 3.5 is blocked for the **sharp** constant, not for every use, the honest scope of the closure.

## Claim 2, the repair is empty, but not for the brief's reason

**The brief's proposed reason is refuted.** $\Theta_n^2$ does carry $n^2$ in a denominator, and division is indeed not a ring operation, but nonnegativity and SOS-ness are invariant under multiplication by the positive scalar $n^2$, so the denominator is clearable by rescaling the target:
$$n^2G_{\mu,n} \;=\; 2(n-1)(n-2)\,A\;-\;n^2B\;+\;\mu\,n^2(C-B),$$
every coefficient now a **polynomial** in $n$ (I28 checks $n^2\Theta_n^2=2(n-1)(n-2)$ exactly at $n=12,24$; at odd $n$ one clears $(n+1)(n-2)$ instead). So "the denominator cannot be cleared" is false, and the repair cannot be closed that way.

**The repair still fails, for a reason that is strictly stronger.** Free description is a condition on the sequence of **values under restriction**, not on the existence of a uniform formula. The identity $I_n$ is freely described ($P_{V_n}(I_{n+1})=I_n$, verified in T5 at $n=5,6,7,11,12$) but its **trace is not**: $\mathrm{tr}(I_{n+1})=n+1$ while $\mathrm{tr}(P_{V_n}(I_{n+1}))=\mathrm{tr}(I_n)=n$. The trace therefore fails to be a morphism of consistent sequences into the trivial sequence $\mathbb R$, and $\{\delta^a_a\}=\{n\}$ is not a freely-described scalar. Writing $n$ as a contraction changes the notation and not the sequence: $\delta^a_a$ still evaluates to $n+1$ in dimension $n+1$, and its restriction to the $n$-block is still $n+1$, not $n$.

**Why that is the stronger reason, and which one the record should carry.** The denominator argument, if it worked, would leave "clear the denominator and try again" open, and I28 shows that move succeeds algebraically, so the record would have been left with a false sense of an open repair. The trace argument closes the whole class at once: **any** $n$-dependent coefficient breaks Definition 1.4, polynomial or rational, however it is written. The file's sentence "the free extension is the target by construction" conflates the invariant-theoretic notion of a uniform $O(n)$ contraction formula with Levin–Chandrasekaran's inverse-limit notion, and those two agree only when the formula contains no closed $\delta$ loop.

**The escape hatches are closed too.** $\theta$ is degree-$0$ homogeneous, so no rescaling of $W$ moves the constant; and rescaling the inner product on $\mathrm{Weyl}_n$ by $\lambda_n$ is forbidden by Definition 1.3(b), which requires $\varphi_n$ to be an isometry and hence $\lambda_n$ constant.

## What the record should say

1. The route is closed **for the sharp constant**, and the reason is that $\{G_{\mu,n}\}$ fails Definition 1.4 because $\Theta_n^2$ varies with $n$, with the balanced sphere product at dimension $n$ as the witness that the free extension is false for every $\mu$, since $C-B$ vanishes there.
2. "Pinned by equivariance" should read "pinned by Proposition 2.9, valid for $n_0\ge k$", which surfaces the presentation-degree hypothesis the route needs anyway.
3. Free description of $C$ holds for the unprojected $Q=W^2+W^\#$ and fails for the Weyl-projected one; the convention must be stated.
4. The $\delta^a_a$ repair is dead because the trace is not compatible with the projections, not because of the denominator, and the denominator is in fact clearable.
5. $\Theta_n^2$ is parity-dependent; the file's formula is the even branch only.
