**One-line summary: CONFIRMED WITH GAP.** `Bmat (ringW R)` is $\mathring W$ on $S^2$ in an orthonormal basis, `sum_omega_sq` and `L50_spectral` are L50's $\sum\omega^2=3$ and bi-spectral display with $\omega$ on $S^2$, a correct generalisation that agrees with the record's $S^2_0$ spectrum in power sums at Ricci-flat $R$, on the standard three axioms, and the gaps are an unproved (true) orthonormality claim, an over-general `Bmat_comp` header line, and two semicolon splices.

## 1. The basis and the matrix

`symB a b` is $\tfrac12(E_{ab}+E_{ba})$ (SecondKind.lean line 155) and `Pairs n` is $\{(a,b):a<b\}$ (L54Schur.lean line 28). For $a<b$, `e (inl (a,b)) = √2 · symB a b` $=(E_{ab}+E_{ba})/\sqrt2$, with Frobenius norm squared $2\cdot\tfrac12=1$. For each $a$, `e (inr a) = symB a a` $=E_{aa}$. Two off-diagonal elements with distinct pairs have disjoint supports, an off-diagonal element and a diagonal one are disjoint, and two diagonal ones with $a\ne b$ are disjoint. So the family is orthonormal and has $\binom n2+n=\dim S^2$ members. That is the whole argument, and it is not a Lean lemma. The header's "the orthonormal basis" and the row's "in the orthonormal basis" are true statements that the file does not prove.

`expand_sym` proves $h=\sum_u\langle h,e_u\rangle e_u$ for symmetric $h$, which is spanning. The theorems downstream do not need orthonormality at all: `Bmat_comp` rewrites `L' (e u)` with `expand_sym` and linearity of `ringW`, so $\mathrm{Bmat}(\mathring W\circ L')=\mathrm{Bmat}(L')\,\mathrm{Bmat}(\mathring W)$, and `trace_Bmat` identifies the trace of `Bmat L` with `trS2 L` by `frob_symB` and `sum_pairs_diag`. With `Bmat_sq` and `Bmat_cube` these make $\mathop{\mathrm{tr}}\mathrm{Bmat}^k=\mathop{\mathrm{tr}}_{S^2}\mathring W^k$ at $k=2,3$. Orthonormality is what lets one call the eigenvalues of `Bmat` "the spectrum of $\mathring W$ on $S^2$", and 1a supplies it by hand.

`Bmat L u v = ⟨L e_u, e_v⟩` is the $(v,u)$ coefficient of $Le_u$, the transpose of the conventional matrix. For $\mathring W$, self-adjoint on all matrices by `ringW_selfAdjoint`, the two coincide, and `Bmat_isHermitian` proves the symmetry directly. The reversed product order in `Bmat_comp` is the transpose convention and is internally consistent.

The header describes `Bmat_comp` as holding "for linear `L` and `L'` mapping symmetric to symmetric". The theorem is stated with `L = ringW R` fixed and `L'` arbitrary save that each `L' (e u)` is symmetric. The docstring on the theorem says this correctly. The header line should match it.

## 2. The spectrum on $S^2$ against the record's on $S^2_0$

L50 defines $\omega$ as the spectrum of $\mathring W$ on trace-free symmetric tensors, for $W\in\mathrm{Weyl}_n$. At a Weyl, hence Ricci-flat, operator, `ringW_one` gives $\mathring Wg=-\mathrm{Ric}=0$. Since $\mathring W$ is self-adjoint on $S^2$, $g^\perp=S^2_0$ is invariant, which `trace_ringW` ($\mathop{\mathrm{tr}}\mathring Wh=-\langle\mathrm{Ric},h\rangle=0$) also shows directly. So $\mathring W|_{S^2}=\mathring W|_{S^2_0}\oplus0_{\mathbb Rg}$, the $S^2$ spectrum is the record's $\omega$ with one $0$ appended, and every power sum $p_k$ with $k\ge1$ agrees. The header and the row say exactly this.

`L50_spectral` carries no Ricci-flatness hypothesis. Its content is `L50_bispectral` ($P=\tfrac32\mathop{\mathrm{tr}}M^3-\tfrac12\mathop{\mathrm{tr}}_{S^2}\mathring W^3$, r1403 and r1404), which holds for every linear, bivector-preserving, self-adjoint operator satisfying first Bianchi, plus `sum_mu_cube` and `sum_omega_cube`. So the theorem is true on that whole class with $\omega$ the $S^2$ spectrum. Off the Ricci-flat locus $g$ need not be an eigenvector and the $S^2$ spectrum need not contain $0$. At $R=\mathrm{Id}$, `ringW_id` gives $\mathring Wh=h^{\mathsf T}-(\mathop{\mathrm{tr}}h)g$, so $\mathring Wg=(1-n)g$ and the spectrum is $1$ on $S^2_0$ and $1-n$ on $g$. Reading $\omega$ on $S^2$ is therefore the correct general statement, and every text in the commit names $S^2$. It is a generalisation of L50, not a misstatement of it, and it specialises to the record's display at Weyl operators through the passage above.

The L50 row's Owed clause lists that passage as prose, scoped to "a Ricci-flat $R$", citing `ringW_one` and `trace_ringW`. That is accurate. What a Lean statement of it would need is the orthogonal splitting $S^2=S^2_0\oplus\mathbb Rg$ at the matrix level, which no file builds.

## 3. $\sum\omega^2$

`sum_omega_sq` rewrites $\sum\omega^2$ as $\mathop{\mathrm{tr}}\mathrm{Bmat}^2$, then as `trS2` of $\mathring W^2$ by `Bmat_sq` and `trace_Bmat`, and closes with `L50_norm`, $\mathop{\mathrm{tr}}_{S^2}\mathring W^2=3\lVert R\rVert^2$. With `normSq R` equal to $\lVert M\rVert_F^2$ (r1415), this is $\sum\omega^2=3\lVert M\rVert_F^2$, the record's $\sum\omega^2=3$ at $\lVert M\rVert_F=1$. The record adds that the ratio is $3$ "at every $W$ and every $n$", and the theorem needs only first Bianchi, matching that.
