# Brendle, Ricci flow with surgery on manifolds with positive isotropic curvature

- **Link**: [arXiv:1711.05167](https://arxiv.org/abs/1711.05167)
- **Identifier**: arXiv:1711.05167, Annals of Mathematics 190 (2019).
- **Author**: Simon Brendle.
- **Retrieved**: 2026-09-10, as [brendle-1711.05167.pdf](https://arxiv.org/abs/1711.05167), extracted to [brendle-1711.05167.txt](https://arxiv.org/abs/1711.05167).
- **Consulted for**: whether the dimension threshold $n\ge12$ in the PIC surgery theorem comes from a cubic estimate on the Weyl part of Hamilton's ODE, which would be this programme's object, found during the [2026-09-10b sweep](../../reviews/2026-09-10b-LIT-step8-sweep.md).
- **Statements that matter**: the transformation $\ell_{a,b}$ and its effect on the three components of a curvature operator (p. 7), Definition 3.1 of the cone family, Theorem 3.2, and **Lemma 3.3**, the one place $n\ge12$ is used.
- **Verdict**: **the threshold is Ricci bookkeeping and the Weyl tensor never enters.** $\ell_{a,b}$ rescales scalar curvature and trace-free Ricci and leaves the Weyl tensor unchanged, so every pinching-cone argument built on it is blind to the Weyl part by construction, and Lemma 3.3 is an elementary polynomial inequality in the cone parameters alone. *A fence post against chasing the $n\ge12$ coincidence again.*
