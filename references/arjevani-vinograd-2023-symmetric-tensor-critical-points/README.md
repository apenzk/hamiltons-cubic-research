# Arjevani–Vinograd 2023, Symmetry & critical points for symmetric tensor decomposition problems

- **Link**: [arXiv:2306.07886](https://arxiv.org/abs/2306.07886)
- **Identifier**: arXiv:2306.07886 [math.OC], v5 August 2025
- **Consulted for**: the step 8 sweep, whether anyone has classified symmetry-breaking critical points of an invariant functional on a sphere-like constraint set and controlled their Hessian spectra analytically, which is V2's exact situation (graded non-equivariant critical points sitting below their equivariant parents).
- **Verdict**: **the working model for symmetry-broken critical families.** For the $S_d\times S_d$-invariant tensor-decomposition loss they construct infinite families of critical points with *proper* isotropy subgroups, given as Puiseux series in the dimension $d$, and tabulate every Hessian spectrum **per irreducible representation** of the isotropy group, the multiplicities in the isotypic decomposition are dimension-independent, so the number of distinct eigenvalues is fixed as $d$ grows. Their observed law "the larger the critical value, the larger the Morse index" is the qualitative shape of what Conjecture A needs above $\theta_0$.
