**Why it does not reach the record's question.** The record's stratum is at $n\ge12$ and the lemma is an $S^2$ statement resting on four points in general position. What it exhibits is the SHAPE of an exclusion: a cubic's critical points cannot have an arbitrary Gramian, so a stratum whose points would need a forbidden Gramian is empty. No higher-dimensional version is stated in the paper.
