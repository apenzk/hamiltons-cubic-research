# Ivanova, Hildebrand. Extremal cubics on the circle and the 2-sphere

- arXiv [2110.07946](https://arxiv.org/abs/2110.07946), retrieved 2026-09-21, stored as `paper.pdf`, extracted to `paper.txt`.
- **Consulted for**: the step 9 sweep's target 2, excluding a stratum of critical points rather than bounding on it.
- **What matters**: Lemma 4.9, a constraint on the Gramian of critical points of a homogeneous cubic on $S^2$.
- **Verdict**: DIMENSION $n=2,3$ ONLY, by the paper's first sentence, and the lemma needs four affinely independent critical points on $S^2$. **What transfers is the shape**, that critical points of a cubic on a sphere have a constrained Gramian, which is an exclusion-type statement. **The result itself does not reach $n\ge12$.**
