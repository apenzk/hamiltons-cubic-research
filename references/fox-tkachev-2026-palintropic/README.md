## Consulted for

**Whether the Weyl algebra satisfies the $(2,3)$-palintropic identity $(x^3)^2=(x^2)^3$**, a weakening of mediality, which [I88](../../plan.md) showed the Weyl algebra lacks. If it did, every idempotent would carry explicit fusion rules for its Peirce spaces at $\lambda\ne\tfrac12$ and $L(c)$ would be an algebra homomorphism at every nonsingular idempotent, structure the record has never had at a critical point.

## Verdict

**It does not.** *The relative defect $|(x^3)^2-(x^2)^3|/|(x^3)^2|$ at random unit Weyl tensors is $0.49$ at $n=5$ and $0.68$ at $n=6$, order one, against a mediator defect of $1.6$ for comparison.* **So none of the paper's fusion rules apply to the Weyl algebra**, and Proposition 1.4's list of $(2,3)$-palintropic classes, nilpotent, power-associative, Jordan, medial, J-medial, and those satisfying (12), does not reach it.
