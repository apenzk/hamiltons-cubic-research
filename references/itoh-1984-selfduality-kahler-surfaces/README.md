# Itoh, *Self-duality of Kähler surfaces* (1984)

- **Source.** Mitsuhiro Itoh, *Self-duality of Kähler surfaces*, Compositio Mathematica **51** (1984), no. 2, 265–273. [Numdam item](http://www.numdam.org/item?id=CM_1984__51_2_265_0), PDF at [www.numdam.org/article/CM_1984__51_2_265_0.pdf](https://www.numdam.org/article/CM_1984__51_2_265_0.pdf).
- **Files.** [CM_1984__51_2_265_0.pdf](http://www.numdam.org/item?id=CM_1984__51_2_265_0); text extraction [CM_1984__51_2_265_0.txt](http://www.numdam.org/item?id=CM_1984__51_2_265_0) (`pdftotext -layout`).
- **Consulted for.** L176 Corollary 2, item 7 of [BRIEF-g2-l176.md](../../reviews/BRIEF-g2-l176.md): a printed statement that $\mathbb{CP}^2$ with the Fubini–Study metric is self-dual, i.e. $W^-=0$.
- **Verdict.** Carries $W^-=0$ for $\mathbb{CP}^2$ in print, twice (the constant-holomorphic-curvature sentence on p. 266 and Theorem C's list). Carries $W^+\ne0$ via Theorem 2. Does **not** carry the three eigenvalues of $W^+$ in a form the extraction can read, that half of item 7 is settled instead by a closed-form derivation from [bourguignon-karcher-1978-pinching](../bourguignon-karcher-1978-pinching/notes.md) §5.1.
