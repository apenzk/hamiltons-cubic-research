# Notes on Aazami, Higher self-duality (arXiv:2512.19050v2)

**Read for one question only: does it characterise pure curvature operators? It does not.** No transcription of numbered statements is given, because none bears on this record.

## What the paper does

Defines higher self-duality on oriented Riemannian $4n$-manifolds using the totally trace-free component of Thorpe's middle curvature operator $\hat R_{2n}:\Lambda^{2n}\to\Lambda^{2n}$. Higher self-dual metrics are absolute minimisers of a conformal energy whose Bach tensor is symmetric, trace-free and divergence-free, which resolves a question of Kastor. The self-dual and anti-self-dual blocks give a signed-square formula for the top Pontryagin density. Compact self-dual examples include Fubini–Study, and Einstein metrics need not be higher Bach-flat.

## The dimension-four background it recalls

On an oriented Riemannian $4$-manifold the Hodge star splits $\Lambda^2=\Lambda^2_+\oplus\Lambda^2_-$, the Weyl operator $\hat W$ preserves the splitting, and $p_1(M)=\tfrac1{2}\int(\lvert\hat W^+\rvert^2-\lvert\hat W^-\rvert^2)\,dV_g$. *The record's own self-dual and anti-self-dual conventions are at [SelfDual.lean](../../lean/HamiltonsCubic/Base/SelfDual.lean) and are unaffected by anything here.*
