## Verdict

**It is not about purity and the sweep misattributed it.** The subject is Thorpe's middle curvature operator $\hat R_{2n}:\Lambda^{2n}\to\Lambda^{2n}$, used to define higher self-duality on oriented Riemannian $4n$-manifolds, with results on conformal energy minimisation, the Bach tensor, and a signed-square formula for the top Pontryagin density. **The word *pure* does not occur in the text.** *An earlier version appears to have carried the title "On the curvature operator in dimensions $4n$", which is how a search index still lists it, and a search summary attributed purity content to it that the paper does not contain.*

**What is worth noting anyway.** The abstract records the Fubini–Study metric as a compact self-dual example. *That is adjacent to, and independent of, the record's own fact that $W_{\mathbb{CP}^2}$ is Plücker-obstructed and so not coordinate-diagonal, since the literature separately holds Fubini–Study to be NOT pure.* **Nothing here is used by the record.**
