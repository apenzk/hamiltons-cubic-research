# Zaslavsky, *Matrices in the Theory of Signed Simple Graphs* (arXiv:1303.3083)

- **Source.** Thomas Zaslavsky, arXiv:[1303.3083](https://arxiv.org/abs/1303.3083) [math.CO]; expanded from *Advances in Discrete Mathematics and Applications: Mysore, 2008* (Ramanujan Math. Soc., 2010), 207–229. PDF [zas-1303.3083.pdf](https://arxiv.org/abs/1303.3083), text [zas-1303.3083.txt](https://arxiv.org/abs/1303.3083) (`pdftotext -layout`).
- **Consulted for.** One fact only: the rank of the **unsigned, unoriented** $(0,1)$ incidence matrix $N$ of a graph, $\operatorname{rank}N=\lvert V\rvert-b$ with $b$ the number of bipartite components. This is L177's rank identity, and Gate 2 brief [BRIEF-g2-l177-l178.md](../../reviews/BRIEF-g2-l177-l178.md) item 2 asks for it to be read at a source rather than recalled.
- **Verdict.** **Confirms the identity, and supplies the two side conditions the record leaves unstated**: the field must not have characteristic 2, and the graph must be loopless. See [notes.md](notes.md).
