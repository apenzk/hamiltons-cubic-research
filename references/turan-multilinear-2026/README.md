## Consulted for

Whether Lemma 36, the maximal dimension of a Cartan-3-form isotropic subspace of $\mathfrak{so}(n)$ is $\lfloor n^2/4\rfloor$, is known. **Found late**: the cap was derived and proved before this literature was searched, because the search used Lie-theoretic vocabulary rather than the vocabulary of the answer.

## Verdict

**The right field, and it does not contain our statement.** These study the *isotropy index* $\alpha_\Lambda(F,n,d,m)$, the **minimum** over all alternating $d$-forms of the largest totally-isotropic subspace, a universal guarantee. Lemma 36 is the **maximum** for one distinguished form. Their results bound ours from below and cannot give its value.

Chen's Theorem 1.2(b) settles $m=1$ at every order: $\lceil n/2\rceil$ for $d=2$; $4$ at $(d,n)=(3,7)$; $n-2$ when $d=n-2$ is even; and $\max\{s:\ s(n-s)\ge\binom sd\}$ otherwise. The $d=2$ ancestor is Buhler–Gupta–Harris, $\alpha_\Lambda(F,n,2,m)=\lfloor(2n+m)/(m+2)\rfloor$, earlier over finite fields by Ol'šanskiĭ.

**Placement**: Lemma 36 is a Turán-type theorem for a single distinguished form, and its coordinate case is Mantel's theorem. The lineage to cite is Ol'šanskiĭ, Buhler–Gupta–Harris, Feldman–Propp, Tevelev, Qiao, Chen.
