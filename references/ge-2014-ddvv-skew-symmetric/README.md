## Consulted for

**The sharp bound on a family of commutators in $\mathfrak{so}(n)$, with its equality case.** Cited by the outside C-sector report in an outside report archive (unpublished, held in the private record), whose commutator-energy decomposition rests on a bound of this shape. $\mathrm{Rm}^\#$ is built from the structure constants of $\mathfrak{so}(n)$, so a sharp inequality on $\sum\lVert[B_r,B_s]\rVert^2$ is a statement about the same object as $\langle W^\#,W\rangle$.

## Statements that matter

- **Theorem 1.1.** For real skew-symmetric $n\times n$ matrices $B_1,\dots,B_m$,
  $$\sum_{r,s=1}^m\lVert[B_r,B_s]\rVert^2\le c_n\Big(\sum_{r=1}^m\lVert B_r\rVert^2\Big)^2,\qquad c_3=\tfrac13,\quad c_n=\tfrac23\ (n\ge4),$$
  invariant under the $O(n)\times O(m)$ action $(P,R)\cdot(B_r)=(PB_rP^t)\cdot R$.
- **The equality case, and it is the whole value of the theorem.** Equality forces all but three of the $B_r$ to vanish, and at $n\ge4$ the survivors to be $\mathrm{diag}(D_i,0)$ with the $D_i$ supported on a common **four-dimensional** block. At $n=3$ they are the three coordinate rotations scaled equally.
- **Theorem 1.2** transports this to a Simons-type inequality for Riemannian submersions with totally geodesic fibres, which this project does not use.

## Verdict

**A candidate input, unconsumed, and the equality case is why it is worth holding.** The record's [L31](../../results.md) caps a single bracket, $\lVert[X,Y]\rVert^2\le2$ for orthonormal $X,Y$, with the equality case forcing rank four and anticommutation. **Theorem 1.1 is the $m$-matrix form of the same phenomenon with a sharp constant**, and its equality case lands on a four-dimensional block, which is where the record's $\sqrt{3/2}$ branch and the outside archive's half-Weyl branch both live. Whether the constant $\tfrac23$ is the one a Weyl tensor sees is not checked here: the $B_r$ are unconstrained skew matrices, while a Weyl tensor's blocks carry Bianchi and Ricci-flatness, so **the inequality is available but its sharpness on $\mathcal W_n$ is open**.
