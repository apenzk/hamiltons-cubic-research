# Schwahn 2020, Stability of Einstein metrics on symmetric spaces of compact type

- **Link**: [arXiv:2012.10524](https://arxiv.org/abs/2012.10524)
- **Identifier**: arXiv:2012.10524 [math.DG], published J. Geom. Anal.
- **Consulted for**: the step 8 sweep after the shared-mechanism stall on V3, the record needed a worked template for deciding a second-variation sign at a symmetric critical point *without naming a test direction*, because six routes died on exactly that: every direction the programme could name was equivariant, and equivariant directions cannot see the violation.
- **Verdict**: **the template exists and is this paper's Section 3–4.** The Lichnerowicz Laplacian commutes with the isometry group, so Peter–Weyl splits the tensor bundle into isotypes $V_\gamma\otimes\mathrm{Hom}_K(V_\gamma,V)$ and Schur reduces $\Delta_L$ on each isotype to a finite matrix $L_\gamma$; in the symmetric case $\Delta_L=\mathrm{Cas}^G$ and Freudenthal's formula $\mathrm{Cas}_\gamma=\langle\gamma,\gamma+2\delta\rangle$ makes the whole spectrum highest-weight arithmetic. Stability of $\mathrm{SU}(n)$ and $E_6/F_4$ is decided by listing the finitely many $\gamma$ with $\mathrm{Hom}_K(V_\gamma,\mathrm{Sym}^2_0\mathfrak m)\neq0$ and comparing Casimirs to the threshold $2E$. The structural match to this programme is exact: their critical threshold $2E$ with null space = infinitesimal Einstein deformations parallels our threshold $\tfrac12$ with the orbit tangent as the exact $\tfrac12$-eigenspace of $L_e$ (Lemma 24 side).
