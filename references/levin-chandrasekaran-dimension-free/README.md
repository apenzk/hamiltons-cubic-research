# Levin & Chandrasekaran, Dimension-free descriptions of convex sets

- **Link**: [arXiv:2307.04230](https://arxiv.org/abs/2307.04230)
- **Identifier**: arXiv:2307.04230v3 [math.OC], 23 Oct 2025. 56 pages.
- **Retrieved**: 2026-08-30 (file date of [paper.pdf](https://arxiv.org/abs/2307.04230)). Text extraction is in [txt.txt](https://arxiv.org/abs/2307.04230).
- **Consulted for**: the programme's open crux after the stability theorem, a certificate at $n=12$ settles Conjecture A at $n=12$ and nothing carries it upward. This paper's stated contributions include extendability of a single set in a given dimension to a freely-described sequence.
- **Verdict**: **the right machinery, with two hypotheses that are not free and a threshold that looks larger than $12$.** Theorem 3.5 parametrises freely-described and compatible sequences: hypotheses are checkable at a single $n_0\ge k$ (the presentation degree) and yield unique extensions to every $n$, conditions in one dimension, conclusions in all of them. $O(n)$ is listed among their consistent sequences, so the setting is covered. **Two obstructions found on the second reading.** (1) The packaged SOS corollary, Theorem 1.11, is stated only for the finite classical Weyl groups $\{S_n\},\{B_n\},\{D_n\}$; the continuous case would have to be worked from the general Theorem 3.5. (2) The gate is $n\ge d+k$, and building $\mathcal W_n$ from $\mathbb R^n$ through $\Lambda^2\to\mathrm{Sym}^2(\Lambda^2)\to$ Bianchi $\to$ traceless puts $\mathrm{Sym}^3(\mathcal W_n)$ at generation degree about 12, landing the gate near $n\ge24$, *an estimate from the construction, not a computation of $d$ and $k$.* Separately, $u_n$ must be **freely described**, and the target carries $\Theta_n=\sqrt{2(n-1)(n-2)}/n$, irrational and $n$-dependent; whether $F_{\mu,n}$ qualifies is unsettled and independent of the degree question.
