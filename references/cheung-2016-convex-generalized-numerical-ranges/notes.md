# Notes, Cheung 2016

Setting: $M_n$, $U_n$ the unitary group, $W_C(B)=\{\mathop{\mathrm{tr}}(CX):X\in U(B)\}$ the $C$-numerical range, $U(B)$ the unitary orbit of $B$. Convex by Toeplitz–Hausdorff when $C=E_{11}$; convex when $C$ and $B$ are both Hermitian (Westwick); the paper asks for further convex pairs.

> **Theorem 11.** Let $A,B\in M_2$. Suppose $B$ is Hermitian. Then $W_A(B)=W_{A\oplus0}(B\oplus0)$ is equivalent to $(\mathop{\mathrm{tr}}A)W(B)\cup(\mathop{\mathrm{tr}}B)W(A)\subseteq W_A(B)$. If, in addition, both $A$ and $B$ are nonzero matrices, then it is also equivalent to $0\in W(A)\cap W(B)$.

(page 9). Theorems 7 to 10 are the containment lemmas it rests on. **None reaches the record's object**, which is a sum of two $C$-numerical ranges of a $3\times3$ pair sharing one frame.
