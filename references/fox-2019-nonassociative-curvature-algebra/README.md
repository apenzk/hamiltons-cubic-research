## Consulted for

Treats $\langle W,Q(W)\rangle$ in generality, without this specific maximization conjecture. Source of the $\mathcal S^4(\alpha)$ competitor and the hyper-Kähler subalgebras.

## Statements that matter

- Weyl (Ricci-flat) tensors form a **simple nonassociative subalgebra**; its idempotents are the critical points of $P$ on the unit sphere.
- Idempotents are identified with points fixed by orthogonal complex / hyper-Kähler-type structures.
- **$\mathcal S^4(\alpha)$**, the persistent low-dimensional competitor, matched term-by-term here with zero floating-point residual up to the $1/(r+2)=1/6$ prefactor.
- **Lemma 10.7(4b)**, the $r\ge8$ hyperkähler subalgebra $\mathbb B_r$, genuinely 6-dimensional, unlike the $r=4$ case.

## Verdict

Both hyper-Kähler families were eliminated: every nontrivial idempotent of the $5$-dimensional $\mathbb B$ is a rotated copy of $\mathcal S^4(\alpha)$ at $\sqrt{3/2}$, and $\mathbb B_r$ has exactly three idempotent magnitudes at $r=8,12$, all below both named competitors. One branch is the $\mathbb{HP}^{r/4}$ tensor, tying the two families together.
