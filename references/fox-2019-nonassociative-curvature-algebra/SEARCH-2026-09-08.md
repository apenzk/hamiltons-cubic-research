## What it returned: nothing usable, and that is the finding

**No Lean or Mathlib formalisation of algebraic curvature tensors, Weyl tensors or the curvature-tensor symmetries was found.** Mathlib carries some 115,000 definitions and 232,000 theorems and none of them is this object. *So [B1](../../plan.md) is not a matter of finding the right import: the base layer this record would need does not exist anywhere, and building it is the whole cost.* **That is worth knowing before another cycle prices B1 as small.**

## The one relevant hit was already on file

[Fox, *The commutative nonassociative algebra of metric curvature tensors*](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1901.04012), which this folder already holds, *the search fetched it a second time before the duplicate was found and removed.* Its abstract states the connection this record works inside: *"For a particular choice of parameters these algebras recover the polarization of the quadratic map on metric curvature tensors that arises in the work of Hamilton on the Ricci flow."* **That quadratic map is $Q(W)=W^2+W^\#$**, so the programme's central object is a member of a two-parameter family of metrized commutative nonassociative algebras.
