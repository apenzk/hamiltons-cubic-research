# The degenerate self-dual Weyl operator, and $\mathbb{CP}^2$'s spectrum

**Consulted 2026-09-08 while deriving [Lemma L176](../../findings/diagonal-half.md), to check whether the coordinate-diagonal rank criterion is known and whether $\mathbb{CP}^2$'s self-dual spectrum is standard.**

## What was checked

**$W^+$ is self-adjoint and trace-free on the three-dimensional $\Lambda^+$**, so its eigenvalues $\lambda_1,\lambda_2,\lambda_3$ satisfy $\lambda_1+\lambda_2+\lambda_3=0$ and it is pointwise diagonalisable. **A four-manifold is *degenerate* when two of the three coincide**, which is the class $\mathbb{CP}^2$ sits in, its spectrum is proportional to $(2,-1,-1)$, each entry nonzero, and $W^-=0$ by self-duality in the standard orientation.
