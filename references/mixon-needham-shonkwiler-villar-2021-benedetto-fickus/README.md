# Mixon, Needham, Shonkwiler, Villar, Three proofs of the Benedetto–Fickus theorem

- **Link**: [arXiv:2112.02916](https://arxiv.org/abs/2112.02916), also Springer (2024), [chapter](https://link.springer.com/chapter/10.1007/978-3-031-41130-4_14)
- **Authors**: Dustin G. Mixon, Tom Needham, Clayton Shonkwiler, Soledad Villar. arXiv:2112.02916 [math.FA]. Retrieved 2026-09-23 as a PDF, extracted to [2112.02916.txt](https://arxiv.org/abs/2112.02916), the statements transcribed in [notes.md](notes.md).
- **Consulted for**: the second-round step 9 sweep of 2026-09-23, looking for a SECOND-ORDER argument that forces a spectrum to collapse. The frame potential's global minimisers are tight frames, and the theorem says no local minimiser is anything else.
- **What matters**: Theorem 1 and the first proof, section 2. At a first-order critical non-tight frame it takes two eigenspaces of the frame operator with eigenvalues $\alpha>\beta$, a direction $Y=uv^*$ coupling them and lying in the constraint kernel, and computes the Lagrangian Hessian along $Y$ as $2(\beta-\alpha)<0$.
- **Verdict**: the mechanism is one the record does not name, and its ingredients all exist here. Proposed as route [R33](../../routes.md), the eigenspace-pair second-order test, with the pair direction $\pi_{\mathcal W_n}(u\odot v)$ in place of $uv^*$ and [L125](../../findings/second-order.md) in place of the Hessian computation. **Proofs 2 and 3 do not transfer**: convexity through Schur–Horn is closed by the first round's batch 2, and Kempf–Ness by [post F66](../../findings/fence-posts.md).
