# Li–Peng, Refinement on spectral Turán's theorem

- **Link**: [arXiv:2204.09194](https://arxiv.org/abs/2204.09194), published SIAM J. Discrete Math. 37 (4) (2023) 2462–2485
- **Identifier**: arXiv:2204.09194 [math.CO]
- **Consulted for**: the step-8 sweep after R12 closed, the search for an analytical *kind* of argument the programme has not tried. This paper collects the spectral extremal graph theory whose central statement has exactly the conjecture's shape one category down: a spectral quantity, maximized under triangle-freeness, by the balanced complete bipartite graph.
- **Verdict**: **the method family the programme lacks, laid out cleanly.** Nosal: triangle-free with $m$ edges $\Rightarrow\lambda(G)\le\sqrt m$, equality iff complete bipartite, proved by a walk count that triangle-freeness truncates. With Rayleigh's $2m/n\le\lambda$ this gives spectral Mantel in two lines. Nikiforov 2007: $K_{r+1}$-free $\Rightarrow\lambda(G)\le\lambda(T_r(n))$, equality iff the Turán graph. Methods: Zykov symmetrization (a rearrangement move increasing the objective while preserving the forbidden-subgraph constraint), Motzkin–Straus, and walk counting. Every ingredient of the analogy already exists on this record: Lemma 36 is the continuous Mantel theorem, $S_{\mathfrak{mmm}}=0$ is triangle-freeness of the Cartan 3-form, the $\lambda$-weighted identity is the Rayleigh step, and the eigenbasis identity $\langle v,W^\#v\rangle=\tfrac12\sum\lambda_a\lambda_b c_{1ab}^2$ is a bracket-walk count.
