# Chayes. Matrix Rearrangement Inequalities Revisited

- [arXiv:2009.04032v3](https://arxiv.org/abs/2009.04032), math.FA, 2 February 2021. Retrieved 2026-09-15 as [rearrangement-2009.04032.pdf](https://arxiv.org/abs/2009.04032), text in [rearrangement-2009.04032.txt](https://arxiv.org/abs/2009.04032).
- **Consulted for**: whether a maximum of a trace form over the relative frames of several symmetric matrices with fixed spectra is attained at commuting, aligned tuples, the question [I106](../../plan.md) at three or more outer indices turns on ([position](../../reviews/2026-09-15-I106-m3-position.md)). A search-engine summary asserted this for three matrices, and this was the one returned source that could carry such a statement.
- **What matters**: Theorems 2.1 and 2.4 (majorisation tools), Theorem 3.1 (the commuting case), and the counterexamples of Section 3.
- **Verdict**: **no such theorem, and a caution against one.** The paper treats Schatten-norm rearrangements of two matrices, proves them for commuting pairs, and shows they fail in general, because non-commutativity breaks the commutative rearrangement identity. It says nothing about trilinear trace forms such as $\operatorname{tr}(X_aX_bX_c)$. The search summary's claim for three matrices has no support here and is not to be cited.
