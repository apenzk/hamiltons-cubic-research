# Notes, Michel and Zhilinskii 2001

**Read at section 4, "Compact group smooth actions; their critical orbits; their linearization", lines 1181 to 1330 of the extraction.**

**The setting** (line 1236). For $m\in M$ with stabiliser $G_m$, the tangent space at $m$ splits into the tangent to the orbit, the tangent to the stratum of $m$'s orbit type, and a complement; the stratum's tangent plane coincides with the orbit's exactly when the orbit is isolated in its stratum, no other orbit of the same type lying in a neighbourhood of $m$. *In that case the gradient of any invariant function vanishes at $m$, independently of the function.*

> **Theorem 4b (Michel, 1971).** In the smooth action of a compact (or finite) group $G$ on a finite-dimensional manifold $M$, the gradient of every $G$-invariant function vanishes on the orbits which are isolated in their strata. These orbits are called critical.

*Line 1243. The survey adds that the converse was also proven by Michel (1971), and that a stratum containing finitely many orbits is closed, orbits being closed; a stratum is of maximum symmetry exactly when it is closed (Theorem 4a), and on a compact $M$ the closed strata are compact.*

> **Theorem 4c (Michel, 1970).** In the smooth action of a compact (or finite) group $G$ on a finite-dimensional compact manifold $M$, on a closed stratum containing an infinity of orbits, the gradient of each $G$-invariant function vanishes at least on two orbits of the stratum.

*Line 1256. "These orbits might be different for different functions." First proof in Michel (1970), Theorem 2, p. 133.*

## How this bears on the record

**Symmetric criticality, as the record uses it at every sphere product and at padded $\mathbb{CP}^2$, is Theorem 4b**: each of those orbits has isotropy $H$ with $\dim\mathcal W_n^H=1$, so it is isolated in its stratum and critical for every $O(n)$-invariant function, $P$ among them. **Theorem 4c adds existence on infinite closed strata and nothing about location.** *A localisation of the high critical set, which is what [the 2026-09-13c position](../../reviews/2026-09-13c-step8-position.md) asked this field for, is neither theorem's content.*
