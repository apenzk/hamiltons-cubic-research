## Consulted for

**Michel's theorem on critical orbits of invariant functions, stated by its author**, which the record has used as symmetric criticality since the start and cited to no source. *The [2026-09-02 sweep](../../reviews/2026-09-02-LIT-step8-sweep.md) called it "the only general tool" for maximisers of an invariant cubic on an irreducible module and left it unfiled.*

## Verdict

**The two theorems are here verbatim, Theorem 4b (1971) and Theorem 4c (1970), and both are necessity or existence statements.** *4b: orbits isolated in their stratum are critical for every invariant function. 4c: a closed stratum with infinitely many orbits carries at least two critical orbits of every invariant function.* **The record's rigid orbits, the balanced product, every $S^p\times S^q$ and padded $\mathbb{CP}^2$, are exactly 4b's critical orbits**, each having a one-dimensional isotropy-fixed space in $\mathcal W_n$. **Neither theorem bounds where the other critical points lie**, which is what the localisation node needs, *so this source names the known critical set and says nothing about the rest of it.*
