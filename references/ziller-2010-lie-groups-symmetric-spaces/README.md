## Verdict

**The mechanism is here, stated as a theorem with the $\mathfrak{so}$ cases worked as examples.** Proposition 4.43: $\mathrm{Aut}(K)/\mathrm{Int}(K)\cong\mathrm{Aut}(\mathfrak k)/\mathrm{Int}(\mathfrak k)\cong\mathrm{Aut}(\mathfrak g)/\mathrm{Int}(\mathfrak g)\cong\mathrm{Sym}(D(\mathfrak g))$, the symmetry group of the Dynkin diagram. Example 4.45 then reads it off for the orthogonal algebras: type $B_n$ has no diagram symmetry so every automorphism is inner; for $\mathfrak{so}(2n)$ the outer class is represented by conjugation with $\mathrm{diag}(-1,1,\dots,1)$, an element of $O(2n)\smallsetminus SO(2n)$; **and $D_4=\mathfrak{so}(8)$ has $S_3$.** Exercise 2.5(2) states the same $O(n)$ element lies in $\mathrm{Aut}(\mathfrak{so}(n))$ and outside $\mathrm{Int}(\mathfrak{so}(n))$.

**What it does not state is the consequence the record draws.** No sentence here says that two involutions of $\mathfrak{so}(8)$ can be $\mathrm{Aut}$-conjugate without being $O(8)$-conjugate, nor exhibits such a pair. The source supplies the ingredients ($\mathrm{Out}=\mathrm{Sym}(D)$, the $O(2n)$ realisation for $D_{n\ne4}$, the $S_3$ at $D_4$) and the record's step from them remains the record's own. The notes cover the whole extent of what is printed.

**AND THE SECOND READING FOUND THE OWED CLAUSE STATED OUTRIGHT**, as Proposition 6.38(c): *if $(G,K)$ is of compact type, then $G$ is semisimple and $G$ and $M$ are compact*, with a four-line proof resting on the same effectiveness clause the OIL definition carries. **So the Theorem's last clause is a citation rather than a derivation**, and it was on this shelf five days before the item that needed it opened. *Clauses (b) and (e) come with it: the simply connected splitting into euclidean, compact and non-compact parts, and euclidean type characterised by $[\mathfrak p,\mathfrak p]=0$.* Transcribed in [notes.md](notes.md).
