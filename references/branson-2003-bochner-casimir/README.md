# Homma 2003, Bochner–Weitzenböck formulas and curvature actions on Riemannian manifolds

- **Link**: [arXiv:math/0307022](https://arxiv.org/abs/math/0307022)
- **Identifier**: arXiv:math/0307022v1 [math.DG], 2 Jul 2003. 34 pages.
- **Author**: Yasushi Homma. **The folder slug is wrong**, the search result that surfaced this paper attributed it to Branson, and the slug kept that name; the paper is Homma's.
- **Retrieved**: 2026-08-23 (file date of [paper.pdf](https://arxiv.org/abs/math/0307022)).
- **Consulted for**: the enveloping-algebra home of the project's operator $T=2(n-2)\mathrm{Id}-\tfrac12\mathrm{Cas}$, whether the higher Casimir elements $\hat c_q$ of $U(\mathfrak{so}(n))$ supply constraints on $\mathrm{Weyl}_n$ beyond the quadratic one.
- **Verdict**: **right ambient algebra, second Casimir already spent.** Homma's Theorem 3.4 gives the commutation relations for the translated elements $\hat e^q_{ij}$ in terms of $\hat c_{q}$, and Proposition 3.2 lists the generators $\{c_2,c_4,\dots,c_{2m-2},\mathrm{pf}\}$ for $n=2m$. The record's [fence post 18](../../findings/INDEX.md) already fixes $\mathrm{Cas}=4(n-1)$ on $\mathrm{Weyl}_n$ as a consequence of $A(W)=2W$ and the Killing form, so $c_2$ yields nothing new. What remains live is $\hat c_4$ and above, degree-4 invariants of $W$ unreachable from the quadratic identity. Statements transcribed in [notes.md](notes.md).
