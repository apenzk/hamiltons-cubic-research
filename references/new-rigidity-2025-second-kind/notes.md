# Notes, Fu–Lu, arXiv:2512.21496

## Main theorem (abstract, p. 1)

> A closed Einstein manifold of dimension $n\ge4$ satisfying
> $$k^{-1}(\lambda_1+\cdots+\lambda_k)\ \ge\ -\theta(n,k)\,\bar\lambda,\qquad\text{some }k\le\lfloor\tfrac{n+2}4\rfloor,$$
> is flat or a spherical space form. $\lambda_1\le\cdots\le\lambda_{(n-1)(n+2)/2}$ the second-kind eigenvalues, $\bar\lambda$ their average, $\theta(n,k)>0$.

Also: Einstein of dimension $n\ge4$ with a cone condition strictly weaker than nonnegativity has constant curvature (Bochner), and a classification of 4-dimensional Einstein manifolds under a cone condition.

Generalises Dai–Fu [DF24] and Chen–Wang [CW24, CW25]; employs a result of Li [Li25].
