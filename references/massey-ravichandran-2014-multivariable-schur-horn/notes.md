# Notes, Massey and Ravichandran 2014

All statements are in a type II$_1$ factor $\mathcal M$ with a masa $\mathcal A$ and trace-preserving conditional expectation $E$, or in $B(H)$ with an atomic masa. $A\prec S$ is the multivariable majorisation the paper defines.

> **Theorem 23.** Let $\mathcal M$ be a type II$_1$ factor, let $\mathcal A$ be a masa in $\mathcal M$ and let $E$ denote the trace preserving conditional expectation onto $\mathcal A$. Let $A$ and $S$ be $n$-tuples of commuting hermitian operators with finite spectrum with $A\in\mathcal A^n$ and $S\in\mathcal M^n$ such that $A\prec S$. Then, there is a unitary $U$ in $\mathcal M$ such that $E(USU^*)=A$.

(page 25.) *The finite-spectrum multivariable Schur–Horn theorem.* **Not applicable**: the setting is II$_1$, and the record's targets do not commute.
