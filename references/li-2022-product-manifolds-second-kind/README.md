# Li 2022, Product manifolds and the curvature operator of the second kind

- Xiaolong Li. [arXiv:2209.02119](https://arxiv.org/abs/2209.02119), retrieved 2026-08-26. Pacific J. Math. (2024).
- Consulted for: the second-kind spectrum of the conjectured maximiser $S^{n/2}\times S^{n/2}$, needed for the sharpness condition of the transfer inequality (plan item 1(iv)).
- **Theorem 1.8 is the load-bearing statement** and it is a closed form: for Einstein $(M_i,g_i)$ with $\mathrm{Ric}(g_i)=\rho_ig_i$, the eigenvalues of $\mathring R$ on $M_1\times M_2$ are **precisely those of $\mathring R_1$ and $\mathring R_2$, together with $0$ of multiplicity $n_1n_2$ and $-\frac{n_1\rho_2+n_2\rho_1}{n_1+n_2}$ of multiplicity one.** Applies repeatedly to multi-factor products.
- Also: Theorem 1.5 (the constant $A_{n_1,n_2}=1+n_1n_2+\frac{n_1(n_2-1)+n_2(n_1-1)}{n_1+n_2}$ and $S^{n_1}\times S^{n_2}$ rigidity), Theorem 1.7 (the Kähler analogue, $B_{m_1,m_2}$, with $\mathbb{CP}^{m_1}\times\mathbb{CP}^{m_2}$ as the model).
- Li records that the spectrum of $\mathring R$ is known for only a few examples, space forms, Kähler and quaternion-Kähler space forms (Bourguignon–Karcher), $S^2\times S^2$, $S^{n-1}\times\mathbb R$, and $S^p\times S^q$, which is the list this project's two competitors sit in.
- Verdict: **supplies the maximiser's $\omega$-spectrum analytically**, so the sharpness condition of a transfer inequality can be written in closed form rather than measured.
