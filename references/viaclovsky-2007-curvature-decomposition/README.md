# Viaclovsky, Math 865, Topics in Riemannian Geometry

- **Source**: Jeff A. Viaclovsky, *Math 865, Topics in Riemannian Geometry*, lecture notes, Fall 2007, §4.2 "Algebraic study of the curvature tensor".
- **Link**: [math.uci.edu/~jviaclov/courses/865_Fall_2007.pdf](https://www.math.uci.edu/~jviaclov/courses/865_Fall_2007.pdf).
- **Consulted for**: whether the first Bianchi identity is exactly the vanishing of the $\Lambda^4$ component of $W\in S^2\Lambda^2$, and what constant relates the Bianchi symmetrisation to the wedge $\omega\wedge\eta$, L179(b).
- **Verdict**: **carries both.** Equations (4.15)–(4.20) give the Bianchi symmetrisation $b$, the identity $b(\alpha\odot\beta)=\tfrac16\alpha\wedge\beta$, $\mathop{\mathrm{Im}}b=\Lambda^4$, and $S^2\Lambda^2=\ker b\oplus\Lambda^4$ with $\ker b$ the curvature-like tensors.
