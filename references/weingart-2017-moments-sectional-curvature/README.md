# Weingart 2017, Moments of sectional curvature

- **Link**: [arXiv:1707.06369](https://arxiv.org/abs/1707.06369)
- **Identifier**: arXiv:1707.06369v1 [math.DG], 20 Jul 2017. 23 pages.
- **Author**: Gregor Weingart. Companion to [graphical-calculus-stable-invariants-2024](../graphical-calculus-stable-invariants-2024/README.md).
- **Retrieved**: 2026-08-23 (file date of [paper.pdf](https://arxiv.org/abs/1707.06369)).
- **Consulted for**: closed forms for the moments of sectional curvature, after [b_theta3_identification.py](../../scripts/lib/b_theta3_identification.py) tied $\theta$ to $\Theta_3$ and Weingart's 2024 §6 said the third moment "is related to" $\Theta_3$, the hope being a closed form for $\theta$ across the graded class.
- **Verdict**: **the identification the folder was opened for is refuted; the closed forms remain interesting for a different invariant.** $\theta$ is **not** the third moment: $S^3\times S^3$ and $\mathbb{CP}^3$ have identical $\theta=1.054093$ and third moments a factor 13 apart, $264$ standard errors ([b_third_moment_refuted.py](../../scripts/route-b/b_third_moment_refuted.py)). On Ricci-flat $W$ the cubic invariants form a 2-plane spanned by $\mathrm{tr}\,W^3$ and $\langle W,W^\#\rangle$; $\theta$ is their sum, $\Psi_3$ a different point of it. What the paper still supplies: Corollary 2.6 gives the moments as $O(V,g)$-invariant polynomials $\Psi^V_k$ on algebraic curvature tensors, so they evaluate **pointwise** and can reach $\mathrm{Weyl}_n$; Lemma 4.1 gives the moments of a product in closed form, so a sphere product's third moment is a rational function of $(p,q)$, the family Conjecture A says maximises $\theta$; Lemma 3.2 is Weyl's integration formula, the route to all symmetric spaces. **Two standing obstructions**: $\Psi_k$ is a moment of the **full** curvature tensor while $\theta$ uses the Weyl part alone, so the Ricci decomposition must be carried through explicitly; and graded critical points here are indexed by symmetric **pairs** $\mathfrak h\subseteq\mathfrak{so}(n)$, not symmetric spaces, so a closed form over symmetric spaces cannot close the graded enumeration by itself.
