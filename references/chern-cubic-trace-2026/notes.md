# Tan–Tang–Xie–Yan, what was checked

> **Theorem 1.1.** For every integer $n\ge2$ and every finite $\Lambda\ge0$, the set of values of $S=\lvert A\rvert^2$ attained by closed embedded minimal hypersurfaces in $S^{n+1}(1)$ with constant $S\le\Lambda$ and constant $f_3=\mathop{\mathrm{tr}}A^3$ is finite. (p. 3, paraphrased from the abstract and the statement's first line; the full statement carries a bound $\Lambda$ on $S$.)

The proof is by normal variations, a rolling-radius estimate and real-analytic sets (Sections 2 to 5). No algebraic statement about $\mathop{\mathrm{tr}}A^3$ is proved or used beyond its constancy.
