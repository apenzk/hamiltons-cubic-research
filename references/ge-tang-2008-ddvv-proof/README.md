# Ge, Tang. A proof of the DDVV conjecture and its equality case

- [arXiv:0801.0650](https://arxiv.org/abs/0801.0650), Pacific J. Math. 237 (2008). Retrieved 2026-09-14 as [ge-tang-0801.0650.pdf](https://arxiv.org/abs/0801.0650), text in [ge-tang-0801.0650.txt](https://arxiv.org/abs/0801.0650).
- **Consulted for**: whether the DDVV literature holds a cubic inequality on tuples of symmetric matrices with a commuting equality case, the shape of the $\lvert J\rvert=3$ obstruction after [L224](../../findings/second-order.md).
- **Verdict**: **quadratic in commutators, not cubic; the equality case is a specific non-commuting pair.** The mechanism (a rearrangement over rotations reducing to a combinatorial extremum) does not transfer to a cubic. Nothing here reaches the block question.
