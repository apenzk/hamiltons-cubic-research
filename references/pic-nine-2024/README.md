# Chen, Manifolds with positive isotropic curvature of dimension at least nine

- **Link**: [arXiv:2410.21078](https://arxiv.org/abs/2410.21078)
- **Identifier**: arXiv:2410.21078v3 [math.DG], 15 May 2026.
- **Author**: Zhengnan Chen.
- **Retrieved**: 2026-09-10, as [2410.21078.pdf](https://arxiv.org/abs/2410.21078), extracted to [2410.21078.txt](https://arxiv.org/abs/2410.21078).
- **Consulted for**: what was sharpened to lower Brendle's $n\ge12$ to $n\ge9$, since the thresholds match this programme's, found during the [2026-09-10b sweep](../../reviews/2026-09-10b-LIT-step8-sweep.md).
- **Statements that matter**: the sentence locating the threshold ("the restriction $n\ge12$ appears in Step 2"), Theorem 1.2, and the scalar evolution $\tfrac{d}{dt}\operatorname{scal}(S)=P\lvert\mathrm{Ric}\rvert^2+Q\operatorname{scal}^2$.
- **Verdict**: **the improvement is entirely on the Ricci side.** Sharper bounds on $\tfrac{d}{dt}(\mathrm{Ric}_{11}\pm\mathrm{Ric}_{22})$, using a lower bound for $\lvert\mathrm{Ric}_0\rvert^2$ and the simple scalar evolution, let the two cone families join at $9\le n\le11$. *No Weyl estimate anywhere, which is what the sweep needed to know.*
