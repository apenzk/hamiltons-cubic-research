# Lehrer & Zhang 2011, The second fundamental theorem of invariant theory for the orthogonal group

- **Link**: [arXiv:1102.3221](https://arxiv.org/abs/1102.3221)
- **Identifier**: arXiv:1102.3221v1 [math.GR], 16 Feb 2011. 21 pages.
- **Authors**: G. I. Lehrer and R. B. Zhang. Categorical companion: [lehrer-zhang-brauer-category](../lehrer-zhang-brauer-category/README.md).
- **Retrieved**: 2026-08-30 (file date of [paper.pdf](https://arxiv.org/abs/1102.3221)). Text extraction is in [t.txt](https://arxiv.org/abs/1102.3221).
- **Consulted for**: section 2 item 1, whether a family of $O(n)$-covariants is linearly independent **as functions**. Six attempts at that question were built and refuted (r247, r251, r253, r254, r255, r256) before anyone searched for the theorem.
- **Verdict**: **the threshold is settled in the literature, and it explains every collapse the record measured, but it does not close the record's question.** Theorem 3.4: for $r\le n$ the $\gamma_D$ are a **basis** of the $O(V)$-invariants on $(V^{\otimes 2r})^*$ and $\nu:B_r(n)\to\mathrm{End}_{O(V)}(V^{\otimes r})$ is an isomorphism; for $r\ge n+1$ every relation is a consequence of the single generator $E$, the antisymmetriser over $n+1$ indices. Sharp in both directions. This retrodicts r250's $I_2=I_1/2$ at $n=4,5$ with independence from $n\ge6$ ($r=3$ against $n$) and r251's measured law "matchings on $2k$ legs are independent exactly at $n=k$", **those measurements were rediscovering the SFT one dimension at a time.** The project's covariants are degree 3 in $W$, four legs each, so $r=6+\lvert\lambda\rvert/2$ per block $\lambda$; at $n=12$ the widest blocks sit at $r=n$ with zero slack. **Two gaps remain, both fatal to citing it as settling the item**: restriction along the degree-3 diagonal has a huge kernel (r260 measured $10{,}395$ upstairs-basis diagrams restricting to rank 2 at $n=6,7$), and it is unestablished whether the construction's weight-space vectors are honestly the $\gamma_D$ at all.
