> **Definition 1.9.** A non-zero tensor $A\in\bigotimes_{j=1}^dR^{n_j}$ is critical if $A/\lVert A\rVert_F$ is a critical point of the spectral norm function on the Frobenius unit sphere in the sense that $\lambda A$ belongs to the subdifferential of the spectral norm at $A/\lVert A\rVert_F$ for some $\lambda\in\mathbb R$.

> **Theorem 1.10.** A non-zero tensor $A\in\bigotimes_{j=1}^dR^{n_j}$ is critical if and only if the rescaled tensor $\lVert A\rVert_2^2/\lVert A\rVert_F^2\,A$ can be written as a finite convex linear combination of some best rank-one approximations of $A$.

> **Theorem 1.11.** A non-zero tensor $A\in\mathrm{Sym}^d(\mathbb R^n)$ is critical in $\mathrm{Sym}^d(\mathbb R^n)$ if and only if the rescaled tensor $\lVert A\rVert_2^2/\lVert A\rVert_F^2\,A$ can be written as a convex linear combination of some symmetric best rank-one approximations of $A$. Then $A$ is also critical in the space $\bigotimes_{j=1}^d\mathbb R^n$.

Pages 7 to 8 of the extraction. The best rank-one approximations $Y_\ell$ satisfy $\lVert Y_\ell\rVert_F=\lVert A\rVert_2$ and $\langle A,Y_\ell\rangle_F=\lVert A\rVert_2^2$, which is the pair of conditions that characterise them.
