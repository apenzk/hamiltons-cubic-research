## Consulted for

D6/D7's near-star statement for [I73](../../plan.md): a DDVV inequality restricted to a subspace of $\mathfrak{so}(n)$, whose constant improves when the subspace is spread.

## Verdict

**Confirms the mechanism, at the wrong dimension.** Theorem 4.4 gives a subspace DDVV constant $\tfrac4l(1-\tfrac1N)$ for a Clifford-algebra subspace of $\mathfrak o(l)$, of order $1/l$ against Ge's $\tfrac23$ for arbitrary skew matrices: a subspace whose isotropy is perfectly spread carries order $q^2/l$ bracket mass. Clifford subspaces have dimension at most the Radon–Hurwitz number, logarithmic in $l$, so the theorem does not reach eigenspaces of dimension $p\sim n$. Detail in [notes.md](notes.md).
