# Nienhaus–Petersen–Wink 2022, Betti numbers and the curvature operator of the second kind

- Jan Nienhaus, Peter Petersen, Matthias Wink. [arXiv:2206.14218](https://arxiv.org/abs/2206.14218), retrieved 2026-08-26. J. London Math. Soc. 108 (2023).
- Consulted for: the second-kind Bochner technique, Proposition 3.10 (the orthonormal basis $\{\varphi_{ij},\psi_k\}$ of $S^2_0$ and $g(\mathring R\varphi_{ij},\varphi_{ij})=R_{ijij}$, so sectional curvatures are diagonal entries of $\mathring R$), Lemmas 3.11 and 3.14 (Ricci bounds from partial sums of $\mathring R$-eigenvalues), Proposition 3.13.
- Verdict: **no cubic trace identity here**, the cubic form of the second-kind Bochner term on Einstein manifolds is Dai–Fu's Lemma 3.3, which cites this paper for the technique. Proposition 3.10 is the frame-level dictionary between $\mathring W$ and sectional curvature that makes the record's $K_{ab}$ matrix (fence post 21's vicinity) a restriction of $\mathring W$ to a diagonal subspace.
