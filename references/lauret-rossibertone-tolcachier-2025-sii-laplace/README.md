**Consulted for.** A legible modern transcription of Wolf's classification of non-symmetric strongly isotropy irreducible spaces (the 10 families and 33 isolated cases) since the 1968 original OCRs badly. Used here to enumerate the pairs $(\mathfrak{so}(n),\mathfrak h)$ that index the two-eigenvalue critical points of $P$.

**What matters.** Table 1 (the 10 families) and Table 3 (isolated cases with $G$ classical), transcribed in [notes.md](notes.md). Its own subject (bounds on $\lambda_1$ of the standard metric), is not used here.

**Verdict.** It supplies the enumeration, restated from [Wolf 1968](../wolf-1968-isotropy-irreducible/notes.md) and [Wo84], with the embeddings named. It carries nothing about $Q$, $P$ or curvature-operator extremality, so it constrains this project only through which subalgebras exist.
