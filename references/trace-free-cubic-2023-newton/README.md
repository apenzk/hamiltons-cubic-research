# Case, Tyrrell. A sharp inequality for trace-free matrices with applications to hypersurfaces

- [arXiv:2305.03861v2](https://arxiv.org/abs/2305.03861), math.DG, 13 May 2023. Retrieved 2026-09-14 as [tracefree-2305.03861.pdf](https://arxiv.org/abs/2305.03861), text in [tracefree-2305.03861.txt](https://arxiv.org/abs/2305.03861).
- **Consulted for**: the sharp bound on the cubic trace of a trace-free symmetric matrix, the term $\sum_a\operatorname{tr}(M_a^0)^3$ that stops the Gram argument of [L224](../../findings/second-order.md) at three-index blocks ([block note](../../reviews/2026-09-14-I104-block-J.md)).
- **What matters**: Lemma 2.1 (sharp Newton), Proposition 2.2 and its proof (the $\lambda$-method), Theorem 1.1.
- **Verdict**: **the per-matrix tool, with its equality case.** $\operatorname{tr}\mathring A^3\le\frac{n-2}{\sqrt{n(n-1)}}\lvert\mathring A\rvert^3$ for trace-free $\mathring A$ on $\mathbb R^n$, equality exactly at an eigenspace of dimension $n-1$, which for $3\times3$ is $\operatorname{tr}(M^0)^3\le\tfrac1{\sqrt6}\lvert M^0\rvert^3$ at $\operatorname{diag}(2,-1,-1)$ up to scale and frame. It bounds each block's cubic separately and says nothing about coupled frames.
