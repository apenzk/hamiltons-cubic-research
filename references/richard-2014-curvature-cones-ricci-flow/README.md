## Consulted for

Consulted during the search for a polar-representation or section technique applicable to a single critical point.

## Statements that matter

- A survey of curvature cones preserved by Ricci flow, and the cone-invariance framework.

## Verdict

**Negative.** No paper treats $(O(n),\mathcal W_n)$ as a polar representation, section, or cohomogeneity problem, and no precedent exists for applying such a technique to a single critical point rather than to a whole invariant cone.
