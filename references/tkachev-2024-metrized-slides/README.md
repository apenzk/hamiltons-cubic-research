## Transcription, 2026-09-02

The slides turned out to carry **labelled statements with proofs**, so [notes.md](notes.md) transcribes them: the $\mathrm{CMA}(u)$ correspondence, Theorem A (a minimal idempotent is semisimple and primitive with $L_c\le\tfrac12$ on $c^\perp$, with its Hessian proof), Theorem B (the Jordan-type fusion law $A_c(\tfrac12)A_c(\tfrac12)\subset A_c(\tfrac12)^\perp$, with its proof), the Hsiang identity and Theorem C's defining identity, Theorem D's Peirce fusion table, and Theorems E, F and H on the hidden Jordan and Clifford structures and the Hurwitz--Radon finiteness of exceptional Hsiang algebras.
