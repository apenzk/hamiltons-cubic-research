## Consulted for

Checked because the conjecture is attributed to a Beitz thesis in the Böhm–Wilking group.

## Statements that matter

- **Remark 6.3.15**, states the conjecture once, in the *cone-invariance* formulation ("the cones $\Omega_a$ are invariant under the ODE for $a\in[0,\tfrac n4]$"), never in the $P(W)$-maximization form.
- Its own contribution is a local Hessian computation near one boundary point.
- Never mentions sphere products, $\mathbb{CP}^2$, or diagonal Weyl operators.

## Verdict

**Not the source of the diagonal proof.** This closes the "Beitz thesis was not locatable" gap with a negative: the attribution is real but the proof lives in [Jäger](../jaeger-2016-diagonal-case/README.md).
