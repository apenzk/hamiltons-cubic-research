# Poon, Convexity of the joint numerical range

- **Link**: Yiu-Tung Poon, "Convexity of the joint numerical range", [faculty page copy](https://faculty.sites.iastate.edu/ytpoon/files/inline-files/21.pdf). Retrieved 2026-09-16 (`poon-convexity-joint-numerical-range.pdf`, 10 pages, extraction in the `.txt`).
- **Consulted for**: the 2026-09-16 step 9 sweep, batches 2 and 3, as the classical home of the record's $\lvert J\rvert=3$ frame problem.
- **Which statements matter**: Theorem 1.2 (convexity iff the linear image of the convex hull of the projection orbit equals the image of the orbit), Theorem 4.2 (three Hermitian operators is where convexity is generic and a fourth breaks it), Theorem 5.1 (the real symmetric case).
- **Verdict**: **the right field, and not yet a theorem the record can apply.** Theorem 1.2 is exactly the question the record's three-alignment relaxation asked; the record's functional is a joint $c$-numerical range of a non-commuting pair over full frames, which is the object the paper's $W_k$ generalises. What it does not cover is the norm trade between the two blocks, which post F68 shows is load-bearing.
