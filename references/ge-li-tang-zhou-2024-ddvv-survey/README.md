# Ge, Li, Tang, Zhou. A survey on the DDVV-type inequalities

- [arXiv:2402.01085](https://arxiv.org/abs/2402.01085), 2024. Retrieved 2026-09-14 as [survey-2402.01085.pdf](https://arxiv.org/abs/2402.01085), text in [survey-2402.01085.txt](https://arxiv.org/abs/2402.01085).
- **Consulted for**: any DDVV-type or Böttcher--Wenzel-type inequality that is cubic in symmetric matrices or whose equality case is a commuting tuple.
- **Verdict**: **none.** Every inequality surveyed (Sections 1, 3, 4: DDVV, BW, Hermitian, skew, quaternionic, weighted, Lu's inequality) is a bound on commutator norms, quadratic in each matrix, with equality at maximally non-commuting pairs. The open questions of Section 4 stay in that family. The cubic block question is outside this literature.
