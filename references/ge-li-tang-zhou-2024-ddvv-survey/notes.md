# Ge–Li–Tang–Zhou survey, transcription of what was checked

> **Theorem 1.2** (DDVV inequality [36, 56]). Let $B_1,\dots,B_m$ be arbitrary $n\times n$ real symmetric matrices. Then $\sum_{r,s}\lVert[B_r,B_s]\rVert^2\le\big(\sum_r\lVert B_r\rVert^2\big)^2$. (p. 2)

> **Theorem 1.4** (BW inequality [59]). Let $X,Y$ be arbitrary real (or complex) matrices. Then $\lVert[X,Y]\rVert^2\le2\lVert X\rVert^2\lVert Y\rVert^2$. (p. 3)

Tables 1 and 2 (pp. 4–5) list the optimal constants of DDVV-type and BW-type inequalities over the real, complex, quaternionic, symmetric, skew and Hermitian cases. Section 3.4 is Lu's inequality, a refinement of DDVV with the same commutator left side. Section 4 lists open questions, all on commutator norms. **Grepping the text for "cubic", "third" and "simultaneous" returns nothing beyond the anti-commutativity of quaternions.**
