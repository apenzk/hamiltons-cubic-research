# Li 2021, Manifolds with nonnegative curvature operator of the second kind

- Xiaolong Li. [arXiv:2112.08465](https://arxiv.org/abs/2112.08465), retrieved 2026-08-26. Commun. Contemp. Math. (2023).
- Consulted for: the definition and conventions of $\mathring R$ on $S^2_0(V)$ (eq. (2.2), p. 8: $\mathring R(e_i\odot e_j)=R_{iklj}\,e_k\odot e_l$), the $O(V)$-decomposition of $S^2(V)$, and the three-nonnegativity sphere theorems (Theorems 1.5–1.7).
- Verdict: **conventions source** for the second-kind operator used in the bi-spectral identity; its theorems are sphere/rigidity results under lower eigenvalue bounds and carry nothing about the maximum of the cubic.
