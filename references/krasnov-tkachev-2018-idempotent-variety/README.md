# Krasnov, Tkachev, Variety of idempotents in nonassociative algebras

**Consulted for.** The spectral theory of idempotents in Euclidean metrised commutative algebras (Fox's algebra is one), found by searching for machinery matching the reduction "maximizing $P$ = minimizing the idempotent norm".

**What matters.** §6.2: Proposition 6.6 is this project's Lemma 24, published, extremal idempotents have $\sigma(L_c|_{c^\perp})\subset(-\infty,\tfrac12]$ with $1$ simple. Proposition 6.7 is new to the project: a fusion rule $A_c(\tfrac12)A_c(\tfrac12)\subset A_c(\tfrac12)^\perp$ at extremal idempotents, proven by third/fourth variations. Transcribed in [notes.md](notes.md).

**Verdict.** Lemma 24 must be relabelled as independent confirmation of Prop 6.6. The higher-variation calculus and the fusion rule are tools the project has never used, and they act on products of eigenvectors, non-invariant structure, which is exactly what the recorded obstruction demands.
