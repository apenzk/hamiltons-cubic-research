The question was whether the route the file closed reopens.

## Verdict

**It does not, and this paper is the cleanest witness for why.** Theorem 1.2 takes $\lambda_1\ge-\theta(n)\bar\lambda$ as a **hypothesis** on a closed Einstein manifold and concludes global rigidity; it derives no bound on $\lambda_1$. And its own Proposition 2.1, $\mathrm{Scal}=n(n-1)\bar\lambda$, makes the normalising quantity vanish identically on $\mathrm{Weyl}_n$, where $\mathrm{Ric}=0$. The proof of Proposition 3.4 records the consequence in one line: *"If $\bar\lambda=0$, then $\lambda_1=\lambda_2=\cdots=\lambda_N=0$."* So on the record's class the hypothesis is not a weak lower bound but the statement $W=0$.
