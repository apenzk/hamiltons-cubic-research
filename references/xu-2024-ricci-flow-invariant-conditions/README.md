## Consulted for

Partial progress toward the conjecture; source of the Tachibana-trick estimate that the threshold mechanism runs through.

## Statements that matter

- The Tachibana-trick eigenvalue inequality $|\mathop{\mathrm{tr}}(T^3)|\le\frac{m-2}{\sqrt{m(m-1)}}\|T\|^3$ on the trace-free operator built from $W$, building on Huisken.
- The polynomial obstruction $(n+1)(n+2)(n-10)-2$, whose sign flips positive only near $n\approx11$, why his theorem needs a piecewise bound split at $n=10/11$.

## Verdict

Bounds $\mathop{\mathrm{tr}}(W^3)$ sharply, but that term is asymptotically negligible at the conjectured maximizer, essentially all of $P$'s value sits in $\langle W^\#,W\rangle$. This is the origin of **fence post 1**: no purely eigenvalue-based inequality can close the gap.

## A claim about a later version, checked and not found

**an outside report archive (unpublished, held in the private record) says Xu "still describes the full Böhm–Wilking statement as an unpublished conjecture" in "the 2025 published paper, and in the March 2026 arXiv version".** Checked on 2026-09-23: arXiv 2412.13633 has **one version**, v1 of 2024-12-18, and a search of arXiv for every paper by a Yiyan Xu returns no geometry paper after it, the 2026 entries under that name being machine-learning papers by a different author. **So the March 2026 version is unverified**, and what it is said to contain, that the endpoint is still open, is not a claim this record needs from it: [beitz-2018-bianchi-convexity](../beitz-2018-bianchi-convexity/) and this paper already establish it. A published 2025 journal version of 2412.13633 may well exist and was not searched for.
