- **Verdict**: **companion source, not yet read past the abstract and table of contents.** Its abstract states the results the project needs in categorical form: a presentation of the Brauer diagram category in four generators and seven relations; full tensor functors to tensor representations of $O(V)$ and $Sp(V)$ in characteristic zero; the first and second fundamental theorems generalised categorically; and new presentations of $\mathrm{End}(V^{\otimes r})$ obtained by appending **one** relation to the standard Brauer-algebra presentation, that relation stipulating the vanishing of an explicitly described quasi-idempotent. In the symplectic case with $\dim V=2n$ that element is the central idempotent of the degree-$(n+1)$ Brauer subalgebra (the highest degree at which the Brauer algebra is semisimple), an exact analogue of the Jones idempotent for Temperley–Lieb.
