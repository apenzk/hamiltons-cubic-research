## Verdict

Rigidity theorems (A, B, C) for **compact manifolds with harmonic Weyl curvature** under $k$-nonnegativity cones on $\mathring R$, Theorem A at $\frac{3(n-1)(n+2)}{4(3n-1)}$-nonnegativity for $n\ge8$. The machinery is the **Bochner technique**: it consumes $\nabla$ and compactness, neither of which exists in the algebraic problem. No bound on the cubic $p_3(\omega)$ appears that is not already in Li / Dai–Fu, and [post 26](../../findings/INDEX.md) already fences one-sided second-kind caps. **Nothing here clears posts 33/34.** Not extracted further; filed so the next sweep does not re-fetch it.
