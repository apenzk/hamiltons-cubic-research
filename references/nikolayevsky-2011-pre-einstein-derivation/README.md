## Consulted for

Plan item [T9](../../plan.md): if the pinned relaxation ((A), (B), (C) plus $T\ge0$), is exact at every catalogued spectrum, is the conjecture a statement about which *spectra* are realizable? The question put to this source is what the closest literature knows about the same two-layer situation: a linear system in the squared structure constants with a positivity constraint, sitting inside a realizability question.

## Statements that matter

- **Theorem 3**, a nonabelian nilpotent Lie algebra with a nice basis is an Einstein nilradical **if and only if** there is $\alpha\in\mathbb R^m$ with all coordinates positive satisfying $YY^{\mathsf T}\alpha=[1]_m$, equivalently the projection of the origin to the affine span of the root vectors lies in the interior of their convex hull. **At a fixed support the linear-system-plus-positivity relaxation is not a relaxation at all, it is the criterion.**
- **Theorem 1(c)**, all eigenvalues of the pre-Einstein derivation are **rational numbers**; and by 1(2), if $\mathfrak n$ is an Einstein nilradical its Einstein derivation is positively proportional to the pre-Einstein derivation, with $\varphi>0$ and $\mathrm{ad}\,\varphi\ge0$. **A published realizability obstruction on a spectrum, and it is arithmetic, no convex relaxation can see it.**
- **Theorem 1(2), following remark**, those inequalities are "necessary, but not sufficient" for a nilpotent Lie algebra to be an Einstein nilradical. The easy spectral test does not close the question.
- **Theorem 2**, the exact characterization is GIT: $\mathfrak n$ is an Einstein nilradical iff the orbit $G_\varphi.\mu$ is closed, iff $g\mapsto\lVert g.\mu\rVert^2$ has a critical point.

## Verdict

**The one published theorem of the shape "linear system in the squares, with a strictly positive solution, is equivalent to the geometric condition", and it holds at a fixed support, which is exactly where this record measured zero overshoot.** It says the pinned relaxation being exact at each spectrum is the expected behaviour of this kind of system rather than a coincidence, and it says nothing whatever about the maximum over supports. Its second contribution is the more disruptive one: the realizable spectra in the analogue problem are cut out in part by an *arithmetic* condition (rationality of the pre-Einstein eigenvalues), a kind of obstruction invisible to any linear-programming argument.
