# Bloch and Iserles, Commutators of skew-symmetric matrices

- **Source**: A. M. Bloch and A. Iserles, *Commutators of skew-symmetric matrices*, International Journal of Bifurcation and Chaos 15 (2005), [doi:10.1142/S0218127405012417](https://www.worldscientific.com/doi/10.1142/S0218127405012417); preprint DAMTP NA2004/04, [NA2004_04.pdf](https://www.damtp.cam.ac.uk/user/na/NA_papers/NA2004_04.pdf), retrieved 2026-09-28.
- **Consulted for**: [I121](../../plan.md), the sharp commutator constant behind the screened maximum $\tfrac56$ of $P$ on the surviving $+c_*$ modes ([note](../../reviews/2026-09-28-I121-MG-audit.md)).
- **Statements that matter**: Theorem 6 (the radius of $\mathfrak{so}(n)$, $n\ge4$) and the introduction's normalisation remark. Transcribed in [notes.md](notes.md).
- **Verdict**: settles the constant. In the plain Frobenius norm, $\lVert[X,Y]\rVert_F\le\lVert X\rVert_F\lVert Y\rVert_F$ for skew-symmetric $X,Y$ with $n\ge4$, sharp on an embedded $\mathfrak{so}(4)$, against $\tfrac1{\sqrt2}$ at $n=3$. It does not bound the triangle-minor cubic $F$ by itself.
