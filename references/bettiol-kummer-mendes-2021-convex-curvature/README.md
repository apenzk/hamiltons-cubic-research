## Verdict

The sets of algebraic curvature operators with a sectional-curvature bound are spectrahedral shadows only for $n\le4$; for $n\ge5$ they are counterexamples to the Helton–Nie conjecture, and only hierarchies of inner/outer SDP approximations exist. **Ambient confirmation of the analytic gate that closed route A**: even the quadratic (sectional) condition on curvature operators escapes exact semidefinite description at $n\ge5$, so a cubic extremal problem admitting one readable certificate for all $n\ge12$ was the wrong shape to hope for.
