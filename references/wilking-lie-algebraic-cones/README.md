# Wilking, A Lie algebraic approach to Ricci flow invariant curvature conditions and Harnack inequalities

- **Link**: [arXiv:1011.3561](https://arxiv.org/abs/1011.3561)
- **Identifier**: arXiv:1011.3561v2 [math.DG], 10 May 2011. Published in J. reine angew. Math.
- **Author**: Burkhard Wilking.
- **Retrieved**: 2026-08-31 (file date of [paper.pdf](https://arxiv.org/abs/1011.3561)).
- **Consulted for**: a bound on $\langle M^\#,M\rangle$, the term route B must couple, found while searching for the Lie-algebraic form of the sharp operation $\#$.
- **Verdict**: **the citation for a formula the record already had, and it opens the one target [fence post 33](../../findings/INDEX.md) leaves standing.** The definition quoted from the body is $R'=R^2+R^\#$ with $\langle R^\#x,y\rangle=-\tfrac12\operatorname{tr}(\mathrm{ad}_x R\,\mathrm{ad}_y R)$ for $x,y\in\mathfrak g$, **already implemented in [rsharp_basis_free.py](../../scripts/lib/rsharp_basis_free.py) and already stated in sources filed here**, so this note records the citation rather than a discovery. Why it still matters: the bi-spectral identity puts the target as $\theta=p_3(\mu)+\langle M^\#,M\rangle$, so the entire coupling problem is a statement about $\langle M^\#,M\rangle=\tfrac12(p_3(\mu)-p_3(\omega))$, and the $\mathrm{ad}$-form rewrites that term through structure constants and the Killing form rather than the spectrum alone, exactly the operator-level structure post 33 proves is necessary. **The margin to beat is known**: separate Okumura caps on the two cubics reach $4.0129$ at $n=12$ against $\theta_0(12)=1.236$, a factor $3.25$ ([findings/bispectral.md](../../findings/bispectral.md)), so any bound treating the two terms independently already fails.
