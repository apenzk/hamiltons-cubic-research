# Notes, König, On the sharp constant in the Bianchi–Egnell stability inequality

**Setting (p. 1–2).** $\dot H^s(\mathbb R^d)$, $0<s<d/2$, Sobolev inequality $\|(-\Delta)^{s/2}f\|_2^2\ge S_{d,s}\|f\|_{2^*}^2$ with optimisers the $(d+2)$-dimensional manifold $\mathcal M$ of Talenti bubbles (1.3). The Bianchi–Egnell stability inequality (1.4): $E(f):=\big(\|(-\Delta)^{s/2}f\|_2^2-S_{d,s}\|f\|_{2^*}^2\big)/\mathrm{dist}(f,\mathcal M)^2\ge c_{BE}(s)>0$ for $f\notin\mathcal M$, proved by compactness in Bianchi–Egnell (s=1) and Chen–Frank–Weth [6], hence with no explicit constant. The test sequence $f_\varepsilon=(1+|x|^2)^{-(d-2s)/2}+\varepsilon\rho$ with $\rho$ in the next eigenspace of the linearisation gives the **local (spectral-gap) upper bound** (1.5): $c_{BE}(s)\le 4s/(d+2s+2)$.

**Theorem 1 (p. 2, verbatim).** *Let $s\in(0,d/2)$ with $d\ge2$ and let $c_{BE}(s)$ be the optimal constant in (1.4). Then $c_{BE}(s)<\frac{4s}{d+2s+2}$.*

**The structural sentence (p. 2).** "Theorem 1 shows that the study of the sharp constant $c_{BE}(s)$ cannot be reduced to a local analysis near the manifold $\mathcal M$." Analogous to the planar isoperimetric stability inequality (see Dolbeault–Esteban–Figalli–Frank–Loss [9], [arXiv:2209.08651](https://arxiv.org/abs/2209.08651), introduction). Chen–Lu–Tang [7] give an abstract condition under which the global constant equals the local one; that condition fails for (1.4).

**Proof mechanism (§2, p. 3–5).** A precise second-order-plus expansion of the quotient $E(f_\varepsilon)$ along a two-parameter test family that mixes the second eigenfunction direction with a scaling of the bubble, showing the quotient dips strictly below the spectral-gap value at third order in $\varepsilon$. The distance $\mathrm{dist}(f_\varepsilon,\mathcal M)$ is expanded to the same order; the cross terms that decide the sign are odd in the bubble's radial profile and computed in closed form.

**What transfers.** The dichotomy, and only the dichotomy: for a functional with a manifold of optimisers and a defect $\delta$ vanishing exactly on it, the stability constant is the smaller of (i) the local constant, the extremal generalised eigenvalue of the pencil (Hessian of the functional, Gram of $D\delta$) on the normal bundle of the optimiser manifold, and (ii) the value at any interior critical point of the quotient. König's theorem is that (ii) wins for Sobolev; the record's Conjecture L16 asserts that for $P$ the reversed-sign analogue is attained at a specific interior point (the $\mathbb{CP}^2$-type competitor), so both (i) and (ii) must clear $\kappa_n$. What does **not** transfer: the test family, the eigenfunctions, the scaling invariance, $P$ has no scale and the balanced orbit is compact.
