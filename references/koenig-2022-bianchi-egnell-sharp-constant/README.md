# König 2022, On the sharp constant in the Bianchi–Egnell stability inequality

- **Source:** [arXiv:2210.08482](https://arxiv.org/abs/2210.08482), Tobias König, Bull. London Math. Soc. 55 (2023). Retrieved 2026-09-03, six pages.
- **Consulted for:** the step 8 sweep of 2026-09-03b, batch 3, the structure of a *stability-constant* problem $c^*=\inf (E(f)-E_{\min})/\mathrm{dist}(f,\mathcal M)^2$ read as the mechanism behind Conjecture L16, whose constant $\kappa_n$ is the same object with the sign reversed (a maximum, and a penalty $\kappa_n\delta^2$ with $\kappa_n<0$).
- **Statements that matter:** Theorem 1 (the global constant is *strictly* below the local Hessian constant $4s/(d+2s+2)$), and the sentence after it: *the study of the sharp constant cannot be reduced to a local analysis near the manifold* $\mathcal M$; reference [7] (Chen–Lu–Tang, [arXiv:2210.06727](https://arxiv.org/abs/2210.06727)) gives an abstract condition under which global constant = local constant.
- **Verdict:** a citation for the dichotomy the sweep uses to split Conjecture L16 into a local part at the balanced orbit (a Hessian pencil, computable from the record's L121 blocks) and an interior part. It settles nothing about $P$; its value is the *shape*, the local constant is a necessary bound on the global one, and whether it is the global one is the whole remaining question.
