## Consulted for

The diagonal case that the problem statement grants. Located by a citation chain through Kevin Poljsak's 2022 thesis.

## Statements that matter

- **Theorem A** (p. 113 of the extraction, quoted verbatim): *"Let $n\ge16$ be an even integer, and let $W\in\langle W\rangle_d$ with $\|W\|=1$ be given such that $W^2+W^\#=\theta W$ for a $\theta\ge\theta_0(n)$. Then $\theta=\theta_0(n)$, and $W$ is the normalized Weyl curvature of $S^{n/2}\times S^{n/2}$."*
- The sentence immediately after: *"The conjecture is that this is also true for all even integers $n\ge12$."*, so $n=12,14$ are open **even in the diagonal case**.
- $\theta_0(n)=\sqrt{2(n-1)(n-2)}/n$.
- **§§3.1–3.6**, the proof, ~50 pages, by contradiction via a scalar-curvature gap phenomenon, chained $\operatorname{tr}\mathrm{Ric}^3/\operatorname{tr}\mathrm{Ric}^4$ estimates, and a final appeal to the classification of compact symmetric spaces.
- His setup is this project's vertex matrix: symmetric $n\times n$ $(w_{ij})$ with zero diagonal and vanishing row sums.

## Verdict

The granted assumption is weaker than the problem statement implies, even $n$ only, and $n\ge16$. This project addresses $n=12,14$ by showing the threshold is rational rounding slack rather than a real obstruction, *conditionally, and **two** constraints carry Theorem A rather than one.* Odd $n$ remains open here.
