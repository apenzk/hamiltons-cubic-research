## Consulted for

The appeal to **Cartan's classification** made bare in [L23](../../findings/ungraded-slack.md), [L27](../../findings/nondegeneracy.md), [L08](../../findings/graded-half.md) and [L69](../../findings/graded-half.md), and recorded as sourceless in [conventions.md](../../findings/conventions.md). Specifically: the involutions of the compact $\mathfrak{so}(n)$ up to conjugacy, and the finiteness of the list of compact irreducible symmetric spaces.

## Verdict

**It states both, for the compact form, with the isotropy algebra printed in each case.** Chapter 8 runs the Vogan-diagram classification family by family and each family's summary paragraph names the compact form alongside the noncompact ones. For $\mathfrak{so}(n)$ the four cases the record consumes, $\mathfrak{so}(2n+1)$ with $\mathfrak k=\mathfrak{so}(p)\oplus\mathfrak{so}(q)$; $\mathfrak{so}(2n)$ with $\mathfrak k=\mathfrak{so}(p)\oplus\mathfrak{so}(q)$ at $p,q$ even and at $p,q$ odd; $\mathfrak{so}(2n)$ with $\mathfrak k=\mathfrak u(n)$, appear on pp. 96–99 and p. 103, and the twelve exceptional pairs on pp. 99–105.

**What it does not settle is the equivalence relation.** The classification is of *irreducible orthogonal symmetric Lie algebras up to isomorphism of the pair*; the notes never distinguish $\mathrm{Aut}(\mathfrak g)$-conjugacy of the involution from $SO(n)$-conjugacy of the subalgebra, and say nothing about $n=8$ or triality. That gap, the one [ATTEMPTS.md](../../ATTEMPTS.md) records paying for, is covered by [ziller-2010-lie-groups-symmetric-spaces](../ziller-2010-lie-groups-symmetric-spaces/), [etingof-2024-real-forms-involutions](../etingof-2024-real-forms-involutions/) and [yokota-2009-exceptional-lie-groups](../yokota-2009-exceptional-lie-groups/) instead.
