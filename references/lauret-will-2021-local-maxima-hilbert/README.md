# Lauret and Will 2021, Homogeneous Einstein metrics and local maxima of the Hilbert action

- **Source**: Jorge Lauret and Cynthia E. Will, *Homogeneous Einstein metrics and local maxima of the Hilbert action*, [arXiv:2111.07842](https://arxiv.org/abs/2111.07842), 8 pages. Retrieved 2026-09-27 as [lauret-will-2111.07842.pdf](https://arxiv.org/abs/2111.07842), extracted to [lauret-will-2111.07842.txt](https://arxiv.org/abs/2111.07842).
- **Consulted for**: [I117](../../plan.md)'s U$_{\mathrm s}$ ([L264](../../findings/second-order.md)), in the 2026-09-27 step 9 sweep: which critical points of a cubic-in-structure-constants functional on invariant metrics are local maxima, the question U$_{\mathrm s}$ asks of Hamilton's $\#$.
- **Verdict**: **the scalar-curvature analogue of U$_{\mathrm s}$ is open, and its known answers point both ways.** The bi-invariant metric on $SU(n)$, $n\ge3$, is neutrally stable and not a local maximum among left-invariant metrics (Theorem 1.2 (i)), by a curve with an inflection point: the same shape as [L265](../../findings/second-order.md)'s marginal $\mathfrak{su}(m)$ and the third-order saddle a screen found at $\mathfrak{su}(3)$. Stable irreducible symmetric spaces are the only known local maxima of the full Hilbert action, but G-stable non-symmetric local maxima of the invariant restriction exist (two isotropy summands, §3; the G-stable metrics of their earlier papers), so the analogue of U$_{\mathrm s}$ fails for scalar curvature on invariant metrics. The functional differs from $F/\lVert\cdot\rVert^3$ (weights $x_k/(x_ix_j)$ against $\lambda_i\lambda_j\lambda_k$), so nothing transfers as a theorem; the method (explicit curves through a neutrally stable point, Hessian formulas on invariant variations) is the one the record uses.
