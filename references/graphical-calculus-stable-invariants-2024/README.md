# Weingart 2024, A graphical calculus for stable curvature invariants

- **Link**: [arXiv:2404.16355](https://arxiv.org/abs/2404.16355)
- **Identifier**: arXiv:2404.16355v1 [math.DG], 25 Apr 2024. 38 pages.
- **Author**: Gregor Weingart. Companion to [weingart-2017-moments-sectional-curvature](../weingart-2017-moments-sectional-curvature/README.md).
- **Retrieved**: 2026-08-23 (file date of [paper.pdf](https://arxiv.org/abs/2404.16355)).
- **Consulted for**: whether Conjecture A's cubic functional is a named object in the invariant-theory literature, and what the full space of cubic invariants of an algebraic curvature tensor is, the class-ceiling question in clean form.
- **Verdict**: **the functional is named, and the degree count is the record's ceiling.** Lemma 6.2 defines $\Theta_3(R)=g(q(R)\star R,R)$ as a stable cubic invariant, and $\Theta_3(W)=-2\theta(W)$ exactly at every $n$ with no Ricci correction ([b_theta3_identification.py](../../scripts/lib/b_theta3_identification.py)). *An earlier version of the note recorded $+32$, wrong by a factor of 16 and a sign, corrected at Gate 2 by [Reviewer 69](../../reviews/REVIEW_r69.md) and [Reviewer 70](../../reviews/REVIEW_r70.md) independently.* The further reading that $\theta$ **is** the third moment is **false**: the paper says only "is related to", and $S^3\times S^3$ and $\mathbb{CP}^3$ share $\theta=1.054093$ with third moments a factor 13 apart ([b_third_moment_refuted.py](../../scripts/route-b/b_third_moment_refuted.py)). The paper's generator counts $1,2,5,15,54,270,1639$ in degrees $1..7$ give $26$ degree-4 invariants against the two the operator spectrum of $W$ supplies. **Two traps recorded**: Theorem 6.4 is an integrated identity carrying $\lVert\nabla R\rVert^2$, so it says nothing pointwise; and the paper's own $\Theta_3$ sign is internally inconsistent between Lemma 6.2 and line 2154, with Definition 6.1 the reliable branch, [fence post 19](../../findings/INDEX.md) flips on it.
