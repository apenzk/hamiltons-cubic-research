# Dai–Fu 2023, Einstein manifolds and curvature operator of the second kind

- Zhi-Lin Dai, Hai-Ping Fu. [arXiv:2311.18235](https://arxiv.org/abs/2311.18235), retrieved 2026-08-26. Published in Calc. Var. PDE (2024).
- Consulted for: the algebraic relation between the Bochner cubic of the Weyl tensor and the spectrum of the curvature operator of the second kind $\mathring R$ on $S^2_0$.
- Load-bearing statements: **Lemma 3.3**, for Einstein $(M,g)$, $\operatorname{tr}\mathring R=\frac{n+2}{2n}s$, $\operatorname{tr}\mathring R^2=\frac34|R|^2-\frac{s^2}{n^2}$, $\operatorname{tr}\mathring R^3=-\beta+\frac18\alpha+\frac{s^3}{n^3}$ with $\alpha=R_{tpsq}R_{tpkl}R_{sqkl}$, $\beta=R_{tspq}R_{tjpl}R_{sjql}$; **Proposition 3.1**, $\langle\Delta T,T\rangle=2R_{lt}T_{ijkl}T_{ijkt}-\alpha-4\beta$ for harmonic $T$; **Proposition 3.4**, the Weyl Bochner term on Einstein manifolds written through $\sum\lambda_\alpha^3$ of $\mathring R$.
- Verdict: **supplies the literature form of the bi-spectral identity.** On $\mathrm{Weyl}_n$ the two cubic invariants $\alpha$ (the $\Lambda^2$-trace of $W^3$) and $\beta$ are exactly the two spectral cubics, and $P(W)=\tfrac32\operatorname{tr}_{\Lambda^2}W^3-\tfrac12\operatorname{tr}_{S^2_0}\mathring W^3$ follows, **the second sign is minus**, in Li's $iklj$ order, which this file declares two lines above; see [notes.md](notes.md). Their bounds on $\sum\lambda_\alpha^3$ (Lemmas 4.4, 4.6) are one-spectrum bounds under sign hypotheses and are not sharp for Conjecture A.
