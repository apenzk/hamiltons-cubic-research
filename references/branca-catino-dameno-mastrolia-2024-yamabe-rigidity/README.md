# Branca, Catino, Dameno, Mastrolia 2024, Rigidity of Einstein manifolds with positive Yamabe invariant

- **Source**: [arXiv:2403.08647v1](https://arxiv.org/abs/2403.08647), L. Branca, G. Catino, D. Dameno, P. Mastrolia, 13 March 2024. Retrieved 2026-09-28 as [2403.08647.pdf](https://arxiv.org/abs/2403.08647), extracted to [2403.08647.txt](https://arxiv.org/abs/2403.08647).
- **Consulted for**: [I121](../../plan.md)'s small-mass node, whose threshold is $\sqrt{3/2}$, in a search for sharp inequalities between the two cubic Weyl invariants.
- **Statements that matter**: the estimate (2.7), $\lvert Q\rvert\le C(n)\lvert W\rvert^3$ for $Q=2W_{pqrs}W_{ptru}W_{qtsu}+\tfrac12W_{pqrs}W_{pqtu}W_{rstu}$, with Huisken's and Tran's constants, and the conjecture of Section 4.2 that $C(n)=\tfrac{\sqrt6}4$ at every $n$, from numerics at $n=5,6$.
- **Verdict**: **the conjecture is false at $n\ge12$, and it is the programme's own question.** $Q/\lvert W\rvert^3$ is $\tfrac12P/\lVert W\rVert^3$ (the full norm is twice the operator norm, [Huisken's note](../huisken-1985-ricci-deformation/notes.md)), so $C(n)=\tfrac{\sqrt6}4$ reads $\theta\le\sqrt{3/2}$, the padded $W_{\mathbb{CP}^2}$ value. The balanced product exceeds it from $n=12$ ($\theta_0(12)=1.236033$, and $S^5\times S^7$ at $1.228239$, [INDEX-values.md](../../findings/INDEX-values.md)), by Nienhaus's Proposition 2.25. Below $n=12$ it agrees with the record, where padded $W_{\mathbb{CP}^2}$ is the largest catalogued value. The paper supplies no argument for the sharp constant beyond $n=4$.
