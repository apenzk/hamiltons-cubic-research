## Consulted for

Plan item [T6](../../plan.md): whether the literature records any identity on products of structure constants beyond the Killing form, in particular anything that would give a second relation on the row marginals of $T_{abc}=c_{abc}^2$.

## Statements that matter

- **Main theorem (abstract, verbatim)**, "*We consider products of structure constants of a finite-dimensional compact simple Lie algebra, in which all indices except a few are contracted in pairs. We prove that such a product is zero if only one index is free, is proportional to the Cartan–Killing tensor if two indices are free and is proportional to a structure constant itself if three indices are free.*"

## Verdict

**It closes the invariant-theory side of T6 and supplies nothing new.** Every pairwise-contracted product of structure constants (of any length), collapses to $0$, to $\delta_{ab}$, or to $c_{abc}$. The record's relation (A), $\sum_{b,c}T_{abc}=2(n-2)$, is the two-free-index case of this theorem, and the theorem says there is no second, independent contraction to find. What the theorem does **not** reach is $T$ itself: $T_{abc}=c_{abc}^2$ is a Hadamard square in a chosen frame, not a contraction, so it is outside the class the theorem describes. That gap is the whole of T6.
