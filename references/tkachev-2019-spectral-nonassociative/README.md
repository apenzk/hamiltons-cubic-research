**Consulted for.** The survey companion to Krasnov–Tkachev: the cubic-form-to-algebra dictionary $V(u)$, and Proposition 2.5, existence of idempotents from the variational problem $\langle x,x^2\rangle\to\max$ on the sphere, with the extremal spectral bound $\mathrm{spectrum}(L_c|_{c^\perp})\subset(-\infty,\tfrac12]$ at local maxima. Transcribed in [notes.md](notes.md).

**Verdict.** Confirms the identification: Fox's algebra is a $V(u)$ for the cubic $P$, this project's critical points are its stationary idempotents, and the maximizer is an extremal idempotent in their sense. Their programme proves nonexistence/classification results at extremal idempotents by third- and fourth-order calculus and hidden Clifford structure, the same questions gate G2 asks, in a body of literature the project had not touched. Cites [56] (Jordan algebras of Clifford type) and [57] (the universality of one half, J. Algebra 2021) as the deeper entries.
