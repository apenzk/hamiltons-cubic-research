## Consulted for

A bound on the ordered bracket total of an orthonormal seven-frame in $\mathfrak{so}(n)$, the lemma owed by I73(c) ([L238](../../findings/second-order.md#lemma-l238-the-gaussian-form-of-a-seven-frames-bracket-total-and-the-isotropic-bound-42)).

## Verdict

**A row bound, weaker than the record's own for skew matrices.** The Lu–Wenzel conjectures bound $\sum_\alpha\lVert[B,B_\alpha]\rVert^2$ for one matrix against an orthogonal family, equivalently partial sums of the spectrum of $T_X(Y)=[X^*,[X,Y]]$. Conjecture 4 gives $\sum_{i\le 2k}\lambda_i(T_X)\le2k+2$ for general real matrices; for skew $X$ and six partners the record's [L237](../../findings/second-order.md#lemma-l237-the-ky-fan-bracket-cap-and-the-exact-ceiling-it-puts-on-l173s-ray) cap $\kappa_6=8$ is sharper. Nothing here bounds a whole frame at fixed size.
