(6) the ONE hypothesis the step needs, and a counterexample to dropping it
      the carrier must have n-INDEPENDENT coefficients.  Let I1, I2 be the
      two diagrams above, independent as functions for every n >= 6 (rank
      is non-decreasing in n by padding itself).  Put
          J1^(n) = I1 + (1/n) I2,      J2^(n) = I1 + (1/24) I2.
      At n=12 these are I1 + I2/12 and I1 + I2/24: INDEPENDENT.
      At n=24 they are the same function: DEPENDENT.
      Nothing is wrong with the padding map; the level-12 family is simply
      not the restriction of the level-24 family once coefficients see n.
      Trace removal introduces exactly such coefficients (1/(n-2), 1/n),
      so the 184 covariants must be carried by PROJECTOR-FREE diagrams.
