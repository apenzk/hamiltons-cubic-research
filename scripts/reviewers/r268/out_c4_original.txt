  ODD f REACHES NO EMPTY LABEL -- an up-down path of odd length cannot end
  at the empty partition -- so f = 3 and f = 5 contribute no row here.  That
  leaves TWO distinct leg counts, which is not a trend.

  AND `fraction of the diagram set` IS THE WRONG STATISTIC: the total grows
  factorially, so any fixed cost looks like a vanishing fraction.  The
  meaningful ratio is used/m_lambda, and at f = 4 it is about 10 -- flat in n
  across n = 2, 3, 4, which is the one thing these rows do show.

  WHAT THIS SCREENS, AND WHAT IT DOES NOT.  At the sizes reachable the
  extraction costs about ten diagrams per dimension of the block and does
  not grow with n.  NOTHING HERE REACHES f = 12, where the diagram set is
  316,234,143,225; two leg counts warrant no extrapolation.  The measurement
  says the pipeline is worth building, not that it will terminate cheaply.
