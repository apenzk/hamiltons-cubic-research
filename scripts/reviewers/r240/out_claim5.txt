==============================================================================
C.  MISREPORT IN THE 'NOT NESTED' DIRECTION -- the fatal one, because it
    is an assert that kills the run.  A pure CHANGE OF BASIS of V, which
    cannot change fact (ii), is turned into a failure by the 1e-9 rank
    tolerance
==============================================================================
    Basis change T_b:  q1' = q1,  q2' = b * (q2 - 0.5*q1).
    Invertible for every b != 0, and q2 - 0.5*q1 is the relation itself, so
    at n = 4, 5 the second column is PURE ROUNDOFF, of size ~1e-15*|q1|.
    Multiplying it by b lifts that roundoff through the 1e-9 rank tolerance
    at a rate that differs between n = 4 and n = 5, because the roundoff
    does.  ker eps_n is unchanged as a subspace in every row.

    Two steps.  First, how far apart the two roundoff floors sit -- they are
    independent samples of the same 1e-15-scale noise, so which of n = 4 and
    n = 5 is larger is a coin toss over the sampling seed, and the screen
    draws from an unseeded global rng:

    over 120 seeds, the n=4 floor exceeds the n=5 floor in 44
    of them (36%).  For any such seed there is a window of b
    with rank 2 read at n = 4 (kernel EMPTY) and rank 1 at n = 5 (kernel a
    line), and nested() returns False on an empty K_small by construction.

      seed    floor n=4    floor n=5           b   ker dims 4,5   ker5 in ker4
         0     2.37e-15     7.80e-16    4.43e+05         [0, 1]   False   <-- ASSERTION FIRES
         2     2.66e-15     1.98e-15    3.95e+05         [0, 1]   False   <-- ASSERTION FIRES
         4     1.81e-15     1.64e-15    5.79e+05         [0, 1]   False   <-- ASSERTION FIRES
         5     3.07e-15     9.76e-16    3.42e+05         [0, 1]   False   <-- ASSERTION FIRES
         6     2.35e-15     1.88e-15    4.46e+05         [0, 1]   False   <-- ASSERTION FIRES
         9     1.76e-15     1.69e-15    5.95e+05         [0, 0]   True

    FALSE ALARM WITNESS: 5 of those 6 abort with 'ker eps_5 is NOT
    contained in ker eps_4 -- fact (ii) is the padding step the whole
    reduction rests on', on a RELABELLING of the diagram basis that moves no
    kernel anywhere.  The tolerance, not the mathematics, decided it.
    b ~ 4e5 is not exotic: it is the ratio between the largest and smallest
    entry of a Brauer Gram whose entries carry n^0 ... n^6 at n = 12, which
    is 12^6 = 3.0e6 before any coefficient is counted.

==============================================================================
E.  THE PROJECTOR ITSELF IS FINE
==============================================================================
    K_small's rows come out of an SVD, so they are orthonormal; pinv(K^T)
    is then K itself and P = K^T K is an exact orthogonal projector.
    n=4: ||K K^T - I|| = 2.22e-16   ||P^2 - P|| = 0.00e+00   ||P - P^T|| = 7.85e-17
    n=5: ||K K^T - I|| = 0.00e+00   ||P^2 - P|| = 0.00e+00   ||P - P^T|| = 7.85e-17
    So the 1e-6 is a pure angle tolerance, not a conditioning artefact of
    pinv: the rcond default never bites here.  The failure modes are B, C, D.
