  restituting the basis functional e_{i_1..i_d}^* gives the MONOMIAL x_{i_1}..x_{i_d},

  At all nine cells assert-2 holds exactly when assert-1 holds, and it is the

  in particular it never looks at ker(R_d) as a SUBSPACE -- only at its dimension.

  rank is ALWAYS dim Sym^3(S*) -- the dimension of the SUBSPACE's symmetric
  cube, never dim Sym^3(U*).  So for every proper S the kernel has dimension
     total - dim Sym^3(S*)   >   total - dim Sym^3(U*),
