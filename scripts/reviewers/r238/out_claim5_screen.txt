A.  THE KERNEL DIRECTION -- the statement fact (ii) actually makes
==========================================================================
The screen reports dim(image).  Fact (ii) is about the KERNEL as a subspace.
At n=4 and n=5 the rank is 1, so each kernel is a line (a:b) with a*q1+b*q2=0.
Fact (ii) with n_0=4, n=5 demands ker eps_5 contained in ker eps_4, i.e. the
SAME line.  Measured, from 400 samples each, normalised to b = 1:

   n=4   rank 1   kernel direction  q1 : q2  =  -0.5000000000 : 1
   n=5   rank 1   kernel direction  q1 : q2  =  -0.5000000000 : 1
   n=6   rank 2   kernel is trivial
   n=7   rank 2   kernel is trivial
   n=8   rank 2   kernel is trivial
   n=10  rank 2   kernel is trivial
   n=12  rank 2   kernel is trivial

   |ratio(n=4) - ratio(n=5)| = 4.996e-16
   -> the two kernels are THE SAME LINE (fact (ii) holds here)
   The screen tests NONE of this: it only compares two integers.

B.  THE CEILING -- how much can the freeze assert actually reject?
==========================================================================
   rank sequences over 7 dimensions with at most 2 columns: 33 survive
   of those, the FREEZE assert rejects 8
   -> the freeze assert has 24.2% discriminating power at K=2 columns
   The ceiling is the NUMBER OF COLUMNS, not an attained universal value.
   Same enumeration with a wider diagram set:
      K=2    freeze rejects 8 of 33  (24.2%)
      K=3    freeze rejects 36 of 116  (31.0%)
      K=5    freeze rejects 330 of 786  (42.0%)
      K=10   freeze rejects 11440 of 19437  (58.9%)
