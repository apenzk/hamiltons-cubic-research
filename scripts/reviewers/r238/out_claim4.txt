CONCLUSION.  Contracting the free indices does land the check among complete
contractions -- but not among the ones r232 audited.  r232's grammar has every
one of the 4d slots on a Weyl factor, which is why no delta-loop can form.  A
bare metric on a covariant's free indices is a delta whose endpoints belong to
no tensor, and pairing two of them closes the loop:  g_ab g_ab = delta^a_a = n.
The free-index worry is not dissolved by the passage to complete contractions;
it is RELOCATED into the pairing, and that is exactly where plan.md's A2 says
it lives -- 'every Gram entry of two diagram covariants is a closed Brauer
diagram, VALUED A POLYNOMIAL IN N'.  Those two sentences cannot both describe
the same objects.
